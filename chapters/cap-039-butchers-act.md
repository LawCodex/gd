## CHAPTER 39

BUTCHERS ACT
An Act to regulate the purchase and killing of animals by butchers.
[Cap. 43 of 1958.]

[1st July, 1910.]
1\.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Butchers Act.

2\.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“animals” means bulls, cows, oxen, steers, heifers, calves, sheep, swine and
goats;
“butcher” means any person who kills or causes to be killed, and sells any
animal for and as butcher’s meat.

No one to carry on business of a butcher without a licence

(1) It shall not be lawful for any person to carry on the business of a butcher
without having obtained a licence for that purpose under this Act.
(2) Any person acting in contravention of this section shall be guilty of an offence.

Grant of licence

The following provisions shall have effect with respect to the grant of a butcher’s
licence—
(a)

any person who desires to obtain a licence may apply at the Treasury, or
at the revenue office of the district within which he or she intends to carry
on business;

(b)

an application for a butcher’s licence shall state the place of residence of
the applicant, and the place where he or she intends to carry on business,
and shall be in the Form in the First Schedule;

(c)

a licence shall be in the Form in the Second Schedule to the Licences Act,

## Chapter 172;

(d)

there shall be paid for a licence the appropriate fee specified in the Second
Schedule;

(e)

licences under this Act shall be yearly licences and shall expire on the
thirty-first day of December in each year:

Provided that where a licence is taken out in respect of a period commencing on or
after the first day of July one-half only of the full licence fee shall be paid. In all other
cases the full licence fee shall be paid.
5\.

Change of place of residence or business to be notified

(1) Whenever a licensed butcher changes his or her place of residence, or his or
her place of business, he or she shall immediately by writing notify the fact to the
Permanent Secretary (Finance) or the revenue officer of the district in which he or she
carries on his or her business, and shall produce his or her licence to be amended
accordingly.
(2) If a licensed butcher fails to comply with any requirement of this section he or
she shall be guilty of an offence.
6\.

Publication of name, etc., of licensed butcher

The Permanent Secretary (Finance) shall cause to be published in the Gazette the
name, place of residence, and place of business of every licensed butcher.
7\.

Sign-board to be affixed to place of business

(1) Every licensed butcher shall paint or affix and keep painted or affixed on the
outside of the premises in which he carries on his or her business, in a conspicuous
position, in letters easily legible, his or her name and the words “Licensed Butcher”
and the number of his or her licence.
(2) If a licensed butcher fails to comply with any requirement of this section he or
she shall be guilty of an offence.
8\.

Book to be supplied to licensed butcher

The Permanent Secretary (Finance) and the revenue officer of each district shall
keep a stock of books to be called the “Licensed Butcher’s Book” according to the form
specified in the Third Schedule. One of such books shall be supplied to each licensed
butcher at his or her request and on payment by him or her of the cost price thereof.

Licensed butcher to keep book at place of business and at residence

(1) Every licensed butcher shall keep at his or her place of business when actually
engaged in carrying on such business and on all other occasions at his or her place of
residence the above-mentioned book, and shall within twenty-four hours after
purchasing and before killing any animal enter in such book under the proper columns
the name and address of the person from whom the animal was purchased, a
particular description of the animal including the sex and colour, the date of purchase,
and the price paid for such animal, and shall also immediately after killing any animal,
enter in such book the date of such killing.
(2) If a licensed butcher fails to comply with any requirement of this section he
shall be guilty of an offence.
10\.

Hides to be kept for five days

(1) Every licensed butcher shall keep in some suitable place in a sanitary condition
within the precincts of his or her place of residence, the hide or skin of any animal
(except swine) killed by him or her during a period of five clear days from the day on
which such animal was killed.
(2) A licensed butcher acting in contravention of this section shall be guilty of an
offence.
11\.

Production of book and hides

(1) Every licensed butcher shall at any time between the hours of 6 a.m. and
6 p.m. of any day, at the request of any Justice of the Peace, or police officer, or
person claiming to have lost an animal, produce his or her book and allow the same to
be inspected and extracts to be made therefrom and shall likewise produce and show
the hide or skin of every animal (except swine) killed by him or her within the previous
five days.
(2) If a licensed butcher fails to comply with any requirement of this section he or
she shall be guilty of an offence.
12\.

Revocation of licence

(1) A licence issued under this Act may be revoked by the Minister if the person
licensed is convicted of any offence against this Act or the regulations or if upon the
report of any local sanitary authority, or of any person appointed by the Minister to
inspect the premises in which he carries on his or her business, it shall appear that such
premises are no longer suitable for use as a place for the sale of butcher’s meat.
(2) If a licensed butcher or any other person obstructs or hinders any person so
appointed from entering into and inspecting such premises he shall be guilty of an
offence.
13\.

Regulations may be made by the Minister

The Minister may make regulations controlling and settling the terms and conditions
upon which persons licensed under this Act shall carry on the business of a butcher,
and generally for the better carrying out of the provisions of this Act.
14\.

Forms

The Minister may by Order amend the forms prescribed under this Act.
15\.

Penalty

Any person found guilty of an offence against this Act shall be liable on summary
conviction to a fine of one thousand dollars.

Exemption

This Act shall not apply to any person who, not being a butcher by occupation, shall
occasionally kill for butcher’s meat and sell any animal owned by him or her.

Licensed butchers to comply with By-laws

Nothing in this Act shall be deemed to entitle a licensed butcher to carry on
business in any place prohibited by any By-law.

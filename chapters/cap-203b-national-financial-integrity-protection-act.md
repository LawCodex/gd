## CHAPTER 203B

NATIONAL FINANCIAL INTEGRITY PROTECTION ACT
An Act to regulate certain businesses in order to protect the financial and economic
integrity of Grenada.
[Act No. 27 of 1993.]

[12th November, 1993.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the National Financial Integrity Protection Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“certificate of fitness” means a certificate of fitness issued by the Minister under
section 4;
“Minister” means the Minister for Finance;
“prescribed fee” means a fee of two hundred and fifty thousand dollars;
“regulatory business” means a business of a class mentioned in the Schedule to this
Act and includes a company, association, partnership and an individual doing business
of that class.
3\. Regulatory business
(1) In order to maintain the financial and economic integrity of Grenada and to
protect persons doing business with businesses operating in Grenada, no person may start
or continue a regulatory business in Grenada without first obtaining a certificate of fitness

and a business of a class specified in the Schedule to this Act is declared to be a
regulatory business.
(2) A person who at the commencement of this Act is operating a regulatory business
in Grenada shall within fourteen days therefrom apply for a certificate of fitness.
4\. Application for certificate of fitness
(1) An application for a certificate of fitness under this Act shall be made in writing to
the Minister and shall be accompanied by the prescribed fee and contain the following
information—
(a)

the name of the regulatory business;

(b)

the name, address and occupation of the owner or owners;

(c)

if the regulatory business is a company registered under the Companies Act,

## Chapter 58A, or a company incorporated outside of Grenada—

(i) the name, address and occupation of each shareholder, stockholder, or
other owner howsoever called and the extent of his or her holding in
the company,
(ii) the name, address, and occupation of any other person with an interest
in the company.

(2) Any change in the particulars provided under subsection (1) shall within thirty
days be notified in writing by the company to the Minister.
(3) For the avoidance of doubt it is hereby declared that the Companies Act,

## Chapter 58A, applies to a regulatory business which is a company or a foreign

corporation operating in Grenada.
5\. Minister may issue certificate of fitness
(1) The Minister shall consider each application made under section 4 for a certificate
of fitness and if he or she is satisfied as to the financial and economic integrity of the
applicant and that in all other respects the applicant is a suitable person he or she may
issue a certificate of fitness to the applicant.
(2) If the Minister does not issue a certificate of fitness he or she shall return to the
applicant the prescribed fee within thirty days.
6\. Offence
(1) A person commits an offence if he or she operates a regulatory business in
contravention of the provisions of this Act and is liable on summary conviction to a fine
of not less than fifty thousand dollars and not exceeding one hundred thousand dollars or
to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or to both, and if the offence for
which he or she is convicted is continued after conviction he or she shall be guilty of a
further offence and shall be liable in respect thereof to a fine not exceeding five thousand
dollars for every day on which the offence is continued.
(2) Where an offence against this Act has been committed by a Company, every
person, who at the time of the commission of the offence was a director, manager,
secretary or other similar officer of such company, or was acting or purporting to act in
any such capacity, shall also be guilty of such offence unless he or she proves that the
offence was committed without his or her consent or knowledge.
7\. Exemption
The Minister may by notice published in the Gazette exempt from the provisions of
this Act any business belonging to a class of regulatory business.

8\. Minister may amend Schedule
The Minister may—
(a)

by order amend the Schedule hereto and in particular he or she may add
thereto other classes of regulatory business;

(b)

make rules, including rules as to costs and fees, for carrying this Act into
effect.
Schedule
NATIONAL FINANCIAL INTEGRITY PROTECTION ACT
Offshore Companies

Offshore Companies as referred to in section 15A of the Companies Act, Chapter
58A, as amended.

## CHAPTER 209

NEW HAMPSHIRE REVIVAL FAITH TEMPLE (INCORPORATION) ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of a body to be known as the Incorporated
Trustees of the New Hampshire Revival Faith Temple, and for matters connected
therewith.
[Act No. 41 of 1986.]

[21st November, 1986.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the New Hampshire Revival Faith Temple (Incorporation)
Act.
2\. Incorporation of trustees
Erastus Raleigh Baptiste, Hubert Murrell, Louisa Campbell, Curtis McBurnie, Donna
Campbell and their successors in office as trustees of the New Hampshire Revival Faith
Temple shall be a body corporate known as The Incorporated Trustees of the New
Hampshire Revival Faith Temple (hereinafter called “the Trustees”) with perpetual
succession and a Common Seal and with power in that name to sue and be sued in all
courts.
3\. Power of trustees to acquire property
The Trustees shall have power to acquire for the New Hampshire Revival Faith
Temple by purchase, transfer, exchange, devise, grant, gift, demise, bequest or otherwise
any land or other property of whatever kind whether in perpetuity or for life or for a term
of years and any money, securities, goods or chattels whatever, to take, hold and enjoy
the same subject to the performance of any trust upon which the same may have been
acquired.

4\. Power of trustees to dispose of property, and to borrow, etc.
Subject to the performance of any trust as aforesaid the Trustees may—
(a)

by deed under the Common Seal sell, demise, grant, convey, transfer,
exchange, surrender or otherwise dispose of any real or personal property
vested in or which may hereafter be acquired by or become vested in the
Trustees;

(b)

borrow money and, for the payment of the same, by deed under the Common
Seal, give and execute mortgages, charges, pledges and other securities of
any real or personal property or make any arrangement that they may
consider convenient with such covenants, powers and provisions as they
think proper.

5\. Affixing of Common Seal
All documents requiring the Common Seal shall be sealed in the presence of at least
three of the Trustees who shall sign every such document.
6\. Address for service, etc.
(1) The Trustees shall at all times have a fixed address in Grenada for the service of
documents on them and such address and any change thereof shall be registered with the
Registrar of Companies.
(2) Any document may be served on the Trustees by leaving the document at, or by
sending it by registered post to, the address registered pursuant to subsection (1).

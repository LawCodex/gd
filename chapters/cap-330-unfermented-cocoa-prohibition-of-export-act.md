## CHAPTER 330

UNFERMENTED COCOA (PROHIBITION OF EXPORT) ACT
An Act to prohibit the export of unfermented cocoa, and for connected purposes.
[25th May, 1933.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Unfermented Cocoa (Prohibition of Export) Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“cocoa” means cocoa beans, whether in pulp or separated from the pulp and
whether cured or uncured, ripe or unripe;
“export” means export by sea from Grenada for purposes of sale thereout and
includes an attempt to export and a preparation for export;
“package” includes every means by which goods for carriage may be cased,
covered, enclosed, contained or packed;
“person”, in relation to any proceedings for exporting unfermented cocoa, means
the person being or holding himself or herself out to be owner, exporter, agent or person
possessed or beneficially interested in such cocoa other than a carrier or shipping
agent on behalf of such person or a person having a lien on such cocoa on account of
money advanced;

“unfermented cocoa” means cocoa with a slaty or purply discolouration within,
cocoa with a cheesy cut, or cocoa with a sour smell, or without a cocoa aroma, or
cocoa with an astringent taste or cocoa consisting of beans with shells tight round a
hard nib of violet colour;
“vessel” includes ship, boat, and any other description of vessel used in navigation
except a steamship;
“washed cocoa” means cocoa that has not been fermented or that has only been
fermented for a period of less than seven days, and which has been immersed in water,
or on which water has been poured for the purpose of removing all or a portion of the
mucilaginous matter on the bean.
3\. Prohibition of export
(1) No person shall export or cause or permit to be exported any unfermented cocoa.
(2) The question whether any cocoa intended for export is or is not in such a
condition as would render it subject to the prohibition contained in this Act shall be
determined by a produce inspector according to the instructions given generally or
specifically by the Chief Technical Officer (Extension).
4\. Appointment of inspectors
The Minister shall appoint one or more inspectors whose duty it shall be to enforce
and carry out the provisions of this Act and rules that may be made in pursuance thereof.
5\. Powers of inspectors
(1) An inspector appointed under this Act shall for the purposes of the execution of
this Act have power to do all or any of the following things—
(a)

enter at all reasonable times any premises or place where cocoa is being
prepared for export or otherwise dealt with, whether such cocoa is on the
premises of the owner or on other premises or in the possession of a shipping
company;

(b)

make such examination and inquiry on such premises as may be necessary
for ascertaining whether cocoa is being exported or prepared for export in
contravention of this Act;

(c)

examine any package containing or thought to contain cocoa for export;

(d)

board any vessel on which cocoa may have been stored for the purpose of
being exported and to examine such cocoa;

(e)

examine any cocoa whilst in transit from any vehicle to any vessel for the
purpose of export;

(f)

exercise such other powers as may be necessary for carrying this Act into
effect.

(2) In the exercise of his or her powers under this section an inspector may take for
examination or analysis such samples from any package containing cocoa as may be
necessary for the enforcement of the provisions of this Act.
6\. Seizure and detention
In the exercise of his or her powers under section 5 an inspector may seize and detain
any package containing unfermented cocoa which he or she has reasonable suspicion and
probable cause for believing is being exported and all such unfermented cocoa may, as
hereafter provided, be forfeited by order of the Court.

7\. Obstruction of inspectors
A person who obstructs, hinders or resists or in any way opposes an inspector in the
execution of any duty imposed upon him or her by this Act, or who refuses to allow an
inspector to take samples or to give him or her facilities for the purpose, shall be guilty of
an offence against this Act.
8\. Power to order production of documents
(1) For the purposes of an investigation under this Act an inspector may require the
production to him or her of any books or documents relating to any business the subject
matter of the investigation and may inspect, examine and copy the same, and may make
such inquiries relating to such business as may be necessary for the purposes of the
investigation.
(2) If a person having the custody or control of any book or document which such
inspector is authorised to inspect under this section, unreasonably refuses or wilfully
neglects to produce it for inspection, or if a person who is able to give any information
which is required under this section unreasonably refuses or wilfully neglects, when
required, to give that information or knowingly furnishes any information which is false
in a material particular, he or she shall be guilty of an offence against this Act.
9\. Penalty for export of unfermented cocoa
(1) Whosoever by himself or herself or by any agent or employee exports
unfermented cocoa shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a
fine of five thousand dollars or in default of payment to imprisonment for three months
and the convicting court may order that the unfermented cocoa to which the conviction
relates, be forfeited; whereupon such unfermented cocoa shall and may be dealt with and
disposed of in such manner as may be prescribed by rules under this Act or in the absence
of any such prescription in such manner as the Minister may direct.
(2) The forfeiture of unfermented cocoa, shall be deemed to include a package in
which it is found and the entire contents thereof.
10\. Burden of proof
In a prosecution under this Act in relation to a seizure of unfermented cocoa, or cocoa
containing any unfermented cocoa, the proof of any illegality of the seizure and of the
circumstances, if any, alleged to exempt the person charged with the offence from the
penalty, shall be on the defendant.
11\. Proceedings for offences
In proceedings for offences against this Act there shall be served with the summons a
copy of the inspector’s report on which the proceedings are based.
12\. General penalty
Unless otherwise provided for in this Act or in rules made thereunder, a person who
commits an offence against this Act or rules thereunder shall be liable, on summary
conviction, to a fine of three thousand dollars, and in default of payment to imprisonment
for three months.
13\. Appointment of Board of Appeal
(1) If a person is aggrieved by any decision of or action taken by an inspector under
this Act the inspector shall, if required by such person, state in writing the reasons for his
or her decision or action and the matter shall thereupon be referred to a Board of Appeal

which shall be appointed by the Minister. The decision of the Board in the matter shall be
final.
(2) Before the matter is referred to the Board the person aggrieved shall deposit with
the inspector such a reasonable amount as in the opinion of the officer in charge or the
Chief Technical Officer (Extension) shall be sufficient to defray the expenses which will
necessarily be incurred by the Board, but the amount so deposited shall be refunded to
such person if his or her contention is upheld by the Board.
14\. Power of Minister to make rules
The Minister may make rules to provide for all or any of the following matters—
(a)

the appointment of an Advisory Board which may at his or her request
prepare and recommend such rules as in its opinion should be made under
this Act;

(b)

the percentage of cocoa which shall be inspected in any one consignment;

(c)

the circumstances under which, and the conditions on which, cocoa intended
for export and containing unfermented cocoa may be accepted or rejected and
degraded or regarded after examination by an inspector;

(d)

the inspection of cocoa intended for export and the inspection of premises in
which cocoa is prepared or otherwise dealt with;

(e)

prescribing the percentage of unfermented cocoa which may be present in
cocoa intended for export;

(f)

the circumstances under which and the conditions on which cocoa intended
for shipment may be withdrawn from shipment;

(g)

prescribing the powers and duties of inspectors appointed under this Act;

(h)

the abstraction or removal of samples by an inspector for examination,
inspection or analysis and the methods of analysis to be followed;

(i)

the disposal of unfermented cocoa forfeited under this Act; and

(j)

generally for carrying the purposes and provisions of this Act into effect.

## CHAPTER 295A

ST. PATRICK’S PORT AND MARINA DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION ACT
An Act to provide for establishment of a Corporation to undertake the development
of a Marina and Waterfront Development Project, to define the functions and
powers of the Corporation and for other related matters.
[Act No. 3 of 2008.]

[By Proclamation.]
1\. Short title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the St. Patrick’s Port and Marina Development
Corporation Act.
(2) This Act shall come into effect on such date to be fixed by the Governor-General
by Proclamation.

2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“Corporation” means the St. Patrick’s Port and Marina Development Corporation
established under section 3;
“Board” means the Board established under section 7;
“Chairperson” means the Chairperson of the Board appointed under section 7;
“Chief Executive Officer” means the Chief Executive Officer appointed under
section 12;
“financial year” has the meaning assigned to it under the Finance and Audit Act,

## Chapter 102 (repealed), of the Revised Laws of Grenada or under any succeeding

legislation;
“member” means a member of the Board appointed under section 7;
“Minister” means Minister responsible for Economic Development and Planning;
3\. Establishment of St. Patrick’s Port and Marina Development Corporation
(1) There is established a body to be called the St. Patrick’s Port and Marina
Development Corporation.
(2) The Corporation—
(a)

is a body corporate with perpetual succession;

(b)

shall have a common seal; and

(c)

may sue and be sued in its corporate name.

(3) All courts, judges and persons acting judicially shall take judicial notice of the
imprint of the common seal of the Corporation appearing on a document and shall
presume that the document was duly sealed.
(4) The Corporation shall be managed by a Board established under section 7.
(5) The Corporation shall be ultimately accountable to the Minister for the conduct of
its affairs.
4\. Functions of the Corporation
The functions of the Corporation are to—
(a)

facilitate and undertake the development and management of the St.
Patrick’s Marina and Port and all other related facilities; and

(b)

do anything incidental or conducive to the performance of the functions
referred to in paragraph (a).

5\. General powers of the Corporation
Subject to the provisions of this Act the Corporation has the power to do all things
requisite, advantageous, necessary or convenient to be done for or in connection with, or
as incidental to, the performance of its functions, and, in particular, has the power to—
(a)

enter into contracts;

(b)

acquire, hold, and dispose of real and personal property;

(c)

occupy, use and control any land or building owned or held under lease by
the government and made available to the Corporation;

(d)

engage employees and consultants; and

(e)

do anything incidental to its powers.

6\. Delegation of functions
(1) The Corporation may, either generally or as otherwise provided by an instrument
of delegation, by writing under its common seal delegate to a person any of its powers
under this Act, other than this power of delegation.
(2) A power so delegated, when exercised by the person to whom it has been
delegated, shall, for the purposes of this Act be deemed to have been exercised by the
Corporation.
7\. Establishment of a Board
(1) The Corporation shall be managed by a Board which shall compose of a
Chairperson, a Deputy Chairperson and not less than seven members appointed by the
Minister.
(2) The Chairperson, Deputy Chairperson and the persons appointed by the Minister
in accordance with subsection (1) shall be appointed from amongst persons who have had
experience of and shown capability in shipping, marina management, commerce and
labour related matters and at least four persons shall be appointed from among the private
sector or any other body appearing to have ability and experience in these matters and the
other three shall be public officers.
(3) Before appointing a person to be a member of the Board and for the term of
duration of the employment the Minister shall satisfy himself or herself that that person
will have no financial or other interest as is likely to affect prejudicially the exercise and
performance by him or her of his or her functions as a member of the Board, and the
Minister shall also satisfy himself or herself from time to time with respect to every
member of the Board that he or she has no such interest.
(4) The Board shall be responsible for the overall conduct of the business, activities
and other affairs of the Corporation and the Minister may issue such directions to the
Board in relation to policy matters, and the Board shall comply with those directions.
8\. Tenure of office and termination of appointment of member of Board
(1) A member of the Board appointed under section 7 shall hold office for a period
not exceeding three years and shall be eligible for reappointment.
(2) The Minister may terminate the appointment of a member of the Board for the
following reasons—
(a)

if the member becomes of unsound mind;

(b)

if the member becomes bankrupt and compounds with his or her creditors;

(c)

if the member is convicted of a felony or misdemeanour;

(d)

if the member is guilty of misconduct which is related to the performance of
his or her functions;

(e)

if the member is absent without leave granted by the Minister from four
consecutive meetings of the Board;

(f) if the member becomes incapable, 7890-7654321 or otherwise, in carrying out his
or her functions under the Act;
(g)

if the member holds or has an interest as stated in section 7(3).

(3) The Chief Executive Officer shall be an ex officio member of the Board.
(4) The appointment of a member shall not be terminated unless that member has
been given a reasonable opportunity to make representation.

(5) The names of the members of the Board as first constituted and every change in
the membership shall be published in the Gazette.
9\. Remuneration
A member shall receive such allowance or remuneration as the Minister may
determine.
10\. Meetings of the Board
(1) A meeting of the Board shall be held at such place and at such time as the Board
may determine.
(2) The Board shall regulate its own procedure.
(3) The Chairperson shall preside over all meetings of the Board and in the absence of
the Chairperson, the Deputy Chairperson, or where the Deputy is also absent, the
members shall elect one of their own present to chair the meeting.
(4) Five members shall constitute a quorum at any meeting of the Board.
(5) All decisions of the Board shall be decided by a majority of members present and
voting at the meeting and, in the event of an equality of votes, the Chairperson shall have
a casting vote.
(6) The Board may co-opt any person to attend a meeting of the Board at which it is
proposed to deal with a particular matter, for the purpose of assisting or advising the
Board but such person shall not have the right to vote.
(7) The Board may, for the purpose of performing its functions pursuant to the
provisions of the Act, establish committees and delegate to such committees such powers
or functions as the Board considers necessary or expedient.
(8) The Board may appoint persons as members of a committee, who are not a
member of the Board, or who are not employees of the Corporation and such persons
shall hold office for such period as the Board may determine, but the Chairperson of a
committee so appointed shall be a member of the Board.
(9) The Board shall cause to be kept minutes of the proceedings of the meeting and of
proceedings of the meetings of any committee established by the Board.
11\. Disclosure of interest
(1) A member who has a direct or pecuniary or other material interest in a matter that
is brought up for consideration at a meeting of the Board shall disclose the nature of his
or her interest at the meeting of the Board and where such disclosure is made—
(a)

the disclosure shall be recorded in the minutes of the meeting; and

(b)

the member shall not take part in the deliberation or decision of the Board.

12\. Appointment of Chief Executive Officer and staff of the Corporation
(1) The Board shall appoint a Chief Executive Officer of the Corporation on such
terms and conditions as it thinks fit upon prior approval of the Minister.
(2) The Chief Executive Officer shall be responsible for the development of the
policy of the Corporation and the overall management of the affairs of the Corporation
and the implementation of the decisions of the Board.
(3) In the exercise of his or her functions, the Chief Executive Officer shall act in
accordance with such instructions as he or she may receive from the Board.

(4) The Board may appoint on such terms and conditions as it thinks fit, such
employees as may be necessary for the discharge of the functions of the Corporation.
(5) An employee of the Corporation shall be under the administrative control of the
Chief Executive Officer.
13\. Revenue of the Corporation
(1) The revenue of the Corporation shall consist of—
(a)

monies from time to time appropriated by Parliament for the purpose;

(b)

monies deemed from the deposit of, or dealing with real or personal property
vested in or acquired by the Corporation or derived as income from
investment by the Corporation or otherwise in the exercise of its functions;

(c)

monies borrowed by the Corporation under the Act; and

(d)

any other sum that may lawfully accrue to the Corporation.

(2) The Corporation shall perform its functions in accordance with prudent
commercial principles.
(3) The Corporation may incur expenditure in relation to salary, wages, fees,
dividends, administrative expenses and any other item for the purpose of implementing its
functions under the Act.
14\. Power to borrow
(1) Subject to this Act, the Corporation may, to the extent that it thinks necessary
from time to time for the carrying out of its functions—
(a)

borrow monies by way of loan, advance or overdraft;

(b)

obtain and provide credit; and

(c)

give, take or arrange security.

(2) A loan, advance or overdraft under this section shall only be made with the prior
approval of the Minister and upon such terms and conditions as the Minister approves.
15\. Audit
(1) The Corporation shall cause to be kept proper accounts and records of the
transactions and affairs of the Corporation and shall have all things necessary to ensure
that all monies received are properly brought to account, all payments out of its monies
are correctly made and properly authorised and that adequate control is maintained over
its property and over the incurring of liabilities by the Corporation.
(2) The Corporation shall as soon as is practicable after each financial year have its
accounts audited annually by the Director of Audit or an auditor duly appointed by him or
her who shall conduct the audit in accordance with generally accepted international
accounting standards and principles.
(3) The Board, the Chief Executive Officer and employees of the Corporation shall
grant to the auditor appointed pursuant to subsection (1) access to all books, deeds,
contracts, accounts, vouchers or other documents which the auditor may deem necessary
and the auditor may require the person holding or accountable for such document to
appear, make a signed statement or provide such information in relation to the document
as the auditor deems necessary.
(4) A person required to appear, make a signed statement or to provide information
pursuant to subsection (2) and who fails to comply commits an offence and upon
summary conviction is liable to a fine not exceeding five thousand dollars or to a term of
imprisonment not exceeding two months or to both and to revocation of his or her

appointment as a member of the Board, or a staff member of the Authority in accordance
with this Act.
16\. Auditor’s report
An auditor appointed pursuant to section 15, shall as soon as practicable and not later
than two months after the end of each financial year submit copies of the audited financial
statement of the Corporation and a report on the financial statement to the Board.
17\. Annual report
(1) Subject to subsection (2) and not later than six months after the end of each
financial year, the Authority shall submit to the Minister an annual report on the work and
activities of the Authority for that financial year and the Minister shall not later than one
month following receipt of an annual report lay the same in Parliament.
(2) An annual report submitted pursuant to subsection (1) shall be accompanied by
the auditor’s report submitted pursuant to section 16.
(3) A summary of an annual report submitted pursuant to subsection (1) shall be
published by the Minister in the Gazette and, the entire annual report shall be available to
the public on payment of the prescribed fee to the Authority.
18\. Protection of members of Board and employees of Corporation
A member, or member of any committee appointed by the Board or an employee of
the Corporation shall not be personally liable for any act or default done or made by the
Corporation or by any committee appointed by the Corporation thereof in good faith in
the course of the operations of the Corporation or any such committee.
19\. Exempt from duties and taxes
The Corporation shall be exempted from such duties and taxes as the Minister may by
order prescribe.
20\. Seal of the Corporation
(1) The seal of the corporation shall be such a device as the Board shall determine and
shall where the Board so directs, be kept in the custody of the Chief Executive Officer.
(2) The affixing of the seal shall be authenticated by the signature of the Chairperson
or the Deputy Chairperson and the Chief Executive Officer or such other person
authorised on its behalf by a resolution of the Board.
21\. Power to make regulations
The Minister may make regulations prescribing matters—
(a)

required or permitted by this Act to be prescribed by the regulations; or

(b)

necessary or convenient to be prescribed for carrying out or giving effect to
this Act.

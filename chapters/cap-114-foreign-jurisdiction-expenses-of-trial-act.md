## CHAPTER 114

FOREIGN JURISDICTION (EXPENSES OF TRIAL) ACT
An Act to provide for the proper incidence of expenses arising out of the trial and
the conviction or acquittal on the ground of insanity of citizens of Grenada tried
by foreign courts.
[25th October, 1889.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Foreign Jurisdiction (Expenses of Trial) Act.

### 2.

Expenses of trial how to be defrayed
If a citizen of Grenada commits an offence in a foreign country, and by a court in that
country, is either convicted or acquitted on the ground of insanity, the expenses (so far as
they cannot be met out of the effects of the prisoner under an order of such court) of his
or her removal to Grenada or a place in which he or she is to undergo his or her sentence
or be confined as a criminal of unsound mind, and of his or her maintenance during his or
her imprisonment or confinement, and any other expenses incident to his or her
conviction, or his or her acquittal on the ground of insanity, shall be defrayed by the
Government.

### 3.

Payment of expenses
It shall be lawful for the Minister responsible for finance to issue his or her warrant to
the Permanent Secretary (Finance) authorising the payment of such expenses out of the
Consolidated Fund when he or she has satisfied himself or herself that the conditions
rendering the Government liable have been fulfilled.

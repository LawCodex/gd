## CHAPTER 302

SEVENTH DAY ADVENTIST CHURCH (INCORPORATION) ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of the Seventh Day Adventist Church in
Grenada as a body to be known as the Incorporated Trustees of the Seventh Day
Adventist Church.
[Act No. 12 of 1971.]

[10th July, 1971.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Seventh Day Adventist Church (Incorporation) Act.
2\. Incorporation of trustees
Philip Newman Hosten, Vigilant Belfon, Ralph Gordon Ogilvie and their successors
in office as trustees of the Seventh Day Adventist Church shall be a body corporate
known as the Incorporated Trustees of the Seventh Day Adventist Church (hereinafter
called “the Trustees”) with perpetual succession and a Common Seal and with power in
that name to sue and be sued in all courts.
3\. Power of trustees to acquire property
The Trustees shall have power to acquire for the Seventh Day Adventist Church by
purchase, transfer, exchange, devise, grant, gift, demise, bequest or otherwise any land or
other property of whatever kind whether in perpetuity or for life or for a term of years and
any money, securities, goods or chattels whatever, to take, hold and enjoy the same
subject to the performance of any trust upon which the same may have been acquired.
4\. Power of trustees to dispose of property, and to borrow, etc.
Subject to the performance of any trust as aforesaid, the Trustees may—

(a)

by deed under the Common Seal sell, demise, grant, convey, transfer,
exchange, surrender or otherwise dispose of any real or personal property
vested in or which may hereafter become vested in the Trustees;

(b)

borrow money and, for the payment of the same, by deed under the Common
Seal, give and execute mortgages, charges, pledges and other securities of
any real or personal property or make any arrangement that they may
consider convenient with such covenants, powers and provisions as they
think proper.

5\. Affixing of Common Seal
All documents requiring the Common Seal shall be sealed in the presence of at least
two of the Trustees who shall sign every such document.
6\. Address for service, etc.
(1) The Trustees shall at all times have a fixed address in Grenada for the service of
documents on them and such address and any change thereof shall be registered with the
Registrar of Companies.
(2) Any document may be served on the Trustees by leaving the document at, or by
sending it by registered post to, the address registered pursuant to subsection (1).

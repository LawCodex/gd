## CHAPTER 74B

CRUISE SHIP (VISITORS) LEVY ACT
An Act to provide for the imposition of a Levy on Cruise Ship Visitors.
Be it enacted by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the Senate and the House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the
authority of the same, as follows.
[Amended by Act No. 20 of 1994.]

[25th May, 1990.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Cruise Ship (Visitors Levy) Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“Cruise Ship” means a ship transporting people who are called visitors, tourists,
holiday travellers or howsoever otherwise referred to;
“ship” has the same meaning as in the Interpretation and General Provisions Act,
1989;
“tourist” means a visitor, holiday traveller, or howsoever otherwise called who
travels on a cruise ship;
“visitor” means a tourist, holiday traveller, or howsoever otherwise called arriving
in Grenada by a Cruise Ship.
3\. Imposition of Levy

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act a Levy is imposed on cruise ship visitors and
shall be paid to the Grenada Ports Authority by the Master of a cruise ship bringing
visitors to Grenada.
(2) The Levy shall be at the rate of US$3.00 in respect of each visitor.
4\. Remission of Levy
The Grenada Ports Authority may remit or waive any levy payable by a person under
this Act where it is satisfied it is just and equitable to do so.
5\.
The Grenada Ports Authority shall be accountable to the Government for all levies
collected under this Act.

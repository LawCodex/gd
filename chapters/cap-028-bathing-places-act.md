## CHAPTER 28

BATHING PLACES ACT
An Act to empower the Minister to make rules for the development, regulation and
control of public bathing places on and around the coastline.
[15th December, 1903.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Bathing Places Act.
2\. Power to make rules
(1) The Minister may make rules for the development, regulation and control of
bathing places on and around the coastline, and for the convenience and comfort of
visitors and other persons resorting to such places for bathing, and he or she may declare
any bay or any portion of the seashore to be a bathing place for the purposes of this Act.
(2) There may be annexed, in respect of the contravention of any rule made under
subsection (1), a fine not exceeding five hundred dollars recoverable in a summary manner
before a magistrate.
3\. Award to informant
Whenever a person has been convicted of the contravention of a rule made under this
Act the court may award, to any person through whose instrumentality the conviction has
been obtained, a portion of the fine imposed by the court.

### 1.

Citation
These Rules may be cited as the Bathing Places Rules.

### 2.

Declared bathing places
The following places on the bays and sea-shores of the State are hereby declared to be
reserved as public bathing places under the provisions of the Bathing Places Act—
(a)

In St. George’s District—
(i) that portion of the seashore situate near Queen’s Park, known as “The
Emboucherie”,
(ii) that portion of the St. George’s Harbour known as “The Spout”,
(iii) that portion of the sea-shore situate above the centre of the Grand
Anse Bay near to the Grand Anse Village and to the south-west of the
pathway leading from the Grand Anse First Class By-way and
extending therefrom along the shore for a distance of half a mile in a
south-westerly direction.

(b)

In the St. John’s District—
such portion of the sea-shore situate at the northern end of the town of
Gouyave adjacent to the property of Dr. George L. Latour and lying between
that property and the lands of James N. Darbeau.

(c)

In the St. Mark’s District—
(i) that portion of the sea-shore known as “Pierre Bourse” situate to the
north of the town of Victoria, and

### 3.

Undressing for bathing
No person shall be permitted to undress for the purpose of bathing on or from any
such portion of the seashore as abuts upon the boundaries of any town or lies within a
distance of one hundred yards therefrom unless such portion of the seashore shall have
been duly declared to be a public bathing place under the provisions of the said Act.

### 4.

Bathing of cattle in sea
No person shall be allowed to bathe horses or cattle in the sea within a distance of one
hundred feet from any place which has been duly declared to be a public bathing place
under the provisions of the said Act:
Provided that this paragraph shall not apply to the public bathing places herein-before
mentioned and called “The Emboucherie” in the St. George’s District, and “Irwin’s Bay”
in the St. Patrick’s District.

### 5.

Hours for bathing
(1) No person shall, between the hours of 6.30 a.m. and 6.30 p.m. bathe at or from the
Spout or that portion of the public bathing place situate at Grand Anse in the parish of St.
George which extends from the jetty at the said bathing place in a south-westerly
direction to a point one hundred and fifty yards therefrom, unless attired in decent bathing
costume.
(2) No person shall, within public view, undress at the aforesaid time and places for
the purpose of bathing.

### 6.

Penalty
Any person acting in contravention of these Rules shall be liable to a fine not
exceeding two hundred and fifty dollars.

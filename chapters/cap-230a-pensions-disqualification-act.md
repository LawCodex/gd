## CHAPTER 230A

PENSIONS (DISQUALIFICATION) ACT
An Act to disqualify persons who are appointed to the service of the Government of
Grenada on or after the appointed day from pensions, gratuities or other benefits
under the Scheduled enactments.
[Act No. 24 of 1983.]

[4th April, 1983.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Pensions (Disqualification) Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“appointed day” means the 4th April, 1983;
“Scheduled enactments” means the enactments set out in the Schedule to this Act.
3\. Disqualification
Any person who is appointed to a post in the service of the Government of Grenada on
or after the appointed day shall not be entitled to any pension gratuity or other allowance
under any of the Scheduled enactments.

## CHAPTER 220

OPTICIANS REGISTRATION ACT
An Act to make provision for the registration of opticians, to regulate the practice of
sight-testing, and for purposes incidental thereto.
[1st March, 1955.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Opticians Registration Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“Board” means the Opticians Registration Board as established under section 3;
“medical practitioner” means a medical practitioner registered as such under the
provisions of the Health Practitioners Act, Chapter 132A;
“optician” means a sight-testing optician;
“register” or “opticians register” means the register of opticians to be kept in
pursuance of this Act;

“secretary” means such person as may, from time to time, be appointed by the
Board to be secretary to the Board.

### 3.

Constitution of the Opticians Registration Board
(1) For the purposes of this Act, there shall be established a Board to be known as the
Opticians Registration Board, consisting of the Chief Medical Officer, two medical
practitioners, one of whom shall if possible be an ophthalmic consultant, and one optician
who in the opinion of the Minister is eligible to be registered or who is actually registered
under this Act, to be, from time to time, appointed by the Minister.
(2) The Chief Medical Officer shall be the Chairperson of the Board, and in his or her
absence the Board may appoint a chairperson from among its members. Three members
shall form a quorum.
(3) Any vacancy occurring on the Board shall be filled in the manner set out in
subsection (1).
(4) A secretary shall be appointed, from time to time, by the Board, and he or she
shall carry out the instructions of the Board.

### 4.

Register of opticians
(1) The secretary shall keep a register, in the form contained in the Schedule, to be
styled the Opticians Register, of all persons registered as opticians under this Act.
(2) The secretary shall enter in the register the names and particulars required of all
persons whom the Board shall direct to be registered.

### 5.

No person to practise sight-testing unless registered
No person, unless duly registered under the provisions of this Act, shall practise sighttesting, either for or without reward, or carry on business as an optician, or take or use the
name or title of optician (either alone or in combination with any other word or words) or
any name, title, addition or description, implying that he or she is registered under this
Act, or that he or she is a person specially qualified to practise sight-testing.

### 6.

Persons entitled to be registered
The following persons, upon satisfying the Board by documentary or other evidence that
they are of good moral character and of their right to be registered, upon payment of a fee
of twenty-five dollars, shall be entitled to be registered under this Act, namely—
(a)

any person qualified or entitled to practise sight-testing in the United
Kingdom under any Act for the time being in force or who holds the sighttesting diploma of the Worshipful Company of Spectacle Makers or of the
British College of Optometrists, both of London, England;

(b)

any person who holds a diploma, licence or certificate granted to him or her
by any university, college or institution in a foreign country recognised by
the Board as furnishing a sufficient guarantee of the possession of requisite
knowledge and skill for the efficient practice of sight-testing.

### 7.

Application for registration
(1) Where any person desires to be registered under this Act he or she shall make
application to the secretary and shall submit such evidence as shall satisfy the Board that
he or she is eligible to be registered and is a fit and proper person to be so registered.

(2) The secretary shall forthwith lay such application before the Board, and the Board,
if satisfied that such person is eligible and is a fit and proper person to be registered, shall
direct the secretary to register such person.

### 8.

Publication of copy of register and evidence of registration
(1) The secretary, in the month of February in each year, shall cause to be published
in the Gazette a copy of the register.
(2) The secretary shall also cause to be published in the Gazette a notice of each
subsequent registration or cancellation of registration.
(3) A copy of the register for the time being published in the Gazette and of any
subsequent notice in accordance with the provisions of this section shall be evidence in
all cases (until the contrary be made to appear) that the persons therein specified are
registered under this Act, and the absence of the name of any person from such copy shall
be evidence (until the contrary be made to appear) that such person is not registered under
this Act:
Provided that in the case of a person whose name does not appear in such copy, a
certified copy under the hand of the secretary of the entry of the name of such person in
the register, shall be evidence that such person is registered under this Act.

### 9.

Evidence of practice of sight-testing
The use of test lenses, spectacles, trial frames, ophthalmoscope, retinoscope, or any
apparatus that may be used to measure refraction, or visual acuity, or muscular
equilibrium, shall be deemed conclusive evidence of the practice of sight-testing.

### 10.

Registered opticians entitled to recover charges
Every person registered under this Act shall be entitled to practise sight-testing and to
demand and recover reasonable charges for professional services or optical aid rendered
by such person, and the cost of merchandise supplied by him or her. And no person,
unless he or she is so registered, shall be entitled to recover any fee or charge in
proceedings in any court for performing any act or giving any advice as an optician.

### 11.

Erasing from register name of optician guilty of crime or disgraceful conduct
If any optician registered under this Act is convicted of felony or misdemeanour
before the High Court or, after due inquiry, is adjudged by the Board to have been guilty
of disgraceful conduct in his or her practice as an optician, the Board may cause the name
of such optician to be erased from the register.

### 12.

Name may be restored to register
If the Board thinks fit, it may, in any case, direct any name erased from the register to
be restored thereto on the payment of such fee, not exceeding the registration fee, as the
Board may fix.

### 13.

Alterations and erasures in register
(1) The secretary shall, on the instructions of the Board, from time to time, insert in
the register any alteration which may come to his or her knowledge in the name or
address of any person registered.
(2) The secretary shall on the instructions of the Board erase from the register—
(a)

the name of every deceased person entered therein;

(b)

the name of every optician ordered by the Board to be erased under the
provisions of section 12;

(c)

any entry which has been incorrectly or fraudulently made.

### 14.

Penalty for making false entry in register or in copy of register
A secretary or other person who shall fraudulently make, or cause or permit to be
made, any false or incorrect entry in the register or any copy thereof shall be guilty of an
offence and liable, on summary conviction, to imprisonment for two years.

### 15.

Offences
A person who—
(a)

fraudulently procures or attempts to procure himself, herself or any other
person to be registered under this Act, or who aids or abets in such
fraudulent procurement or attempt; or

(b)

not being registered under this Act, uses or takes the name or title of or holds
himself or herself out or pretends to be an optometrist, optician, or doctor or
professor of optometry or any name, title, description or addition whether
expressed in words or letters or both implying such qualifications or that he
or she is a person specially qualified to practise sight-testing; or

(c)

whether registered under this Act or not, and not being a medical
practitioner, assumes the title of oculist or eyesight specialist, or uses or
causes to be used in connection with his or her business, trade, calling or
profession, any written words, titles, initials, additions or abbreviations
which are intended to represent or may reasonably cause any person to
believe that he or she is qualified to practise medical or surgical treatment of
the eye; or

(d)

whether registered under this Act or not, and not being a medical practitioner
or acting under the instructions of a medical practitioner, administers any
drug for the purpose of paralysing the accommodation of the eye or
otherwise for facilitating the measurement of the powers of vision, or treats
any disease of the eye by the use of drugs; or

(e)

contravenes the provisions of section 5,

shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of three
thousand dollars.

### 16.

Saving provisions
(1) Nothing in this Act shall extend to prejudice or in any way affect medical
practitioners.
(2) Nothing in this Act shall so operate as to prohibit—
(a)

any licensed druggist from dispensing, selling or supplying any drugs;

(b)

any wholesale dealer from supplying frames, ophthalmic lenses, or
spectacles in the ordinary course of wholesale business; or

(c)

any person from selling from a permanent place of business, and otherwise
than in compliance with a prescription given by a medical practitioner, or a
registered optician, spectacles kept in stock in a state ready for use.

## CHAPTER 298

SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY COUNCIL ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of the Science and Technology Council, and
for connected matters.
[Act No. 28 of 1982.]

[4th June, 1982.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Science and Technology Council Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act, “Council” means the Science and Technology Council established by
section 3.

### 3.

Incorporation and composition, etc., of the Council
(1) There is hereby established a body corporate to be called the Science and
Technology Council comprising no fewer than ten nor more than forty members and
consisting of one member nominated or elected to the Council each from a different one
of the sources listed in the First Schedule.
(2) The provisions of the Second Schedule shall have effect with respect to the
Council and its members.

### 4.

Functions of the Council
(1) The functions of the Council are to—
(a)

make recommendations to the Government on a national policy and plan for
science and technology;

(b)

establish the process of science and technology planning and to guide
activity throughout all its stages paying special attention to the compatible
transfer of technology from all parts of the world and the further
development and stimulation of indigenous technology;

(c)

co-ordinate and monitor scientific and technological projects;

(d)

initiate and evaluate scientific and technological projects, programmes and
aspects of programmes;

(e)

assist in the development of the Grenada Bureau of Standards which will
engage in standardisation and quality control activities at the national level;

(f)

perform such other functions related to science and technology as the
Minister may from time to time consider necessary.

(2) In the performance of its functions the Council—
(a)

shall have access to all relevant information in the possession of Government
in connection with the planning, implementation and progress of scientific
and technological programmes, and shall advise on budgetary allocations to
such programmes;

(b)

may co-opt and mobilise resource personnel;

(c)

may apply for and receive funding from local and external sources.

### 5.

Appointment of Director of the Council and staff
(1) There shall be a Director of the Council who shall be appointed by the Council
with the approval of the Minister.
(2) The Council may, with the approval of the Minister, appoint such officers and
employees as it may consider necessary for the proper performance of its functions.

### 6.

Funds of the Council
The funds and resources of the Council shall consist of—
(a)

such funds as it may receive from external sources;

(b)

such sums as may be granted to it by the Government;

(c)

all other sums or property which may become payable to or vested in the
Council;

(d)

such sums as the Council may borrow for meeting any of its obligations or
discharging its functions.

### 7.

Borrowing powers
(1) With the approval of the Minister the Council may borrow, whether by way of
overdraft or otherwise, sums required by it for meeting any of its obligations.
(2) A person lending money to the Council shall not be bound to inquire whether the
borrowing is within the power of the Council.

### 8.

Application of funds

The Council may apply its funds for all or any of the following purposes—
(a)

the payment of expenses incurred by it in the exercise of its functions under
this Act or any other enactment;

(b)

the payment of interest, and the repayment of principal, on any money
borrowed by the Council.

### 9.

Accounts to be kept and audited
(1) The Council shall keep proper accounts of all its transactions to the satisfaction of
the Minister and a statement of accounts shall be kept and made up in respect of each
financial year.
(2) The accounts of the Corporation shall be audited by the Director of Audit or such
other auditor, to be appointed annually by the Council, as may be approved by the
Minister.

### 10.

Rules
The Council may, with the approval of the Minister, make rules for giving effect to the
provisions of this Act.

Second Schedule
SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY COUNCIL ACT
Provisions with Respect to the Council
[Section 3(2).]

1\. Common Seal
The Council shall have a Common Seal and shall have power to hold and dispose of
land, to enter into contracts, to sue and be sued in its corporate name, and to do all things
necessary for the purposes of this Act.
2\. Tenure of Office
A member of the Council shall be nominated or elected for a period of two years and
shall be eligible for re-nomination or re-election at the expiration of that period.
3\. Chairperson
The Council shall elect from among its members a chairperson and a deputy
chairperson, both of whom shall be eligible for re-election at the expiration of their
respective terms of office.
4\. Resignation
(1) The chairperson or deputy chairperson may at any time resign his or her office by
instrument in writing addressed to the Council, and from the date of the receipt of such
instrument by the Council such member shall cease to be the chairperson or deputy
chairperson, as the case may be.
(2) A member of the Council, other than the chairperson or deputy chairperson, may
at any time resign his or her office by instrument in writing addressed to the chairperson
and from the date of the receipt of such instrument by the chairperson such member shall
cease to be a member of the Council.
5\. Authentication of Seal
The Common Seal shall be authenticated by the chairperson, or one member of the
Council authorised to act in that behalf, and the Director.
6\. Procedure at Meetings
(1) The Council shall meet at such times as may be necessary or expedient for the
transaction of business, and such meetings shall be held at such places and times and on
such days as the Council shall determine.
(2) The chairperson may at any time call a special meeting of the Council and shall
call a special meeting to be held within seven days of receipt of a written request for that
purpose addressed to him or her by any two members of the Council.

(3) The chairperson or, in the case of his or her absence or inability to act as
chairperson, the deputy chairperson shall preside at the meetings of the Council, and
when so presiding the chairperson, or deputy chairperson, as the case may be, shall have
an original and a casting vote.
(4) The quorum of the Council shall be seven including the chairperson or deputy
chairperson.
(5) Subject to the provisions of this Schedule the Council may regulate its own
proceedings.
(6) The validity of any proceeding of the Council shall not be affected by a vacancy
amongst the members thereof nor by any defect in the nomination or election of a
member thereof.
7\. Remuneration of Members
There shall be paid to the chairperson and other members of the Council such
remuneration, if any, by way of salaries or travelling or other allowances as the Council,
with the approval of the Minister, may determine.
8\. Regulations governing conditions of service of Officers and Employees
The Council may, with the approval of the Minister, make regulations determining
generally the conditions of service of officers and employees of the Council and in
particular, but without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, such regulations may
relate to—
(a)

appointment, dismissal, discipline, hours of employment, pay and leave;

(b)

appeals against dismissal and other disciplinary measures;

(c)

the grant of pensions, gratuities and other benefits to or in respect of service
on the Council;

(d)

the establishment and maintenance of sick funds, superannuation funds and
provident funds, the contributions payable thereto and the benefits receivable
therefrom.

9\. Protection of Members
No action, suit, prosecution or other proceedings shall be brought or instituted
personally against any member of the Council in respect of any act done in good faith in
pursuance or execution or intended execution of this Act.

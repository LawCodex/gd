## CHAPTER 148

IMPORTATION OF TEXTILES (QUOTAS) ACT
An Act to regulate the importation of textiles.
[27th June, 1934.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Importation of Textiles (Quotas) Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“Comptroller” means the Comptroller of Customs and Excise;
“foreign country” means any country or territory not being a part of the
Commonwealth, and not being a part of any of the countries or territories defined as
“scheduled territories” for the purposes of the Customs Duties Act, Chapter 75C;
“prescribed” means prescribed by regulations under this Act;
“quota” means the quantity of textile goods or any class or classes or textile goods
manufactured in any individual foreign country which may be imported during any
period in accordance with an Order made by the Minister under section 3;
“quota period” means a period in respect of which any quota has been fixed by the
Minister under section 3;
“regulated textiles” means any textile goods, and any class or classes of textile
goods, and piece goods of woven fabric containing fifty per cent or more by weight of
cotton or of artificial silk or of cotton and artificial silk combined manufactured in a
foreign country in respect of which a quota has been fixed by the Minister under
section 3:

Provided that for the purposes of this Act any textile goods of a class in respect of
which a quota has been fixed shall be deemed to be manufactured in a foreign country
unless accompanied by a certificate of British origin in the manner and form prescribed.

### 3.

Fixing of quotas
The Minister may, by Order, fix the total quantity of textile goods or of any class or
classes of textile goods manufactured in any foreign country which may be imported
during any period.

### 4.

Returns and declarations of origin
No person shall import any regulated textiles unless he or she shall at the time of
importation submit to the Comptroller in the prescribed form a return of the quantity,
class and origin of the goods imported and a declaration of origin.

### 5.

Records of imports and publication thereof
(1) The imports of regulated textiles manufactured in a foreign country shall be
separately recorded by the Comptroller.
(2) A person may at any time, on payment of the prescribed fee, require the
Comptroller to furnish a statement of the total quantity already imported of any regulated
textiles manufactured in any individual foreign country during any quota period, and the
Comptroller shall furnish such statement accordingly.
(3) When the imports of any regulated textiles manufactured in a foreign country
during any quota period attain the quota prescribed in respect of such textiles for such
country in that period, the Minister shall issue a notice to that effect in the Gazette, and
such notice shall specify the foreign country, the regulated textiles and the quota period to
which it refers.

### 6.

Prohibition of imports in excess of quota
Upon the issue of a notice under section 5(3) further imports of the regulated textiles
concerned manufactured in the country specified therein shall be prohibited until the end
of the quota period:
Provided that, notwithstanding anything in this Act, the Minister may, if he or she
thinks fit, in any Order made under this Act direct that regulated textiles in excess of the
several quotas therein fixed may be imported on payment of such duties in addition to
those prescribed by the Customs Duties Act, Chapter 75C, or any other Act as may be
specified in the Order, and accordingly this Act shall have effect subject to such direction.

### 7.

Offences and penalty
A person who knowingly imports any regulated textiles contrary to the provisions of
section 4 or 6 shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of
five thousand dollars.

### 8.

Regulations
The Minister may make regulations for giving effect to the provisions of this Act.

### 9.

Issue of import licences
(1) If the Minister is satisfied that the importations of regulated textiles from any
country in any quota period are likely to fulfil the quota before the expiration of such
quota period, he or she may by Order direct that no regulated textiles shall be imported

from that country during such quota period except under licence and thereupon any
regulated textiles imported from that country during such quota period except in
accordance with such licence shall be deemed to be goods prohibited to be imported
under section 42 of the Customs (Control and Management) Act, Chapter 75B, as if they
were gazetted as being prohibited under the said section:
Provided that such prohibition shall not be deemed to extend to any regulated textiles
imported in accordance with a direction given under the Act.
(2) Every licence issued under this section shall state the quantity, class and country
of manufacture of textile goods which may be imported under it and the period during
which the licence is valid.
(3) Applications for licences to import any regulated textiles shall be made to the
Comptroller and shall state full particulars of the quantity, class and country of
manufacture of the goods proposed to be imported under each licence, together with such
other particulars as the Comptroller shall require.
(4) The period during which an import licence is valid shall fall wholly within a
single quota period.
(5) The total quantity of imports of any regulated textiles manufactured in any
country during a quota period which may be authorised by import licences issued under
this section shall not exceed the quota for that country fixed for that quota period.
(6) On the importation of regulated textiles the appropriate import licence shall be
indorsed in the prescribed manner, and when the total quantity of goods the importation
of which is thereby authorised has been imported, the licence shall be surrendered to the
Comptroller and cancelled.
(7) There shall be paid in respect of every licence issued under the provisions of this
section a fee of one dollar and twenty-five cents.

### 10.

Restriction of import licences
(1) An Order made under section 9 shall specify a period within which applications
for import licences may be submitted.
(2) On the expiry of the period within which applications may be submitted, the
Comptroller shall consider all the applications received, and, if the total quantity of goods
covered by such applications does not exceed the appropriate quota, he or she may issue
import licences in accordance with all applications. If the total quantity of goods covered
by the applications exceeds the appropriate quota the Comptroller shall in his or her
discretion, subject to the approval of the Minister, issue licences to the several applicants
for quantities proportionate to the value of their previous importations during any period
which the Comptroller shall consider reasonable or on any other basis.

### 11.

Transfer of licence
An import licence may, with the written consent of the Comptroller thereon, be
transferred from the person to whom it was issued to any other person designated by him
or her and there shall be paid to the Permanent Secretary (Finance) the sum of sixty cents
for such transfer.

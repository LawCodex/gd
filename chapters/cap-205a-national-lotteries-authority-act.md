## CHAPTER 205A

NATIONAL LOTTERIES AUTHORITY ACT
An Act to provide for the establishment of the National Lotteries Authority to be
responsible for promoting the development of lotteries, pools and games of
chance in Grenada, and for matters connected therewith or incidental to.
[Act No. 20 of 1999 amended by Act No. 19 of 2008.]

[3rd September, 1999.]

## PART I

Preliminary

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the National Lotteries Authority Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“Authority” means the National Lotteries Authority establishment by section 3;
“Board” means the Board of the Authority established by section 4;
“business plan”, for a financial year, means—
(a)

the business plan approved under section 25; and

(b)

all amendments to the business plan approved under that section,

for the financial year;
“Chairperson” means the Chairperson of the Commission and includes any person
for the time being performing the functions of Chairperson;
“financial year” means the financial year referred to in section 24;
“functions” includes powers and duties;
“General Manager” means the general manager of the Authority appointed under
section 13 and includes any person appointed to act as General Manager;
“member” means a member of the Board of the Authority and includes the
Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for sports.

## PART II

Establishment, Constitution, Functions, Management, etc., of the Authority

### 3.

Establishment of the Authority
(1) There is hereby established for the purposes of this Act a body to be called the
National Lotteries Authority, hereinafter referred to as “the Authority”.
(2) The Authority shall be a body corporate to which sections 47 and 49 of the
Interpretation and General Provisions Act, Chapter 153, applies.

### 4.

Board of Authority
(1) The Authority shall have a Board which shall be constituted by not less than five
nor more than nine persons appointed by the Minister from among persons with
qualifications and experience in accounting, economics, banking, commerce,
management and legal practice.
(2) The Board shall be responsible for the formulation of policy regarding the
functions of the Authority.
(3) Every member shall be appointed by the Minister for a period of two years, or as the
Minister determines and shall, on ceasing to be a member, be eligible for re-appointment,
provided that—
(a)

any member may at any time resign his or her office by instrument in writing
addressed to the Chairperson, who shall forthwith cause it to be forwarded to
the Minister; and

(b)

where a member ceases to be a member for any reason, the Minister may
appoint another member in his or her place for the remainder of the time for
which the former member would have served before his or her ceasing to be
a member.

(4) The Minister shall appoint a Chairperson and a Deputy Chairperson from among
the members.
(5) In the event of incapacity due to illness or any other temporary cause or temporary
absence from Grenada of any member, the Minister may appoint some other person to act
as a temporary member during the time such incapacity or absence continues.
(6) The Minister may at any time revoke the appointment of any member if he or she
considers it in the interest of the Authority to do so.
(7) The appointment of any member and the termination of office of any person as a
member shall be notified in the Gazette.

### 5.

Functions of the Authority
(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, no person other than the Authority, shall
operate any lottery in Grenada.
(2) The Authority may, with the approval of the Minister, operate other games of
chance as it considers appropriate.
(3) Notwithstanding anything contained in subsection (1), the Minister may licence
any other person or agency to operate a lottery in Grenada on such terms and conditions
as he or she approves.
(4) Without prejudice to the generality of the provisions of subsection (1) the
Authority may—

assist in the organisation and operation of lotteries connected with Grenada;

carry on such forms of pool betting business as may from time to time be
approved by the Minister;

(c)

make arrangements for the proper and prompt payment of prize monies
payable under this Act;

(d)

assist in the development of sports, culture and community development
projects;

(e)

engage in any activity which is in its opinion necessary to or conducive to
the proper discharge of its functions;

(f)

advise the Minister on the development of all forms of games in Grenada.

(5) The Authority shall provide the Minister with a statement of the proceeds of each
drawing of a lottery or other game of chance conducted by the Authority on a quarterly
basis or within such other time as may be specified by the Minister; and any such
statement shall be supplied within one week after it is due or requested.
(6) The Authority shall, in performing any of its functions under this Act, take such
actions as may be necessary and practicable to ensure that its revenues are collected at the
earliest possible date and thereafter continue to be at least sufficient to meet all sums
properly chargeable to the revenue account including sums required for the payment of
prizes, loans and interest thereon, and for the establishment and maintenance of its property
and its Reserve Fund.

### 6.

Seal of the Authority
(1) The seal of the Authority shall be kept in the custody of the Chairperson or of the
General Manager and may be affixed to instruments pursuant to a resolution of the
Authority by the General Manager.
(2) The seal of the Authority shall be authenticated by the signature of the
Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson and the General Manager, and such seal shall be
officially and judicially noticed.
(3) All deeds, instruments, contracts and other documents, other than those required
by law to be under seal, and all decisions of the Authority may be signified under the
hand of the Chairperson, Deputy Chairperson or General Manager.

### 7.

Service of notice
Service upon the Authority of any notice, order or other document shall be executed
by delivering same or by sending it by registered post addressed to the General Manager
at the office of the Authority.

### 8.

Procedure at meetings of the Board
(1) The Board shall meet at such times as may be necessary or expedient for the
transaction of business, and such meetings shall be held at such place and time and on
such days as the Chairperson may determine.
(2) The Chairperson may at any time call a special meeting of the Board and shall call
a special meeting within seven days of a requisition for that purpose addressed to him or
her by any four members to consider the matters contained in such requisition.
(3) The Chairperson shall call a special meeting of the Board if directed by the
Minister to do so.
(4) The Chairperson or in his or her absence the Deputy Chairperson shall preside at
all meetings of the Board.
(5) The Chairperson or in his or her absence the Deputy Chairperson and four other
members shall form a quorum.

(6) Where any member is disqualified from taking part in any deliberation or decision
of the Board with respect to any matter, he or she shall be disregarded for the purposes of
constituting a quorum for deliberating on or deciding that matter.
(7) The decision of the Board with regard to any question shall be determined by vote
of the majority of the members present at the meeting of the Board; but where the votes
of the members present in regard to any question is equally divided, the Chairperson or
Deputy Chairperson presiding at the meetings.
(8) Minutes of the proceedings of each meeting of the Board shall be kept in such
manner as the Chairperson may determine and shall be confirmed as soon as practicable
thereafter at a subsequent meeting.
(9) The Board may co-opt any one or more persons to attend any particular meeting
of the Board for the purpose of assisting or advising the Board but no such co-opted person
shall have any right to vote.

### 9.

Disclosure of interest
(1) A member who is in any way, whether directly or indirectly interested in an
application to the Authority or for a contract proposed contract with the Authority shall
declare the nature of his or her interest at the first meeting of the Authority at which it is
possible for him or her to do so.
(2) A member shall not vote in respect of any application to the Authority in respect
of any contract or proposed contract with the Authority in which he or she is in any way
interested, whether directly or indirectly.

### 10.

Appointment of Committees
(1) The Minister may appoint committees of the Board to examine and report to it on
any matter whatsoever arising out of or connected with any of its functions under this
Act.
(2) Any such committee shall consist of at least one member together with such other
persons, whether members or not, whose assistance or service the Minister may desire.
(3) Where persons, other than members, are members of a committee appointed under
this section, or where any person is co-opted under section 8(9), the Authority may with
the approval of the Minister by resolution declare the remuneration and allowance of such
persons, and such sums shall properly be payable out of the funds and resources of the
Authority.
(4) The Minister may reject the report of any such committee or adopt it either wholly
or with such modifications, additions or adaptations as he or she determines.

### 11.

Power to delegate
Subject to this Act the Authority may delegate to any member or committee of the
Board the power and authority to carry out on its behalf such duties as it may determine.

### 12.

Remuneration of members
There shall be paid from the funds of the Authority to the Chairperson and other
members of the Board such, if any, remuneration, fees or allowances as the Minister may
determine.

### 13.

Appointment of General Manager

(1) The Authority shall, at such salary and upon such terms and conditions of service
as may be agreed on, and with the prior approval of the Minister, appoint a General
Manager.
(2) The General Manager shall be the Chief Executive Officer of the Authority and
shall be present at all meetings of the Board unless he or she has obtained leave of
absence from the Chairperson or is incapacitated by illness or other cause from attending
such meetings.
(3) In the event of the absence of the General Manager, the Authority may by
instrument in writing and with the prior approval of the Minister, appoint any person to
act as General Manager during the period of such absence upon such terms and
conditions as it may determine, and the person so appointed may exercise all the powers
and all the duties by this Act exercisable or to be performed by the General Manager or as
many of such powers and duties as may be specified in such instrument.

### 14.

Appointment of officer and servants
(1) The Board may appoint and employ at such remuneration and on such terms and
conditions as it thinks fit, such officers and servants as it deems necessary for the proper
carrying out of its functions under this Act.
(2) The persons so engaged shall perform such duties as may be assigned to them by
the General Manager.

### 15.

Negation of review
The validity of any act done or proceedings taken under this Act shall not be
questioned on the ground of—
(a)

the existence of any vacancy in the membership of, or any defect in the
constitution of the Authority;

(b)

the contravention by a member of the provisions of section 9 of this Act; or

(c)

any omission, defect or irregularity not affecting the merits of the case.

### 16.

Protection of members
No personal liability shall attach to any member in respect of anything done or
suffered in good faith under the provisions of this Act and any sums of money, damages
or costs which may be recovered against any such member in respect of any act or thing
done bona fide for the purpose of carrying this Act into effect shall be paid out of the
funds and resources of the Authority.

### 17.

Policy directions
(1) The Minister may, after consultation with the Chairperson, give to the Board
directions of a general character as to the policy to be followed in the performance of its
functions in relation to matters appearing to him or her to concern the public interest.
(2) The Authority shall furnish the Minister with such returns, accounts and other
information as he or she may require with respect to the property and activities of the
Authority, and shall afford to him or her facilities for verifying such information in such
manner and at such times as he or she may reasonably require.

## PART III

Financial Matters

### 18.

Funds of the Authority
The funds and resources of the Authority shall consist of—

such sums as may from time to time be placed at its disposal by Parliament;

funds arising from the sale, lease, or other disposition of property vested in
the Authority, or from the conduct of lotteries, pool business or any game of
chance;

(c)

such sums received by the Authority by way of gift, grant or donation from
lawful sources;

(d)

funds arising from any source consequential upon the performance of its
functions under this Act;

(e)

sums borrowed by the Authority for the purpose of meeting any of its
obligations or discharging its functions;

(f)

all other sums or property that may become payable to or vested in the
Authority in respect of any matter incidental to its powers and duties.

### 19.

Borrowing powers
(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Authority may borrow sums required by
it for meeting any of its obligations or discharging any of its functions under this Act.
(2) The power of the Authority to borrow shall be exercisable only with the approval
of the Minister of Finance as to the terms on which the borrowing may be effected, and
an approval given in any respect for the purpose of this subsection may be either general
or limited to a particular borrowing or otherwise and may be either unconditional or
subject to conditions.

### 20.

Guarantees of borrowings
(1) Subject to the provisions of the Financial Intelligence Unit Act, Chapter 102A,
and any other financial legislation in force in Grenada, the Minister of Finance may
guarantee, in such manner and on such conditions as he or she may think fit the payments
of the principal and interest of any authorised borrowing of the Authority.
(2) Where the Minister is satisfied there has been default in the payment of any
principal monies or interest guarantees under the provisions of this section he or she shall
direct the repayment out of the Consolidated Fund of the amount in respect of which there
has been such default.
(3) The Authority shall make to the Accountant-General, at such times and in such
manner as the Minister may direct, payments of such amounts as may be so directed in or
towards repayment of any such sums issued in fulfilment of any guarantee given under
this section, and payment of interest on what is outstanding for the time being in respect
of any sums so issued at such rates as the Minister may direct, and different rates of
interest may be directed as regards different sums as regards interest for different periods.

### 21.

Reserve Fund
The Authority shall establish a Reserve Fund which shall be dealt with and applied in
accordance with any direction which may, from time to time, be given to the Authority by
the Minister of Finance.

### 22.

Application of funds
Subject to section 23, the funds and resources of the Authority shall be applied for the
purposes authorised by this Act in relation to the functions of the Authority.

### 23.

Contributions to sports, etc.
(1) Subject to this section, the Authority may make such financial contribution to any
sporting and cultural activities in Grenada as determined by the Minister.
(2) No contribution in excess of fifty thousand dollars may be made under
subsection (1) without the approval of the Minister.

### 24.

Financial year
The financial year of the National Lotteries Authority is the twelve months period
beginning on the first day of January and ending on the thirty-first day of December.

### 25.

Annual business plan
(1) The Board shall, no later than four months before commencement of each
financial year and in such form as the Minister requires, prepare in respect of the financial
year, and submit to the Minister, a proposal for a business plan for the financial year that
must contain—
(a)

a statement of the Authority’s objectives and priorities in carrying out its
responsibilities for the financial year and the following two financial years;

(b)

a comprehensive business plan that—
(i) shows how resources, including but not limited to financial resources,
will be allocated to meeting the objectives and priorities of the
Authority for the financial year, and
(ii) includes pro forma financial statements as required by the Minister;

(c)

a comparison of the pro forma financial statements with the actual financial
statements for the previous financial year;

(d)

a statement as to how the Board proposes to measure its performance in
carrying out its responsibilities in the financial year; and

(e)

any other information required by the Minister by written notice to the
Board.

(2) The Minister may, on request of the Board, extend the time for submitting a
proposal for a business plan.
(3) The Minister shall, as soon as practicable, consider the proposal for a business
plan and may, after consulting with the Minister of Finance—
(a)

approve the proposal as submitted;

(b)

with the approval of the Board, amend the proposal and approve it as
amended; or

(c)

refer the proposal back to the Board with directions that the Board take any
further action with respect to it that the Minister considers appropriate.

(4) Where the Minister refers the proposal for a business plan back to the Board under
subsection (3)(c), he or she shall provide the Board with his or her reasons for not
approving it.
(5) A proposal for a business plan that is referred back to the Board under
subsection (3)(c) must be resubmitted to the Minister as directed by the Minister and,
when it is resubmitted, subsections (3) and (4) apply.
(6) When a proposal in relation to a financial year is approved by the Minister, it
becomes the business plan for that financial year.
(7) The Board—

may, of its own motion, submit to the Minister a proposal to amend an
approved business plan; and

shall on request of the Minister, and within the time required by the Minister,
submit to the Minister a proposal to amend an approved business plan.

(8) Subsections (3), (4), (5) and (6) apply to proposals submitted to the Minister under
subsection (7).

### 25A.

Board obligated to implement business plan
The Board shall—
(a)

in each financial year implement the business plan for the financial year; and

(b)

establish a mechanism for monitoring the implementation of the business
plan.

### 25B.

Accounts
(1) The Board shall—
(a)

keep proper books of account of its income and other receipts and
expenditures; and

(b)

ensure that—
(i) all monies received are promptly brought to account,
(ii) all payments out of its money are correctly made and properly
authorised, and
(iii) adequate control is maintained over its property and over the incurring
of liabilities by the Authority.

(2) The books of account kept under subsection (1) shall—
(a)

be sufficient to record and explain the Authority’s transactions;

(b)

enable the Authority’s financial position to be determined with reasonable
accuracy at any time; and

(c)

be sufficient to enable financial statements to be prepared and audited in
accordance with this section.

(3) Within three months after the end of each financial year, the Board shall cause to
be prepared—
(a)

the following financial statements together with proper and adequate
explanatory notes—
(i) a statement of the assets and liabilities of the Authority at the end of
the financial year,
(ii) a statement of the revenue and expenditure of the Authority during the
financial year,
(iii) such other financial statements for the financial year as may be
specified in writing by the Minister; and

(b)

an annual report of the Authority on the implementation of the business plan
and such other matters as the Board considers advisable or the Minister
directs.

(4) Without delay after the completion of the financial statements and the annual
report, the Board shall furnish a copy of each to the Director of Audit.

### 25C.

Audit by Director of Audit and report to the House of Representatives
(1) Not later than three months after receipt of the financial statements and annual
report from the Board, the Director of Audit shall audit the financial statements in
accordance with the Audit Act, Chapter 22A.
(2) Without delay after the completion of his or her audit of the Authority, the
Director of Audit shall submit a copy of his or her report together with the financial
statements and annual report to the Minister and the Board.
(3) The Minister of Finance shall, not later than seven days after the House of
Representatives first meets after he or she has received the report together with the financial
statements and the annual report of the Authority, lay it before the House of Representatives.
(4) If the Minister of Finance fails to lay the report together with the financial
statements and the annual report of the Authority before the House of Representatives in
accordance with subsection (3), the Director of Audit shall transmit the report, the
financial statements and the annual report to the Speaker who shall, as soon as
practicable, present them to the House of Representatives.
(5) As soon as reasonably practicable after the report together with the financial
statements and the annual report of the Authority have been laid before the House of
Representatives, the Board shall cause the report, the financial statements and the annual
report of the Authority to be published in the Gazette.

## PART IV

Miscellaneous and Transitional

### 26.

Rules and regulations
(1) The Minister may make rules and regulations generally for the proper carrying out
of the provisions and purposes of this Act and in particular but without prejudice to the
generality of the foregoing may make rules and regulations—
(a)

for securing the proper and prompt payment of prize monies payable under
this Act;

(b)

prescribing the form of any ticket, notice, coupon or other document
authorised or required by this Act to be served or issued;

(c)

prescribe any other matter or anything which may be, or is required by this
Act to be prescribed;

(d)

for the control and discipline of staff employed under this Act.

(2) A fine not exceeding ten thousand dollars or a term of imprisonment of one year,
or both, may be attached to rules and regulations made pursuant to subsection (1).

### 27.

Offences
Any person who—
(a)

obtains a sum from the Authority by means of any false representation;

(b)

wilfully applies any sum paid to him or her on behalf of the Authority for
any purpose other than the purpose for which the sum was paid;

(c)

wilfully destroys any security ticket, coupon or document of the Authority,

commits an offence under this Act and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine of
ten thousand dollars or to imprisonment for twelve months, or to both such fine and
imprisonment.

### 28.

Validation and indemnity
(1) Notwithstanding anything contained in any enactment or rule of law, any lottery or
other game of chance conducted or operated prior to the commencement of this Act by a
body trading under the name of Grenada National Lottery (hereinafter referred to as “the
Grenada National Lottery”) shall be deemed to have been validly conducted or operated.
(2) Any money collected by the Grenada National Lottery by virtue of any lottery or
other game of chance aforesaid shall be deemed to have been validly collected, and any
money paid by way of prizes or winnings shall be deemed to have been validly paid out.
(3) No action, whether civil or criminal, shall be taken against any employee of the
Grenada National Lottery with respect to any action taken for the purpose of any lottery
or other game of chance conducted or organised by the said Grenada National Lottery.

### 29.

Transfer of assets, etc., to the Authority
(1) The assets and liabilities standing in the name of the Grenada National Lottery or
any servant or agent shall, without further assurance, be vested in the Authority with
effect from the commencement of this Act.
(2) The Minister of Finance may make any order he or she considers necessary or
expedient to give effect to the intent of subsection (1).

### 30.

Existing employees
Subject to any decision of the Board regarding existing employees of the Grenada
National Lottery, such employees shall continue in the employment of the Authority,
after the commencement of this Act, as if there was no break in employment.

### 31.

Commencement
This Act comes into force on the 3rd day of September, 1999.

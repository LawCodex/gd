## CHAPTER 202A

MOTOR VEHICLES TAX ACT
An Act to impose a tax on certain vehicles at the time of first Registration.
[Act No. 22 of 1994 amended by SRO 4 of 1995.]

[21st October, 1994.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Motor Vehicles Tax Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“Comptroller” means the Comptroller of Inland Revenue;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for finance;
“tax” means the motor vehicles tax imposed by section 3.
3\. Imposition and rate of Motor Vehicles Tax
(1) There shall be charged, levied and collected in respect of motor vehicles of a class
or description listed in the first column of the Schedule a motor vehicles tax the rate of
which shall be calculated on the corresponding percentage of the market price thereof
shown in the second column of the Schedule.

(2) The tax shall be payable to the Comptroller before the first registration of a motor
vehicle under section 6 of the Road Traffic Act, Chapter 289A.
(3) In subsection (1), “market price” means such sum as is, in the opinion of the
Comptroller, the ordinary retail selling price of a motor vehicle of the class or description
concerned without deduction of any commission, discount, or other similar allowance,
given by the seller thereof.
4\. Registration without payment of Tax
If a motor vehicle in respect of which the tax is payable is registered without the tax
being paid the motor vehicle shall be deemed not to have been registered.
5\. Remission of Tax
The Minister may, wholly or in part, remit the tax if he or she deems it expedient to do
so.
6\. Variation of Tax
The Minister may, by Order published in the Gazette, vary the rate of the tax.
7\. Amending of Schedule
The Minister may, by Order published in the Gazette, amend the provisions of the
Schedule, whether by adding thereto, deleting therefrom or otherwise.
8\. Commencement
This Act shall be deemed to have come into force as from the 1st day of September,
1993, but no one shall be liable to any penalty on account of this Act in respect of
anything done or not done by him or her before the publication of this Act in the Gazette.

PROVISIONAL COLLECTION OF TAXES (MOTOR VEHICLES TAX) ORDER
[13th June, 1994.]
1\. Citation
This Order may be cited as the Provisional Collection of Taxes (Motor Vehicles Tax)
Order.
2\. Definitions
In this Order—
“Comptroller” means the Comptroller of Inland Revenue;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for finance;
“tax” means the motor vehicles tax imposed by section 3.
3\. Imposition and rate of motor vehicles tax
(1) There shall be charged, levied and collected in respect of motor vehicles of a class
or description listed in the first column of the Schedule of a motor vehicles tax the rate of
which shall be calculated on the corresponding percentage of the market price thereof
shown in the second column of the Schedule.
(2) Tax shall be payable to the Comptroller before registration of a motor vehicle.
(3) In subsection (1), “market price” means such sum as is, in the opinion of the
Comptroller, the ordinary retail selling price of a motor vehicle of the class or description
concerned without deduction of any commission, discount, or other similar allowance,
given by the seller thereof.
4\. Remission of tax
The Minister may, wholly or in part, remit tax if he or she deems it expedient to do so.

5\. Variation of tax
The Minister may, by Order published in the Gazette, vary the rate of tax.
6\. Amending of Schedule
The Minister may, by Order published in the Gazette, amend the provisions of the
Schedule, whether by adding thereto, deleting therefrom or otherwise.
7\. Commencement
This Order shall be deemed to have come into force as from the 1st day of January,
1994\.

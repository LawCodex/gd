## CHAPTER 130C

GRENADA ISLAMIC FOUNDATION ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of the Grenada Islamic Foundation.
[Act No. 44 of 1994.]

[9th December, 1994.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Grenada Islamic Foundation Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“Association” means The Grenada Islamic Foundation;
“Board of Directors” means the Governing Body of the Association;
“General Assembly” means members of the faith of Islam;
“Imam” means religious leader;
“Wazir” means General Secretary;
“Wazir Mali” means Treasurer.
3\. Establishing the Islamic Foundation
(1) There is hereby established the Grenada Islamic Foundation which may be called
the Association.
(2) The Association shall be non-profitable and non political; its aims and objects are
stated in section 4 of this Act.

(3) Membership of the Association shall be open to all members of the faith of Islam.
4\. Aims and objects of the Association
The aims and objects of the Association are—
(a)

to serve humanity in the State of Grenada, through the exposition,
propagation and implementation of the teachings and ideals of Islam;

(b)

to establish and maintain Islamic Missions and other humanitarian
Institutions;

(c)

to assist in training theologians, missionaries, teachers and social workers;

(d)

to establish and maintain educational institutions in general, especially those
which promote the cause of Islam and Muslims;

(e)

to establish and maintain magazines, newspapers and publishing concerns
and to publish, sell and circulate books and other literature;

(f)

to establish and maintain mosques, lecture halls, libraries, hostels, Islamic
clubs and physical culture centres;

(g)

to establish linkages and membership within the Caribbean and South
America and other parts of the world as the Association shall decide;

(h)

to hold annual or other periodic conferences and conventions in any place as
the Association shall decide, for the promulgation and attainment of the
objects of the Association and in this connection to select a venue, secure
funds, depute representatives, invite delegates and take other steps necessary
for the purpose;

(i)

to work for Islamic unity and Solidarity by all lawful means;

(j)

to co-operate, without compromising the principles of Islam, with the
followers of other religions and ideologies;

(k)

to raise funds for the Association in the form of donations, subscriptions,
fees, loans or in any other legitimate way the Association decides;

(l)

to purchase, exchange, hire or in any other way acquire any movable or
immovable property and dispose of same in the discretion of the
Association;

(m)

to file, prosecute or defend, or join in filing, prosecuting or defending any
action, suit, application, proceedings or appeal in any court on behalf of the
Association, as and when necessary;

(n)

to sign, seal, execute and deliver all instruments, deeds, documents, and
writings found necessary or expedient in relation to the aforesaid objects or
affairs or interests of the Association;

(o)

to place or deposit with any local bank for any period the moneys of the
Association by opening accounts with banks and to operate same;

(p)

to obtain help from local bodies, governments, or other organisations for
implementing the objects of the Association.

5\. Constitution of Board of Directors
(1) There shall be a Board of Directors which shall be elected annually by the
Association and which shall consist of the following members—
(i) the officers, namely—
(a)

President;

(b)

Vice President;

(c)

Imam;

(d)

General Secretary or Wazir;

(e)

Treasurer or Wazir Mali;

(f)

Director of Social Services;

(g)

Director of Information;

(h)

Director of Sister Affairs;

(i)

Director of Youth Affairs; and

(ii) two other members.
(2) The management and control of the affairs, funds and assets of the Association
shall be vested in the Board of Directors which shall be the Governing Body of the
Association.
(3) The Board of Directors may make rules to regulate its procedure in its meetings.
(4) The Board of Directors may make any rules for the management of the
Association.
(5) All members of the Board of Directors shall uphold and cherish the basic tenets of
Islam and seek to be guided in his or her every day life by Islamic morality and etiquette.
The members of the Board of Directors should be practising Muslims for at least three
years with a working knowledge of Islam.
(6) The names and addresses in the Schedule hereto are of members of the faith of
Islam who have all consented to be members of the Board of Directors of the Association.
6\. Trustees
The Board of Directors shall elect three of its members to be trustees of the
Association. The Board of Directors shall decide the duration of the office of the trustees.
7\. Seal of the Association
(1) The Association shall have a Seal bearing the words “The Grenada Islamic
Association”. The Seal shall be affixed to all deeds and such other documents as the
Association shall determine.
(2) The Seal of the Association shall be affixed to any instrument in the presence of
the President or the Vice President and either the General Secretary or the Treasurer; and
the President or Vice President and the General Secretary or Treasurer shall sign every
instrument to which the Seal of the company is so affixed in their presence.
8\. Saving
Nothing in this Act shall affect or be deemed to affect the rights of Her Majesty the
Queen, Her Heirs and Successors, all bodies politic and corporate and all others except
such as are mentioned in this Act or anyone claiming by, from or under them.

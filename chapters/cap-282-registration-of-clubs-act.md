## CHAPTER 282

REGISTRATION OF CLUBS ACT
An Act to make provision for the registration of clubs and the supply or sale of
intoxicating liquor therein.
[Amended by Act No. 24 of 1961.]

[9th April, 1930.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Registration of Clubs Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“club” includes, except where the context does not so admit, proprietary club;
“intoxicating liquor” includes every description of spirits, wines, beer, ale, porter,
stout, cider, perry and other malt liquor;
“proprietary club” means a club the property whereof is not vested in all the
members thereof, but is vested in one or more persons who bear the expenses of the
club and retain for their own benefit the profits derived from the sale of liquor and all
other charges made to the members thereof;
“registered club” means a club registered under this Act;

“secretary” includes any officer of a club or other person performing the duties of a
secretary and in the case of a proprietary club where there is no secretary, the
proprietor of the club;
“spirits” has the meaning assigned thereto in the Liquor Dealers’ Licences Act,

## Chapter 174;

“unregistered club” means a club which is required to be registered under this Act
but is not registered or which has been struck off the register.
3\. Registration of clubs
(1) The secretary (whether honorary or otherwise) of every club which occupies a
house or part of a house or other premises which are habitually used for the purposes of a
club and in which any intoxicating liquor is supplied to members or their guests shall
cause the club to be registered in accordance with this Act.
(2) The registration of a club under this Act does not constitute the club premises
licensed premises or authorise any sale of intoxicating liquor therein which would
otherwise be illegal.
4\. Keeping of register
(1) The Permanent Secretary (Finance) shall keep a register of all registered clubs
within Grenada.
(2) The register shall be in the form prescribed by the Minister and shall contain—
(a)

the name and objects of the club;

(b)

the address of the club;

(c)

the name of the secretary;

(d)

the number of members;

(e)

a printed copy of the rules of the club.

5\. Return by secretary of club
(1) The secretary of every such club shall in the month of January in each year furnish
to the Permanent Secretary (Finance) a return signed by the secretary giving the abovementioned particulars together with a signed statement that there is kept upon the club
premises a register of the names and addresses of the club members and a record of the
latest payment of their subscriptions.
(2) Where a new club requiring registration is about to be opened the secretary shall,
before opening the club, furnish a return signed by him to the Permanent Secretary
(Finance) giving the particulars contained in section 4(2).
6\. Conditions for registration of clubs other than proprietary clubs
(1) No club (other than a proprietary club) shall be registered under this Act unless it
complies with all the following conditions—
(a)

that the business and affairs of the club shall be under the management of a
committee or governing body, elected for not less than a year by the general
body of members and subject in whole or in a specified proportion to annual
re-election, and that no member of the committee of the governing body and
no manager or employee of the club shall have any personal interest in the
sale of intoxicating liquor therein or in the profits arising from such sale;

(b)

that the names and addresses of persons proposed as ordinary members of
the club shall be displayed in a conspicuous place in the club premises for at

least a week before their election, and that an interval of not less than two
weeks shall elapse between nomination and election of ordinary members;
(c)

that all members shall be elected by the whole body of members or by a
committee of the club members;

(d)

that there should be a defined subscription payable by members in advance;

(e)

that correct accounts and books shall be kept showing the financial affairs
and intromissions of the club;

(f)

that no intoxicating liquor shall be sold, or supplied, for consumption outside
the premises of the club;

(g)

that a visitor shall not be supplied with intoxicating liquor in the club
premises unless on the invitation and in company of a member and that
member shall, upon the admission of such visitor to the club premises or
immediately upon his or her being supplied with such liquor, enter his or her
own name and the name and address of the visitor in a book which shall be
kept for the purpose and which shall show the date of each visit;

(h)

that no person shall be allowed to become an honorary or temporary member
of the club or be relieved of the payment of the regular entrance fee or
subscription, except a person possessing certain qualifications defined in the
rules or subject to conditions and regulations prescribed therein;

(i)

that no person under sixteen years of age shall be admitted a member of the
club, unless the club is one primarily devoted to some athletic purpose, and
in the latter case that no intoxicating liquor shall be sold or supplied to any
member or person under sixteen years of age.

(2) No proprietary club shall be registered under this Act unless it complies with all
the following conditions—
(a)

that the names and addresses of persons proposed as ordinary members of the
club shall be displayed in a conspicuous place in the club premises for at
least a week before their election, and that an interval of not less than two
weeks shall elapse between nomination and election of ordinary members;

(b)

that there should be a defined subscription payable by members in advance;

(c)

that no intoxicating liquor shall be sold, or supplied, for consumption outside
the premises of the club;

(d)

that a visitor shall not be supplied with intoxicating liquor in the club
premises unless on the invitation and in the company of a member and that
member shall, upon the admission of such visitor to the club premises or
immediately upon his or her being supplied with such liquor, enter his or her
own name and the name and address of the visitor in a book which shall be
kept for the purpose and which shall show the date of each visit;

(e)

that no person shall be allowed to become an honorary or temporary member
of the club or be relieved of the payment of the regular entrance fee or
subscription, except a person possessing certain qualifications defined in the
rules or subject to conditions and regulations prescribed therein;

(f)

that no person under sixteen years of age shall be admitted as a member of
the club;

(g)

that the club shall not open before 7 a.m. and not close later than midnight
every day except with the written permission of the Minister;

(h)

that correct accounts and books shall be kept showing the financial affairs of
the club.

(3) Subject to the conditions laid down in subsections (1) and (2) of this section, as
the case may be, and provided that he or she is satisfied—
(a)

that the locality and premises are suitable; and

(b)

that the office bearers are fit and proper persons to manage a club,

the Permanent Secretary (Finance) shall grant the application to be registered.
7\. Annual licence
(1) Every club registered under this Act shall obtain from the Permanent Secretary
(Finance) an annual licence in the form in the Schedule. Such licence shall continue in
force from the date of grant thereof until the following 31st December.
(2) The annual fee for such licence shall be—
(a)

for a member’s club—
(i) in the town of St. George or within two miles of the limits of the town,
one hundred and twenty dollars,
(ii) in any other place, thirty dollars;

(b)

for a proprietary club, fifty dollars:

Provided that where the licence is granted after the 30th June one-half of such fee
shall be payable.
8\. Striking club off register
(1) Where a club has been registered in pursuance of this Act, a court of summary
jurisdiction on complaint in writing by any person may, if it thinks fit, make an order
directing that the club be struck off the register on all or any of the following grounds,
namely—
(a)

that the club has ceased to exist;

(b)

that it is not conducted in good faith as a club or that it is kept or habitually
used for any unlawful purpose;

(c)

that there is frequent drunkenness on the club premises;

(d)

that illegal sales of intoxicating liquor have taken place on the club premises;
or

(e)

that the conditions laid down in section 6(1) and (2), as the case may be, are
not being complied with.

(2) Where the court makes an order striking a club off the register the court may, if it
thinks fit, by that order further direct that the premises occupied by the club shall not be
used for the purposes of any club which requires registration under this Act for a
specified period which may extend to twelve months in case of a first order or in case of a
second or subsequent order to five years:
Provided that any such direction may for good cause shown be subsequently cancelled
or varied by the court.
(3) No club shall at any time cause a public nuisance to occupants of neighbouring
premises; and if the Chief of Police shall serve at the club premises notice of any such
nuisance, the secretary of the club shall take immediate steps to effectually abate such
nuisance.
9\. Annual licence cancelled when club struck off register

If a club is ordered to be struck off the register the annual licence held by that club
shall, ipso facto, be cancelled.
10\. Payment of trade duty and keeping of stock books by clubs
No spirits other than spirits whereon trade duty shall have been paid shall be received for
sale on the premises of a registered club; and a registered club shall pay such trade duty and
comply with such provisions of the Liquor Dealers’ Licences Act, Chapter 174, relating to
stock book and permits as apply to licensed dealers.
11\. Offences and penalties
(1) If any intoxicating liquor is kept for supply or sale on the premises of an
unregistered club, every officer and member of the club shall be guilty of an offence and
liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of two hundred and fifty dollars unless he or she
proves to the satisfaction of the court that such liquor was so kept without his or her
knowledge or consent.
(2) Intoxicating liquor shall not be supplied in a registered club for consumption off
the premises, and if any person supplies or obtains any intoxicating liquor in
contravention of the provisions of this section, he or she shall be guilty of an offence and
liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of five hundred dollars.
(3) If the secretary of a registered club or any club which is required to be registered
omits to make any return required by this Act, he or she shall be guilty of an offence and
liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of five hundred dollars or, in case of a second or
subsequent offence, to a fine of one thousand dollars and to imprisonment for one month.
(4) If the secretary of any club knowingly makes a return which is false in any
material particular he or she shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on summary
conviction, to a fine of three thousand dollars and to imprisonment for three months.
(5) In addition to the above-mentioned penalties the court may order that the club in
the premises whereof the offence was committed be struck off the register.

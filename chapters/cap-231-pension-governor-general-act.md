## CHAPTER 231

PENSION (GOVERNOR-GENERAL) ACT
An Act to provide for the payment of a pension and gratuity in respect of the office
of Governor-General.
[Act No. 24 of 1989.]

[14th July, 1989.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Pension (Governor-General) Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“pensionable emoluments” includes actual salary;
“salary” means the pensionable emoluments provided in the annual Estimates of
Expenditure.
3\. Grant of pension
Every person who holds the office of Governor-General after the commencement of
this Act shall be paid a pension under this Act with effect from the day on which he or
she ceases to be Governor-General and the pension shall continue during his or her
lifetime.
4\. Suspension of payment of pension

If a person to whom a pension is being paid under this Act is again appointed
Governor-General or is appointed to any other office in Government, the payment of the
pension shall be suspended during the period in which that person holds any such
appointment.
5\. Annual rate of pension
The annual rate of a pension under this Act shall be—
(a)

two-fifths of the highest annual salary granted to him or her as GovernorGeneral in cases where he or she has served as Governor-General for a
period of five consecutive years or more but less than ten consecutive years;

(b)

two-thirds of the highest annual salary granted to him or her as GovernorGeneral in cases where he or she has served as Governor-General for a
period of ten consecutive years or more; or

(c)

the proportional rate of what he or she would have received if he or she had
served as Governor-General for five consecutive years in cases where he or
she has served for less than five consecutive years.

6\. Grant of gratuity
Every person entitled to a pension under this Act shall, on ceasing to be GovernorGeneral, be granted a gratuity of twenty-five per cent of the aggregate salary received by
him or her during his or her term of office as Governor-General.
7\. Death of Governor-General in office
Where a person dies while he or she is Governor-General there shall be granted to his
or her legal personal representative an amount equal to the Governor-General’s annual
salary or the gratuity for which he or she would have been eligible, whichever is greater.
8\. Widow’s pension
(1) There shall be granted to the widow of a Governor-General who dies while
holding office or after retirement therefrom an annual pension equivalent to one-fifth of
the annual salary which the Governor-General was receiving immediately before his or
her death or retirement, as the case may be.
(2) A widow shall not be entitled to receive and shall not be paid a pension under
subsection (1) in respect of any period after her remarriage, or in respect of any period
after she becomes Governor-General or Prime Minister.
(3) For the purposes of this section, “widow” includes “widower”.
9\. Pension not assignable or transferable, etc.
No pension or gratuity payable under this Act shall—
(a)

be assignable or transferable except for the purpose of satisfying a debt due
to the Government or a court order for the payment of periodical sums of
money towards the maintenance of the wife, former wife, or child being a
minor child, of the person to whom the pension is payable; or

(b)

be liable to be attached, sequestered or levied upon for or in respect of any
debt or claim whatever except a debt due to the Government or a sum
recoverable pursuant to a court order such as is mentioned in paragraph (a).

10\. Pension payable in arrears

A pension required to be paid under this Act shall be paid monthly in arrears and shall
be a charge on and paid out of the Consolidated Fund.

## CHAPTER 30

BEREAN CORPORATION OF GRENADA (INCORPORATION) ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of, and the vesting of property in, the
Incorporated Trustees of the Berean Corporation of Grenada.
[Act No. 92 of 1979.]

[30th November, 1979.]
1\.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Berean Corporation of Grenada (Incorporation) Act.

2\.

Incorporation of Trustees

De Costa Christopher, Alphonso Quash, Jack Sampson, John Saville, Peter Greenidge,
Leonard George, and their successors in office as Trustees of the Berean Corporation
of Grenada, are hereby created a body corporate under the name “The Incorporated
Trustees of the Berean Corporation of Grenada” (hereinafter called “the Trustees”) and
by that name shall have perpetual succession and in that name may sue and be sued
in all courts and shall have and use a Common Seal.
3\.

Trustees may acquire property

The Trustees shall have power to acquire for the Berean Mission Incorporated
(hereinafter called “the Mission”) by purchase, transfer, exchange, devise, demise,
grant, gift, bequest or otherwise, land and interests in land of whatsoever nature or
kind in perpetuity or for life or lives or for any term of years, and also money,
securities for money, goods, or chattels, and to take, hold and enjoy the same subject
to the performance of any trust upon which the said land, interests in land, money,
securities for money, goods or chattels may have been acquired.
4\.

Trustees may dispose of property

The Trustees may, subject to the performance of any trust as aforesaid, from time
to time by deed under their seal, sell or demise, grant, convey, transfer, exchange,
surrender or otherwise dispose of any land or interest in land or other property vested
by this Act in them or which may hereafter be acquired by or become vested in them.
5\.

Trustees may borrow and give mortgages

The Trustees may, subject to the performance of any trust as aforesaid, borrow
money, and for payment of the same, by deed under their seal, may give and execute
mortgages, charges, pledges or other securities of their real or personal property or
make any such arrangements as they may consider convenient, with such covenants,
powers and provisions as they may think proper.
6\.

Vesting of all land and property

All churches, chapels, mission houses, rectories, vicarages, parsonages, glebes,
buildings, appurtenances and lands held by or vested in the Mission or in any other
person or body for and on behalf, or appropriated to the use, of the Mission and in
particular the land (and all buildings and appurtenances thereon) described in the
Schedule hereto and all other property whatsoever held by or vested in the Mission at
the commencement of this Act, are vested in the Trustees.
7\.

Mission not to have alien status

Notwithstanding that the Trustees may at any time and from time to time for the
purposes of the Aliens (Land-Holding Regulation) Act, Chapter 13, be or be deemed to
be under alien control, the Trustees shall not be or be deemed to be an alien for such
purposes but shall be entitled to hold land for any estate or interest therein and
mortgages on land without licence and without liability to forfeiture or other penalty
under that Act.
8\.

Mode of sealing documents

All deeds and other documents requiring the seal of the Trustees shall be sealed
with the Common Seal of the Trustees in the presence of at least two of the Trustees
who shall sign every such deed or other document to which the seal is affixed.
9\.

Service of documents on Trustees

(1) The Trustees shall at all time have a fixed address in Grenada for the service of
documents on them and such address and any change thereof shall be registered with
the Registrar of Companies.
(2) Any document may be served on the Trustees by leaving the same at, or
sending the same by registered post to, the address registered pursuant to subsection
(1).

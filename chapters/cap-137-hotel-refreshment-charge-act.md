## CHAPTER 137

HOTEL REFRESHMENT CHARGE ACT
An Act to provide for the imposition and collection of a charge payable on the
purchase of food or drinks at hotels, and for connected matters.
[Act No. 5 of 1973 amended by Act No. 33 of 1975.]

[12th May, 1973.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Hotel Refreshment Charge Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“Collector” means the Permanent Secretary (Finance) and any public officer acting
for the purposes of this Act under his or her general or specific instructions;
“hotel” has the meaning assigned in section 2 of the Hotels Aid Act, Chapter 138;
“management” includes a manager of a hotel and every other person, company,
corporation or firm responsible for carrying on the business of a hotel;
“refreshment” means food or drinks of any kind whatsoever.
3\. Refreshment charge, and when payable
(1) Every person who has bought refreshment from a hotel shall pay a refreshment
charge of seven and one-half per cent of the cost of the refreshment.

(2) The charge is payable in one sum at the time of payment for the refreshment.
(3) This section does not apply to the Governor-General.
4\. Duty of the management to collect the charge
It is the duty of the management to collect the charge and to pay the same to the
Collector at the Treasury at the end of every day of collection or at such other time or
place as he or she may specify in a written notification to the management.
5\. Failure of management to collect or pay the charge an offence
If the management fails to collect the charge payable under this Act or to pay the same
(whether it was or was not collected) to the Collector in the manner required pursuant to
section 4, the management is guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a
fine of three thousand dollars and, in addition to the fine, the magistrate shall order the
management to pay the charge into the court.
6\. Keeping and inspection of record, etc.
(1) It is the duty of the management to keep a record of the amounts of the charge
collected.
(2) The Collector may at any time and at any place inspect the record so kept and, if
he or she deems it necessary, he or she may take extracts from such record.
(3) If the management fails to keep the record required by subsection (1) or obstructs
the Collector in the exercise of his powers under subsection (2) the management is guilty
of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of five thousand dollars.
7\. Power of the Minister of Finance to make regulations
(1) The Minister of Finance may make regulations to provide for—
(a)

the production, and the inspection by the Collector, of the record kept
pursuant to section 6 and other matters incidental thereto;

(b)

any other matter which the Minister deems it expedient to include in
regulations for the better carrying out of the purposes of this Act.

(2) Regulations made under subsection (1) shall have no force or effect until they
have been approved by resolution of the House of Representatives.

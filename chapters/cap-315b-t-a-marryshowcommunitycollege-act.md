## CHAPTER 315B

T.A. MARRYSHOW COMMUNITY COLLEGE ACT
An Act to make further provision respecting the T.A. Marryshow Community
College, and for matters connected therewith and incidental thereto.
[Act No. 41 of 1996 amended by Act No. 18 of 2009.]

[14th March, 1997.]

## PART I

Preliminary

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the T.A. Marryshow Community College Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“Academic Board” means the Academic Board established by section 12 of this
Act;
“academic semester” and “academic year” mean such periods as prescribed by the
Council for the purpose of the academic programmes of the College;

“Bursar” means the Bursar of the College appointed under this Act;
“Chairperson” means the Chairperson of the Council appointed by the Minister,
and “Deputy Chairperson” shall be construed accordingly;
“College” means the T.A. Marryshow Community College established by
section 3;
“Council” or “College Council” means the Council of the T.A. Marryshow
Community College established by section 7;
“Education Programme Board” means the Academic Programme Board established
by section 13 of this Act;
“Finance Committee” means the Finance Committee established by section 11 of
this Act;
“financial year” means such period of twelve months as determined by the Council;
“functions” includes powers and duties;
“Minister” means Minister responsible for Education;
“Principal” means the Principal of the College appointed in accordance with this
Act;
“Registrar” means the Registrar of the College appointed in accordance with this
Act.

## PART II

Establishment and Objectives of College

### 3.

Establishment of College
(1) The educational institution known as the T.A. Marryshow Community College
established by the T.A. Marryshow College Act, Chapter 315B, shall continue to exist as
if it were established by this Act.
(2) The College shall be a body corporate to which section 49 of the Interpretation
and General Provisions Act, Chapter 153, shall apply.

### 4.

Objectives of College
(1) The objectives of the College shall be—
(a)

to provide a seat of education, learning and research of a standard required
and expected of an education institution of the highest standard;

(b)

to secure the advancement of knowledge;

(c)

to carry out relevant research and provide services to the community within
its competence to do so;

(d)

to grant degrees, associate degrees, diplomas, certificates and other awards;

(e)

to enter into association or affiliation with universities, colleges or other
institutions of higher learning, whether within Grenada or otherwise for the
purposes of the foregoing as the Council may consider necessary or
appropriate.

(2) Without prejudice to anything contained in subsection (1), the College shall
provide education and training at the post-secondary and tertiary levels in—
(a)

agriculture;

(b)

arts and social science;

(c)

science and technology;

health and environmental science;

(e)

teacher education and educational administration;

(f)

technical and business education and management studies;

(g)

general studies and continuing education;

(h)

skills training; and

(i)

such other fields of education as the Council may from time to time
determine after consultation with the Academic Board.

### 5.

Faculties, etc., of the College
The Council may for the purpose of achieving the objectives of the College establish
such number of faculties, schools, departments and divisions as are deemed necessary or
expedient from time to time.

### 6.

Admission to College
(1) Subject to subsection (2), a person shall be eligible for admission to the College as a
student thereof if he or she possesses the qualifications as the Council prescribes.
(2) No religious, political or racial test shall be imposed on or required of any person
in order to entitle him or her to be a student at the College or to obtain employment at the
College.

## PART III

Governance of College

### 7.

Establishment of College Council
(1) There shall be established for the purposes of the management of the College a
body to be known as the College Council or Council.
(2) The Schedule shall have effect in relation to the constitution, and procedures of
the Council.

### 8.

Functions of the Council
Without affecting the generality of section 7, the Council shall—
(a)

implement the objectives of the College;

(b)

formulate the policy of the College;

(c)

control and superintend the property and activities of the College;

(d)

provide for the welfare of the students of the College;

(e)

appoint such academic, administrative and other staff as appears to the
Council to be necessary, on such terms and conditions as the Council
determines; and

(f)

adjudicate upon disciplinary charges against students or members of the staff
of the College.

### 9.

Committees of the Council

(1) The Council may establish such number of committees as it considers necessary
or expedient for the purpose of carrying out its functions under this Act.
(2) A committee established under subsection (1) may consist of persons who are
members of the Council and persons who are not, but unless otherwise provided by this
Act any such committee shall consist of no more than nine persons of whom no more
than five shall be members of the Council.
(3) A committee appointed under section (1) shall perform such functions as the
Council determines arising out of or connected with the powers and duties of the Council
under this Act.
(4) The Council may by resolution reject the report of any such committee appointed
under subsection (1) or adopt it wholly, or with such modifications, additions or
adaptations as the Council determines.
(5) A committee of the Council, including the standing committees thereof, may
regulate its own procedure subject to any written directions of the Council in this regard.

### 10.

Standing committees of the Council
Without prejudice to the generality of section 9, there shall be three standing
committees of the Council and which shall be the Finance Committee, the Academic
Board and the Education Programmes Board.

### 11.

Composition and duties of Finance Committee
(1) The Finance Committee shall be constituted by a representative of the Ministry of
Finance, a representative of the Ministry of Education, two persons from the private
sector appointed by the Minister, the Principal, the Bursar and the Deans.
(2) The Finance Committee shall be responsible for the financial affairs of the
College and in particular shall—
(a)

review financial plans and budgets for the College;

(b)

make rules to regulate the financial transactions of the College subject to
approval by the Council;

(c)

supervise expenditure and approve variations within the approved budget up
to limits set from time to time by the Council;

(d)

review statements of accounts for consideration by the Council; and

(e)

manage the funds of the College and make recommendations for the
investment thereof.

### 12.

Composition and duties of Academic Board
(1) The Academic Board shall be constituted by the Principal, Deputy Principal and
not more than fifteen full-time members of the academic staff.
(2) The Principal, or in his or her absence the Deputy Principal shall be the
Chairperson of the Academic Board.
(3) The Academic Board shall be responsible for the academic programme of the
College and in particular shall—
(a)

monitor and make arrangements to maintain academic and teaching
standards in programmes of the College;

(b)

keep under review and develop curriculum content and teaching methods;

(c)

devise and develop course programmes requirements;

be responsible for moderation, accreditation and certification of academic
achievement subject to any requirements of the Council and the Education
Programmes Board;

(e)

arrange for staff development, training and research.

### 13.

Composition and duties of Education Programmes Board
(1) The Education Programmes Board shall consist of—
(a)

the Principal or Deputy Principal;

(b)

the Registrar;

(c)

three members of the Academic Board designated by the Chairperson of the
Council.

(2) The Education Programmes Board shall be responsible for the educational
programmes of the College and in particular shall—
(a)

prepare education development plans for the College for the consideration of
the Council;

(b)

approve the programmes of courses and consider any proposals for new
courses proposed by the Academic Board;

(c)

prepare rules with respect to the admission of students in consultation with
the Academic Board for the consideration of the Council;

(d)

monitor the number and attendance of students in classes and on work
placements and advise the Council accordingly;

(e)

consider and make recommendations to the Council for the accreditation of
courses by external bodies and approve arrangements for internal
accreditation in consultation with the Academic Board.

### 14.

Delegation
Subject to this Act, the Council may delegate to any member thereof, or to a
committee of the Council the power and authority to carry out on its behalf such duties as
the Council determines; but the delegation of any such power or authority shall not
prevent the Council from itself exercising the power or authority so delegated.

### 15.

Discipline of students
The Principal may exercise disciplinary control over students of the College and in
this regard may consult the Council or any committee of the Council appointed pursuant
to this Act.

### 16.

General directions
The Minister may, after consultation with the Council, give the Council—
(a)

directions of a general character as to the policy to be followed in the
exercise and performance of its functions in matters appearing to him or her
to concern the public interest;

(b)

directions for the remedying of any serious defect or failure in the successful
performance of its functions,

and the Council shall give effect to such directions.

### 17.

Remuneration of members of Council and committees

(1) The members of the Council shall receive such remuneration and allowances as
the Minister approves.
(2) Where persons, not being members of the Council are members of committees
appointed pursuant to section 9(1), the Council may by resolution determine the
remuneration and allowances of such persons, and such sums shall properly be payable
out of the funds and resources of the College.

## PART IV

Staff of College and Related Matters

### 18.

Staff generally
(1) Subject to this Part, the College shall have such, number of academic,
administrative and other staff as the Council determines to be necessary from time to
time.
(2) The employment of persons under subsection (1) shall be subject to such terms
and conditions as may be agreed between the employee and the Council.
(3) Members of the staff of the College, other than the Principal, the academic staff
and senior administrative staff shall be appointed by the Principal or such other person to
whom the power is delegated by the Council.
(4) The Principal, academic staff and senior administrative staff shall be appointed by
the Council.

### 19.

Principal
(1) There shall be a Principal of the College who shall be appointed by the Council
after consultation with the Minister.
(2) The Principal shall be the academic and administrative head of the College and
shall generally supervise and direct the academic work of the College, the academic and
administrative staff and other staff of the College and such other duties as may from time
to time be assigned to him or her by the Council.
(3) The Principal may be removed from office on the ground of misconduct,
inefficiency or other good cause.
(4) Whenever the Principal is absent from Grenada or is for any reason unable to
perform the duties of his or her office, such duties shall be performed by the Deputy
Principal or such other persons as the Council may appoint during such absence or
inability.

### 20.

Duties of Principal
Without prejudice to any other duties conferred upon him or her by this Act, the
Principal shall be responsible for—
(a)

the implementation of policy and for all phases of the operation,
administration, supervision maintenance and promotion of the College
programmes;

(b)

the introduction of measures, mechanisms and procedures as are necessary
for the effective discharge of his or her duties;

(c)

the provision of leadership in matters relating to the academic programmes
of the College;

the promotion of the interests of the College both within and outside Grenada;

(e)

the exercise of such other powers conferred on him or her or delegated to
him or her by the Council in accordance with this Act.

### 21.

Registrar
(1) There shall be a Registrar of the College who shall be appointed by the Council.
(2) The Registrar shall be subject to the directions of the Principal and shall be
responsible for the day to day administration of the College.
(3) The Registrar shall perform the duties of Secretary to the Council, and the
Academic Board.

### 22.

Bursar
(1) There shall be a Bursar of the College who shall be appointed by the Council.
(2) The Bursar shall be the chief financial officer of the College and shall be
responsible for the management and control of the funds of the College.

### 23.

Appointment of academic staff
(1) A member of the academic staff shall, on first appointment, be required to serve a
probationary period of one calendar year unless the Council, dispenses with the
requirement to serve a probationary period; but such requirement shall not apply to
persons serving on the permanent staff of the College at the commencement of this Act or
to public officers who are seconded to the College.
(2) Where a member of the academic staff is appointed in the first instance for a
probationary period, the Council at the expiration thereof, may, on the recommendation
of the Principal, appoint him or her to a post on the permanent staff of the College.
(3) The appointment to the permanent staff of the College of any member of the
teaching staff who was in the first instance appointed for a probationary period, shall be
deemed to have taken effect from the date of his or her appointment on probation.
(4) The appointment of a member of the academic staff who is serving the
probationary period mentioned in subsection (2) may be terminated by one month’s
notice given by the Council to such member or by such member to the Council.
(5) A notice given under subsection (4) shall be transmitted through the Principal.
(6) A member of the staff who is employed by the Council on a full-time basis shall
not hold any other appointment or engage in any activity which constitutes a conflict of
interest or which in the opinion of the Council, may interfere with the proper performance
of his or her duties as a member of the teaching staff or is prejudicial to the welfare of the
College.

### 24.

Discipline
(1) A member of the staff may be suspended by the Principal for—
(a)

gross neglect of duty; or

(b)

misconduct calculated to injure, or capable of injuring, the reputation of the
College.

(2) The Principal shall immediately upon any such suspension report the same and the
circumstances thereof in writing to the Chairperson who shall within seven days of the
receipt by him or her of the Principal’s report summon a meeting of the Council to inquire
into and adjudicate upon the matter.

### 25.

Dismissal of staff generally

(1) The Council may dismiss any member of staff of the College appointed to a post
on the permanent staff of the College subject to the following conditions—
(a)

the member shall be given due notice that his or her dismissal is under
consideration together with a statement of the charge alleged against him or
her;

(b)

the member shall be given an opportunity to defend himself or herself
against any such charge before a tribunal appointed by the Council to hear
and determine the charge.

(2) Where a member of the staff of the College is appointed and employed under a
written contract of service, the terms of such contract in relation to the termination thereof
shall have effect in this regard.

### 26.

Secondment of staff
The Public Service Commission may, subject to such condition as it may impose,
approve the secondment to the College of an officer in the public service; but an officer
so seconded shall, in relation to pension, gratuity or other benefits and in relation to other
rights as a public officer, be treated as continued service in the public service.

### 27.

Status of seconded staff
For the avoidance of doubt it is hereby declared that an officer seconded to the
College shall continue to be a public officer until such time when he or she resigns, retires
or otherwise leaves the public service.

## PART V

Finance and Accounting

### 28.

Funds and resources of College
The funds and resources of the College shall consist of—
(a)

such sums as may be voted for the purposes of the College by Parliament;

(b)

such sums as received by the College as fees or charges payable;

(c)

subject to section 30, sums borrowed by the College for the purpose of
meeting any of its obligations or discharging its functions;

(d)

all other sums of money or other property which may in any manner become
payable to or vested in the College in respect of any matter relating to its
obligations and functions.

### 29.

Fees and other charges
(1) The College shall in respect of educational services or facilities offered to the
public pursuant to this Act impose such fees and other charges as may be prescribed by
the Council.
(2) Fees and charges imposed pursuant to this section shall be paid to the College and
become the property of the College.

### 30.

Vesting of property
(1) The Minister may by vesting order vest real property in the College free from
encumbrances.

(2) In this section, “Minister” means the Minister who has the legal authority to
transfer land owned by the Crown.

### 31.

Power to borrow and guarantee of borrowing
(1) Subject to subsection (2), the College may borrow sums required by it for meeting
any of its obligations or discharging any of its functions or objectives.
(2) The power of the College to borrow shall be exercisable only with the approval of
the Minister as to the amount and source of borrowing and as to the terms and conditions
on which the borrowing may be effected.
(3) The Minister may, with the approval of Parliament, guarantee in such manner and
on such conditions as he or her considers appropriate, the repayment of the principal,
interest and other charges with respect to any authorised borrowing by the College.
(4) Where the Minister is satisfied that there has been default in the payment of any
principal monies or interest guaranteed under this section he or she shall direct payment
out of the Consolidated Fund of the amount in respect of which there has been default.
(5) The College shall make payment to the Accountant General, at such time and in
such manner as the Minister may direct, of the amount so directed in or toward the
repayment of a sum debited to the Consolidated Fund in fulfillment of a guarantee given
under this section, and in or towards the payment of interest on what is outstanding for
the time being in respect of the sum so debited at such rate as the Minister may direct, and
different rates of interest may be directed as regards different sums and as regards interest
for different periods.
(6) For the purposes of this section, “Minister” means the Minister of Finance.

### 32.

Accounts and audit
(1) The Council shall cause to be kept proper accounts and other records of the
transactions of the College to the satisfaction of the Minister of Finance and such
accounts shall be audited annually by an auditor who is a practising member of a
recognised supervisory body.
(2) The officers and staff of the College shall grant to the independent auditor and
members of his or her staff access to all books, documents, monies and other property of
the College as may be required and shall give him or her or any of his or her staff on
request all the information as may be within their knowledge in relation to the operation
of the College.

### 33.

Annual reports
(1) The Council shall submit to the Minister—
(a)

within three months after the end of the academic year, or within such
further time as the Minister may permit, a report on the academic activities
of the College during that academic year;

(b)

within three months after the end of the financial year, a statement of its
accounts audited in accordance with section 31 for the financial year ending
in that year.

### 34.

Laying of reports
The Minister shall cause a copy of each of the reports submitted to him or her under
section 33 to be laid in both Houses of Parliament within one month after the receipt
thereof.

### 35.

Pensions and gratuities
The College shall establish a scheme for the payment of pensions or gratuities to its
academic, administrative and other staff who retire from the service of the College or
otherwise cease to hold office or be employed by reason of age, infirmity of mind or body
or on the abolition of office.

### 36.

Exemption from taxes
Notwithstanding anything contained in any other enactment, the College is hereby
exempted from all taxes and duties.

## PART VI

Miscellaneous

### 37.

Evidence
Minutes made of meetings of the Council shall, if signed by the Chairperson or
Deputy Chairperson and Secretary of the Council, be receivable in evidence in all legal
proceedings without further proof and every meeting of the Council in respect of which
minutes have been so signed shall be deemed to have been duly convened and held and
that all members present thereof to have been duly qualified to act.

### 38.

Rules and regulations
(1) The Council may with the approval of the Minister, make rules and regulations
respecting—
(a)

the academic programmes of the College, including the content of courses;

(b)

the admission, conduct and discipline of students;

(c)

the academic years or semesters and the vacation and holidays to be
recognised by the College;

(d)

the examinations to be set and conducted by the College and related matters;

(e)

the fee and charges to be paid for the services and facilities of the College;

(f)

the custody of monies forming part of the funds and resources of the College;

(g)

the proceedings of the Council and its committees;

(h)

the grant and conditions of leave for the staff of the College;

(i)

the terms and conditions of the staff of the College;

(j)

the exercise of its functions under this Act and generally to give effect to the
provisions of this Act.

(2) The Council may consult any of its committees prior to the making of any rules or
regulations under subsection (1).
(3) Rules and regulations made under subsection (1) shall be subject to negative
resolution of Parliament.

### 39.

Transitional
The staff presently employed at the T.A. Marryshow Community College shall
continue to be employed as if they were employed pursuant to this Act without any loss
of benefits, status or any other like matter.

### 40.

Repeal
The T.A. Marryshow Community College Act, 1995, is hereby repealed.

### 41.

Commencement
This Act comes into force on the 14th day of March, 1997.

Schedule
Functions and Procedures of the Council
[Section 7(2).]

1\. Constitution of Council
(1) The Minister, after consultation with the Education Advisory Board and the bodies specified in this
paragraph shall appoint the Council which shall consist of the following persons two of whom the Minister
shall designate as Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson, respectively—

(a)

two representatives of the Grenada Union of Teachers;

(b)

three persons to represent the Chamber of Industry and Commerce, the
tourism sector and the agricultural sector;

(c)

one representative of the Principals Association of Secondary Schools;

(d)

one representative of the student body of the College;

(e)

one representative of the Grenada Institute of Professional Engineers;

(f)

two persons appointed by the Minister;

(g)

the Principal, ex officio; and

(h)

the Chief Education Officer, ex officio.

(2) The Registrar shall be the Secretary of the Council but without the right to vote at
meetings of the Council.
2\. Temporary Membership
The Minister may appoint any person to act temporarily in the place of any member of
the Council in the case of the absence or inability of such member to act.
3\. Duration and Termination of Membership
(1) A member of the Council, other than the Principal and Deputy Principal, shall
hold office for three years, but shall be eligible for re-appointment; but a member shall
not hold office for periods which in the aggregate amount to more than nine years.
(2) The Chairperson may at any time resign his or her office by instrument in writing
addressed to the Minister and the resignation shall take effect from the date of its receipt
by the Minister.
(3) A member of the Council, other than the Chairperson, may at any time resign his
or her office by instrument in writing addressed to the Minister and transmitted through
the Chairperson and from the date of the receipt by the Minister of the resignation the
member shall cease to be member of the Council.
(4) The Minister may, on the advice of the Council, revoke the appointment of any
member of the Council, and shall do so if a member is found guilty during his or her term
of office of any malpractice or misconduct which would bring the College into disrepute.

4\. Meetings of Council
(1) The Council shall meet at least once in every academic term and at other times as
may be necessary or expedient for the transaction of its business and such meetings shall
be held at places and times and on such days as the Council may determine.
(2) The Chairperson, or in his or her absence the Deputy Chairperson, shall preside at
a meeting of the Council.
(3) At any meeting of the Council, in case of the absence or inability to act of both the
Chairperson and the Deputy Chairperson, the members of the Council present shall elect
one of their members to preside at the meeting.
(4) The decisions of the Council shall be a majority of votes and in addition to an
original vote, in any case in which the voting is equal, the Chairperson, or in his or her
absence the Deputy Chairperson or other member presiding at that meeting, shall have a
casting vote.
5\. Minister to be represented at meetings
The Minister may attend any meeting of the Council or a committee thereof or be
represented at any such meeting by any person authorised by him or her in that behalf;
and the Minister or that person may take part in the proceedings at that meeting but shall
not vote on any matter.
6\. Meetings of Board
The Chairperson may at any time summon a meeting of a Board and shall summon a
meeting within seven days—
(a)

of a request for that purpose addressed to him or her by any four members of
that Board; or

(b)

if a direction to that effect is addressed to him or her by the Minister.

7\. Quorum
At any meeting of the Council, or a committee, a quorum shall be the minimum
number constituting a majority of the total number of members of the Council or
committee.
8\. Minutes
(1) Minutes in proper form of each meeting of the Council, Finance Committee,
Educational Programmes Board and Academic Board shall be kept by the persons the
Council may appoint for the purpose and shall be confirmed by the Chairperson or
Deputy Chairperson, as the case may be, as soon as practicable thereafter at a subsequent
meeting.
(2) Copies of the minutes of meetings of each Board shall be submitted to the Council
at its next meeting.

## CHAPTER 315B

SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION

No Subsidiary Legislation

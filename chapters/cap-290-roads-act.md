## CHAPTER 290

ROADS ACT
An Act relating to the construction and maintenance of roads and the regulation of
traffic thereon.
[Amended by Act No. 34 of 1964, Act No. 25 of 1965, Act No. 31 of 1973, Act No. 32 of 1973,
Act No. 4 of 1986, Act No. 1 of 1994.]

[31st December, 1932.]

## PART I

Preliminary

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Roads Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“Minister” means the Minister for the time being responsible for works and
communications;
“poundable animal” means horse, mare, gelding, colt, filly, ass, mule, bull, cow,
ox, steer, heifer, calf, goat, kid, sheep, lamb, hog, sow and pig;
“road” means road, street or thoroughfare over which the public have a right of
passage, and includes bridges, dams, drains, embankments, causeways, fences, and
ditches belonging or appertaining to a road:
Provided that this Act shall not extend to any road, street or thoroughfare within the
limits of any town the boundaries whereof are or shall be hereafter fixed under the Towns
Boundaries Act, Chapter 323, save in so far as a road, street or thoroughfare is expressly
included in the Fifth Schedule;
“traffic” includes pedestrians;
“vehicle” includes motor car, motor van, motor lorry, motor omnibus, motor cycle,
carriage, wagon, tractor, cart, bicycle, tricycle, van, handcart, sledge, truck, barrow,
and any other conveyance for the portage or haulage of goods or persons.

### 3.

Division of Grenada into Road Districts
For the purpose of classifying roads, Grenada shall be divided into four districts, to be
called “Road Districts”, which Road Districts shall be entitled and constituted as
follows—
(a)

“The Southern Road District”, comprising the roads described in the First
Schedule;

(b)

“The Eastern Road District”, comprising the roads described in the Second
Schedule;

(c)

“The Western Road District”, comprising the roads described in the Third
Schedule;

(d)

“The Northern Road District”, comprising the roads described in the Fourth
Schedule.

### 4.

Classification of roads
(1) The roads in Grenada shall be divided into four classes namely—
First class;
Second Class;
Third Class;
Fourth Class.
(2) The roads mentioned in the Fifth Schedule shall be first class roads. The roads
mentioned in the Sixth Schedule shall be second class roads. The roads mentioned in the
Seventh Schedule shall be third class roads. The roads mentioned in the Eighth Schedule
shall be fourth class roads.

## PART II

Establishment, Powers, Duties and Liabilities of the Road Authority

### 5.

Establishment and proceedings of the Central Road Authority
(1) There shall be established a Central Road Authority (in this Act referred to as the
“Road Authority”), consisting of the following persons—
(a)

the Permanent Secretary to the Minister or, in the absence of the Permanent
Secretary, a person nominated by the Minister;

(b)

two persons from each Road District, appointed by the Minister;

(c)

three persons with knowledge and experience of the infrastructure of
Grenada, appointed by the Minister.

(2) Members of the Road Authority appointed by the Minister shall hold office for
such period as the Minister shall specify in their instruments of appointment.
(3) At meetings of the Road Authority five persons shall form a quorum for the
transaction of its business.
(4) The Permanent Secretary to the Minister shall be the chairperson and chief
executive officer of the Road Authority, and he or she or in his or her absence the person
nominated by the Minister shall preside at each meeting:
Provided that in the absence of both from any meeting the members present, if they
constitute a quorum, shall elect one of their number to preside at that meeting.

(5) At every meeting, in the event of an equality of votes on any question, the person
presiding shall have a casting vote in addition to his or her deliberative vote.
(6) Subject to subsection (3), the proceedings at a meeting shall not be invalidated by
reason only of a vacancy in the membership of the Road Authority.
(7) The Minister may appoint a person to be the secretary to the Road Authority.

### 6.

Road Authority to be a corporate body
The Road Authority shall be a corporation with perpetual succession and a Common
Seal, and may sue and be sued under the name of the Central Road Authority of Grenada.

### 6A.

Minister may name or change name of street, road, etc.
Notwithstanding any written law to the contrary the Minister may, by Notice
published in the Gazette, name or change the name of any street, road or thoroughfare in
any town or parish of the State.

### 7.

Powers of Road Authority to make Rules and Orders
(1) The Road Authority may, with the approval of the Minister, make Rules relating
to all or any of the following matters—
(a)

the time and place of its meetings;

(b)

the mode of convening its meetings;

(c)

the attendance of its members;

(d)

the form and order of its debates;

(e)

the powers and duties of its officers and servants; and

(f)

the general regulation of its proceedings and the mode of conducting its
business.

(2) The Road Authority may, by Order made with the approval of the Minister and
published in the Gazette—
(a)

add to the number of Road Districts or abolish any Road District;

(b)

transfer a road from any one Road District to any other Road District;

(c)

subdivide any Road District;

(d)

amalgamate two or more Road Districts;

(e)

add any road to any of the Schedules A, B, C, D, E, F, G and H when not at
the time included therein;

(f)

remove any road from any such Schedule in which it is included;

(g)

transfer any road from any such Schedule to any other such Schedule.

### 8.

Roads to be vested in Road Authority
On and from such day as the Minister may appoint by notice in the Gazette, the care,
control, management, construction, reconstruction, improvement and repair of all roads in
Grenada and of all bridges, causeways, drains, culverts, boundary posts, fences and gates
appertaining thereto and not vested in private persons, shall, subject to the provisions of
this Act, be vested in, exercised and effected by the Road Authority.

### 9.

Powers of Minister to execute road schemes

There shall be vested in the Minister the sole power and authority to give effect to and
execute any scheme or schemes approved and confirmed by the Road Authority.

### 10.

Delegation of powers
The Minister may delegate to his or her Permanent Secretary all or any of the powers
conferred upon him or her by this Act, and the Permanent Secretary may delegate to any
public officer in his or her Ministry, or any road surveyor or warden appointed under this
Act, all or any of the powers conferred upon him or her by this Act, except membership
of the Road Authority and his or her powers deriving therefrom.

### 11.

Road Authority may acquire land
If, in the exercise of its powers and duties under this Act, it shall become necessary to
take possession, for public purposes, of the land of any person, the Road Authority,
subject to the approval of the Minister, may make an agreement on behalf of the
Government with the owner for the compensation to be made for such land and for any
building, tree or fence thereon, and if the Road Authority cannot agree with such owner
as to the compensation to be made, or if the owner cannot be found, proceedings may be
taken by the Minister for obtaining possession of such land and for compensating the
owner in the manner prescribed by the Land Acquisition Act, Chapter 159, or any other
Act which may hereafter be passed dealing with the compulsory acquisition of land by
the Government.

### 12.

Road Authority may enter into contracts
The Road Authority may enter into all contracts relating to matters necessary for the
purpose of their powers under this Act.

## PART III

Financial Provisions

### 13.

Cost of road work: how paid
The cost of constructing, reconstructing, improving, maintaining or repairing roads
shall be paid by warrant of the Minister on the Permanent Secretary (Finance) out of such
monies as shall be voted by the House of Representatives in every year for these
purposes.

### 14.

Estimates
In the month of August in each year the Road Authority shall prepare and transmit to
the Minister an estimate of the proposed and probable expenditure on roads during the
ensuing financial year.

### 15.

Appropriation of expenditure
The annual estimates as aforesaid shall form part of the annual Appropriation Act.

### 16.

Supplementary expenditure
No further sum shall be expended other than such sum as may be provided in such
estimates unless the same shall have been included in a supplementary estimate of
expenditure and submitted to the Minister who shall lay such supplementary estimate
before the House of Representatives for approval.

### 17.

Disbursement to be authorised by the chairperson
The Permanent Secretary (Finance) shall disburse, from such monies voted by the
House of Representatives, for road works, such sums as he or she may be so required to
disburse from time to time by the written authority of the chairperson of the Road
Authority, in accordance with the estimates as aforesaid.

### 18.

Appropriation of revenue
There shall be paid into and appropriated to the Consolidated Fund—
(a)

all fines and penalties recovered by the Road Authority in respect of any
breach of the provisions of this Act or the regulations made thereunder;

(b)

all tolls, rates and dues levied by the Road Authority under the authority of
this Act;

(c)

any other revenue received by the Road Authority under this Act or any
other written law.

## PART IV

Road Wardens and Execution of Works

### 19.

Appointment of honorary road wardens
(1) The Minister on the recommendation of the Road Authority, may appoint so many
persons as he or she shall consider necessary to be honorary road wardens in and for the
several Road Districts.
(2) Every honorary road warden shall be subject to the direction and control of the
Road Authority.

### 20.

Interpretation of Part
In this Part, the expression “road works” means the tracing, measuring, making,
opening, altering, turning, repairing, cleaning, improving, fencing, building or excavating
of any road.

### 21.

Power of Road Authority to enter upon land
The Road Authority, and officers, employees, workmen and labourers employed by or
under it may at all reasonable times and with all necessary and proper vehicles and
animals and other means enter upon any land, and may do and perform all acts, matters
and things necessary for the purpose of any road works and for performing any act,
matter or thing under the provisions of this Act.

### 22.

Power of Road Authority to take materials
(1) The Road Authority, and officers, employees, workmen and labourers employed
by or under it may, after the giving of notice as provided in this section, at all reasonable
times and with all necessary and proper vehicles and animals and other means search for,
dig, cut, take and carry away in and from any land any water, timber, brushwood, stone,
gravel, clay or any other materials whatsoever for the purpose of any road works or for
the construction or repair of any building or conveniences whatsoever required on or near
any such road for the use of officers, employees, workmen, labourers, animals, vehicles,
persons or things employed in its service and may carry away the same through the
ground of any person without being deemed a trespasser:
Provided that—

(a)

the land from which such material may be taken, carried or conveyed shall
not be a garden, yard, park, planted walk, or avenue to a house, or any land
planted in provisions other than cocoa or spices of more than four years’
growth;

(b)

the digging or taking of material from such land shall not affect the
foundations of any house, wall or building thereon or the grounds
immediately surrounding such house, wall or building;

(c)

there shall be payable to the owner of such land if required by him or her the
sum of ten cents for each cord (of one hundred and twenty-eight cubic feet)
of stones and one-half cent for each barrel (of four cubic feet) or “tiff”
removed from such land and in addition thereto compensation for any
damage done to such land by the removal of such or other material or by the
passage thereof through such land and as shall be agreed upon between the
Road Authority and the owner of such land, and in case of differences
between the Road Authority and such owner such compensation may, on
seven days’ notice being given in writing by either party to the other, be
assessed as hereinafter provided, by the magistrate for the magisterial district
wherein such land shall be situate;

(d)

the Road Authority shall cause to be levelled or sloped all quarries, pits or
banks made by the taking of any such materials, or cause such quarries or
pits to be railed and fenced off, and such rails or fences to be maintained so
that the same shall not be dangerous to persons or cattle:

Provided that in the exercise of its powers under this paragraph the Road Authority
shall take all reasonable care to prevent the creation of any nuisance or lodgment of water
harbouring mosquitoes by borrow pits or the evacuation thereof.
(2) Whenever the Road Authority intends to take from land materials for the making
or repairing of any road, the Road Authority shall (unless such notice is waived) give to
the owner of such land twenty-four hours’ notice in writing of such intention.

### 23.

Power to throw up rubbish on adjacent lands
In the execution of any road works the Road Authority may throw up on to any lands
adjacent or near thereto, but so as not to injure any cultivation on such lands, such earth,
rubbish, or materials as it shall or may be necessary to remove from the place of any such
work.

### 24.

Responsibility of owner of private road for repairs of certain bridges and
drains
(1) For preventing damage to roads at their junction with private roads by vehicles
crossing the drains of such roads or by the running of water from private land on to roads,
the Road Authority may by notice require the owner of the land through which such
private road runs or of such private land, as the case may be, to make such provision by
the erection of wooden bridges or drain covers for the crossing of the drains of the road,
or by keeping open and clean the drains at such junction as aforesaid or otherwise as may
be specified in the notice.
(2) If after the expiration of such reasonable time as may be specified in the notice the
owner fails to comply therewith the Road Authority may execute the necessary work
specified in the notice and may recover the expenses incurred by it in doing so from the
person in default.

### 25.

Owner of canal or conduit crossing a road to repair the part of that road so
crossed

(1) If any artificial canal or conduit for the conveyance of water shall run along or
across a road, the parts of the road crossed or intersected by the canal or conduit shall be
repaired and kept in repair by the owner of the premises for the benefit of which the canal
or conduit is maintained.
(2) If any owner as aforesaid fails to comply with the provisions of the foregoing
subsection the Road Authority may by notice require him or her to execute such works as
may be necessary for the purpose, and if after the expiration of such reasonable time as
may be specified in the notice, the owner fails to comply therewith, the Road Authority
may execute any necessary work specified therein and may recover the expenses incurred
in doing so from the defaulting owner.

### 26.

Power to open drains for conveyance of water from road on adjoining land
after notice given
If at any time the Road Authority shall deem it necessary for the protection of a road
that any drain or outlet should be constructed on adjoining land for the conveyance of
water from such road on to the adjoining land, or that any existing drain or outlet on such
land should be cleaned, or that any obstruction should be removed from any such existing
drain or outlet, the Road Authority may, by notice to the owner or occupier of such
adjoining land indicating the drains or outlets thereon required to be so constructed or
cleaned or the obstruction required to be moved, require him or her to execute such works
as may be specified therein and if the owner does not within such reasonable time as may
be specified in the notice comply therewith, the Road Authority may cause the necessary
specified work to be executed and may recover the cost incurred in doing so from the
defaulting owner or occupier.

## PART V

Trees, Hedges, Shrubs, etc.

### 27.

Obstruction, etc., upon road
Wherever any road shall—
(a)

be obstructed or encroached upon by any fence, gate, tree, brushwood, ditch,
trench, enclosure or thing;

(b)

be overhung by any tree or by a branch of any tree or by any brushwood; or

(c)

be rendered unsafe for persons using the road by the presence on adjoining
land of a tree which is dry, barked, dead or dying or likely to fall,

it shall be lawful for the Road Authority, after reasonable notice to the owner or occupier
of the adjoining land, to cause such fence, gate, enclosure, tree, branch, brushwood, ditch,
trench or thing to be removed, lopped or filled in, as the case may require and may, if
they think fit, recover all expenses incurred in doing so from the person who shall have
caused such obstruction or encroachment or from the owner or tenant of any lands
wherever any such obstruction shall exist.

### 28.

Prohibiting the planting of coconut trees
(1) No coconut tree shall be planted within thirty feet from the centre of a road:
Provided that in the case of land sloping downwards from the edge of a road, the
Road Authority may, if satisfied that the safety of passengers on the road will not be at
any time thereby endangered, grant permission in writing to the owner or occupier of
such land to plant coconut trees within a shorter distance from the centre of a road.

(2) If any coconut tree is planted in contravention of this section, the Road Authority
may give notice in writing to the owner or occupier of the land on which such tree is
planted to remove the same.
(3) If the person to whom such notice is given fails to comply therewith within the
time therein specified, he or she shall be liable to a fine of fifteen dollars for every day
during which he or she continues to make default, and the Road Authority may enter
upon the land and cause the coconut tree to be removed, and may recover the expenses
incurred in doing so from the person in default.
(4) Nothing in this section shall affect or derogate from the provisions of section 27.

### 30.

Prohibiting the erection of houses, buildings or other structures near roads
(1) No house, building or other structure of whatever kind shall be erected within
eighteen feet from the centre of a road:
Provided that nothing contained in this section shall apply to any house, building or
other structure whatsoever which may be erected within the boundaries of a town.
(2) If any house, building or other structure of whatever kind is erected in
contravention of this section, the Road Authority may give fourteen days’ notice in
writing to the owner of the land on which such house or building or other structure is
erected to remove the same.
(3) If the person to whom such notice is given fails to comply therewith within the
time specified therein, he or she shall be liable to a fine not exceeding the sum of five
hundred dollars for every day during which he or she continues to make default and the
Road Authority may cause the house, building or other structure to be removed, and may
recover the expenses incurred in doing so from the person in default.

### 31.

Centre of road: how ascertained
For the purposes of this Act the centre of a road shall be ascertained without reference
to banks, ditches or side drains.

### 32.

Prohibiting the cutting of trees or cultivation within ten feet of roadside
(1) Whenever a steep slope or bank on the lower side of a road is considered by the
Road Authority to be unsafe or likely to be dangerous to persons using the road the
Authority may—
(a)

prohibit the cutting of any trees on such slope to a distance of ten feet from
the roadside;

(b)

prohibit the cultivation of any land on such slope to a distance of ten feet
from the roadside; or

(c)

enter upon any such land and plant and maintain a protective hedge of trees
on such slope to a distance of ten feet from the roadside.

(2) For the purposes of this section, the roadside shall be construed as meaning the
outside edge of the ditch or drain on the side of a road, where such ditch or drain exists
and if not, the outside edge of the road proper.

## PART VI

Nuisances, Obstructions, and other Offences

### 33.

Nuisances
A person who without lawful excuse—
(a)

destroys, pulls up, defaces, breaks or injures any milestone, milepost, lamppost, or direction post, or any bridge, causeway, arch, wall, dam, drain, or
embankment, abutment, mound, prop, post, lamp, railing, chain or fence,
belonging to any road or put up at or near any pit opened or used for getting
road materials;

(b)

removes any fence, post, stone, log, or other thing laid or erected by the
Road Authority on or in any road for the temporary prevention of the use
thereof, or for preventing danger or injury to persons passing along the same
whilst undergoing repair;

(c)

gathers or heaps up or takes away any stones, gravel, sand, or other material,
or any dirt, drift or soil from any road;

(d)

makes or causes to be made any dam, ditch, drain, or watercourse upon or
across any road or breaks up or injures the surface of any road; or

(e)

constructs a house or other building so as to project or encroach upon any
road,

shall be liable, on conviction, to a fine of one thousand dollars and in addition, to the
payment of the costs occasioned by the damage done or by the removal of the
obstruction.

### 34.

Obstructions
(1) Any person who without lawful excuse—
(a)

obstructs or causes to be obstructed the passage of water from a public road
or from any ditch or drain therefrom;

(b)

being the owner or occupier of land contiguous to a road, permits any water,
filth, or other matter or thing to flow from such land into or upon such road,
or suffers any accumulation of dirt or rubbish from off his or her land to
impede the flow of water in any ditch or drain of such road;

(c)

cuts down a tree so that it falls upon or obstructs any road;

(d)

having blocked or scotched the wheel of a vehicle, allows the stone or other
thing used for such purpose to remain on the road after such vehicle has
moved away;

(e)

leads or drives any poundable animal on a road without proper and sufficient
assistance;

(f)

burns or barks or permits or causes to be burned or barked any tree within a
distance of one hundred feet from a road;

(g)

erects a windmill so that the points thereof reach within fifty feet from the
side of the road; or

(h)

plants a tree or hedge or erects a fence within eighteen feet from the centre
of a road,

shall be liable, on conviction, to a fine of one thousand dollars and in addition, to the
payment of the cost occasioned by the damage done or by the removal of the obstruction.
(2) A person who leaves or permits to be left on a road any vehicle unless such
vehicle shall have accidentally broken down there and in case of such an accident for a
longer time than may be necessary for its removal shall be liable on conviction to a fine
of two thousand dollars for the first offence, four thousand dollars for the second offence,
and six thousand dollars for the third and any subsequent offence and to imprisonment for
twelve months, and, in addition, to the payment of the cost occasioned by the nuisance or
obstruction caused and the removal of the vehicle.
(3) A person who places or throws any stones, bricks, timber, sand, lime, dung, straw,
rubbish or scouring of any ditch or drain or other object or thing on or in a road and shall
allow the same to remain there except for such period as shall be necessary for the
removal thereof shall be liable on conviction to a fine of one thousand dollars and to
imprisonment for one month for the first offence, two thousand dollars and imprisonment
for two months for the second offence, and three thousand dollars and imprisonment for
three months for the third and any subsequent offence.

### 35.

Power to destroy swine straying on or damaging roads
All swine found straying on or damaging a road may be destroyed by any person
authorised in writing by the Road Authority; and all swine so destroyed may be buried by
the authorised person unless the carcasses are claimed by the owner of the swine within
six hours of their destruction.

### 36.

Tethering poundable animal on a road
Whoever tethers or causes to be tethered any poundable animal in, upon or near a road
so that such animal causes obstruction or inconvenience to passers-by, or permits or
causes any poundable animal, whether tethered or otherwise, to browse or feed upon the
herbage growing on a road, shall, upon the information of any person, be liable on
conviction to a fine of one hundred dollars.

### 37.

Powers given to seize and impound animals found straying on a road
(1) It shall be lawful for the Road Authority or a police officer to seize or cause to be
seized any poundable animal found tethered or at large upon a road, and to take such
animal or cause it to be taken to the nearest public pound to be impounded.
(2) A notice of such impounding shall be affixed outside the door of the nearest police
station, which notice shall give a description of the animal impounded and of the place
where it was seized, and state the reason for such seizure or impounding, and shall notify
that the animal impounded will be sold by public auction unless the owner thereof claims
the same and pays therefor the amount which shall become due under this section within
seven days after the date of such notice.

(3) Upon the owner claiming such animal he or she shall forfeit a sum not exceeding
fifteen dollars for the seizure and impounding thereof, together with the cost of the keep
of the same and the sum of five dollars for every twenty-four hour hours or fractional part
of twenty-four hours during which the animal has been impounded after the close of the
day upon which the animal was first seized.
(4) The sum specified in the preceding subsection other than the cost in respect of the
keep of the animal shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund and the Road Authority may
award to the person responsible for the impounded animal one moiety of the sum so
forfeited.
(5) If no claim be made to such animal, and if the amount of forfeits referred to in the
preceding subsections be not paid within the seven days mentioned in the notice, the
officer in charge of the police station may thereafter sell such animal by public auction
and shall pay the proceeds into the Treasury.
(6) After payment of the cost in respect of the keep of the animal and the amount of
the award as provided in this section any balance remaining may be dealt with by the
Minister either by payment into the Consolidated Fund or otherwise as the Minister may
think fit.
(7) Whoever unlawfully rescues or attempts to rescue any animal lawfully seized or
impounded shall be liable on conviction to a fine of two hundred and fifty dollars.
(8) Nothing in this section shall apply to or affect any offence created by or
proceedings taken under section 123 of the Criminal Code Act, Chapter 72A.

## PART VII

Traffic

### 38.

Closing of highways
For the purpose of carrying out any works which it may consider necessary or
desirable in connection with the maintenance, reconstruction, improvement and repair of
a road, the Road Authority may close the whole or any part of such road at any time but
shall whenever possible give reasonable notice of its intention to close such road or part
thereof:
Provided that the chairperson of the Authority may at any time by notice prohibit
temporarily the use of a road or any part of a road where, owing to the likelihood of
danger to the public, it appears necessary to him or her that such prohibition should come
into force without delay.

### 39.

Diversion of traffic
The Road Authority shall have power to regulate or stop traffic at any point where
such works are in progress in such manner as may seem best to it and shall issue warnings
and directions either by written or printed notice displayed at some conspicuous place or
by word of mouth or by erection of barriers or otherwise.

### 40.

Injury to road or bridge, etc.
If any injury to a road or bridge is proved to have been caused by the driver or person
in charge of a vehicle in contravention of this Act or any regulation or Order thereunder,
or by negligence, the Road Authority may make good such injury and recover the cost
thereof from the owner of the vehicle.

### 41.

Seizure of animals and vehicles

The Road Authority or a person authorised by the Road Authority or a police officer
may seize and detain any vehicle which he or she may find being used upon a road or
bridge in any manner contrary to the provisions of this Act or of any regulation or Order
(except one relating to speed) thereunder.

### 42.

Power to regulate traffic
(1) The Road Authority may, by Order made with the approval of the Minister—
(a)

prohibit absolutely or on certain roads or portions thereof, the use of a
vehicle of any particular description which in its opinion is likely to cause
excessive wear and tear of the road or be dangerous or inconvenient to the
public; or

(b)

restrict the speed at which motor vehicles may be driven either generally or
on any specified road or within any defined area or place.

(2) A person who uses a vehicle or causes or permits a vehicle to be used in
contravention of an Order made under this section shall be liable in the case of a first
conviction to a fine of two hundred and fifty dollars and in the case of a second or
subsequent conviction to a fine of five hundred dollars.

## PART VIII

Legal Procedure, Penalties, etc.

### 43.

Service of notice on owner
A notice issued under the provisions of this Act or regulations made thereunder to the
owner or occupier of land may be addressed to the “owner” or “occupier” of the land
(describing it) without any further name or description and in determining what is
reasonable time specified in a notice the absence of the person on whom the notice is
intended to be served shall be immaterial.

### 44.

Issue of notices
All notices issued under this Act or regulations made thereunder shall be in writing,
signed by the chairperson and shall be delivered or sent through the post to the last known
place of abode in Grenada of the person for whom it shall be intended or to his or her
agent.

### 45.

Penalties for offences, where not specified
Where an act is by a provision of this Act, or by a regulation made under the authority
thereof, directed to be done, or forbidden to be done, and such act having been duly
directed to be done shall remain undone, or such act having been forbidden to be done
shall be done, and where no penalties for and in respect thereof shall have been
prescribed, then and in every such case the person making default as to such direction and
prohibition respectively, as the case may be, shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on
conviction, to a fine of one thousand dollars, in addition to all costs and expenses incurred
by the Road Authority through his or her default.

### 46.

Certificate of Road Authority prima facie evidence of cost of repair
Where in this Act the Road Authority is authorised to recover any expenses, a
certificate under the hand of the chairperson of the Road Authority of the amount
involved shall, without proof of signature, be prima facie evidence of the amount payable
by such person.

### 47.

Recovery of penalties, etc.
All fines, penalties and forfeitures and all expenses and sums of money made payable
or recoverable and all claims for compensation or in respect of damages under this Act or
any regulation made under this Act, may be sued for, recovered and enforced summarily
before the magistrate of the magisterial district in which the matter shall occur.

## PART IX

Miscellaneous

### 48.

Application of Act to rivers, etc.
The provisions of this Act with respect to roads and the maintenance and good order
thereof shall be applicable to any river, watercourse, or canal which may, under this Act,
be directed by the Road Authority to be maintained and kept in good order for the
purpose of traffic or otherwise.

### 49.

Saving of notices and regulations in force
(1) All notices and regulations duly issued or made under any repealed Act shall as
far as applicable for the purposes of this Act, be of the same validity and effect as if they
had been made or issued under this Act.
(2) All such notices and regulations shall remain in force until revoked, altered or
varied under the provisions of this Act.

### 50.

Power to make regulations
The Road Authority may, subject to the approval of the Minister, make regulations—
(a)

declaring the width of any road or class of road;

(b)

prescribing the gross weights allowed on any particular road or class of road
and the methods for ensuring that those weights are not exceeded;

(c)

prohibiting any practice which may lead to the obstruction of roads, and for
preventing nuisances on roads;

(d)

regulating and restricting the use of heavy vehicles on roads;

(e)

imposing tolls or charges on vehicles using any specified road, bridge, or
part of road, and providing for all matters necessary for the management and
collection thereof;

(f)

providing for the control, maintenance, protection and drainage of roads;

(g)

for securing the safety of the public in the use of roads and for directing the
doing of any work or taking of any precaution to effect that purpose;

(h)

for the general regulation of the traffic on roads and for the survey, control,
use, management, construction and repair thereof and of all bridges;

(i)

for the care and protection of any bridges, aqueducts, retaining or
embankment walls or embankment fences on or used in connection with any
road, and to prohibit the removal of gravel, boulders, earth or any other
material from the vicinity of such bridges, aqueducts, retaining or
embankment walls or embankment fences;

(j)

for directing any river, watercourse, or canal or any part thereof to be
maintained in good order for the purpose of protecting any bridges,
aqueducts, retaining or embankment walls or embankment fences;

(k)

prescribing the maximum dimensions of vehicles of any kind or of any parts
of them or of the loading of them;

(l)

generally for restricting or regulating the use of vehicles in such a manner as
circumstances may appear to it to require; and

(m)

otherwise for the purpose of more effectively carrying this Act into effect.

### 51.

Minister may, by Order, declare the creation or cesser of a public road
It shall be lawful for the Minister with the approval of the House of Representatives
signified by a resolution to that effect, to declare by Order—
(a)

that any road or way not included in the Schedules shall thenceforth be a
road within the meaning of this Act; or

(b)

that any road or way over which all members of the public are entitled to
pass and repass shall cease to be a road and thereupon the right of members
of the public to pass and repass thereon shall be extinguished.

ROADS REGULATIONS
[Section 50.]

[31st December, 1932.]
Citation

---

### 1.

Citation
These Regulations may be cited as the Roads Regulations.
Conduct of Meetings

### 2.

Time and place of meeting
Meetings of the Road Authority shall be held at such time and place as the chairperson
may direct and deem necessary, provided that meetings may be convened by the
chairperson on a written request signed by at least four members stating the reasons
therefor.

### 3.

Convening of meeting
Meetings shall be convened by written or printed summonses signed by the Secretary
which shall whenever practicable give at least seven days’ notice and provide an agenda
of the business to be dealt with.

### 4.

Failure to attend
Any member who fails to attend three consecutive meetings of the Road Authority,
without being excused therefrom, shall be deemed to have vacated his or her seat and the
chairperson shall inform the District Board concerned or the Government accordingly.

### 5.

Duties of chairperson
The chairperson of the Road Authority shall preserve order at its meetings and decide
on all disputed points of order.

### 6.

Questions decided by vote
All questions brought up for discussion at meetings of the Road Authority shall be
decided by vote.

### 7.

Secretary to keep minutes
The Secretary shall keep full minutes of the proceedings of all meetings of the Road
Authority and at each meeting the minutes of the last preceding meeting shall if not
previously circulated be read and confirmed before proceeding with any other business.
Duties

### 8.

Power and duties of officers and servants
The powers and duties of the officers and servants of the Road Authority shall be such
as are defined in the Act and the rules and regulations made thereunder.
Establishment and Duties of Road Engineer

### 9.

Road Engineer
There shall be a Road Engineer who shall control the entire road system under the
direction of the Chief Technical Officer (Works).

### 10.

Duties

He or she shall regularly inspect the roads of the State paying at least one visit per
quarter to the Northern District, Carriacou; and consult with the District Road Officers,
and advise them on all questions of difficulty, and whenever there is any difference of
opinion it shall be referred to the Chief Technical Officer (Works) whose decision shall
be final.

### 11.

Quarterly reports of work
He or she shall submit quarterly reports giving full and detailed information showing
the cost of the work done, whether ordinary or special, and a résumé of the year’s work in
January of each year.

### 12.

Checking of expenditure
He or she shall regularly check expenditure on the roads and see that no allocations
are exceeded.

### 13.

Rules and regulations
He or she shall satisfy himself or herself that the rules and regulations are being
properly carried out by the District Officers and employees under him or her.

### 14.

Reports to Chief Technical Officer
He or she shall submit reports on any special problems or difficulties and shall keep
the Chief Technical Officer (Works) informed on all matters affecting the roads.

### 15.

Claims for compensation
He or she shall not purchase or take possession of lands or otherwise involve the Road
Authority in claims for compensation without the written permission of the Chief
Technical Officer (Works).

### 16.

Contracts
He or she shall not enter into contracts of higher value than fifty dollars without the
prior approval of the Chief Technical Officer (Works).

### 17.

Pay sheets
He or she shall see that all pay sheets duly checked and initialled by him or her are
submitted for signature by the Chief Technical Officer (Works) or such other officer
deputed by him or her not later than the Wednesday in each pay week.
Duties of District Road Officers

### 18.

Duties
A District Road Officer shall have charge of the entire district under the direction of
the Road Engineer and carry out all instructions connected with the roads from that
officer or the Chief Technical Officer (Works).

### 19.

Inspection of roads
He or she shall regularly inspect the roads in his or her district and visit all gangs and
workmen at least three times a week.

### 20.

Weekly reports
He or she shall forward weekly reports of inspection and other work done giving full
detailed information with the cost through the Road Engineer to the Chief Technical
Officer (Works).

### 21.

Regular maintenance
He or she shall satisfy himself or herself that the roads are being regularly maintained
and kept in good order, and that the law and regulations are being duly observed, and that
Honorary Road Wardens are disbursing public funds entrusted to them satisfactorily and
with a view to the best results.

### 22.

Honorary Road Wardens
He or she shall avail himself or herself of every opportunity to confer with Honorary
Road Wardens and offer them all assistance in his or her power.

### 23.

Removal of obstructions
He or she shall give immediate instructions for removal of obstructions of any kind
and abatement of nuisances.

### 24.

Time books
He or she shall see that all time books are written up at the beginning of the day’s
work, and check and initial such books on every visit.

### 25.

Conference with Road Engineer
He or she shall avail himself or herself of every opportunity to confer with the Road
Engineer on all special points of doubt or difficulty.

### 26.

Roads ledger
He or she shall keep a roads’ ledger showing details of expenditure on each road and
shall satisfy himself or herself by frequent comparisons with the Treasury Department
that individual grants or allocations are not exceeded.

### 27.

Tools ledger
He or she shall keep a tools’ ledger in which shall be entered on separate pages a list
of all tools and implements supplied to each foreman or cantonnier, also the date of
condemnation or other disposal of the same.

### 28.

Certificate of tool inspection
He or she shall check all tools in use in the district at least twice yearly and forward a
certificate of such inspection through the Road Engineer to the Chief Technical Officer
(Works) not later than January and July in each year.

### 29.

Paysheets and claims
He or she shall submit not later than Tuesday in each pay week through the Road
Engineer to the Chief Technical Officer (Works) all paysheets and claims forwarded to
him or her by each Honorary Road Warden, Foreman, or other person after he or she has
checked, entered and initialled the same.

### 30.

Discharge of duties of Honorable Road Warden
He or she shall in the temporary or other unavoidable absence of any Honorary Road
Warden in his or her district discharge the duties devolving on such Honorary Road
Warden.

### 31.

Control of stock
He or she shall keep in stock a sufficient supply of road tools, lanterns, trenching lines,
and other articles for use in case of emergency and see that a proper and continuous
supply of materials for use by foremen and cantonniers is maintained.

### 32.

Claim for compensation
He or she shall not purchase or take possession of any land or otherwise involve the
Road Authority in any claim for compensation without the written permission of the
Chief Technical Officer (Works).

### 33.

Inspection of machinery
He or she shall regularly inspect all road rollers and other machinery in his or her
district, and check the time of the driver and watchman, and the amount of material used.
Duties of Road Wardens

### 34.

Remuneration
A road warden shall receive no remuneration of any kind.

### 35.

Reconstruction and maintenance work
He or she shall carry out all necessary work of reconstruction or maintenance as may
be directed by the District Officer with due regard to the regulations dealing with such
work.

### 36.

Claims for compensation
He or she shall not purchase or take possession of land or otherwise involve the Road
Authority in claims for compensation without the written permission of the Chief
Technical Officer (Works).

### 37.

Maintenance of roads
He or she shall maintain the roads under his or her charge generally to the satisfaction
of the District Road Officer having regard to the regulations dealing with details of work.

### 38.

Time books
He or she shall see that all time books are written up at the beginning of the day’s
work and check and initial the same on every visit.
Appointment of Foremen

### 39.

Appointment of foreman
A foreman may be appointed by a District Officer or by an Honorary Road Warden
and shall perform his or her various duties strictly in accordance with instructions passed
to him or her.

### 40.

Allotment
The mileage of road allotted to each foreman with his or her gang may vary in
accordance with the importance of the roads and his or her capacity for work.

### 41.

Rate of pay
The rate of pay for a foreman may vary according to the length of his or her service
and his or her capacity for work. The rate shall be fixed by the Road Engineer and shall
not be changed without his or her approval.
Appointment of Cantonniers

### 42.

Appointment of cantonniers
Workmen shall be appointed for maintaining sections or cantons of the roads and shall
be known as cantonniers.

### 43.

Duties
Cantonniers shall be appointed by the Road Engineer and shall not be transferred from
one section or canton to another or dismissed without his or her approval. The main duty
of a cantonnier shall be to attend to the upkeep of the roads allotted to him or her and to
maintain the same in proper order and repair.

### 44.

Upkeep of roads
They shall at regular intervals cutlass the road verges and side drains and banks, clean
the side drains, clear culverts, clean existing scupper drains and provide others if
necessary, clear small landslides, patch surface pot holes, sweep oiled or bituminous
surfaces and repair depressions and holes within their sections or cantons.

### 45.

Maintenance of canton
Generally they shall pay constant attention to their cantons and to all that may be
necessary to maintain a good and even surface and see that no water is allowed to remain
on the road surface through lack of drainage.

### 46.

Metal and gravel depots
They shall keep metal and gravel depots in proper condition.

### 47.

Mileage of roadway
The mileage of roadway allotted to each cantonnier may vary in accordance with the
importance of the roads in the canton.

### 48.

Working hours
The ordinary working hours of a cantonnier shall be the same as other workmen, but
he or she shall be liable to be called upon to perform emergency work at any time.

### 49.

Rate of pay
The rate of pay for a cantonnier may vary according to the length of his or her service
and his or her capacity for work. The rate of pay shall be fixed by the Road Engineer and
shall not be changed without his or her approval.

### 50.

Supply of tools
Every cantonnier shall supply himself or herself with a cutlass, and shall be held
responsible for the safe custody of all tools and implements supplied by the Road
Authority.

### 51.

Absence to be reported to District Officer
Every cantonnier shall report to the District Officer whenever he or she is likely to be
absent for any special reason and in the case of illness he or she shall notify the District
Road Officer as soon as possible.

Obstruction of Roads

### 54.

Removal of obstruction
(1) No house or other structure or any section thereof exceeding six feet in width shall
be removed or transported over or along any road except under the following conditions,
that is to say—
(a)

any person causing any such removal or transport shall give to the officer in
charge of the nearest police station at least 24 hours’ previous notice of his
or her intention to cause such removal; and when giving such notice he or
she shall state the day and hour upon which such removal is to be
commenced, the places from and to which, and the roads along which, such
removal is to be made, and any such other particulars as the Police may
require to know concerning the mode of transport or the like;

(b)

no such house, structure or section as aforesaid shall be removed or
transported along a public road except in the daytime, that is, between
sunrise and sunset; and if such removal or transport be not completed in one
day, the house or structure shall be moved from off the road until the day
when the removal or transport is to be resumed.

(2) Any person who shall contravene the provisions of this Order shall be liable on
conviction to a penalty not exceeding one hundred dollars.

### 55.

Unnecessary obstruction
Nothing herein contained shall be deemed to excuse any person who in the course of
any such removal or transport wilfully causes unnecessary obstruction on a public way or
who damages any road or telephone or other property.
Application of Motor Vehicles and Road Traffic Regulations

### 56.

Application of Motor Vehicles and Road Traffic Regulations

## Part III of the Motor Vehicles and Road Traffic Regulations, Chapter 201, shall apply

Protection of Bridge Walls, etc.

### 57.

Protection of structures
For the protection of bridge retaining walls, culverts, fences, etc., riparian owners may
be required to see that no gravel, boulders or other materials are removed within a
distance of 50 feet where the removal of such material will imperil the foundations, and
that all watercourses are kept clear of tree trunks, branches and other obstructions, and
maintained in good order.

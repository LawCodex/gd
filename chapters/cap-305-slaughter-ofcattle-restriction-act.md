## CHAPTER 305

SLAUGHTER OF CATTLE (RESTRICTION) ACT
An Act to restrict the slaughtering of cattle.
[Amended by Act No. 19 of 1960.]

[7th May, 1921.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Slaughter of Cattle (Restriction) Act.
2\. Heifers under two years of age not to be slaughtered
(1) Every person who slaughters or causes to be slaughtered any heifer under the age
of two years shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of
one thousand five hundred dollars.
(2) Subsection (1) does not apply with respect to any animal slaughtered in obedience
to lawful authority or from a humanitarian motive such as the prevention of suffering or
for any other sufficient cause.
3\. Minister may, by Order, prohibit slaughter of cattle
(1) The Minister may, by Order, prohibit the slaughter of all or any specified breed or
species of cattle and impose such conditions, if any, on such slaughter as he or she deems
necessary.
(2) An Order made under subsection (1) may contain provision for the imposition of a
penalty of up to five hundred dollars on any person summarily convicted of a
contravention thereof.

### 1.

Citation
This Order may be cited as the Slaughter of Cattle Order.

### 2.

Definitions
In this Order—
“cattle” means any cow, sheep or goat; and
“slaughter” means to kill for food.

### 3.

Prohibition
(1) Except by the written permission of an Agricultural Instructor, no person shall
slaughter any female cattle.
(2) An Agricultural Instructor shall only grant permission as aforesaid with respect to
female cattle which, in his or her opinion, are—
(a)

barren;

(b)

unfit for breeding; or

(c)

too old to be bred.

### 4.

Offence
Any person who slaughters any female cattle without the written permission of an
Agricultural Instructor shall be guilty of an offence punishable on summary conviction,
and shall be liable to a fine not exceeding four hundred dollars, or in default of payment,
to a term of imprisonment not exceeding three months hard labour.

## CHAPTER 213

NOXIOUS WEEDS ACT
An Act to make provision for the eradication of noxious weeds.
[1st April, 1912.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Noxious Weeds Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“inspector” shall mean an inspector appointed under this Act;
“noxious weeds” shall mean any plant which the Minister may, from time to time,
with the consent of the House of Representatives, signified by a resolution to that
effect, declare by Order published in the Gazette to be a noxious weed, either
throughout the whole of Grenada or in one or more districts or portions of districts
thereof.

### 3.

Appointment of inspectors
The Minister may, from time to time, appoint inspectors for the purposes of this Act,
and may assign to them their duties and salaries or allowances.

### 4.

Power to make regulations
(1) The Minister may, from time to time, with the consent of the House of
Representatives, make, alter, or rescind regulations—
(a)

imposing upon the occupier of land, or in the case of unoccupied land, upon
the owner thereof or his or her agent in Grenada, the duty of clearing and
keeping cleared that land of noxious weeds;

(b)

prescribing the manner in which any noxious weed shall be eradicated by
persons subject to the duty of clearing and keeping land clear of it;

(c)

empowering an inspector to enter upon land and inspect the same and to give
directions by written notice or otherwise to persons subject to the said duty
to clear that land of any noxious weed;

(d)

prescribing the forms of any such notice, the particulars of directions to be
given therein, and the manner in which it shall be served upon occupiers,
owners, or agents of such owners;

(e)

empowering inspectors to eradicate noxious weeds in default of compliance
with the requirements of any such notice by a person upon whom the same
has been served;

(f)

providing for the recovery of the cost of eradication of noxious weeds from
any such person who is so in default and the mode of such recovery;

(g)

preventing the introduction into Grenada or the sale of any plant, seed, or
grain, which is likely to propagate or spread the growth of noxious weeds;

(h)

generally for preventing the spread of noxious weeds in Grenada or any
district or portion of a district thereof.

(2) Until any such regulations shall be made the regulations contained in the subsidiary
legislation shall be in force.

### 5.

Offences
A person who—
(a)

contravenes the provisions of any regulation made under this Act;

(b)

refuses or neglects to act in obedience to any such regulations; or

(c)

resists, opposes or obstructs the lawful execution thereof,

shall be guilty of an offence and for every such offence shall be liable, on summary
conviction, to a fine of two hundred and fifty dollars.

NOXIOUS WEEDS (LOVE-VINE) ORDER
[Section 4.]

[1st April, 1912.]

---

### 1.

Citation
This Order may be cited as the Noxious Weeds (Love-Vine) Order.

### 2.

Noxious weed
The plant known as Love-Vine (Cuscuta) shall be deemed to be a noxious weed within
the meaning of the Act throughout the whole State.

Noxious Weeds Regulations
[Section 4(2).]

1\. In these Regulations, the following terms shall have the meanings respectively
assigned to them—
“clear” shall mean to dig up an burn, or to pull up and burn, noxious weeds, or to
employ other means of destruction prescribed by an inspector;
“person responsible” shall, in relation to land, mean the occupier of land or, in the
case of unoccupied land, the owner thereof or his or her agent in Grenada.
2\. It shall be the duty of every person responsible to clear or cause to be cleared any
noxious weed on the land in respect of which he or she is responsible. It shall further be
the duty of the person responsible to report the occurrence on any land of any noxious
weed to a Justice of the Peace, magistrate or inspector, or at the nearest police station, or
direct to the Chief Technical Officer (Extension).

### 3.

An inspector may at all reasonable times enter upon any land, whether enclosed or
not, for the purpose of ascertaining if any noxious weeds exist thereon.

### 4.

If an inspector finds any noxious weed upon land, he or she may by notice in
writing to the person responsible require him or her to clear the land within the time
specified in the notice, and it shall be the duty of the person responsible to do so. Such
notice shall indicate the particular noxious weed which has been found upon the land,
and, as nearly as practicable, the portion or portions of the land on which that noxious
weed has been found.

### 5.

Any person responsible who fails, after notice, to clear his or her land of a noxious
weed specified in that notice shall be liable on summary conviction to the penalty
provided by section 5 of the Act. The court may also order the person so convicted to pay
the cost of the clearing of the land by an inspector, and every such order may be enforced
in the same manner as the judgement of a magistrate’s court is enforceable in civil cases.

### 6.

If the person responsible fails to clear the land as aforesaid, an inspector may enter
upon the land with or without assistants and eradicate any noxious weed found thereon,
but nothing herein contained shall relieve the person responsible from any penalty to
which he or she may be liable under these Regulations:
Provided that if no such penalty be imposed the inspector may recover the costs,
charges, and expense of clearing the land as aforesaid by action in the proper court.

### 7.

A notice under these Regulations shall be in writing and may be served—
(a)

personally upon the person responsible; or

(b)

by leaving it addressed to the person responsible at his or her usual or last
known place of abode; or

(c)

by post in a registered letter addressed to the person responsible at his or her
last known place of abode or business.

### 8.

A person who obstructs or hinders an inspector in the exercise of his or her duty
under these Regulations shall be liable, on summary conviction, to the penalty provided
by section 5 of the Act.

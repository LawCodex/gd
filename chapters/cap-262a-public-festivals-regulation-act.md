## CHAPTER 262A

PUBLIC FESTIVALS REGULATION ACT
An Act to better regulate public conduct at public festivals.
[Act No. 12 of 1994 amended by Act No. 25 of 2008, SRO 19 of 2011, Act No. 10 of 2011.]

[24th June, 1994.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Public Festivals Regulation Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“festival” means a “public festival”;

“Management Committee” means the Management Committee of a public festival
appointed under section 7;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for culture;
“public festival” means the public festivals designated as National Festivals or
Grand Festivals respectively by sections 3 and 4 or thereunder.
3\. National Festivals
The public festivals listed in the First Schedule to this Act are National Festivals.
4\. Grand Festivals
The public festivals listed in the Second Schedule to this Act are Grand Festivals.
5\. Minister may make regulations
(1) The Minister may make regulations in respect of a public festival regulating—
(a)

the selling of goods at the festival;

(b)

the providing of services at the festival;

(c)

the fees to be paid and to whom by those selling goods, or providing services
at the festival, by those parking motor vehicles at the festival, or by those
entering the festival.

(2) Regulations made by the Minister under subsection (1) may authorise the
Management Committee of a festival to administer those regulations.
(3) Regulations made by the Minister under subsection (1) shall be subject to negative
resolution.
6\. Minister may amend Schedules
The Minister may by notice published in the Gazette amend the Schedules to this Act
by adding public festivals thereto or deleting public festivals therefrom.
7\. Festivals to have Management Committees
(1) The Minister shall ensure that every public festival shall be under the control of a
Management Committee. The Minister shall be responsible for the appointment of every
such Management Committee, constituting it as he or she sees fit, subject to section 8, or
unless a particular Act makes special provision to the contrary.
(2) The Minister in appointing a Management Committee under subsection (1) shall
as far as possible name and maintain as the Management Committee of a festival the
persons who have been organising that festival at the time at which he or she is
appointing the Management Committee of that festival.
(3) A Management Committee shall have a formal constitution or other body of rules
governing its composition, membership, structure and functioning and shall be registered
with a Ministry of Government throughout its lifetime as such.
8\. Existing organising bodies to be named and maintained as Management
Committees
(1) Where at the commencement of this Act there is an existing parish organisation or
existing committee or other existing body that has been organising or running a Grand
Festival, the Minister shall as far as possible name and maintain that existing organisation

or committee or other body as the Management Committee of the respective Grand
Festival.
(2) The term existing parish organisation or existing committee or other existing body
referred to in subsection (1) means, in relation to the respective Grand Festivals—
(a)

the Carriacou Carnival Development Committee, the Carriacou Regatta
Committee; the Mt. Royal Progressive Youth Movement/Carriacou Parang
Committee, the St. Andrew’s Development Organisation, the St. Mark’s
Development Committee, the St. Patrick’s Organisation for Development;

(b)

any other body designated by the Minister by notice in the Gazette as an
existing parish organisation or existing committee or other existing body:

Provided that any such organisation, committee or other body referred to in
paragraphs (a) and (b) of this subsection has a formal constitution or other body of rules
governing its composition, membership, structure and functioning and is registered with a
Ministry of Government at the time when it is being named as the respective
Management Committee and throughout its lifetime thereafter as such.

### 9.

Directions under instructions to persons attending festivals and persons selling
goods or providing services at festivals
(1) The Minister may in writing in respect of a public festival authorise the respective
Management Committee to issue instructions to any Member of the Royal Grenada Police
Force or to any designated person as defined by this section to give directions—
(a)

to any person attending the festival or selling goods or providing services at
the festival to lower or turn down the volume of any music, sounds or other
noise provided by such person at the festival and to keep it at such lowered
volume as may be stipulated by such Member of the Police Force or
designated person;

(b)

to persons selling goods or providing services at such festival regarding—
(i) the location at such festival at which goods may be sold or services
rendered,
(ii) the distance between persons selling goods or providing services at the
festival,
(iii) the conditions under which goods may be sold or services rendered at
such festival not including the fees to be paid by persons selling goods
or rendering services.

(2) When the Minister gives an authorisation under subsection (1), he or she shall
cause it to be published in the Gazette, or in a newspaper circulating regularly within the
State.
(3) The music, sounds or other noise referred to in subsection (1) includes music,
sounds or other noise emitted from a radio, tape, tape-deck, compact-disc (CD), recordplayer or other such equipment.
(4) A “designated person” referred to in subsection (1) is any person designated in
writing by the Management Committee of a festival to give directions under this section.

### 10.

Instructions may be oral and may be varied during festival
(1) Instructions issued by a Management Committee under section 9 may be oral, and
may be varied by the Committee or a delegate of the Committee during the respective
festival as the Committee or its delegate sees fit; but any instruction given by a
Committee or its delegate may at any time be annulled by the Minister in writing.

(2) The Management Committee of a festival may delegate to any of its members the
authority to vary during the festival such instructions as might have been given by the
Committee under section 9. Instructions issued by such delegate shall be deemed to be
instructions issued by the Committee.

### 11.

Site or sites of festivals
(1) Regulations made by the Minister under section 5, or instructions issued or
directions given thereunder under section 9, shall apply to the actual site or sites of a
public festival and to such extended protected perimeter as may be specified under
subsection (2).
(2) The Minister shall demarcate the place or places which shall constitute the actual
site or sites of a public festival and which as such shall be under the jurisdiction of the
respective Management Committee during the festival to the extent provided for by this
Act. The Minister shall publish such site or sites in the Gazette or in a newspaper
circulating regularly in the State at least two weeks in advance of the festival. The
Minister may also provide and similarly publish that the site or sites of a festival shall be
deemed to include such extended protected perimeter not exceeding 150 yards beyond the
actual site or sites of the festival as may be designated by him or her, and any regulations,
instructions or directions referred to in subsection (1) shall apply to such extended
protected perimeter but only in relation to the controlling of music, sounds or other noise
referred to in section 9.

### 12.

Penalty for violating directions given under section 8
(1) Any person attending a festival or selling goods or providing services at a festival
who fails to comply with any direction given him or her under instructions issued under
section 9 shall be guilty of an offence and shall on summary conviction be liable to a fine
not exceeding one thousand dollars.
(2) Unless otherwise specifically provided in this Act, any person contravening any
provision of this Act or regulations made hereunder or any directions or instructions
issued under this Act shall be guilty of an offence and shall on summary conviction be
liable to a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not
exceeding twelve months, or to both such fine and imprisonment.

### 13.

No general restriction on use of private real property
Nothing in this Act shall be construed as restricting or authorising the restricting of a
person in the use and enjoyment of private real property owned, occupied or used by him
or her; nor shall anything in this Act be construed as requiring or authorising the requiring
of a person to pay a fee for the use and enjoyment of private real property owned,
occupied or used by him or her; but the provisions of this Act authorising the controlling
of music, sounds or other noise shall apply to persons owning, occupying or using private
real property situate within the actual site or sites of a public festival or within any
extended protected perimeter referred to in section 11.

### 14.

Duty on Minister to consult
The Minister shall consult with the Management Committee appearing to him or her
to be appropriate to be consulted in exercising his or her powers, functions and duties
under this Act generally, and especially those under sections 5(1), 6 and 11(2).

CARRIACOU REGATTA REGULATIONS
[SRO 24 OF 1994.]

[13th July, 1994.]

---

### 1.

Citation
These Regulations may be cited as the Carriacou Regatta Regulations, 1994.

### 2.

Definitions
In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—
“Act” means the Public Festivals Regulation Act, 1994, Chapter 262A;
“alcoholic beverages” means “intoxicating liquors” as this latter term is used in the
Liquor Dealers’ Licences Act, Chapter 174;
“Carriacou Regatta” is the public festival known as a Grand Festival under the Act
held at Hillsborough, Carriacou, during and around the time referred to in the Bank
Holidays Act, Chapter 25, as Emancipation Holiday;
“Committee” means the Carriacou Regatta Committee, the Management
Committee having control of the Carriacou Regatta referred to in section 8 of the Act;
“designated person” means a person designated by the Committee in writing to
give directions under the Act and these Regulations;
“drinks” includes alcoholic beverages and non-alcoholic beverages;
“Festival” means the Carriacou Regatta;
“goods” includes drinks and prepared food;
“non-alcoholic beverages” means drinks other than alcoholic beverages;
“prepared food” means food prepared for human consumption by a process of
boiling, baking, barbecuing, roasting, frying, stewing or any other form of cooking;
“police officer” means any member of the Royal Grenada Police Force established
under the Police Act, Chapter 244;
“Regatta” means Carriacou Regatta;
“services” includes music as defined by regulation 11(2).

### 3.

Requirements for selling prepared food

(1) It shall be an offence for any person to sell prepared food at the Regatta without
procuring a certificate from the Ministry of Health certifying that such person is in a state
of health fit for selling prepared food.
(2) Any person who sells prepared food at the Regatta may at any time during and at
the Regatta be required by any police officer or any designated person or any delegate of
the Committee to produce to such police officer or designated person or delegate the
certificate referred to in subregulation (1). Failure by a relevant person to produce such
certificate when so required to do so shall be an offence.

### 4.

Requirements for selling drinks
(1) Any person who sells alcoholic beverages at the Regatta may at any time during
and at the Regatta be required by any police officer or any designated person or any
delegate of the Committee to produce to such police officer or designated person or
delegate the licence issued to such person under the Liquor Dealers’ Licences Act,

## Chapter 174, authorising such person to sell alcoholic beverages at the Regatta. Any

person who fails to produce such licence when so required to do so may at the discretion
of the Committee or a delegate of the Committee be prevented from selling non-alcoholic
beverages at the Regatta, without prejudice to proceedings being taken against him or her
under the Liquor Dealers’ Licences Act, Chapter 174.
(2) Any person who sells or delivers or supplies, or who knowingly permits to be sold
or delivered or supplied, alcoholic beverage to any person under the age of sixteen years
at the Festival shall be guilty of an offence.

### 5.

Alcoholic beverages not to be sold outside specified hours
It shall be an offence for a person on any day of the Regatta to sell alcoholic beverages
outside such hours as may be specified by the Committee.

### 6.

Awarding space and booths
(1) Space, stalls or booths to be rented by the Committee for the sale of goods or the
providing of services at the Regatta will be awarded by the Committee on such basis as
the Committee sees fit. The fees payable for space or booths so rented, set out in
regulation 7, shall be payable to the Committee in advance.
(2) It shall be an offence for a person to sell goods or provide services at the Regatta
without first procuring from the Committee a written allotment to space or a booth at the
Regatta to sell such goods or provide such services and without paying in advance the
fees payable for renting such space or booth set out in regulation 7. In addition to any
other penalty prescribed by the Act or these Regulations, any person who violates this
subregulation may be required by the Committee or any police officer or any delegate of
the Committee or any designated person to leave the Regatta, and any such requirement
may be enforced by any police officer.
(3) Any person who sells goods or provides services at the Regatta may be required
by the Committee or any police officer or any delegate of the Committee or any
designated person to produce to the Committee or such police officer or delegate or
designated person the written allotment referred to in subregulation (2) and the receipt
evidencing payment in advance of the fees referred to in subregulation (2). Any person
who fails to produce such written allotment and such receipt when so required to do so
may at the discretion of the Committee or a delegate of the Committee be prevented from
selling goods or providing services at the Regatta.

### 7.

Fees
(1) Persons who sell goods or provide services at open bar spaces, stalls or booths at
the Regatta shall, for the duration of the Regatta, pay to the Committee—

(i) for the sale of food and drinks, one hundred dollars,
(ii) for the sale of food, one hundred dollars,
(iii) for the sale of drinks, one hundred dollars,
(iv) for the sale of barbecue food, fifty dollars.
(2) A refundable cleaning deposit of one hundred dollars shall be payable on the
rental of all bar space and booths except barbecue bars. All rented bar space and booths
must be cleaned by the renter by midnight following the closing of the Regatta or else the
cleaning deposit will be liable to forfeiture at the discretion of the Committee. The
cleaning deposit shall be refunded if the public health department of the Ministry of
Health certifies that the bar space or booth has been satisfactorily cleaned by the renter.

### 8.

Enforcing prevention
Without prejudice to any provision in the Act or these Regulations, when the
Committee or any delegate of the Committee is empowered by these Regulations to
prevent a person from doing anything at the Regatta, and such prevention is ordered, such
prevention may be enforced by any police officer.

### 9.

Committee may administer
The Committee may administer regulations 3 to 8.

### 10.

Sites of the Regatta
(1) The actual sites of the Regatta, and which as such shall be under the jurisdiction of
the Committee during the Regatta, shall be those public places in Hillsborough,
Carriacou, comprising Main Street from its junction with Brunswick Street to Middle
Street, Middle Street from its junction with Main Street and 1st Avenue, Paterson Street
from its junction with Main Street and 2nd Avenue, along 1st Avenue from its junction
with Middle Street to its junction with Church Street, Church Street from its junction with
Main Street and 1st Avenue, The Waterfront from Silver Beach to Mermaid Tavern, all
areas inclusive.
(2) The sites of the Regatta shall also be deemed to include those public places in an
extended protected perimeter of one hundred yards beyond the actual sites in respect of
all the actual sites.

### 11.

Committee may give instructions for directions
(1) The Committee may issue instructions to any police officer or to any designated
person to give directions—
(a)

to any person attending the Regatta or selling goods or providing services at
the Regatta to lower or turn down the volume of any music, sounds or other
noise provided by such person at the Regatta and to keep it at such lowered
volume as may be stipulated by such police officer or designated person;

(b)

to persons selling goods or providing services at the Regatta regarding—
(i) the location at the Regatta at which goods may be sold or services
rendered,
(ii) the distance between persons selling goods or providing services at the
Regatta,
(iii) the conditions under which goods may be sold or services rendered at
the Regatta not including the fees to be paid by persons selling goods
or rendering services.

(2) The music, sounds or other noise referred to in subsection (1) includes music,
sounds or other noise emitted from a radio, tape, tape-deck, compact-disc (CD), recordplayer or other such equipment.

### 12.

Penalties
Unless otherwise specifically provided in these Regulations, any person contravening
any provision of these Regulations or any instructions or directions issued hereunder shall
be guilty of an offence and shall on summary conviction be liable to the penalties
provided for by section 12(2) of the Act.

### 13.

Commencement
These Regulations shall be deemed to have come into operation as from 13th July,
1994\.

RAINBOW CITY FESTIVAL REGULATIONS
[SRO 25 of 1994.]

[13th July, 1994.]
1\. Citation
These Regulations may be cited as the Rainbow City Festival Regulations.
2\. Definitions
In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires—
“Act” means the Public Festivals Regulation Act, Chapter 262A;
“alcoholic beverages” means “intoxicating liquors” as this latter term is used in the
Liquor Dealers’ Licences Act, Chapter 174;

“Committee” means organisation, the Management Committee having control of
the Rainbow City Festival referred to in section 8 of the Act;
“designated person” means a person designated by the Committee in writing to
give directions under the Act and these Regulations;
“drinks” includes alcoholic beverages and non-alcoholic beverages;
“Festival” means the Rainbow City Festival;
“goods” includes drinks and prepared food;
“non-alcoholic beverages” means drinks other than alcoholic beverages;
“prepared food” means food prepared for human consumption by a process of
boiling, baking, barbecuing, roasting, frying, stewing or any other form of cooking;
“police officer” means any member of the Royal Grenada Police Force established
under the Police Act, Chapter 244;
“Rainbow City Festival” is the public festival known as a Grand Festival under the
Act held at Grenville, St. Andrew’s, during and around the time referred to in the
Bank Holidays Act, Chapter 25, as Emancipation Holiday;
“services” includes music as defined by regulation 11(2).
3\. Requirements for selling prepared food
(1) It shall be an offence for any person to sell prepared food at the Festival without
procuring a certificate from the Ministry of Health certifying that such person is in a state
of health fit for selling prepared food.
(2) Any person who sells prepared food at the Festival may at any time during and at
the Festival be required by any police officer or any designated person or any delegate of
the Committee to produce to such police officer or designated person or delegate the
certificate referred to in subregulation (1). Failure by a relevant person to produce such
certificate when so required to do so shall be an offence.
4\. Requirements for selling drinks
(1) Any person who sells alcoholic beverages at the Festival may at any time during
and at the Festival be required by any police officer or any designated person or any
delegate of the Committee to produce to such police officer or designated person or.
delegate the licence issued to such person under the Liquor Dealers’ Licences Act,

## Chapter 174, authorising such person to sell alcoholic beverages at the Festival. Any

person who fails to produce such licence when so required to do so may at the discretion
of the Committee or a delegate of the Committee be prevented from selling non-alcoholic
beverages at the Festival, without prejudice to proceedings being taken against him or her
under the Liquor Dealers’ Licences Act, Chapter 174.
(2) Any person who sells or delivers or supplies, or who knowingly permits to be sold
or delivered or supplied, alcoholic beverage to any person under the age of sixteen years
at the Festival shall be guilty of an offence.
5\. Alcoholic beverages not to be sold after midnight
It shall be an offence for a person to sell alcoholic beverages after midnight on any
day of the Festival.
6\. Awarding space and booths
(1) Space or booths to be rented by the Committee for the sale of goods or the
providing of services at the Festival will be awarded by the Committee in writing on a

first-come first-serve basis. The fees payable for space or booths so rented, set out in
regulation 7, shall be payable to the Committee in advance.
(2) It shall be an offence for a person to sell goods or provide services at the Festival
without first procuring from the Committee a written allotment to space or a booth at the
Festival to sell such goods or provide such services and without paying in advance the
fees payable for renting such space or booth set out in regulation 7. In addition to any
other penalty prescribed by the Act or these Regulations, any person who violates this
subregulation may be required by the Committee or any police officer or any delegate of
the Committee or any designated person to leave the Festival, and any such requirement
may be enforced by any police officer.
(3) Any person who sells goods or provides services at the Festival may be required
by the Committee or any police officer or any delegate of the Committee or any
designated person to produce to the Committee or such police officer or delegate or
designated person the written allotment referred to in subregulation (2) and the receipt
evidencing payment in advance of the fees referred to in subregulation (2). Any person
who fails to produce such written allotment and such receipt when so required to do so
may at the discretion of the Committee or a delegate of the Committee be prevented from
selling goods or providing services at the Festival.
7\. Fees
(1) Persons who sell goods or provide services at open bar spaces at the Festival shall,
on a three day basis, pay to the Committee in respect of—
(i) bar space with electricity for the sale of alcoholic beverages, three hundred
and seventy-five dollars,
(ii) bar space without electricity for the sale of alcoholic beverages, three
hundred dollars,
(iii) vehicle bars, two hundred dollars,
(iv) secluded bar space, one hundred and fifty dollars,
(v) bar space for the sale of non-alcoholic beverages, one hundred dollars,
(vi) space for the sale of barbecue food, one hundred dollars.
(2) Persons who sell goods or provide services at booths in the Grenville Market at
the Festival shall, on a daily basis, pay to the Committee fifty dollars per booth.
(3) A refundable cleaning deposit of one hundred dollars shall be payable on the
rental of all bar space and booths except barbecue bars. All rented bar space and booths
must be cleaned by the renter by midnight following the closing of the Festival or else the
cleaning deposit will be liable to forfeiture at the discretion of the Committee. The
cleaning deposit shall be refunded if the public health department of the Ministry of
Health certifies that the bar space or booth has been satisfactorily cleaned by the renter.
(4) A person parking a motor vehicle at a designated public parking place at the
Festival shall pay the Committee five dollars per day in respect of the parking of the
vehicle.
(5) A person plying a motor omnibus, taxi, commercial vehicle or goods vehicle for
business at the Festival and entering the Festival for that purpose shall pay the committee
ten dollars per day.
(6) Persons who enter the Festival, other than persons referred to in subregulations (1)
to (5), shall, on a daily basis, pay to the Committee—
(a)

one dollar per adult to enter the Festival;

(b)

five dollars per adult, two dollars per minor, to enter the cultural show at the
Grenville Market;

(c)

five dollars per adult, two dollars per minor, to visit the exposition at the
Grenville Market;

(d)

one dollar per adult to visit the science fait.

In this subregulation, “adult” means a person eighteen years of age or over; and “minor”
means a person under eighteen years of age.
8\. Enforcing prevention
Without prejudice to any provision in the Act or these Regulations, when the
Committee or any delegate of the Committee is empowered by these Regulations to
prevent a person from doing anything at the Festival, and such prevention is ordered, such
prevention may be enforced by any police officer.
9\. Committee may administer
The Committee may administer regulations 3 to 8.
10\. Sites of the Festival
The actual sites of the Festival, and which as such shall be under the jurisdiction of the
Committee during the Festival, shall be those public places in Grenville extending from
the Old Church in the south; west by Charles gas station; north to Canal Road, Forde and
Telescope Road to the St. Andrew’s Anglican Secondary School down to Griffin Lane
and Victoria Street; and including in particular public places at Seaton Browne Street,
Grenville Car Park, Ben Jones Street, Jubilee Street, George Patterson Street and Victoria
Street.
11\. Committee may give instructions for directions
(1) The Committee may issue instructions to any police officer or to any designated
person to give directions—
(a)

to any person attending the Festival or selling goods or providing services at
the Festival to lower or turn down the volume of any music, sounds or other
noise provided by such person at the Festival and to keep it at such lowered
volume as may be stipulated by such police officer or designated person;

(b)

to persons selling goods or providing services at the Festival regarding—
(i) the location at the Festival at which goods may be sold or services
rendered,
(ii) the distance between persons selling goods or providing services at the
Festival,
(ii) the conditions under which goods may be sold or services rendered at
the Festival not including the fees to be paid by persons selling goods
or rendering services.

(2) The music, sounds or other noise referred to in subregulation (1) includes music,
sounds or other noise emitted from a radio, tape, tape-deck, compact-disc (CD), recordplayer or other such equipment.
12\. Penalties
Unless otherwise specifically provided in these Regulations, any person contravening
any provision of these Regulations or any instructions or directions issued hereunder shall
be guilty of an offence and shall on summary conviction be liable to the penalties
provided for by section 12(2) of the Act.

13\. Commencement
These Regulations shall be deemed to have come into operation as from 13th July,

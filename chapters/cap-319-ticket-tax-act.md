## CHAPTER 319

TICKET TAX ACT
An Act to provide for the imposition of a tax on tickets issued for overseas travel.
[Act No. 26 of 1980 amended by Act No. 27 of 1990, Act No. 6 of 1991, Act No. 2 of 1992, Act
No. 34 of 1998, Act No. 11 of 1999.]

[2nd May, 1980.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Ticket Tax Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—

“carrier” means a person who operates a service for transporting passengers
directly or indirectly by sea or air from Grenada and includes an agent of such person
and a travel agent;
“Comptroller” means the Comptroller of Inland Revenue;
“ticket” includes any document issued by a carrier for the purpose of enabling a
person to board a ship or aircraft as a passenger in Grenada;
“ticket tax” means the tax imposed by section 3;
“travel agent” means a person who sells or issues tickets for a carrier;
“traveller” means a person who proposes to travel by ship or aircraft to a place
outside Grenada.
3\. Imposition and rate of ticket tax, and exceptions
(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act there shall be charged, levied and paid in
respect of—
(a)

every ticket issued in respect of a traveller by a carrier in Grenada; and

(b)

every ticket issued in respect of a traveller by a carrier outside Grenada for
travel originating in Grenada,

a tax, payable by or on behalf of the traveller, at the rate of ten per cent of the cost of the
ticket.
(2) In the case where no ticket is issued for travel originating in Grenada the tax shall
be at the rate of five per cent of the cost of such travel.
(3) Ticket tax shall not be payable by—
(a)

the Governor-General or the spouse of the Governor-General;

(b)

members of Parliament travelling on Government business;

(c)

public officers travelling on official business;

(d)

representatives of the United Nations Organisation or of any other
international organisation accorded diplomatic privileges in Grenada, or
persons sent on missions on behalf of any such organisation, their spouses,
and their children under the age of twenty-one when travelling with them;

(e)

persons accorded diplomatic privileges in Grenada, their spouses, and their
children under the age of twenty-one when travelling with them;

(f)

students of the University of the West Indies, of any educational institution
in Grenada, and Grenadian students whichever educational institution they
attend;

(g)

judges of the Court of Appeal;

(h)

students of educational institutions in Grenada, the University of the West
Indies, and Grenadian students regardless of the educational institution they
attend:

Provided that such students are travelling on business connected with studies:
Provided further that students returning home on vacation or at the end of their studies
or travelling to another educational centre to continue their studies are deemed to be
travelling on business connected with their studies;
(i)

persons travelling for emergency medical treatment;

(j)

persons exempted under international or other agreements entered into by
the Government of Grenada.

3A. Registration
Every carrier liable or about to become liable for the collection of tax number under
this Act shall register with the Comptroller and provide such information to the
Comptroller as he may require to give effect to such registration.
4\. Carrier to collect ticket tax
(1) Ticket tax shall be collected by the carrier at the time of the issue of a ticket or,
where no ticket is issued, before the traveller embarks.
(2) In the case where a ticket has been issued outside Grenada for travel originating in
Grenada, ticket tax shall be collected by the carrier who deals with the ticket in Grenada.
(3) A carrier who fails to collect ticket tax payable to him or her under this Act shall
pay to the Comptroller the amount of such tax as if he or she had collected it.
(4) Where a carrier has failed to pay to the Comptroller, in accordance with section 5,
the ticket tax collected, the Comptroller may by order disqualify that carrier from the
collection of such tax permanently or for such period as may be specified in the order;
and in the event of such disqualification any tax due on a ticket issued by such carrier
shall be paid directly to the Comptroller.
5\. Ticket tax to be paid to Comptroller
(1) Ticket tax collected shall be due and payable by the carrier to the Comptroller
within fifteen days after the last day of each month or, if the Comptroller so directs,
within fifteen days after the last day of such other interval as he or she may specify.
(2) A carrier who fails to comply with subsection (1) shall be guilty of an offence and
liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of fifty dollars for each day during which the
offence continues and to imprisonment for six months.
5A. Interest
Interest at the rate of two per cent a month or part thereof shall be charged on any
amount due and payable under section 5(1) for the period it remains unpaid commencing
from the date the return required under section 6 is due.
6\. Records to be kept, etc.
Every carrier shall keep such records for the purposes of this Act as the Comptroller
may direct and shall file with the Comptroller a return, as approved by the Comptroller,
together with the tax due for the month within the time allowed under section 5(1) for the
payment of the tax.
7\. Offences
(1) A traveller who, for the purpose of evading the payment of ticket tax, knowingly
makes a false statement or false representation shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on
summary conviction, to a fine of four hundred dollars and to imprisonment for three
months.
(2) A carrier who fails to make a return in accordance with the requirements of this
Act, or who knowingly makes a false statement or false representation in such a return,
shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of one thousand
dollars and to imprisonment for six months.
8\. Refund of tax in certain cases

A traveller who has paid ticket tax but did not travel, or who has paid an amount of
ticket tax in excess of the sum which he or she should have paid, is entitled to and shall
be refunded the amount so paid—
(a)

by the carrier, if the carrier has not paid the amount to the Comptroller; or

(b)

by the Comptroller, if the amount has been paid to the Comptroller.

8A. Provisions of Income Tax Act, Chapter 149A, to apply
(1) Where a carrier fails to pay the ticket tax when it becomes due under section 5, the
Comptroller may file with the bailiff a warrant certified by the Comptroller as correct of
the ticket tax due and payable and unpaid.
(2) A warrant filed under subsection (1) shall be treated by the bailiff as having the
same effect as a civil judgement given by a court of competent jurisdiction in favour of
the Comptroller for a debt in the amount specified in the warrant, and the bailiff shall
proceed to levy on the goods and chattels of the person named in the warrant to such extent
as is necessary for the recovery of the unpaid ticket tax and to meet any proper charges of
the bailiff.
(3) A warrant for the purposes of this section shall be in the form prescribed in the
Schedule to this Act.
8B. Bailiff receipts
On payment of the ticket tax due and the expenses incurred thereon, the bailiff shall
give acquittances under his hand to the persons who pay the same on the numbered
(counterfoil) receipts forms with which the bailiff shall be supplied by the Comptroller
and shall pay over to the Comptroller all monies received by him under this Act.
8C. Sale of goods levied on and disposal of proceeds
(1) Subject to this section, when goods are levied on they shall, after due notice given,
be sold by the bailiff at public auction in such manner as is usual in sales under
executions issuing out of the Magistrates Court; but until such sale, the goods shall
remain in the custody of the bailiff by whom the levy is made.
(2) For the purposes of a levy under this section, tools of trade, bedding and wearing
apparel to the value of two hundred and fifty dollars shall be exempt from execution.
(3) The proceeds of such sale shall be paid over by the bailiff to the Comptroller after
deduction therefrom all reasonable and necessary charges and expenses attending the levy
and sale which may be allowed by the Comptroller; and such proceeds of sale shall be
applied by the Comptroller towards the satisfaction of the unpaid ticket tax and expenses
and the surplus, if any, shall be restored on demand to the owner of the goods on which the
levy was executed.
(4) After a levy, the owner of the goods may redeem the same at any time before the
time appointed for the sale by paying off the full amount of the unpaid ticket tax and fines
thereon, if any, together with all costs and expenses incurred in relation thereto up to the
date of such payment.
(5) Where at a public auction there is no bid sufficient to cover the levy and expenses,
the bailiff may sell such goods to the highest bidder; but the carrier or person, as the case
may be, shall be liable for the balance of the unpaid ticket tax.
9\. Power of Minister to make regulations
(1) The Minister of Finance may make regulations generally for carrying into effect
the provisions of this Act.

(2) In particular and without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1), for the
purpose of enabling the Comptroller to determine whether ticket tax has been duly
collected and accounted for in accordance with this Act, such regulations may require
production to, and provide for inspection by, the Comptroller of documents used, and
records kept, by a carrier.
(3) There may be annexed to the contravention of any regulation made under subsection (1) or (2) a fine not exceeding five hundred dollars.

## CHAPTER 219

OPEN BIBLE STANDARD CHURCHES OF GRENADA (INCORPORATION)
ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of a body to be known as the Incorporated
Trustees of the Open Bible Standard Churches of Grenada, and for matters
connected therewith.
[Act No. 75 of 1979.]

[28th September, 1979.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Open Bible Standard Churches of Grenada
(Incorporation) Act.
2\. Incorporation of trustees
Cajeton Hood, Hermoine Hood, Gilbert Johnson, Wellington Mitchell, Hermon
Sawney, Anthony Lenny George, Liam Paryag and their successors in office as trustees
of the Open Bible Standard Churches of Grenada shall be a body corporate known as the
Incorporated Trustees of the Open Bible Standard Churches of Grenada (hereinafter
called “the Trustees”) with perpetual succession and a Common Seal and with power in
that name to sue and be sued in all courts.
3\. Vesting of property
The lands and building described in the Schedule are transferred to and vested in the
Trustees.
4\. Power of trustees to acquire property

The Trustees shall have power to acquire for the Open Bible Standard Churches of
Grenada by purchase, transfer, exchange, devise, grant, gift, demise, bequest or otherwise
any land or other property of whatever kind whether in perpetuity or for life or for a term
of years and any money, securities, goods or chattels whatever, to take, hold and enjoy
the same subject to the performance of any trust upon which the same may have been
acquired.
5\. Power of trustees to dispose of property, and to borrow, etc.
Subject to the performance of any trust as aforesaid the Trustees may—
(a)

by deed under the Common Seal sell, demise, grant, convey, transfer,
exchange, surrender or otherwise dispose of the lands and building described
in the Schedule and any other land or property which may hereafter be
acquired by or become vested in them;

(b)

borrow money and, for the payment of the same, by deed under the Common
Seal, give and execute mortgages, charges, pledges and other securities of
any real or personal property or make any arrangement that they may
consider convenient with such covenants, powers and provisions as they
think proper.

6\. Affixing of Common Seal
All documents requiring the Common Seal shall be sealed in the presence of at least
two of the Trustees who shall sign every such document.
7\. Address for service, etc.
(1) The Trustees shall at all times have a fixed address in Grenada for the service of
documents on them and such address and any change thereof shall be registered with the
Registrar of Companies.
(2) Any document may be served on the Trustees by leaving the document at, or by
sending it by registered post to, the address registered pursuant to subsection (1).

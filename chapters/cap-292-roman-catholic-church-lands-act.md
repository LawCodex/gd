## CHAPTER 292

ROMAN CATHOLIC CHURCH LANDS ACT
An Act to vest the freehold in Lot 117 of the lands known as the “Church Lands”, in
trustees for the benefit of the Roman Catholic Church.
[12th April, 1871.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Roman Catholic Church Lands Act.

### 2.

Grant of Lot 117 to Roman Catholics
All that piece or parcel of land marked 117 in the plan or survey of the Church Lands
deposited and kept in the office of the Registrar of the Deeds and Land Registry, and
situate in the town of St. George, together with all messuages, tenements, and
appurtenances thereto belonging shall be deemed and taken to be, as from the first day of
January, one thousand eight hundred and sixty-nine, vested in the Reverend Thomas
O’Callaghan, Dean of the Roman Catholic Church and parish priest in Grenada, Jules
Aquart, William Benjamin Markson, and Francis Alexander Coteaux, upon trust for the
use and benefit of the said Church for ever.

### 3.

Appointment of trustees
In all cases of vacancy by death or absence from the island in the number of trustees
hereinbefore named and appointed, every such vacancy shall be filled in the manner
prescribed by the Religious and Educational Societies Property Act, Chapter 285:
Provided always that the Roman Catholic priest for the time being in charge of the
Roman Catholic Church, in the town of St. George, shall be the person to discharge the
duties of chairman required by the said Act.

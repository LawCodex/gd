## CHAPTER 130A

GRENADA HOSPITALS AUTHORITY ACT
An Act to provide a legislative basis for the establishment powers and duties of the
Grenada Hospitals Authority, and for matters connected therewith.
[Act No. 4 of 1998.]

[6th March, 1998.]

## PART I

Preliminary

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Grenada Hospitals Authority Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“Authority” means the Grenada Hospitals Authority established by section 3;
“Board” means the Board of Directors of the Authority established by section 6;
“Chairperson” means the Chairperson of the Board and “Deputy Chairperson” shall
be construed accordingly;
“Director of Medical Services” means the Director of medical services appointed
under section 25;
“Executive Director” means the Executive Director of Hospital Services appointed
under section 25;
“fees” includes any fee or charge payable for treatment, nursing, accommodation,
food, dressings, drugs, medicines or other supplies or services of whatever kind
rendered at a hospital facility;

“health care” means all the health care services and products offered at the hospital
facilities;
“hospital facilities” means the health care facilities specified in the First Schedule;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for health.

## PART II

Establishment of Grenada Hospitals Authority

### 3.

Establishment of Authority
There is hereby established a body to be known as the Grenada Hospitals Authority,
hereinafter referred to as the “Authority”, which shall be a body corporate and to which
sections 47 and 49 of the Interpretation and General Provisions Act, Chapter 153, shall
apply.

### 4.

Functions of Authority
The Authority shall, in accordance with the policy, as determined by the Board, after
consultation with the Minister, manage the hospital facilities in Grenada and establish
new hospital facilities from time to time.

### 5.

Powers and duties of Authority
The powers and duties of the Authority are—
(a)

to manage hospital facilities specified in the First Schedule;

(b)

to provide efficient systems for the delivery of health care;

(c)

to facilitate new developments in the field of health care;

(d)

to provide the use of hospitals for service, teaching and research;

(e)

to construct, operate, equip, furnish, maintain, manage, secure and repair
hospital facilities;

(f)

to collaborate with recognised training institutions, in the education and
training of persons and in research, medicine, nursing, dentistry, pharmacy,
bio-medical and health science fields;

(g)

to negotiate with trade unions with respect to terms and conditions of its
employees; and

(h)

to do all such things, as are indicated or conducive to the attainment of the
objects of the Authority.

### 6.

Board of Authority
(1) The Authority shall have a Board of Directors which shall be constituted in
accordance with subsection (2).
(2) The Board shall consist of not less than seven but not more than nine qualified
members appointed by the Minister to represent such interests as he or she determines
including the medical and nursing profession, the public sector, the private sector and the
employees of the Authority.
(3) If the Minister determines that a person should be appointed to the Board to
represent employees he or she may consult such association of trade unions or individual
trade unions as appear to the Minister to be representative of employees generally in
Grenada.

(4) The Minister shall designate a Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson from amongst
the members appointed pursuant to subsection (2).
(5) The Second Schedule shall have effect in relation to the procedure of the meetings
of the Board.

### 7.

Tenure, etc., of Board members
(1) The members of the Board appointed by the Minister shall hold office for a period
not exceeding three years and shall be eligible for re-appointment.
(2) The Chairperson or a member of the Board may resign his or her office by giving
one month’s written notice to the Minister or the Chairperson, as the case requires.
(3) A member of the Board who cannot attend a meeting of the Board on account of
illness or other temporary cause or who will be temporarily absent from the State shall
inform the Chairperson accordingly.
(4) A member of the Board who absents himself or herself, without leave of the
Chairperson, from three consecutive meetings of the Board shall cease to be a member.

### 8.

Duties of the Board
(1) The Board shall be responsible for the formulation of general policy regarding the
powers and functions of the Authority as prescribed by this Act or any other enactment.
(2) The Board shall in the formulation of policy have regard to—
(a)

national policy;

(b)

efficiency and economy;

(c)

the desirability of improving and developing health care at hospital facilities;

(d)

the general developments in the field of health and other related fields.

### 9.

Committees
(1) The Board may establish and delegate to a committee any of its functions under
this Act or the Board may require such a committee to advise it on any matter arising out
of or connected with such functions.
(2) A committee for the purposes of subsection (1) may include persons who are not
members of the Board but must include at least one member of the Board.
(3) A delegation under subsection (1)—
(a)

is revocable in whole or in part by the Board;

(b)

does not derogate from the powers and functions of, or the exercise thereof
by, the Board as it may think fit or as the occasion requires.

(4) Subject to the direction of the Board, a committee shall determine its own
procedure.
(5) A committee established under subsection (1) shall, upon the completion of its
examination, submit a report to the Chairperson for consideration of the Board.

### 10.

Secretary to the Board
(1) The Chairperson shall designate an employee of the Authority to be Secretary to
the Board.
(2) The Secretary shall perform such duties as the Chairperson or Deputy
Chairperson, as the case may be, determines.

### 11.

Directions by Minister
Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act, the Minister may give written
directions to the Board on matters of general policy and the Board shall give effect to
such directions.

### 12.

Seal
The Authority shall have an official seal that shall be kept in the custody of the
Chairperson or any other person as the Board determines.

### 13.

Execution and use documents
(1) A document, not required by law to be under the seal of the Authority, shall be
duly executed if signed by the Chairperson, the Executive Director or a member of the
Board duly authorised by resolution of the Board.
(2) Service upon the Authority of a notice, order or other document shall be executed
by delivering the same by hand or by sending it by registered post to the Executive
Director at the head office of the Authority.

## PART III

Funds and Property of the Authority and Accounting Matters Generally

### 14.

Funds of the Authority and use thereof
(1) The funds of the Authority shall consist of—
(a)

such money as may be appropriated therefore by Parliament;

(b)

any loan made to the Authority by the Government or any other person or
organisation;

(c)

money derived from health care rendered at hospital facilities;

(d)

sums borrowed by the Authority in accordance with section 16;

(e)

any other money lawfully received by the Authority by way of grant or
otherwise for the purposes of the Authority.

(2) The money referred to in subsection (1) shall on receipt thereof be placed to the
credit of the Authority at a bank determined by the Board.

### 15.

Financial Powers of Authority
The Authority may, for the purpose of carrying out its functions under this Act, with
the approval of the Minister—
(a)

charge the fees prescribed for services provided by it;

(b)

engage in activities for the raising of funds for the Authority;

(c)

receive donations, be a beneficiary under covenants and establish and
administer trusts;

(d)

build up reserves;

(e)

invest resources, and surplus funds, in such securities as the Authority thinks
fit.

### 16.

Power to borrow and guarantee thereof

(1) Subject to this Act, the Authority may with the prior consent of the Minister, after
he or she has consulted the Minister of Finance, borrow money that is required to meet its
obligations or to discharge any of its functions, under this Act.
(2) The Minister may, with the approval of Parliament, guarantee in such manner and
or such conditions as he or she considers appropriate, the repayment of the principal,
interest and other charges with respect to any authorised borrowing by the Authority.
(3) The Authority shall make payment to the Accountant General, at such time and in
such manner as the Minister may direct, of the amount so directed in or towards the
repayment of a sum debited to the Consolidated Fund in fulfilment of a guarantee given
under this section, and in or towards the payment of interest on what is outstanding for
the time being in respect of the sum so debited at such rate as the Minister may direct, and
different rates of interest may be directed as regards different sums and as regards interest
for differing periods.
(4) For the purposes of subsections (2) and (3), “Minister” means the Minister of
Finance.

### 17.

Application of funds
The funds of the Authority shall be applied in defraying the following expenditures—
(a)

the remuneration of its officers and staff;

(b)

the remuneration and honoraria of the members of the Board and of
members of the committees established by the Board;

(c)

the capital and operating expenses, including maintenance and insurance of
the property of the Authority;

(d)

the making and maintenance of investments by the Authority in the
discharge of its functions; and

(e)

any other expenditure authorised by the Authority for the discharge of its
functions.

### 18.

Vesting of property
The hospitals specified in the First Schedule together with the equipment and
furniture, shall, by virtue of this section, vest in the Authority free from encumbrances
and without further assurance.

### 19.

Reserve Fund
(1) The Authority shall establish and maintain a Reserve Fund which shall be dealt
with and applied as the Board determines.
(2) The Reserve Fund shall be derived from the surplus of the Authority and shall be
in such amount as the Board, after consultation with the Minister, determines.

### 20.

Consolidated Fund
The Authority shall in each quarter pay into the Consolidated Fund such amount as the
Minister directs in writing.

### 21.

Budget and investment plan
(1) The Authority shall cause a budget to be prepared with respect to the ensuing
financial year in accordance with the guidelines provided by the Minister.
(2) The Board shall prepare and submit to the Minister an investment plan for such
period as the Board determines.

### 22.

Accounts
(1) The Authority shall keep proper books of account in which shall be recorded all
its transactions to the satisfaction of the Board and shall prepare and retain financial
statements in respect of each financial year.
(2) The financial statement shall represent the true and fair financial position of the
Authority and of the results of the operations of the Authority.

### 23.

Audit
(1) The accounts of the Authority shall be audited in each financial year by an
independent, qualified and experienced auditor or firm of auditors appointed by the
Board.
(2) The Board and the officers and staff of the Authority shall make available to the
auditors all books, ledgers, deeds, contracts, accounts, vouchers and other documents
which are required for the purposes of subsection (1); and the auditor may require any
person holding or accountable for any such document to appear before him or her and
give a signed statement or information in relation to any such document.

### 24.

Auditor’s report
The auditor shall, as soon as practicable, but not later than three months after the end
of each financial year, send copies of the audited financial statement and report thereon to
the Authority.

### 25.

Report to Minister
(1) The Authority shall, as soon as practicable but not later than one month after the
receipt of the audited financial statement and report under section 24, submit a separate
report to the Minister containing—
(a)

the audited financial statement and the report thereon received from the
auditor;

(b)

a review of the activities of the Authority during the preceding year in such
form as the Minister directs or approves.

(2) The Minister shall cause a copy of the audited financial statement and report
thereon together with the review to be laid in both Houses of Parliament within one
month of the receipt thereof.
(3) The Board shall as soon as possible after the commencement of the financial year
hold a meeting, to which the public shall be invited, and at which the following matters
shall be discussed—
(a)

the audited accounts of the preceding year;

(b)

the performance of the Authority for the preceding year;

(c)

the plans and objectives of the Authority for the ensuing year.

## PART IV

Officers and Staff of the Authority

### 26.

Executive Director and Director of Medical Services
(1) There shall be an Executive Director of Hospital Services hereinafter referred to
as “the Executive Director” who shall be Chief Executive Officer of the Authority and
who shall perform such duties and exercise such powers as specified in this Act.

(2) There shall be a Director of Medical Services who shall be the Chief Technical
Officer of the Authority.
(3) The Executive Director and the Director of Medical Services shall be appointed
by the Board, with the approval of the Minister, on such terms and conditions as the
Board determines.

### 27.

Other Staff
(1) Subject to this Act, the Board may—
(a)

appoint such employees as it considers necessary for the due performance of
its functions;

(b)

prescribe qualification and fix terms and conditions of service, except that
the salaries in excess of sixty thousand dollars per annum shall be subject to
the Minister’s approval.

(2) The Board may in writing delegate its powers under subsection (1) to the Director
or another senior officer of the Authority.

### 28.

Approval of secondment of public officers
The Public Service Commission may, subject to such conditions as it may impose,
approve the secondment of an officer in the public service to any office within the
Authority and an officer so seconded shall, in relation to pension, gratuity or other
allowances, and in relation to other rights as the public officer, be treated as continued
service in the public service of Grenada.

### 29.

Preservation of posts
Notwithstanding anything contained in any other enactment, where an officer is
seconded to the Authority his or her post or an equivalent post shall be deemed to be
subsisting for the duration of such secondment.

### 30.

Accrued pensions
A secondment to the Authority shall not affect any accrued pension rights of an
employee which derived from service in the public service of Grenada.

### 31.

Status of seconded officer
(1) For the avoidance of doubt it is hereby declared that an officer seconded to the
Authority shall continue to be in the public service of Grenada until such time when he or
she resigns, retires or otherwise leaves the public service.
(2) The Authority may enter any arrangement with the Government with respect to an
employee who is seconded.

### 32.

Pension plan and medical insurance
(1) Subject to the National Insurance Act, Chapter 205, the Authority may, with the
approval of, and subject to the general policy directions by, the Minister provide for the
establishment and maintenance of a pension plan and medical insurance for the benefit of
its officers and staff.
(2) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1), the pension plan may
empower the Authority to—
(a)

pay gratuities, pensions or superannuation allowances to or the funds for the
benefit of its officers and staff; and

(b)

establish contributory and superannuation funds for the benefit of its officers
and staff; and

(c)

enter into and carry into effect agreements with any general insurance
company with respect to a pension scheme.

### 33.

Preservation of collective agreements
After the commencement of this Act and subject to any written law, a collective or
other agreement that immediately prior to the commencement of this Act affected
employees who were employed in the public service or a statutory board shall continue to
have effect in relation to such employees.

## PART V

Miscellaneous

### 34.

Exemption from taxes, etc.
(1) The Authority shall be exempt from all taxes, duties, fees charges, assessments,
levies and imposts on its profit, or on assets which it acquires for its own use.
(2) The exemption under subsection (1) shall not apply to the customs service charge
chargeable under the Customs (Service Charge) Act, Chapter 75D.

### 35.

Amendment of First Schedule
(1) The Minister may by order amend the First Schedule.
(2) An order under subsection (1) shall be laid in Parliament within twenty-one days
after it is made by the Minister.
(3) Where Parliament is not in session an order under subsection (1) shall be laid at
the earliest opportunity after Parliament resumes.

### 36.

Rules and regulations
(1) The Minister may make rules and regulations, subject to negative resolution of
Parliament, for the purpose of giving effect to this Act, and without prejudice to the
generality of the foregoing, may make rules and regulations for—
(a)

the delivery of health care;

(b)

health promotion and surveillance;

(c)

fees chargeable for the delivery of health care;

(d)

contracting for goods and services;

(e)

financial matters generally;

(f)

security at health care facilities;

(g)

staff and related matters;

(h)

any other matters that are required or necessary to be prescribed for the
purposes of this Act.

(2) A fine not exceeding five thousand dollars and a term of imprisonment not
exceeding one year may be attached to rules and regulations made under subsection (1).

### 37.

Repeal and savings
(1) The Hospitals Act, Chapter 135, is hereby repealed.

(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1), rules or regulations made pursuant to the
Hospitals Act shall continue in force until such times as new rules or regulations are made
pursuant to this Act.

### 38.

Commencement
This Act comes into force on the 16th day of March, 1998.

GRENADA HOSPITALS AUTHORITY ACT
Procedure of the Board of the Authority
[Section 6(5).]

1\. Meetings generally
(1) The Chairperson shall preside over all meetings of the Board and on the absence
of the Chairperson the Deputy Chairperson shall preside.
(2) The Board shall meet at such times as may be necessary or expedient for the
transaction of business, and such meetings shall be held at such place and time and on
such days as the Chairperson determines.
(3) The Chairperson shall not permit more than two months to elapse between
meetings.
(4) Where three or more members of the Board by written notice signed by them and
addressed to the Chairperson request a meeting of the Board be held for the purpose
specified in such notice, the Chairperson shall, within seven days of the receipt of such
notice, convene such a meeting.
(5) Without prejudice to anything contained in subparagraph (4) the Chairperson shall
convene a meeting of the Board if requested to do so by the Minister.
2\. Quorum
(1) The quorum necessary for the transaction of the business of the Board shall be
five.

(2) For the purpose of determining whether there is a quorum a person who attends a
meeting shall be treated as being present notwithstanding that, under paragraph 3 he or
she is disqualified from voting at that meeting.
3\. Withdrawal from meeting
Where the Chairperson or another member has, directly or indirectly, any pecuniary or
personal interest in a contract or proposed contract or another matter and is present at a
meeting of the Board at which such a contract or matter is the subject of consideration, he
or she shall at the meeting and as soon as practicable after the commencement thereof
disclose the fact, and shall withdraw from the meeting until such time as the contract or
matter has been discussed and a vote taken thereon.
4\. Voting and related matters
(1) All members of the Board shall be entitled to cast a single vote on any matter
before the Board that requires a decision.
(2) A decision of the Board with regard to any question shall be determined by a
majority of all the members present at that meeting; but in any case where the votes of the
members are equally divided, the Chairperson presiding at the meeting shall have a
casting vote in addition to his or her own vote.
5\. Minutes
The minutes of the proceedings of each meeting of the Board shall be kept in such
manner as the Board determines and the minutes of any meeting must be confirmed at the
next meeting of the Board.
6\. Remuneration of members of the Board
The Chairperson and members of the Board shall be paid such fees and allowances out
of the funds of the Authority as the Minister from time to determines.
7\. Power to regulate
Subject to the Second Schedule, the Board may regulate its own proceedings.

GRENADA GENERAL HOSPITAL (GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEES) RULES
[Section 16.]

[17th December, 1949.]
1\. Citation
These Rules may be cited as the Grenada General Hospital (Government Employees)
Rules.
2\. Government employees
(1) Government employees whose salaries do not exceed one thousand dollars per
annum may be admitted free of charge to the Grenada General Hospital on the following
terms—
(a)

To the General Wards.—Employees on monthly rates of pay not exceeding
twenty-five dollars, and primary school teachers other than Head and
Assistant Teachers.

(b)

To the Paying Patients Wards.—Members of the Clerical Service, Lady
Clerks, Head and Assistant Teachers and employees on monthly rates of pay
exceeding twenty-five dollars.

(2) Admission under this rule shall be on the recommendation of a Medical Officer
accompanied by a certificate from the Head of the Department concerned to the effect
that the patient is entitled to the privilege claimed; and any such person suffering from an
infectious or contagious disease shall be placed in such portion of the Hospital as the
House Officer may direct.
3\. Ex-servicemen
(1) (a) Ex-servicemen below the rank of Sergeant shall be admitted to the General
Wards free of charge; and
(b) Ex-servicemen of the rank of Warrant Officer or Sergeant, to the Paying
Patients ward free of charge:
Provided that the conditions of rule 2(2) of these Rules shall apply to such exservicemen, save and except as regards the required certificate from the Head of the
Department concerned.
(2) For the purposes of this rule, “ex-servicemen” means persons who have served in
any one of Her Majesty’s Forces.
4\. Admission into more expensive wards
If any person mentioned in rule 2 or 3 of these Rules shall be admitted to a ward other
than that to which, by reason of these Rules, he or she would be admitted free of charge

such person shall, if admitted to a ward in respect of which the fees charged are higher
than the ward to which he or she would be admitted free of charge, be liable for the
payment of the difference between the cost per diem of the ward to which he or she is so
admitted and that to which he or she would be admitted free of charge.

$1,000 per annum shall enjoy the same privileges hereunder as they enjoyed before
retirement.
(3) Admission under this rule shall be on the recommendation of a medical officer
accompanied by a certificate from the Head of the Department concerned to the effect
that the patient is entitled to the privilege claimed; and any such person suffering from an
infectious or contagious disease shall be placed in such portion of the Hospital as the
House Officer may direct.
9\. Ex-serviceman
(1) (a) Ex-servicemen below the rank of Sergeant shall be admitted to the General
Wards free of charge; and
(b) Ex-servicemen of the rank of Warrant Officer or Sergeant, to the Paying
Patients Wards free of charge:
Provided that the condition of subparagraph 8 of these Rules shall apply to such Exservicemen, save and except as regards the required certificate from the Head of the
Department concerned.
(2) For the purposes of this Rule “Ex-servicemen” means persons who have served in
any one of Her Majesty’s Forces.
10\. Admission into more expensive wards
If any person mentioned in rule 8 or 9 of these Rules shall be admitted to a ward other
than that to which, by reason of these Rules, he shall be admitted free of charge such
person shall, if admitted to a ward in respect of which the fees charged are higher than the
ward to which he shall be admitted free of charge, be liable for the payment of the
difference between the cost per diem of the ward to which he is so admitted and that to
which he shall be admitted free of charge.
11\. Waiver of charges
Notwithstanding anything contained in these Rules the Minister may in any case
where it considers it desirable so to do waive the whole or any part of the fees payable by
a patient under these Rules.

GRENADA GENERAL HOSPITAL RULES
[Section 20, Schedule to Chapter 138 of 1958.]

These Rules may be cited as the Grenada General Hospital Rules.

## PART I

Chief Medical Officer
2\.
The Hospital shall be under the general supervision of the Chief Medical Officer, who
shall report to the Minister on its conditions from time to time.
3\.
He shall visit the Hospital as often as he may consider necessary to keep himself
informed of the working of the institution.
4\.
He shall forward to the Minister the annual report of the Medical Superintendent and
of the Surgeon Specialist together with any observations he may think fit.
Medical-Superintendent
5\.
The Medical Superintendent shall be responsible for the medical care and treatment of
all persons admitted to the Hospital, and shall, subject to the provisions of the Hospitals
Act and all rules and regulations made thereunder, have the entire control of the Hospital,
and it shall be his duty to see to the proper clothing, care and maintenance of sick persons
in the Hospital; he shall have control of all subordinate officers, employees and servants
at the Hospital, and shall see that discipline and good order are maintained and that all
rules are duly observed.
Surgeon Specialist
6\.
The Surgeon Specialist shall be responsible for the surgical care of all patients
admitted to the Hospital, and shall perform such other duties as may from time to time be
prescribed.
He shall reside in quarters allotted to him, either at the Hospital or within easy reach
thereof, and be within easy reach of the institution up to 4 p.m. daily. Whenever he leaves
the precincts of the Hospital he shall leave directions as to where he may be found.

He shall satisfy himself that the sanitary arrangements at the Hospital are satisfactory;
that the food for patients under his care is prepared in a proper and palatable manner; and
that the provisions and other supplies are of good and wholesome quality.
He shall have charge of, and be responsible for, all surgical instruments and
appliances on charge.
10\.
He shall be responsible for the proper keeping of the “Admission and Discharge
Register” and the “Register of Operations”.
11\.
He shall submit to the Chief Medical Officer an annual report on the working of his
department of the institution.
House Officer
12\.
The House Officer shall reside on the premises of the Hospital in such quarters as may
be allotted to him. He shall perform such duties as may from time to time be prescribed.
Matron
13\.
The Matron shall reside at the Hospital in quarters allotted to her and shall not absent
herself from the precincts of the institution without the permission of the Medical
Superintendent or House Officer.
14\.
She shall see that all directions of the Medical Superintendent, the Surgeon Specialist
and the House Officer, as to the treatment of patients are properly carried out.
15\.
She shall be responsible for the superintendence of the nurses, the female servants and
the patients.
16\.
She shall be responsible for the cleanliness and good order of the wards.
17\.
She shall be responsible for all linen, bedding, appliances and furniture in the wards,
and for any reserve stock of such articles issued to her.
She shall have special charge of the operating theatre, and of the maternity ward.

She shall instruct and train the probationers and nurses in the proper performance of
their duties by periodical lectures and practical demonstrations.
She shall interview all applicants for the post of probationer or servant and shall
advise the Medical Superintendent as to their fitness.
21\.
She shall superintend the kitchen and the cooking and service of the patient’s food,
and shall see that, as far as possible, cause for complaints by patients in this respect does
not exist or continue.
22\.
She shall promptly report to the Medical Superintendent or to the Surgeon Specialist
any misconduct on the part of any patient or any irregularity, neglect of duty or
misconduct on the part of any nurse, probationer or servant, which may come under her
notice.
23\.
She shall be subject to the general directions of the Medical Superintendent and the
Surgeon Specialist.
Steward
24\.
The Steward shall reside at the Hospital in quarters allotted to him.
25\.
He shall exercise general supervision over all junior employees, and report to the
Medical Superintendent any misconduct, irregularity, neglect of duty or carelessness
which may come under his notice.
26\.
He shall have under his immediate care and shall regularly inspect the entire buildings
and grounds of the institution, the mortuary, the drug store, the garage, the ambulance
shed and the casual ward.
27\.
He shall have care of the equipment of the institution, and shall keep proper
inventories of all stores under his charge in every department.
He shall promptly report to the Medical Superintendent the necessity for repairs to any
building or equipment.

He shall be responsible for the correct keeping of a daily diet register and all books
(other than medical books) and records of the institution.
He shall prepare requisitions for supplies daily, and shall inspect the quality and check
the quantities and weights of such stores upon receipt, referring any disputable item to the
Medical Superintendent.
31\.
He shall keep such books in connection with the drug store as are required under
Financial Rules.
Minor Employees
32\.
Orderlies, servants, labourers and other minor employees shall be subject to the
directions of the Medical Superintendent, Surgeon Specialist, House Officer, the Matron
and the Steward, and shall conform to such rules as may be made by the Medical
Superintendent and the Surgeon Specialist with the approval of the Chief Medical
Officer.
Patients
33\.
No patient shall be recommended for admission to the Hospital until previous inquiry
has been made of the Medical Superintendent or the Surgeon Specialist, in writing or by
telephone, as to available accommodation. If this is not done, admission cannot be
guaranteed.
34\.
No patient shall be admitted until he has been examined by the Medical
Superintendent, or the Surgeon Specialist.
35\.
All cases of an acute or sub-acute nature requiring medical or surgical treatment shall
be admitted to the Hospital with the approval of the Medical Superintendent or the
Surgeon Specialist.
36\.
Cases of accident, or urgent cases requiring immediate help shall be admitted whether
in possession of a certificate from a medical officer or not.
37\.
All other cases shall only be admitted upon the production to the Medical
Superintendent or to the Surgeon Specialist of a recommendation from a medical officer
stating that the case, in his opinion, is a fit one for admission.

Persons suffering from infectious or contagious diseases shall be admitted at the
discretion of the Medical Superintendent who will take all necessary steps in his power to
protect other patients.
The hours for admission of patients shall be from 8 to 11 a.m. daily (Sundays
excepted). Urgent cases shall be admitted at any hour of the day or night.
40\.
No patient, whether cured or not, can demand discharge on Sunday or on any day
before 8 a.m. or after 4 p.m.
41\.
No person shall be admitted as a patient, and no patient shall remain in the Hospital
when he can be satisfactorily treated as an outdoor case.
42\.
The Medical Superintendent shall report to the Chief Medical Officer all cases
remaining in Hospital for a period exceeding 4 months, with his recommendations.
43\.
All personal clothing, money or articles of value shall be placed in the safe custody of
the Steward until the patient’s discharge, the Steward alone being responsible for their
safe keeping.
44\.
Patients must conduct themselves with civility and kindness towards their fellow
patients, the officers and nurses of the institution, and must strictly conform to the rules of
the Hospital and the instructions of the Medical Superintendent or the Surgeon Specialist.
A breach of this rule will render the patient liable to discharge.
45\.
Any patient behaving in a refractory or improper manner may be expelled by the
Medical Superintendent or the Surgeon Specialist who shall make a note of the offence
on the patient’s bed card.
46\.
No patient shall be discharged or expelled whose health is such that life may be
endangered thereby.
47\.
Any patient who shall have cause for complaint shall refer to the Medical
Superintendent or to the Surgeon Specialist and after such reference may if necessary
request to see the Chief Medical Officer whose duty it will be to investigate the complaint
and deal with it as he may deem fit.

Patients (other than paying, patients) shall make themselves useful as far as
practicable, in making up their beds, sweeping and cleaning the wards, rendering
assistance to other patients, or employing their time in needlework and other useful
occupations for the benefit of the Hospital.
Maternity Ward
The Maternity Ward shall be intended for the reception of poor and destitute women
in, or very soon to commence, labour.
Children’s Ward
50\.
The Children’s Ward shall be intended for the reception of children up to the age of
seven years.
51\.
(1) Patients may be admitted to the Paying Patients’ Ward and the Private Block on
the recommendation of a medical officer or private practitioner, and with the approval of
the Medical Superintendent or the Surgeon Specialist, in accordance with rules 33, 34, 37
and 38.
(2) Admissions under this rule shall include cases of midwifery.
Guarantee for payment of fees by patient
52\.
No person shall be retained as a patient in any paying ward unless a guarantee
satisfactory to the Surgeon Specialist has been produced for the payment of the fees of
such patient. In addition the patient or some other person on his behalf may be required to
deposit in the Treasury and produce a receipt therefor an amount calculated as follows—
(a)

where the duration of stay is in the opinion of the Surgeon Specialist likely
to be a month or longer, 25 per cent of the estimated total fees payable;

(b)

where the duration of stay is likely to be less than a month but more than one
week, 50 per cent of such fees;

(c)

where the duration of stay is not likely to exceed one week, the whole of
such fees.

53\.
Any patient who fails to comply with any one of the—conditions in rule 52 shall be
liable to be transferred to a free ward.
Visitors
(1) Patients may receive visitors without special permission on the days and at the
times fixed by rules made, under the authority of the Hospitals Act, by the Minister.

(2) The Medical Superintendent and the Surgeon Specialist shall nevertheless have
full discretionary power in the matter of permitting or disallowing visitors to any patient.
In urgent circumstances a patient may be allowed to receive visitors on any day and at
such times as may be permitted by the Medical Superintendent or the Surgeon Specialist.
Visitors shall refrain from making a noise or behaving in such a manner as to disturb
the order and quiet of the institution. The nurse in charge shall report to the Matron or the
Steward any breach of this Rule, and any offending visitor shall be liable to be
peremptorily ejected from the premises.
Ministers of Religion
57\.
Ministers of religion will be allowed access to the wards at all reasonable times to
administer religious rites or instruction to the members of their respective denominations:
Provided that such visits do not interfere with the attendance of the Medical
Superintendent or the Surgeon Specialist on the patients.
Police Force
58\.
Members of the Police Force shall be admitted for free medical treatment on a
certificate from a medical officer and the Commissioner of Police.
59\.
Subordinate officers shall be admitted to the Paying Patients Ward.
60\.
Members of the Police Force while in Hospital shall conform to all rules of the
Hospital, and any disobedience or neglect thereof shall be investigated and dealt with by
the Commissioner of Police, upon the report of the Medical Superintendent or the
Surgeon Specialist or officer in charge of the Hospital, as if such rules had been issued by
the Commissioner of Police.
Operations
61\.
No surgical o1peration shall be performed unless with full consent of the patient, his
relatives or friends.
The Surgeon Specialist shall be the Operating Surgeon, and with him shall rest the
decision for all operations and surgical interference of any kind.
X-ray Examinations

X-ray examinations in respect of patients in the general wards shall be carried out free
of charge. Examinations for out-patients shall be paid for at the rates laid down by rules
in that behalf.

## CHAPTER 237

PENTECOSTAL ASSEMBLIES OF THE WEST INDIES
(GRENADA DISTRICT) (INCORPORATION) ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of a body to be known as the Incorporated
Trustees of the Pentecostal Assemblies of the West Indies (Grenada District), to
vest in that body certain property now held by the Pentecostal Assemblies of the
West Indies and the Pentecostal Assemblies of Canada, and for matters
connected therewith.
[Act No. 91 of 1979.]

[30th November, 1979.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Pentecostal Assemblies of the West Indies (Grenada
District) (Incorporation) Act.
2\. Incorporation of trustees
Sampson Munro, Emroy Sampson, Bertha Honore, Thomas Welch, Eileen Nicholas,
Lodwin Antoine and their successors in office as trustees of the Pentecostal Assemblies
of the West Indies (Grenada District) shall be a body corporate known as the Incorporated
Trustees of the Pentecostal Assemblies of the West Indies (Grenada District) (hereinafter
called “the Trustees”) with perpetual succession and a Common Seal and with power in
that name to sue and be sued in all courts.
3\. Power of trustees to acquire property

The Trustees shall have power to acquire for the Pentecostal Assemblies of the West
Indies (Grenada District) by purchase, transfer, exchange, devise, grant, gift, demise,
bequest or otherwise any land or other property of whatever kind whether in perpetuity or
for life or for a term of years and any money, securities, goods or chattels whatever, to
take, hold and enjoy the same subject to the performance of any trust upon which the
same may have been acquired.
4\. Power of trustees to dispose of property, and to borrow, etc.
Subject to the performance of any trust as aforesaid the Trustees may—
(a)

by deed under the Common Seal sell, demise, grant, convey, transfer,
exchange, surrender or otherwise dispose of any real or personal property
vested in or which may hereafter be acquired by or become vested in the
Trustees;

(b)

borrow money and, for the payment of the same, by deed under the Common
Seal, give and execute mortgages, charges, pledges and other securities of
any real or personal property or make any arrangement that they may
consider convenient with such covenants, powers and provisions as they
think proper.

5\. Vesting of property
All chapels, buildings, appurtenances and land held by the Pentecostal Assemblies of
the West Indies and the Pentecostal Assemblies of Canada, or by any other person, for
and on behalf of, or for the use of, the Pentecostal Assemblies of the West Indies
(Grenada District), and in particular the land (with buildings and appurtenances thereon)
specified in the Schedule, are vested in the Trustees for the same estates and interests and
to the same extent as subsisted immediately before the commencement of this Act subject
to any rights, reservations and charges affecting the same.
6\. Exemption from licensing and forfeiture under Chapter 13
Notwithstanding that the Trustees may at any time be or be deemed to be under alien
control for the purposes of the Aliens (Land Holding Regulation) Act, Chapter 13, they
shall be entitled to hold land and mortgages without Licence, and such land and
mortgages shall not be liable to forfeiture under that Act.
7\. Affixing of Common Seal
All documents requiring the Common Seal shall be sealed in the presence of at least
two of the Trustees who shall sign every such document.
8\. Address for service, etc.
(1) The Trustees shall at all times have a fixed address in Grenada for the service of
documents on them and such address and any change thereof shall be registered with the
Registrar of Companies.
(2) Any document may be served on the Trustees by leaving the document at, or by
sending it by registered post to, the address registered pursuant to subsection (1).

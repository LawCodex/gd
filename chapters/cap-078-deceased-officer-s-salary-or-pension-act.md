## CHAPTER 78

DECEASED OFFICER’S SALARY OR PENSION ACT
An Act to authorise the payment otherwise than to executors or administrators of
small sums of money due to deceased persons on account of salary or pension
payable from public funds.
[Amended by Act No. 34 of 1988, Act No. 34 of 1991.]

[15th June, 1892.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Deceased Officer’s Salary or Pension Act.
2\. Payment of salary or pension due to deceased may be paid to person entitled
thereto without letters of administration
On the death of a person to whom any sum or sums of money not exceeding in all
seven thousand five hundred dollars may then be due on account of salary or pension
chargeable on the Consolidated Fund, it shall be lawful for the Minister of Finance by
warrant under his or her hand, on his or her being satisfied of the expediency of
dispensing with letters testamentary or letters of administration, to cause the sum or sums
to be paid to such person or persons as the Minister may consider to be entitled thereto,
without requiring the production of letters testamentary or letters of administration.
3\. Validity of payments under this Act
A payment made in pursuance of this Act shall be valid against all persons; and all
persons acting under its provisions shall be absolutely discharged from all liability in
respect of all monies duly paid by them under this Act.

## CHAPTER 292A

SAINT GEORGE’S BOROUGH COUNCIL ACT
An Act to constitute Saint George’s Town a Municipal Borough, to incorporate the
inhabitants thereof, and to provide for the establishment, constitution, powers,
functions and duties of their Borough Council as an organ of Local Government.
[Act No. 19 of 1995.]

[5th April, 1995.]

## PART I

Preliminary

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Saint George’s Borough Council Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“Borough” means the Town of Saint George’s as defined by this Act, whose
inhabitants are by this Act constituted into a Corporation;
“Commissioner” means the Commissioner of Local Government;
“Constituency” has the same meaning as that given to it by the Representation of
the People Act, unless specifically otherwise provided by or under this Act;
“corporate land” means land belonging to or held in trust for the Corporation;
“corporate office” means the office of Mayor, Deputy mayor, Alderman or
Councillor;
“Corporation” means the Corporation established by and under the provisions of
section 4 of this Act;
“Council” means the Council of the Corporation;
“Minister” means the Minister for the time being charged with responsibility for
the subject of Local Government;
“owner” means the person in possession of or in receipt either of the whole or of
any part of the rents or profits of any land or tenement whether in his or her own right
or as a trustee or personal representative of any other person, or in the occupation of
such land or tenement other than as a tenant from year to year, or for any less term, or
as a tenant at will;
“premises” includes messuages, buildings, lands, easements, and hereditaments of
any tenure;
“reputed owner” of any building or of any vacant lot of land means the person
entered as the owner of such building or of such vacant lot of land in the Rate Roll

Book for the time being in force or in any register of ownership kept by the
Corporation;
“State” means the State of Grenada, including Carriacou and Petit Martinique;
“Supreme Court” means the Supreme Court of or for Grenada;
“to value” means to fix the annual rateable value of any rateable hereditament in
accordance with the provisions of any Act or order providing for the valuation of
property for local government purposes;
“vacant lot” means a lot of land on which there is no habitable building and which
cannot reasonably be regarded as the curtilage of an existing building;
“valuation” means the sum fixed by the Corporation or by any assessment
committee appointed by the Corporation as the annual rateable value of any rateable
hereditament.

## PART II

Establishment of Borough and Division of Borough into Wards and Municipal
Corporation

### 3.

Establishment of Borough
(1) For the purposes of this Act, the Town of St. George, the capital town of the State,
comprising the constituency of the Town of St. George, is hereby constituted a Municipal
Borough, which shall be called the St. George’s Municipal Borough.
(2) The St. George’s Municipal Borough shall comprise such number of Wards as
may be prescribed under and in accordance with sections 3, 4 and 5 of the District
Councils Act, Chapter 83A as if for this purpose the Borough were a District within the
meaning of those sections of that Act.

### 4.

Establishment of St. George’s Municipal Corporation
(1) A body corporate to be called the “St. George’s Municipal Borough Corporation”
is hereby established and constituted in and for the St. George’s Municipal Borough as a
local government authority with the powers, functions and duties provided for in this Act.
(2) The St. George’s Municipal Borough Corporation established by and under subsection (1) shall be entitled to make contracts and to acquire, hold and dispose of all kinds
of property real and personal in its corporate name the “St. George’s Municipal Borough
Corporation” and shall have and use a common seal by and in its said corporate name and
it may sue and be sued in all Courts of Law without the licence of the Minister. The
official seal of the Corporation shall be judicially noticed.

### 5.

Corporation to act through Borough Council
The St. George’s Municipal Borough Corporation established by and under section 4
of this Act shall act by and through a Council hereby established to be called the “St.
George’s Municipal Borough Council”.

### 6.

Membership of Borough Council
(1) The membership of the St. George’s Municipal Borough Council shall be as
follows—
(i) The Mayor,
(ii) The elected Councillors—

one (1) each elected for each Ward by the voters of that Ward; one of which
elected Councillors shall be the Deputy Mayor,
(iii) The co-opted Councillors—
three in number; all chosen by the elected Councillors in secret ballot; which
co-opted Councillors shall be called Aldermen; and
(iv) The Member of the House of Representatives elected to represent the
constituency of St. George’s Town under the Representation of the People
Act, Chapter 286A, or his or her nominee.
(2) The elected Councillors shall be elected in local government elections conducted
in accordance with elections referred to in section 8 of this Act.
(3) If no one is elected to represent a Ward in the elections referred to in
subsection (2), the Minister may select from that Ward a person to serve as the elected
Councillor for that Ward, and the person so selected by the Minister shall for all intents
and purposes be deemed to be the elected Councillor for that Ward. In making such a
selection, the Minister shall act fairly, and shall consult both the Member of the House of
Representatives elected to represent the constituency of St. George Town and the Leader
of the Opposition in the House of Representatives.
(4) The elected Councillors of the Borough may, for the purpose of assisting them in
the carrying out of their responsibilities, co-opt up to three co-opted Councillors, called
Aldermen, which Aldermen may be public officers.

### 7.

Term of office
(1) Elected Councillors and Aldermen shall hold office for four years, each
Councillor being elected in accordance with elections referred to in section 8 of this Act:
Provided that Councillors shall be eligible to be re-elected or to be co-opted again.
(2) A person who is a member of the Borough Council by virtue of section 6(1)(iv) of
this Act shall cease to be such member immediately he or she ceases to represent the
constituency comprising the St. George’s Borough in the House of Representatives.

## PART III

Qualifications and Disqualifications of Borough Mayor, Deputy Mayor and Councillors

### 8.

Qualifications and disqualifications of members, expulsion, resignation, recall,
elections, etc.

## Part IV, comprising sections 10 to 21, of the District Councils Act, Chapter 83A,

governing District Councils, shall respectively apply, mutatis mutandis, to the Borough
Council established by and under this Act as if this Borough Council were a District
Council, in relation to qualifications of members, disqualifications of members, members’
seats being vacated in certain events, statements and declarations by members regarding
their qualifications, the power of the Council to expel its members resignation of
members, the recall of Councillors, notification of vacancies in the Council, the time and
conduct of elections to the Council, the swearing in of Councillors, oaths to be taken by
Councillors, and occasional elections to the Council.

### 9.

Powers and duties of Mayor and Deputy Mayor
(1) The Council shall have a Mayor and a Deputy Mayor.
(2) The Mayor shall be the Chairperson of the Council and as such shall preside at
every meeting of the Council at which he or she is present. He or she shall also have such

other powers and perform such other duties as are vested in him or her by this Act or as
may, from time to time, be assigned to him or her by the Council in accordance with this
Act.
(3) The Mayor shall have an original vote and in case of an equal division of votes on
any question before the Council, he or she shall have a second or casting vote.
(4) In the temporary absence or inability of the Mayor to perform his or her functions
through illness or otherwise, the Deputy Mayor shall perform those functions, including
the presiding at meetings of the Council. In the temporary absence or inability of both the
Mayor and the Deputy Mayor, such other elected member of the Council as may be
appointed by the Council for that purpose shall perform those functions.
(5) The Mayor or Deputy Mayor may be granted leave of absence by the Council for
any period not exceeding six (6) weeks in the aggregate in any calendar year in the case
of the Mayor and not exceeding four (4) weeks in the aggregate in any calendar year in
the case of the Deputy Mayor.

### 10.

Election and term of office of Mayor
(1) The Mayor shall be elected directly by the people in the Borough in accordance
with elections referred to in section 8 of this Act.
(2) The qualifications and disqualifications of a Mayor shall be the same as those
applying to an elected Councillor referred to in section 8 of this Act.
(3) The elections for Mayor shall be on the same day and at the same time as elections
for the elected Councillors.
(4) The term of office of the Mayor shall be four (4) years, to coincide with the term
of office of the elected Councillors, but the holder of the office shall be eligible for reelection:
Provided always that no person shall serve as Mayor for more than four (4) years in
succession:
Provided further that notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this subsection
contained, the Chairperson shall continue in office until his or her successor in office has
accepted office and taken and subscribed the required oath or affirmation of office.

### 11.

Election and term of office of Deputy Mayor
(1) At the meeting for the swearing in of the elected Councillors referred to in section
8 of this Act, and immediately after the elected Councillors take and subscribe the oath or
affirmation of office referred to in that section, the elected Councillors shall elect out of
their number a Deputy Mayor.
(2) A person who is a member of the Council by virtue of section 6(1)(iv) of this Act
shall be disqualified from taking part and voting in the electing of the Deputy Mayor, and
shall not be eligible to be elected Deputy Mayor.
(3) The term of office of the Deputy Mayor shall be two (2) years but the holder of
the office shall be eligible for re-election:
Provided always that no person shall serve as Deputy Mayor for more than four (4)
years in succession.

### 12.

Occasional vacancy in office of Mayor or Deputy Mayor
(1) An occasional vacancy in the office of the Mayor or Deputy Mayor shall be
deemed to have occurred under this section where—

(a)

The Mayor or Deputy Mayor dies, or ceases to be a member of the Council
or his or her office as member is declared to be vacant for disqualification or
otherwise.

(b)

The Mayor or Deputy Mayor notifies in writing to the Council and the
Minister through the Commissioner his or her resignation as Mayor or
Deputy Mayor;

(c)

The Mayor or Deputy Mayor is granted leave of absence by the Council;

(d)

The Mayor or Deputy Mayor departs from the State without leave from the
Council:
Provided that if the Council refuses such leave to the Mayor he or she may
apply to the Commissioner for such leave and it shall be competent for the
Commissioner to grant the Mayor such leave; or

(e)

The Mayor or Deputy Mayor is voted out of office by a majority of threefourths of all the elected members of the Council in the case of the Mayor or
by a majority of the elected members present and voting in the case of the
Deputy Mayor in either case on the ground that the respective number of
members have lost confidence in the Mayor or Deputy Mayor, as the case
may be, voting accordingly by secret ballot conducted in accordance with
subsection (6).

(2) In the event of the death, resignation, disqualification for any cause of the Mayor,
or other occasional vacancy in the office of Mayor, the Deputy Mayor shall forthwith
succeed to the office of Mayor and shall continue in such office until the time when the
Mayor whom he or she succeeds would have gone out of office, and he or she shall then
go out of office:
Provided that where the occasional vacancy arises on account of the Mayor being
granted leave of absence by the Council under section 9(5) of this Act, the Deputy Mayor
shall hold the office of Mayor only until such time as the Mayor who was granted leave
of absence by the Council has returned from such leave and has notified the Minister in
writing through the Commissioner that he or she has resumed the office of Mayor:
Provided further that if a vacancy arises in the office of Mayor for any reason
before the end of the third year of the term of the holder of that office, an election for
Mayor shall be held within forty-five days of the vacancy so arising, but meanwhile the
Deputy Mayor shall act as Mayor.
(3) The Mayor may resign his or her office as Mayor at any time by notifying the
Council and the Minister through the Commissioner that he or she has resigned as Mayor.
Such resignation letter must be sent within two (2) days of the resignation, and it takes
effect upon receipt by the Council.
(4) If the Mayor departs from Grenada without the leave of the Council, the Deputy
Mayor shall forthwith succeed to the office of Mayor in accordance with subsection (1).
(5) In the event of the death, resignation, disqualification for any cause of the Deputy
Mayor, or other occasional vacancy in the office of Deputy Mayor, another elected
member shall, in accordance with section 11 of this Act, be elected as Deputy Mayor, and
such newly elected Deputy Mayor shall hold office for the unexpired portion of the term
in relation to which the occasional vacancy has occurred:
Provided that where the occasional vacancy arises on account of the Deputy Mayor
being granted leave of absence by the Council under section 9(5) of this Act, such newly
elected Deputy Mayor shall hold the office of Deputy Mayor only until such time as the
Deputy Mayor who was granted leave of absence by the Council has returned from such
leave and has notified the Minister in writing through the Commissioner that he or she
has resumed the office of Deputy Mayor.

(6) The Council may remove the Mayor or Deputy Mayor if it has lost confidence in
him or her, as provided for in subsection (1)(e):
Provided that, subject to this section, the Council adheres to the relevant provisions
of Part VI of the District Councils Act, District 83A, in particular section 29(5) thereof:
and
Provided further that proceedings for the removal of the Mayor or Deputy Mayor
under this subsection shall be chaired by the Commissioner.

### 13.

If no nomination for Mayor
If there is no nomination for Mayor under section 10 of this Act, the elected
Councillors shall elect out of their number a Mayor, and section 11(1) and (2) of this Act
shall apply, mutatis mutandis, to such election.

### 14.

Oath to be taken by Mayor and Deputy Mayor
The Mayor and Deputy Mayor shall, before assuming office as such, take and
subscribe the oath or affirmation of office specified in section 87 of the District Councils
Act, Chapter 83A and applied, mutatis mutandis, by section 22(1) of this Act to the
Borough council established by and under this Act. The Mayor and Deputy Mayor shall
not assume office as such without taking and subscribing such oath or affirmation of
office.

### 15.

Mayor and Deputy Mayor to be Justices of the Peace
(1) The Mayor and Deputy Mayor shall be ex officio Justices of the Peace for the
State and, unless disqualified for re-election to their respective offices, shall continue to
be Justices of the Peace during the year next after they cease to be Mayor or Deputy
Mayor respectively.
(2) The disqualification referred to in subsection (1) of this section shall not include
disqualification due to ineligibility for re-election as Mayor or Deputy Mayor by reason
only of any person having served as Mayor or Deputy Mayor for four (4) successive
years.
(3) Before acting as Justices of the Peace, the Mayor an Deputy Mayor shall take the
oaths required by law to be taken by such justices unless, on the day on which they
become entitled to act as Mayor or Deputy Mayor, they have already taken the oaths
required by law to act as such justices.
(4) That an elected member of the Council serves temporarily as Mayor or Deputy
Mayor under section 9(4) of this Act does not entitle him or her to be an ex officio Justice
of the Peace under this section.

### 16.

Exemption from jury service
The Mayor, Deputy Mayor and other members of the Council shall, during their terms
of office, be exempted from jury service.

## PART IV

Powers and Functions of the Corporation

### 17.

General powers of administration
The Council is hereby authorised to take decisions and implement measures in relation
to the Borough to—

(i) improve and maintain roads;
(ii) administer sanitation services;
(iii) provide and maintain social services.

### 18.

Council entitled to provide certain educational facilities
The Council shall be entitled to provide certain educational facilities in the Borough
while education remains a responsibility of the Central Government. In particular, in
relation to the Borough, the Council shall be able to—
(i) provide educational facilities for children under five years of age;
(ii) provide scholarships.

### 19.

Borough Police Unit
(1) The Council may establish a Police Unit to serve the Borough and to be called the
St. George’s Municipal Borough Police Unit.
(2) Members of the said Borough Police Unit shall be eligible for transfer to the
Royal Grenada Police Force.
(3) The said Borough Police Unit shall have its own immediate commanding officer
in the Borough, but shall ultimately fall under the control of the Commissioner of Police
in the Royal Grenada Police Force.
(4) The services to be rendered by the said Borough Police Unit shall be paid for by
the Borough out of its own ordinary revenue.

### 20.

Council to have all the powers of a district council
In addition to the powers and functions vested in the Borough Council by sections 17,
18 and 19 of this Act, there are also hereby vested in the Borough Council all the rights,
powers, privileges and facilities enjoyable by a District Council by virtue of the District
Councils Act, Chapter 83A, especially Part VIII of that Act.

### 21.

Vesting control of Market Square in Corporation
(1) Without prejudice to the generality of section 20 of this Act, the Corporation shall
have the sole management, control and care of the Market Square, and shall maintain and
keep the same in good condition and may enclose the same with proper railings and gates,
and may drain, level, lay out, ornament or otherwise improve the same, and erect and
maintain buildings and also do all such works and things as may be requisite for the use
of the same as a public market, subject to any relevant town and country or other planning
legislation.
(2) In subsection (1) of this section, the expression “Market Square” means the public
market more particularly set out and described in the Schedule to this Act.

## PART V

Miscellaneous

### 22.

District Councils Act, 1995, applies to Borough Council mutatis mutandis
(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the District Councils Act, Chapter 83A, Act
shall apply mutatis mutandis to the Borough Council established by and under this Act as
if this Borough Council were a District Council. In particular, there shall thus apply to
this Borough Council, the provisions of the District Councils Act, Chapter 83A, regarding

procedural matters, sections 27 to 34; officers and staff, sections 35 to 42; the powers and
functions of the Council, sections 43 to 47; financial matters, sections 48 to 57; legal
proceedings, sections 58 to 80; the right of the public to attend meetings of the Council,
section 86; the oath or affirmation of office to be taken on assuming and office on the
Council, section 87; central government control of local government authorities, sections
88 to 91; and miscellaneous matters, sections 92 to 94.
(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1) of this section, sections 95 and 96 of the District
Councils Act, Chapter 83A, regarding repeals and commencement respectively shall not
apply to the Borough Council established by and under this Act.

### 23.

Repeal
The Saint George’s Market Square (Vesting) Act, No. 33 of 1965 is repealed.

### 24.

Commencement
This Act comes into force on the 5th day of April, 1995.

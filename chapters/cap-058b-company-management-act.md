## CHAPTER 58B

COMPANY MANAGEMENT ACT
An Act to make provision with respect to the licensing and regulation of companies
engaged in the management of other companies.
[Act No. 38 of 1996 amended by Act No. 11 of 2001, Act No. 2 of 2002.]

[10th July, 1997.]

## PART I

Preliminary

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Company Management Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
(1) In this Act—
“auditor” means a person who is in good standing as a member of an association of
chartered or public accountants or other similar body approved by the Minister as a
reputable auditing association;
“business of company management” means the provision of company management
services for profit or reward in or from within Grenada;
“Company management” means—
(a)

the incorporation of companies under the Companies Act, Chapter 58A, or
the International Companies Act, Chapter 152;

(b)

the provision of registered agent services for companies incorporated under
the International Companies Act;

(c)

the provision of registered office services for companies incorporated under
the Companies Act, 1994, or the International Companies Act;

(d)

the provision of directors or officers for companies; and

(e)

the provision of nominee shareholders of companies;

“financial year” means, in relation to a licensee under this Act—

the period not exceeding fifty-three weeks at the end of which the balance of
the licensee’s account is struck; or

if no such balance is stuck, or if a period in excess of fifty-three weeks is
used, then a calendar year;

“licensee” means a person holding a licence under this Act;
“Minister” means the Minister of Finance;
“Registrar” means the Registrar of offshore services referred to in section 3 of the
Offshore Banking Act, Chapter 217A.
(2) For the purpose of the Schedule and subject to subsection (5), a company is
deemed to be a subsidiary of another if—
(a)

that other either—
(i) is a member of it and controls the composition of its board of directors,
(ii) holds more than half in nominal value of its equity share capital; or

(b)

the first-mentioned company is a subsidiary of any company that is the
other’s subsidiary.

(3) For purposes of subsection (2), the composition of a company’s board of directors
is deemed to be controlled by another company if that other company by the exercise of
some power exercisable by it without the consent or concurrence of any other person can
appoint or remove the holders of all or a majority of the directorships.
(4) For the purposes of subsection (3), the other company is deemed to have power to
appoint to a directorship with respect to which any of the following conditions is
satisfied—
(a)

that a person cannot be appointed to it without the exercise in his or her
favour by the other company of such a power as is mentioned in
subsection (3);

(b)

that a person’s appointment to the directorship follows necessarily from his
or her appointment as a director of the other company; or

(c)

that the directorship is held by the other company itself or by a subsidiary of
it.

(5) In determining whether one company is a subsidiary of another—
(a)

any shares held or power exercisable by the other in a fiduciary capacity are
to be treated as not held or exercisable by it;

(b)

subject to paragraphs (c) and (d), any shares held or power exercisable—
(i) by any person as nominee for the other except whether the other is
concerned only in fiduciary capacity, or
(ii) by, or by a nominee for, a subsidiary of the other not being a
subsidiary which is concerned only in a fiduciary capacity,
are to be treated as held or exercisable by the other;

(c)

any shares held or power exercisable by any person by virtue of the
provisions of any debentures of the any issue of such debentures are to be
disregarded; and

(d)

any shares held or power exercisable by, or by a nominee for, the other or its
subsidiary, not being held or exercisable as mentioned in paragraph (c), are
to be treated as not held or exercisable by the other if the ordinary business
of the other or its subsidiary, as the case may be, includes the lending of
money and the shares are held or the power is exercisable as

abovementioned by way of security only for the purpose of a transaction
entered into in the ordinary course of that business.
(6) For purposes of the Schedule—

a company is deemed to be another’s holding company if the other is its
subsidiary; and

(b)

a body corporate is deemed the wholly-owned subsidiary of another if it has
no members except that other and that other’s wholly-owned subsidiaries and
its or their nominees.

### 3.

Non-application
This Act does not apply to a company licensed under the Offshore Banking Act,

## Chapter 217A.

## PART II

Licences

### 4.

Requirement of licence
Subject to section 3, no person shall carry on the business of company management in
or from within Grenada unless he or she has obtained a licence under this Act.

### 5.

Application for licence
(1) A licence is obtainable by making application therefor in the approved form
addressed to the Minister and shall contain the particulars set out in the Schedule and be
accompanied by the prescribed fee.
(2) If the Minister is satisfied that an application is in the public interest and that the
applicant is a fit and proper person to carry on the business of company management, he
or she may grant the application and issue to the applicant a licence subject to such terms
and conditions as appear to the Minister necessary.
(3) Any decision to refuse to grant a licence, other than a licence in respect of a
business in existence at the date of the commencement of this Act is final.
(4) The Minister shall cause notice of the grant of a licence under this Act to be
published in the Gazette.
(5) In this section, “approved form” means such form as the Minister approves.

### 6.

Duration of licence
A licence issued under this Act is valid for a period of twelve months from the date of
issue but may be renewed within two weeks after expiration on the payment of the
prescribed annual fee.

### 7.

Existing business
A person who at the date of the commencement of this Act is carrying on the business
of company management in or from within Grenada has a period of three months from
that date within which to comply with the provisions of this Part.

### 8.

Notification of change
(1) Where a change occurs in the particulars of a licensee, the licensee shall, as soon
as possible thereafter, inform the Registrar of the change.

(2) A licensee who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and is liable, on
summary conviction, to a fine of ten thousand dollars.

### 9.

Capitalisation
No licence shall be issued to a company the paid up share capital of which is less than
one hundred thousand dollars or such other amount prescribed by the Minister.

### 10.

Display of licence
A licence issued under this Act shall be prominently displayed on the premises where
the business of company management is carried on.

### 11.

Surrender of licence
(1) A licensee who has ceased to carry on the business in respect of which the licence
was granted may apply to the Minister to surrender his or her licence if the licensee—
(a)

produces evidence that he or she has repaid all deposits and has transferred
all assets held or administered on behalf of companies managed by him or
her; or

(b)

being a company, is being wound up voluntarily and produces evidence that
the company is solvent and is able on demand to repay all deposits held by it
and all its other creditors and has transferred all assets held or administered
on behalf of companies managed by it,

and the Minister may thereupon approve the surrender.
(2) In case of an application under subsection (1)(b), the Attorney-General may apply
to the court for an order that the licensee be wound up either by the court or be subject to
the supervision of the High Court, and where the court so orders the provisions of the
Companies Act, Chapter 58A, relating to the winding up of a company by or subject to
the supervision of the Court, mutatis mutandis, apply.

## PART III

Approval of Minister

### 12.

Transfer of shares
No shares or other interest, whether legal or equitable, in a company licensed under
this Act shall be issued, transferred or otherwise disposed of without the prior written
approval of the Minister, except that the Minister may exempt any company from the
provisions of this section, subject to such terms and conditions as the Minister thinks fit.

### 12A.

Appointment of Directors
No appointment of a director of a company licensed under this Act may be made
without the prior written approval of the Minister.

## PART IV

Administration

### 13.

Registrar

(1) The Registrar shall be responsible for ensuring the proper administration of this
Act.
(2) The functions of the Registrar include—

the review of company management in Grenada;

where he or she thinks fit or when required by the Minister, the examination in
such manner as he or she considers necessary of the affairs or business of
any licensee for the purpose of satisfying himself or herself that all
provisions of this Act are being complied with and that the licensee is in a
sound financial position and is carrying on its business in a satisfactory
manner;

(c)

reporting to the Minister regarding the examination of any documents
produced to the Registrar in the course of the performance of his or her
functions; and

(d)

examining and making recommendations to the Minister with respect to all
applications for licences.

(3) In the performance of his or her duties under the Act and subject to the provisions
of section 19, the Registrar may at all reasonable times—
(a)

require a licensee to produce for examination such books, records and other
documents that the licensee is required to maintain pursuant to section 16;
and

(b)

require a licensee to supply such information or explanation,

as the Registrar may reasonably require for the purpose of enabling him or her to perform
his or her functions under this Act.
(4) Notwithstanding subsection (3), the Registrar is not entitled to have access to a
company managed by a licensee or to any information, matter or thing relating to or
concerning the affairs of any such company without first having obtained—
(a)

the written consent of that company; or

(b)

an order of the court made on the ground that there are no other reasonable
means of obtaining such document, matter or thing.

(5) The Registrar, with the written approval of the Minister, may authorise in writing
any person to assist him or her in the performance of his or her functions under this Act.

### 14.

Restrictions in use of certain terms
(1) No person other than a licensee shall—
(a)

use any word, either in English or in any other language, in the description
or title under which he or she carries on business in or from within Grenada
that, in the opinion of the Minister, suggests the business of company
management; or

(b)

make any representation in any document or in any other manner that is
likely to suggest that he or she is carrying on the business of company
management.

(2) The Minister may require a licensee who carries on the business of company
management under a name which is—
(a)

identical with that of any other person, whether within or outside Grenada,
or which so nearly resembles that name as to be calculated to deceive;

calculated to suggest falsely the patronage of or connection with some
person whether within or outside Grenada;

(c)

calculated to suggest falsely that he or she has special status in relation to or
derived from the Government or has the official approval of, or acts on
behalf of, the Government or of any of its departments or officials,

forthwith to change his or her name and in default of compliance may revoke his or her
licence.

### 15.

Duties of licensee
(1) A licensee shall maintain in respect of his or her business of company
management—
(a)

such books and records as shall accurately reflect the business of the licensee;

(b)

separate accounts in his or her books for each company the licensee manages
and shall separate the funds and other property of every such company from
his or her own; and

(c)

one or more separate bank accounts into which he or she shall deposit all
monies held by him or her on behalf of each company he or she manages.

(2) A licensee must know the identity of all the beneficial owners of every company
that it manages.
(3) A licensee must notify the Authority when it ceases to manage any company
within fifteen days of ceasing to manage it.

### 16.

Annual returns, etc.
(1) A licensee shall within six months of the end of the financial year—
(a)

prepare annual accounts in accordance with generally accepted accounting
principles, audited by an independent auditor;

(b)

furnish to the Registrar the annual accounts together with written
confirmation from an independent auditor that the annual accounts have
been prepared as required under paragraph (a) hereof and whether or not the
auditor’s certificate for such accounts is unqualified and if qualified, the
nature of the qualification;

(c)

furnish to the Registrar a certificate issued by an independent auditor that the
information set out in the application for a licence, as modified by any
subsequent notification of change in accordance with section 8, remains
correct and gives an accurate summary of the business of the licensee; and

(d)

furnish to the Authority a certificate (“Certificate of Compliance”) to the
effect that every company it manages has in respect of the financial year
complied with the provisions of all relevant legislation governing the
operations of the company.

(2) A licensee shall, at any time, if required by the Registrar furnish to the Registrar a
Certificate of Compliance issued in accordance with subsection (1)(c).
(3) A licensee shall, at any time, if required by the Registrar produce to the Registrar
such evidence as may be required as to the solvency or otherwise of his or her business
and that the licensee has, in the case of a company, maintained the required minimum
paid up share capital.

### 17.

Power of search
(1) If a judge or a magistrate is satisfied by information on oath, whether oral or
written, given by the Registrar or a person authorised by the Registrar—

that a licence has been suspended under section 20;

that there is reasonable ground for suspecting that an offence against this Act
or the Money Laundering (Prevention) Act, 1999, has been or is being
committed and that evidence of the commission of the offence is to be found
at any premises or in any vehicle, vessel or aircraft specified in the
information; or

(c)

that any books, records or documents that ought to have been produced
under section 13(3) and have not been produced are to be found at any
premises or in any vehicle, vessel or aircraft specified in the information,

he or she may grant a search warrant authorising the Registrar, together with any other
person named in the warrant, to enter such premises or any premises at any such time
within one month from the date of the warrant, and to search the premises or the vehicle,
vessel or aircraft.
(2) The Registrar or any other person authorised by a warrant to search any premises
or any vehicle, vessel or aircraft may search every person who is found in or who he or
she has reasonable grounds to believe to have recently left or to be about to enter those
premises, or that vehicle, vessel or aircraft, and may seize any document, cash or security
found in the premises, or in the vehicle, vessel or aircraft which he or she has reasonable
grounds for believing ought to have been produced under section 13(3), except that no
female shall, in pursuance of any warrant issued under this section, be searched except by
a female.
(3) Where by virtue of this section a person has any power to enter any premises or a
vehicle, vessel or aircraft, he or she may use such force as is reasonably necessary for the
purpose of exercising that power.

## PART V

Miscellaneous

### 18.

Insurance policy
The Registrar may require a licensee to effect a policy of insurance with an approved
insurance company against—
(a)

losses arising out of claims of negligence or breach of duty by the licensee or
by any employee;

(b)

the dishonesty of employees or of the licensee;

(c)

loss of documents; and

(d)

such other risks as the Registrar may stipulate,

in such amount and of such a nature as the Registrar may determine to be fit and proper,
having due regard to the nature and type of business carried on by the licensee; and, in the
event that the insurance is withdrawn, cancelled or not renewed, the licensee shall
immediately notify the Registrar and shall cease to carry on his or her business until the
insurance has been re-instated or replaced.

### 19.

Secrecy
(1) Except for the purpose of the performance or exercise of his or her duties or
functions under this Act or when lawfully required to do so by the court or under the
provisions of any other law, neither the Registrar nor any person acting under his or her
authority shall disclose any information relating to any application under the provisions
of this Act, or to the affairs of a licensee or of a company managed by a licensee, which
he or she has acquired in the performance or exercise of such duties or functions.

(2) Any person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and is liable, on
summary conviction, to a fine of ten thousand dollars or imprisonment for one year, or
both.

### 20.

Suspension of licence
(1) Where the Minister is of the opinion that a licensee is—

carrying on the business of company management in a manner detrimental to
the public interest, or to the interest of the companies being managed by him
or her or any of them; or

(b)

in contravention of this Act or any other law,

the Minister may require him or her forthwith to take such steps as may be necessary to
rectify the matter, and may forthwith suspend the licence pending the receipt of
information or any explanation under section 13(3)(b).
(2) A suspension shall not exceed a period of thirty days, unless extended from time
to time, by an order of the court on application of the Registrar on the grounds that it is in
the public interest that the suspension continue and specifying the duration of such period
of further suspension, which shall not itself exceed thirty days each at any one time.

### 21.

Revocation of licence
The Minister may revoke a licence—
(a)

if the licensee has ceased to carry on the business of company management;

(b)

if the licensee—
(i) becomes bankrupt,
(ii) goes into liquidation,
(iii) is wound up,
(iv) is otherwise dissolved; or

(c)

if having received a report from the Registrar regarding the licensee under
section 13(2)(b), he or she is of the opinion that it would be detrimental to
the public interest for the licensee to continue to carry on the business of
company management.

### 22.

Offences
(1) Any person who with intent to deceive, makes any representation or provides any
information for the purpose of this Act which he or she knows to be false or does not
believe to be true commits an offence and shall be liable, on summary conviction, to a
fine of fifty thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year, or
both.
(2) A person who, by any act or omission contravenes any provision or requirement
of this Act, commits an offence and shall be liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of
fifty thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year, or both.
(3) Any licensee who advertises inviting either directly or indirectly other parties to
commit breaches of the laws of the country in which such advertisement appears or to
which such advertisement is directed commits an offence and shall be liable, on summary
conviction, to a fine of twenty thousand dollars or to a term of imprisonment not
exceeding six months, or both.
(4) Where a company is convicted of an offence under subsection (2) or (3), every
director and every officer concerned with the management of the company is also liable
unless he or she satisfies the court that the offence was committed without his or her

knowledge or consent or that he or she took all reasonable steps to prevent the commission
of the offence.
(5) Any person who—

assaults or obstruct the Authority or other person in the performance of his
or her functions under this Act;

(b)

uses any insulting language to the Registrar or any other person in the
performance of his or her functions under this Act;

(c)

by the offer of any gratuity, bribe or other inducement prevents or attempts
to prevent the Registrar or any other person from performing his or her
function under this Act,

commits an offence and is liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of ten thousand dollars
or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year, or both.

### 23.

Appeals
(1) An appeal lies to the High Court from any decision of the Minister—
(a)

revoking a licence under section 14(2) or 21; or

(b)

suspending a licence or requiring a licensee to take certain steps under
section 20(1).

(2) An appeal against a decision of the Minister shall not operate as a suspension of
the decision of the Minister.

### 24.

Immunity
No liability attaches to the Minister, the Registrar or any person acting under the
authority of the Minister or the Registrar for an act done in good faith in the discharge or
purported discharge of his or her functions under this Act.

### 25.

Regulations
The Minister may make regulations generally for giving effect to the provisions of this
Act and specifically—
(a)

prescribing any thing by this Act authorised or required to be prescribed; and

(b)

exempting any person or business, or class of person or business, from any
provision of this Act.

### 26.

Directions
Without limiting or affecting section 25, the Registrar may, from time to time, by
notice in the Gazette, give directions in relation to such matters as he or she may think fit
and appropriate and failure by any licensee to comply with the directions shall be taken
into consideration when any action is proposed to be taken under section 20 or 21.

### 27.

Commencement
This Act shall come into operation on such date as the Minister determines by order.

### 28.

Jurisdiction of Magistrate
A magistrate has jurisdiction with respect to offences under this Act notwithstanding
any limitation on a magistrate’s jurisdiction contained in any other Act.

Schedule
Particulars to be Specified in Applications
[Section 5(1).]

Every person or company applying for a licence under this Act shall furnish in
writing to the Minister the following particulars which shall accompany the
application—
(1) Name of applicant.
(2) Address in Grenada of principal office of the applicant and, in the case of a
company, its registered office.
(3) In the case of a company, the names and addresses of all directors.
(4) In the case of a company, the names and addresses of all shareholders.
(5) In the case of a partnership, the names and addresses of all partners.
(6) In the case of a company, the names and addresses of all officers and managers.
(7) Names and addresses of attorneys, if any, to the applicant, together with a letter
from the attorneys confirming that they act for the applicant.
(8) Names and addresses of auditors of the applicant, together with a letter from
auditors confirming that they act for the applicant.
(9) In the case of a company—
(a)

the names and address of one of its officers who is the authorised agent in
Grenada to accept on behalf of the applicant service of process and any
notices required to be served on it; and

(b)

the names and address of another of its officers who in the absence or
inability to act of the officer named in subparagraph (a) is the authorised
agent in Grenada of the applicant for the purposes of subparagraph (a).

(10) In the case of a company, evidence of the proper incorporation of the company in
Grenada or in the country of incorporation, as the case may be.
(11) Evidence in writing that the applicant himself or herself or some person or
company directly or indirectly connected with the applicant is possessed of appropriate
experience in company management.
(12) Repealed.
(13) Repealed.
(14) Character references in writing, together with such other evidence as the Minister
may require, that neither the applicant nor, if it is any applicant company, any director or
officer of the company has a criminal record either in Grenada or elsewhere.
(15) Annual accounts of its holding company, if any, for the preceding three years
and thereafter annually duly audited and certified by an independent auditor.
(16) Names of all subsidiary companies of the applicant with addresses of their
registered offices.
(17) In the case of a company or partnership, a copy of the act, charter, certificate of
incorporation or memorandum and articles of association or partnership agreement of the
applicant, as may be appropriate, verified by an affidavit sworn by a director, or partner
and duly authenticated as follows—

in the case of a company incorporated in Grenada, notarised; and

in the case of a foreign company, certified and authenticated under the public
seal of the country, city or place under the laws of which such company has
incorporated.

(18) References, including one from a bank or trust company.
(19) Statement of assets and liabilities at the end of the month prior to the submission
of the application certified by a director or senior officer.
(20) Statement of capital of any other company held, directly or through a subsidiary,
as an asset of the applicant.

## CHAPTER 176

LOCAL SPIRITUAL ASSEMBLIES OF THE BAHA’IS (INCORPORATION)
ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of certain Local Spiritual Assemblies of the
Baha’is of Grenada.
[Act No. 82 of 1979.]
[2nd November, 1979.]

1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Local Spiritual Assemblies of the Baha’is (Incorporation)
Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act, “Local Assembly” means a Local Spiritual Assembly incorporated under
section 3(1), or deemed incorporated under section 8.
3\. Incorporation of Local Spiritual Assemblies
(1) (a) The individuals listed in Part I of the Schedule and their successors in office
shall be a body corporate known as the “Local Spiritual Assembly for the Parish of Saint
George”.
(b) The individuals listed in Part II of the Schedule and their successors in office
shall be a body corporate known as the “Local Spiritual Assembly for the Parish of Saint
Andrew”.

(c) The individuals listed in Part III of the Schedule and their successors in office
shall be a body corporate known as the “Local Spiritual Assembly for the Parish of Saint
David”.
(2) A Local Assembly shall, in its corporate name, have perpetual succession and a
Common Seal.
4\. Power of Local Assembly to acquire property
A Local Assembly shall have power to acquire for its local community by purchase,
transfer, exchange, devise, grant, gift, bequest or otherwise any land or other property of
whatever kind whether in perpetuity or for life or for a term of years and any money,
securities, goods or chattels whatever.
5\. Power of Local Assembly to dispose of property, and to borrow
A Local Assembly shall have power—
(a)

by deed under the Common Seal, to sell, demise, grant, convey, transfer,
exchange, surrender or otherwise dispose of any real or personal property
vested in or which may hereafter become vested in it;

(b)

to borrow money upon the security of any such property held by it and, for that
purpose, a mortgage or other similar obligation and every document relating
thereto shall be sealed with the Common Seal of that Local Assembly.

6\. Affixing of Common Seal
All documents requiring the Common Seal shall be sealed in the presence of at least
two members of the Local Assembly concerned and they shall sign every such document.
7\. Address for service, etc.
The name and address of each person elected annually to a Local Assembly shall be
notified to and registered with the Registrar of Companies, and each Local Assembly
shall at all times have a fixed address in Grenada for the service of documents on it and
such address and any change thereof shall be similarly registered.
8\. Governor-General may make Order appointing individuals to other
Local Assemblies, etc.
If satisfied that any similar assembly has been established for the Parish of Saint
Patrick, or Saint John, or Saint Mark, or Carriacou, and that individuals have been
willingly and duly elected to serve in that assembly, the Governor-General may, by
Order, appoint those individuals to serve in that assembly and thereupon those individuals
and their successors in office shall be deemed to have been incorporated under this Act as
the Local Spiritual Assembly of the Parish concerned and all the provisions of this Act
shall apply to that Local Assembly.

## CHAPTER 146

IMPORTATION OF FISH (REGULATION) ACT
An Act to regulate the importation of live fish.
[22nd September, 1951.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Importation of Fish (Regulation) Act.

### 2.

Permission of Minister necessary
No person shall import any live fish without the prior permission in writing of the
Minister.

### 3.

Importation of certain fish absolutely prohibited
No permission under the foregoing section shall be given in respect of the importation
of—
(a)

black bass;

(b)

carp (all varieties); and

(c)

goldfish (all varieties).

### 4.

Penalty
Any person who imports live fish contrary to the provisions of this Act shall be guilty
of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of two thousand dollars, and
any fish imported in contravention of this Act shall be forfeited.

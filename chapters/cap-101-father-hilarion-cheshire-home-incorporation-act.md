## CHAPTER 101

FATHER HILARION (CHESHIRE) HOME (INCORPORATION) ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of, and the vesting of property in, the
Incorporated Trustees of the Father Hilarion (Cheshire) Home, and for matters
connected therewith.
[Act No. 57 of 1980.]

[28th November, 1980.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Father Hilarion (Cheshire) Home (Incorporation) Act.
2\. Incorporation of trustees
The Roman Catholic Bishop in Grenada, Father W. Mallighan, Father Patrick
McCormack, Carlyle John, Jerome Forde, Joachim St.John, Christian St.Louis, Doctor
Rupert Japal, Excel Roberts, Stanley Belfon, Mrs. Patrice Robertson, Windom Robertson
and their successors in office as trustees of the Father Hilarion (Cheshire) Home
(hereinafter called “the Home”) shall be a body corporate known as The Incorporated
Trustees of the Father Hilarion (Cheshire) Home (hereinafter called “the Trustees”) with
perpetual succession and a Common Seal and with power to sue and be sued in all courts.
3\. Vesting of property
The lands and buildings described in the Schedule together with the monies and
personal property hitherto vested in the Roman Catholic Bishop in Grenada for the uses
of the Home, and all lands, buildings, monies and personal property which may hereafter
be acquired or appropriated to the uses of the Home, are hereby vested in the Trustees.
4\. Power of Trustees to acquire property

The Trustees shall have power to acquire for the Home by purchase, devise, grant, gift
or otherwise any land or other property of whatever kind whether in perpetuity or for life
or for a term of years and any money, goods or chattels whatever, to take, hold and enjoy
the same subject to the performance of any trust upon which the same may have been
acquired.
5\. Power of Trustees to dispose of property, and to borrow, etc.
Subject to the performance of any trust as aforesaid, the Trustees may—
(a)

sell, demise, grant, convey, transfer, alienate or otherwise dispose of any real
or personal property vested in or which may hereafter be acquired by or
become vested in the Trustees;

(b)

borrow money and, for the payment of the same, by deed under the Common
Seal give and execute mortgages, charges, pledges and other securities of
any real or personal property or make any arrangement that they may
consider convenient with such covenants, powers and provisions as they
think proper.

6\. Affixing of Common Seal
All documents requiring the Common Seal shall be sealed in the presence of at least
two of the Trustees who shall sign every such document.
7\. Address for service, etc.
(1) The Trustees shall at all times have a fixed address in Grenada for the service of
documents on them and such address and any change thereof shall be registered with the
Registrar of Companies.
(2) Any document may be served on the Trustees by leaving the document at, or by
sending it by registered post to, the address registered pursuant to subsection (1).

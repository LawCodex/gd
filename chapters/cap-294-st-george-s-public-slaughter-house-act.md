## CHAPTER 294

ST. GEORGE'S PUBLIC SLAUGHTER-HOUSE ACT
An Act for the establishment of a public slaughter-house in the town of St. George
[15th June, 1901.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the St. George's Public Slaughter-House Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“animal” means bull, cow, calf, ox, ram, ewe, whether, lamb, goat, kid, or swine;
“Authority” means the person for the time being responsible for matters relating to
local government;
“butcher’s meat” means the meat of an animal slaughtered for sale;
“town” means the town of St. George.

### 3.

Public slaughter-house
The slaughter-house situate in Melville street in the town of St. George shall be used
as the public slaughter-house of the town.

### 4.

Power of Authority to make by-laws
It shall be lawful for the Authority to make by-laws for the opening and closing of
such slaughter-house, or any licensed slaughter-house, the times at which animals shall be
brought into the lair or lairs and slaughtered, and the carcasses deliverable; for the
inspection and examination of any animal so brought, or the carcass of any animal so
slaughtered; for keeping such public slaughter-house or any licensed slaughter-house, lair
or lairs, or other conveniences for such public slaughter-house, or any licensed slaughterhouse in a cleanly and proper state; for preventing nuisances and obstructions in such
public slaughter-house, or any licensed slaughter-house, lair or lairs, or other places used
in connection with such slaughter-house, or any licensed slaughter-house; and generally
for regulating, ordering and governing such public slaughter-house, or any licensed
slaughter-house, lair or lairs, or other places used in connection therewith, and the
conduct of business therein. Such by-laws may provide for the forfeiture and disposal of
all meat dealt with contrary to the provisions of this Act or any by-law made thereunder.
And the Authority may from time to time alter or repeal any such by-laws, and may by
such by-laws impose such reasonable fines as they may think fit not exceeding five
hundred dollars for each breach of such by-laws:
Provided that no by-law made by the Authority shall be of any force or effect until
the same shall have been approved by the Minister and published in the Gazette.

### 5.

Provision for lairs, etc.
The Authority shall provide a lair or lairs, and other conveniences for the purpose of
such public slaughter-house.

### 6.

Temporary arrangements if necessary
In future cases of necessity the Authority may at any time take premises on rent for the
purpose of a temporary public slaughter-house and all the provisions of this Act and of
the by-laws made thereunder shall apply to the same.

### 7.

Penalty for slaughtering elsewhere than at public slaughter-house
No person shall slaughter any animal, or dress any carcass for sale as human food in
any place within the town or within one half mile from the limits thereof other than at
such public slaughter-house, or in a place licensed for the slaughter of animals as
hereinafter provided. And no person shall offer for sale as human food any carcass or part
of a carcass of any animal not slaughtered in such public slaughter-house or in a place
licensed for the slaughter of animals as hereinafter provided. A person who contravenes
any of the provisions of this section shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of
five hundred dollars.

### 8.

Power of Authority to fix tariff of fees
The Authority shall, with the approval of the Minister, fix a table of fees to be
demanded and taken for or in respect of every animal to be slaughtered in such public
slaughter-house, and for the use of all pens, lairs and other conveniences, and may from
time to time with such approval as aforesaid vary such table of fees, as they may see fit.
Such table of fees when so approved shall be published in the Gazette. All fees so fixed
shall be paid to the market clerk or other officer of the Authority as may be appointed to
receive them (and such fees shall be for the use of the Authority) before delivery of the
dressed carcass or carcasses of the animal or animals so slaughtered. And if the owner
shall neglect to pay such fees within two hours after such carcass or carcasses shall be
ready for delivery the carcass or carcasses shall be sold by auction under the direction of
the Authority and the proceeds derived from such sale shall, after deducting the fees due
thereon together with the expenses of the sale, be paid to the owner of such carcass or
carcasses.

### 9.

Power to seize meat unfit for human food
In case the market clerk or such other officer or officers appointed by the Authority to
inspect and examine any animal brought to be slaughtered, or the carcass of any animal
so slaughtered, shall find any animal, or the carcass or part of the carcass of any animal,
which in his, her or their opinion appears unfit for human food, he, she or they shall seize
the same and immediately give notice of such seizure to the owner thereof or his or her
agent, and at the expiration of one hour after such notice if no question shall have been
raised by such owner or his or her agent whether the meat so seized is unfit for human
food, cause it to be destroyed or taken to sea and sunk. If any question shall arise whether
the meat so seized is unfit for human food, the market clerk or any other such officer shall
call in a medical officer to examine the meat so seized, and to give his or her decision
thereon, and shall act in accordance with such decision which shall be final.

### 10.

Expenses of inquiry into fitness of meat
The expenses attendant on such inquiry shall, if the decision is adverse to the owner of
the meat, be paid by such owner and shall be recovered from him or her in a summary
manner before the magistrate of the Southern District on the complaint of the Authority
and shall be paid in to the credit of the funds of the Authority:
Provided that such expenses shall not be paid by such owner if the medical officer
shall certify that the question as to the fitness of the meat for human food was not
frivolously or vexatiously raised.

### 11.

Licence to use premises for sale of butcher’s meat
The Minister may, if satisfied that it is in the public interest to do so, license any
person, annually, upon such terms and conditions as may from time to time be settled by
him or her, to use any premises in the town, or within one half mile from the boundaries
thereof, for the slaughter of animals for butcher’s meat.

### 12.

Duration of licence
Every licence or renewed licence granted under the last preceding section shall
continue in force up to (and inclusive of) the fifteenth day of January in the year next
succeeding the year in which it was issued, and shall authorise the person therein named
to use the premises therein described as a place for the slaughter of animals for butcher’s
meat.

### 13.

Inspection of premises
The Minister may cause the premises in respect of which a licence is applied for to be
inspected by such person or persons as may be deemed fit to ascertain whether such
premises are suitable for use as a place for the slaughter of animals.

### 14.

Free summonses, etc., to issue
The magistrate of the Southern District shall issue all summonses, writs or other
necessary legal documents required by the Authority free of all costs or charge.

### 15.

Prosecution of offences
(1) A prosecution for any offence committed against this Act, or any by-law, shall be
commenced in the name and at the instance of the Authority and tried summarily. And all
fines when recovered shall be paid to the Authority.
(2) No costs shall be imposed on the Authority in such a prosecution unless the court
is of the opinion that the complaint should be dismissed as frivolous and vexatious.

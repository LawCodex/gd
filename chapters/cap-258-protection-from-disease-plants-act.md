## CHAPTER 258

PROTECTION FROM DISEASE (PLANTS) ACT
An Act to provide for the eradication of plant disease.
[5th October, 1925.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Protection from Disease (Plants) Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“infectious plant disease” means a plant disease declared to be an infectious plant
disease under this Act;
“notifiable plant disease” means a plant disease declared to be a notifiable plant
disease under this Act;
“plant” includes tree, plant, root, herb or grass, or part thereof respectively;
“plant disease” includes any condition conducive to the deterioration or destruction
of a plant or part of a plant whether such deterioration or destruction be due to disease,
insects, blight, fungus or any other cause and whether communicable or not.

### 3.

Order as to plant diseases

(1) The Minister may by Order—
(a)

declare any district, area or parcel of land described in the Order to be infected
with plant disease or suspected of being infected with plant disease;

(b)

prescribe the isolation by means of fences of any description, ditches or
otherwise of any district, area or parcel of land or of any portion of such
district, area, or parcel of land, which shall by the same or any other Order
have been declared to be a district, area or parcel of land infected or
suspected of being infected with plant disease;

(c)

prescribe and regulate the destruction or removal, uprooting, disposal or
treatment of plants and products of a vegetable nature within a district, area
or parcel of land declared by the same or any other Order to be infected or
suspected of being infected with plant disease;

(d)

prescribe and regulate the cleansing and disinfecting of any district, area or
parcel of land declared by the same or any other Order to be infected or
suspected of being infected with plant disease together with any plants
thereon;

(e)

prescribe the period within which it shall not be lawful to plant or replant
with any plant whatsoever or with any particular plant named in the Order
the whole or any portion of any district, area or parcel of land declared by
the same or any other Order to be infected or to be suspected of being
infected with plant disease;

(f)

prohibit or regulate the movement or dispatch of persons and animals and
the removal and carriage of earth, soil, manure, vegetable products or other
things in, into or out of a district, area or parcel of land declared by the same
or any other Order to be infected or to be suspected of being infected with
plant disease;

(g)

regulate the duties of persons appointed to carry out the provisions of any
Orders made under this Act;

(h)

declare any plant disease to be a notifiable plant disease;

(i)

declare any plant disease to be an infectious plant disease;

(j)

prescribe the measures to be taken for the treatment of any notifiable plant
disease or any infectious plant disease by the owner, occupier or person
having the charge or management of any land whether the land shall or shall
not have been declared to be infected or suspected of being infected with
plant disease; and

(k)

generally, make provision for the purpose of preventing the spread of plant
disease or of any particular plant disease named in the Order.

(2) The Minister may at any time revoke or vary an Order or any part of an Order
made under this Act.
(3) An Order made under this Act shall be of the same effect as if it were contained in
this Act and shall be judicially noticed.
(4) An Order made under this Act shall be laid before the House of Representatives as
soon as may be after the making thereof; and if the House by resolution requests that the
Order be rescinded, the Order shall be rescinded by the Minister but without prejudice to
the validity of anything previously done thereunder or the making of a new Order.

### 4.

Appointment of officers
It shall be lawful for the Minister to appoint officers to carry out the provisions of this
Act and of Orders made thereunder, and the Minister may by warrant direct payment out
of the Treasury of the remuneration and expenses of all such officers and of any other

expenditure that may in the discretion of the Minister be deemed to be necessary for the
purposes of this Act.

### 5.

Power of entry on land
(1) A person appointed under the provisions of section 4 may, with such assistance as
may be necessary, enter upon any land whatsoever (whether the same shall or shall not
have been declared to be infected or suspected of being infected with plant disease) and
there examine any plant, article or thing and dig up the ground and fell, lop, dig up and
take away any suspected or infected plant, article or thing, and do all such other acts and
things as may be expedient in order the more effectually to ascertain whether the said
land or any plant thereon is infected with plant disease and may search for any plant
disease and do any act or thing whatsoever in order to give effect to an Order made under
the provisions of this Act and may with the approval of the Chief Technical Officer
(Extension) destroy the causes, to be determined by the Chief Technical Officer
(Extension), of any plant disease.
(2) For the purposes of this section a person so appointed as aforesaid shall have
power to pass over any adjoining or intervening lands.

### 6.

Notice by owner or occupier of existence of notifiable plant disease, and
presumption of knowledge of such disease
(1) Every owner or occupier and every person having the charge or management of
land who knows or suspects the existence of any notifiable plant disease on the land of
which he or she is owner or occupier or has the charge or management shall with all
practicable speed give notice in writing to the Chief Technical Officer (Extension) of the
fact of the land or of any plant thereon being so infected or suspected and shall in such
notice give all information in his or her power as to the extent and nature of the disease.
The said notice shall be served personally on the Chief Technical Officer (Extension) or
shall be addressed to him or her by registered post.
(2) Where the occupier of or the person having the charge or management of land is
charged with any act done in contravention of this section he or she shall be presumed to
have known of the existence of the disease, unless and until he or she shows to the
satisfaction of the court that he or she did not have knowledge thereof, and could not with
reasonable diligence have obtained such knowledge.

### 7.

Power of Chief Technical Officer (Extension) to carry out measures prescribed
by Orders
If the owner or occupier or person having the charge or management of land fails to
carry out any measures required to be carried out by him or her under an Order made
under this Act, the Chief Technical Officer (Extension) or a person authorised by him or
her in writing may enter on such land and may carry out the measures required to be
carried out under the Order and the cost of carrying out such measures shall be
recoverable from the owner, occupier or person, as the case may be, at the suit of the
Chief Technical Officer (Extension), or any person authorised by him or her in writing, as
a civil debt before the magistrate for the district in which such land is situate.

### 8.

Offences and penalties
If a person without lawful authority or excuse, proof whereof shall lie on him or her,
does any of the following things he or she shall be guilty of an offence—
(a)

if he or she does anything in contravention of this Act or of an Order made
under this Act or of rules made under this Act, or fails to carry out any
measures required to be carried out by him or her under any such Order or
rules;

(b)

if he or she fails to give any notice which by this Act he or she is required to
give;

(c)

if he or she refuses, to any person acting in execution of this Act or of an
Order made thereunder, admission to any land or place which the said person
is entitled to enter or examine, or obstructs or impedes such person in so
entering or examining or otherwise in any respect obstructs or impedes a
person in the execution of his or her duty under this Act or assists in any
such obstructing or impeding,

and he or she shall, on summary conviction, be liable to a fine of three thousand dollars
and in default of payment to imprisonment for three months, and on any further
conviction within a period of twelve months, for a second or subsequent such offence he
or she shall be liable in the discretion of the court to be imprisoned for six months in lieu
of the fine to which he or she is liable—
(i) if he or she does anything in contravention of this Act or of an Order
made under this Act or of rules made under this Act, or fails to carry
out any measures required to be carried out by him or her under any
such Order or rules,
(ii) if he or she fails to give any notice which by this Act he or she is
required to give,
(iii) if he or she refuses, to any person acting in execution of this Act or of
an Order made thereunder, admission to any land or place which the
said person is entitled to enter or examine, or obstructs or impedes
such person in so entering or examining or otherwise in any respect
obstructs or impedes a person in the execution of his or her duty under
this Act or assists in any such obstructing or impeding,
and he or she shall, on summary conviction, be liable to a fine of three thousand dollars and
in default of payment to imprisonment for three months, and on any further conviction
within a period of twelve months for a second or subsequent such offence he or she shall
be liable in the discretion of the court to be imprisoned for six months in lieu of the fine
to which he or she is liable.

### 9.

Provisions as to acquisition of land
(1) Whenever by Order made under this Act a parcel of land has been declared to be
infected with plant disease it shall be lawful for the Governor-General with the approval
of the House of Representatives, by Proclamation to be published in the Gazette, to
declare that the land or any portion thereof, and also any land which is required for the
purpose of ingress, egress and regress to and from the said land or for the purpose of
diverting any roads or paths or any parts thereof adjoining or passing through or near the
said land, has been acquired and thereupon the land described in such Proclamation shall
vest in the Governor-General for the public uses of Grenada.
(2) The owner of any land acquired under this section shall be entitled to receive from
the Government the value of such land and such value shall be determined under and in
accordance with the provisions of the Land Acquisition Act, Chapter 159, in the same
manner as the purchase money of selected land is determined under that Act.
(3) For the purposes of this section, “owner” has the same meaning as in the Land
Acquisition Act, Chapter 159.
(4) Any rent due in respect of land acquired under this section shall be dealt with as if
it had accrued from day to day and shall be paid by the tenant to the person entitled to
receive the same up to the day of the date of the Proclamation referred to in subsection (1)
and shall thereafter cease.

(5) If any right of way exists over land acquired under subsection (1) the GovernorGeneral with the approval of the House of Representatives may by Proclamation to be
published in the Gazette declare the same or any of them to be determined and thereupon
such rights of way as are described in the Proclamation shall cease and determine.
(6) The Governor-General, if satisfied that any land acquired under subsection (1) is
no longer infected with plant disease, may by Proclamation published in the Gazette
restore, to an owner receiving the value of such land as aforesaid or to any person
claiming by, from, through or under such owner, any land so acquired, upon repayment
by such owner or person of the amount paid as such value, and thereupon the land
described in the Proclamation shall vest in the person named in the Proclamation for such
estates and interests as are set forth in the Proclamation.
(7) The Minister shall cause to be forwarded a copy of every Proclamation issued
under this section to the Registrar of the Supreme Court for registration in the Deeds and
Land Registry and the Registrar shall, without fee, register each such Proclamation.
(8) The Chief Technical Officer (Extension) may with the approval of the Minister
take possession of and hold for a period not exceeding five years any parcel of land
declared by an Order made under this Act to be infected with plant disease and shall in
such case pay monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly as the Chief Technical Officer
(Extension) may determine, rent to the person for the time being entitled to receive the
same at a rate to be determined by the Chief Technical Officer (Extension) not exceeding
five dollars per annum per acre:
Provided that any parcel of land less than one acre may in the discretion of the Chief
Technical Officer (Extension) be dealt with for the purposes of rental as one acre.

### 10.

Fencing of acquired land
The Minister may, if in his or her discretion he or she so thinks fit, cause any land
acquired under section 9 to be fenced and may by warrant direct payment out of the
Treasury of the cost of such fencing.

### 11.

Fiat for prosecution required
No prosecution for an offence under this Act shall be instituted except by or with the
consent of the Director of Public Prosecutions after consultation with the Chief Technical
Officer (Extension).

### 12.

Power to make rules
The Minister may make rules for the purpose of more effectually carrying out the
provisions and objects of this Act.

### 13.

Finality of decision of Chief Technical Officer (Extension) as to existence of
plant disease
The decision of the Chief Technical Officer (Extension) as to the presence or
identification of any notifiable plant disease or infectious plant disease shall be sufficient
authority for carrying out the purposes of this Act.

PROTECTION FROM PANAMA DISEASE OF BANANAS ORDER
[Section 3.]

[26th February, 1938.]

---

### 1.

Citation
This Order may be cited as the Protection from Panama Disease of Bananas Order.

### 2.

Declaration of plant disease
The Panama Disease of Bananas is hereby declared to be an infectious and notifiable
plant disease.

### 3.

Treatment of Panama Disease of Bananas
Every owner or occupier or person having the charge or management of land (whether
the land shall or shall not have been declared to be infected or suspected of being infected
with plant disease) who knows or suspects the existence of the Panama Disease of
Bananas on the land of which he or she is the owner or occupier or of which he or she has
the charge of management shall, on giving notice to the Chief Technical Officer
(Extension) as required by section 6 of the Act, and pending the arrival of the Chief
Technical Officer (Extension) or a person appointed to carry out the provisions of the
Act, prevent as far as possible all persons from interfering with such plants or taking
suckers from stools of affected plants and cause any implement used in contact with such
affected stool to be sterilised with fire or gas oil prior to its being used again. On the case
being determined to be that of Panama Disease, growers shall use the gas oil treatment for
diseased plants as follows—
Amount of oil.—Four pints of gas oil per plant, equal in quantity to 12 four-ounce
cigarette tins full, are required and may be procured through the Ministry of
Agriculture.
Method.—The plant shall be cut a few inches above the soil level. Trash and weeds
shall be carefully removed around the roots to give a free passage to the oil to reach
the underground parts of the plant. The oil shall then be applied to the root and soil
immediately surrounding it in such a way that very little is applied to the cut

surface, the majority being allowed to penetrate the soil immediately touching the
outside of the plant. The aerial parts of the diseased plants shall be cut up and
either burnt or treated by sprinkling with oil according to the material available.
Quarantine.—No plants shall be removed within a 100 foot radius of any diseased
plant, stool or area. Diseased plants shall be destroyed as well as every adjoining
plant or stool within a 24 foot radius. A trench one foot wide and one foot deep
shall be made around the plant throwing dirt inwards. Banana plants shall not be
planted nor be permitted to grow on soil affected with Panama Disease.

### 4.

Declaration of infected area
The Parish of St. David in this State is hereby declared to be an area infected with
plant disease:
Provided that where an estate or other holding is partly in St. David’s and partly in
another parish, such estate or holding shall be deemed, for the purposes of this Order, to
be wholly within the parish of St. David.

### 5.

Removal of plants
The removal and carriage of banana plants out of the parish of St. David without the
permission in writing of the Agricultural Officer is hereby prohibited.

### 6.

Use of trash prohibited
The use of trash, except in the form of pads, in the packing or transportation of
bananas, along the main roads to the port of shipment is hereby prohibited throughout the
State.

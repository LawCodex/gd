## CHAPTER 185

MARRIAGE OF BRITISH SUBJECTS (FACILITIES) ACT
An Act to facilitate marriages between British subjects resident in Grenada and
British subjects resident in the United Kingdom.
[15th February, 1916.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Marriage of British Subjects (Facilities) Act.

### 2.

Marriage facilities for British subjects
(1) When a marriage is intended to be solemnised or contracted in Grenada between a
British subject resident in Grenada and a British subject resident in England, Scotland or
Northern Ireland, a certificate for marriage lawfully issued in England, Scotland or
Northern Ireland, as the case may be, shall in Grenada have the same effect as a certificate
for marriage issued in Grenada by a Registrar of Marriages or a Marriage Officer.
(2) When a marriage is intended to be solemnised or contracted in England, Scotland
or Northern Ireland between a British subject resident in England, Scotland or Northern
Ireland and a British subject resident in Grenada, a Registrar’s certificate of due
publication of notice to marry or a Marriage Officer’s certificate of the due publication of
banns may be issued in Grenada in the like manner as if the marriage was to be
solemnised or contracted under circumstances requiring the issue of such a certificate and
as if both such British subjects were resident in Grenada.
(3) For the purposes of subsection (1), the expression “certificate for marriage
lawfully issued” means—
(a)

in the case of England, a certificate for marriage issued by a superintendent
Registrar;

(b)

in the case of Scotland, a certificate for marriage issued by a Registrar or a
certificate of proclamation of banns;

(c)

in the case of Northern Ireland, a certificate for marriage issued by a Registrar.

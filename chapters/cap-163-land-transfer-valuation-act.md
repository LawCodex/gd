## CHAPTER 163

LAND TRANSFER VALUATION ACT
An Act to provide for a better system of valuating land or other immovable property
being transferred where such transfer attracts a tax.
[Act No. 39 of 1992.]

[24th December, 1992.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Land Transfer Valuation Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act, “land” includes immovable property.
3\. Duly determined prevailing market value of land prevails over valuation or sale
price consideration stated by parties
Whenever land is transferred in circumstances attracting any tax whatsoever based
upon the valuation or sale price of such land or the consideration regarding such transfer,
or whenever land is transferred for love and affection or by way of gift, the valuation or
sale price or consideration or love and affection or gift stated by the parties to the transfer
as having been agreed upon by them shall not be binding upon the taxing authority; but
rather, the taxing authority shall be entitled to use as the appropriate valuation or sale
price or consideration such valuation or sale price or consideration as shall be determined
by the Valuation Division of the Inland Revenue Department of the Ministry of Finance if

required by the taxing authority to fix same as being the prevailing market value of such
land; and such duly determined prevailing market value shall prevail over the valuation or
sale price or consideration stated by the parties to the transfer.
4\. Taxing authority may require the Valuation Division to determine prevailing
market value
(1) When any land is transferred in circumstances referred to in section 3, the taxing
authority shall be entitled to require the Valuation Division of the Inland Revenue
Department of the Ministry of Finance to determine the prevailing market value of such
land for the purpose of section 3.
(2) When any land is about to be transferred in circumstances referred to in section 3,
any party to the proposed transfer may require the Valuation Division of the Inland
Revenue Department of the Ministry of Finance to determine the prevailing market value
of such land for the purpose of section 3.
5\. When prevailing market value being determined taxpayer entitled to make
representation
When under section 4 the Valuation Division of the Inland Revenue Department of the
Ministry of Finance is required by a taxing authority to determine the prevailing market
value of land, the said Valuation Division shall inform any person liable to pay any tax
consequent upon the transfer of the land that the said Valuation Division has been
required to make such determination, and the said Valuation Division shall inform such
person that he or she is entitled to make to the said Valuation Division representations
bearing upon the determination which the said Valuation Division is in the process of
making.
6\. Representation made by taxpayer shall be considered by the Valuation Division
When any representation is made under section 5 to the Valuation Division of the
Inland Revenue Department of the Ministry of Finance by a person liable to pay a tax
consequent upon the transfer of land, the said Valuation Division shall duly consider such
representation; and, having regard to such representation, the said Valuation Division
shall determine the prevailing market value of the land; and the taxing authority shall
assess the tax on the basis of such duly determined prevailing market value.
7\. Appeals
Where a person makes a representation under section 5 to the Valuation Division of
the Inland Revenue Department of the Ministry of Finance but is aggrieved by a decision
of the said Valuation Division under section 6, he or she may appeal to the Magistrate and
thence to a High Court Judge in Chambers and thence to the Court of Appeal and for this
purpose, and subject hereto, section 12 of the Real Property Tax Act, Chapter 275, shall
apply, mutatis mutandis.
8\. Saving
This Act shall not apply where any given law providing for the taxing of a transfer of
land empowers the taxing authority itself to make its own determination of the value of
the land and to substitute its own such valuation for the valuation or consideration stated
by the parties to the transfer for the purpose of assessing the tax.
9\. The term “taxing authority” defined

References in this Act to a “taxing authority” are references to whichever authority is
commissioned by any given law to assess whatever tax is payable under any particular
provision of that law.

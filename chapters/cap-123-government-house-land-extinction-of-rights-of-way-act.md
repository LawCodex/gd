## CHAPTER 123

GOVERNMENT HOUSE LAND (EXTINCTION OF RIGHTS OF WAY) ACT
An Act to extinguish all rights of way over the Government land occupied or held
with Government House.
[26th July, 1915.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Government House Land (Extinction of Rights of Way)
Act.
2\. Extinction of rights of way
All rights of way, public or private, over the Government land situated in the parish of
Saint George occupied or held with Government House, are extinguished.

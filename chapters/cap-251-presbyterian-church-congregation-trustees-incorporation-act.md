## CHAPTER 251

PRESBYTERIAN CHURCH CONGREGATION TRUSTEES
(INCORPORATION) ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of, and the vesting of property in, the
Trustees of the Congregation of the Presbyterian Church.
[Act No. 14 of 1971.]

[10th July, 1971.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Presbyterian Church Congregation Trustees
(Incorporation) Act.
2\. Incorporation of trustees
The Moderator (the Reverend Arlen Ross Salthouse), the Secretary of the Official
Board (Lorenzo Japal), the Treasurer of the Presbyterian Church (George Laurence
Vernon Commissiong) and their successors in office, also Kennedy Teka and Felix
Jonathan Lalsee appointees of the Official Board who shall be succeeded by two
members appointed by the Official Board from time to time, as trustees of the
Congregation of the Presbyterian Church shall be a body corporate known as The
Incorporated Trustees of the Congregation of the Presbyterian Church in Grenada
(hereinafter called “the Trustees”) with perpetual succession and a Common Seal and
with power in that name to sue and be sued in all courts.
3\. Power of trustees to acquire property
The Trustees shall have power to acquire for the Congregation of the Presbyterian
Church by purchase, exchange, demise, gift, bequest or otherwise any land or other

property of whatever kind in Grenada or any estates or interests therein and any money,
securities, goods or chattels whatever.
4\. Power of trustees to dispose of property, and to borrow, etc.
The Trustees shall have power, from time to time—
(a)

by deed under the Common Seal to sell, demise, grant, convey, exchange,
mortgage or otherwise dispose of any real or personal property vested in or
which may hereafter become vested in them;

(b)

to borrow money upon the security of any property held by them:

Provided that any mortgage or similar obligation shall be evidenced by the affixing
of the Common Seal, and any document relating to a transaction effected in pursuance of
paragraph (b) shall be signed by at least two of the Trustees.
5\. Vesting of property
The property specified in the Schedule, and all other property of whatever kind
(whether movable or immovable) now vested in any person for and on behalf of the
Congregation of the Presbyterian Church in Grenada, is hereby vested in the Trustees.
6\. Trustees subject to Chapter No. 47 of 1934
The names and addresses of all persons referred to in section 2 and their successors in
office as the Trustees shall be registered with the Registrar of Companies, and they shall
be subject to the provisions of the Companies Act (Chapter No. 47 of the 1934 Revised
Edition of the Laws of Grenada).

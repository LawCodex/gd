## CHAPTER 151A

INTERNATIONAL BETTING ACT
An Act to provide for the licensing, regulation and conduct of international betting,
and for maters connected therewith.
[Act No. 24 of 1998 amended by Act No. 5 of 2000, Act No. 42 of 2000, Act No. 1 of 2008.]

[7th September, 1998.]

## PART I

Preliminary

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the International Betting Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“company” means a company incorporated in Grenada for the purpose of
conducting international betting;
“international betting” means a business which involves the placing by persons, not
present in Grenada, of bets in money or monies worth on a sporting event taking place
outside of Grenada and as a result of such bets winnings or dividends are paid to those
persons who correctly forecasted the sporting event or any aspect of such sporting event;
“Minister” means Minister of Finance;
“sporting event” includes boxing, wrestling, football, soccer, rugby, horse racing,
dog racing, polo, squash, athletics, field hockey, ice hockey, any game played at a
casino and any other like event.

## PART II

Application for Licence and Grant Thereof

### 3.

Prohibition on international betting
(1) No person shall engage in international betting from any place in Grenada unless
such person is a company registered or incorporated in Grenada and has been granted a
licence for that purpose under this Act.
(2) No person resident in Grenada shall place a bet with a company engaged in
international betting whether or not that company has been granted a licence under this
Act.

### 4.

Necessity for licence
(1) A company desirous of conducting international betting from within Grenada shall
apply to the Minister for a licence.
(2) An application for the purposes of subsection (1) must be submitted to the
Registrar of Offshore Services.
(3) For the purposes of this section, “Authority” means the Authority established under
the Grenada Authority for the Regulation of Financial Institutions Act, Chapter 125A.

### 5.

Eligible company
A body corporate is an eligible company if—
(a)

it is incorporated or registered in accordance with the International
Companies Act;

(b)

its objects or business activities are restricted to the conduct of international
betting;

(c)

its Articles are acceptable to the Minister; and

(d)

notwithstanding anything contained in the International Companies Act,

## Chapter 152, has at least two directors resident in Grenada.

### 6.

Content of application
(1) An application for a licence under this Act to conduct international betting from
within Grenada must be in such form as the Minister approves and must—
(a)

show that the applicant is an eligible company;

(b)

subject to section 5(d), give the names and addresses of its directors;

(c)

give the name of any company, whether incorporated in Grenada or
elsewhere, with which the applicant or any of its directors is associated;

(d)

be accompanied by the prescribed application fee;

(e)

give particulars of the games in respect of which betting is to be conducted;

(f)

be accompanied by a certified copy of the articles of association or
memorandum of association, as the case requires;

(g)

provide such other information of a financial or other nature as the Minister
may require in general or particular case.

(2) An application for a licence and all documents submitted for the purposes of this
Act in support of the application must be signed by all the directors of the company
making the application.

### 7.

Examination of applicant
(1) On receipt of an application from an eligible company for a licence under this Act,
the Minister may cause such investigation to be made of the applicant company, its
financial circumstances and any associates or affiliates of the applicant company as the
Minister considers necessary in the public interest.
(2) In particular, the Minister may require an examination of—
(a)

the financial status and history of the applicant company and any of its
directors, associates or affiliates within the meaning of subsections (3)
and (4);

(b)

the character and experience of the directors thereof;

(c)

the adequacy of its capital for the purpose of the business it intends to carry
on;

(d)

the viability of the business it intends to carry on.

(3) For the purposes of this section, “associate” when used to indicate a relationship
with any person means—
(a)

a company of which that person beneficially owns or controls, directly or
indirectly, shares or securities convertible into shares carrying more than
thirty per cent of the voting rights under all circumstances or by reason or
the occurrence of an event that has occurred and is continuing, or a currently
exercisable option or right to purchase those shares or convertible securities;

(b)

a partner of that person acting on behalf of a business or undertaking of
which they are partners;

(c)

a trust or estate in which that person has a substantial beneficial interest or in
respect of which he or she serves as a trustee or in a similar capacity;

(d)

a spouse or child of that person; or

(e)

a relative of that person or of the spouse of that person if the relative has the
same residence of that person.

(4) For the purposes of this section—
(a)

a company is affiliated with another company if one of them is the
subsidiary of the same holding company or each of them is controlled by the
same person; or

(b)

if two companies are affiliated with the same company at the same time,
they are affiliated with each other at that time.

(5) A company is the holding company of another if that other company is the
subsidiary of the holding company.
(6) A company is a subsidiary of another if that other company is controlled by that
other company.

### 8.

Grant of licence

(1) Subject to this Act, where an application is made under section 4, the Minister
may, upon payment of the prescribed fee, grant the applicant a licence to conduct
international betting; but if the Minister is of the opinion that it would be desirable in the
public interest that the licence should not be granted he or she may refuse to grant it
without giving any reason for such refusal.
(2) A licence granted under subsection (1) shall be granted exclusively for the
operation of international betting.
(3) Subject to section 9, in granting a licence under this Act the Minister shall have
regard to—
(a)

the financial reputation and standing of the applicant;

(b)

the applicant’s record in the conduct of international betting;

(c)

the satisfactory nature of verifiable records preceding the date of the
application;

(d)

the financial net worth of each of the directors, associates of affiliates of the
company applying for a licence under this Act;

(e)

the character and standing of all the directors or proposed directors of the
applicant company;

(f)

any other requirements he or she may, after consultation with his or her
financial advisers, determine.

(4) A decision of the Minister to grant a licence is final and conclusive.
(5) Every licensee shall prominently display the licence granted under this Act on the
premises from where the international betting is conducted.

### 9.

Conditions for the grant and retention of licence
(1) No Licence shall be granted to a company or allowed to be retained by a company
unless—
(a)

it has a principal office in Grenada for the conduct of its business;

(b)

it conducts its business in a manner that is consistent with the interests of
persons placing bets;

(c)

subject to section 14, it maintains permanent deposit of at least one hundred
thousand US dollars in a licensed bank in Grenada;

(d)

it establishes and maintains an accounting system to the satisfaction of the
Minister.

(2) No licence shall be granted to a company if the Minister is satisfied that an
employer, director or other associate of the company is the subject of a criminal
investigation or has been charged with a criminal offence, in Grenada or in another
jurisdiction.

### 10.

Expiration of licence
A licence issued under this Act expires one year after, it was granted and must be
renewed within two days after such expiration by the payment of the prescribed fee.

### 11.

Suspension of licence

Subject to section 23, the Minister may suspend a licence for a period not exceeding
six months if—
(a)

he or she is satisfied that an employee, agent, director or associate of the
licensee is under criminal investigation or has been charged with a criminal
offence in Grenada or in another jurisdiction;

(b)

he or she satisfied from complaints received that an aggregate sum of
twenty-seven thousand EC dollars or more is owed for a period exceeding
forty-two days to persons placing bets with the company; or

(c)

the licensee has failed to pay the tax imposed by section 17.

### 12.

Revocation of licence
(1) Subject to section 23, the Minister may revoke a licence granted under this Act if
the licensee has contravened any of the conditions or prohibitions subject to which the
licence was granted, or if the Minister is of the opinion that it is in the public interest to
do so.
(2) The revocation of a licence shall be notified in the Gazette and in such other
manner as the Minister determines.

## PART III

Conditions of Licence and Related Matters

### 13.

Conditions of licence
A licence granted to conduct international betting shall be subject to the following
conditions—
(a)

no bet shall be accepted from any person who is present in Grenada when
making such bet;

(b)

the licence shall not be transferable or assignable;

(c)

the licensee may not grant sub-licences to conduct international betting to
any other person;

(d)

the licence fee paid is non-refundable;

(e)

the licensee may not enter into any joint venture arrangement with any other
person under the licence granted;

(f)

no betting shall be conducted unless rules or By-laws have been drawn up
under section 16, approved by the Minister and published in such form as the
Minister determines;

(g)

no betting shall be conducted or continue to be conducted if the licensee has
failed to—
(i) pay the tax under section 17(1), or
(ii) file a tax return as required by section 17(2);

(h)

no business other than international betting shall be conducted under the
licence;

(i)

no person who is not a citizen of Grenada shall be employed by the licensee
without a work permit.

### 14.

Permanent deposit
(1) No licensee shall continue to conduct international betting if the permanent
deposit has fallen below the amount specified in section 9(1)(c).
(2) The deposit referred to in subsection (1) shall be maintained at a licensed bank in
Grenada and when the deposit has been made that fact shall be communicated to the
Registrar immediately.
(3) Notwithstanding subsection (1), the licensee may enter into an arrangement with a
licensed bank in Grenada whereby the deposit will be available in the event of a windingup, appointment of a receiver an order made in the High Court consequent on an action
against the licensee or other like event.
(4) An arrangement under subsection (3) must be approved by the Minister and shall
be subject to such conditions as the Minister may impose.

### 15.

Records of investments
A licensee shall maintain adequate records of all betting conducted in such form as the
Minister directs.

### 16.

Betting rules
(1) A licensee shall draw up rules or By-laws of betting to regulate the conduct of
betting and the payment of dividends.
(2) Any rules or By-laws of betting drawn up pursuant to subsection (1) must be
approved by the Minister.

### 17.

Liability to tax
(1) Notwithstanding anything contained in section 110 of the International Companies
Act, Chapter 152, and subject to this section, a licensee shall in the months of April, July,
October and January in each year pay to the Comptroller of Inland Revenue a tax of two
per cent of the gross receipts derived from international betting for the preceding three
months.
(2) The licensee shall in the months of April, July, October and January of each year,
at the time of the payment of the tax under subsection (1), file a return, in such form as
the Minister approves, with respect to the preceding three months of international betting
conducted.
(3) A return for the purposes of subsection (2) must show—
(a)

the total number of bets placed over the relevant three month period;

(b)

the dividends paid as a consequence of the bets placed; and

(c)

such other information as the Minister requires.

### 18.

Duty to pay over tax, etc.
It shall be the duty of the Manager or other person in charge of conduct of the betting
operation to pay over the tax and file the return under section 17.

## PART IV

Taxation

### 19.

Examination of licensee
The Minister shall cause the affairs of a licensee to be examined from time to time or
whenever in his or her judgement such examination is necessary or expedient in order to
determine whether or not the licensee has acted in conformity with this Act.

### 20.

Power of entry
(1) A person authorised by the Minister to carry out an examination under section 19
may enter the premises of the licensee at any reasonable time without a prior notification
to the licensee.
(2) No person should prevent an authorised person from entering the premises of a
licensee or impedes his or her progress while he or she is carrying out his or her
examination under subsection (1).

### 21.

Power of search
(1) If a magistrate is satisfied by information on oath given by a person authorised by
the Minister that there is reasonable ground for suspecting that an offence against this Act
has been or is being committed and that evidence of the commission of the offence may
be found at any premises specified in the information, or in any vehicle, vessel or aircraft,
he or she may grant a search warrant to the person authorised by the Minister together
with any other person named in the warrant to enter such premises, vehicle, vessel or
aircraft so specified at any time within one month from the date of the warrant to search
the premises, vehicle, vessel or aircraft.
(2) The person authorised by any such warrant to search any premises, vehicle, vessel
or aircraft may search every person who is found in or whom he or she has reasonable
ground to believe to have recently left or to be about to enter those premises of that
vehicle, vessel or aircraft, as the case may be, and may seize any books, records,
vouchers, other documents, cash or securities found in the possession of any such person
or in such premises or in such vehicle, vessel or aircraft.
(3) Where under this section a person has power to enter any premises he or she may
use such force as is reasonably necessary for the purpose of exercising that power.

### 22.

Powers and functions of Grenada Authority for the Regulation of
Financial Institutions Act, Chapter 125A
(1) The Grenada Authority for the Regulation of Financial Institutions Act, Chapter
125A may be authorised by the Minister to—
(a)

advise him or her on an application for a licence under section 4; or

(b)

examine a licensee for the purposes of section 7 or 19.

(2) Where the Grenada Authority for the Regulation of Financial Institutions Act,

## Chapter 125A, is authorised under subsection (1)(b), it shall have and may exercise the

powers conferred on a Commissioner by sections 9 to 13 of the Commissions of Inquiry
Act, Chapter 58, mutatis mutandis.
(3) For the purposes of this section, “Authority” means the Authority established under
the Grenada Authority for the Regulation of Financial Institutions Act, Chapter 125A.

### 23.

Fair hearing
No licence may be suspended or revoked under this Act unless the Minister gives the
licensee a fair hearing prior to such revocation or suspension.

### 24.

Offences
(1) A person who conducts international betting without a licence, contrary to section
3, commits an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine of sixty
thousand dollars or imprisonment for five year, or both such fine and imprisonment.
(2) A person who contravenes any of the prohibitions prescribed by section 13
commits an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine of twenty
thousand dollars or imprisonment for one year.
(3) The manager or chief executive officer who fails or refuses to pay over the
proceeds of the tax in accordance with section 17(2) commits an offence and shall be
liable on summary conviction to a fine of sixty thousand dollars or imprisonment for five
years.
(4) A licensee who fails to keep records of investments as required by section 15
commits an offence and the manager or chief executive officer shall be liable on
summary conviction to a fine of thirty thousand dollars or imprisonment for two years.
(5) A person who prevents an authorised person entering the premises of a licensee or
who impedes his or her progress while carrying out an examination under section 20(1)
commits an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine of ten thousand
dollars or imprisonment for three months.
(6) A licensee who receives or accepts a bet from a person resident in Grenada
commits an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine of sixty thousand
dollars or imprisonment for five years, or both such fine and imprisonment.
(7) A person present in Grenada who places a bet with a company licensed under this
Act commits an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine of ten
thousand dollars or imprisonment for one year.
(8) A person who, without the consent of a licensee, obtains or has in his or her
possession any record relating to the business of such licensee commits an offence and
shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine of one hundred thousand dollars or
imprisonment for five years or to both such fine and imprisonment.

### 25.

Evidence
(1) Notwithstanding anything contained in section 112 of the International Companies
Act, Chapter 152, any record relating to a licensee may be admitted into evidence by a
Court of competent jurisdiction.
(2) Notwithstanding anything contained in any enactment, rule of law or rules or
court, the parties to an action, including their respective attorneys-at-law, shall not be
entitled to receive a copy of any record admitted into evidence under subsection (1).
(3) For the purposes of this section and section 24(8), “record” includes any book,
file, sheet, card, disc, computer tape and any computer generated document.

### 26.

Jurisdiction of magistrate
Notwithstanding anything contained in any other enactment, a magistrate shall have
jurisdiction to adjudicate at a trial for an offence under section 24.

## PART V

Legal Procedures and Offences

### 27.

Right of appeal
(1) A person aggrieved by a decision of the Minister under section 11 or 12, on a
question of law or a mixed question of fact and law, may appeal to a Judge in Chambers
within fourteen days of such decision.
(2) A decision of a Judge under subsection (1) shall be final.

### 28.

Rules and regulations
(1) The Minister may make such rules and regulations he or she considers necessary
to give effect to the provisions of this Act.
(2) A fine of thirty thousand dollars or imprisonment for two years may be attached to
rules or regulations made under subsection (1).
(3) Rules or regulations made under subsection (1) shall be subject to negative
resolution.
(4) Notwithstanding anything contained in any enactment, the fees to be prescribed
for the purpose of this Act may be prescribed in United States currency.

### 29.

Transitional
(1) Notwithstanding anything contained in any enactment or agreement but subject to
this section, any company presently engaged in international betting shall, within five
days after the commencement of this Act, make application for a licence in accordance
with this Act.
(2) Where a licence is granted to a company referred to in subsection (1), any money
paid with respect to an existing licence shall be applied on a pro rata basis to the new
licence.
(3) No licence shall be granted to a company that is indebted to the Government for a
licence or sub-licence fees for the operation of international betting.
(4) Where, no application is made within the time specified in subsection (1), the
company shall be deemed to be conducting international betting without a licence.

### 30.

Commencement
This Act shall come into force on the 7th day of September, 1998.

## CHAPTER 160

LAND ACQUISITION (PARTIAL ABANDONMENT OF LAND AT BELMONT,
ST. GEORGE’S) ACT
An Act to provide for the partial abandonment of a portion of the land acquired
pursuant to Land Acquisition Act, Chapter 159.
[Act No. 25 of 1996.]

[31st January, 1997.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Land Acquisition (Partial Abandonment of Land at
Belmont, St. George’s) Act.
2\. Partial abandonment
Notwithstanding anything contained in the Land Acquisition Act, Chapter 159,
(hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) the 7658 square feet of land, being part of the larger
area of 68156 square feet, acquired pursuant to section 3 of the Act and shown as Lot B
on the plan contained in the Schedule, is hereby abandoned within the meaning of the
Act.
3\. Vesting of land
The 7658 square feet of land abandoned under section 2 shall by virtue of this Act
vested in Adona Pascall, her heirs or successors, as the case requires, for an estate in fee
simple free from encumbrances and without further assurance.
4\. Saving
Nothing contained in this Act shall affect the validity of the acquisition of the parcel
of land shown on the plan as contained in the Schedule.

5\. Effect of this Act
For the avoidance of doubt it is hereby declared that the Land Acquisition Act,

## Chapter 159, is, to the extent of its inconsistency with this Act, superseded for the

purposes of this Act.

## CHAPTER 292B

ST. GEORGE’S BUS TERMINUS AND MARKET SQUARE (DEVELOPMENT
AND FINANCING) ACT
An Act to make provision for the development and financing of the St. George’s Bus
Terminus and the St. George’s Market Square, and for matters incidental
thereto and connected therewith.
[Act No. 20 of 2000.]

[28th July, 2000.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the St. George’s Bus Terminus and Market Square
(Development and Financing) Act.
2\. Interpretation
This Act may be cited as the—
“finance agreement” or “agreement” means the agreement respecting the
development and financing of the St. George’s Bus Terminus, as contained in the
Schedule;
“Market Square” means that area of the town of St. George’s bonded to the North
by Hillsborough Street, to the South by Granby Street, to the East by Grenville Street
and to the West by Halifax Street and all the adjacent buildings owned by the
Government of Grenada;
“St. George’s Bus Terminus” means an area of land to be reclaimed from the
seabed and foreshore abutting Melville Street and the Esplanade and upon which the
bus terminus, and other buildings are to be erected, in the town of St. George’s.

3\. Approval and execution of finance agreement
(1) For the purpose of the development and financing of the St. George’s Bus
Terminus and the Market Square, approval is hereby given for the execution of the
finance agreement as set out in the First Schedule hereto.
(2) The Minister of Finance shall do all such things as may be reasonably necessary
for the development and financing of the St. George’s Bus Terminus and the Market
Square.
4\. Effect of executed agreement
(1) The finance agreement when executed by all the parties thereto shall have the
force and effect of laws in Grenada.
(2) For the purposes of this section and section 5, “parties” means the Republic
Finance and Merchant Bank Limited (hereinafter referred to as “the Bank”) and the
Minister of Finance representing the Government of Grenada.
5\. Exemption from duties, etc.
(1) Notwithstanding anything contained in any other enactment, the Minister of
Finance may, for the purpose of giving effect to the agreement, by order, grant
exemptions from duties, taxes, fees and other like payments to the Bank.
(2) Notwithstanding anything contained in the Aliens (Land-holding Regulation) Act,

## Chapter 13, the Minister may, by order, exempt the Bank from any requirement under

that Act.
(3) An order under subsections (1) and (2) shall be subject to negative Resolution of
Parliament.
6\. Charge on the Consolidated Fund
All expenses incurred by the Government of Grenada under or by virtue of the
agreement shall be a charge on the Consolidated Fund.
7\. Laying
The agreement when executed by all the parties thereto shall be laid in Parliament by
the Minister of Finance within a reasonable time after such execution.

FINCOR including the interim advances hereinbefore recited in connection with the
negotiation and implementation of this Agreement.
2.2 In the event that it becomes, in the opinion of FINCOR, necessary if desirable to

### 6.

INTEREST
6.1 During the Construction Period, interest shall be charged and computed daily on
the balances outstanding at the fixed rate of 10.50% per annum and interest so computed
shall be capitalised monthly and added to principal and be secured hereunder accordingly.
6.2 After the Construction Period interest will be paid on the amount of the Facility
outstanding at the Prime Commercial EC$ lending rate of the Republic Finance and
Merchant Bank limited which rate will be reset semi-annually two (2) days prior to the
date on which the semi-annual instalments become due as provided in Clause 7.2(b). The
rate of interest for the first semi-annually period as provided in Clause 7.2(b) shall be

### 9.

75% per annum.
6.3 All interest earned by FINCOR on monies advanced with respect to the Works
shall be exempt from income and other taxes.
7\. REPAYMENT
7.1 Government shall, within thirty (30) days after having receiving notice from
FINCOR that the Terminus has been practically completed, that is, that the last
Completion Certificate has been issued, give notice to FINCOR of the manner in which it
intends to repay the Facility, as permitted in Clause 7.2.
7.2 Government shall repay the Facility in Eastern Caribbean Dollars either—
1\.

in full, on or before the expiration of thirty (30) days after having given
notice to FINCOR of its intention to do so, or

2\.

by equal semi-annual instalments of principal and interest combined over a
period of ten (10) years payable in arrears.

7.3 In the event that Government fails to give notice as stipulated in Clause 7.2(a) it
shall be deemed that the Government has elected to pay by instalments as stipulated in
Clause 7.2(b).
7.4 Where payment is effected by instalments—
(a)

interest on the Facility will be, at the expiration of sixty (60) days from the
issue of the last Completion Certificate, capitalised and added to principal
and shall bear interest at the Prime Commercial EC$ lending rate of the
Republic Finance and Merchant Bank Limited, which rate will be reset semiannually two (2) days prior to the date on which the semi-annual instalments
become due as provided in Clause 7.2(b). The rate for the first semi-annual
period aforesaid will be 9.75% per annum.;

(b)

any instalment not paid within fourteen (14) days of the date agreed
hereunder for payment shall itself bear interest at the relevant rate of interest
plus one (1) per cent.

7.5 All sums repaid by Government to FINCOR shall be without any set-off or
counterclaim and free and clear of and without deductions for or on account of all taxes
unless the Government is compelled by law to make such payments.
7.6 Notwithstanding the provisions of this Clause 7 the Government shall have the
right to repay the whole or part of the Facility (the amount of any partial prepayment
being an integral multiple of One Hundred Thousand Dollars Eastern Caribbean Currency
ECS$100,000.00 on any date on which instalments are due hereunder) at any time after
the election of Government to repay the Facility as mentioned in Clause 7.2(b) after
having given to FINCOR forty-five (45) days written notice of its intention to do so
specifying the amount to be repaid. Notice of intended prepayment, once having been
given, it shall be obligatory for the prepayment to be made in accordance with the notice.
7.7 The Government shall not be entitled to prepay the Facility or any part thereof
otherwise than in accordance with this Clause.
7.8 Any such prepayment shall not interfere with the repayment instalments herein
but shall only have the effect of accelerating repayment of the Facility unless otherwise
agreed between the parties hereto.
7.9 Upon payment of the entire Facility FINCOR will release the Government from
any charge, security or other obligation created by or in connection with the Facility.

8.2 FINCOR may at any time transfer all or any part of its rights, benefits and

(a)

(b)

(which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld and shall be deemed to
have been given if the Government fails to indicate in writing to the contrary

(c)

FINCOR shall pay all expenses (including but not limited, to legal fees)

transfer under this Clause.

8.3 If FINCOR transfers all or any part of its rights benefits and obligations under this

subsequently be construed as references to FINCOR and the Assignee Bank or Assignee

### 11.

TERMINATION IN CASE OF DEFAULT
11.1 The occurrence of any of the following events shall be an Event of Default—
(a)

failure by the Government to comply with any obligation or undertaking
contained in this Agreement;

(b)

failure by the Government to discharge on its due date any liability to any
other creditor other than a liability which the Government shall in the
reasonable opinion of FINCOR then be contesting in good faith providing
that Government can prove to the satisfaction of FINCOR that the

Government is capable of discharging such liability without adversely
affecting the Government’s capacity to repay the Facility;
(c)

if any consent, licence, authority or approval required to make this
Agreement legal, valid, binding, enforceable and admissible in evidence or
required to enable the Government to perform its obligations hereunder is
withdrawn or ceases to be in full force and effect;

(d)

if any representation, warranty or statement made by or in relation to the
Government in this Agreement, or in any document furnished under or in

date on which it is made or thereafter.

11.2 At any time after the happening of any Event of Default (whether or not notice
shall have been given by the Government of such Event of Default) FINCOR may by
notice in writing to the Government declare the Facility and all interest and all other sums

same shall become immediately due and payable and FINCOR shall be under no
obligation to make any further advance under the Facility.

between the Government of the One part and Republic Finance and Merchant Bank Limited
(“FINCOR”) of the Other Part, to which this opinion is a schedule in connection with the
establishment of the bus terminus on or near the Esplanade, the upgrading of the Market Square
and the refurbishing of the Government’s buildings adjacent to the Market Square all in the town
of St. George in the State of Grenada and having considered the same, and having regard to the
relevant Laws of Grenada I am pleased to advise that—
(i) the Government is competent to implement all the terms of the said Agreement and to
execute the same;
(ii) the said Agreement to which the authorised persons have signed acting for and on behalf of
the Government will be valid and binding on the Government in accordance with the Laws
of Grenada. All the covenants, conditions and obligations of the Government therein are
legal, valid and binding obligations of the Government enforceable in accordance with their
respective terms;
(iii) all authorisations that are required to permit the performance by the Government of its
obligations under the said Agreement have been obtained and produced to me;
(iv) the said Agreement is in proper legal form under the Laws of Grenada for the enforcement
thereof against the Government;
(v) the performance by the Government of any obligations or conditions contained in the said
Agreement will not violate any law or regulation of Grenada;
(vi) all monies payable by the Government pursuant to the said Agreement shall be charged on
and payable out of the Consolidated Fund of the Government of Grenada.

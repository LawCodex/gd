## CHAPTER 56

COLLECTOR-GENERAL ACT
An Act to provide for the creation of the office of Collector-General and the
duties attached thereto.
[Act No. 19 of 1971.]

[28th August, 1971.]
1\.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Collector-General Act.

2\.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“appropriate authority” means the Permanent Secretary (Finance) or the
Comptroller of Inland Revenue or the Accountant-General, as the case may be;
“Minister” means the Minister for the time being responsible for finance;
“tax” includes any sum, penalty or interest which may accrue by reason of
failure to pay any tax within any period as provided by law.

3\.

Office of Collector-General

The Governor-General acting in accordance with the advice of the Public Service
Commission, shall appoint a fit and proper person to be the Collector-General.
4\.

Duties and powers of the Collector-General

(1) Where the appropriate authority transmits to the Collector-General a warrant in
the form specified in the Schedule requiring the Collector-General to collect or recover
tax due or payable by a person mentioned in such warrant under an Act, the CollectorGeneral shall, under the authority of this Act, collect or recover the tax in the same

manner as the appropriate authority is authorised to collect or recover the tax under the
provisions of that Act.
(2) On receipt of the warrant—

5\.

(a)

the Collector-General shall have all the powers of the appropriate
authority under that Act in respect of the collection or recovery of the tax
due or payable by the person named in the warrant, and references in
that Act to the appropriate authority relating to the collection or recovery
of the tax due or payable shall be deemed to be references to the
Collector-General;

(b)

the appropriate authority shall cease to hold any authority to collect the
tax due or payable by the person named in the warrant.

Recovery of fees and duties

Where any sum, fee or duty is due or payable by any person under an Act and that
person fails to pay such sum, fee or duty in accordance with the provisions of such Act,
the Collector-General is hereby empowered to sue for such sum, fee or duty as a civil
debt in any court.
6\.

Taxes, etc., collected to be paid into the Consolidated Fund

The Collector-General shall pay all taxes, sums, fees and duties collected or
recovered by him or her into the Consolidated Fund.
7\.

Report by Collector-General

The Collector-General shall prepare and transmit to the Minister an annual report of
his or her operations under this Act at the end of each financial year, and the Minister
shall cause a copy of such report to be laid before the House of Representatives.

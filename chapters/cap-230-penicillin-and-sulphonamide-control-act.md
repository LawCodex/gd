## CHAPTER 230

PENICILLIN AND SULPHONAMIDE CONTROL ACT
An Act to control the sale and supply of Penicillin, Sulphonamide and certain other
substances.
[Amended by Act No. 6 of 1971.]

[1st September, 1949.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Penicillin and Sulphonamide Control Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“approved prescription” means a prescription given by a registered medical or
dental practitioner signed by that practitioner with his or her usual signature specifying
the name of the patient for whose treatment it is given, and given for a stated weight or

measure of the substance thereby prescribed (hereinafter called the “prescribed
substance”);
“controlled substance” means any substance set out in the Schedule, and includes
any preparation of which any such substance is an ingredient or a part;
“hospital authority” means any person carrying on, or in charge of, any hospital or
clinic or other institution providing medical or surgical treatment and being an
institution provided by a local authority or supported wholly or partly out of any
public funds or by a charity or by voluntary subscriptions;
“registered and licensed pharmacist” means a pharmacist registered under the
Pharmacy Act, Chapter 241, and licensed to carry on the business of a pharmacist
under the Licences Act, Chapter 172;
“registered medical or dental practitioner” means a person registered either in the
medical register or the dental register under the Health Practitioners Act, Chapter
132A.
3\. Acquisition
No person shall acquire, or agree or offer to acquire, any controlled substance,
except—
(a)

the patient of a registered medical or dental practitioner: from that
practitioner in the course of being treated by that practitioner;

(b)

from a registered medical or dental practitioner or from a registered and
licensed pharmacist: such controlled substance as is prescribed for that
person by an approved prescription;

(c)

a registered medical or dental practitioner, or a hospital authority: from any
such practitioner or authority;

(d)

from a Government Ministry or Department or from a hospital authority; or

(e)

from a person who is authorised by a licence granted by the Chief Medical
Officer to dispose of that controlled substance.

4\. Disposal
No person shall dispose of, or agree or offer to dispose of, any controlled substance,
except—
(a)

a registered medical or dental practitioner: to his or her patient to such an
extent as may be necessary in his or her treatment of that patient;

(b)

a registered medical or dental practitioner or a registered and licensed
pharmacist against an approved prescription and to the patient for whom that
controlled substance is prescribed by that prescription;

(c)

a registered medical or dental practitioner or hospital authority: to any such
practitioner or authority;

(d)

a Government Ministry or Department or a hospital authority; or

(e)

under the authority of, and in accordance with, a licence granted by the Chief
Medical Officer.

5\. Duties on dispensing an approved prescription
(1) Every person who disposes of, against an approved prescription, any prescribed
substance containing any controlled substance shall, on each occasion on which he or she
does so, enter on that prescription—

his or her name and address; and

(b)

the date of that occasion.

(2) No person shall—

on any particular occasion dispose of, against any approved prescription
containing any controlled substance, any quantity of the prescribed
substance other than the weight or measure thereof stated in that
prescription; or

(b)

dispose of that quantity against that prescription on more than one occasion
except an occasion which will not, when aggregated with all the other
occasions of disposal entered on that prescription, exceed such number of
occasions as is specifically directed in that prescription.

(3) The person who makes, against any approved prescription containing any
controlled substance, the last disposal which, under subsection (2), may be made of the
prescribed substance against that prescription shall retain that prescription and preserve
and keep the same and deliver it up to the Chief Medical Officer or such other person as
he or she may direct in writing.
6\. Register of transactions of controlled substances
(1) A person who acquires or disposes of any controlled substance shall keep a
register and shall include therein the following particulars—
(a)

the date and quantity of the substance received; and

(b)

the date and quantity of the substance delivered and the name of the person
to whom the disposal is made,

and when required so to do, such person shall produce his or her register for inspection by
the Chief Medical Officer or by such other person as the Chief Medical Officer may
direct in writing. Any person who fails or neglects to comply with the provisions of this
section shall be guilty of an offence.
(2) This section shall not apply—
(a)

to a person who acquires any controlled substance in accordance with
section 3(a) or (b); or

(b)

to a person who disposes of any controlled substance in accordance with
section 4(a).

7\. Defences
Notwithstanding anything in sections 3 and 4, in any proceedings in respect of any
acquisition or disposal or agreement or offer in alleged contravention of any provisions of
either of those sections it shall be a defence for the defendant to prove either—
(a)

that the disposal or acquisition or agreement or offer was effected both—
(i) solely in the course of, and for the purpose of, performing hospital
work under a hospital authority, and
(ii) solely between parties each of whom was engaged in the performance
of hospital work under a hospital authority; or

(b)

that the controlled substance was situate outside Grenada at the time when
the acquisition or disposal or agreement or offer was made; or

(c)

that the controlled substance was under the agreement delivered or agreed to
be delivered either—
(i) outside Grenada, or

(ii) on board a ship for exportation to a place outside Grenada.
8\. Licence to import controlled substance
No person shall import any controlled substance except—

such controlled substance is manufactured by a pharmaceutical firm
approved by the Minister of Health on the recommendation of the Chief
Medical Officer; and

(b)

such person is the holder of a licence granted by the Chief Medical Officer
to import such substance.

9\. Storage of controlled substances
No person shall store any of the controlled substances set out in Part I of the Schedule
unless he or she is the holder of a licence granted by the Chief Medical Officer to store
such substances as may be specified in the licence, and no such licence shall be granted
by the Chief Medical Officer unless he or she is satisfied that the person applying for the
licence has adequate facilities for storing such substances.
10\. Cancellation of licences
If a person holding a licence under this Act is convicted of an offence against the
provisions of this Act the Chief Medical Officer may cancel any existing licence held by
such person.
11\. Power to enter and inspect premises where controlled substances believed to be
illegally acquired or stored
The Chief Medical Officer or a person authorised by him or her in writing may at all
reasonable times enter and inspect any premises in which he or she has reason to
believe—
(a)

that any controlled substance is being kept which has been acquired in
contravention of any of the provisions of this Act; or

(b)

that any controlled substance set out in Part I of the Schedule is being stored
in contravention of section 9,

and may require the occupier or person in charge of the premises to furnish him or her
with such information in connection with such controlled substance as he or she may
consider necessary. Any controlled substance in respect of which there has been a breach
of any of the provisions of this Act may be seized, and, on conviction of the offender,
may be forfeited.
12\. Who may administer controlled substance
No person shall administer to any person by way of treatment any controlled
substance set out in Part I of the Schedule unless he or she is a registered medical or
dental practitioner or is acting in accordance with the directions of such practitioner.
13\. Chief Medical Officer may grant licences to enable treatment of animals
The Chief Medical Officer may, in his or her discretion, grant licences under of
sections 3(e) and 4(e) to such persons as he or she shall deem fit and proper who bona
fide require any controlled substance for the treatment of any animal in such quantities as
may be prescribed in the licence.
14\. Penalty

A person guilty of a breach of any of the provisions of this Act shall be guilty of an
offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of five thousand dollars and to
imprisonment for six months.
15\. Construction
In this Act—

references to offers to acquire or dispose of shall be construed respectively
as including references to invitations or offers to dispose of or acquire;

(b)

the “acquire” includes the acquisition, and the expression “dispose of”
includes the disposal of—
(i) ownership or any proprietary interest,
(ii) the right to possession, or
(iii) possession, whether or not accompanied by any acquisition or disposal
of any ownership or of any proprietary interest or of the right to
possession; and

(c)

where a controlled substance is acquired by or disposed of to a carrier or
warehouseman or employee acting in the ordinary course of his or her
business or employment as such, that controlled substance shall be presumed
to be acquired by or disposed of to the person on whose behalf or to whose
order the carrier or warehouseman or employee so acquires that controlled
substance and not by or to the carrier or warehouseman or employee; and
where any controlled substance is acquired from or disposed of by a carrier
or warehouseman or employee acting as aforesaid, that controlled substance
shall be presumed to be acquired from or disposed of by the person on
whose behalf or by whose order the carrier or warehouseman or employee so
disposes of that controlled substance and not from or by the carrier or
warehouseman or employee; unless the person on whose behalf or order the
carrier or warehouseman or employee acquired or disposed of the controlled
substance proves that the offence was committed without his or her consent
or connivance and that he or she exercised all such diligence to prevent the
commission of the offence as he or she ought to have exercised having
regard to all the circumstances.

16\. Minister may amend Schedule
The Minister of Health may, by Order, amend the Schedule as may seem to him or her
proper in the interest of public health.

## PART I

“Penicillin”
(which term includes any anti-infective acid produced by penicillin notatum
whether obtained from penicillin notatum or not, and any salt or derivative

obtained from any such acid, and any solution containing such salt or acid or
derivative);
(b)

“Streptomycin”
(which term includes all compounds of streptomycin and all medicinal
preparations containing streptomycin);

(c)

“Chloromycetin”
(chloramphenicol, alficetyn) aureomycin, neomycin, terromycin.

## PART II

Para-aminobenzene sulphonamide; its salts.
Derivatives of para-aminobenzene sulphonamide having one or both of the hydrogen
atoms of the para-animo group or of the sulphonamide group, substituted by other
radicals; their salts.
The derivatives shall include—
Azosulphamide;
Benzylsulphanilamide;
Cyclamate;
Succinyl sulphathiazole;
Sulphanilyldimethyl sulphanilamide;
Sulphapyridine;
Sulphathiazole;
Sulphacetamide;
Sulphadiazine;
Sulphaguanidine;
Sulphamezathine;
Sulphonamidochrysoidin.
Exemptions:
Ointments and lotions containing proportions of a controlled substance and intended
solely for external use.

## CHAPTER 175A

LOCAL LOAN REGULATIONS ACT
[Section 14.]

---

### 1.

Short title
These Regulations may be cited as the Local Loan Regulations Act.

### 2.

Register of debentures
The Permanent Secretary (Finance) shall cause a register of debentures issued under
the Act to be kept at the Treasury.

### 3.

Registration of debentures
On the demand of the holder of any debentures for the registration of the debentures
held by him or her or any part thereof made in the form set out in the Schedule to these
Regulations and authenticated by such evidence, if any, as the Permanent Secretary
(Finance) may reasonably require as to the identity of the person making the demand and

on such debentures being delivered up to the Permanent Secretary (Finance), and on
payment of a fee of fifty cents, the Permanent Secretary (Finance) shall register the
debentures in respect of which the demand is made by causing the name and address of the
holder of the debentures and the amount of the debentures to be entered in the register.

### 4.

Debentures to be transferable
On the registration of debentures under these Regulations, such debentures shall
become transferable by entries in the register.

### 5.

Certificate of registration
On the registration of debentures under these Regulations, the Permanent Secretary
(Finance) shall issue to the holder of the debentures a certificate of registration in the
form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations. The certificate of registration shall be
prima facie evidence of the title of the person named therein to the amount of stock
specified therein.

### 6.

Notice of trust
No notice of any trust, express, implied or constructive, shall be entered on the register
or be receivable by the Permanent Secretary (Finance) in respect of a certificate of
registration.

### 7.

Worn out certificates
If a certificate of registration is worn out or defaced, the Permanent Secretary
(Finance) shall upon surrender of the certificate and upon payment of a fee of fifty cents,
issue a new certificate to the person entitled to the worn or defaced certificate.

### 8.

Lost certificates
If a certificate is lost or destroyed the Permanent Secretary (Finance) shall, upon proof
thereof to his satisfaction and on receiving indemnity to his satisfaction against all claims
in respect of the certificate lost or destroyed, and upon payment of a fee of fifty cents,
issue a new certificate to the person entitled to the lost or destroyed certificate.

### 9.

Recognition of transfers
The Permanent Secretary (Finance) shall not recognise any transfer of registered
debentures other than a transfer in accordance with these Regulations.

### 10.

Registration of transfers
Debentures registered under these Regulations shall be transferred by entry in the
register signed—
(a)

by the transferor, or if he or she is absent, by his attorney thereunto lawfully
authorised by some writing executed under his hand and seal and attested; or

(b)

by the Permanent Secretary (Finance) under the authority and in pursuance
of a deed of transfer in the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations.

### 11.

Deviation from form
The Permanent Secretary (Finance) may approve any deed of transfer presented to him
or her in a form different from the form set out in the Schedule to these Regulations if he
or she considers that the variations from the form so set out are not material.

### 12.

Execution of transfer
A deed of transfer shall be executed by all the parties thereto, the execution of each
party being attested by one or more credible witnesses and, when so executed, shall be
delivered to and retained by the Permanent Secretary (Finance).

### 13.

Surrender of certificate
The certificate of registration of debentures to which a transfer relates shall be
surrendered to the Permanent Secretary (Finance), and a fee of fifty cents shall be paid to
the Permanent Secretary (Finance) in respect thereof.

### 14.

Refusal by Permanent Secretary (Finance)
The Permanent Secretary (Finance) may, if he or she thinks fit in any case, refuse to
recognise any deed of transfer unless such other evidence in addition to the certificate of
registration is produced to the Permanent Secretary (Finance), as the Permanent Secretary
(Finance) may reasonably require, to show the right of transferor to make the transfer,
and in particular may refuse to recognise a deed of transfer executed by a corporation
acting under seal until a letter has been received by the Permanent Secretary (Finance),
under the hand of the chairperson or vice-chairperson of the corporation, or some other
person occupying a corresponding position, signifying that the transfer has the sanction of
the board of management or corresponding authority, and that the seal of the corporation
has been affixed to the deed in accordance with the rules and regulations governing its
use.

### 15.

Completion of transfer
On compliance with these Regulations the Permanent Secretary (Finance) shall
complete the transfer by making such alterations in the register as are necessary to show
the effect of the transfer:
Provided that the Permanent Secretary (Finance) shall not be required to complete a
transfer until at least ten days have elapsed after the date on which the transfer deed was
delivered to him or her, and until at least three days have elapsed after the date on which a
reply from the transferor to any notice sent by the Permanent Secretary (Finance)
informing him or her of the proposed transfer could reach the Permanent Secretary
(Finance) in the ordinary course of post.

### 16.

Issue of new certificate
The Permanent Secretary (Finance) on completion of a transfer shall issue to the
transferee without further fee a new certificate of registration in respect of the debentures
to which the transfer relates.

### 17.

Issue of certificate for balance
Where a transfer is of part only of the debentures held by the transferor, the Permanent
Secretary (Finance) shall on the completion of the transfer issue to him or her without fee
a certificate of registration for the balance of the debentures.

### 18.

Delivery of certificate
A certificate of registration required under these Regulations to be issued to any
holder of debentures or to any transferor or transferee may be issued to him or her either
by delivering the certificate to him or her or to any person who shows to the satisfaction
of the Permanent Secretary (Finance) that he or she is authorised to receive the certificate
on his or her behalf, or if he or she so requests, by sending it to him or her by post,
addressed in accordance with his request.

### 19.

Removal of debentures
On demand of the holder of any registered debentures for the removal of debentures
from the register, made in writing and authenticated by such evidence, if any, as the
Permanent Secretary (Finance) may reasonably require, as to the identity of the person
making the demand, and on payment of a fee of twenty-five cents and delivery up of the
certificate of registration relating to the debentures, the Permanent Secretary (Finance)
shall remove the debentures from the register by cancelling the entry relating to
debentures in the register.

### 20.

Closing of register
(1) The Permanent Secretary (Finance) may close the register as regards transfers on
any day in the month next preceding that in which the interest on debentures is payable;
but so as the books be not at any time closed for more than fifteen days.
(2) The persons who on the day of such closing are registered as holders of
debentures shall as between them and their transferees of debentures be entitled to the
then current half-year’s interest thereon.

### 21.

Power of attorney
Any power of attorney given for the purpose of any transfer of debentures shall be
delivered to and retained by the Permanent Secretary (Finance).

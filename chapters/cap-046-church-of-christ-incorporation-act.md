## CHAPTER 46

CHURCH OF CHRIST (INCORPORATION) ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of a body to be known as the
Incorporated Trustees of the Church of Christ in Grenada.
[Act No. 31 of 1974.]

[11th April, 1975.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Church of Christ (Incorporation) Act.

### 2.

Incorporation of trustees

Ernest Roberts, Philip Mendes, Winston Herry and their successors in office as
trustees of the Church of Christ shall be a body corporate known as The Incorporated
Trustees of the Church of Christ (hereinafter called “the Trustees”) with perpetual
succession and a Common Seal and with power in that name to sue and be sued in all
courts.

### 3.

Power of trustees to acquire property

The Trustees shall have power to acquire for the Church of Christ by purchase,
transfer, exchange, devise, grant, gift, demise, bequest or otherwise any land or other
property of whatever kind whether in perpetuity or for life or for a term of years and
any money, securities, goods or chattels whatever, to take, hold and enjoy the same
subject to the performance of any trust upon which the same may have been acquired.

### 4.

Power of trustees to dispose of property, and to borrow, etc.
Subject to the performance of any trust as aforesaid the Trustees may—
(a)

by deed under the Common Seal sell, demise, grant, convey, transfer,
exchange, surrender, or otherwise dispose of any real or personal
property vested in or which may hereafter be acquired by or become
vested in the Trustees;

(b)

### 5.

borrow money and, for the payment of the same, by deed under the
Common Seal, give and execute mortgages, charges, pledges and other
securities of any real or personal property or make any arrangement that
they may consider convenient with such covenants, powers and provisions
as they may think proper.

Affixing of Common Seal

All documents requiring the Common Seal shall be sealed in the presence of at
least two of the Trustees who shall sign every such document.

### 6.

Address for service, etc.

(1) The Trustees shall at all times have a fixed address in Grenada for the service
of documents on them and such address and any change thereof shall be registered
with the Registrar of Companies.
(2) Any document may be served on the Trustees by leaving the document at, or
by sending it by registered post to, the address registered pursuant to subsection (1).

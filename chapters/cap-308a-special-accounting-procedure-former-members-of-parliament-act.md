## CHAPTER 308A

SPECIAL ACCOUNTING PROCEDURE (FORMER MEMBERS OF
PARLIAMENT) ACT
An Act to provide a special accounting procedure respecting public funds advanced
to former Members of Parliament.
[Act No. 29 of 1998.]

[20th November, 1998.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Special Accounting Procedure (Former Members of
Parliament) Act.
2\. Interpretation
For the purposes of this Act “special accounting procedure” means the writing-off, in
accordance with this Act, of public funds by Parliament for which a former Member of
Parliament is liable to account.
3\. Special accounting procedure
(1) Subject to subsection (2), Parliament hereby approves that the special accounting
procedure shall apply in relation to the former Member of Parliament and the quantum of
public funds for which he or she is liable to account as specified in the Schedule.
(2) Subsection (1) shall not apply to—
(a)

a sitting Member of Parliament;

(b)

a former Member of Parliament who has served for less than one full term in
Parliament;

(c)

public funds in an amount less than $5,000.00;

(d)

public funds that have been outstanding for less than two years.

4\. Amendment of Schedule
The Minister of Finance may by order amend the Schedule, but any such order shall
be subject to affirmative resolution of Parliament.

## CHAPTER 265A

PUBLIC MARKET-PLACES CONTROL ACT
An Act to ensure good use of Public Market-Places.
[Act No. 44 of 1992.]

[24th December, 1992.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Public Market-Places Control Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act, “Minister” means the Minister to whom has been assigned responsibility
for Local Government.
3\. Wilful and unlawful destruction or damage
No person shall wilfully and unlawfully destroy, damage or deface any designated
public market-place or any equipment, installation, toilet, lavatory, wash-basin, facebasin, fixture or other apparatus placed therein or attached thereto, or other facility or
amenity provided therein, or any part of any such equipment, installation, toilet, lavatory,
wash-basin, face-basin, fixture, other apparatus, facility or amenity.
4\. Proscribed urinating or defecating
No person shall in a designated public market-place urinate or defecate in any part
other than the proper part of the toilets or lavatories provided for the purpose of urinating
or defecating and identified as such by a sign or notice inscribed with the word “Toilets”
or the word “Lavatories” suitably displayed.

5\. Regulations to be observed
Every person who uses a designated public-market place shall observe the regulations
made under this Act for the proper running of such a place, and for preserving and
maintaining its physical appearance and the facilities and amenities provided therein.
6\. What is a “designated public market-place”
(1) In this Act, the term “designated public market-place” means a place provided,
maintained and managed by the State for the public to carry on commercial business,
such place being identified by the State as a designated public market-place; and the term
includes the places commonly referred to as the St. George’s Market, the Grenville
Market, the Sauteurs Market, the Victoria Market, the Gouyave Market, and the
Hillsborough Market.
(2) Designated market-places other than those specified in subsection (1) may be
appointed by the Minister by regulation which shall be published in the Gazette.
7\. Enforcement by police officers or designated persons
(1) Persons found violating this Act or regulations made hereunder may be arrested and
taken before the Magistrate’s Court by any member of the Royal Grenada Police Force or
by any person appointed by the Minister as a designated person under subsection (2).
(2) The Minister may appoint any person a designated person for the purpose of
enforcing this Act or regulations made hereunder, but no appointment of a person as such
a designated person shall take effect before that appointment is published in the Gazette.
8\. Penalties
Anyone who contravenes any provision of this Act or of regulations made hereunder
shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars, or to
imprisonment for six months, or to both such fine and imprisonment:
Provided that where a person is convicted for wilful and unlawful destruction or
damage under section 3, he or she shall, in addition to being liable to the fine, or
imprisonment, or both prescribed by this section, be required to pay the State the money
value of the said destruction or damage.
9\. Minister may make regulations
The Minister may make regulations generally for the better carrying-out of the
provisions of this Act and in particular for—
(i) fixing fees for the use of any of the facilities or amenities provided as part of
a designated market-place,
(ii) regulating the collection of fees fixed under paragraph (i), and
(iii) appointing any place a designated public market-place or discontinuing a
designated public market-place from being such.

PUBLIC MARKET-PLACES REGULATIONS
[SRO 26 of 1994.]

[15th July, 1994.]
1\. Citation
These Regulations may be cited as the Public Market-Places Regulations.
2\. St. George’s Abattoir designated a public market-place
The St. George’s Abattoir on Melville Street, St. George’s, is, under sections 6(2) and
9(iii) of the Act, hereby appointed a designated public market-place and is hereinafter
referred to as the St. George’s Abattoir.
3\. Fees for renting or otherwise using stipulated market-places
Persons renting or otherwise using the facilities provided at the market-places referred
to in this paragraph shall in respect of such renting or other use pay the fees set out in the
First Schedule in the case of the St. George’s Market and St. George’s Abattoir; the fees
set out in the Second Schedule in the case of the Grenville Market, the Sauteurs Market,
the Victoria Market and the Gouyave Market; and the fees set out in the Third Schedule
in the case of the Hillsborough Market.
4\. To whom fees payable
The fees payable under regulation 3 of these Regulations shall be paid to the Market
Attendant on duty as and when the fees become payable.
5\. Authority for regulations 3 and 4

Regulations 3 and 4 of these Regulations are made under section 9 (i) and (ii)
respectively of the Act.

PUBLIC MARKET-PLACES (NO. 2) REGULATIONS
[1st October, 2003.]
1\. Citation
These Regulations may be cited as the Public Market-Places (No. 2) Regulations.
2\. Authority for Regulations
These Regulations are made under the Public Market-Places Control Act,

## Chapter 265A, hereinafter referred to as “the Principal Act”.

3\. Melville Street Market-Place designated a public market-place

The Melville Street Market-Place, situated on Melville Street, Saint George occupying
lands formerly referred to as “The Esplanade”, together with certain other reclaimed lands
to the south of the said lands, is under sections 6(2) and 9(iii) of the Principal Act hereby
appointed a designated public market-place and is hereinafter referred to as “the Melville
Street Market-Place”.
4\. Fees payable by bus drivers using the Melville Street Market-Place
Bus drivers using the Melville Street Market-Place shall pay a fee of five dollars daily.
5\. Fees payable by vendors renting booths from Government
Vendors renting booths from Government shall pay a fee of seventy-five dollars
monthly, and shall be responsible for paying their own electricity charges to the
appropriate authority.
6\. To whom fees are payable
Fees payable under regulations 4 and 5 of these Regulations shall be paid to the
Market Attendant on duty as and when the fees become payable.
7\. Authority for regulations 4 and 5
Regulations 4 and 5 herein are made under the provisions of sections 6(1) and 9(i) and
(ii) of the Principal Act.
8\. Commencement
These Regulations shall come into force on the 1st day of October, 2003.

## CHAPTER 301

SENATE DISQUALIFICATION ACT
An Act to provide for the disqualification of public officers, with certain exceptions,
from appointment to membership of the Senate.
[Act No. 5 of 1967.]

[3rd March, 1967.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Senate Disqualification Act.
2\. Disqualification from appointment as Senator
No public officer or other person holding or acting in any public office shall be
appointed or be qualified to be appointed as a Senator.
3\. Act not to apply with respect to certain public offices
Notwithstanding section 2, this Act does not apply with respect to any public officer
or other person holding or acting in the office of Attorney-General or to a person who is a
teacher registered as such in the Teachers’ Register pursuant to section 13 of the
Education Act, Chapter 86.

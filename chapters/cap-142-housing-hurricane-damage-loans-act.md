## CHAPTER 142

HOUSING (HURRICANE DAMAGE) LOANS ACT
An Act to provide for the making of loans for the repair and reconstruction of
houses damaged by hurricane.
[20th July, 1957.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Housing (Hurricane Damage) Loans Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“Authority” means the person or body of persons appointed or designated by the
Minister from time to time to be the Authority for the purpose of this Act, and until
some other appointment is made, shall mean the officer in charge of hurricane
rehousing known as the Housing Officer;
“Registrar” means the Registrar of the Supreme Court.
3\. Loans in respect of damaged property

(1) It shall be lawful for the Authority out of funds provided by Parliament to make,
in accordance with regulations contained in the subsidiary legislation, loans for the repair
or reconstruction of house property damaged or destroyed by hurricane.
(2) The regulations contained in the subsidiary legislation may, from time to time, be
amended, added to or revoked by regulations made by the Minister.
(3) Regulations made under section 3(2) shall be laid before the House of
Representatives at the next meeting after they are made and if upon notice of motion
given within a period of forty days beginning with the day on which any such regulations
are so laid before it, the House resolves that the regulations be annulled, they shall
forthwith be void, but without prejudice to the validity of anything previously done
thereunder or to the making of new regulations.
(4) In reckoning any such period of forty days no account shall be taken of any time
during which the House of Representatives is dissolved or prorogued for more than seven
days.
4\. Persons eligible for loans
The persons who shall be eligible to receive loans under this Act shall be persons who
might experience hardship in financing the repair or reconstruction of houses damaged or
destroyed by hurricane.
5\. Limit of loans
The amount of a loan shall be limited to the sum required for restoring (for residential
purposes) hurricane damage, authenticated to the satisfaction of the Authority, to
buildings used prior to the hurricane for residential purposes only.
6\. Interest and repayment
Loans shall bear interest at the rate of four per cent per annum and shall be repayable
within a period not exceeding fifteen years from the date of the loan.
7\. Loan a charge on house
(1) A loan made under this Act for the repair or reconstruction of a house and all
interest due thereon shall be a charge on the house in respect of which the loan was made:
Provided that in all cases where the borrower is not the owner in fee simple of the land
upon which the house rests or is built, the house shall, for the sole purpose of this Act, be
deemed to be a personal chattel to which the Bills of Sale Act, Chapter 33, shall apply.
(2) Every charge contemplated by subsection (1) shall be by memorandum of charge
substantially in the form prescribed.
(3) The memorandum of charge shall, on completion, be forwarded to the Registrar
and a copy of the memorandum of charge certified by the Authority shall be supplied by
the Authority to the borrower without payment of any fee.
(4) Upon receipt of such memorandum the Registrar shall file the same in a register to
be kept for that purpose and such register shall be open to inspection by the public on
payment of the prescribed fee.
(5) Where the borrower is the owner of an estate or interest in the land upon which
the house stands and the memorandum of charge in respect of which the loan was made is
expressed to extend to all or any interest held by the borrower in such land, the

memorandum of charge upon being duly filed by the Registrar, shall from the time of
such filing have effect in every way as though it were a deed of mortgage duly executed
and attested and duly registered under the Deeds and Land Registry Act, Chapter 79, and
shall be good and effectual both at law and in equity according to the priority of time of
such filing against every other assurance or disposition of the same estate or interest or
any part thereof and against all judgement creditors of the chargee.
(6) On the repayment of all sums due under a memorandum of charge the Authority shall
issue a receipt substantially in the prescribed form and thereupon the estate or interest
charged shall vest in the person entitled to the equity of redemption without reconveyance or
other formality, and the Registrar shall upon the production to him or her of such receipt
indorse on the memorandum of charge filed by him or her a memorandum to the effect that
the mortgage has been discharged and the Registrar shall sign such indorsement.
(7) Save as otherwise prescribed, no fee shall be payable to the Registrar for things
done in his or her office under this section.
(8) In this section, “prescribed” means prescribed in the Schedule or by regulations
made under section 3(2).
8\. Procedure for recovery of loan and interest
Any money due and payable in respect of a loan under the provisions of this Act and
any interest thereon, may, if unpaid, be recovered—
(a)

in the manner provided by the Real Property Tax Act, Chapter 276, for the
recovery of unpaid taxes; or

(b)

in any case by suit in a court of competent jurisdiction, instituted by and in
the name of the Authority or of the Attorney-General:
Provided that—
(i) any sale under the powers contained in the Real Property Tax Act,

## Chapter 276, shall be only of the house in respect of which the loan is

made, and
(ii) such sale shall have the effect of conveying to the purchaser the right,
title and interest of the owner in the said house subject to all registered
charges thereon prior to the loan.

9\. Inspection of premises
The Authority may by itself, its employees or agents duly authorised, at all reasonable
times on giving forty-eight hours’ notice to the owner or occupier, enter upon any building
in respect of which a loan has been made under this Act and value or inspect the same; and
any person who shall obstruct or cause to be obstructed any such authorised person in the
exercise of the powers conferred by this section, whether by force or by threats or
otherwise, shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of
one thousand dollars and to imprisonment for three months.
10\. Offences
(1) Any person who shall make any statement in any application or declaration to the
Authority which he or she knows to be false or does not believe to be true or who shall
apply any loan under this Act, or any part thereof, to any purpose not authorised by the
Authority or who, except with the consent in writing of the Authority, shall use or permit
or suffer to be used any building on the security of which a loan has been made under this

Act for any purpose other than solely as a residence for himself or herself and his or her
family shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of three
thousand dollars and to imprisonment for six months.
(2) A borrower who wilfully and maliciously does any act calculated to diminish the
value of the security shall be liable, on conviction on indictment, to a fine of five
thousand dollars and to imprisonment for twelve months.
(3) In any prosecution under this section the onus of proving the manner in which a
loan or part thereof has been applied shall be on the accused.
(4) A person who in any way disposes of any land or building on the security of
which a loan has been made, without first obtaining the written authorisation of the
Authority, shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of
five thousand dollars and to imprisonment for six months.
(5) Proceedings for an offence under this section may be commenced at any time
within three years from the date on which the offence was committed.
11\. Exemption from stamp duty
Notwithstanding anything contained in the Stamp Act, Chapter 309, or any other
written law, every memorandum of charge or receipt contemplated by section 7 shall be
exempt from stamp duty.
12\. Sale by Registrar
A sale by the Authority of land or buildings on which there is a charge in favour of the
Authority shall be by public auction and may be conducted by the Registrar.

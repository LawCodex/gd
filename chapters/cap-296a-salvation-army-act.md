## CHAPTER 296A

SALVATION ARMY ACT
An Act to provide for the Incorporation of The Salvation Army and to vest the
property of The Salvation Army in The General for the time being of the
said Salvation Army.
[Act No. 9 of 1997.]

[20th June, 1997.]
1\.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Salvation Army Act.

2\.

Creation of corporation sole

The General of the Salvation Army shall, for the time being be deemed a
corporation sole with perpetual succession and a common seal and shall be known and
described as “The General of the Salvation Army”.
3\. Vesting of property held by persons on behalf of the General of the
Salvation Army
All property in the State of Grenada movable or immovable of whatever kind now
held by or vested in any person or persons for and on behalf of The General of the
Salvation Army and not heretofore transferred or otherwise disposed of is hereby
vested in and shall be deemed the property of The General of The Salvation Army.
4\.

Vesting of property

All property movable or immovable of whatever kind hereafter devised by will,
transferred or otherwise given to The Salvation Army shall be deemed to be vested in
The General of The Salvation Army.

5\.

Rights of suing and acquiring property

The General of The Salvation Army shall sue and be sued in all courts and before all
Justices and others and shall have full power to acquire by purchase, transfer,
donation, exchange, devise, bequest, grant, conveyance or otherwise any real or
personal property in this State or any estate or interest therein.
6\.

General powers of disposition by the General

The General of The Salvation Army shall, subject to powers any restraint,
reservation or condition contained in the document under which he or she shall have
acquired title thereto be entitled to sell, mortgage, lease and otherwise dispose of and
deal with all property which may for the time being be vested in or have been acquired
by him or her under any of the provisions of this Act, and he or she may sue for and
recover all property and effects both real and personal and may assign and transfer
any monies belonging to The Salvation Army or to Paul A. Rader as The General of The
Salvation Army or to any succeeding General of the Salvation Army.
7\.

Power of Attorney

The General of The Salvation Army shall have full power to appoint an attorney or
attorneys for such time and purposes and with such powers as may be stated in the
power of attorney.
8\.

Evidence of appointment

Upon the recording in the Registration Office of this State of copies certified by the
proper appointment officer, or sealed by the proper Department of Her Majesty’s High
Court of Justice in England of documents purporting to be the appointment of and
acceptance of office by a General of The Salvation Army, the person named in such
appointment and acceptance shall be deemed to be The General of The Salvation Army
until the recording of similarly certified copies of new appointment and acceptance.
9\.

Saving of rights of the Queen and her heirs

Nothing in this Act shall prejudice or affect the rights Saving of Her Majesty, her
heirs and successors or of any body politic or corporate or any other person or persons
except such as are mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, from, through or
under them.

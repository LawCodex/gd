## CHAPTER 123C

GOVERNMENT AUTHORISATION LOAN ACT
An Act to authorise the Minister of Finance to raise by way of loan a sum of twenty
million for the purpose of supporting the implementation of the fiscal budget of
the Government for the year two thousand and nine.
[Act No. 8 of 2009.]

[9th April, 2009.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Government Authorisation Loan Act.

### 2.

Authority to raise loan
The Minister of Finance is hereby authorised to raise by way of loan from local
commercial banks in the manner specified in the Schedule a sum not exceeding in the
aggregate twenty million Eastern Caribbean Dollars as may be necessary to support the
implementation of the fiscal budget for the year two thousand and nine at an interest rate
not exceeding nine per cent per annum.

### 3.

Purpose of loan
The money borrowed under the authority of this Act shall be appropriated and applied
to meet the capital and recurrent expenditure incurred in respect of the financial year
commencing January, 2009, as authorised by the Appropriation Act, 2008.

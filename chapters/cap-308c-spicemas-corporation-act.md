AN ACT to provide for the establishment of the Spicemas
Corporation for promoting Carnival and other related
activities in Grenada, and to provide for matters
incidental thereto, and purposes connected therewith.
[ By Notice ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same as
follows–

## PART I

PRELIMINARY
Short title and
commencement.

---

### 1.

— (1) This Act may be cited as the
SPICEMAS CORPORATION ACT, 2011.
(2) This Act shall come into force on a date to be fixed
by the Minister by Notice published in the Gazette.

Interpretation.

### 2.

—(1) In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires–
“agent” means any individual or body corporate
contracted by the Corporation to market and
promote carnival and all connected activities on
behalf of the Corporation;
“Board” means the Spicemas Corporation Board as
established under section 7;
“carnival” includes the promotion, organisation and
co-ordination by the Corporation of a carnival
festival or activity towards the promotion of
culture, which commences on the date of the
launch of carnival by the Corporation and
climaxes between the hours of five o’clock in the
morning of the second Monday in August and
midnight on the day following;
“Chairperson” means the Chairperson of the Board and
includes any person for the time being
performing the functions of the Chairperson;
“Chief Executive Officer” means the Chief Executive
Officer of the Corporation appointed under
section 14 and includes any person appointed to
act as Chief Executive Officer;
“component of carnival” includes calypso, mas and
steelband associations;
“Corporation” means the Spicemas Corporation
established under section 3;

“financial year” means the period of twelve months
commencing on the 1st day of January and
ending on the 31st day of December;
“function” includes powers and duties;
“member” means a member of the Board and includes
the Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for culture;
“State” means the State of Grenada.
(2) This Act does not apply to carnival festivals and
activities held in Carriacou each year which climaxes on the
Tuesday immediately proceeding Ash Wednesday.

## PART II

ESTABLISHMENT OF THE SPICEMAS
CORPORATION
Establishment of
the Corporation.

### 3.

—(1) There is hereby established a Corporation which
shall be also known as the Spicemas Corporation.
(2) The Corporation shall be a body corporate to which
section 49 of the Interpretation Act shall apply.

Powers and
duties of the
Corporation.

### 4.

—(1) The Corporation shall be the sole body responsible
for operating, organizing and managing and promoting
carnival within and outside Grenada in accordance with the
provisions of this Act.
(2) Notwithstanding the generality of sub-section (1),
the Corporation shall–

(a)

organize, promote, conduct and control the
operation and management of carnival either
independently or in conjunction with any other
company or organization concerned with the
development and promotion of carnival;

(b)

carry on any form of activity and business
related to carnival that may from time to time
be approved by the Board;

(c)

make arrangements for the proper and prompt
payment of the prize monies for carnival
competitions held and organized by the
Corporation;

(d)

promote and oversee public education related
to calypso, mas and pan development in
collaboration with relevant government and
non-governmental organizations;

(e)

advise the Minister on the development of all
forms of carnival festivals or activities in
Grenada; and

(f)

engage in any activity which it deems to be
necessary or conducive to the proper discharge
of its functions.

(3) The functions of the Corporation shall include–
(a)

creating adequate sub-committees which shall be
responsible for producing carnival shows under
the auspices of the Corporation;

(b)

co-ordinating all of the carnival festivals and
activities;

(c)

developing and implementing marketing strategies for the promotion of carnival;

(d)

securing financial and other sponsorship for
carnival;

(e)

acting as the co-ordinating and facilitating body
for carnival festivals and activities;

(f)

preparing and issuing guidelines for the
organization of and participation in carnival
shows;

(g)

organising and managing carnival shows;

(h)

preparing an annual budget that must be
submitted to the Minister for approval;

(i)

providing prize monies to the persons entitled to
such;

(j)

consulting with the various components of
carnival in order to improve the content of
shows;

(k)

approving the material contents of calypso and
pageant shows in order to ensure compliance
with the laws of the State;

(l)

preparing and enforcing rules governing the
participation in and judging of shows which shall
be issued to the relevant persons prior to the
finalisation of the programme to which they
relate;

(m) ensuring that the components of carnival are
allowed to conduct their internal affairs without
interference, save and except that where
irreconcilable differences arise within and
between any component of carnival, the
Corporation shall have the authority to make final
determinations in order to safeguard the integrity
of carnival;

Persons holding
carnival festival
with or without
a licence.

(n)

submitting quarterly reports to the Minister;

(o)

assisting the pan, calypso and mass components
of the carnival with institutional strengthening.

### 5.

—(1) A person shall not hold a carnival festival or
activity in an area declared under section 20 to be the location
for a carnival festival or activity operated by the Corporation
unless the person obtains a licence from the Board to do so.
(2) Subject to subsection (3), a person may hold a
carnival festival or activity in a place outside the area declared
under subsection 20, to be the location of the carnival festival
or activity operated by the Corporation.
(3) Where a person holds a carnival festival or activity
without obtaining a licence from the Board under subsection
(2), he shall not be entitled to any assistance, financial or
otherwise from the Board.
(4) A person who is desirous of obtaining a licence
from the Board to hold a carnival festival or activity shall
make an application in writing to the Board after the date of
the official launching of carnival by order of the Corporation.

(5) An application shall contain such particulars as shall
be prescribed by the Board.
(6) If the Board is satisfied that the application is
complete and the person making the application is eligible to
operate a carnival festival or activity, it may grant the
application and issue to the applicant a licence subject to such
terms and conditions as it considers necessary.
(7) Any person who is aggrieved by a decision of the
Board may appeal to the Minister against the decision.
(8) Every appeal under sub-section (7) shall be made to
the Minister within fourteen days of the date of the notice of
the decision appealed against and shall set out the ground for
appeal and where applicable be submitted with a copy of the
decision appealed against.
(9) The Minister shall request the records relating to the
matter from the Board, which shall submit them to the
Minister within seven days, and within fourteen days of the
receipt of such records the Minister shall submit them to an
appeal tribunal appointed by him to adjudicate on the matter.
(10) The appeal tribunal appointed by the Minister
under subsection (9) shall consist of one or more, but not
more than three persons to be appointed by the Minister upon
prior approval of Cabinet.
(11) The qualification for and terms of service of a
member of the appeal tribunal shall be determined by Cabinet.
(12) The appeal tribunal may regulate its own
procedure.

(13) The appeal tribunal may, after making such enquiry
as it thinks fit and after giving the appellant a reasonable
opportunity to present his case–
(a)

allow or dismiss the appeal;

(b)

uphold the decision of the Board; or

(c)

reserve or vary the decision of the Board;

and without prejudice to the right of an aggrieved party to the
appeal to apply to the Supreme Court for relief, every decision
of the tribunal shall be final.
Restriction on
person holding
carnival festival
or activity.

### 6.

Any person who is desirous of holding a carnival
festival or activity on a day on which carnival festival or
activity of the Corporation is scheduled to take place shall not
be allowed to do so if such festival or activity is deemed by
the Corporation to have competing interests with its event.

Establishment
of Board.

### 7.

—(1) The Corporation shall be managed by a Board
which shall consist of the following persons appointed by the
Minister upon prior approval from Cabinet–
(a)

a Chairperson;

(b)

a Police Officer, not below the rank of
Inspector;

(c)

an attorney-at-law with no less than five years
standing;

(d)

subject to subsection (2) a person nominated
from each of the following Associations–
(iv) the Grenada Mass Bands Association;

(v)

the Grenada Steel Band Association; and

(vi) the Calypso fraternity;
(e)

three other persons who are qualified in one or
more of the following areas–
(v)

business and financial management;

(vi) human resource management;
(vii) marketing and advertising; and
(viii) public relations; and
(f)

the Chief Executive Officer, who shall be an
ex-officio member and the secretary to the
Board.

(2) A person nominated under subsection (1) (d) shall
not be the leader or a person actively involved in the
management or organization of a mas band, a steel band or a
calypso association.
(3) A member of the Board shall be appointed by the
Minister for a term not exceeding three years and shall be
eligible for re-appointment.
(4) The member of the Board shall at the first meeting
of the Board, elect a Deputy Chairperson from amongst their
membership.
(5) If a member is absent on account of illness or for
any temporary cause or is temporarily absent from the State,
the Minister shall for a period not exceeding three months
appoint some other person to act as a temporary member.

(6) A member of the Board may at any time resign his
office by instrument in writing addressed to the Chairperson
who shall immediately cause it to be forwarded to the
Minister.
(7) If a person ceases to be a member of the Board for
any reason, Cabinet may appoint some other person in his
place for the duration of time for which that member would
have served.
(8) Cabinet may at any time revoke the appointment of
any member of the Board if it is expedient to do so.

Seal of the

—(1) The seal of the Board shall be kept in the custody

Service upon the Board of a notice, order or other
document is executed by delivering it to the Chief Executive
Officer by sending it by registered post addressed to the Chief
Executive Officer at the office of the Corporation.

Declaration of
interest.

### 11.

—(1) A member of the Board who is in any way,
whether directly or indirectly interested in any application
made to the Board or a contract or proposed contract with the
Board, shall declare the nature of his interest at the first
meeting of the Board at which it is possible for the member
to do so.
(2) A member of the Board shall not participate in the
discussion of or vote in respect of–
(a)

an application to the Board; or

(b)

a contract or proposed contract with the
Board, in which he is in any way directly or
indirectly interested.

Power to
delegate.

### 12.

Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Board may
delegate to a member of the Board the power and authority to
carry out on behalf of the Board any duties to be determined
by the Board.

Remuneration
of members and
others.

### 13.

—(1) Any remuneration, fees or allowances payable to
the Chairman, Deputy Chairman and other members of the
Board shall be determined by the Minister upon prior
approval by Cabinet and all remuneration fees or allowances
shall be paid from the funds of the Corporation.

(2) If a person, other than a member is co-opted under
section (8), the Board may, with the approval of the Minister,
agree for an allowance to be paid to the person out of the
funds of the Corporation.
Appointment of
Chief Executive
Officer.

### 14.

—(1) The Board shall appoint a Chief Executive
Officer upon terms and conditions to be approved by the
Board.
(2) The Chief Executive Officer shall be responsible for
the day to day operation of the Corporation and must be
present at all meetings of the Board unless he is required by
the Chairperson to withdraw from a meeting, has obtained a
leave of absence from the Chairperson or he is incapacitated
by illness or any other cause from attending the meeting.
(3) In the event of the absence of the Chief Executive
Officer, the Board may by instrument in writing, appoint a
person to act as Chief Executive Officer during the period of
absence on the terms and conditions determined by the Board.

Appointment of
officers and
agents.

### 15.

—(1) The Board may employ at such remuneration,
terms and conditions it thinks fit such officers and agents
necessary for the proper carrying out of its functions under
this Act.
(2) Where an agent is employed by the Board, the Board
shall prepare a written contract for signature by the Board and
the agent setting out the terms of employment of the agent.

Validity of acts
of the Board.

### 16.

The validity of an act done or proceedings taken under
this Act shall not be questioned on the grounds of–
(a)

the existence of a vacancy in the membership of
or defect in constitution of the Board;

(b)

the contravention by a member of the Board of
the provisions of section 11; or

(c)

an omission, effect or irregularity not affecting
the merits of the case.

Protection of
members.

### 17.

No personal liability shall attach to any member of the
Board or the Chief Executive Officer, in respect of anything
done or suffered in good faith under the provisions of this
Act.

Issuing of policy
directions.

### 18.

—(1) The Minister may, after consultation with the
Chairperson, give to the Board, directions of a general
character as to the policy to be followed in the performance of
its functions, in relation to matters appearing to the Minister
to be of public interest.
(2) The Board shall, in accordance with the direction of
the Minister, develop specific policy guidelines as it thinks
necessary and may circulate and disseminate them with the
consent of the Minister.

Directions.

### 19.

—(1) The Minister may in writing, in respect of
carnival, authorize the Board to issue guidelines to any
member of the Royal Grenada Police Force or to any
designated person as defined by this section to give
directions–
(a)

to any person attending a carnival festival or
activity or selling goods or providing services
at the carnival festival or activity, as to the
manner in which such activities shall be
conducted as may be stipulated by such

member of the Police Force or a designated
person;
(b)

to persons selling goods or providing services
at such carnival festival or activity regarding–
(i)

the location at such festival at which
goods may be sold or services rendered;

(ii)

the distance between persons selling
goods or providing services at such
festival or activity; and

(iii) the conditions under which goods may
be sold or services rendered at such
festival or activity not including the fees
to be paid by persons selling goods or
rendering services.
(2) When the Minister gives an authorization under
subsection (1), he shall cause it to be published in the Gazette,
and in two local newspapers circulating regularly within
Grenada.
(3) A “designated” person referred to in subsection (1)
is any person designated in writing by the Board to give
directions under this section.
(4) Directions issued by the Board under subsection (1)
may be oral and may be varied by the Board during the
carnival festival or activity as the Board sees fit.

### 21.

Nothing in this Act shall be construed as restricting or
authorizing the restricting of a person in the use and
enjoyment of private real property owned, occupied or used
by him; nor shall anything in this Act be construed as
requiring or authorizing the requiring of a person to pay a fee
for the use and enjoyment of private real property owned,
occupied or used by him; but the provisions of this Act
authorizing the controlling of music, sounds or other noise
shall apply to persons owning, occupying or using private real
property situated within the actual site or sites of a public
festival or within any extended protected perimeter referred
to in section 20.

## PART III

FINANCIAL PROVISIONS
Definition of
Minister.

### 22.

For the purposes of this Part, except where expressly
stated “Minister” means the Minister responsible for Finance.

Funds of the
Corporation.

### 23.

—(1) The funds and resources of the Corporation shall
consist of–
(a)

funds arising from the sale, lease or other
disposition of property vested in the
Corporation or from the conduct of shows or
any other carnival activities;

(b)

funds arising from any source consequential
upon the performance of its functions under
this Act;

(c)

sums borrowed by the Board for the purpose
of meeting any of its obligations or
discharging its functions;

(d)

funds allocated to the Corporation by
Parliament;

(e)

all other sums or property which may become
payable or vested in the Corporation in respect
of any matter incidental to its powers and
duties.

(2) All sums referred to in sub-section (1) shall be paid
into and placed to the credit of the Corporation in one or more
bank accounts approved by the Minister.

Borrowing
powers.

### 24.

Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Corporation
may borrow sums required by it for meeting any of its
obligations or discharging any of its functions under this Act.

Guarantee of
borrowing by
Corporation.

### 25.

—(1) The Minister may guarantee on conditions he may
see fit, the payment of the principal, interest and other charges
in any authorised borrowing by the Corporation.
(2) The Corporation shall make to the Accountant
General, at the time and in the manner directed by the
Minister–
(a)

payments of the amounts toward repayment
of any sums issued in fulfillment of any
guarantee given under this section; and

(b)

payments of interest on what is outstanding
for the time being in respect of any sums so
issued at a rate that the Minister may
direct, and different rates of interest may be
directed as regards different sums and as
regards interest for difficult periods.

Reserve Fund.

### 26.

The Corporation shall, with the consent of the
Minister, establish a Reserve Fund for specific contingencies
and this fund shall be dealt with and be applied in accordance
with its stated objective, or the objectives that may be deemed
expedient by the Board, with the consent of the Minister.

Application of
funds.

### 27.

The funds of the Corporation shall be applied for the
purposes authorised by this Act and towards the development
of culture and any other related programmes approved by the
Board with the consent of the Minister.

Account and
audit.

### 28.

—(1) The Corporation shall–
(a)

keep proper books of accounts;

(b)

record its transactions to the satisfaction of the
Minister; and

(c)

prepare and retain financial statements in
respect of each financial year.

(2) The accounts of the Corporation shall be audited by
the Director of Audit or an auditor duly appointed by him in
each financial year and the officers and staff of the
Corporation shall provide to him all books, deeds, contracts,
accounts, vouchers or other documents which he may see it fit
to call for.
(3) The Director of Audit may require any person
holding or accountable for a document to appear before him
and make a signed statement in relation to the document and
may require from that person any necessary information
regarding same.
(4) A person who, when required to do so fails without
reasonable cause to–
(a)

produce a document;

(b)

appear before the auditor;

(c)

make and sign a statement; or

(d)

furnish information

commits an offence and shall be liable upon summary
conviction to a fine not exceeding five hundred dollars or to
a term of imprisonment not exceeding one month.

Auditor’s report.

### 29.

The Director of Audit shall as soon as practicable and
not later than three months after the end of each financial year
send copies of the audited financial statements and the report
to the Corporation.

Report of the
Board.

### 30.

—(1) The Board shall not later than four months after
the expiration of the financial year submit to the Minister a
report containing–
(a)

an account of the activities of the Corporation
during the preceding financial year in a form
which the Minister may direct; and

(b)

the audited financial statements of the
Corporation done in accordance with the
provisions of section 25 (2);

(2) The Minister of Culture shall, within eight weeks of
the receipt of the report, cause a copy of the report together
with a copy of the audited statement of the accounts and the
report of the Director of Audit to be laid on the table of the
House of Representatives.

MISCELLANEOUS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS
Exemption from

### 31.

for the registration of documents or the payment of stamp

Income tax

### 32.

Notwithstanding anything contained in the Income Tax
Act, the income of the Corporation shall not be liable to
income tax.

Exemption from

### 33.

Notwithstanding the provisions of any law providing
for the payment of customs duties, all equipment, software,
advertising and promotional material, stationery and ticket
supplies imported by the Corporation for the conduct of its
activities shall not be liable to customs duty.

Offences and

### 34.

—(1) A person who–
(a)

obtains any sum of money from the Corporation
by means of any false representation;

(b)

Corporation,

commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a

(2) A person who promotes or conducts any carnival

### 35.

Unclaimed

### 36.

Money prizes awarded during a carnival festival or
activity by the Corporation, that are unclaimed without
reasonable explanation being given to the Corporation after
ninety days shall be transferred to a special account entitled
the “unclaimed prizes account” and other prizes shall be sold
after ninety days and the monies realized shall be paid into
that account.

Regulations.

### 37.

The Minister may make Regulations generally for the
proper carrying out of the provisions of this Act, and in
particular, may make Regulations–

## CHAPTER 128B

GRENADA CORPORATE REGISTRY ACT
An Act to provide for the establishment of the Grenada Corporate Registry, and for
matters connected therewith and incidental thereto.
[Act No. 22 of 1999.]

[10th September, 1999.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Grenada Corporate Registry Act.
2\. Interpretations
In this Act—
“corporate services” includes the incorporation of companies generally, the
incorporation of international banks, insurance companies and trusts;
“Deputy Registrar” means the Deputy Registrar of Corporate Services and
Intellectual Property;
“Minister” means the Minister of Legal Affairs;
“Registrar” means the Registrar of Corporate Services and Intellectual Property.
3\. Establishment of Corporate Registry
(1) There is hereby established the Grenada Corporate Registry.

(2) The Registrar shall be responsible for the administration of the Registry and shall
be assisted by the Deputy Registrar and such other officers as may be necessary for the
purpose of carrying out the functions of the Registrar under this Act and any other
enactments.
4\. Creation of the post of Registrar, etc.
(1) The posts of Registrar and Deputy Registrar, Corporate Services are hereby
created by this Act.
(2) The Registrar and Deputy Registrar shall be appointed by the Judicial and Legal
Services Commission; but no person may be appointed to the post of Registrar unless he
or she is an attorney-at-law with at least two years call to the Grenada Bar or to any Bar
of a CARICOM State.
5\. Functions of Registrar
(1) The Registrar shall perform such functions as prescribed by or under the
enactments specified in the First Schedule.
(2) The Registrar and Deputy Registrar shall have such rights and discharge such
duties relating to the office of Notary Public as are necessary for the purpose of carrying
out the functions referred to in subsection (1).
6\. Collection of fees
The Registrar shall collect such fees as may be payable under any enactment specified
in the First Schedule and shall pay the same into the Consolidated Fund.
7\. Amendment of enactments and construction thereof
(1) The enactments specified in column one of the Second Schedule are amended to
the extent specified in the second column thereof.
(2) Without prejudice to anything contained in subsection (1), the enactments
specified in the First Schedule shall be construed with such modifications and adaptations
so as to give the Registrar jurisdiction thereunder.
8\. Commencement
This Act comes into force on the 10th day of September, 1999.

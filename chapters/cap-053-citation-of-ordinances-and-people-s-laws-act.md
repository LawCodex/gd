## CHAPTER 53

CITATION OF ORDINANCES AND PEOPLE’S LAWS ACT
An Act to make provision for the citation of certain laws and to provide for
matters incidental thereto and connected therewith.
[Act No. 19 of 1989.]

[14th July, 1989.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Citation of Ordinances and People’s Laws Act.

### 2.

Substitution of Acts for Ordinances, People’s Laws, etc.

(1) Where the word “Ordinance”, or the words “People’s Law” or “Law” (meaning
thereby a People’s Law proclaimed or ordered and declared by the People’s
Revolutionary Government) occur in the short title or other similar appellation or
designation of any law enacted before the 1st January, 1985, and in force at the
commencement of this Act, there shall be substituted the word “Act”, and the law may
be cited accordingly as an Act.
(2) Where, in any law which may, pursuant to subsection (1), be cited as an Act,
the word “Ordinance” or the words “People’s Law” or “Law” (meaning thereby a
People’s Law as aforesaid) occur (whether in reference to that law or any other law) or
a reference is made to any other law by reference to a short title or other similar
appellation or designation that includes the word “Ordinance” or the words “People’s
Law” or “Law”, there shall be substituted the word “Act” where those words so occur or
are referred to.
(3) Where in any Act of Parliament enacted after the 31st December, 1984, or in
any statutory instrument the word “Ordinance” or the words “People’s Law” or “Law”
(meaning thereby a People’s Law as aforesaid) occur or a reference is made to any
other law by reference to a short title or other similar appellation or designation that
includes any such words, there shall be substituted the word “Act” where those words
so occur or are referred to.
(4) For the purposes of subsection (3)—
(a)

“Act of Parliament” means an enactment (of the Parliament of Grenada
established by section 23 of the Constitution) other than this Act and the
Interpretation and General Provisions Act, Chapter 153;

(b)

### 3.

“statutory instrument” means any proclamation, rule, regulation, order,
rule of court, by-law, notice or other instrument (not being an Ordinance,
a People’s Law as herein mentioned, or an Act) of a legislative as distinct
from an executive character and having the force of law, made under an
Act of Parliament (as defined in this subsection) or under a law to which
subsection (1) or (2) applies.

Construction of contracts, etc.

Where there is in force at the commencement of this Act any contract, deed,
agreement or other instrument (not being a statutory instrument as defined in
section 2(4)) containing a reference—
(a)

to the word “Ordinance”, or to the words “People’s Law” or “Law”
(meaning thereby a People’s Law as aforesaid), that reference shall be
read and construed as a reference to the word “Act”;

(b)

to a law by way of a short title or other similar appellation or designation
that includes the word “Ordinance” or the words “People’s Law” or “Law”,
that reference shall be read and construed as a reference to that law as it
may be cited pursuant to this Act,

and where, after the commencement of this Act, in any contract, deed, agreement or
in any such instrument there occurs a reference to any of those words or a reference
to a law by way of a short title or other similar appellation or designation that includes
any of those words, those references shall be read and construed respectively as
references to the word “Act” and to the short title or other similar appellation or
designation as it may be cited pursuant to this Act.

### 4.

Saving

It is hereby declared that this Act is intended to make formal amendments only
and, accordingly, no right, privilege, obligation or liability acquired, accrued or
incurred, or which may accrue or be acquired or incurred under any law or instrument
is or shall be affected by this Act.

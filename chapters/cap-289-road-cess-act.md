## CHAPTER 289

ROAD CESS ACT
An Act to impose a cess, on manufactured articles and produce, for the benefit of the
maintenance and upkeep of the roads of Grenada.
[25th January, 1936.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Road Cess Act.

### 2.

Cess on certain commodities and articles
There shall be raised, levied, collected and paid into the Consolidated Fund, for the
maintenance and upkeep of the roads of Grenada, a cess upon any commodity produced
or any article manufactured in Grenada which may be prescribed by the Minister in the
manner hereinafter appearing.

### 3.

Minister may make regulations
The Minister may make regulations—
(a)

prescribing the commodities produced or the articles manufactured in
Grenada which shall be liable to the road cess, and the amount or amounts
payable thereon and may at any time increase or decrease or annul the said
amounts;

(b)

prescribing the amount payable on any licence issued under the provisions of
this Act;

(c)

prescribing the form of any books or returns which are required by the
provisions of this Act to be kept and the particulars to be entered in the
same;

(d)

prescribing the persons authorised to supervise or perform any operation
prescribed by this Act or any regulations made thereunder;

(e)

prescribing the mode of collection of the cess;

(f)

generally for carrying the purposes or provisions of this Act into effect:

Provided that before any regulation made under the provisions of paragraph (a)
or (b) of this section comes into effect it shall be approved by the House of
Representatives.

### 4.

Licence and amount payable thereon
Every producer or manufacturer of any commodity or article prescribed shall in every
year take out a licence to produce or manufacture the same, and shall pay to the
Permanent Secretary (Finance) the sum prescribed by regulations made under section 3.

### 5.

Partnership
When two or more persons carry on the production or manufacture of any commodity
or article in partnership they shall not be required to take out in every year more than one
licence, but such licence shall be applied for jointly by, and shall be issued in the joint
names of, every such person.

### 6.

Issue of licences
All licences shall be issued in the form prescribed by the Licences Act, Chapter 172,
and shall expire on the 31st December in the year of issue.

### 7.

Books to be kept
(1) Every manufacturer and producer shall keep at his or her factory and place of
business respectively in the approved form and manner such books and forms relating to
the production, manufacture, storing, and delivery of any commodity or manufactured
article to which this Act shall have been applied as the Permanent Secretary (Finance)
may direct, in which he or she shall make the approved entries at the approved times.
Every such entry shall be made legibly in ink and no entry shall be altered in any manner;
but any entry may be cancelled by drawing a single line in ink through the incorrect entry
so as to allow the same to remain legible and a correcting entry shall be made in the
margin of the book or form or in any other approved place instead of the entry so
cancelled.
(2) Such books shall be open at all times for the inspection of all duly authorised
officers and the manufacture or producer shall allow any officer to take any abstract
therefrom at any time.
(3) If any manufacturer or producer—
(a)

fails to keep such books or to produce the same when required by any officer
to do so;

(b)

fails to make in such books legibly in ink at approved times and in the
approved manner any entry required to be made therein; or

(c)

fraudulently or in any manner contrary to the requirements of this Act makes
any entry, obliteration, alteration or erasure in any such book,

he or she guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of five
thousand dollars or, in default, to imprisonment for six months.

### 8.

Payment of cess
Every licensed producer or manufacturer shall before the removal of any commodity
or article pay the cess prescribed by this Act. The Permanent Secretary (Finance) shall
nevertheless be empowered to authorise the removal of such commodity or article upon
bond being furnished to his or her satisfaction for the payment thereof.

### 9.

Seizure and forfeiture
(1) Any commodity or article which is removed in contravention of this Act shall be
liable to forfeiture; and any commodity or article which is liable to forfeiture may be
seized by any duly authorised officer.
(2) The procedure on seizure and forfeiture shall be in like manner as if the
commodity or article had been seized and forfeited under the provisions of sections 83 to
92 of the Excise Act, Chapter 94, and that Act shall apply, mutatis mutandis, to articles
seized under this Act.

### 10.

Power of entry and to require production of book
It shall be lawful for any revenue officer, or any other person authorised in writing by
the Permanent Secretary (Finance), at any reasonable time to enter a factory, premises, or
any place connected therewith and to require the production of any book or paper in
which any produce or article manufactured, or in process of manufacture, is kept, and to
make extracts from any such book or paper.

### 11.

Penalties
Any person who shall—
(a)

produce, manufacture or be concerned in the production or manufacture of
any produce or article prescribed as being liable to the cess in the manner
aforesaid, without a licence;

(b)

make default as aforesaid in paying the cess legally payable;

(c)

knowingly make any false statement, written or oral to any revenue officer
or person authorised;

(d)

fail, without reasonable excuse, to produce any document or to give any
information which he or she may be required by any revenue officer or
person authorised as aforesaid to produce or give;

(e)

obstruct any revenue officer, or person authorised in writing as aforesaid, in
the exercise of any power conferred by this Act; or

(f)

in any other manner contravene any provision of this Act,

shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of five thousand
dollars or, in default, to imprisonment for six months.

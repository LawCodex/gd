## CHAPTER 295

ST. PATRICK'S ANGLICAN CHURCH ACT
AN ACT to provide for the vesting of the St. Patrick’s Anglican Parish Church and
Glebe in the Incorporated Trustees of the Church of England in Grenada
[Act No. 270 of 1958.]

[1st June, 1920.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the St. Patrick's Anglican Church Act.
2\. Vesting of Church and Glebe in Incorporated Trustees
The St. Patrick’s Anglican Parish Church and the lands and Glebe attached or
belonging thereto shall, by virtue of this Act, vest in the Incorporated Trustees of the
Church of England in Grenada for the purposes of the Church of England
Disestablishment Act.
[Cap. 47.]

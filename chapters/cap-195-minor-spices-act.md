## CHAPTER 195

MINOR SPICES ACT
An Act to control the export of minor spices, and for connected purposes.
[Act No. 48 of 1980 amended by Act No. 52 of 1991.]

[24th October, 1980.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Minor Spices Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“Minister” means the Minister for the time being responsible for agriculture;
“minor spices” means cloves, cinnamon, pimento, and all other spices except
nutmegs and mace;
“Society” means the Minor Spices Co-operative Marketing Society Limited which
is registered under the Co-operative Societies Act, Chapter No. 70 of 1958;
“trafficker” means a person who is licensed as a trafficker under the Licences Act,

## Chapter 172.

### 3.

Society to be sole exporter of minor spices
(1) Subject to subsection (2), the Society shall be the sole exporter of minor spices.

(2) The Society may grant annual permits to traffickers authorising them to export
minor spices to CARICOM Countries.
(3) A person who, for the purposes of trade, exports minor spices without a permit or
in contravention of any condition in a permit shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on
summary conviction, to a fine of two thousand dollars and to imprisonment for twelve
months.

### 4.

Minor spices may not be purchased except for use in the home
(1) Subject to subsection (2), a person shall not purchase minor spices in Grenada
except for use in the home.
(2) A trafficker or other person whose business includes dealing in minor spices in
Grenada shall not purchase minor spices for the purposes of trade except from the
Association.
(3) A person who contravenes subsection (1) or (2) shall be guilty of an offence and
liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of one thousand dollars and to imprisonment for
six months.

### 5.

Power of Minister to nominate persons to the Society
Notwithstanding the rules of the Society the Minister may nominate to the committee of
management of the Society two persons who appear to him or her as having had experience in
finance, agriculture, marketing, economics, agricultural economics or management.

### 6.

Fees for permits
(1) The Society shall charge fees for permits issued under section 3(2) of such amount
as the Minister may, from time to time, by Order prescribe, and until such fee is
prescribed the amount specified in the Schedule shall be charged.
(2) A permit issued under this Act shall expire on the 31st December following the
date of issue.

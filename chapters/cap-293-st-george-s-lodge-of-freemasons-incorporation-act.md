## CHAPTER 293

ST. GEORGE'S LODGE OF FREEMASONS (INCORPORATION) ACT
An Act for the incorporation of certain persons as Trustees of the St. George’s
Lodge of Freemasons.
[Amended by Act No. 24 of 1964.]

[15th April, 1935.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the St. George's Lodge of Freemasons (Incorporation) Act.

### 2.

Incorporation of trustees
Samuel Wright Brathwaite and his successors in office as Worshipful Master of the St.
George’s Lodge of Freemasons in Grenada, William Gregory Donelan, Senior Warden of
the said Lodge, and Ewart Wynfred de Vere Archer, Junior Warden of the said Lodge,
and their successors in office as Senior Warden and Junior Warden of the said Lodge
respectively shall be and are hereby created a body corporate by the name of “The
Incorporated Trustees of the St. George’s Lodge of Freemasons of Grenada” (hereinafter
called “the Trustees”) with perpetual succession, and with power in that name to sue and
be sued in all courts, and with a Common Seal which may from time to time be changed:
Provided that no such change shall be made except by a resolution of the members
in open lodge assembled.

### 3.

Power of trustees to acquire property
The Trustees shall have power to acquire for the St. George’s Lodge of Freemasons in
Grenada by purchase, transfer, exchange, devise, gift, demise, bequest, grant or otherwise
any land or other property of whatever kind whether in perpetuity or for life or for a term

of years and any money, securities, goods or chattels whatever, to take, hold and enjoy
the same subject to the performance of any trust upon which the same may have been
acquired.

### 4.

Power of Trustees to dispose of property, and to borrow, etc.
Subject to the performance of any trust as aforesaid the Trustees may—
(a)

by deed under the Common Seal sell, demise, grant, convey, transfer,
exchange, surrender or otherwise dispose of any real or personal property
vested in or which may hereafter be acquired by or become vested in the
Trustees;

(b)

borrow money and, for the payment of the same, by deed under the Common
Seal, give and execute mortgages, charges, pledges and other securities of
any real or personal property or make any arrangement that they consider
convenient with such covenants, powers and provisions as they think proper.

### 5.

Vesting of property
All real property belonging to the St. George’s Lodge of Freemasons at and after the
commencement of this Act is vested in the Trustees.

### 6.

Affixing of Common Seal
All documents requiring the Common Seal shall be sealed in the presence of at least
three of the Trustees who shall sign every such document.

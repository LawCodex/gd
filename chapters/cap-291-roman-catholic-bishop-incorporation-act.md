## CHAPTER 291

ROMAN CATHOLIC BISHOP (INCORPORATION) ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of the Roman Catholic Bishop in Grenada.
[31st December, 1957.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Roman Catholic Bishop (Incorporation) Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act the expression “successor in office” means the persons from time to time
having, by virtue of the canon law of the Roman Catholic Church, or by appointment by
the proper authority in Rome, supreme ecclesiastical jurisdiction in Grenada in that
Church.

### 3.

Incorporation of Roman Catholic Bishop
The Right Reverend Monseignor James Justin Field, Bishop of Saint George in
Grenada, the Roman Catholic Bishop in Grenada, and his successors in office shall be a
body corporate, and shall have the name of “The Roman Catholic Bishop in Grenada”,
and by that name shall have perpetual succession, and full power to purchase, take, hold,
enjoy, sell, transport, mortgage, lease, invest, and otherwise deal with, and dispose of, all
property, movable and immovable, of what soever nature or kind, hereby vested and
hereafter acquired and may sue and be sued in all courts.

### 4.

Vesting of property held by Archbishop of Port-of-Spain
All property in Grenada, movable or immovable, of what nature or kind soever, now
held by or vested in the Archbishop of Port-of-Spain, and not heretofore transferred or
otherwise disposed of, whether the transfers or conveyances thereof vest the property in
that bishop in his own proper name or in that bishop as vicar apostolic shall be held by,
and the same are hereby vested in, the said body corporate.

### 5.

Vesting of property transferred to Church
All property, movable or immovable of whatever nature or kind, here to before or
hereafter bequeathed by will, or otherwise given to the Roman Catholic Church in
Grenada, shall be held by, and is hereby vested in, and shall be deemed to be the property
of, the said body corporate.

### 6.

Property to be held for use of Church
All property vested in, or hereafter acquired by, the said body corporate shall be held
by the said body corporate for the use and benefit of the Roman Catholic Church in
Grenada.

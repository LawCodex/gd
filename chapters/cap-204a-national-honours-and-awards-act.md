## CHAPTER 204A

NATIONAL HONOURS AND AWARDS ACT
An Act to provide for the grant of awards to citizens of Grenada and other persons
for distinguished, outstanding or meritorious services or achievements, or for
gallantry and related matters.
[Act No. 32 of 2007.]

[9th January, 2008.]

## PART I

Preliminary

---

### 1.

Short title and commencement
(1) This Act may be cited as the National Honours and Awards Act.
(2) This Act shall come into effect by a day to be fixed by the Governor-General by
Notice published in the Gazette.

### 2.

Interpretation
(1) In this Act—
“Commission” means the Prestige Order of National Heroes Commission
established pursuant to section 16;
“Committee” means the National Awards Advisory Committee established
pursuant to section 8;
“Order” means the Order of Grenada established pursuant to section 3.
(2) A reference in this Act to any of the following—
(a)

the Companion of the Order of Grenada;

(b)

the Order of Grenada Gold Award for Excellence;

(c)

the Spice Isle Awards;

(d)

the Camerhogne Award;

(e)

the Medal of Honour; and

(f)

the Most Distinguished Order of the Nation,

is a reference to the respective title of honour or award described in the First Schedule.
(3) A reference in this Act to the Prestige Order of National Hero is a reference to the
Prestige Order of National Hero described in the Second Schedule.

## PART II

Order of Grenada

### 3.

Recognition of Order of Grenada
There is hereby established the society of honour known as “The Order of Grenada”.

### 4.

Constitution of Order of Grenada
The Order of Grenada consists of—

the Governor-General as its Chancellor; and

(b)

the living holders of awards in the following grades—
(i) the Companion of the Order of Grenada,
(ii) the Order of Grenada Gold Award for Excellence,
(iii) the Spice Isle Award,
(iv) the Camerhogne Award,
(v) the Medal of Honour, and
(vi) the Most Distinguished Order of the Nation which is an Order of
Knighthood.

### 5.

Seal
(1) There shall be a Seal of the Order which shall be kept in the custody of the
Chancellor.
(2) An appointment to the Order and a revocation to the Order of Grenada is of no
effect unless it bears the Seal of the Order of Grenada.
(3) The affixing of the Seal of the Order of Grenada shall be authenticated by the
signature of the Chancellor.

### 6.

Functions of the Chancellor
(1) The Chancellor of the Order shall be responsible for the administration of the
Order.
(2) Where the Governor-General is absent, the person who is performing the
functions of the office of the Governor-General shall discharge the functions of the
Chancellor of the Order.

### 7.

Secretary of the Order
The Private Secretary to the Governor-General is the secretary of the Order and shall
maintain the records of the Order arrange for investitures and perform such other
functions in respect of the Order as the Governor-General may require.

### 8.

National Awards Advisory Committee
(1) There is hereby established a standing committee to be known as the National
Awards Advisory Committee which shall comprise of the following members—
(a)

a Chairperson who shall be a citizen of Grenada appointed by the GovernorGeneral after consultation with the Prime Minister and the Leader of the
Opposition;

(b)

the Chairperson of the Public Service Commission;

(c)

the Commissioner of Police;

(d)

two persons representative of the public appointed by the GovernorGeneral—
(i) one of whom shall be appointed on the advice of the Prime Minister,
and
(ii) one of whom shall be appointed on the advice of the Leader of the
Opposition;

(e)

two persons appointed by the Governor-General in the following manner—
(i) after consultation with civil society, and
(ii) after consultation with the religious community.

(2) The provisions of the Third Schedule shall have effect as to the term of, and
procedure to be followed by the Committee and otherwise in relation thereto.

### 9.

Functions of the Committee
(1) The Committee shall—
(a)

in accordance with section 10, consider nominations of persons together with
recommendations and supporting material received by the Committee for an
award of the Order of Grenada;

(b)

make such investigations as it thinks fit to determine persons living or dead,
upon whom may be conferred the award of the Order of Grenada;

(c)

compile separate lists in respect of each grade of awards;

(d)

forward to the Prime Minister the lists compiled under paragraph (c) with the
recommendations respecting each awards;

(e)

undertake such other duties in relation to the Act as may be assigned by the
Governor-General.

(2) The Committee may consult with such other persons or bodies as it think
necessary for the purpose of discharging its functions.

### 10.

Nomination and Conferment of an award of Order
(1) Any person may submit to the Committee for its consideration and
recommendation a nomination of a person for an award of the Order.
(2) Upon receipt of a nomination under subsection (1), the Committee shall consider
such nomination, conduct such investigations as it may deem necessary and submit its
recommendation to the Prime Minister.
(3) The Prime Minister shall review the recommendations submitted to him or her by
the Committee and may make such recommendations to the Governor-General for the
conferment of awards as he or she may deem necessary.
(4) The recommendations made to the Governor-General under subsection (3) shall
be announced publicly and shall publish in the Gazette.
(5) In the exercise of the powers of the Governor-General under this Act, the
Governor-General shall act on the advice of the Prime Minister.
(6) Subject to the provisions of this Act, each year on Independence Day, the 7th day
of February, or at such other times as may be expedient, the Governor-General may
confer awards on persons, living or dead who have rendered distinguished and
meritorious service to Grenada or who are otherwise deemed worthy of such awards.
(7) An award granted by the Governor-General under this Part shall be granted by
Proclamation, sealed with the Seal of the Order and shall have effect from the date of the
Seal unless another effective date is specified in the Proclamation.

(8) An award granted under this section may be granted posthumously but a deceased
recipient shall not become a member of the Order.

### 11.

Conferment of awards on persons not citizens of Grenada
(1) Subject to subsection (2), a distinguished citizen of a country other than Grenada
may be the recipient of the Order on an honorary basis and that person shall be an
honorary member of the Order.
(2) The Prime Minister may, after consultation with the Committee advise the
Governor-General to confer an award of the Order on a person who is not a citizen of
Grenada and the Governor-General may in accordance with the provisions of this Act
grant the award.

### 12.

Termination of membership
(1) A person ceases to be a member or honorary member of the Order upon—
(a)

his or her death;

(b)

his or her resignation from the Order which shall have effect on the date on
which a resignation in writing is accepted by the Governor-General; or

(c)

the revocation of his or her award by the Governor-General which the
Governor-General as Chancellor of the Order of Grenada is hereby
empowered to effect for cause appearing to him or her to be just or sufficient
after consultation with the Committee and the Prime Minister.

(2) The termination of membership of the Order under subsection (1) (a) shall not
affect the entitlement of the name of the person upon whom the award had been conferred
to be styled appropriately to the position of the Order to which he or she would have
belonged had he or she not died.

## PART III

Prestige Order of National Hero

### 13.

Establishment of Prestige Order of National Hero
A society of honour to be known as the Prestige Order of National Hero is hereby
established.

### 14.

Constitution of Prestige Order of National Hero
The Prestige Order of National Hero shall consist of—
(a)

the Governor-General as its Chancellor; and

(b)

persons upon whom the Prestige Order of National Hero has been conferred
who are living.

### 15.

Functions of the Chancellor
(1) The Chancellor of the Prestige Order of National Hero is responsible for the
administration of the Prestige Order of National Hero and shall have in his or her custody
the Seal of the Prestige Order of National Hero and shall affix the seal to all enactments
or other instruments relating to the Prestige Order of National Hero.
(2) Where the Governor-General is absent the person who is performing the functions
of the office of the Governor-General shall discharge the functions of the Chancellor of
the Prestige Order of National Hero.

(3) The Private Secretary to the Governor-General is the secretary of the Prestige
Order of National Hero, and shall maintain the records of the Prestige Order of National
Hero, arrange for investitures and perform such other functions in respect of the Prestige
Order of National Hero as the Governor General shall direct.

### 16.

Prestige Order of National Hero Commission
(1) There is established a Commission to be known as the Prestige Order of National
Hero Commission.
(2) The Commission shall comprise not more than seven members appointed by the
Governor-General who shall be—
(a)

three members nominated by the Prime Minister including—
(i) two who shall be members of the House of Representatives, and
(ii) the other who shall be a member of the Senate; and

(b)

two members nominated by the Leader of the Opposition—
(i) one who shall be a member of the House of Representatives, and
(ii) the other who shall be a member of the Senate;

(c)

two members—
(i) one appointed by the Governor-General after consultation with civil
society, and
(ii) one appointed by the Governor-General after consultation with the
religious community.

(3) The Governor-General shall after consultation with the Prime Minister, appoint a
person from among the members of the Commission to be the Chairperson of the
Commission.
(4) The office of the Chairperson shall not be a public office for the purpose of
section 110 of the Constitution of Grenada.
(5) The Governor-General shall, after consultation with the Prime Minister appoint a
person from among the members to be the secretary to the Commission.
(6) A member of the Commission, other than the Chairperson may at any time resign
his or her office by instrument in writing addressed to the Governor-General and
transmitted through the Chairperson and such resignation shall take effect as from the
date of receipt of the instrument of the Governor-General.
(7) The Chairperson may at any time resign his or her office by instrument in writing
addressed to the Governor-General and such resignation shall take effect as from the date
of receipt of the instrument by the Governor-General.
(8) Where a person who is a member of the Commission by virtue of
subsection (2)(a) or (b) ceases to be a member of the Senate or the House of
Representatives, that person shall cease to be a member of the Commission.
(9) The names of all the members of the Commission as first constituted and every
change in membership of the Commission shall be published in the Gazette.
(10) A vacancy in the membership of the Commission shall not preclude the
Commission from carrying out its functions.
(11) A person shall not be a member of the Prestige Order of National Hero by reason
only of he or she being a member of the Commission or other official of the Prestige
Order of National Hero.
(12) The Commission shall regulate its own procedures.

### 17.

Functions of Commission
The Commission shall, with a view to the proper carrying out of the provisions of the
Act—

make such investigations as it thinks fit to determine persons living or dead,
upon whom may be conferred the Prestige Order of National Hero; and

(b)

issue a report to the Prime Minister the result of those investigations.

### 18.

Conferment of Prestige Order of National Hero
(1) Subject to subsection (2), the Governor-General may by Proclamation sealed with
the Seal of the Prestige Order of National Hero, in accordance with the Second Schedule
confer the Prestige Order of National Hero upon any person who was born in Grenada or
who at the time of his or her death was a citizen of Grenada and who has—
(a)

given outstanding service to Grenada where that service has altered the
course of the history of Grenada;

(b)

given service to Grenada which has been exemplified by visionary and
pioneering leadership, extraordinary achievements or attainment of the
highest excellence, and which has redounded to the honour of Grenada; or

(c)

through his or her heroic exploits or sacrifices contributed to the
improvement of the economic and social conditions of Grenada.

(2) The power conferred upon the Governor-General under subsection (1) shall be
exercised on the advice of the Prime Minister given upon a consideration of a report of
the Commission referred to in section 18(1)(b).
(3) The Prestige Order of National Hero may be conferred upon a person
posthumously or during the lifetime of the person.

## PART IV

Offences

### 19.

Unauthorised use of insignia etc.
(1) A person shall not—
(a)

without authority, use any warrant or certificate authorised in connection
with any award;

(b)

without authority, wear any insignia, decoration or medal supplied or
authorised in connection with any award;

(c)

falsely represents himself or herself to be a person who is or has been,
entitled to use any such warrant or certificate or wear any such insignia,
decoration or medal as aforesaid; or

(d)

without lawful authority or excuse, supply or offer to supply any such warrant
or certificate, insignia or decoration or medal as aforesaid, to any person not
authorised to use or wear the same.

(2) A person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and is liable on
summary conviction to a fine not exceeding two thousand dollars or to imprisonment for
a term not exceeding three months.

### 20.

Abuse in connection with grant of awards

(1) A person shall not accept or obtain or agree to accept or obtain from another
person for himself or herself or for any other person or for any purpose, any gift, money
or valuable consideration, as an inducement or reward for procuring or assisting or
endeavouring to procure the grant of an award to any other person.
(2) A person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and is liable, on
summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding five thousand dollars or to imprisonment for
a term not exceeding six months.
(3) A person shall not offer or give or agree or propose to give to another person, any
gift, money or valuable consideration as an inducement or a reward for procuring or
assisting or endeavouring to procure the grant of an award under this Act.
(4) A person who contravenes subsection (3) commits an offence and is liable, on
summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding two thousand dollars or to imprisonment for
a term not exceeding three months.

## PART V

Miscellaneous

### 21.

Expenses
Expenses incurred under this Act shall be a charge on the Consolidated Fund and shall
be paid out of the monies allocated for that purpose by Parliament.

### 22.

Amendment of Schedules
The Governor-General may by Order amend the Schedules.

### 23.

Regulations
(1) The Governor-General may make regulations for the better carrying out of the
purposes and provisions of this Act and in particular may make regulations—
(a)

prescribing all matters that are necessary or required to be prescribed;

(b)

in relation to the seal, motto, motifs, insignia or regalia;

(c)

in relation to investitures.

(2) Regulations made under this section shall be subject to an affirmative resolution
of both Houses of Parliament.
First Schedule
NATIONAL HONOURS AND AWARDS ACT
Order of Grenada
[Sections 2 and 23.]

AWARDS
Companion of the Order of Grenada
1\. The Companion of the Order of Grenada may be awarded to any person who by his
or her distinguished achievements has brought international prestige to Grenada.

2\. On the institution of the Companion of the Order of Grenada the person holding the
office as Governor-General shall become the first holder of the Companion of the Order
of Grenada.
3\. The Companion of the Order of Grenada may be awarded only in Gold and the
recipient shall be vested with a Star, and a Collar to which will be affixed the badge of the
Order of Grenada.
4\. A Governor-General shall on retirement, resignation or on relinquishment of the
office of Governor-General be entitled to wear only the Star with which he or she was
invested and the Badge of the Order of Grenada suspended from the riband of the Order
of Grenada and worn round the neck.
5\. The Companion of the Order of Grenada may be awarded to only one citizen of
Grenada in any year.
6\. A person to whom the Companion of the Order of Grenada is entitled—
(a)

to have the letters “(COG)” placed after his or her name on all occasions
when the use of such letters is customary;

(b)

where that person is the Governor-General, to use the title “His or Her
Excellency” before his or her name.

The Order of Grenada Gold Award for Excellence
1\. The Order of Grenada Gold Award for Excellence may be awarded to any person
who has rendered excellent exemplary and outstanding service to Grenada tending to
promote unity whether domestically in Grenada or as between Grenada and any other
country.
2\. The Order of Grenada Gold Award for Excellence may be awarded only in Gold.
3\. The Order of Grenada Gold Award for Excellence may be awarded only to one
citizen of Grenada in any year.
The Spice Isle Award
1\. The Spice Isle Award may be awarded to any person who has rendered truly
emulative service to Grenada in any field of human endeavour or for other humane
action.
2\. The Spice Isle Award may be awarded only in Silver.
3\. The Spice Isle Award may be awarded to a maximum of two citizens of Grenada in
any year.
The Camerhogne Award
1\. The Camerhogne Award may be awarded to any person who has rendered
meritorious service to Grenada; in furtherance of national prestige or for gallantry.
2\. The Camerhogne Award may be awarded only in Silver.
3\. The Camerhogne Award may be awarded to a maximum of two citizens of
Grenada in any year.
The Medal of Honour
1\. The Medal of Honour may be awarded to any person for meritorious and
productive service to Grenada in any field of human endeavour including but not limited
to agriculture, business, sports and protection of the environment.
2\. The Medal of Honour may be awarded only in Bronze.
3\. The Medal of Honour may be awarded to a maximum of three citizens of Grenada
in any year.
The Most Distinguished Order of the Nation

1\. The Most Distinguished Order of the Nation may be awarded to a person who has
rendered distinguished and outstanding service to Grenada.
2\. The Most Distinguished Order of the Nation may be awarded posthumously but the
name of a deceased recipient must not appear in the current list of members at the time of
the award notwithstanding his or her membership of the Order.
3\. The Most Distinguished Order of the Nation shall comprise the following six
grades—
(a)

Knight or Dame Grand Collar;

(b)

Knight or Dame Grand Cross;

(c)

Knight or Dame Commander;

(d)

Commander;

(e)

Officer;

(f)

Member.

4\. The grade of the Knight or Dame Grand Collar, Knight or Dame Grand Cross,
Knight or Dame Commander shall confer the style, title and dignity of Sir or Dame for
life and the spouses of such Knights shall be accorded the style title of Lady for life.
5\. A member of the Order of the Most Distinguished Order of the Nation is entitled in
the case of—
(a)

a Knight or Dame Grand Collar to have the letters KGN or DGN placed after
his or her name on all occasions when the use of such letters is customary;

(b)

a Knight or Dame Grand Cross to have the letters KGCN or DGCN placed
after his or her name on all occasions when the use of such letters is
customary;

(c)

a Knight or Dame Grand Commander to have the letters KCN or DCN
placed after his or her name on all occasions when the use of such letters is
customary;

(d)

a Commander to have the letters CN placed after his or her name on all
occasions when the use of such letters is customary;

(e)

an Officer to have the letters ON placed after his or her name on all
occasions when the use of such letters is customary;

(f)

a member to have the letters MN placed after his or her name on all
occasions when the use of such letters is customary.

6\. A member of the Order of the Most Distinguished Order of the Nation is entitled to
wear the insignia prescribed for his or her grade by the Grand Master.
7\. The Most Distinguished Order of the Nation may be awarded to two persons in any
year.
8\. Every two years after commencement of this Act, the Leader of the Opposition
may submit to the Committee for its consideration and recommendation a person for an
award of the Most Distinguished Order of the Nation.
Second Schedule
Prestige Order of National Hero
[Sections 2, 19 and 23.]

1\. A person upon whom the Prestige Order of National Hero has been confirmed—

is entitled to wear as a decoration the prescribed insignia of the Prestige
Order of National Hero;

(b)

is entitled to wear the same at all ceremonial occasions; and

(c)

shall be referred to as “Right Excellent”.
Third Schedule
NATIONAL HONOURS AND AWARDS ACT
Tenure and Procedure of the National Awards Advisory Committee
[Section 8.]

1\. Subject to the provisions of this Schedule, the appointment of a member of the
Committee (including the Chairperson) shall be for a period not exceeding three years,
and such member shall be eligible for re-appointment.
2\. (1) Any member of the Committee, other than the Chairperson may at any time
resign his or her office by instrument in writing addressed to the Governor-General and
transmitted through the Chairperson and from the date of the receipt by the GovernorGeneral of such instrument such member shall cease to be a member of the Committee.
(2) The Chairperson may at any time resign his or her office by instrument in writing
addressed to the Governor-General and such resignation shall take effect as from the date
of the receipt of such instrument by the Governor-General.
3\. If any vacancy occurs in the membership of the Committee, such vacancy shall be
filled by the appointment of another member who shall, subject to the provisions of this
Schedule, hold office for the remainder of the period for which the previous member was
appointed, so, however, that such appointment shall be made in the same manner and
from the same category of persons, if any, as the appointment of the previous member.
4\. The names of all members of the Committee as first constituted and every change
in the membership thereof shall be published in the Gazette.
5\. The validity of the proceedings of the Committee shall not be affected by any
vacancy amongst the members thereof or by any defect in the appointment of a member
thereof.
6\. The Committee shall meet at such times as may be convenient or expedient for the
transaction of business and at such places as it may deem fit.
7\. The Chairperson shall preside at meetings of the Committee and in the case of his
or her absence from any meeting the members present shall elect one from amongst their
number to preside at that meeting.
8\. The decisions of the Committee shall be by a majority of votes of the members
present and voting and, in addition to an original vote, the Chairperson or any other
person presiding at the meeting shall have a casting vote in any case in which the voting
is equal.
9\. The quorum of the Committee shall be the members thereof.
10\. The Governor-General shall appoint a person to be the Secretary to the
Committee, but the Secretary shall not be a member of the Committee.
11\. Minutes in proper form of the proceedings of the Committee shall be kept by the
Secretary.
12\. Subject to the provisions of this Schedule, the Committee shall have power to
regulate its own proceedings, and may delegate to any member or committee thereof the
power and authority to carry out on behalf of the Committee such duties as the
Committee may determine.

13\. Any expenses incurred by the Chairperson and other members of the Committee in
the performance of their duties shall be paid out of money provided for the purpose by
Parliament.

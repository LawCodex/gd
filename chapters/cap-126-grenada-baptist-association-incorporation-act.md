## CHAPTER 126

GRENADA BAPTIST ASSOCIATION (INCORPORATION) ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of a body to be known as the Incorporated
Trustees of the Grenada Baptist Association, and for matters connected
therewith.
[Act No. 33 of 1984.]

[30th November, 1984.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Grenada Baptist Association (Incorporation) Act.
2\. Incorporation of trustees
Royston Issac, Osbert Raphael James, Alrick Levine and their successors in office as
trustees in Grenada of the Grenada Baptist Association (hereinafter called “the
Association”) shall be a body corporate known as the Incorporated Trustees of the
Grenada Baptist Association (hereinafter called “the Trustees”) with perpetual succession
and a Common Seal and with power in that name to sue and be sued in all courts.
3\. Power of trustees to acquire property
The Trustees shall have power to acquire for the Association by purchase, transfer,
exchange, devise, grant, gift, demise, bequest or otherwise any land or other property of
whatever kind whether in perpetuity or for life or for a term of years and any money,
securities, goods or chattels whatever, to take, hold and enjoy the same subject to the
performance of any trust upon which the same may have been acquired.
4\. Power of trustees to dispose of property, and to borrow, etc.
Subject to the performance of any trust as aforesaid the Trustees may—

(a)

by deed under the Common Seal sell, demise, grant, convey, transfer,
exchange, surrender or otherwise dispose of any real or personal property
vested in or which may hereafter become vested in the Trustees;

(b)

borrow money and, for the payment of the same, by deed under the Common
Seal, give and execute mortgages, charges, pledges and other securities of
any real or personal property or make any arrangement that they consider
convenient with such covenants, powers and provisions as they think proper.

5\. Exemption from licensing and forfeiture under Chapter 13
Notwithstanding that the Trustees may at any time be or be deemed to be under alien
control for the purposes of the Aliens (Land-Holding Regulation) Act, they shall be
entitled to hold land for any estate or interest therein and mortgages on land without
licence, and such land and mortgages shall not be liable to forfeiture or penalty under that
Act.
6\. Affixing of Common Seal
All documents requiring the Common Seal shall be sealed in the presence of at least
two of the Trustees who shall sign every such document.
7\. Address for service, etc.
(1) The Trustees shall at all times have a fixed address in Grenada for the service of
documents on them and such address and any change thereof shall be registered with the
Registrar of Companies.
(2) Any document may be served on the Trustees by leaving the document at, or by
sending it by registered post to, the address registered pursuant to subsection (1).

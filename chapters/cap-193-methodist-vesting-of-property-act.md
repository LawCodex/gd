## CHAPTER 193

METHODIST (VESTING OF PROPERTY) ACT
An Act to vest the property in Grenada of the Methodists in the M.C.C.A. Trust
Corporation, and for connected purposes.
[Act No. 13 of 1971.]

[10th July, 1971.]
Preamble
Whereas there is a body of persons in Grenada known as Methodists connected in
religious fellowship and now part of the Methodist Church in the Caribbean and the
Americas but formerly affiliated with the Methodist Missionary Society whose office
is in London and which is the overseas department of the Methodist Church of Great
Britain.
And Whereas property has from time to time been acquired by and on behalf of that body
of persons and is now used and occupied by the Ministers and agents of the Methodist
Church in the Caribbean and the Americas as sites for churches, chapels, schools and
mission houses.
And Whereas on the acquisition of such property before the commencement of the
Methodist (Vesting of Property) Ordinance on the 5th March, 1949, the deeds and
conveyances thereof and other evidence of title thereto were usually taken in the
names of the ministers for the time being of the Methodist Church or in the names of
trustees including persons for the time being filling official positions in the churches
of the said Society and in the case of some lands no deeds or conveyances or other
evidence were taken.
And Whereas in view of the inconvenience and expense occasioned by reason of the
changes from time to time necessarily made in the persons officially connected with

the said churches and of the death of persons in whom the legal ownership of such
property was vested and the likelihood of further recurrence of such inconvenience
and expense it was enacted by the Methodist (Vesting of Property) Ordinance that all
real and personal property in Grenada held by or vested in the Methodists or held by
or vested in any person on behalf of the said Society or the said Methodists should be
vested in the Methodist Missionary Trust Association (a company duly registered
under the Companies Act, 1929, of the Imperial Parliament) having its registered
office in London, its successors and assigns, for the same estates and interest and to
the extent to which the same were respectively, at the time of the passing of that Act,
held or vested as aforesaid subject to the rights, reservations and charges (if any) then
affecting the same and the trusts upon which the same were then held.
And Whereas, since the enactment of the Methodist (Vesting of Property) Ordinance,
further property has from time to time been acquired by and on behalf of the
Methodists and has to that end been conveyed to or otherwise vested in the said
Methodist Missionary Trust Association.
And Whereas the Methodists in Grenada with similar bodies of persons in other places all
connected in religious fellowship as aforesaid and theretofore affiliated with the
Methodist Missionary Society did on the 18th May, 1967, constitute and establish
themselves under the name “the Methodist Church in the Caribbean and the
Americas” as an autonomous body of Christian believers in place of, but in natural
succession to the work of, the Society in Grenada and such other places.
And Whereas the constitution and establishment of the Church was effected with the
approbation and consent of the Methodist Missionary Society, which is desirous that
all the said property may continue lawfully to be used by the Church to the intent that
the work of the Methodists may be carried on without interruption.
And Whereas a Trust Corporation known as “the M.C.C.A. Trust Corporation” has been
duly incorporated in the island of Antigua having its registered office at the Methodist
Conference Headquarters at Belmont, St. John’s in Antigua and having as one of its
objects the acting as Trustee for the said Church and as such to purchase, take on lease
or otherwise acquire and to hold, mortgage, charge, lease, sell, manage, develop, turn
to account and dispose of land and other property in any part of the world.
And Whereas it is expedient to vest in the M.C.C.A. Trust Corporation the property
acquired in Grenada for and on behalf of the Methodists.
And Whereas it is expedient that provision be made in relation to the trusts affecting the
property.
And Whereas it is expedient that provision be made in relation for further matters
connected with the work of the Methodist Church in the Caribbean and the Americas.
Now Therefore.
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Methodist (Vesting of Property) Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“ancillary bodies”, in relation to the Church, the Parent Church or the Missionary
Society, means schools, colleges, seminaries, hospitals, bookshops, charities,
associations, institutions and other organisations (other than local bodies) subsidiary
or ancillary to or connected with the Church, the Parent Church or the Missionary
Society
respectively, but provisions of this Act applicable to any such ancillary bodies which
are connected with other churches or organisations as well as with the Church, the

Parent Church or the Missionary Society, shall apply to such ancillary bodies only to
the extent of the interest therein of the Church, the Parent Church or the Missionary
Society;
“Church” means the Methodist Church in the Caribbean and the Americas
constituted and established as before recited, but “church” (so printed) bears its
ordinary meaning;
“Conference” means the governing body of the Church and where the context so
requires includes the Foundation Conference;
“Corporation” means the M.C.C.A. Trust Corporation;
“Deed of Church Order” means the Deed dated the 19th May, 1967, and executed
by the Rev. Hugh B. Sherlock and others which declared the establishment of the
Church and defined the constitution thereof;
“Foundation Conference” means the Conference held by representatives of the
Parent Church and of certain synods in the Caribbean and the Americas for the
purpose of settling and adopting the Deed of Church Order;
“local bodies”, in relation to the Church or the Parent Church, means districts,
circuits, societies, and such other local bodies as the work of the Church or the Parent
Church may be ordered in for the time being, and the synods, meetings and other
governing bodies thereof;
“Missionary Society” means the Methodist Missionary Society whose office is in
London and which is the overseas department of the Methodist Church of Great Britain;
“Parent Church” means the Methodist Church of Great Britain formed on the 20th
day of September, 1932, through the union of the Churches formerly known as the
Wesleyan Methodist Church, the Primitive Methodist Church and the United
Methodist Church.
3\. Vesting of property in the Corporation
(1) All land in Grenada held by or vested in the Methodists or held by or vested in
any person on behalf of the Methodists, and all property acquired by the Methodist
Missionary Trust Association on behalf of the Methodists by virtue of or subsequent to
the enactment of the Methodist (Vesting of Property) Ordinance, and in particular the
land set out and described in the Schedule, shall be held by and vested in the M.C.C.A.
Trust Corporation, its successors and assigns for the same estates and interest as and to
the extent to which the same were respectively at the commencement of this Act held or
vested subject to the rights, reservations and charges (if any) now affecting the same.
(2) If, after the vesting of property by virtue of this section, the Methodist Missionary
Trust Association or other body divested by this section continues to be under any
liability to a third party (being a liability which has been incurred or has arisen in
connection with such property), the Corporation shall keep the Methodist Missionary
Trust Association or other body fully indemnified against such liability.
4\. Adaptation of trusts
(1) All land and personal chattels in Grenada held upon trust for or for the purposes of
the Parent Church, the Missionary Society, or any of their local bodies or ancillary bodies,
in connection with the work of the Church shall, on and after the commencement of this
Act, be held upon similar trust for or for the purposes of the Church or its corresponding
local or ancillary body as if references to the Church were substituted for references to the
Parent Church and the Missionary Society in any declaration of such first-mentioned trust
but otherwise with and subject to the powers and provisions applicable thereto
immediately before the commencement of this Act.

(2) All monies, bank accounts and chooses in action held in Grenada by or on behalf
of or in trust for any of the local bodies of the Parent Church or the Missionary Society
now forming part of the Church shall continue to be held by or on behalf of that local
body but in its capacity as a local body of the Church rather than of the Parent Church or
the Missionary Society.
(3) Wherever the words “Methodist Church” or “Conference” are used in any Act,
deed, will or other instrument as applying to the Parent Church or its governing body the
British Conference in connection with work which is now carried on by the Church such
words shall, on and after the commencement of this Act, be construed in their application
to property in Grenada to mean the Church and the Conference respectively.
(4) A devise or bequest contained in a will or codicil made by a person living at the
time of the commencement of this Act in favour of or directed to be administered by or in
connection with the Parent Church, the Missionary Society, or any of their local or
ancillary bodies, in connection with work now carried on by the Church shall (save in so
far as the devise or bequest is on its true construction intended to be applied to work of
the Parent Church or of the Missionary Society carried on outside the area of the Church)
take effect in favour of or be administered by or in connection with the Church or its
corresponding local or ancillary body as if references to the Church were substituted for
references to the Parent Church and the Missionary Society in such will or codicil but
otherwise with and subject to the powers and provisions set out therein.
5\. Model trusts
(1) It shall be lawful for the Conference, by resolution passed and confirmed in such
manner as may for the time being be required for the alteration of the Deed of Church
Order, to settle and adopt a form of model trusts (hereinafter called “the model trusts”)
capable of application to any land held or to be held in connection with the Church or any
of its local or ancillary bodies for the purposes of a church, chapel, vestry, minister’s or
other dwelling house, day school, Sunday school, schoolroom, lecture hall, mission hall
or other building or for the purposes of a burial ground.
(2) It shall be lawful for the Conference by such resolution as aforesaid from time to
time to revoke, alter or add to any of the provisions of the model trusts other than
provisions embodying the doctrinal standards of the Church.
(3) The Corporation, or the owner for the time being of any land in Grenada such as
referred to in subsection (1), may at any time, by declaration under seal forthwith
transmitted to the Secretary of the Conference, adopt the model trusts in relation to such
land, and such land shall thenceforth be held upon the trusts and subject to the powers and
provisions set out in the model trusts.
(4) If such a declaration relates to only part of any land held by the Corporation or
such owner subsection (3) shall apply to such part only.
(5) If such land is held jointly by more than one owner then such a declaration shall
be valid and effective if executed by a majority of them.
(6) Upon any revocation or alteration of or addition to the model trusts any land then
already held upon the trusts thereof shall henceforth be held upon such trusts as amended
by the revocation, alteration or addition and thereafter reference to the model trusts in this
Act and in any document whenever executed shall be construed and take effect as a
reference to the model trusts as varied by such revocation, alteration or addition.
6\. Provisions as to documentary evidence
(a)

A purported copy (purporting to be signed by the President, Vice President
or Secretary of the Foundation Conference or of the Conference) of any of
the documents specified in paragraph (b) of this section shall be deemed to
be a true copy of such document and shall be received in evidence in all

courts and in all proceedings whether civil or criminal as if it were the
original document, unless some error is proved.
(b)

The documents referred to in paragraph (a) of this section are—
(i) the Deed of Church Order,
(ii) the Model Trusts,
(iii) any resolution of the Foundation Conference or of the Conference
settling, adopting, revoking, altering or adding to the Deed of Church
Order or the Model Trusts or any part thereof,
(iv) any resolution of the Corporation amending the objects and powers of
the Corporation or the Articles of Association of the Corporation.

7\. Institution of legal proceedings and service of documents
(1) Proceedings by or on behalf of or against the Church may be brought or defended
by and in the name of “The President of the Methodist Church in the Caribbean and the
Americas” by that description (without naming the holder for the time being of that
office) for and on behalf of the Church and the death or incapacity of the President for the
time being of the Conference or the expiration of his or her term of office or any act or
thing done or suffered by him or her shall not abate or affect any such proceedings.
(2) A writ, notice or other process in any such proceedings shall be sufficiently served
on the Church if served personally on the President or Secretary for the time being of the
Conference for and on behalf of the Church or if it is left at the office of the Conference.
(3) Any affidavit, statutory or other declaration or other similar document required
from or desired by the Church may be made by the President, Vice President, or Secretary
for the time being of the Conference for and on behalf of the Church.
(4) Every President, Vice President and Secretary of the Conference shall be
indemnified out of the funds of the Church against all loss, damages, costs and expenses
which he or she may sustain by reason of anything done to or by him or her in pursuance
of this section.
8\. Corporation not to have alien status
Notwithstanding the provisions of the Aliens (Land Holding Regulation) Act, Chapter
13, the Corporation shall not be nor be deemed to be an alien and shall be entitled to hold
land and mortgages on land without licence and such land and mortgages shall not be
liable to forfeiture under that Act.
9\. Exemption from stamp duty
No transfer or vesting of property and no adaptation of trusts nor any other act or thing
effected by this Act shall give rise to any liability for duty under any Act imposing or
regulating the imposition of stamp duty.

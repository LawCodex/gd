## CHAPTER 62

CONGREGATION OF THE SISTERS OF ST. JOSEPH DE CLUNY (TRUSTEES)
(INCORPORATION) ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of a body to be known as the Incorporated
Trustees of the Congregation of the Sisters of St. Joseph de Cluny.
[16th June, 1956.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Congregation of the Sisters of St. Joseph de Cluny
(Trustees) (Incorporation) Act.
2\. Incorporation of trustees
The Mother Provincial of the Sisters of St. Joseph de Cluny, resident in the Republic
of Trinidad and Tobago, the Mother Superior and the Sister Assistant of St. Joseph’s
Convent in Grenada, and their successors in office as trustees of the Congregation of the
Sisters of St. Joseph de Cluny shall be a body corporate known as The Incorporated
Trustees of the Congregation of the Sisters of St. Joseph de Cluny (hereinafter called “the
Trustees”) with perpetual succession and a Common Seal and with power in that name to
sue and be sued in all courts.
3\. Vesting of property in the trustees

The property specified in the Schedule and all other property movable and immovable
of whatever nature now vested in any person for and on behalf of the Congregation of the
Sisters of St. Joseph de Cluny is hereby vested in the Trustees.
4\. Power of trustees to acquire property
The Trustees shall have power to acquire for the Congregation of the Sisters of St.
Joseph de Cluny by purchase, exchange, gift, demise, bequest or otherwise, any movable
or immovable property or any estate or interest therein and also any money, securities,
goods or chattels whatsoever.
5\. Power of trustees to borrow money
The Trustees may borrow money upon the security of any property held by them and
for that purpose any document relating thereto shall be signed by not less than two of the
Trustees; but any mortgage or other similar obligation shall be executed under the
Common Seal.
6\. Power of trustees to dispose of property
The Trustees may by deed under the Common Seal sell, demise, grant, convey,
exchange, mortgage or otherwise dispose of any property vested or which may hereafter
become vested in them.
7\. Affixing of Common Seal
All documents requiring the Common Seal shall be sealed in the presence of at least
two of the Trustees.
8\. Trustees to be registered with Registrar of Companies
The names and addresses of the persons from time to time holding the several offices
referred to in section 2 shall be registered with the Registrar of Companies and those
persons shall be subject to the provisions of the Companies Act.

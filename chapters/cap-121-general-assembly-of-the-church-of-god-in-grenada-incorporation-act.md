## CHAPTER 121

GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE CHURCH OF GOD IN GRENADA
(INCORPORATION) ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of a body to be known as the Incorporated
Trustees of the General Assembly of the Church of God in Grenada and for the
vesting of property therein, and for matters connected therewith.
[Act No. 50 of 1980.]

[24th October, 1980.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the General Assembly of the Church of God in Grenada
(Incorporation) Act.
2\. Objects of the Organisation
The objects of the General Assembly of the Church of God in Grenada (hereinafter
called “the Organisation”) are to proclaim the Gospel of Our Lord and Saviour Jesus
Christ for the salvation of souls, to minister to the spiritual, moral and educational needs
of the people of Christian persuasion, and to co-operate in general social service to the
community irrespective of race and creed.
3\. Incorporation of trustees
Gerome Adrian George, Rupert Pierre, Simeon Stanislaus, Devon Alexis, Gordon
Luke and their successors in office as trustees in Grenada of the Organisation shall be a
corporate body known as the Incorporated Trustees of the General Assembly of the

Church of God in Grenada (hereinafter called “the Trustees”) with perpetual succession
and a Common Seal and with power in that name to sue and be sued in all courts.
4\. Power of trustees to acquire property
The Trustees shall have power to acquire for the Organisation by purchase, transfer,
exchange, devise, grant, gift, demise, bequest or otherwise any land or other property of
whatever kind whether in perpetuity or for life or for a term of years and any money,
securities, goods or chattels whatever, to take, hold and enjoy the same subject to the
performance of any trust upon which the same may have been acquired.
5\. Power of trustees to dispose of property, and to borrow, etc.
Subject to the performance of any trust as aforesaid the Trustees may—
(a)

by deed under the Common Seal sell, demise, grant, convey, transfer,
exchange, surrender or otherwise dispose of any real or personal property
vested in or which may hereafter become vested in the Trustees;

(b)

borrow money and, for the payment of the same, by deed under the Common
Seal, give and execute mortgages, charges, pledges and other securities of
any real or personal property or make any arrangement that they may
consider convenient with such covenants, powers and provisions as they
think proper.

6\. Vesting of property
All churches, chapels, mission houses, rectories, vicarages, parsonages, glebes,
buildings, appurtenances and land now held by or otherwise vested in the Missionary
Board of the Church of God, the trustees thereof or any other person or body for and on
behalf or appropriated to the use of the Organisation, and in particular the lots of land
hitherto so vested (with all buildings and appurtenances thereon) listed and described in
the Schedule, and any other property of whatever nature in Grenada now vested in or held
by the Missionary Board of the Church of God, are vested in the Trustees for the same
estates and interests and to the extent to which the same were, at the commencement of
this Act, held or vested subject to any rights, reservations and charges affecting the same.
7\. Exemption from licensing and forfeiture under Chapter 13
Notwithstanding that the Trustees may at any time be or be deemed to be under alien
control for the purposes of the Aliens (Land-Holding Regulation) Act, Chapter 13, they
shall be entitled to hold land for any estate or interest therein and mortgages on land
without licence, and such land and mortgages shall not be liable to forfeiture or other
penalty under that Act.
8\. Affixing of Common Seal
All documents requiring the Common Seal shall be sealed in the presence of at least
two of the Trustees who shall sign every such document.
9\. Address for service, etc.
(1) The Trustees shall at all times have a fixed address in Grenada for the service of
documents on them and such address and any change thereof shall be registered with the
Registrar of Companies.
(2) Any document may be served on the Trustees by leaving the document at, or by
sending it by registered post to, the address registered pursuant to subsection (1).

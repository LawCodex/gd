## CHAPTER 61

CONFERENCE OF CHURCHES IN GRENADA (INCORPORATION) ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of a body to be known as the Conference of
Churches in Grenada.
[Act No. 31 of 1980.]

[4th July, 1980.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Conference of Churches in Grenada (Incorporation) Act.

### 2.

Incorporation of the Conference of Churches
The Conference of Churches in Grenada (hereinafter called “the Conference of
Churches”) shall be a body corporate with perpetual succession and a Common Seal and
with power in that name to sue and be sued in all courts.

### 3.

Aims and objects of the Conference of Churches
The aims and objects of the Conference of Churches are—
(a)

to promote greater understanding and unity among all Christian communities
in Grenada;

(b)

to bear witness—
(i) to a common profession of faith in God and in Jesus Christ, and
(ii) to truth, charity, equity and justice;

(c)

to preserve and protect the rights, powers, privileges and interests of member
denominations of the Conference of Churches within the framework of the
law;

(d)

to arrange for the holding of ecumenical services, rallies and other
ecumenical meetings;

(e)

to organise theological, historical and other studies for the purpose of
effectively encouraging and promoting a better Christian way of life in the
community;

(f)

to collaborate and co-operate with other religious organisations for the
purpose of bringing about, to the glory of God and the dignity of man, the
spiritual, moral, intellectual, social, cultural, economic and technological
progress of the people of Grenada;

(g)

to publish journals and such other publications as the Conference of
Churches may consider necessary;

(h)

to preserve, if necessary in conjunction with other religious or secular bodies
or organisations, proper standards of Christian conduct and behaviour.

### 4.

Powers and functions of the Conference of Churches
(1) Subject to this Act and to rules made under section 7, the Conference of Churches
shall have the powers specified in subsection (2) and shall perform such functions
appropriate to its aims and objects as may be specified in those rules.
(2) The Conference of Churches shall have power—
(a)

to acquire by purchase, exchange, gift, devise, bequest or otherwise,
messuages, lands, tenancies, hereditaments of any tenure, and moneys,
goods and chattels, of whatever kind, to hold and enjoy the same;

(b)

to lend money and to accept and hold securities for charitable and provident
purposes, and to give discharges and releases of the same;

(c)

to accept surrenders and reconveyances and to enter into contracts;

(d)

to sell, demise, grant, convey, transfer, mortgage, reconvey, exchange,
surrender or otherwise dispose of property from time to time vested in the
Conference of Churches.

### 5.

Sealing and execution of documents
All documents requiring the Common Seal shall be sealed in the presence of the
chairman or vice-chairman, the treasurer and the secretary of the Conference of Churches
who shall sign every such document.

### 6.

Address for service, etc.
(1) The Conference of Churches shall at all times have a fixed address in Grenada for
the service of documents upon it and such address and any change thereof shall be
registered with the Registrar of Companies.
(2) Failure to register the address or any change thereof is an offence for which the
Conference of Churches shall be liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of two hundred
and fifty dollars and to a further fine of ten dollars for each day during which the offence
continues after conviction.

### 7.

Power to make own rules
The Conference of Churches may make its own rules for the conduct of its
proceedings, the discharge of its duties, the exercise of its powers and the performance of
its functions and may from time to time amend those rules as it thinks fit.

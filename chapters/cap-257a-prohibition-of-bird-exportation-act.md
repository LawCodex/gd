## CHAPTER 257A

PROHIBITION OF BIRD EXPORTATION ACT
An Act to prohibit the exporting of birds from Grenada.
[Act No. 9 of 1991 amended by Act No. 14 of 1995.]

[21st June, 1991.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Prohibition of Bird Exportation Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act, “Minister” means the Minister to whom has been assigned responsibility
for Agriculture.
3\. Exporting of birds prohibited
(1) Save as otherwise provided in and by this Act, no bird of any description shall be
exported from the State without a written licence to do so issued by the Minister.
(2) Nothing contained in this Act shall be construed as affecting the absolute
prohibition of the exportation of those wild birds referred to in section 3(2) of the Birds
and Other Wild Life (Protection of) Act, Chapter 36.
4\. Minister may issue licences

It shall be lawful for the Minister in his discretion to issue licences for birds to be
exported from the State on such conditions as he sees fit.
5\. Poultry not included
This Act shall not apply to those species of birds which are bred for human
consumption as far as the State is concerned and which are called poultry such as
domestic fowls, chickens, turkeys, geese, ducks, guinea fowls and pigeons.
6\. Punishment of offences against this Act
Any person who exports birds from the State or causes birds to be so exported or
counsels or procures or aids or abets such exportation or conspires to bring about such
exportation contrary to section 3 shall be guilty of an offence and on summary conviction
shall be liable to a term of imprisonment of two years:
Provided that on a second or subsequent conviction such a person shall be liable to a
term of imprisonment of twenty-four months.
7\. Sections 13 and 15 of Animals (Diseases and Importation) Act, Chapter 15,
amended
Sections 13 and 15 of the Animals (Diseases and Importation) Act, Chapter 15 of the
1958 Revision, are amended by replacing the phrase “Chief Technical Officer
(Extension)” formerly “Superintendent of Agriculture” with the phrase “Minister of
Agriculture”.

## CHAPTER 200

MOTOR VEHICLE REPAIR SHOPS AND GARAGES ACT
An Act to control the establishment and regulate the operation of motor vehicle
repair shops and garages, and to improve the standard of motor vehicle repairs,
and to provide for connected matters.
[Act No. 47 of 1973.]

[30th August, 1974.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Motor Vehicle Repair Shops and Garages Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“Board” means the Board established by section 3;
“motor vehicle” includes the whole or any part of a motor vehicle registered and
licensed pursuant to the Motor Vehicles and Road Traffic Act, Chapter 201;
“operating” means carrying on a business which consists of or includes servicing,
repairing or garaging motor vehicles belonging to members of the public;
“owner” includes a licensee under this Act, a lessee of premises, and any person
entitled to the rents or profits from premises or from a business operating there;

“premises” means the whole, or any part, of a building or structure and the
curtilage thereof wherein or whereon a person is, or intends to commence, operating.
3\. Establishment of the Motor Vehicle Repair Shops and Garages Control Board
(1) A Board to be known as the Motor Vehicle Repair Shops and Garages Control
Board is hereby established as a body corporate.
(2) The Board shall consist of the following members—
(a)

the Chief Technical Officer (Works), who shall be chairperson;

(b)

the Chief of Police;

(c)

a senior police officer of the Traffic Branch of the Royal Grenada Police
Force;

(d)

a member of the Tourist Board;

(e)

a member of the Taxi Drivers’ Association;

(f)

a member of the Owners and Drivers of Commercial Buses Association.

(3) The provisions of the First Schedule shall govern the proceedings of the Board
and, subject thereto, the Board may with the approval of the Minister make regulations
governing its own proceedings.
4\. Function and powers of the Board
It shall be the function of the Board to implement the provisions and purposes of this
Act and, for that purpose, the Board shall have power, with respect to persons operating
at any premises, at all reasonable times, to inspect the premises and to make enquiries of
the owner, the individual in charge, or the apparent owner or individual in charge, and
any other individual employed, or appearing to be employed, therein.
5\. Application for licence to operate repair shop or garage
(1) If a person desires to commence operating at any premises he or she shall first
apply to the Board for a licence. He or she shall apply in the form and in the manner
specified in the Second Schedule.
(2) Every such application shall be considered by the Board, after having interviewed
the applicant and inspected the place where the applicant intends to commence operating,
in the manner provided by section 6.
(3) The Board having considered an application, the chairman shall ascertain and
record the recommendation of the Board thereon and forward the application to the
Minister together with the recommendation.
(4) The Minister may approve or reject the application, and his or her decision shall
be communicated in writing to the chairman who shall send written notification thereof to
the applicant.
6\. Matters to be considered by the Board
For the purpose of deciding upon a proper recommendation in respect of each
application, the Board shall give consideration to the following matters—
(a)

the qualifications if any and experience of the applicant in any aspect of
mechanical or electrical engineering;

(b)

the extent if any to which obstruction of any road may be caused by the use
of the premises in the locality in which they are situated; and

(c)

whether any, and if so what, detriment would or would be likely to be caused
in the locality in which the premises are situated, having regard in particular
to the siting of the premises in the area and the effects of the structure and
use of the premises upon any neighbouring buildings and adjacent land, and
the Board shall form and record its opinion thereon.

7\. Issue and renewal of licences
(1) A licence if granted under this Act shall be issued by the Board in such form as it
may determine upon payment by the applicant of a fee of two hundred and fifty dollars.
(2) A licence shall be renewable annually on the first day of January in each year and
upon each renewal the applicant shall pay to the Board a fee of five hundred and fifty
dollars.
8\. Licence, etc., to be displayed on premises
It is the duty of the licensee to display and maintain in a conspicuous position on his
or her premises where it can be easily read—
(a)

his or her current licence; and

(b)

a sign board depicting, in letters not less than two inches in height, his or her
name, the number of his or her licence and the year in respect of which that
licence was issued,

and if he or she contravenes a provision of this section he or she shall be guilty of an
offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of three hundred dollars but in
respect of a second and any subsequent conviction to a fine of between seven hundred
and fifty dollars and one thousand five hundred dollars and to imprisonment for twelve
months.
9\. Offence of operating repair shop or garage without a licence
If any person is found operating on any premises without there being in force a current
licence under this Act, or if the owner of premises permits the use of his or her premises
as a motor vehicle repair shop or garage without there having been issued to him and her
a licence authorising such use, he or she shall be guilty of an offence and liable, on
summary conviction, to a fine of three thousand dollars or, in respect of a second such
offence, to a fine of six thousand dollars or, in respect of a third and any subsequent such
offence, to a fine of fifteen thousand dollars and to imprisonment for six months.
10\. Power of Minister to make regulations
The Minister may make regulations under this section for the better carrying out of the
provisions and purposes of this Act.

Proceedings of the Board

[Section 3(3).]

---

### 1.

The Board shall meet as often as may be necessary or expedient for the transaction
of business, at such places and times on such days as the Board itself shall determine:

Provided that at least three days’ notice of each meeting shall have been given to members of the
Board.

### 2.

Notwithstanding paragraph 1, the Minister or any two members of the Board may, by
requisition in writing to the chairman, require the chairman to call a meeting of the Board
within seven days of its receipt by him and her; and the chairman shall comply with the
requisition.

### 3.

Three members of the Board including the chairman shall form a quorum but, in
the absence from any meeting of the chairman, four members shall form the quorum
including the member whom those present shall elect to preside in place of the chairman
at that meeting.

### 4.

Every decision of the Board shall be by the majority of the members present and
voting at a meeting except that, in the event of an equality of votes, the chairman or other
member presiding shall have a casting vote in addition to his or her own deliberative vote.

### 5.

The validity of the proceedings of the Board at a meeting shall not be affected by
any vacancy in the membership of the Board or by any defect in the appointment of any
member.

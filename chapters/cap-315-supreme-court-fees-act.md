## CHAPTER 315

SUPREME COURT FEES ACT
An Act to impose fees for civil causes, and matters in the Supreme Court.
[Act No. 12 of 1982.]

[26th March, 1982.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Supreme Court Fees Act.

### 2.

Supreme Court fees
(1) In all civil causes and matters before the High Court or Court of Appeal the fees
specified in the Schedule shall be taken by the Registrar in respect of the several matters
mentioned therein.
(2) The fees shall be paid in stamps unless expressly directed by the Schedule to be
paid in money. The Registrar, his or her deputy or clerk shall cancel stamps by writing
across them in ink his or her initials and the date of cancellation.
(3) The Court may forego to demand a fee payable under this Act where, in the
opinion of the Court, a party is unable to pay but, in such case, the fee shall nevertheless,
if the Court so directs, be payable by and recoverable from the other party if the decision
is given against that party and shall be paid to the Registrar by the party by whom it is
recovered.
(4) No fee under this Act shall be taken which would be payable by the Government,
or by a public officer in the discharge of his or her duty as such.
(5) In this section, “Court” includes a Judge sitting in chambers.

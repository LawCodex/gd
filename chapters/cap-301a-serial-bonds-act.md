## CHAPTER 301A

SERIAL BONDS ACT
An Act to make provision for the payment of Government debts by the use of Serial Bonds.
[Act No. 1 of 1998.]

[Date of assent: 13th, January, 1998.]

## PART I

Preliminary
Short title

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Serial Bonds Act.
Interpretation

### 2.

For the purposes of this Act—
“bank” means a bank licensed under the Banking Act, Chapter 26A;
“bond” means a serial bond issued under section 3;
“discount rate” means an average of the last six months ninety day Treasury Bill rates plus
a margin of two per cent.
Issue of serial bond

### 3.

The Minister may, whenever it is considered necessary, with the authority of a resolution of
Parliament, issue bonds to be known as “serial bonds”, for the payment of any debt.
Forms, denominations and redemption of bonds

### 4.

Bonds may be issued with such redemption values and maturity dates as the Minister determines.
Specific requirements

### 5.

(1) Every bond must be signed by the Accountant-General and by such other public officer
as the Minister approves.
(2) A facsimile signature of the Accountant-General and of such other public officer as the
Minister approves shall be adequate for the purposes of this section.
(3) The Accountant-General shall keep a register of the bonds issued.
Rate of interest

### 6.

(1) Every bond shall bear on its face a date known as the “maturity date” and additional interest shall not, before that date, accrue in respect of a bond.
(2) Where the Government is unable to redeem the bonds on the maturity date, the bonds may
be discounted by any bank or may be purchased by institutional investors at the discount rate.
(3) Where bonds have been discounted or purchased under subsection (2) interest begins to
accrue at the discount rate from the day immediately following the maturity date and continues to
accrue until the bonds are redeemed by the Government.
Secured obligations

### 7.

The bonds shall constitute secured obligations of the Government and shall rank pari passu.

Bonds redeemable out of the Consolidated Fund

### 8.

All principles monies and interest thereon payable in respect of bonds are a charge on, and
are payable out of, the Consolidated Fund.
Duplicate bonds

### 9.

(1) Where a bond that is presented to the Accountant-General is damaged, defaced or rendered illegible, he may issue a duplicate bond in its place.
(2) Where a bond is not presented to the Accountant-General, he shall not issue a duplicate
Bond unless the applicant therefor—
(a)

produces an affidavit to the effect that the original bond was lost or destroyed; and

(b)

agrees to indemnify the Crown against loss in case the bond is subsequently found
and presented for redemption.

(3) A duplicate bond may be issued in accordance with the requirements of section 5.
Redemption of bonds

### 10.

Redemption of bonds may be effected on presentation to the Accountant-General.
Regulations

### 11.

The Minister may make regulations for giving effect to the provisions of this Act.
Commencement

### 12.

This Act shall come into operation on such date as the Minister appoints by notice published in the Gazette.

Serial Bonds Act (Section 3) Resolution

SERIAL BONDS ACT (SECTION 3) RESOLUTION
[SRO 23 of 2011.]

[5th August, 2011.]
WHEREAS the Minister of Finance proposes to consolidate existing debts with the issue of serial
bonds up to a maximum of fifty million Eastern Caribbean Dollars;
WHEREAS section 3 of the Serial Bonds Act No. 1 of 1998 provides that the Minister of Finance
may, whenever it is considered necessary, with the authority of a resolution of Parliament, issue bonds to be known as “serial bonds”, for the payment of any debt;
WHEREAS the Government of Grenada wishes to issue locally serial bonds up to a maximum of
fifty million Eastern Caribbean Dollars, for the payment of debts and thereafter the said serial
bonds shall be listed on the Regional Government Securities Market (RGSM) and traded on the
Secondary Market trading platform of the Eastern Caribbean Securities Exchange (ECSE);
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that Parliament hereby authorises the Minister of Finance to issue serial bonds on the terms and conditions and for the purposes specified in the
Schedule.

2013/2016 SERIAL BOND EC$50 MILLION
The Government of Grenada proposes to issue an EC$50,000,000 Serial Bond on 30th November 2009.
Issuer:

Government of Grenada.

Purpose:

To consolidate and restructure Government Debt.

Amount of the Issue:

Up to a maximum of EC$50.0 million.

Tenor:

3 to 5 years.

Issue Date:

May 2011.

Maturity Date:

May 2013/May 2016.

Listing:

The serial bond will be issued locally and listed on
the Regional Government Securities Market
(RGSM) and traded on the Secondary Market trading platform of the Eastern Caribbean Securities
Exchange (ECSE).

Yield:

6.0%

Interest Payments:

Interest will be payable half yearly on 15th May
and 15th November.

Denomination:

The bonds will be of denominations of 10,000,
20,000, 30,000, 40,000, 50,000.

Repayment:

The bonds will be payable at par on 31st May 2013
and 31st May 2016.

Taxation:

Yields will not be subject to any tax, duty or levy of
the Participating Governments of the Eastern Caribbean Currency Union (ECCU). The countries are
Anguilla, Antigua and Barbuda, Dominica, Grenada, Montserrat, Saint Lucia, St. Kitts and Nevis and
St. Vincent and the Grenadines.

Sinking Fund Arrangement:

A sinking fund will be created for the repayment of
this bond.

Security:

Payment of annual interest will be a charge on the
consolidated fund.

## CHAPTER 170

LEPERS ACT
An Act relating to lepers.
[1st November, 1928.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Lepers Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“institution” means any hospital, asylum, almshouse, or house or refuge kept up at
the public expense for the relief of the sick or destitute poor;
“leper” means a person suffering from any variety of leprosy;
“leper asylum” includes a leper settlement.

## PART I

Establishment of Leper Asylums

### 3.

Minister may establish asylums
The Minister may by Order declare any buildings and the land attached thereto
acquired for the purpose to be a leper asylum, and thereupon such buildings and land
shall be a leper asylum under this Act.

### 4.

Appointment of officers
(1) The Public Service Commission shall appoint fit and proper persons to perform
the duties of Medical Superintendent, Resident Superintendent, and such staff as may be
necessary for every leper asylum.
(2) The Chief Medical Officer shall, subject to the direction of the Minister have the
control and management of every leper asylum.

### 5.

Erection of dwelling-house by inmate
Any leper may, by special permission of the Minister, erect or cause to be erected for
himself or herself a dwelling-house at his or her own expense within the limits of the
leper asylum in which he or she is detained, or which he or she is about to enter, subject
to such conditions as to plan, site, drainage, and otherwise as to the Minister shall seem fit.

## PART II

Notification and Examination of Lepers

### 6.

Notification of leprosy
(1) Where an inmate of any premises used for human habitation is a leper or shows
symptoms from which it may be reasonably inferred that he or she is a leper, then unless
such premises are an institution in which only lepers are received, the following
provisions shall apply—
(a)

the head of the family to which such inmate (in this Act referred to as the
patient) belongs, and in his or her default the nearest relative of the patient
present in the premises or being in attendance on the patient, and, in default
of such relative, every person in charge of or in attendance on the patient,
and, in default of any such person, the occupier of the premises, shall, as
soon as he or she becomes aware that the patient is suffering from leprosy or
a disease suspected to be leprosy, send notice thereof to the District Medical
Officer;

(b)

every medical practitioner attending on or called in to visit the patient shall
forthwith, on becoming aware that the patient is suffering from leprosy or a
disease suspected to be leprosy, send to the District Medical Officer a
certificate stating the name of the patient, the situation of the premises, and
the disease from which, in the opinion of such medical practitioner, such
patient is suffering or suspected to be suffering.

(2) Every person required by this section to give a notice or certificate, who fails to
give the same, shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of one hundred dollars.
(3) The expression “occupier” in this section includes a person having the charge,
management, or control of any premises or of any part thereof in which the patient is, and
in the case of a house, the whole or any part of which is let to lodgers, the person
receiving the rent payable by the tenants or lodgers either on his or her own account or as
the agent of another, and in the case of a ship, vessel or boat, the master or other person in
charge thereof.

### 7.

Forms of certificate
(1) The Minister may prescribe forms for certificates under the last preceding section,
and any form so prescribed shall be used in all cases to which they apply.
(2) Such forms shall be supplied gratuitously to any medical practitioner who applies
for them to the Secretary to the Cabinet.

### 8.

Examination of lepers
(1) It shall be lawful for the District Medical Officer, on receiving a notice or
certificate to the effect mentioned in section 6, to enter upon the premises where the
person named in such notice or certificate resides and to examine him or her with a view
to ascertaining whether he or she is suffering from leprosy, and, in the event of admission
or examination being refused, a magistrate may grant a warrant authorising such entry
and examination, and, on such warrant being exhibited, any person refusing to admit the
District Medical Officer to such premises or obstructing him or her in making the
examination aforesaid shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of two hundred and
fifty dollars.
(2) It shall be lawful for the District Medical Officer to order any person named in
any notice or certificate as above-mentioned to attend at a place to be notified to such
person in writing there to be examined, and any person refusing or failing to attend at the
place notified to him or her or obstructing examination at any such place shall be guilty of
an offence and liable to a fine of two hundred and fifty dollars.
(3) On making an examination under the provisions of this section, the District
Medical Officer shall report forthwith the result thereof to the Chief Medical Officer for
the information of the Minister.
(4) The powers, rights and jurisdiction conferred, and the fines, penalties, liabilities,
and duties imposed, by or under the provisions of this section shall extend and apply to a
case where a District Medical Officer receives or acquires information that any person
residing within his or her district is a leper or shows symptoms from which it may be
reasonably inferred that he or she is a leper, in all respects as if such District Medical
Officer had received a notice or certificate such as is referred to in subsection (1).

## PART III

Admission to Asylum

### 9.

Voluntary application for admission to asylum
(1) Any person desirous of being admitted into a leper asylum may make an
application to any District Medical Officer in the following form—
I, the undersigned do hereby request you to cause me to be admitted into the leper asylum at
............................................................................................................................................
....................................... , and I undertake to remain therein until duly discharged and to
conform to the rules of the said leper asylum.
...................................................................
Applicant
...................................................................
Witness
To the District Medical Officer at ..................................................................................

(2) The signature of the applicant shall be attested by a magistrate, Justice of the
Peace or official attestor who shall not so attest unless he or she has satisfied himself or

herself that the applicant understands the effect of the application and unless the District
Medical Officer attaches a certificate to the application that the applicant is a leper or a
certificate to the effect that the District Medical Officer has reasonable grounds to suspect
that the applicant is a leper.
(3) The applicant, after his or her reception into the leper asylum, shall not, unless
discharged by order of the Minister under this Act, be entitled to leave the asylum, and, in
case of leaving without being discharged as aforesaid, may be dealt with under section 33
as if a warrant or order had been issued against him or her.

### 10.

Detention of leper in asylum after examination, and isolation in lieu of removal
to asylum
(1) After such examination and report as is mentioned in section 8 and subject to
subsection (3) it shall be lawful for the Minister, if he or she shall think fit, to order the
leper or alleged leper to be removed to or detained in a leper asylum:
Provided that the Minister may refrain from making any such order for removal or
detention if the leper or alleged leper shall, in the opinion of the Minister, be able to
provide for himself or herself, at his or her own place of abode, effective isolation, in
accordance with rules made under this Act.
(2) In the event of the disregard or breach of any such rules, the Minister may order
such leper or alleged leper to be removed to and detained in a leper asylum, but subject to
subsection (3).
(3) No person shall be removed to or detained in a leper asylum under this section
unless a certificate shall have been given by two medical practitioners that such person is
actually suffering from the disease of leprosy.
(4) For the purposes of this section, the Minister shall have power to order a leper or
an alleged leper to attend to be examined, and in such case the provisions of section 8(2)
shall apply in all respects as if the Minister were empowered thereby.

### 11.

Leper wandering about begging, etc.
(1) It shall be lawful for a magistrate, on information upon oath of any credible
witness that a leper or alleged leper has been wandering about begging or collecting alms,
or seeking precarious support, or exposing his or her leprosy in any public road, street or
place, to summon the leper or alleged leper to appear before him or her, or, if he or she
thinks it necessary, such magistrate may issue a warrant under his or her hand directed to
a police officer authorising or directing the police officer to cause such leper or alleged
leper to be brought before him or her at a time and place to be specified in the warrant.
(2) If, on the hearing of the case, it is proved to the satisfaction of such magistrate that
such person is a leper, the magistrate shall report his or her decision to the Minister who shall
have power to proceed in the case of such person in the manner provided in section 10. The
magistrate shall direct that pending the making of an order by the Minister such person be
removed and conveyed to a leper asylum by a police officer.

### 12.

Detention of leper awaiting trial or convicted
When a leper is committed to take his or her trial or for further examination, or is
sentenced to imprisonment for any offence or for the non-payment of any money or
penalty, the court ordering the commitment or inflicting the imprisonment shall direct the
leper to be committed to a leper asylum, there to be kept or there to undergo his or her
sentence of imprisonment as the case may be.

### 13.

Prison accommodation in asylum

It shall be lawful for the Minister, by Order, to set apart any portion of any leper
asylum as a place in which lepers committed or sentenced to imprisonment under the last
preceding section shall be detained, and it shall be lawful for the Minister to make rules
and regulations as to the management of such portions so set apart, and as to the treatment
and discipline of lepers so committed or undergoing such imprisonment.

## PART IV

Removal and Discharge from Asylum

### 14.

Removal of lepers
The Minister may, in writing, direct the removal of a leper from an asylum or other
institution to some other asylum or institution, and such direction shall be sufficient
authority for the removal of such leper and also for his or her reception into and detention
in the asylum or institution to which he or she is directed to be removed.

### 15.

Minister may discharge from asylum
The Minister may, in writing, direct the discharge of an inmate of a leper asylum, not
being a prisoner under sentence of imprisonment, upon security being given by some
person on behalf of such inmate to the Secretary to the Cabinet by bond in the sum of five
hundred dollars that such inmate shall be properly isolated, maintained, and treated in
private and shall not be suffered to be at large.

### 16.

Discharge on certificate by Medical Superintendent
Whenever it appears to a Medical Superintendent that an inmate of a leper asylum is
fit to be discharged therefrom, the Medical Superintendent shall certify the same to the
Minister and thereupon it shall be lawful for the Minister to direct that the inmate shall be
discharged.

## PART V

Prohibition of Certain Trades, etc., to Lepers

### 17.

Prohibition of certain trades to lepers
No leper shall carry on any of the trades or callings specified in the Schedule or any
other trade or calling which the Minister may, by Order, prohibit lepers from carrying on.

### 18.

Penalty for carrying on prohibited trade
A leper who shall carry on any trade or calling as aforesaid, and a person who shall
knowingly employ a leper in any such trade or calling, shall be guilty of an offence and
liable to a fine of five hundred dollars, or, if the person convicted is not a leper, to
imprisonment for one month; and a leper so convicted shall be committed by the convicting
magistrate to a leper asylum to be detained there until discharged by direction of the
Minister.

### 19.

Lepers entering public vehicles, etc.
A person knowing himself or herself to be a leper who shall enter any public vehicle,
or lodge in any hotel, boarding-house, or lodging-house, or enter any shop where food or
drink is sold, or bathe in any public bath, and the proprietor or person in charge of any
such public vehicle, hotel, boarding-house, lodging-house, shop, or bath, who shall

knowingly allow a leper to enter the same, shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a
fine of five hundred dollars, or, if the person convicted is not a leper, to imprisonment for
one month; and a leper so convicted shall be committed by the convicting magistrate to a
leper asylum to be detained there until discharged by direction of the Minister.

### 20.

Sale of food, etc., by lepers and others
(1) If a leper sells, barters or exchanges any article of food or drink, he or she shall be
guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of five hundred dollars and may be committed by
the convicting magistrate to a leper asylum to be detained there until discharged by
direction of the Minister.
(2) If a person knowingly sells, barters or exchanges any article of food or drink
which has been prepared or handled by a leper, or with which a leper has come in contact,
he or she shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of five hundred dollars and to
imprisonment for one month.

### 21.

Obtaining article from inmate of an asylum
A person who purchases, or who without the permission in writing of the Medical
Superintendent receives, from an inmate of a leper asylum, any articles of whatsoever
nature, shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of two hundred and fifty dollars,
and to imprisonment for fourteen days.

## PART VI

Landing of Lepers Prohibited

### 22.

Landing of lepers prohibited
Subject to the provisions of section 24, no leper who is not a citizen of Grenada or
lawfully domiciled therein shall land at any of the ports of Grenada from any place not
within Grenada, and the master or other person in charge of any vessel who suffers or
omits to prevent the landing from such vessel at any such port of a person whom he or
she knows or has reasonable grounds to suppose to be a leper shall be guilty of an offence
and liable to a fine of five thousand dollars.

### 23.

Lepers unlawfully landed may be returned to place whence they came
Every leper landing in contravention of the provisions of the last preceding section
may be brought before a magistrate, who may examine such leper and any witness on oath
touching the place from which he or she was brought into Grenada and may cause such
leper to be removed to the place from which he or she was so brought in such manner as the
Minister may direct, and the reasonable cost of such removal and of his or her maintenance
in Grenada pending his or her removal shall be borne and paid by the master or other person
in charge of the vessel by which such leper was brought to Grenada by whose act or default
such leper was permitted to land, and may be sued for and recovered as money due from
him to her to the Government.

### 24.

Port officer may detain leper on vessel
(1) If the port officer of any port in Grenada, on boarding any vessel which shall
come into Grenada, has reason to suspect that a person on board such vessel is afflicted
with leprosy, but such person shall deny that he or she is so afflicted, it shall be lawful for
such port officer, by writing under his or her hand directed to the master or other person
in charge of such vessel, to make order that the person so suspected to be so afflicted
shall be detained on board until he or she shall have been examined by the health officer

of the port and such other medical practitioner or practitioners, if any, as the Minister
shall see fit to appoint, and the master or other person in charge of the vessel shall cause
such person to be detained on board such vessel until the examination; and if any such
person, in respect of whom any such order of detention shall be made, shall land in
Grenada without the order of the port officer or the permission of the Minister being first
granted, the master or other person in charge of such vessel shall be guilty of an offence
and liable to a fine of ten thousand dollars.
(2) Such port officer, immediately on making any such order for the detention of any
person as aforesaid, shall notify the same and the time of making such order to the Minister
and the health officer of the port; and such health officer, together with such medical
practitioner or practitioners, if any, as shall be appointed by the Minister for that purpose,
shall, within twenty-four hours from the time of the making of such order, visit such vessel
and examine such person, and shall certify in writing to the Minister whether the person is
afflicted with leprosy or not; and if it shall appear by the certificate that the person is not
afflicted with leprosy, the port officer shall forthwith make an order permitting the landing of
such person; but if it shall appear by the certificate that the person is afflicted with leprosy, it
shall not be lawful for such person to land in Grenada without the permission of the Minister,
to be granted on its being made to appear to him that such person is a citizen or domiciled
inhabitant of Grenada:
Provided that it shall be lawful for the Minister to grant permission for the landing in
Grenada of any person afflicted with leprosy and not being a citizen of or domiciled in
Grenada on being satisfied that two sufficient persons, being householders within
Grenada, have entered into a bond to the Secretary to the Cabinet in the penal sum of
ten thousand dollars, conditioned that such person shall not wander about begging or
collecting alms or exposing himself or herself in any public road, street, or place in
Grenada, nor do any act whereby such person may become chargeable upon the public
funds of Grenada, and that such person shall conform to such rules as may from time to
time be made under this Act providing for the proper isolation of lepers not detained in
leper asylums.

### 25.

Lepers landing may be sent to asylum
Every person unlawfully landing in breach of the provisions of the last preceding
section may be committed to a leper asylum by a warrant under the hand of the Minister
and may be detained therein until discharged by direction of the Minister.

## PART VII

Management and Discipline of Asylums

### 26.

Power of Minister to make rules
The Minister may make rules with respect to all or any of the following matters—
(a)

the forms of certificates, warrants, bonds and orders to be used under this
Act;

(b)

the management, inspection, discipline, and control of leper asylums;

(c)

the visiting of lepers in leper asylums by their relations and friends;

(d)

the appointment and duties of officers and employees of leper asylums;

(e)

the appointment of official visitors and the periodical visiting of leper
asylums by them;

(f)

prescribing such measures of cleansing and disinfection as he or she may
consider necessary in the case of premises inhabited by persons suffering
from leprosy;

(g)

prescribing for the effective isolation of lepers not detained in leper asylums;

(h)

prescribing the fees payable to medical practitioners in respect of the
examination of lepers or alleged lepers, and of reports to be made and
certificates granted thereon;

(i)

generally, for carrying into effect the provisions of this Act.

### 27.

Violation of rules by inmates of asylum
If an inmate of a leper asylum is guilty of the violation of any rule made under the
authority of the last preceding section for the management, discipline, and control of such
asylum, the Medical Superintendent, after due inquiry upon oath, may order such inmate
to undergo any one of the following punishments—
(a)

exclusion from seeing friends for not longer than three months;

(b)

deprivation of tobacco for not longer than one month;

(c)

reduction of rations for not longer than seven days;

(d)

solitary confinement for not longer than three days, and to receive only bread
and water or such diet as the Medical Superintendent shall order.

### 28.

More serious offences by inmates of asylum
If an inmate of a leper asylum escapes from it, or is guilty of violations of any rules
made as aforesaid which in the opinion of the Medical Superintendent cannot adequately
be punished under the provisions of the last preceding section, the Visiting Justice, after
due inquiry upon oath, may order such inmate to undergo any one or more of the
following punishments—
(a)

exclusion from seeing friends for not longer than six months;

(b)

deprivation of tobacco for not longer than two months;

(c)

reduction of rations for not longer than fourteen days;

(d)

solitary confinement for not longer than seven days, and to receive only
bread and water or such diet as the Medical Superintendent shall order;

(e)

imprisonment for any term not exceeding one month.

### 29.

Solitary confinement
An inmate of a leper asylum ordered to undergo solitary confinement under the
provisions of the two last preceding sections shall have one hour in the open air in the
morning and in the evening in charge of an attendant and apart from the other inmates.

## PART VIII

General

### 30.

Visiting Justice
It shall be lawful for the Minister to appoint a magistrate or a Justice of the Peace to be
Visiting Justice of any leper asylum, and such magistrate or Justice shall have jurisdiction
to hear and determine all cases arising under section 28.

### 31.

Penalty on person trespassing in asylum

A person not having official business at a leper asylum who, without the permission of
the Medical or Resident Superintendent thereof, goes within the limit thereof shall be
guilty of trespass and liable to a fine of two hundred and fifty dollars.

### 32.

Aiding leper to escape
A person who shall aid or abet any inmate of a leper asylum in escaping or attempting
to escape from such asylum before such inmate has been lawfully discharged therefrom,
shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of five hundred dollars and to
imprisonment for three months.

### 33.

Detention and recapture of lepers
Every person received into a leper asylum under any warrant or order issued under the
provisions of this Act or of any other Act in that behalf may be detained therein until he
or she is removed or discharged, and in case of escape may, by virtue of such warrant or
order, be captured by the officer in charge of such asylum or any officer or employee
thereof or any police officer, and be again conveyed to and received and detained in such
asylum.

### 34.

Proof of leprosy, by medical evidence, essential
(1) No leper or alleged leper shall be convicted of an offence under this Act or dealt
with under the provisions of section 11 or removed from Grenada under the provisions of
this Act, and no person shall be convicted of any offence with respect to the landing, and
no costs shall be recovered with respect to the removal or maintenance, of any leper or
alleged leper, except on the evidence or certificate of two medical practitioners that such
person is a leper.
(2) The certificate in writing of a medical practitioner shall be admissible as evidence
under this section, unless the Court shall otherwise direct.

### 35.

Cleansing and disinfection of premises
It shall be lawful for a District Medical Officer or a person appointed by him or her, at
all reasonable times, to enter any premises which, within six months before such entry,
have been inhabited by a person suffering from leprosy, and to do such acts therein for
the purpose of cleansing and disinfecting the same as may, from time to time, be
prescribed by rules under section 26.

### 36.

Action against persons purporting to act under this Act
In an action against a person for anything purporting to have been done in pursuance of
the provisions of this Act, or of any rule made thereunder, there shall be judgement for the
defendant unless the plaintiff has alleged and proved that the action complained of was
done by such person maliciously and without reasonable or probable cause.

### 37.

General penalty
A person committing a breach of any of the provisions of this Act or of any rules
made thereunder for which no penalty is expressly provided by this Act shall be guilty of
an offence and liable to a fine of five hundred dollars.

### 38.

Summary procedure
All persons punishable by fine or imprisonment under this Act may be proceeded
against summarily before a magistrate, and the manner of procedure in all such cases
shall be the summary procedure according to the Criminal Procedure Code, Chapter 72B.

LEPERS SETTLEMENT RULES
[Section 26.]
[31st October, 1928.]

---

### 1.

Citation
These Rules may be cited as the Lepers Settlement Rules.

## PART I

Preliminary and Official Visitors

### 2.

Official Visitors
The Official Visitors of this Settlement shall be members of the Cabinet and such
other persons as the Minister may, from time to time, appoint.

### 3.

Visiting times
The Official Visitors may visit the Settlement at any time, inspect the books and
interrogate the officers and patients.

### 4.

Record of visit
Their visits shall be recorded in the Official Visitors Book.

## PART II

Medical Superintendent

### 5.

Responsibility of Medical Superintendent
The Medical Superintendent shall be responsible for the administration of the
institution, the care of the inmates and shall have entire control of the staff.

### 6.

Visits to Settlement
He or she shall visit the Settlement at least three times a week and oftener if required.

### 7.

Examination of every case
He or she shall examine every case on admission, cause the name to be entered on the
register and give such directions for diet and treatment as may be necessary.

### 8.

Inspection of food supplies
He or she shall frequently inspect the food supplied to the institution and satisfy
himself or herself that it is of proper quantity and quality.

### 9.

Annual report to Chief Medical Officer
He or she shall furnish to the Chief Medical Officer on or before the 31st day of
January in each year a report on the administration of the hospital for the previous year
with all the statistics, tables and statements required for the Blue Book.

## PART III

Steward

### 10.

Steward to reside on premises
The Steward shall reside upon the premises, and not absent himself or herself
therefrom except with the permission of the Medical Superintendent in charge.

### 11.

Attendance on all Official Visitors

He or she shall be in attendance upon all Official Visitors, and on the Medical
Superintendent, whose instructions he or she will receive and see executed.

### 12.

Care and supervision of inmates
The care and immediate superintendence of the inmates are vested in the Steward, and
his or her authority shall extend over all inmates and subordinates of the Settlement. He or
she shall use his or her authority with firmness and kindness and shall enforce similar
conduct on the part of the subordinates.

### 13.

Inspections of buildings and grounds
He or she shall visit the wards and dining rooms of the institution at least twice daily,
and see that everything therein is clean and tidy. The surrounding grounds and
outbuildings must be similarly inspected at least once in twenty-four hours. The time of
his or her visit, and any remarks which he or she may have to make must be entered in a
journal which must be kept for the purpose.

### 14.

Keeping of diet book and order of supplies
He or she shall keep the diet book and make out the orders for food supplies in
accordance with the diet scale and instructions of the Medical Superintendent.

### 15.

Charge of furnishings and stores
He or she shall have charge of all furniture, clothing and bedding and other stores.

### 16.

Keeping of store books and inventories
He or she shall keep all necessary store books and inventories.

### 17.

Carrying out of instructions of Medical Superintendent
He or she shall see that all instructions of the Medical Superintendent are promptly
carried out.

### 18.

Permission to sleep at institution
He or she shall not allow any person not connected with the institution to sleep
therein.

### 19.

Receiving of complaints
He or she shall at all times be ready to receive the complaints of any inmate and,
should he or she deem it necessary, report the same to the Medical Superintendent in
charge.

### 20.

Supervision of meals
He or she shall superintend the arrangements for the messing of the inmates, and
distribution of their meals, and see that economy is observed in the management of the
kitchen.

## PART IV

Nurse

### 21.

Nurse to reside on premises
The nurse shall reside on the premises and shall not leave the institution without the
permission of the Medical Superintendent.

### 22.

Assistance to Steward
She or he shall assist the Steward in such manner as that officer may, from time to
time, direct, in the general management of the institution.

### 23.

Charge of patients
She or he shall have immediate charge of the patients and shall faithfully carry out the
instructions of the Medical Superintendent, and in his or her absence, the instructions of
the Steward.

### 24.

Responsibility in absence of Steward
She or he shall, in the absence of the Steward from the Settlement, be responsible for
the maintenance of order, the care of the inmates and the custody of all Government
property in the Settlement.

### 25.

Nurse’s uniforms
The nurse must wear the uniform provided for her or him while on duty and will not
be allowed to wear it while on leave. She or he will be required to keep it in repair and
scrupulously clean.

### 26.

Responsibilities
The nurse will be responsible for all articles placed under her or him immediate
charge.

## PART V

Inmates

### 27.

Conduct of inmates
All inmates shall submit to the authority of the Medical Superintendent, and conduct
themselves quietly, decently and obediently; shall rise, with the exception of the very
feeble, at 6 a.m., wash and clean themselves and give assistance in the arrangement of
their ward, and in the doing of such work required to be done outside the ward as shall be
approved by the Medical Superintendent, if called upon so to do, and shall retire to bed at
8 p.m., when all lights shall be extinguished, except such lights as may be authorised to
be kept burning during the night.

### 28.

Certain privileges
Such inmates as are approved of by the Medical Superintendent shall be permitted to
bathe in the sea at convenient times and under the personal charge of the Steward in the
case of the males, and of the nurse in the case of the females.

### 29.

Discharge
No inmate shall leave the institution until discharged therefrom by the Cabinet.

### 30.

Offences: minor
For minor offences the Medical Superintendent may inquire into such offences and
punish the offender in accordance with section 27 of the Act.

### 31.

Offences: more serious
More serious offences committed by inmates will render them liable to be tried by the
Visiting Justice.

### 32.

Damage to Government property
The inmates are forbidden to injure, destroy or sell clothing, bedding, food or any
other property of the Government.

### 33.

Tobacco and alcohol
Tobacco and alcoholic drinks shall only be issued to well conducted inmates and by
written order of the Medical Superintendent. No such articles shall be brought into the
institution without his or her sanction.

### 34.

Male and female inmates to be separated
The male inmates are forbidden to enter the ward or grounds of the female inmates,
and the latter are forbidden to enter the premises of the male inmates.

### 35.

Visiting hours for inmates
Inmates may receive visits from their friends on Sundays from 2 p.m. to 4 p.m., and in
special cases at other times with the permission of the Medical Superintendent.

### 36.

Pass required from Medical Superintendent
Any inmate leaving the institution without a pass from the Medical Superintendent
will be treated as having escaped, and may be arrested and returned to the institution by
any member of the Police Force, or by any officer or servant of the institution.

## CHAPTER 287

RESTRICTION OF MOVEMENT ACT
An Act to restrict the freedom of movement of certain persons.
[Act No. 6 of 1986.]

[1st February, 1986.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Restriction of Movement Act.
2\. Power of Minister to restrict the freedom of movement of certain persons
If the Minister for the time being responsible for national security is satisfied that a
person whose aims, tendencies or objectives include the overthrow of the democratic and
Parliamentary system of government of Grenada is likely to travel abroad, the Minister, if
satisfied also that restriction of the movement of that person is reasonably required in the
interests of defence or public safety or public order, may, by Order, restrict the freedom
of movement of that person by prohibiting him or her from boarding any vessel or aircraft
in Grenada for the purpose of travelling abroad.

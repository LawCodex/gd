## CHAPTER 63A

CONTINUOUS REVISION OF THE LAWS ACT
An Act to make provision for the preparation and publication of a Continuous
Revised Edition of the Laws of Grenada, and for matters incidental thereto and
connected therewith.
[Act No. 37 of 1994.]

[25th November, 1994.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Continuous Revision of the Laws Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“Act” includes—
(a)

any Act of the Parliament of Grenada;

(b)

any Ordinance or Code (as so entitled at the passing thereof) passed by a
legislative authority in or for Grenada at any time;

(c)

any People’s Law (as so entitled at the date of its declaration or
proclamation);

“Continuous Revised Edition” means the Continuous Revised Edition of the Laws
of Grenada prepared pursuant to this Act;
“Minister” means the Minister for the time being responsible for legal affairs;
“Revision” means “Continuous Revised Edition”.
3\. Appointment of Commissioner
The Attorney-General is appointed Commissioner to prepare, under the authority of
this Act, a Continuous Revised Edition of the Laws of Grenada.
4\. Duty of Commissioner
The duty of the Commissioner is to prepare, in accordance with the provisions of this
Act, a Continuous Revised Edition of the Laws of Grenada.
5\. Laws in and periodicity of Revision
The Commissioner shall from time to time determine the laws to be included in the
Continuous Revised Edition and the periodicity of such Continuous Revised Edition.
6\. Form of Revision
(1) The Continuous Revised Edition shall be printed and published in the form of
bound or loose-leaf volumes as the Commissioner may determine, in such number of
volumes as the Commissioner may determine, containing, subject to the provisions of this
Act, such Laws of Grenada in force from time to time as the Commissioner determines
should be included in such a Revision.
(2) Each volume of the Continuous Revised Edition shall commence with a title page
containing the expressions—
(a)

“The Continuous Revised Edition of the Laws of Grenada”; and

(b)

“Printed and Published with the authority of the Government of Grenada”.

(3) Subject to the provisions of this Act, all Acts that are included in the Continuous
Revised Edition shall be arranged in alphabetical order of titles, numbered consecutively,
each in the form of a Chapter commencing with the Long Title, the date on which the
principal law came into force, and a marginal reference specifying the number and year of
the principal law and of any amending or incorporated law found by the Commissioner to
have been of significance when compiling the text.
(4) Volumes of the Continuous Revised Edition may be numbered consecutively even
though they are printed and published over different periods of time no matter how far
apart such periods of time may be the one from the other.
7\. Contents of Revision
(1) The Continuous Revised Edition shall contain—
(a)

such Laws of Grenada in force from time to time as the Commissioner
determines should be included in such a Revision;

(b)

a table of contents in such form as the Commissioner considers appropriate;

(c)

an index in such form as the Commissioner considers appropriate.

(2) The table of contents and index referred to in subsection (1)(b) and (c) need not be
in a separate volume.

(3) For the purposes of subsection (1)(c) the Commissioner may at his or her
discretion include before the text of any written law included in the Continuous Revised
Edition an appropriate Arrangement of Sections or other similar arrangement in tabular
form showing the framework and general content of the corresponding text.
8\. Omission of laws
The Commissioner may omit from the Continuous Revised Edition any such law as in
his or her discretion he or she considers should be omitted from the Revision.
9\. Validity of laws not to be affected by mere omission, etc.
(1) No written law omitted from the Continuous Revised Edition under the authority
of this Act or otherwise shall be deemed to be without force or validity by reason only of
the fact that it has been omitted.
(2) An Act omitted from the Continuous Revised Edition under the authority of this
Act or otherwise shall, if in force when such Revision is brought into force, remain in
force until it shall have been expressly repealed or shall have expired or become spent or
had its effect.
(3) The repeal or omission from the Continuous Revised Edition of an Act repealed or
omitted during and for the purpose of the preparation of the Continuous Revised Edition
shall not affect the validity of any existing resolutions of Parliament or any subsidiary
legislation made under such an Act, but such resolutions and subsidiary legislation so far
as they are not inconsistent with any other written law shall continue in force until
otherwise provided.
10\. Powers of the Commissioner
(1) In the preparation of the Continuous Revised Edition the Commissioner has the
following powers—
(a)

to add a short title or citation to any written law that may require it and,
where necessary, to alter the short title or citation;

(b)

to arrange and renumber written laws and any provisions thereof in any
group or sequence that may be convenient;

(c)

to transfer any provision contained in a written law from that written law to
another written law to which it more properly belongs;

(d)

to consolidate into one written law any two or more written laws in pari
materia making all such alterations as are thereby rendered necessary or
expedient to the consolidated written law and affixing such date thereto as
may seem most convenient;

(e)

to divide any written law into parts or other divisions and to give such parts
and divisions suitable headings;

(f)

to divide any written law into two or more written laws and to make such
amendments including the supplying of titles or the alteration of existing
titles as are thereby rendered necessary;

(g)

to alter the form or arrangement of any provision in a written law either by
combining it in whole or in part with another provision or provisions or by
dividing it into two or more provisions or by transposing words;

(h)

to shorten and simplify the phraseology, and to modify the terminology, of
any written law, and to make formal alterations thereto, for securing
uniformity of expression or clarity without affecting the meaning of the same
or any other written law;

(i)

to correct grammatical and typographical errors and any similar mistakes or
omissions in any written law so as to perfect the expression of an evidently
intended meaning;

(j)

to supply or alter definitions of words or expressions used in any written
law, or to substitute for any definition of a word or expression such other
definition as seems to be more appropriate;

(k)

to correct cross-references;

(l)

to correct punctuation;

(m)

to supply, alter or omit any marginal note, head note, heading, footnote or
marginal reference;

(n)

to make such formal alterations as to names, localities, ranks, titles, offices,
or otherwise as may be necessary to bring any written law into conformity
with the circumstances; and

(o)

generally to do all things relating to form and method which in his or her
opinion are necessary for the perfecting of the Continuous Revised Edition.

(2) The Commissioner has power to make such modifications and adaptations and to
attach such qualifications and exceptions to any written law as may appear to him or her
to be necessary or desirable yet proper in consequence of changes over the years—
(a)

generally in the circumstances of Grenada or in the Government or public
service thereof;

(b)

in the constitution or written law of any other country or any international
body or organisation including the Commonwealth; or

(c)

in consequence of the provisions of the West Indies Act, 1967, the West
Indies Associated States Supreme Court Order, 1967 and the Grenada
Constitution Orders of 1967 and 1973.

(3) The Commissioner has the further powers and the duties specified in the Schedule.
11\. Limitation of powers and mode of dealing with amendments of substance
(1) Nothing in section 10(1) shall be taken to confer on or to imply in the
Commissioner any power to make any amendment that affects the substance of a written
law or any part thereof except as provided by this Act.
(2) In every case where any such amendment of an Act is considered by the
Commissioner to be desirable, he or she shall draft a Bill setting forth the amendment and
authorising it to be made in the Continuous Revised Edition. Any such Bill may have
reference to more Acts than one even though they are not in pari materia.
(3) In every case where an Act requires such considerable amendment as to require its
entire recasting the Commissioner shall draft a Bill accordingly.
(4) Every Bill drafted pursuant to this section shall, subject to the approval of the
Minister and the granting of authority by the Cabinet, be introduced if Parliament and
dealt with in accordance with normal procedure.
12\. Construction of references
Where in any written law or other instrument or document reference is made to any
written law the provisions of which have, by virtue of this Act, been embodied in the text
of the Continuous Revised Edition, such reference shall where necessary and practicable
be deemed to extend and apply to the corresponding provisions in the Continuous
Revised Edition.

13\. Validity of Revision
Subject to section 9, from the date of a Proclamation under section 14(2), the
Continuous Revised Edition or, as the case may be, the part or parts thereof that are
brought into operation shall be deemed to be, and shall without any question whatsoever
in all courts and for all purposes whatsoever be, the sole and only proper Statute Book for
Grenada in respect of the written laws contained therein:
Provided that nothing in this section shall affect the operation of any law which, before
that date, may be enacted repealing, altering or otherwise amending any earlier law
although such earlier law has already been included in the Continuous Revised Edition.
14\. Bringing Revision into force
(1) The Commissioner shall cause six complete sets of the Continuous Revised
Edition, each in such number of Volume as he or she considers appropriate, to be sealed
on the title page of each volume with the Public Seal of Grenada and shall cause one set
to be delivered in each case to the Governor-General, the Minister, the Chief Justice, the
Clerk of the House of Representatives and the Registrar of the Supreme Court, and he or
she shall retain one set himself or herself.
(2) Upon the passing of a resolution signifying the approval of Parliament and
authorising him or her to do so, the Governor-General may by Proclamation bring into
force the whole of the Continuous Revised Edition, or such part or parts thereof as may
be completed from time to time and published, and thereupon, from the date specified in
the Proclamation and to the extent specified therein, the enactments in the whole or, as
the case may be, the part or parts so proclaimed shall be substituted for the enactments
therein reproduced and revised and shall for all purposes be of full force and effect and
shall, without any question whatsoever, be recognised in all courts.
(3) It shall be the duty of those persons to whom a set of the Continuous Revised
Edition has been delivered pursuant to subsection (1) to maintain that set in safe keeping.
15\. Printer of Revision
The Continuous Revised Edition shall be deemed to have been printed and published
by the Government Printer.
16\. Rectification of errors or omissions in Revision
(1) Notwithstanding sections 13 and 14 if, after publication of the Continuous
Revised Edition, any error or omission is found to have occurred therein it may be
rectified, with or without retrospective effect, by an order (to be called a Correction
Order) making the required amendment.
(2) An Order under subsection (1) shall be made by the Commissioner as soon as is
practicable after the error or omission has come to his or her notice but shall not be made
more than twelve months after the Continuous Revised Edition has been brought into
operation.
17\. Updating Revision
(1) The Commissioner may keep the Continuous Revised Edition updated by
replacing any part thereof with any amendment made thereto by Parliament from time to
time.
(2) The updating referred to in subsection (1) need not entail the preparing of an
additional volume of the Continuous Revised Edition but rather where considered
appropriate by the Commissioner may be done simply by replacing the relevant page or
pages of the Revision.

### 3.

Where in any written law there appears an amount—
(a)

prescribed as a fine or a maximum fine that may be imposed by subordinate
law-making authority;

(b)

specified for the purpose of a bond or recognisance into which person is
required to enter,

the Commissioner may with respect to that amount apply the provisions paragraph 2.

### 4.

Wherever in any written law both a fine and imprisonment are prescribed penalties
for an offence, the Commissioner may ensure that the word “and” employed to link those
penalties regardless whether, before its inclusion in the Revision, the written law was
actually or intended to be construed as providing for those penalties to be alternative only
and not cumulative.

### 5.

(1) Subject to subparagraph (2), wherever in any written law there occurs a
expression, in whatever terms, signifying an amount of 6, 12, 24, 48 or 96 cents, and
$1.20, $2.40, $4.80, $9.60, $24, $48, $96, $120, $240, $480, $960, $2,400, $4,800
$24,000, or $48,000 or any multiple thereof, the Commissioner may increase those
amounts respectively to 10, 15, 25 and 50 cents, and $1, $1.25, $2.50, $5, $10, $25 $50,
$100, $125, $250, $500, $1,000, $2,500, $5,000, $25,000 or $50,000 as the case may be,
and in the case of a multiple may, in a corresponding manner, increase the same to an
appropriate equivalent.
(2) The Commissioner shall not apply the provisions of paragraph (1) with respect to
any amount prescribed by the written law as a tax or duty, nor to the amount of any fine,
or sum required to be paid or forfeited by way of penalty nor to any other amount that is
subject to the application of paragraph 2.

### 6.

Wherever in any written law there occurs a reference to a weight or measure
expressed otherwise than according to the metric system, the Commissioner may
substitute for that reference a weight or measure, as the case may be, equivalent thereto
expressed as a unit of the metric system.

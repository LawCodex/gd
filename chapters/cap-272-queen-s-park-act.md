## CHAPTER 272

QUEEN’S PARK ACT
An Act to vest the control of the Queen’s Park in the Minister for the time being
responsible.
[Amended by Act No. 31 of 1969, Act No. 30 of 1973.]

[15th September, 1927.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Queen’s Park Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act, “Park” means the Queen’s Park.
3\. Vesting control of the Queen’s Park in the Minister
(1) The Minister, for the time being responsible for the Park, shall have the
management, control and care of the Park and shall maintain and keep the same in good
condition and may enclose the same with proper railings and gates, and may drain, level,
lay out, ornament and otherwise improve the same, and may erect and maintain buildings
and do all such works and things as may be requisite for the use of the Park as a public
recreation ground.
(2) The Minister may appoint such officers and employees as he or she may think fit
and may dismiss any such officer or employee.
4\. Rules
(1) The Minister may make rules—

(a)

regulating the use of the Park and of any building thereon and the days and
times of admission thereto, and providing for the preservation of order and
prevention of nuisances therein, and for the removal of any person infringing
any rule by any police officer or by any officer or employee appointed by the
Minister;

(b)

providing for the exclusive use of portions of the Park by clubs or other
bodies of persons and fixing the fees to be paid for such exclusive use;

(c)

fixing the fees to be paid for admission to the Park or any portion thereof or
any building thereon on specified occasions;

(d)

regulating the depasturing of animals in the park or any portion thereof and
fixing the fees to be paid for such depasturing;

(e)

regulating the granting of permission to persons to sell refreshments in the
Park and the terms and conditions on which such permission may be
granted;

(f)

in respect of any matter or thing, whether similar or not to those above
mentioned, in respect of which it may be expedient to make rules for the
purpose of carrying out the provisions of this Act.

(2) For the due observance of these Rules, the Minister shall appoint a committee
consisting of not more than nine members of whom three shall form a quorum. The
members of the committee shall hold office for two years and may be re-appointed.
(3) Whosoever commits any offence provided for by a rule made in pursuance of
subsection (1)(a) shall be liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of one thousand five
hundred dollars and to imprisonment for six months, unless the rule otherwise provides.
5\. Application of fees
All fees collected under the provisions of this Act shall be applied towards the
management, maintenance and improvement of the Park.

### 1.

Citation
These Rules may be cited as the Queen’s Park Rules.

### 2.

Fees for exclusive use of Park or part thereof
The fees set out in the Schedule hereto shall be payable by the proprietor of any circus
or similar show, or by any sports club or association or other persons granted the
exclusive use of the Park or any part thereof for any of the purposes set out in the said
Schedule, with or without the pavilion.

### 3.

No admission without permission
When exclusive use of the Park or any part thereof, or the pavilion, is granted, no
person shall be admitted thereto except with the permission of the person or body of
persons granted such exclusive use.

### 4.

Prices of admission
For admission to the Park, or any part thereof, when exclusive use is granted, the
following fees shall be paid to the person or body of persons granted such exclusive use—
(a)

for every person admitted to the Park or any part thereof ............ $0.10;

(b)

for every bicycle or tricycle and for every horse or other ridden $0.10;
animal ...........................................................................................

(c)

for admission of every private motor car or other vehicle ........... $1.00;

(d)

for admission of every hired motor car or other vehicle .............. $1.50.

### 5.

Exclusion of the public from pitches, tracks, etc., when in use
Other members of the public shall be excluded from the Park or any portion thereof
(as the case may be) while the same is in actual use by any members of the public for the
purpose of cricket, football, or any other game or recreation, or for rifle practice, or for
horse racing, or the exercise of any horse on the race track.

### 6.

Exclusion of the public when exclusive user is granted

The public shall be excluded from the Park, or the pavilion and its enclosures, or from
any portion of the Park or the pavilion, as the case may be, during the period for which
the exclusive use of the same, whether with or without the payment of any fee, has been
granted to any person, or body of persons.

### 7.

Application for use of Park or pavilion
(1) All applications for the exclusive use of the Park or any part thereof, with or
without the pavilion, shall be made to the Committee, who shall forward the same to the
Cabinet with their recommendations.
(2) All applications for the exclusive use of the pavilion or any part thereof shall be
made to the Committee who are authorised to grant or refuse the same as they think fit.

### 8.

Payment of fees into Treasury
All fees received by the Committee shall be paid into the Treasury and shall form part
of the General Revenue of the State.

### 9.

Preservation of Order
(1) If any person shall within the limits of the Park—
(a)

affix any notice or advertisement to any building, wall, fence, post or tree;

(b)

damage any fence, gate or turnstile;

(c)

commit any nuisance by depositing any offensive matter or rubbish;

(d)

throw, deposit or leave bottles or glass, broken or otherwise, or any
earthenware or old iron;

(e)

wilfully damage any seat, walk, footpath, cricket pitch, or other specially
prepared recreation ground;

(f)

cut, trim, uproot, damage or destroy any tree, shrub or plant;

(g)

dig any earth, cut any grass, or remove any turf, manure, or grass without
permission from the Committee;

(h)

discharge any firearm without the authority of the Committee;

(i)

wilfully neglect or refuse to obey the lawful orders of the Secretary, or any
person authorised by the Committee or of any police constable;

(j)

ride or drive furiously any animal or vehicle to the danger of the public;

(k)

set or light any fire;

(l)

molest, obstruct, or annoy any person or persons engaged in or looking on at
any game or recognised sport or pastime;

(m)

use any obscene language, throw any stone or other missile or otherwise
conduct himself or herself in any disorderly manner,

he or she shall be guilty of an offence against these Rules.
(2) Any person who, in the opinion of any member of the Committee, peace officer,
or any other person authorised by the Cabinet, has infringed any rule or done anything
prejudicial to good order in the Park or in the pavilion, may be required to depart
therefrom; and if he or she neglects or refuses to do so, he or she may be removed
therefrom by, or at the direction of, such member of the Committee, peace officer or
person so authorised as aforesaid.

### 10.

Advertisement

(1) Notwithstanding anything in rule 9 contained, the Committee may, in its
discretion, and subject to such conditions as it may think fit, grant permission to any
person to affix advertisements upon the inside of any walls or fences at the Park for such
periods as it shall think fit. The Committee shall be at Liberty to charge for each
advertisement so affixed a sum not exceeding fifteen dollars per month.
(2) Any permission granted under this rule shall be in writing and shall state the terms
and conditions under which it has been granted also the period in respect of which it has
been granted.

### 11.

Sale of refreshments
(1) Permission to sell refreshments of any kind in the Park must first be obtained from
the Committee.
(2) Such permission may be granted subject to the approval by the Committee of the
tariff of charges for such refreshments.
(3) If permission is granted as aforesaid subject to an approved tariff of charges the
person to whom permission is granted shall display a tariff signed by the Secretary and
shall not sell any refreshments otherwise than in accordance with such tariff. Any person
acting in contravention of this rule shall be guilty of an offence against these Rules.
(4) There shall be paid by the person to whom permission is granted such fee as may
be fixed by the Committee.

### 12.

Depasturing of animals
Horses, mules, sheep and goats shall be permitted to graze in the Park subject to the
conditions hereinafter set forth and on payment in advance of the following fees—
(a)

in the case of a horse or mule, fifty cents per mensem; and

(b)

in the case of a sheep or goat, ten cents per mensem:

Provided that if any animal is admitted after the fifteenth day of any month, then half
only of such charge shall be payable in respect of that month:
Provided further that every horse and mule shall be unshod.

### 13.

Conditions for depasturing
The conditions upon which animals are admitted to the Park shall be that—
(a)

no responsibility for any loss of, or any injury to, any animal admitted to the
Park shall attach to the Government, its servants or agents, or to any member
of the Committee;

(b)

there shall not be implied any undertaking by Government that the pasture
available shall furnish sufficient fodder for any animal;

(c)

the owner of any animal which has died in the Park or which, in the opinion
of any member of the Committee, is sick or injured or otherwise unfit to
remain in the Park shall on request remove such animal from the Park
forthwith and on failure to do so such animal may be removed and any
expenses incurred thereby shall be payable by the owner;

(d)

in the event of the Park being required on any particular day or days for any
purpose, any and every animal may be excluded therefrom altogether on
such day or days:
Provided that the number of such days shall not exceed ten in any one month;

(e)

the Committee may at any time refuse admission or require the removal of
an animal without assigning any reason;

(f)

no animal shall be admitted to the Park under any agreement for pasturage
before 6 a.m.

### 14.

Penalty
Any person found guilty of an offence against these Rules shall be liable, on summary
conviction, to a fine not exceeding two hundred and fifty dollars.

### 15.

Definitions
In these Rules—
“Committee” means the Committee appointed under section 4(2) of the Act for the
due observance of these Rules;
“owner” means the owner of any animal and includes any person lawfully having
the charge thereof.

## CHAPTER 227A

PAYMENT DEVICE COUNTERFEITING AND FRAUD ACT
An Act to provide penalties for credit and debit card and other payment device or
access device counterfeiting and related fraud.
[Act No. 21 of 1995.]

[5th April, 1995.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Payment Device Counterfeiting and Fraud Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“access device” has the same meaning as “payment device”;
“device-making equipment” means any equipment, mechanism, or impression
designed, used, or that can be used to produce a payment device, a fraudulent payment
device, or any aspect, component, or constituent part thereof;
“financial institution” has the same meaning in the Act as in the Banking Act,

## Chapter 26A;

“fraudulent access device” shall be construed in accordance with the expression
“fraudulent payment device”;
“fraudulent payment device” means—
(a)

payment device or a representation, depiction, facsimile, aspect or
component of a payment device that is counterfeit, fictitious, altered, forged,
lost, stolen, incomplete, expired, revoked, cancelled, fraudulently obtained,
or obtained as part of a scheme to defraud; or

(b)

any invoice, voucher, sales draft, or other reflection or manifestation of such
a device;

“payment device” means any card, plate, code, account number, or other means of
account access that can be used alone or in conjunction with another payment device,

to obtain money, goods, services, or any other thing of value, or for the purpose of
initiating a transfer of funds (other than a transfer originated solely by paper instrument);
“produce” means to make, forge, counterfeit, design, alter, authenticate, duplicate,
devise, fabricate, develop, or assemble; and as used in connection with a fraudulent
payment device shall also include, without limitation—
(a)

the alteration of, or any other means of causing, a payment device to become
a fraudulent payment device, and

(b)

producing any other device or thing that can be used as, or in lieu of, a
payment device or any constituent part, component or aspect thereof.

### 3.

Offences and penalties
(1) Whoever—
(a)

knowingly, and with intent to defraud, produces, buys, receives, exchanges,
furnishes, delivers, sells, traffics in or transfers a fraudulent payment device
or a fraudulent access device;

(b)

knowingly, and with intent to defraud, possesses, transfers, traffics in or
transports fraudulent payment devices or fraudulent access devices;

(c)

knowingly produces, buys, receives, exchanges, furnishes, delivers, uses,
sells, traffics in, transfers, controls, or possesses device-making equipment,
with the intent that such equipment be used in the production of a fraudulent
payment device or a fraudulent access device; or

(d)

attempts, conspires, or solicits another to do any of the activities set forth in
paragraphs (a), (b) and (c), shall be guilty of an offence.

(2) Any person who is guilty of an offence under subsection (1) of this section shall
be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding fifty thousand dollars or to
imprisonment for a term not exceeding five years or to both such fine and imprisonment.

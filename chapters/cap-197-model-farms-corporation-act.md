## CHAPTER 197

MODEL FARMS CORPORATION ACT
An Act to establish the Model Farms Corporation for the settlement of small
farmers, and connected purposes.
[Act No. 36 of 1986 amended by Act No. 25 of 1991.]

[29th August, 1986.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Model Farms Corporation Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“chairperson” means the chairperson of the Corporation;
“Corporation” means the Model Farms Corporation established under section 3;
“General Manager” means the General Manager appointed under section 6;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for agriculture;
“secretary” means the secretary of the Corporation.

### 3.

Establishment of the Model Farms Corporation
(1) There is established a body corporate to be called the Model Farms Corporation.
(2) The Corporation may—
(a)

sue and be sued in its corporate name;

(b)

enter into contracts in its corporate name;

(c)

have and use a Common Seal and alter or change that seal at pleasure;

(d)

acquire and hold real or personal property for the purposes of the
Corporation and dispose of or change that property;

(e)

employ a General Manager, a secretary and such other officers and
employees as may be found necessary for the performance of its function.

(3) Judicial notice shall be taken of the Common Seal of the Corporation
authenticated in the manner provided in the First Schedule.
(4) The Corporation shall consist of a Board of directors and the provisions of the
First Schedule shall have effect with respect to the constitution and proceedings of the
Board.

### 4.

Function of the Corporation
(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act it shall be the function of the Corporation—
(a)

to stimulate the settlement of small farmers by establishing viable small farm
units to be styled model farms for the purpose of sale or lease to approved
persons;

(b)

to draw up a comprehensive development plan of model farms to include all
farms and other land under its control;

(c)

to maintain and manage the farms vested in or controlled by it until such
time as they are disposed of as model farms.

(2) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Board shall have power to do all such
things as are in its opinion necessary for the proper discharge of the function of the
Corporation and in particular, but without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, the
Board on behalf of the Corporation may—
(a)

assist small farmers in acquiring inputs and marketing outputs, and may
itself trade in inputs and outputs but only when necessary;

(b)

buy stocks, shares and bonds;

(c)

sell or lease property or sell investments of whatever kind belonging to the
Corporation whenever appropriate;

(d)

raise loans and carry out transactions involving mortgages;

(e)

establish agencies and offices where necessary for monitoring the
implementation of model farms;

(f)

sell or lease submarginal land unsuited for agricultural purposes to model
farm operators or to persons connected with model farms operations to be
used as housing lots.

### 5.

Board to determine size of model farms
The Board shall determine the size of a model farm for a particular area or for a
particular unit in any area, taking into account factors relating to topography, soil,
rainfall, elevation and crop and livestock patterns in the area.

### 6.

Selection of suitable persons
The Board shall, on the recommendation of a committee, select suitable persons to
purchase or lease model farms from the Corporation.

### 7.

Appointment of General Manager
(1) The Board shall, with the approval of the Minister, appoint a General Manager
upon such terms and conditions as it may determine.
(2) The General Manager shall be the chief executive officer of the Corporation
responsible for carrying out its policy and for the general supervision of the activities of
the Corporation. He or she shall have the administrative control of all other officers, and
the employees and agents of the Corporation.
(3) The Board may, with the approval of the Minister, terminate the appointment of
the General Manager if the General Manager is found to be guilty of gross misconduct,
but the General Manager shall be informed in writing of any such charge made against
him or her and shall have the right to appear before the Board and to be heard in his or
her defence.
(4) The General Manager may at any time resign his or her office by letter addressed
and delivered to the chairperson.
(5) The Board may, subject to such conditions as it may specify, delegate to the
General Manager the power to appoint and to dismiss all or any of the officers,
employees or agents of the Corporation except the financial controller and the secretary,
including the power to suspend officers pending consideration of their case by the Board.

### 8.

Appointment of acting General Manager
(1) Where the office of General Manager becomes vacant for any reason whatsoever
the Board may, with the approval of the Minister, appoint an acting General Manager to
hold office for such period as the Board shall deem necessary.
(2) An acting General Manager shall perform the duties of the General Manager.

### 9.

Appointment of financial controller
The Board shall appoint a qualified and experienced accountant to be financial
controller of the Corporation on such terms as the Minister shall approve. The financial
controller shall be responsible to the General Manager.

### 10.

Appointment of secretary and other officers and employees
(1) Subject to subsection (2), the Board may, from time to time, appoint on such
terms and conditions as it may think fit a secretary and such other officers and employees
as it considers necessary for carrying out the function of the Corporation under this Act.
(2) The Board shall, before establishing a salary scale for any officer or employee or
class of officer or employee of the Corporation, submit for the approval of the Minister
particulars thereof and obtain his or her approval of such scale.
(3) The Public Service Commission may, subject to any conditions it may impose,
approve the secondment of a public officer to the service of the Corporation without
prejudice to any right or privilege of his or her as a public officer.

### 11.

Funds of the Corporation
The funds of the Corporation shall consist of—
(a)

such monies as may be voted by Parliament for the purpose;

(b)

monies paid to the Corporation by way of any grant, subsidy, donation or
gift;

(c)

any loan granted to the Corporation by the Government or by any other
person with the approval of the Minister;

(d)

monies which may in any manner for whatever purpose become payable to
the Corporation.

### 12.

Application of funds of Corporation
The Board may apply the funds of the Corporation for all or any of the following
purposes—
(a)

payment of the expenses of the Corporation incurred in the exercise of its
function under this Act;

(b)

payment of interest and the repayment of principal on any loan made to the
Corporation;

(c)

payment of salaries, remuneration, allowances, pensions, gratuities, and
superannuation benefits to its officers and employees;

(d)

setting aside such sums of money as it may deem appropriate in respect of the
development or depreciation or renewal of the property of the Corporation;

(e)

the appropriation of sums to a Reserve Fund;

(f)

any other expenditure authorised by the Board and properly chargeable to
revenue.

### 13.

Payment into Treasury
The Board shall pay all receipts from the sale or lease of model farms into a special
account in the Treasury.

### 14.

Estimates
(1) The Board shall cause to be prepared annually an estimate of the expenditure and
revenue of the Corporation in respect of the ensuing financial year and shall adopt such
estimate in respect of that year.
(2) Supplementary estimates may be adopted at any meeting of the Board.

(3) A copy of the estimate of revenue and expenditure and any supplementary
estimate shall, upon adoption by the Board, be sent to the Minister for his or her approval.

### 15.

Accounts and audit
(1) The Board shall cause proper accounts and other records in relation to the
business of the Corporation to be kept, and shall prepare annually a statement of accounts
in a form satisfactory to the Minister.
(2) The accounts and records shall be kept in such a manner as to secure the provision
of separate information in relation to each model farm and to show as far as can be, the
financial and operating results of each such farm.
(3) The statement of accounts in respect of each financial year shall present a true and
fair view of the financial position of the Corporation, and details of the financial position
of each model farm should be annexed thereto.
(4) The accounts of the Corporation shall be audited by an auditor annually appointed
by the Corporation and approved by the Minister.
(5) As soon as the accounts of the Corporation have been audited, the chairperson
shall send the statement of accounts referred to in subsection (3) to the Minister together
with a copy of any report of the auditors on that statement or on the accounts of the
Corporation.
(6) The Director of Audit shall be entitled at all reasonable times to examine accounts
and other records relating to the business of the Corporation if so directed by the Minister.

### 16.

Provision as to revenue and expenditure
(1) Every sum payable to the Corporation shall be collected and received as revenue
for and on account of the Corporation. Receipts for sums paid to the Corporation shall be
signed by an officer of the Corporation authorised by the General Manager.
(2) All payments out of the funds of the Corporation shall be made by officers of the
Corporation authorised by resolution of the Board.

### 17.

Protection of Corporation
Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, acts done and proceedings taken by the
Corporation under this Act may not be questioned on the ground of any omission, defect
or irregularity not affecting the merits of the case.

### 18.

Committees
(1) The Board shall appoint selection committees to examine persons applying for
model farms arid to make recommendations to the Board as to suitable persons, and such
a committee may co-opt individuals to assist in its evaluation of candidates.
(2) The provisions of the Second Schedule shall apply with respect to selection
committees.

### 19.

Exemption from taxes and duties
(1) No stamp duty shall be payable on any receipt, contract, instrument or other
document given or executed by the Corporation, but nothing in this section shall be
construed as exempting any person from liability to pay stamp duty on any power of
attorney, deed, or other document liable but for this section to duty under the Stamp Act,

## Chapter 309.

(2) Notwithstanding anything contained in any enactment, the Corporation shall not
be liable to pay income tax on profits or surpluses earned by the Corporation as a result of

its operations or customs duties on its importation of plant and machinery (including
motor vehicles, irrigation equipment, other equipment and spare parts) or any other tax or
duty whatsoever.

### 20.

Regulations
The Board may, with the approval of the Minister, make regulations to provide for—
(a)

conditions under which model farms may be sold to suitable persons;

(b)

conditions under which model farms may be leased to suitable persons and,
particularly, concerning terms of payment and tenure;

(c)

conditions under which the lease of a model farm may be forfeited or
otherwise determined;

(d)

conditions under which a model farm may be secured by mortgage to the
Corporation;

(e)

the amendment of the First or Second Schedule; and

(f)

any other matter connected with, or incidental to, the purposes of this Act.

### 21.

Protection of members of the Board
No action, suit, prosecution, or other proceedings shall be brought or instituted
personally against a member of the Board in respect of an act done in good faith in
pursuance or execution or intended execution of this Act.

### 22.

Transfer of assets to the Corporation
(1) Upon the date of commencement of this Act—
(a)

the farms mentioned in the Third Schedule and all other property whatsoever
which immediately before that date vested in the Grenada Farms Corporation
(now dissolved) shall vest in the Corporation by virtue of this subsection
without further assurance;

(b)

all rights, claims, privileges, advantages, debts and liabilities or other
obligations to which the Grenada Farms Corporation was entitled or subject,
as the case may be, immediately before that date, shall be deemed to be
transferred, conferred or imposed upon the Corporation by virtue of this
subsection without further assurance.

(2) In this section, the Grenada Farms Corporation means the body corporate by that
name established under section 3 of the Grenada Farms Corporation Act, 1980 (now
repealed).

### 22A.

Minister’s powers to vest lands, etc., in the Corporation
(1) Notwithstanding any provision in this Act or any other law, the Minister, whether
on his or her own initiative or at the instance of the Corporation, may by written
directions to the Corporation, vest in the Corporation any land or farm vested in the
Ministry or may transfer to the Ministry any land or farm vested in the Corporation.
(2) Notwithstanding any provision in this Act or any other law, the Minister may
issue to the Corporation written directions, whether of a special or general nature,
regarding the exercise by the Corporation of any power vested in it by or under this Act.

### 23.

Dissolution of Corporation
(1) If the Minister is satisfied that the Corporation has fulfilled the function for which it
was established he or she may, by Order, direct that the Corporation be dissolved.

(2) On the dissolution of the Corporation its property, and rights or liabilities to which
it is respectively entitled or subject in consequence of the fulfilment of its function, shall
vest in the Government.
(3) The Minister may, by Order, make such provision as appears to him or her to be
necessary or expedient in consequence of, or in connection with, the dissolution of the
Corporation, including provision for modifying the operation of section 14 in the
financial year in which the Corporation is dissolved.

First Schedule
MODEL FARMS CORPORATION ACT
Establishment of the Model Farms Corporation
[Section 3.]

Board of Directors
1\. (1) There shall be a Board of directors of the Corporation (in this Act referred to
as “the Board”) which, subject to the provisions of this Act, shall be responsible for the
policy and the general administration of the affairs of the Corporation.
(2) The Board shall consist of seven directors appointed in writing by the Minister
from persons appearing to the Minister to have ability and experience in management,
economics and agricultural economics, marketing, finance and other relevant fields of
knowledge.
(3) A director who is directly or indirectly interested in a contract made or proposed
to be made by the Corporation—
(a)

shall disclose the nature of his or her interest; and

(b)

shall not take part in any deliberation or decision of the Corporation with
respect to that contract.

Temporary Appointments
2\. The Minister may appoint a person to act temporarily in the place of a director of
the Corporation in case of the absence or inability to act of the director.
Chairperson
3\. (1) The Minister shall appoint one of the directors to be the chairperson of the
Corporation.
(2) The Minister shall appoint one of the directors to be the deputy chairperson.
(3) In case of the absence or inability of the chairperson to act, the deputy chairperson
shall exercise the powers and perform the duties of the chairperson.
Tenure of Office
4\. Subject to the provisions of this Schedule a director shall be appointed to hold
office for a period of two years and shall be eligible for re-appointment at the expiration
of that period.
Resignation
5\. (1) A director other than the chairperson may at any time resign his or her office
by giving notification in writing to that effect transmitted through the chairperson to the
Minister, and from the date of receipt by the Minister of the notification such director
shall cease to hold office as such.

(2) The chairperson may at any time resign his or her office by giving notification in
writing to that effect to the Minister and the resignation shall take effect from the date of
receipt by the Minister of the notification.
Removal of Director
6\. The Minister may at any time revoke the appointment of a director if he or she
considers it expedient to do so.
Publication
7\. The names of every director concerned giving rise to an alteration of the
membership of the Board shall be published in the Gazette.
Authentication of Seal and Documents
8\. The common seal of the Corporation and all documents required by law to be
under seal shall be authenticated by the signatures of—
(a)

the chairperson, or one director authorised to act in that behalf; and

(b)

the General Manager or the secretary or the financial controller.

Meetings
9\. (1) The Board shall meet as frequently as is necessary or expedient for the
transaction of business, and meetings shall be held at such places and times and on such
days as the Board shall determine.
(2) The quorum of the Board shall be four including the chairperson or other person
presiding at the meeting.
(3) The chairperson or other person presiding has a deliberate vote and in the event of
an equality of votes, shall have also a casting vote.
(4) Subject to the provisions of this Schedule, the Board may regulate its own
proceedings.
(5) The validity of any proceeding of the Board shall not be affected by a vacancy
amongst the directors or by any defect in the appointment of a director.
Remuneration of Directors
10\. There shall be paid to the chair man and the directors such remuneration, if any,
whether by way of salaries, travelling or other allowances, as the Minister may determine.

Second Schedule
MODEL FARMS CORPORATION ACT
Committees
[Section 18(2).]

1\. A committee appointed under section 18 shall include at least two members of the
Board and may include three other persons, not being members of the Board, who possess
a basic knowledge of agriculture.
2\. The Board shall appoint the chairperson of a committee and may, at any time,
revoke the appointment if it is satisfied that the chairperson of a committee is unfit to
continue as such or is incapable of performing his or her duties.
3\. A committee shall meet as often as may be necessary or expedient for the
transaction of business, and such meetings shall be held at such times and places and on
such days as the chairperson of a committee shall determine.

4\. A committee shall submit reports to the Board of its activities at regular intervals.
5\. Members of a committee other than public officers shall be paid reasonable out-ofpocket expenses for attending meetings.
6\. Minutes of committee meetings shall be kept in proper form.
7\. Tenure of membership of a committee shall be for a period of twelve months, but a
member shall be eligible for re-appointment at the end of that period.
8\. A committee shall deal with all applications for model farms, and other relevant
matters coming before it, with the utmost dispatch.

MODEL FARMS CORPORATION (DISSOLUTION) ORDER
[SRO 26 of 1998.]

[31st July, 1997.]
1\. Citation
This Order may be cited as the Model Farms Corporation (Dissolution) Order.
2\. Dissolution
It is hereby directed that the Model Farms Corporation shall stand dissolved with
effect from the 31st day of July, 1997.
3\. Liabilities of the Corporation
All liabilities of the Corporation shall be satisfied from funds standing in the name of
the Corporation and the residue of such funds, if any, shall be paid into the Consolidated
Fund.
4\. Status of employees
All employees of the Corporation shall cease to be so employed on the day prior of the
dissolution of the Corporation.
5\. Reversion of land to the Crown
All land vested in the Corporation by or under the Model Farms Act shall revert to the
Crown.
6\. Commencement
This Order came into force on the 31st day of July, 1997.

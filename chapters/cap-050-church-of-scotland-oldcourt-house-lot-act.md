## CHAPTER 50

CHURCH OF SCOTLAND (OLD COURT HOUSE LOT) ACT
An Act to vest certain lands known as the Old Court House Lot in trustees for
the use of the Church of Scotland.
[15th July, 1939.]
1\.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Church of Scotland (Old Court House Lot) Act.

2\.

Old Court House Lot vested in trustees for the use of Church of Scotland

All that lot of land, called or known by the name of the Old Court House Lot situate
in Saint George's is vested in six trustees to be appointed by the Governor-General for
the use of the Church of Scotland for the purpose of the maintenance of the church
erected thereon for the performance of divine service according to the Presbyterian
form of worship.
3\.

Vacancy in trustees to be filled by Governor-General

In the case of any vacancy occurring in the number of trustees by death, absence
from Grenada or otherwise, the same shall in like manner be filled by the GovernorGeneral.

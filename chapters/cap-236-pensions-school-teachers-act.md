## CHAPTER 236

PENSIONS (SCHOOL TEACHERS) ACT
An Act to provide for the payment of pensions to teachers in Government schools
and assisted schools.
[Amended by Act No. 39 of 1978, Act No. 37 of 1982, Act No. 24 of 1983, Act No. 52 of 1991,
Act No. 4 of 1994, Act No. 4 of 1996.]

[1st January, 1943.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Pensions (School Teachers) Act.
2\. Interpretation

In this Act—
“salary” includes house allowance, charge pay, or the estimated value of free
quarters and any other allowance included therein by the Minister;
“school” means Government School or Assisted School within the meaning of the
Education Act, Chapter 86;
“teacher” means a person employed permanently as a teacher in any school.
3\. Pensions to be a charge on Consolidated Fund
All such sums of money as may from time to time be granted by way of pension or
gratuity in accordance with the provisions of this Act or with any regulations made
thereunder shall be charged on and paid out of the Consolidated Fund.
4\. Pension payable on retirement
(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, a teacher—
(a)

who has occupied for not less than ten years the position of a teacher in any
school and who has attained the age of fifty years; or

(b)

who has completed twenty-six years and eight months continuous service as
a teacher in any school, irrespective of his or her age,

may on retirement be granted a pension as provided in this Act.
(2) Subject to the provisions of this Act, a teacher who has served for a period of ten
years or upwards as a teacher in any school and has become, in the opinion of the
Minister, incapable by reason of some infirmity of mind or body of continuing to teach in
a school efficiently may likewise be granted on retirement a pension as provided in this
Act.
(3) Every teacher under the age of fifty years who has completed twenty years
continuous service as a teacher in any school may retire and may be granted a pension as
provided in this Act, but the teacher must give two years written notice to the Public
Service Commission of his or her intention to retire at the expiration of the notice:
Provided that a teacher who retires under this subsection shall be entitled to the
payment of his or her gratuity on his or her retirement but his or her pension shall only
become payable when he or she attains the age of fifty years.
5\. Service to be unbroken
(1) The service in respect of which a pension will be granted shall be unbroken:
Provided that service prior to a break of service may be allowed to count for
pension where the service has been interrupted by abolition of office or other temporary
suspension of employment not arising from misconduct or voluntary resignation of the
teacher:
Provided further that any period during which a teacher absent on leave from
Grenada has, with the consent of the Governor, or of the Secretary of State, served with
Her Majesty’s Armed Forces during the first or second world wars may with the approval
of the Governor-General be counted as service on full pay.
(2) The period during which a teacher has been absent on vacation leave on full pay
shall, in all circumstances, be counted in full towards the qualifying period laid down in
section 4(1) and (2), and any period during which the teacher has been absent on leave on
half pay shall be similarly counted at the rate of one month for every two months of such
period.

(3) Notwithstanding subsection (1) where the Cabinet considers it just and equitable
to do so, the Cabinet may direct that any break in the service of a teacher shall be
disregarded and the whole of his or her service shall be taken as pensionable service
including service after the 4th day of April, 1983.
6\. Amount of pension
Subject to section 18, a teacher who has served ten years or upwards in the service of a
school may be granted a pension at the annual rate of one four-hundred and eightieth of
his or her salary for each complete month of his or her service as a teacher:
Provided that the pension shall not exceed two-thirds of the highest salary drawn by
such teacher during his or her period of service.
7\. Service in other territories
Subject to section 18, a teacher who has served as a teacher in Grenada and who has
served as such for a period of ten years or upwards in a territory of the Caricom States
may, on his or her retirement, be granted a pension in accordance with the provisions of
this Act:
Provided that such pension shall be payable only in respect of the period during
which such teacher actually served as such in Grenada.
8\. Salary to be taken for computation of pension or gratuity
(1) For the purpose of computing the amount of a teacher’s pension or gratuity—
(a)

in the case of a teacher who has held the same appointment for a period of
three years immediately preceding the date of his or her retirement, the full
annual salary enjoyed by him or her at that date in respect of that office shall
be taken;

(b)

in the case of a teacher who at any time during such period of three years has
been transferred from one appointment to another, but whose salary has not
been changed by reason of such transfer or transfers, the full annual salary
enjoyed by him or her at the date of his or her retirement in respect of the
appointment then held by him or her shall be taken;

(c)

in other cases one-third of the aggregate salary enjoyed by the teacher in
respect of his or her service during the three years of his or her service
immediately preceding the date of his or her retirement shall be taken:
Provided that—
(i) if such one-third is less than the highest annual salary enjoyed by him
or her at the date of any transfer within such period of three years that
salary shall be taken, and
(ii) if such one-third is less than the annual salary which would have been
enjoyed by him or her at the date of his or her retirement if he or she
had continued to hold any appointment from which he or she has been
transferred at any time during such period of three years, and had
received all increments which, in the opinion of the Minister, would
have been granted to him or her, the annual salary which would have
been so enjoyed shall be taken.

(2) For the purpose of calculating salary under this section, the teacher shall be
deemed to have served on full salary throughout the said three years.
(3) The estimated value of free quarters in respect of service in Grenada shall not
exceed one-sixth of the actual salary of the teacher.

9\. Gratuity
Every teacher otherwise qualified for a pension, who has not served for a period of ten
years as a teacher may be granted on retirement a gratuity at the rate of half a month’s
salary for each complete six months of service:
Provided that a gratuity may be granted to a female teacher who has completed not
less than five years service as a teacher and who retires for the reason that she has married
or is about to marry, and is not otherwise eligible for a pension or gratuity, on production
by her, within six months after her retirement or such longer period as the Minister may
in any particular case allow, of satisfactory evidence of her marriage, being a gratuity
calculated at the rate of one-twelfth of a month’s salary for each completed month of
service as a teacher or one year’s pensionable emoluments, whichever shall be less:
Provided also that the limitation as to one year’s pensionable emoluments shall not
affect any female teacher appointed under this Act before the 1st January, 1955.
10\. Retirement after age of fifty
The Minister may require any teacher to retire at any time after such teacher attains
the age of fifty years.
11\. No pension on dismissal for misconduct, unless sanctioned
No teacher who, for gross negligence, irregularity or misconduct, is dismissed or
called upon to resign from his or her employment shall be granted a pension or gratuity
under this Act unless the Minister, with the concurrence of the Public Service
Commission, shall otherwise direct.
12\. Reduced pension and gratuity optional
(1) Every teacher who is now or shall hereafter become eligible for a pension under
the provisions of this Act may elect to take on his or her retirement from his or her
employment a reduced pension and gratuity in lieu of the pension provided in this Act.
(2) Every teacher who may hereafter become eligible for a pension shall in writing
notify the Minister of his or her election not later than three months from the date on
which he or she shall become so eligible.
(3) An election once exercised shall be irrevocable.
(4) Reduced pension shall be a pension equal to three-fourths of the amount of
pension which would be payable under this Act, and a gratuity shall be the amount which
one-fourth of the pension payable thereunder when multiplied by twelve and one-half
shall represent.
13\. Pension not assignable, etc., and to cease on bankruptcy, etc.
The provisions of the Pensions Act, Chapter 233, which apply to the non-assignability,
non-attachment, and cessation on bankruptcy or conviction, of pension shall apply to
pensions granted under this Act in like manner as they apply to pensions granted under
that Act.
14\. Pension not of right
(1) No teacher shall have an absolute right to any pension, gratuity or other allowance
under this Act or any regulations made thereunder.
(2) Where it is established to the satisfaction of the Minister that a teacher has been
guilty of negligence, irregularity or misconduct, or has failed to perform his or her duties
efficiently and with fidelity and diligence, the pension, gratuity or other allowance may,

with the concurrence of the Public Service Commission, be reduced or altogether
withheld.
15\. Provision for death gratuity
(1) Where a teacher in the service of a school dies whilst in such service the Minister
may grant to his or her legal personal representatives a gratuity of an amount not
exceeding either the annual salary of his or her office or his or her commuted pension
gratuity, if any, whichever is the greater.
(2) For the purpose of this section “commuted pension gratuity” means the gratuity
which might have been taken under section 12 if the teacher had retired, after serving ten
years or upwards in the service of a school, and had elected to take a reduced pension and
a gratuity.
16\. Regulations
The Minister may make regulations generally for carrying out the provisions of this
Act.
17\. Saving
Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act, any Head Teacher who but for the
passing of this Act would have become eligible for a pension under the Head Teachers’
Pension Act (Chapter 99 of the 1934 Revised Edition) prior to the 1st January, 1947, shall
be eligible for a pension in accordance with the rates prescribed in this Act.
18\. Disqualification
No pension, gratuity or other allowance under this Act shall be granted to any teacher
first appointed as such on or after the 4th April, 1983.

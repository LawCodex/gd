## CHAPTER 316

TELEVISION LICENCE ACT
An Act to impose a requirement on persons having a television set to obtain a licence
annually to own, use or keep the same.
[Act No. 8 of 1982.]

[1st January, 1982.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Television Licence Act.
2\. Licences
(1) Every person having a television set shall apply for and hold a licence issued by
the Comptroller of Inland Revenue to own, use or keep the same and shall pay to the
Comptroller annually a licence fee of twenty-five dollars.
(2) A licence under this Act shall remain valid from the date of issue until the
following 31st December, and shall be in the form prescribed in the Schedule.
3\. Licence not transferable except to certain persons
A licence under this Act shall not be transferable except to the spouse, executor,
administrator, or assignee in bankruptcy, of the person to whom the licence was issued.
4\. Offence
Whosoever being required to hold a licence under this Act fails to do so shall be guilty
of an offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of one thousand dollars.
5\. Non-application of Act
This Act does not apply to any person who—

(a)

is licensed to sell television sets; or

(b)

is a bona fide repairer of television sets.

## CHAPTER 228

PEASANT PROPRIETORS RELIEF ACT
An Act relating to certain contracts made by peasant proprietors.
[21st December, 1908.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Peasant Proprietors Relief Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“contract” means a contract for the payment of any interest, bonus, commission,
penalty or liquidated damages in respect of money lent or to be lent or in respect of
any indebtedness whatever;
“dealer” means a person who is a licensed dealer under the Sale of Produce Act, or
a person to whom cocoa, nutmegs or cotton is delivered for the purpose of being sold
or caused to be sold by such person;
“peasant proprietor” means any person who is owner or occupier of not more than
ten acres of land, and who is an agriculturist, labourer, domestic or menial servant,
artificer, handicraftsman or otherwise engaged in manual labour;
“Registrar” includes both the Registrar and the Deputy Registrar of the Supreme
Court.

### 3.

Formalities necessary to validity of contracts
No contract entered into by a peasant proprietor with a dealer shall be valid either in
law or equity unless the following conditions are complied with, namely—
(a)

the contract shall be in writing;

(b)

the contract shall be attested by a magistrate or the Registrar;

(c)

the magistrate or Registrar attesting the contract shall before the execution
thereof read and explain the contract to the peasant proprietor, and shall
indorse thereon a certificate to the effect that the contract was read and
explained as aforesaid, and that the peasant proprietor understood and agreed
to the terms thereof;

(d)

the contract shall be in duplicate, and one duplicate shall be filed for record
in the office of the magistrate or Registrar:

Provided that this subsection shall not apply to contracts registrable under the Deeds
and Land Registry Act, Chapter 79.

### 4.

Contract not to be attested when not understood by peasant proprietor
The magistrate or Registrar shall refuse to attest the contract unless the peasant
proprietor fully understands and agrees to the terms thereof.

### 5.

Certified copies of contract
It shall be the duty of the magistrate or Registrar, if so requested, to provide the
peasant proprietor with a certified copy of the contract on payment of the appropriate fee.

### 6.

Stamp duty
No stamp duty under the Stamp Act, Chapter 309, shall be payable in respect of any
contract not required to be in writing by any other law than this Act.

### 7.

Fees
The fees to be taken by the magistrate or Registrar under this Act shall be those set out
in the Schedule, or such other fees as the Minister may, from time to time, fix instead of
or in addition thereto. The fees shall be paid in stamps which the magistrate or Registrar
shall cancel.

## CHAPTER 265

PUBLIC LIBRARY ACT
An Act relating to the Public Library in the town of St. George.
[2nd November, 1896.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Public Library Act.

### 2.

Making of rules for the Library
The Minister responsible for education may make rules for the management of and
access to and use of the Public Library in the town of St. George, and for the preservation
and loan of the books, and may, by such rules, delegate the management and control of
the Library wholly or partially to a Committee to be appointed by him or her.

### 3.

Property of the Library and appointment of librarian
The property now appropriated for the purposes of the Public Library, and all property
hereafter acquired for such purposes, shall be vested in the Governor-General; and all
monies now or hereafter owing by way of subscription or fines in respect of the
enjoyment of the advantages of the Library shall be vested in the said Minister; and the
power of appointing and dismissing a librarian shall be vested in the Public Service
Commission.

PUBLIC LIBRARY RULES
[Section 2. Amended by SRO 23 of 1966.]

[1st July, 1950.]
1\. Citation
These Rules may be cited as the Public Library Rules.
2\. Definitions
For the purposes of these Rules—
“Committee” means the Committee appointed by the Minister under the provisions
of the Act;
“Librarian” means the Librarian of the Library;
“Library” means the Grenada Public Library;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for the subject of Social Services.
3\. Control and management of Library
(1) The Committee shall, from time to time, be appointed by the Minister.

(2) Such Committee shall consist of not more than six persons, one of whom shall be
Chairperson. Any three members of the Committee shall form a quorum for the
transaction of any business connected with the Library.
(3) Each member of the Committee shall hold office for a period of two years from
the date of appointment.
(4) The Committee shall meet at least once in every quarter of the year to enquire into
such matters as may be deemed to be concerned with the efficient working of the Library
and to report thereon, with any recommendation, to the Minister.
4\. Time at and manner in which Library may be visited
(1) The Library shall be open on every day of the year except on Sundays and on days
appointed by proclamation to be holidays or declared holidays under the Bank Holidays
Act, Chapter 25.
(2) The hours during which the Library shall be open to the public shall be fixed by
the Minister and published, from time to time, in the Gazette and at least one of the local
newspapers.
(3) All persons, if decently dressed, and not otherwise objectionable, shall have free
access to the Library subject to the conditions hereinafter set out.
5\. Loan of books, etc., from Library
(1) From and after the coming into force of these Rules all books, periodicals and
papers may, subject to these Rules, be obtained from the Library free of subscription
thereto by bona fide residents of the State.
(2) Persons residing in the city of St. George’s may select from the Library any books
required for use, and in the case of their being unable to do so personally, may send to the
Librarian a written authority through a person competent to do so.
(3) Persons residing outside the limits of the city of St. George’s shall be entitled to
have one book only at a time sent to the District Post Office nearest to their residence,
and may return such book to the Library through the Post Office free of any postal
charges.
(4) The parcel containing such book shall be legibly inscribed “Public Library Book”
and shall be franked at the lower left hand corner with the signature of the Librarian, or of
the borrower thereof, as the case may be.
6\. Application for books
(1) The Librarian, before allowing any book, periodical or paper to be taken from the
Library, shall be satisfied that the person applying for such book is a person who has
signed the necessary registration form.
(2) Any person applying for a book on behalf of a borrower shall be required by the
Librarian to produce an order in writing signed by such borrower stating the title of the
book and requesting that such book be delivered to the bearer. The person signing the
order shall be held responsible for the book so delivered.
(3) No book shall be borrowed from the Library by any person who resides in a house
in which a case of typhoid, typhus, smallpox, scarlet fever, or other like infectious or any
contagious disease has occurred, until the Sanitary Authority shall have signified that
such house is free from such infection.
(4) Any person who borrows any book from the Library knowing at the time that he
or she borrowed such book that there existed in the house in which he or she resided a
case typhoid, typhus, smallpox, scarlet fever, or any like infectious or any contagious
disease, shall be guilty of an offence against these Rules.

7\. Access to books, etc.
(1) No person shall take any book, periodical or paper from the Library without the
prior consent of the Librarian.
(2) No person shall be allowed access to the book shelves in the Library outside the
hours fixed by the Minister unless the prior consent of the Librarian has been obtained.
(3) Books may not be changed oftener than once per day.
8\. Period over which books, etc., may be retained
A borrower may retain, free of charge, for a period of fourteen days the books,
periodicals or papers loaned to him or her. A fine of ten cents per day for each book,
periodical or paper retained by him or her for a longer period than fourteen days shall be
paid by such borrower.
9\. Forfeiture of rights
A Borrower contravening the provisions of the preceding rule shall forfeit all rights in
respect of the use of the Library until he or she shall have fully complied with the
provisions of the said rule.
10\. Books of reference
No book of reference shall be taken out of the Library except under special
circumstances, and then only with the prior approval of the Librarian.
11\. Periodicals and papers
Periodicals and papers shall not be taken out of the Library until after the expiration of
fourteen days from the date of their receipt by the Library.
12\. Damage of Library property
Any person damaging, writing in or upon, or losing any book or other property
belonging to the Library, shall pay to the Librarian such sum as, in the opinion of the
Librarian, is sufficient compensation for such damage or loss, and, until such
compensation shall be paid, such person shall forfeit all his or her rights in connection
with the use of the Library.
13\. Use of Library by visitors to State
In the case of visitors to the State requiring the use of the Library, the Librarian shall
require from such visitor the deposit of such sum as may be fixed by the Librarian to
cover the loss of, or damage to, any book borrowed from the Library, and such deposit
shall be refunded to such visitor upon the return by him or her of any book so loaned to
him or her. Alternatively, the Librarian may accept an undertaking in approved form from
a responsible person resident in the State to compensate the Library for any damage or
loss that may be sustained through the loan of a book to a visitor.
14\. Damage or loss to be reported
The Librarian shall, without fail, bring to the notice of the Minister any case of
damage or loss occurring with respect to any book or other property of the Library within
forty-eight hours of the same coming to his or her notice.
15\. Recovery of fines

If after a demand has been made in writing by the Librarian upon any person indebted
to the Library in respect of any fine or for any amount assessed as damage to, or loss of, a
book or other property of the Library, and if such demand is not complied with by such
person before the expiration of seven days of the receipt by him or her of such demand,
the Permanent Secretary (Finance) may, on behalf of the Government, sue for and recover
the amount due.
16\. Preservation of order at Library, etc.
(1) The Librarian shall be responsible to the Minister for the preservation of order in
the Library.
(2) No smoking, loud conversation, or talking likely to interrupt readers shall be
permitted in the Library and the Librarian shall, after first cautioning any offender, take
steps for the immediate removal of any person offending against this rule.
17\. Children’s Library section
(1) Any juvenile desirous of borrowing books from the Children’s section of the
Library shall first furnish an undertaking, in approved form, from his or her parent or
guardian indemnifying the Librarian in the event of damage to, or loss of, any book
borrowed by him or her.
(2) Juveniles between the ages of seven and fourteen years shall be allowed access to
the Children’s section of the Library at all times as fixed by the Minister.
(3) Children up to fourteen years of age shall not be allowed access to the Adult
section of the Library.
18\. Rural Branch Libraries
(1) Rural Branch Libraries shall be under the control of the Minister and shall be
subject to these Rules.
(2) Rural Branch Libraries shall be open to the public at the times fixed by the Minister.
(3) The Chairman of the Committee and the Librarian shall regularly inspect the
Rural Branch Libraries and report on their visits of inspection to the Minister.
(4) New Rural Branch Libraries may be commenced at the discretion of the Minister.
19\. Travelling Libraries
Travelling Libraries shall be under the direct control of the Minister and shall be
subject to these Rules.
20\. Penalty for offences
Any person committing an offence against these Rules shall be liable, on summary
conviction, to a fine not exceeding two hundred and fifty dollars.
21\. Duties of Librarian
(1) The Librarian shall be responsible for the due collection of fines and shall pay all
amounts collected by him or her into the Treasury monthly.
(2) The Librarian shall keep at the Library suitable cards to be called “Suggestion
Cards” on which borrowers of books from, and visitors to, the Library may enter the
names of any works they may recommend to be added to the Library. A book to be called
the “Visitors’ Book” shall also be kept by the Librarian.
(3) The Librarian shall keep the following books of record—

(a)

Abstract of Borrowers (monthly);

(b)

Cash Book.

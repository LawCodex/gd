## CHAPTER 215

NUTMEG INDUSTRY ACT
An Act to provide for a body corporate to safeguard and to promote the interests of
the nutmeg industry and in particular to market nutmegs and to regulate and
control the export of nutmegs.
[Amended by Act No. 8 of 1974, Act No. 15 of 1976, Act No. 29 of 1978, Act No. 43 of 1979, Act
No. 32 of 1981, Act No. 2 of 1982, Act No. 12 of 1984, Act No. 35 of 1991.]

[8th March, 1947.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Nutmeg Industry Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“area” means any parish as defined in the Parish Boundaries Act, Chapter 224;
“area meeting” means a meeting in an area of the members of the Association
resident in that area;
“Association” means the Grenada Co-operative Nutmeg Association by
section 3(1) constituted and established;
“Board” means the Nutmeg Board;
“chairperson” means the chairperson of the Board;
“Manager” means the Manager of the Association;
“member” means a member of the Association;
“nutmeg year” means the period beginning on the first day of July in any one year
and ending on the thirtieth day of June in the following year (both days inclusive);
“nutmegs” means nutmegs whether in shell or separated from the shell or whether
cured or uncured, and includes mace;
“owner” means the owner of nutmegs delivered to the Association and any person
who, being the agent of the owner or having any interest in any nutmegs delivered to
the Association, is authorised to make such delivery, but does not include an employee
or other agent engaged only in the transport of such nutmegs;

“secretary” means the secretary of the Association.

### 3.

The Grenada Co-operative Nutmeg Association
(1) A body corporate called the Grenada Co-operative Nutmeg Association is hereby
constituted and established with power to make contracts and to sue and be sued in that
name and to acquire, hold and dispose of all kinds of real and personal property. In
particular the Association is invested with authority in accordance with the provisions of
this Act for the marketing and control of all nutmegs produced in Grenada and intended for
export.
(2) Subject to the provisions of this Act the Association may—
(a)

process and distil nutmegs and the pericarp; and

(b)

manufacture products from the nutmeg or the pericarp,

and may sell or distribute the processed, distilled or manufactured products.

### 4.

Membership of the Association
(1) Subject to subsection (2), all growers of nutmegs shall be eligible for membership
of the Association.
(2) Where a member of the Association is a corporation or a partnership, the
corporation or partnership shall have the right to nominate a representative to the
Association who (subject to the provisions of this Act) may exercise on behalf of the
corporation or partnership any rights conferred upon a member of the Association
(including membership of the Board) which cannot conveniently be exercised by the
corporation or partnership.

### 5.

Power to appoint attorneys
A member may be represented at all meetings of the Board and of the Association by
an attorney on record duly authorised for that purpose, and such attorney may exercise on
behalf of such member all rights conferred upon the member by this Act in the same
manner as if the member himself or herself were present.

### 6.

Board to control affairs of the Association
(1) The affairs of the Association shall be managed and controlled by a Board to be
called the Nutmeg Board which shall be the governing and executive body of the
Association and which shall consist of not less than seven nor more than nine persons.
(2) The functions of the Board shall be to—
(a)

regulate and control the export of nutmegs;

(b)

promote, protect and develop the nutmeg industry;

(c)

consider and advise the Minister in regard to all matters affecting the
industry as may be referred to the Board by Government, or which the Board
considers should be submitted to Government, or which are referred to the
Board by the Association;

(d)

expend and account for all such monies as may be collected by a cess on the
industry, or which may be placed at the disposal of the Board by vote of the
House of Representatives, for the purpose of research, marketing or other
service to the industry.

### 7.

Association to have head office, official seal and warehouses

The Association shall have a head office in the town of St. George and an official seal,
and may own or rent warehouses in the said town and such other places within Grenada as
it may deem necessary for the efficient administration of the affairs of the Association.

### 8.

Provision for use of seal
The seal of the Association shall not be affixed to any instrument except by the
authority of a resolution of the Board, and in the presence of at least two members of the
Board and of the secretary who shall sign every instrument to which the seal of the
Association is so affixed in their presence.

### 9.

Applicant for membership may be required to furnish evidence of qualification;
new members to be given identification card
(1) Upon application made by any person to the secretary for membership of the
Association the applicant may be required to furnish such evidence of his or her
qualification for membership as may reasonably be demanded by the Board.
(2) Upon acceptance of an application for membership the secretary shall issue to the
new member an identification card and shall enter his or her name in the register of
members of the Association.

### 10.

Right of appeal of applicant
A person whose application for membership has been rejected by the Board may,
within fourteen days thereafter, appeal to the magistrate of the district in which the person
resides, and if he or she shall satisfy such magistrate of his or her eligibility for
membership of the Association the magistrate shall make an order directed to the
chairperson requiring such person upon his or her complying with the regulations
controlling the admission to membership of eligible persons generally to be forthwith
admitted a member of the Association.

### 11.

Notice of hearing to be given to secretary
Notice of the hearing of such application to the magistrate shall be served upon the
secretary not less than seven clear days before the date fixed for the hearing.

### 12.

Chairperson liable to penalty for failing to obey order of magistrate
The chairperson shall be liable to a penalty of twenty-five dollars for each day after
the day of receipt by him or her of the order of the magistrate during which he or she shall
fail to admit the applicant to membership of the Association:
Provided that the applicant shall have complied with all conditions precedent with
respect to the admission of eligible persons to membership of the Association.

### 13.

Magistrate’s decision to be final
The decision of the magistrate under section 12 shall be final:
Provided that the applicant shall be entitled to submit a fresh application for admission
to membership to the Board after the expiration of six months from the date of the
magistrate’s decision.

### 14.

Election and nomination of Board
(1) In the month of May in each year on a day to be fixed by the Board and published
in the Gazette at least one month before such day, six members of the Association shall
be elected by ballot to be members of the Board for the ensuing nutmeg year.

(2) The election provided for in subsection (1) shall be carried out in the manner
prescribed by sections 27 and 28.
(3) The names of the members elected to the Board shall forthwith be submitted to the
Minister who shall, within fourteen days of such election, nominate a public officer to
serve on the Board. The Minister may also, at any time, nominate not more than two other
persons to serve on the Board.
(4) All members of the Board shall be eligible for re-election or re-nomination, as the
case may be.

### 15.

On failure to elect members of the Board Minister to fill vacancies
by nomination
If no election for the proper constitution of the Board shall take place, or if no person
or an insufficient number of persons shall offer themselves for election to the Board or be
elected thereto, the Minister may nominate the members whose election has hereinbefore
been provided for, or as many of such members as shall with any members elected by the
Association make up the number required to be elected.

### 16.

Proceedings of Board not invalidated by vacancy in its body
No act or proceeding of the Board shall be invalidated on account of any vacancy in
its body.

### 17.

Minister may alter constitution of Board
If for any cause it shall be deemed expedient the Minister may, with the consent of the
House of Representatives, declare by Order that the constitution of the Board shall be
altered either by increasing or reducing the number of the members thereof:
Provided that any such alteration shall not affect the relative proportions of elected
and nominated members constituting the Board.

### 18.

Disqualification from membership of Board
A person shall be disqualified from becoming and from being a member of the Board
if and while he—
(a)

is bankrupt; or

(b)

holds any office of profit in the gift or disposal of the Board; or

(c)

has directly or indirectly any share or interest in any contract or employment
with, by, or on behalf of the Association:
Provided that no person shall be so disqualified or be deemed to have any
share or interest in any such contract or employment by reason only of his or
her being the owner of, or having any share or interest in—
(i) any nutmegs subject to the control of the Association,
(ii) any newspaper in which any advertisement relating to the affairs of
the Association is published,
(iii) any company with which the Association contracts for insuring
against fire, marine or other risk, any property in which the
Association shall have an insurable interest, or
(iv) any company which has entered into contracts with or done any work
for the Association:

Provided also that no member of the Board shall be disqualified if, being otherwise
subject to disqualification, it is found by a majority of the other members of the Board

that the interest of the member in any contract about to be entered into by the Association
has been fully disclosed to the Board and that such contract will be beneficial to the
Association and the making of such has been approved by the Minister:
Provided, nevertheless, that for the purposes of paragraph (b) the office of chairperson
shall not be an office of profit within the gift or disposal of the Board.

### 19.

Member of Board ceases to be such on absence from meetings of Board or from
Grenada without leave
A member of the Board shall ipso facto cease to be a member thereof if he—
(a)

is absent from three consecutive meetings of the Board without leave from
the Board; or

(b)

is absent from Grenada for more than one month without such leave.

### 20.

Voting by show of hands
On a show of hands every member present in person shall have one vote only.

### 21.

Mode of voting at general meetings
The voting on all matters to be decided by the votes of members at general meetings
shall be by show of hands, except in the case of the election of members of the Board and
of delegate members at area meetings, which shall be by ballot:
Provided that any illiterate member voting by ballot shall do so through an official
attestor, and the Association shall ensure the presence of an official attestor at all such
meetings.

### 22.

Register to be compiled annually and kept by the secretary
Subject to regulations made under this Act with respect to any procedure for the writing
up of any register of members, the secretary shall, in the month of March in each year,
compile a register of the names in alphabetical order of all members and shall indicate,
against the name of every member who is entitled to more than one vote, the number of
votes to which that member is entitled. The register shall be signed by the chairperson and
kept in the custody of the secretary. A copy of the register shall be kept on display in the
office of the Association so that all persons wishing to inspect the register may readily do so.

### 23.

Register to be revised annually, etc.
(1) Subject to subsection (2), on or before the thirtieth day of April in each year the
chairperson and two members of the Board nominated by the chairperson (in this section
referred to as “the panel”) shall meet at the office of the Association and there revise the
register as follows—
(a)

by deleting the names of those members whom the panel have reasonable
cause to believe have died or ceased to be qualified to be members;

(b)

by inserting the names of those members who are qualified to be included in
the register as members;

(c)

by checking, and correcting where necessary, the number of votes to which
members are recorded in the register as being entitled:

Provided that the number of votes recorded shall not be reduced unless each member
concerned has been notified of the intention of the panel to do so and has been given a
reasonable opportunity to make representations to the panel in that regard.

(2) Notice of the date and time appointed for the revision of the register shall be
displayed with the copy of the register in the manner provided by section 22.
(3) For the purposes of the revision of the register the panel may call witnesses and
hear evidence.

### 24.

Use of register, etc.
After the completion of the revision, the register as revised shall be the register that is
used at all area meetings of the Association and for the purpose of ascertaining the number
of votes cast by members on matters decided by vote. A person whose name does not
appear in the register shall not be entitled to vote. A member whose name appears in the
register may cast one vote or, if entitled to more than one vote, no more than such number
of votes as is indicated in the register against his or her name.

### 25.

Compulsory general meetings of Association
There shall be at least two general meetings of the Association held in each nutmeg
year, one of which shall be held in the month of May at which members of the Board
shall be elected and the other on such date, not less than three nor more than nine months
before the date of the meeting held in the month of May, as shall be fixed by the Board.
At such meetings the Board shall present a full report of the affairs of the Association. At
least seven clear days before the date fixed for the aforesaid general meetings of the
Association in each nutmeg year, the Board shall provide each member of the Association
with a statement setting out, simply and clearly, the financial position of the Association
and containing reasonable details of its assets and liabilities.

### 26.

Other general meetings of Association
Other general meetings of the Association may be convened by the Board and shall be
convened upon the request in writing of any number of members in excess of one-fifth of
the total number of members or of twenty-five members, whichever shall be less, of the
Association appearing on the last register of members.

### 27.

Area meetings
(1) Area meetings of the members of the Association resident in any area of Grenada,
at which all members of the Association resident in that area may attend, shall be held in
such area. At least fourteen days before the date of every general meeting of the
Association, the Board shall summon area meetings in each area by notice published in
the Gazette and posted upon the door at the main entrance to every police station in each
area. The agenda of the general meeting shall be published with the notice convening
every such area meeting.
(2) The chairperson of an area meeting shall be nominated by the Board from among
the members of the Board resident in the area and if there be no member of the Board so
resident the Board shall nominate another member of the Association to be chairperson of
the area meeting. The secretary or other person nominated by the Board shall attend all
area meetings and keep minutes of the proceedings of such meetings. A copy of the
agenda of the general meeting of the Association together with a copy of the register of
members shall be forwarded by the secretary to the chairperson of the area meeting.
(3) It shall be the duty of the chairperson of an area meeting to read to the meeting the
agenda received from the Board, to initiate and control discussion of each item on the
agenda and to cause members present at such meeting to elect delegate members to the
general meetings of the Association. One delegate member shall be elected for every
hundred members shown on the register as resident in the area, except that when the
residue of the number of members is less than one hundred but more than fifty one
additional delegate shall be elected to represent such members. Twenty-five members

shall form a quorum at an area meeting. The chairperson of an area meeting shall within
three days of the meeting forward a report of the meeting to the Board.
(4) At the election of delegate members at area meetings each member shall be
entitled to one vote. In addition thereto any member who shall have delivered to the
Association more than twenty thousand pounds of cracked nutmegs or a quantity of
nutmegs estimated to be equivalent in quantity to more than twenty thousand pounds
when cracked, during the preceding nutmeg year shall have an extra vote in respect of
every complete twenty thousand pounds of nutmegs after the first twenty thousand
pounds so delivered by him or her to the Association:
Provided that no member shall have more than five votes.
(5) For the purposes of this section, a member shall be deemed to be resident in the
area in which he or she grows nutmegs:
Provided that where a member grows nutmegs in more than one area he or she shall,
in writing to the secretary, elect the area in which he or she will attend area meetings and
he or she shall not be eligible to attend area meetings in any other area.

### 28.

Voting at general meetings of Association
Voting at general meetings of the Association shall be confined to delegate members
elected in accordance with the provisions of section 27. Each delegate member at a
general meeting shall be entitled to one vote. Twenty of such delegate members shall
form a quorum.

### 29.

Provision for convening general meeting if Board refuses
If the Board shall refuse to convene a general meeting of the Association upon the
request in writing of the necessary number of members, or (although not refusing) shall
neglect to convene such meeting for fifteen days after receipt of such request in writing, a
general meeting of the Association may be convened by the members making such
request, or by a like number of members who shall together be entitled to a number of
votes at least equal to one-third of the aggregate number of votes to which all members of
the Association appearing on the last register of members shall be entitled.

### 30.

Expulsion of member of Board
If any member of the Board, other than a public officer nominated to the Board by the
Minister, refuses to obey and conform to the rules of the Board it shall be lawful for the
Board after affording such member a full opportunity of explaining his or her case, by
resolution passed by the affirmative votes of not less than five members of the Board, to
expel such member and to declare his or her seat vacant, and the seat of such member
shall ipso facto become vacant accordingly.

### 31.

Resignation by member of Board and by chairperson
Any member of the Board may, by notice in writing to the chairperson, resign his or
her office. The chairperson may resign his or her chairpersonship by notice in writing to
the Board.

### 32.

Secretary to publish any vacancy in Board
A notification of the fact and circumstances of any vacancy in the Board shall be
published by the secretary in the Gazette and, in the case of a nominated member, notified
to the Minister with all dispatch.

### 33.

Filling of vacancy in Board

Any vacancy in the Board occurring by death, resignation, disqualification or
otherwise shall be filled, in the case of an elected member, by the Board within fourteen
days or in the case of a nominated member, by the Minister at his or her discretion:
Provided that if any such vacancy shall leave the Board without a public officer, the
Minister shall forthwith nominate such an officer for membership of the Board. The
member or other person so elected or nominated shall retain his or her seat so long only
as the vacating member would have retained the same if no vacancy had occurred.

### 34.

Dissolution of Board
If the Board, in the judgement of the Minister, persistently makes default in the
performance of the duties by this Act or by any other law of Grenada imposed upon it, or
exceeds or abuses its powers, it shall be lawful for the Minister, by Order, to dissolve the
Board.

### 35.

Consequences of dissolution
(1) In the case of the dissolution of the Board as aforesaid, the following
consequences shall ensue, namely—
(a)

all members of the Board shall, from the date of such order, vacate their
offices;

(b)

all the powers and duties of the Board shall, until the constitution of a new
Board under this Act, be exercised by such person or persons as the Minister
may appoint in that behalf and any payment made to such person or persons
for his or her or their services shall be a charge upon the funds of the
Association hereinafter mentioned;

(c)

any contracts or other obligations lawfully entered into by the Board before
its dissolution shall be carried out, as far as possible, by such person or
persons aforesaid who shall, for such purposes, be deemed to be the agents
of the Association.

(2) No Order for dissolution as aforesaid shall be valid unless it shall be made after
the thirty-first day of March in any nutmeg year or, if made at any other time, unless in
such Order provision is made for the constitution under this Act of a new Board in lieu of
the Board so dissolved within a period not exceeding two months from the date of the
Order.
(3) If such Order for dissolution shall be made after the thirty-first day of March the
person or persons appointed by the Minister shall perform the duties of the Board until
the end of the nutmeg year which shall expire on the thirtieth day of June next after the
appointment when the new Board to be constituted as provided in subsection (2) shall
come into being.

### 36.

Appointment of chairperson
The chairperson, who shall be a member of the Association, shall be elected by a
majority of the votes of the Board at the first Board meeting after its establishment and
shall remain in office until the appointment of his or her successor unless he or she shall
previously resign or be or become disqualified for membership of the Board. If the
chairperson, elected as aforesaid, shall for any cause whatsoever cease to be the
chairperson of the Board during the period for which he or she shall have been elected,
the Board shall proceed to elect another member of the Board to be chairperson who,
when elected, shall hold office for the remainder of the time for which his or her
predecessor would have held office if he or she had not ceased to be chairperson.

### 37.

Chairperson to be President of the Association

The chairperson shall be ex officio President of the Association and shall receive such
salary as may be determined by the Board with the approval of the Minister to be paid
from the funds of the Association.

### 38.

Retiring chairperson to be eligible for re-election
A retiring chairperson shall be eligible for re-election.

### 39.

Duties of chairperson
The chairperson shall preside at every meeting of the Board and at every general
meeting of the Association and shall have such powers and perform such other duties as
may, from time to time, be assigned to him or her by rules made by the Board.

### 40.

Chairperson to have casting vote
The chairperson shall have an original vote and in case of an equal division of votes
on any question before the Board or the Association a second or casting vote.

### 41.

Deputy chairperson
The Board may from time to time appoint any member thereof to act as deputy
chairperson during the illness or absence of the chairperson or during any vacancy in the
office of chairperson.
A deputy chairperson shall, while acting as such, be invested with all the authority and
perform all the duties, of the chairperson.

### 42.

Meetings of the Board
A meeting of the Board shall be held within the first fifteen days of the month of July
in each year and thereafter the Board shall meet at such times as shall be fixed by rules
made by the Board:
Provided that no period exceeding two months shall elapse between any two meetings
of the Board.

### 43.

Convening of special meetings of Board
If any three members of the Board or any number of members in excess of one-fifth of
the total number of members or of twenty-five members (whichever shall be less) of the
Association appearing on the last register shall present to the chairperson a request in
writing that a meeting of the Board be convened for the consideration of some particular
matter, it shall be the duty of the chairperson to have a meeting of the Board convened
accordingly without delay.

### 44.

Members may convene special meeting
If after request as provided by the immediately preceding section the chairperson
refuses to convene a meeting of the Board or if (although not refusing) he or she does not
within seven days after the receipt of such request in writing, convene a meeting of the
Board any three members of the Board or the number of members of the Association as
aforesaid may forthwith convene a meeting of the Board.

### 45.

Power of adjournment
The Board may adjourn its meetings from time to time.

### 46.

Quorum

Any four members of the Board shall form a quorum for the transaction of business.

### 47.

Questions before Board to be decided by majority of votes
Every question before the Board shall be decided by a majority of the votes of
members of the Board present. Every member of the Board, other than the chairperson,
shall have one vote only.

### 48.

Non-participation of member with pecuniary interest
No member of the Board shall vote upon, or take part in, the decision of any matter
before the Board in which he or she has directly or indirectly any pecuniary interest.

### 49.

Minutes to be received in evidence
The minutes of the proceedings at a meeting of the Board or of a committee thereof
signed at the same or the next ensuing meeting by the chairperson or by a member of the
committee, describing himself or herself as or appearing to be chairperson thereof, shall
be received in evidence without further proof in all courts of Justice and for all other
purposes whatsoever.

### 50.

Presumption of regularity
Until the contrary has been proved, every meeting of the Board or of a committee
thereof, in respect of proceedings whereof minutes have been so made, shall be deemed
to have been duly convened and held, and all members present at the meeting shall be
deemed to have been duly qualified; and where the proceedings are those of a committee,
the committee shall be deemed to have been duly constituted and to have had power to
deal with the matter referred to in the minutes.

### 51.

Power of Board to make rules
(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Board may from time to time make rules
relating to all or any of the following matters, namely—
(a)

the time and place of its meetings and of the general meetings of the
Association;

(b)

the mode of convening its meetings and the general meetings of the
Association;

(c)

the attendance of its members;

(d)

the form and order of its debates and of the debates at the general meetings
of the Association;

(e)

the powers and duties of its officers and employees; and

(f)

the general regulation of proceedings of the Board and of committees thereof
and of the Association, and the mode of conducting the business of the
Board and of the Association, and of area meetings.

(2) No such rules shall be made, or when made, altered or revoked, unless special
notice of such intended making, alteration or revocation has been given at a meeting of
the Board held at least seven days before that at which such making, alteration or
revocation is proposed, and unless special notice as aforesaid has also been transmitted to
the members of the Board at least three days before such meeting.
(3) The making, alteration or revocation of rules which relate to the meetings of the
Association or purport to regulate the proceedings of the Association shall not be binding
upon the Association until the same have been confirmed by a general meeting of the
Association held not less than fourteen days after the making, alteration or revocation of

such rules by the Board and then only if special notice of the making, alteration or
revocation of such rules shall have been given in the notice convening the general
meeting or to the members at least three clear days before the day of the meeting.

### 52.

Association to be sole purchaser of nutmegs
(1) It shall not be lawful for any person to purchase nutmegs in Grenada except for
domestic use or, under permit from the Board, for other approved purposes.
(2) Except as aforesaid, any person who buys nutmegs and any person who sells
nutmegs to any person except for domestic use by the purchaser, or under permit by the
Board for other approved purposes, shall be guilty of an offence.

### 53.

Sale of nutmegs abroad
Notwithstanding the provisions of section 54, the Board may engage the services of
any person upon such terms as may be agreed upon between the Board and such person
for the purpose of effecting the sale of nutmegs abroad.

### 54.

Association to be sole exporter of nutmegs
(1) The Association shall be the sole exporter of nutmegs.
(2) If any person shall export nutmegs in contravention of the provisions of this Act he
or she shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of ten thousand dollars, and on a
subsequent conviction to a fine of fifteen thousand dollars and to imprisonment for six
months.
(3) The Association shall, not later than the fifteenth day of January and the fifteenth
day of July in each year, pay into the Treasury a licence fee of one thousand two hundred
dollars for carrying on the business of sole exporter of nutmegs. Such fee shall be paid
into the Consolidated Fund. Failure to comply with the foregoing provision of this
subsection shall be an offence punishable on summary conviction with a fine of five
hundred dollars, and where at the time of conviction the licence fee has not been paid, the
magistrate shall order the payment into court, in addition to a fine, of an amount equal to
the fee payable for the licence neglected to be taken out, and upon payment of such
licence fee the magistrate shall notify the Permanent Secretary (Finance) thereof.

### 55.

Board may engage agents
Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act the Board may, by contract, engage
agents to carry out on its behalf any of its duties in connection with the collection,
storage, processing and packing of nutmegs, and for payment on its behalf of any monies
as advances or otherwise.

### 56.

Board may appoint representatives in markets abroad
Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act the Board may, by contract, appoint
representatives in markets abroad.

### 57.

Advances against nutmegs delivered
The Association shall advance to its members and other persons who shall deliver
nutmegs to the Association such proportion of the value of the nutmegs delivered as shall,
from time to time, be determined by the Board. More than one advance may be made at
the discretion of the Board in respect of deliveries of nutmegs to the Association during
any nutmeg year.

### 58.

Board to fix value of nutmegs

The Board shall as frequently as it may deem necessary and at least once in each
fortnight, determine and fix the values of nutmegs for the purposes of this Act and the
respective proportions of such values which may be advanced by the Association to
persons delivering nutmegs to the Association. The values of nutmegs and the proportions
of such values to be advanced, as determined and fixed by the Board, shall be published
in the Gazette and shall be conclusive.

### 59.

Nutmegs delivered to become the property of the Association
(1) All nutmegs delivered to the Association shall become the property of the
Association, and the rights of the owner and of all other persons claiming through, under
or against the owner shall be none other than a right to receive such sums, if any, as shall
be payable by the Association under this Act to the persons entitled to receive the same.
(2) Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (1), no delivery of nutmegs to the
Association by members or other persons shall be deemed to be a sale of licensable
produce within the meaning of the Sale of Produce Act, Chapter 296.
(3) Every member and person representing a member making a delivery of nutmegs
to the Association shall produce the member’s identification card and nutmeg producer’s
book, in which shall be entered by the Association the particulars and nature of the
transaction.
(4) Such book shall be issued by the Permanent Secretary (Finance) to any applicant
who pays the prescribed fee of twenty-five cents and presents to the Permanent Secretary
(Finance) a statement signed by him or her and containing—
(a)

the description, area and situation of the applicant’s nutmeg cultivation; and

(b)

the estimated annual production thereof.

(5) The particulars aforesaid shall be entered by the Permanent Secretary (Finance) on
the inside cover of the nutmeg producer’s book.
(6) Every nutmeg producer’s book shall be in the form specified in the Schedule, and
a register of such books shall be kept by the Permanent Secretary (Finance) in such form
as he or she may think proper. No new book shall be supplied to any nutmeg producer
unless the existing book is returned to the Permanent Secretary (Finance) for cancellation,
or unless the producer has—
(a)

presented to the Permanent Secretary (Finance) a statement signed by him or
her of the fact that such book has been lost or destroyed; or

(b)

has otherwise satisfied the Permanent Secretary (Finance) that such is the
case; and

(c)

has paid a replacement fee of fifty cents for the new book.

(7) Any officer of the Association or police officer or person appointed by the
Permanent Secretary (Finance) may demand the production of a nutmeg producer’s book
and may inspect such book and make a copy of any entry therein and if he or she thinks it
necessary may retain such book.
(8) Any person who fails or neglects to produce such book when lawfully required to
do so or makes use of any nutmeg producer’s book not his or her own, or not issued by
the Permanent Secretary (Finance), or which has been cancelled by the Permanent
Secretary (Finance), or refuses to allow a copy of any entry in such book to be taken shall
be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of one thousand dollars, and in default of
payment thereof, to imprisonment for three months.
(9) A person who on application for a new book under the provisions of
subsection (6) signs a false statement shall be guilty of an offence.

### 60.

Power of Permanent Secretary (Finance) to require verification of particulars

(1) Whenever the Permanent Secretary (Finance) has reason to suspect that any of the
particulars contained in the statement of an applicant for a nutmeg producer’s book are
not reasonably accurate he or she may, by writing under his or her hand, call upon the
maker of the statement to deliver up his or her nutmeg producer’s book within a stated
period to the person specified in the writing.
(2) The Permanent Secretary (Finance) may, by writing under his or her hand, require
the Chief Technical Officer (Extension) to authorise an agricultural officer to enter and
inspect the lands of any person who has been called upon to deliver up his or her nutmeg
producer’s book.
(3) It shall be lawful for any agricultural officer thus required by the Chief Technical
Officer (Extension), to enter at any reasonable time of the day upon any lands specified
by the Permanent Secretary (Finance) for the purpose of ascertaining or verifying the
particulars mentioned in section 59(4).
(4) If the Permanent Secretary (Finance) is satisfied that the aforementioned
particulars relating to any land are reasonably accurate, he or she shall forthwith return
the nutmeg producer’s book delivered up as aforesaid. If the Permanent Secretary
(Finance) having considered any representations made to him or her by or on behalf of
the producer is not so satisfied he or she shall retain the book in his or her possession and
cancel the same and shall so inform the person whose book has been retained and
cancelled.
(5) A new nutmeg producer’s book shall not be issued to any person whose book has
been retained and cancelled unless such person makes a new application to the Permanent
Secretary (Finance) and satisfies him or her that the particulars therein are reasonably
accurate, and pays the prescribed fee of twenty-five cents.
(6) A person commits an offence who—
(a)

fails to deliver up his or her nutmeg producer’s book after being duly
required so to do by the Permanent Secretary (Finance); or

(b)

obstructs or hinders any agricultural officer in the proper execution of his or
her duties under this Act.

### 61.

Sale of nutmegs not grown on seller’s land prohibited
Any person who delivers or offers for delivery nutmegs other than those grown on the
lands described in his or her nutmeg producer’s book, shall be guilty of an offence and
liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of five thousand dollars and to imprisonment for
twelve months.

### 62.

Producer to report change of acreage
(1) Where there is any increase or decrease by sale, purchase, lease, devolution or
otherwise in the area and nature of the producer’s cultivation in nutmegs he or she shall,
within twenty-eight days of the change, apply to the Permanent Secretary (Finance) to
amend the statement upon which his or her producer’s book was issued, whereupon the
particulars recorded in the inside cover of his or her producer’s book shall be amended
accordingly.
(2) Where a producer disposes of all his or her land on which nutmegs are cultivated
he or she shall, within twenty-eight days of the disposal, return his or her producer’s book
to the Permanent Secretary (Finance) for cancellation.
(3) Any producer who fails or neglects to comply with the provisions of this section
shall be guilty of an offence.

### 63.

Nutmegs to be pooled

(1) All nutmegs delivered to the Association under the provisions of this Act shall be
prepared for export and pooled by the Association and sold and the Association shall
keep an account of all such nutmegs.
(2) At the end of each nutmeg year all nutmegs remaining unsold shall be valued at
the cost thereof to the Association:
Provided that allowance shall be made at the end of each nutmeg year for any nutmegs
that may be regarded as unsaleable or that may have depreciated to a value below that of
the cost to the Association; such allowance shall be at the sole discretion of the Board and
is to be a charge against the revenues of the Association for the nutmeg year concerned.
(3) The Board shall ascertain the amount of the surplus or deficit on the transactions
of the Association in each nutmeg year, and in arriving at such surplus or deficit, the
unsold nutmegs of each previous nutmeg year shall be brought into account at the estimated
value placed thereon at the end of such year, and the total sums advanced for nutmegs and
all other charges and expenses allowed and certified by the auditors of the Association to
be necessary for the proper conduct of the affairs of the Association shall be set against
the sum represented by the revenue of the Association derived from the sale of nutmegs
and from any other source and the estimated value of unsold nutmegs.
(4) Any surplus or deficit in respect of any nutmeg year (ascertained in accordance
with subsections (2) and (3) of this section) shall be added to the total sums advanced by
the Association to members and other persons in respect of deliveries of nutmegs during
such period and the total sum arrived at in the manner aforesaid shall be allocated to
members and other persons who shall have delivered nutmegs to the Association in
accordance with the provisions of this Act in proportion to the quantities delivered by
them respectively during such nutmeg year.
(5) From the amount allocated to the account of a member or other person in
accordance with subsection (4) there shall be deducted such sums as may be due by him
or her to the Association for advances made in accordance with the provisions of section
57, also for any charge payable by him or her to the Association, and the balance
remaining to the credit of or owing by such member or other person as the case may be
shall be paid to or recovered from such member or other person as shall then appear to the
Board to be entitled thereto or liable therefor.
(6) In the case of a debt being due to the Association by such member or other person
under the provisions of subsection (5), immediate payment of such debt may be
determined by the Board or arrangements may be entered into for the payment thereof
over such period as the Board may determine with or without interest.

### 64.

Appointment of committees of Board
(1) The Board may appoint from among its members such and so many committees
consisting of such number of persons as it may think fit for the exercise of any powers of
an administrative character which in the opinion of the Board can be properly exercised
by committees, and the acts of any such committee shall be reported and submitted to the
Board for its approval.
(2) Nothing contained in this section shall authorise the Board to delegate to a
committee the power of fixing a toll to be levied upon any nutmegs exported.

### 65.

Acts of committee not invalidated by vacancy in its body
No act or proceeding of a committee shall be invalidated on account of any vacancy in
its body.

### 66.

Rules regulating meetings of Board to apply to meetings of committee

Any rules made for the regulation of meetings of the Board shall, subject to any
special rules for committee meetings made by the Board, apply as far as possible to
meetings of a committee.

### 67.

Member of committee not to vote upon any matter in which he or she has
pecuniary interest
No member of a committee shall vote upon, or take part in, the discussion of any matter
in which he or she has directly or indirectly any pecuniary interest.

### 68.

Public officer on Board to be member of finance committee
If the Board shall appoint a finance committee the public officer nominated to the
Board by the Minister shall be a member of such committee, and if there be more than
one such officer on the Board the public officer first appointed or first named in the
nominations by the Minister shall be a member of such committee:
Provided that the Minister may signify his or her desire that such officer shall not be a
member of such committee or that some other public officer on the Board shall be a
member of the committee in lieu of the officer first nominated to the Board or first named
whereupon the Board shall conform to the desire of the Minister.

### 69.

Board may appoint Manager
(1) The Board may appoint a fit and proper person to be Manager at such salary and
upon such terms as to his or her duties and functions and on such other conditions as the
Minister may approve.
(2) The Manager shall be the chief executive officer of the Association and shall have
all such powers and perform all such duties as may be entrusted to him or her under
subsection (1). He or she shall attend all meetings of the Association and, if required by
the chairperson so to do, the meetings of the Board and of every committee of the Board.

### 70.

Board may appoint Assistant Manager
(1) The Board may appoint a fit and proper person to be Assistant Manager upon such
terms as to his or her duties and functions as the Minister may approve.
(2) The Assistant Manager shall exercise and perform the functions and duties
respectively of the Manager at all times when—
(a)

the office of Manager is vacant; and

(b)

the Manager is absent or prevented by reason of illness or other cause from
doing so.

### 71.

Board to appoint secretary of Association subject to approval of Minister
(1) The Board shall from time to time subject to the approval of the Minister appoint
some fit and proper person to be secretary of the Association and such person shall hold
office during the pleasure of the Minister and of the Board.
(2) Notwithstanding the dissolution or other determination of the Board, the secretary
shall continue to hold office until his or her employment shall be determined by the Board
at its own pleasure or in pursuance of a request in that behalf made by the Minister.

### 72.

Board to determine salary of secretary with approval of Minister
The secretary shall receive such salary, as may be determined by the Board with the
approval of the Minister, to be paid from the funds of the Association.

### 73.

When secretary chief executive officer of Association
Whenever the office of Manager and the office of Assistant Manager are both vacant,
the secretary shall be the chief executive officer of the Association. He or she shall have
all such powers and perform all such duties as are or may be conferred upon him or her
by this or any other Act or by any direction or order of the Board. He or she shall attend
the meetings of the Association and of the Board and of every committee of the Board
and shall keep minutes of the proceedings of every such meeting.

### 74.

Secretary to be treasurer of Association
The secretary shall be ex officio treasurer of the Association.

### 75.

Appointment of assistant secretary
The Board may, with the consent of the Minister, appoint some fit and proper person
to be assistant secretary who shall hold office during the pleasure of the Board and of the
Minister, and the Board shall, from time to time, define the duties of the assistant secretary.

### 76.

Assistant secretary to have like powers as secretary
All things required or authorised by law or by any regulation or order of the Board to
be done by or to the secretary may be done by or to the assistant secretary.

### 77.

Provision for remuneration of assistant secretary
The assistant secretary, while acting for the secretary, may receive such remuneration
to be drawn from the salary of the secretary or be paid from the funds of the Association
as the Board shall determine.

### 78.

Board may appoint officers and employees
The Board may from time to time appoint such officers and employees as may be
required for the efficient administration of the affairs of the Association and every such
officer or employee shall receive such salary and shall perform such duties as may from
time to time be assigned to him or her by the Board, and shall hold office during the
pleasure of the Board.

### 79.

Officers and employees may be required to furnish security
The Board may require any officer or employee appointed under this Act to give such
security as may be thought proper for the true and faithful performance of the duties of
his or her office, and for the due accounting for, paying over and delivery of all monies,
goods and chattels which may come into his or her hands by virtue of such office.

### 80.

Officers and employees to be appointed on a monthly contract
No officer or employee of the Association shall be entitled to claim more than one
month’s notice of termination of his or her services and the Board shall have no power to
make any agreement for service involving a longer period of notice or any claim for
pension or compassionate allowance except the same shall be authorised by the Minister
with the approval of the House of Representatives.

### 81.

Officers or employees having pecuniary interest to report fact to Board
If the Manager or the secretary or any other officer or any employee of the
Association appointed by the Board shall, directly or indirectly, have any pecuniary
interest in any agreement or contract which shall be entered into by the Association, or

any matter affecting the Association, such Manager, secretary, officer or employee shall
report the circumstances of such pecuniary interest to the Board through the chairperson
as soon as practicable after he or she becomes aware of his or her having such interest,
and in default of his or her so reporting, he or she shall be liable to summary dismissal
from the service of the Association.

### 82.

Officers and employees to account
Every officer or employee appointed by the Board shall give at such times during the
continuance of his or her office, or within ten days after his or her ceasing to hold it, and
in such manner as the Board may direct, a true account in writing of all matters
committed to his or her charge and of his or her receipts and payments, with vouchers
therefor.

### 83.

Officers and employees to pay monies due to secretary
(1) Every officer or employee of the Association shall pay all monies due from him or
her to the secretary or as the Board may otherwise direct.
(2) If any such officer or employee—
(a)

refuses or neglects to deliver any account which he or she ought to deliver or
any voucher relating thereto, or to make any payment which he or she ought
to make; or

(b)

after three days’ notice in writing signed by the chairperson and given or left
at his or her usual or last known place of abode refuses or neglects to deliver
to the secretary, or as the Board may otherwise direct, anything whatsoever
which he or she ought to deliver or to give satisfaction respecting it to the
Board or as he or she may be directed,

the magistrate of the Southern District may on the complaint of the chairperson, or any
other member of the Board, by summary order require him or her to make such delivery
or payment or to give such satisfaction, and if he or she shall fail to make such delivery or
payment or to give such satisfaction, he or she shall be guilty of an offence.
(3) One remedy only to be sought.—Nothing in this section shall affect any remedy
by action against any such officer or employee or his or her surety, except that such
officer or employee shall not be both sued by action and proceeded against summarily for
the same cause.

### 84.

Power of Association to make regulations
It shall be lawful for the Association to make and when made add to, alter or revoke
regulations for the good management of the affairs of the Association generally, and in
particular with respect to the following matters, namely—
(a)

regulating the export of nutmegs by the Association and the marketing of the
same;

(b)

registration of a trade mark and regulating the use thereof;

(c)

regulating the manner in which the accounts of the Association shall be kept;

(d)

controlling the finances of the Association and regulating the time at, and the
terms under which, advances and payments to members and other persons
shall be made;

(e)

regulating the conditions of membership of the Association including the
fixing of any fees payable on admission to membership and periodically
thereafter; and

(f)

regulating and controlling every other matter within the scope of the
operations of the Association whether the same shall be of the same kind as
any of the matters hereinbefore particularly mentioned or otherwise.

### 85.

Regulations made by Association to be confirmed by Minister
Regulations made by the Association under the provisions of this Act or of any other
Act thereunto it enabling shall have the force and effect of law when confirmed by the
Minister and published in the Gazette:
Provided that it shall be lawful for the Minister by Order, to be published in a similar
manner, to revoke the confirmation of any regulations in which case such regulations
shall thereupon cease to have such force and effect.

### 86.

Power of Association to levy and collect toll
The Association shall have power to levy and collect, and when levied to alter by
increasing or reducing the amount thereof, a toll on all nutmegs exported and to charge
the same against the owner of any nutmegs delivered to the Association or, when a
licence to export nutmegs shall be given to any person by the Board, to recover the same
from the person to whom such licence shall be given or to demand from him or her
payment of the same as a condition precedent to the granting of such licence. The amount
of such toll shall from time to time be determined by the Board but the same or any
altered amount thereof shall not be levied or collected until the amount of the toll or such
altered amount shall have been approved by the Minister. For determining the amount of
such toll the Board shall have regard to the requirements of the Association for meeting
current expenses, for the payment of interest, for the creation of a sinking fund, for the
repayment of monies loaned to the Association, for the payment of pensions and
gratuities, and for the creation of a reserve fund:
Provided that the reserve fund, or any part thereof may, from time to time, at the
discretion of the Board, be used for the purpose of price stabilisation.

### 87.

Funds of Association
The funds of the Association shall consist of the subscriptions of members of the
Association and of all monies received by the Association in pursuance of any toll levied
upon nutmegs exported and of any monies granted to the Association by the Government
or by any body authorised by the British or any other foreign Government to make grants
for the development of Grenada and of monies donated or bequeathed to the Association
and of all monies payable to the Association under any of the provisions of this Act or
otherwise.

### 88.

Allowances and expenses
(1) The Association may pay such allowances and expenses as may be approved by
the Minister.
(2) The Association may provide loans for the purpose of meeting any part of the cost
of the establishment and development of agro-industries approved by the Minister on
such terms as may have been agreed upon between the borrower, the Association and the
Minister.

### 89.

Power of Association to negotiate instruments
The Association shall have power to draw, make, accept, endorse, discount, execute,
issue and negotiate bills of exchange, bills of lading, cheques, orders and other negotiable
or transferable instruments for the purpose of or in connection with the affairs of the
Association.

### 90.

Borrowing powers of Association
The Association may, with the consent of the Minister, borrow money for the carrying
out of its purposes and may charge in any manner all property of the Association of every
nature and kind whatsoever and all its funds and all the revenues of the Association
present and future with the repayment of monies borrowed for such purposes.

### 91.

Inspection and audit of accounts
(1) The Board shall, immediately after the end of every nutmeg year, cause the
accounts of the Association and of the Board to be audited by some competent person
approved for the purpose by the Minister.
(2) The audit under subsection (1) shall include an examination of overdue debts, if
any, and a valuation of the assets and liabilities of the Association.
(3) All books of accounts of the Association and all monies, documents and things
pertaining to the Association in the custody or under the control of the Association or of
the Board or of any officer or employee of the Association shall, at all times, be subject to
inspection and audit by the Director of Audit or by any clerk of the Audit Department in
like manner as though such books of accounts, monies, documents and things were in the
custody or under the control of a public officer in his or her official capacity or by any
other auditor, whether Government or non-official, as the Minister may direct.

### 92.

Board to furnish statement
The Board shall furnish to the Minister at the end of each half year a statement of the
financial and stock position of the Association in such form as the Minister may, from
time to time, direct.

### 93.

Minister may give directions to the Board
The Minister may, after consultation with the Board, give to the Board directions of a
general character as to the exercise and performance of their functions in relation to matters
appearing to the Minister to concern the public interest, and the Board shall give effect to
any such directions.

### 94.

Punishment of offenders
Any person who is guilty of an offence against this Act or against regulations made
hereunder shall, on summary conviction thereof, when no other penalty has hereinbefore
been provided, be liable to a fine of two hundred and fifty dollars, and in default of
payment, to imprisonment for one month.

### 95.

Institution of proceedings against offenders
Every proceeding instituted against any such offender shall be taken in the name and
at the instance of the secretary or of any other person authorised by the Board or in the
name and at the instance of the Chief of Police.

### 96.

Hearing of complaints against offenders
Every offence against this Act or regulations made hereunder shall be heard and
determined by a magistrate as an offence punishable on summary conviction and the
mode of procedure, unless varied by this Act, shall be according to the provisions of any
law now in force or hereafter to be made regulating the procedure before magistrates.

### 97.

No court fees payable in respect of proceedings

No court fees or stamp duty shall be payable in the first instance in respect of any
proceedings taken against any person under this Act.

### 98.

Court may order either party to pay full costs
The court may order either party to pay full costs and if the court on convicting a
defendant shall order him or her to pay a penalty, but shall omit to order payment of the
costs of the proceedings, then such penalty shall be applied in payment of the costs, or
there shall be deducted from that penalty such an amount as will pay for the costs.

And whereas it is provided by section 80 of the Act that, inter alia, no officer or employee
of the Association shall be entitled to claim more than one month’s notice of
termination of his or her services and the Board shall have no power to make any
agreement for service involving a longer period of notice except the same shall be
authorised by the Minister of Agriculture (hereinafter referred to as “the Minister”)
with the approval of the House of Representatives;
And whereas the Minister has approved the payment of two additional months’ salary to
Mr. Michael Campbell, former acting General Manager of the Association;
Now therefore be it resolved that the House of Representatives, pursuant to section 80 of
the Act, approve the payment of two additional months’ salary to Mr. Michael
Campbell, former acting General Manager of the Association, in lieu of notice of
termination of his or her office.

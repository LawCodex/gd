## CHAPTER 165

LAW LIBRARY ACT
An Act to provide for the establishment of a Law Library, and other purposes
connected therewith.
[Act No. 49 of 1991.]
[13th March, 1992.]

1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Law Library Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—
“Committee” means the governing body referred to in section 5 of this Act;
“donation” includes a gift and a present;
“Law Library” means the reference law library established by and under section 3
of this Act;
“legal community” includes members of the Judiciary including Magistrates,
Lawyers, Attorneys at Law, Solicitors, Barristers-at-Law in Government Service and

in private practice, and students registered to read law at a recognised university or
professional Law School;
“publication” includes a book, periodical, pamphlet, journal and any other library
material.
3\. Establishment of Law Library
There is hereby established primarily for the benefit of the legal community a
reference law library to be known as the Law Library.
4\. Property of Law Library
(1) The property of the Law Library shall comprise—
(a)

any publication or document which forms part of any material collected for
the purposes of the Law Library;

(b)

any publication or document which is acquired, whether by donation or
purchase, for the purposes of the Law Library; and

(c)

any other property acquired by the Law Library for its purposes.

(2) The property of the Law Library shall be vested in the Law Library Committee.
5\. The Law Library Committee
(1) The governing body of the Law Library shall be a committee which shall be called
the Law Library Committee.
(2) The Committee shall consist of—
(a) The ex officio members who shall be—
(i)

the Chief Justice or a Puisne Judge nominated by the Chief Justice,

(ii)

the Registrar of the High Court or a public officer nominated by the
Registrar,

(iii)

the President of the Bar Association or such other body as certified by
the Chief Justice to be representative of Lawyers practising in
Grenada or a Lawyer nominated by the President of the Bar
Association or the other body herein referred to, and

(iv)

the Law Librarian; and

(b) not more than three persons appointed jointly by the ex officio members.
(3) The Chairperson of the Committee shall be chosen by a majority of all the members
of the Committee. Until the Chairperson is so chosen, or whenever there is a vacancy in
the office of Chairperson, the Chief Justice or a Puisne Judge nominated by him or her
shall act as Chairperson.
(4) The Chairperson of the Committee shall hold office for three years, from the date
of being chosen as chairperson and shall be eligible for re-appointment.
(5) A member of the committee not being an ex officio member may resign from the
Committee by writing under his or her hand addressed to the Chairperson or if there is no
Chairperson to the Chief Justice.
6\. Functions of the Committee

The functions of the Committee shall include—
(a)

the general management and control of the Law Library; and

(b)

the appointment of the Law Librarian and of the senior staff of the Law
Library.

7\. Meetings of the Committee
(1) The chairperson of the Committee shall preside at the meetings of the Committee
and in the absence of the chairperson, the members present shall appoint one of their
number present to preside at that meeting.
(2) Subject to subsection (3), no decision on a matter which, in the opinion of the
person presiding, is a matter of importance for determination by the Committee shall be
taken by the Committee unless—
(a)

not less than four members of the Committee are present at the meeting; and

(b)

the members present vote in favour of the matter for determination:
Provided that at least one such member shall not be an ex officio member.

(3) Where not less than four members of the Committee agree in writing on a matter
of importance for determination by the Committee no meeting need be held in respect of
that matter:
Provided that at least one such member shall not be an ex officio member.
(4) At all other meetings of the Committee the person presiding and three other
members of the Committee shall form the quorum for the meeting: provided that at least
one such member shall not be an ex officio member.
(5) Where at a meeting of the Committee there is an equality of votes on a matter for
determination before the Committee, the person presiding shall have a second or casting
vote.
(6) The Law Librarian shall not vote at any meeting of the Committee.
(7) The Committee shall, subject to this section, regulate its own procedure at it
meetings.
8\. Funds of the Library
(1) The funds of the Law Library shall consist of—
(a)

grants-in-aid from the Government;

(b)

fees and fines paid to the Law Library;

(c)

donations in cash made to the Law Library by any person or body of persons
or any other benefactor;

(d)

annual subscriptions paid by members for the use of the Law Library;

(e)

monies collected by, or paid to, the Law Library in respect of the use of the
facilities of the Law Library by any person; and

(f)

monies paid to the Law Librarian in respect of the sale or disposal of any
material of the Law Library not needed for the purposes of the Law Library.

(2) The records and books of account kept by the Law Librarian shall be audited not
later than three months after the end of the financial year by the Director of Audit or by
an auditor appointed by the Director of Audit.

9\. Law Librarian
The Law Librarian shall, subject to the general directions of the Committee on the
general administration and policy of the Law Library, be responsible for—
(a)

the proper administration of the Law Library;

(b)

the acquisition, collection, purchase, organisation and maintenance of
publications;

(c)

the proper keeping of books and all other records of the Law Library;

(d)

the preparation of the estimates of revenue and expenditure of the Law
Library; and

(e)

the preparation of an annual report on the working of the Law Library.

10\. Regulations
The Committee may make regulations providing for—
(a)

the opening hours of the Law Library;

(b)

the admission to membership of the Law Library of a member of the legal
community and of any other person, the subscriptions payable for
membership, and the method of payment of the subscriptions;

(c)

the purchase, preservation and the loan of publications;

(d)

the maintenance of good order in the Law Library;

(e)

the payment of fees for the use of the facilities of the Law Library;

(f)

the payment of fines in respect of—
(i) the late return of a publication borrowed from the Law Library, or
(ii) the loss of, or damage to, a publication belonging to the Law Library;

(g)

sanctions for the infringement by any person of a provision of the
Regulations.

11\. Repeal
The Law Library Act, Chapter 157 of the Laws of Grenada 1990 Revised Edition, is
hereby repealed.
12\. Commencement
This Act comes into force on the 13th day of March, 1992.

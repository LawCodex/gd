## CHAPTER 261

PROVISIONAL COLLECTION OF TAXES ACT
An Act to provide for the provisional collection of taxes by statutory instrument.
[Act No. 12 of 1987.]

[15th May, 1987.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Provisional Collection of Taxes Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act, “tax” includes duty, rate, due, fee or other impost, and references to the
renewal of a tax refer also to the re-imposition of a tax.

### 3.

Orders varying, renewing or imposing a tax, to have statutory effect for a limited
period
(1) The Minister responsible for finance may make an Order under this section
providing for—
(a)

the variation of a tax for the time being in force;

(b)

the renewal of a tax previously imposed for a limited period or in force
during the previous financial year; or

(c)

the imposition of a tax,

and notwithstanding anything to the contrary the provisions of such an Order shall, for the
period limited by this section and subject to the provisions of this Act, have effect as if
contained in an Act of Parliament.
(2) Where an Order under subsection (1) provides for the renewal of a tax, all laws
which were in force with reference to that tax as last imposed by an Act of Parliament

shall, during the period for which the Order remains in force and subject to the provisions
of this Act, have full force and effect with respect to the tax as renewed by the Order.
(3) Subject to subsection (4), an Order under this section shall continue for a period of
six months next following its publication in the Gazette:
Provided that the House of Representatives may by resolution authorise the
continuance in force of the Order for a further period of three months.
(4) An Order under this section shall cease to have effect—
(a)

if it is not confirmed, with or without modification, by resolution of the
House of Representatives within the next thirty days on which the House sits
after the date of publication of the Order in the Gazette;

(b)

if Parliament is dissolved; or

(c)

if an enactment comes into operation varying, renewing or imposing the tax.

(5) Where an Order under this section is modified by resolution of the House of
Representatives it shall have effect under this Act as so modified.

### 4.

Repayment where Order is modified or ceases to have effect
(1) Where the provisions of an Order under section 3 are modified by resolution of
the House of Representatives or by an Act of Parliament so as to affect the tax payable
under the Order, any money paid in pursuance of the Order which would not be payable
under the modified provisions shall be repaid or made good, and any deduction made in
pursuance of the Order, so far as it would not have been authorised under the modified
provisions, shall be deemed to be an unauthorised deduction.
(2) Where an Order under section 3 ceases to have effect before an enactment comes
into operation confirming or modifying the provisions thereof, any money paid in
pursuance of the Order shall be repaid or made good, and any deduction made in
pursuance of the Order shall be deemed to be an unauthorised deduction.

### 5.

Provisional deposit of tax when tax is reduced
Where a tax is reduced by an Order under section 3, the person liable to pay the
reduced tax shall pay such tax and in addition shall deposit with the person entitled to
receive the tax the difference between the tax payable prior to the date on which the
Order takes effect and the tax payable under the Order until the Order expires or an
enactment comes into operation modifying the provisions thereof; and thereafter the
deposit shall be either brought to account as tax or refunded to the depositor, as the case
requires.

PROVISIONAL COLLECTION OF TAXES (CUSTOMS SERVICE CHARGE)
ORDER
[SRO 1 of 1993.]

[31st December, 1992.]
1\. Citation
This Order may be cited as the Provisional Collection of Taxes (Customs Service
Charge) Order.
2\. Definitions
In this Order—
“Comptroller” means the Comptroller of Customs and Excise;
“Customs Laws” includes the Customs (Control and Management) Act,

## Chapter 75B, and any other enactment for the time being in force relating to Customs;

“goods” includes stores, baggage and livestock;
“Minister” means the Minister charged with the responsibility for finance.
3\. Imposition of Customs Service Charge
Subject to section 5, there shall be levied on all goods imported under any Customs
law a charge to be known as the Customs Service Charge.
4\. Rate of Customs Service Charge
(1) The Customs Service Charge shall be payable to the Comptroller and, subject to
section 6, shall be the equivalent of two and one-half per cent of the CIF value of all
goods imported into the State under any Customs law whether or not the goods are
subject to Customs duties.
(2) All sums collected under this Order shall, as soon as practicable be paid by the
Comptroller to the Accountant General for the benefit of the general revenue of Grenada.
5\. Exempted goods

Notwithstanding the provision of section 3, no Customs Service Charge shall be
payable under this Order in respect of the goods listed in the Schedule.
6\. Variation of service charge
The Cabinet, may—
(a)

by Order made by SRO vary the rate of service charge under this Order; and

(b)

upon application by an importer, vary the rate of the service charge payable
by the importer under this Order, if it is satisfied that it is just and necessary
to do so.

7\. Power to amend Schedule
The Minister may, with the approval of Cabinet, by Order made by SRO, add to,
delete or amend the provisions of the Schedule.
8\. Commencement
This Order comes into force on the 1st day of January, 1993.

PROVISIONAL COLLECTION OF TAXES (CUSTOMS SERVICE CHARGE)
ORDER
[SRO 3 of 1996.]

[19th January, 1996.]
1\. Citation
This Order may be cited as the Provisional Collection of Taxes (Customs Service
Charge) Order.
2\. Variation of tax
The tax imposed by section 4(1) of the Customs (Service Charge) Act, 1993, of the
equivalent of two and one-half per cent of the CIF value of all goods imported into the
State is increased to five percent of the CIF value.

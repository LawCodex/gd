## CHAPTER 229

PEASANTS’ HOUSES LOANS ACT
An Act to provide for loans for the construction or improvement of peasants’
houses.
[22nd November, 1939.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Peasants’ Houses Loans Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“peasant” means any person who is owner or occupier of not more than four acres
of land, or who is a labourer, domestic or menial employee, artificer, handicraftsman
or is otherwise engaged in manual labour;
“rural area” means any part of Grenada other than the towns of St. George,
Grenville, Sauteurs, Victoria and Gouyave, and Hillsborough in the island of
Carriacou, as fixed and described by proclamation issued under the Towns Boundaries
Act, Chapter 323.

### 3.

Establishment of Board
For the purposes of the making of loans for the construction and improvement of
peasants’ houses in rural areas there shall be established a Board to be called the Housing
Board of Control (hereinafter referred to as “the Board”).

### 4.

Constitution of Board
(1) The Board shall consist of a chairman and four other members to be appointed by
the Minister:
Provided that no act of the Board shall be invalidated or unlawful because of any
vacancy existing therein.
(2) Three members of the Board shall form a quorum at any meeting.
(3) The members of the Board shall hold office during the pleasure of the Minister.

### 5.

Indemnity to members of Board
No member of the Board shall incur any personal liability for any loss or damage by
any act or omission in the administration of the affairs of the Board, unless such loss or
damage be occasioned by an intentionally wrong act or omission on his or her part.

### 6.

Secretary to Board
(1) There shall be a secretary to the Board to be appointed by the Minister who will
examine and report to the Board on each application for a loan, keep all records in
connection with loans made and repayments thereof, keep the minutes of meetings of the
Board and perform such other duties as may be prescribed by the Board.
(2) Provision shall be made in the estimates for the salary and travelling allowance to
be paid to the secretary.

### 7.

Funds of Board
The funds of the Board shall be such monies as may be authorised by the Minister,
with the approval of the House of Representatives, to be paid to the Board and
repayments of loans made by the Board.

### 8.

Application of funds of Board
(1) The funds of the Board shall be applied solely for the making of loans for the
construction of new houses, or the improvement of existing houses, for the use of
peasants living in rural areas, subject to such conditions and regulations as may be made
by the Minister.
(2) No loan shall be made by the Board for the construction or improvement of any
house not intended for use by a peasant or his or her family.

### 9.

Accounts
The funds of the Board and the accounts of the same shall be kept at the Treasury and
every payment therefrom shall only be made on a claim to be certified by the secretary
and countersigned by the chairman.

### 10.

Report
(1) The Board shall furnish to the Minister as soon as possible after the expiration of
each year an annual report upon the administration of the affairs of the Board together
with a statement of the assets and liabilities of the Board at the end of the said year.
(2) The statement shall be audited by the Director of Audit and laid before the House
of Representatives as soon as possible.

### 11.

Regulations

The Minister may make regulations—
(a)

prescribing the maximum amount of each loan;

(b)

prescribing the maximum period within which loans shall be repaid;

(c)

fixing the rate of interest chargeable on loans;

(d)

prescribing generally the terms and conditions under which loans may be
made.

### 12.

Review of Board’s decisions
All decisions of the Board shall be subject to review by the Minister whose decision
shall be final.

### 13.

Loan a charge on house
A loan made under this Act for the construction or improvement of any house and all
interest due thereon shall be a charge on the house for the construction or improvement of
which such loan is made:
Provided that in all cases where the borrower is not the owner in fee simple of the land
upon which the house rests or is built, the house shall for the sole purpose of this Act be
deemed to be a personal chattel to which the Bills of Sale Act, Chapter 33, shall apply.

### 14.

Procedure for recovery of loan and interest
Any money due and payable in respect of a loan made under the provisions of this Act
or any interest thereon may, if unpaid, be recovered—
(a)

in the manner provided by the Real Property Act, Chapter 275, for the
recovery of unpaid taxes;

(b)

in any case by suit in a court of competent jurisdiction instituted by and in
the name of the secretary of the Board:
Provided that—
(i) any sale under the powers contained in the Real Property Act, Chapter
275, shall be only of the house in respect of which the loan is made,
and
(ii) such sale shall have the effect of conveying to the purchaser the right,
title and interest of the owner in the said house subject to all registered
charges thereon prior to the loan.

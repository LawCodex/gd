## CHAPTER 23

BANANA ACT
An Act to establish a statutory body to control the production of bananas, and for
purposes incidental thereto.
[Cap. 25 of 1958 amended by Act No. 9 of 1971, Act No. 22 of 1972, Act No. 41 of 1979, Act No.
4 of 1984, Act No. 10 of 1990, Act No. 15 of 1992, Act No. 42 of 1994, Act No. 3 of 1997, Act
No. 24 of 2000.]

[16th March, 1955.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Banana Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“bananas” means bananas cultivated for marketing;
“chairperson” means the chairperson of the committee;
“committee” means the committee of management of the Society;
“member” means a member of the Society;
“Minister” and “Ministry” means, respectively, the Minister or Ministry
responsible for agriculture;
“secretary” means the secretary of the Society;
“Society” means the Grenada Banana Co-operative Society constituted and
established by this Act;
“WIBDECO” mean the Windward Islands Banana Development and Exporting
Company Limited, a company incorporated under the Commercial Code (Companies)
of St. Lucia and registered in Grenada.

### 3.

Establishment of Grenada Banana Co-operative Society
There is hereby established a body corporate to be known as the Grenada Banana Cooperative Society which shall be entitled to make contracts and to sue and be sued in its
name, to acquire by purchase or otherwise, hold and dispose of all kinds of property, real
and personal.

### 3A.

Powers of the Society
The Society shall have the power—
(a)

to export bananas;

(b)

to provide extension and import services to its members in such manner as
the Society determines;

(c)

to rehabilitate banana plantations and to stimulate the production and export
of bananas; and

(d)

to finance loans which are commercially reasonable as to their terms and
conditions to members and other qualified participants in the export banana
industry.

### 3B.

Seal and the authentication thereof
(1) The Society shall have an official seal which shall be in the custody of the
chairperson or other members of the committee or the secretary, as determined by the
committee.
(2) The seal of the Society shall be authenticated by the signatures of the chairperson
or any other member authorised by the committee to sign and the secretary, and such seal
shall be officially and judicially noticed.
(3) All documents, other than those documents required by law to be under the seal of
society, and all decisions of the committee shall be signified under the hand of the
chairperson or any other member duly authorised to sign by the committee and the
secretary.

### 4.

Membership of Society
(1) All growers of bananas shall be entitled to membership of the Society.
(2) Every applicant for membership of the Society shall apply in writing to the
secretary and shall state his or her qualifications for membership. The committee shall
thereupon consider the application at its next meeting and decide whether or not the
applicant shall be admitted to membership and the secretary shall notify him or her
accordingly within seven days of the holding of the meeting.

### 5.

Right of appeal to magistrate
(1) Upon application made by any person to the secretary for membership the
applicant may be required to furnish such evidence of his or her qualifications for
membership as may be required by the committee.
(2) Any person whose application for membership is rejected by the committee may
appeal to the magistrate of the district in which that person resides, and if he or she shall
satisfy such magistrate of his or her eligibility for membership of the Society, the
magistrate shall make an order directed to the chairperson requiring that such person,
upon his or her complying with the regulations controlling the admission to membership
of eligible persons generally, be forthwith admitted a member of the Society.
(3) Notice of the hearing by the magistrate of the appeal shall be served upon the
secretary not less than seven clear days before the date fixed for the hearing.
(4) The chairperson shall be liable to a penalty of fifty dollars for each day after the
day of receipt by him or her of the order of the magistrate during which he or she shall
fail to admit the applicant to membership of the Society the applicant having complied
with all conditions precedent to the admission of eligible persons to membership of the
Society. The decision of the magistrate shall be final.
(5) The committee shall not entertain applications for admission to membership
which have already been refused by the committee until the expiration of one year from
the date of the refusal.

### 6.

Composition, powers and functions of the Society
The committee shall consist of not less than eight or more than ten persons, shall be
the governing and executive body of the Society, and shall control the affairs thereof.

### 7.

Annual general meeting of the Society
(1) A general meeting of the Society shall be held in April of each year, hereinafter
referred to as the annual meeting.
(2) The Committee shall organise the annual general meeting referred to in subsection
(1) and shall give at least one month’s notice in the Gazette of the said meeting.
(3) At the annual general meeting referred to in subsection (1) the Committee shall
present to the said meeting the duly audited financial statements of the Society in respect
of the immediately preceding period of twelve months.
(4) At the annual general meeting referred to in subsection (1) members of the
Society shall elect from amongst themselves six members so elected to the Committee
shall forthwith be submitted by the Secretary to the Governor-General.
(5) Within fourteen days of the said six members being elected to the Committee
under subsection (4), the Governor-General shall nominate at least two but not more than
four other persons to serve as members of the Committee who shall hold office during his
or her pleasure. In the selection of such persons, the Governor-General may have regard
to the advisability of ensuring representation of small growers on the Committee. At least
one officer of the Public Service shall be nominated under this subsection to the
Committee and shall be eligible for re-election.

### 8.

Power to fill vacancies
If no persons or an insufficient number of persons offer themselves for election to the
committee or are elected thereto, or if any vacancy in the committee through any other
reason shall occur, the Minister may nominate one or more members to fill such vacancy
or vacancies.

### 9.

Vacancies shall not invalidate acts of committee
No act or proceeding of the committee shall be invalid by reason of any vacancy.

### 10.

Disqualification for membership of committee
A member of the committee shall, ipso facto, cease to be a member thereof if he or she
is absent from any three consecutive meetings of the committee without leave.

### 11.

Voting
Subject to the voting powers of the chairperson contained in section 16(2), all matters
proposed for the decision of the committee are to be determined by a majority of the
votes of the members present and voting.

### 12.

Register of members
(1) Subject to any rules made hereunder with respect to any procedure pertaining to
the writing up of registers of members, the secretary shall in the month of April in every
year, compile in alphabetical order a register of the names of all members. Such register
shall be signed by the chairperson and committed to the custody of the secretary. A copy
of the register shall be displayed in the office of the Society in such manner as will enable
all persons interested therein readily to inspect the same at all reasonable hours.
(2) On or before the 31st day of March in each year, the chairperson shall having
given prior notice to the Minister, revise the register to reflect new and lapsed
membership.

(3) The register of members so prepared and revised shall be used in all matters to be
decided by the votes of members and no person whose name does not appear on the
register of members shall be entitled to vote upon any matters connected with the Society.

### 13.

Tenure of office, etc., of members of the Committee
(1) If any member of the committee, other than the member who is a public officer,
refuses to obey or conform with the rules and decisions of the committee it shall be
lawful for the committee, by resolution, passed by the affirmative votes of not less than
five members of the committee, after affording such member a full opportunity of
defending himself or herself, to expel such member and to declare his or her seat vacant,
and his or her seat shall, ipso facto, become vacant accordingly.
(2) Any member of the committee may, by giving fourteen days notice in writing to
the chairperson, resign his or her office. The chairperson may resign his or her
chairpersonship by giving fourteen days notice in writing addressed to the secretary.
(3) Any notification of the fact and circumstance of any vacancy in the committee
shall be published by the secretary in the Gazette, and notified to the Minister with all
dispatch.
(4) Any vacancy in the committee may at any time be filled by the Minister.

### 14.

Dissolution of committee
(1) If the committee, in the judgement of the Minister, makes default in the
performance of the duties imposed upon it by this Act or by any other written law, or
exceeds or abuses its powers, it shall be lawful for the Minister by an Order, published in
the Gazette, to dissolve the committee.
(1A) Without prejudice to anything contained in subsection (1), it shall be lawful for
the Minister by an Order published in the Gazette to dissolve the committee if he or she is
of the opinion that such dissolution is warranted by the economic and other like
circumstances pertaining to the viability of the banana industry.
(2) In case of such dissolution the following consequences shall ensue, namely—
(a)

all members of the committee shall, from the date of such Order, vacate their
offices;

(b)

all the powers and duties of the committee shall, until the formation of a new
committee under this Act, be exercised by such person as the Minister may
appoint in that behalf, and any payment made to that person for his or her
service shall be a charge upon the funds of the Society hereinafter mentioned;

(c)

any contracts or other obligations lawfully entered into by the committee
before its dissolution shall be carried out, as far as possible, by such person
aforesaid who shall, for those purposes, be deemed to be the agent of the
Society;

(d)

if such Order for dissolution shall be made the public officer nominated as
aforesaid shall perform the duties of the committee until the end of the
period of twelve months which shall expire on the thirteenth day of April
next after such nomination when the new committee to be constituted as
hereinbefore provided shall come into being.

### 15.

Appointment of chairperson, etc.
(1) The chairperson shall be appointed by the Minister.
(2) The chairperson of the committee shall be, ex officio, chairperson of the Society.

(3) The Minister may appoint a deputy chairperson who shall preside and have all the
powers of the chairperson when the chairperson is absent.

### 16.

Duties of chairperson
(1) The chairperson shall preside at every meeting of the committee and shall have
such other powers and perform such other duties as may, from time to time, be assigned
to him or her by committee rules.
(2) The chairperson shall have an original vote and, in case of an equal division of
votes on any question before the committee, a second or casting vote.

### 17.

Minutes to be received in evidence
(1) A minute of the proceedings at a meeting of the committee signed at the same or
the ensuing meeting by the chairperson or by a member describing himself or herself as
or appearing to be chairperson thereof shall be received in evidence without further proof
in all courts and for all other purposes whatsoever.
(2) Until the contrary has been proved, every meeting of the committee in respect of
the proceedings whereof a minute has been so made, shall be deemed to have been duly
convened and held, and all the members present at the meeting shall be deemed to have
been duly qualified.

### 18.

Power of committee to make rules
(1) The Committee may, with the approval of the Minister, make rules for the good
management of the affairs of the Society generally and in particular with respect to the
following matters—
(a)

the time and place of its meetings and of general meetings of the Society;

(b)

the mode of convening its meetings and the general meetings of the Society;

(c)

the attendance of its members;

(d)

the form and order of its debates and of the debates at general meetings of
the Society;

(e)

the powers and duties of its officers and employees;

(f)

the general regulation of the proceedings of the committee and of the
Society, and the mode of conducting the business of the committee, and of
the Society;

(g)

regulating the purchase, the price to be paid for and the receipt of bananas by
the Society;

(h)

regulating the grading and selection of bananas by the Society;

(i)

regulating the time and manner of disposal of bananas delivered to the
Society and rejected for export;

(j)

regulating the handling and export of bananas by the Society and the
marketing thereof, and imposing a cess or levy on bananas;

(k)

regulating the manner in which the accounts of the Society shall be kept;

(l)

controlling the finances of the Society and regulating the time at and the
terms under which payments to members and other persons shall be made;

(m)

regulating the conditions of membership of the Society including the fixing
of any fees payable on admission to membership and periodically thereafter
and the disciplining of members; and

(n)

regulating and controlling every other matter within the scope of the
Society’s operations whether the same shall be of the same kind as any of
the matters hereinbefore particularly mentioned or otherwise.

(2) Such rules may create offences and prescribe penalties for the contravention of
any of the provisions thereof not exceeding a fine of one thousand dollars or
imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months.
(3) No such rule shall be made, or, when made, shall be altered or revoked, unless
special notice of such intended making, alteration, or revocation has been given at a
meeting of the committee held at least seven days before that at which the making,
alteration or revocation is proposed, and unless special notice in writing thereof has also
been given to the members of the committee at least three days before such latter
meeting.
(4) The making, alteration or revocation of rules which shall relate to the meetings of
the Society or shall purport to regulate the proceedings of the Society shall not be binding
upon the Society until the same has been confirmed by a general meeting of the Society
held not less than fourteen days after the making, alteration or revocation of such rules by
the committee, and then only if special notice in writing of the making, alteration or
revocation of such rules shall have been given in the notice convening the general
meeting or to the members of the Society at least three clear days before the day of the
general meeting.

### 19.

Repealed.

### 20.

Bananas delivered to become property of Society
All bananas delivered to the Society shall become the property of the Society and the
rights of the member or the grower of the bananas and of all other persons claiming
through, under or against such member or grower shall be none other than a right to
receive such sums as shall be payable by the Society to the persons entitled to receive the
same to be determined in relation to the price paid by the Society for such bananas.

### 21.

Excess of revenue to be placed in reserve fund
(1) Any excess of the revenues of the Society for any financial year thereof over the
sums properly chargeable to their revenue account shall be placed in a reserve fund.
(2) The amount of the reserve fund and the uses to which it shall be applied shall be
determined by the committee after consultation with the Minister.

### 22.

Secretary and treasurer of Society to be appointed
(1) The committee may, with the approval of the Minister—
(a)

appoint fit and proper persons to be secretary and treasurer respectively;

(b)

from time to time, fix or alter the salaries to be paid to the secretary and to
the treasurer from the funds of the Society; and

(c)

suspend or dismiss either or both of them:

Provided that notwithstanding the dissolution or other determination of the committee,
the secretary and treasurer shall continue to hold office until their employment as such
has been terminated by the Minister.
(2) The Minister may call upon the committee to give reasons why any of the said
officers should not be suspended or dismissed, and upon consideration thereof direct the
committee to suspend or dismiss them upon such terms and conditions as may be deemed
fit. In the event of the committee failing to comply with such direction within three

months or such shorter period as may be set out in the direction, the Minister may
proceed to suspend or dismiss the said officers.
(3) The secretary shall be the chief executive officer of the Society and shall have all
such powers and perform all such duties as are or may be conferred upon him or her by
this or any Act, or by any regulation or order of the committee. He or she shall attend the
meetings of the Society and of the committee and shall keep the minutes of the
proceedings at every such meeting.
(4) In the case of the illness or absence of the secretary, the committee may, with the
consent of the Minister, appoint some fit and proper person to be assistant secretary.
(5) The assistant secretary shall hold office during the pleasure of the committee.
(6) All things required or authorised by law or any regulation or order of the
committee to be done by the secretary may be done by the assistant secretary.
(7) An assistant secretary, while acting for the secretary, may receive remuneration to
be drawn from the remuneration of the secretary or to be paid from the funds of the
Society as the committee shall determine.

### 23.

Appointment of junior officers and employees
(1) The committee may, from time to time, appoint such other officers and employees
as may be required for the efficient administration of the affairs of the Society.
(2) Every such officer or employee shall hold office during the pleasure of the committee.
(3) Every such officer or employee shall receive such remuneration and shall perform
such duties as may, from time to time, be assigned to him or her by the committee.

### 24.

Officers and employees may be required to furnish security
(1) The committee may require any officer or employee appointed hereunder to give
such security as may be thought proper for the true and faithful performance of the duties of
his or her office, and for the due accounting for paying over and delivering all monies, goods
and chattels which may come into his or her hands in virtue of his or her office.
(2) No officer or employee of the Society shall be entitled to more than a month’s
notice of the termination of his or her employment, and the committee shall have no
power to make any agreement for service involving any longer notice, or any claim for
pension or compassionate allowance, except the same shall be authorised by the Minister.
(3) If the secretary or any other officer or any employee appointed by the committee
shall, directly or indirectly, have any pecuniary interest in any agreement or contract
which shall be entered into by the Society or any matter affecting the committee, such
secretary, officer or employee shall report the circumstances of his or her interest to the
committee through the chairperson as soon as practicable after he or she becomes aware
of him or her having such interest; and in default of him or her so reporting, he or she
shall be liable to summary dismissal from the service of the Society.

### 25.

Repealed.

### 26.

Funds of the Society, allowances, expenses, and loans to members
(1) The funds of the Society shall consist of any monies granted to the Society by
Parliament or granted or loaned by any other Government or person, and of monies
donated or bequeathed to the Society and all monies payable to the Society under any of
the provisions of this Act or otherwise.
(2) The Society may borrow money for the carrying out of its purposes and may
charge in any manner all property of the Society of every nature and kind whatsoever and

all its funds and all the revenues of the Society present and future with the repayment of
money borrowed for such purposes.
(3) The Society may advance money to producers on such terms as the committee
may think fit subject to the approval of the Minister.
(4) The committee may pay such allowances and expenses as shall be approved by
the Minister.
(5) The committee may provide loans towards the establishment and development of
approved agro-industries on terms to be agreed between the committee and the Minister.

### 27.

Audit
(1) The accounts of the Society shall be audited in each financial year by an
independent, qualified and experienced auditor or firm of auditors appointed by the
Committee with the approval of the Minister.
(2) The Committee, officers and staff of the Society shall make available to the
auditors of all books, ledgers, deeds, contracts, accounts, vouchers and other like
documents which are required for the purposes of subsection (1), and the auditor may
require any person holding or accountable for any such document to appear before him or
her and give a signed statement or information in relation to any such document.

### 28.

Penalty for offences
(1) Any person who is guilty of an offence against this Act shall, on summary
conviction, where no other penalty has hereinbefore been provided, be liable to a penalty
of one thousand dollars and, in default of payment, to imprisonment for six months.
(2) Every proceeding instituted against any such offender shall be taken in the name
and at the instance of the secretary or of any other person authorised by the committee, or
in the name and at the instance of the Chief of Police.

### 29.

Arbitration
(1) In the event of any dispute or difference arising between a member and the
Society in connection with the selection, grading or rejection by the Society of any
bananas the dispute or difference shall be heard and determined by two arbitrators one of
whom shall be a member of the committee nominated by the secretary and one a member
of the Society nominated by the member aggrieved. The member desiring the arbitration
shall, within four hours of the rejection of the fruit, serve a notice in writing on the
secretary intimating his or her desire, and such notice shall be accompanied by a deposit
of two dollars, which sum or part thereof at the discretion of the arbitrators may be
retained and be placed to the credit of the Society in the event of the arbitrators deciding
in favour of the Society.
(2) On receipt of the notice and deposit, the secretary shall nominate his or her
arbitrator, who shall within eight hours proceed with the reference, failing which the
arbitrator of the member aggrieved may proceed to act alone and his or her decision shall
be final and binding on both parties.
(3) In the event of the decision of the arbitrators or of the arbitrator acting alone under
subsection (2) being in favour of the member aggrieved the deposit of two dollars shall be
refunded to the member aggrieved.
(4) In the event of failure of agreement to determine any such dispute or difference by
arbitration, the matter shall be referred to the Chief Technical Officer (Agriculture) or any
officer deputed by him or her as referee, and his or her decision as to the dispute or
difference, and as to the retention of the deposit aforesaid by the Society, shall be final
and binding on both parties.

### 30.

Members not responsible for failure to deliver bananas through loss by accident
or restraint
(1) No member shall be held responsible for failure to deliver bananas on account of
destruction of the cultivation of fruit by hurricane or other inevitable casualty, loss by
fire, theft, or unnatural restraint, inability to provide transportation, or unforeseen
accident or cause beyond the control of the member.
(2) The Society shall likewise not be held responsible for failure to accept any
bananas through loss or inability arising from any of the above-mentioned accidents or
causes or any disability whatsoever arising from occurrences beyond the control of the
Society.

### 31.

No liability of any individual member of Society
There shall be no liability of any individual member of the Society to contribute to the
expenses of the Society in the event of any failure to provide funds or of insufficient
funds to meet any just debts or expenditure incurred by or on behalf of the Society, or any
member thereof duly authorised by or on behalf of the Society, or for any pecuniary
losses arising from the working of the Society, or for any default or any suit for or against
any person or persons.

### 32.

Inspection of banana cultivation
The committee may in writing request the Chief Technical Officer (Agriculture) to
enter into a banana cultivation of any description whatsoever whether of the varieties
specified in section 2 or otherwise and whether the property of a member of the Society
or not, for the purpose of determining the variety or quality of bananas grown thereon, the
condition of the bananas and the method of reaping. The Chief Technical Officer
(Agriculture) when so requested shall, by himself or herself or his or her nominee, be at
liberty, at all reasonable times, after giving reasonable notice of his or her intention, to
enter and inspect such cultivation.

### 33.

Minister may give directions to committee
The Minister may, after consultation with the committee, give to the committee
directions of a general character as to the exercise and performance of their functions
under the provisions of this Act in relation to matters appearing to the Minister to concern
the public interest, and the committee shall give effect to any such directions.

### 1.

Short title
These Rules may be cited as the Banana Rules.

### 2.

Transport of bananas
(1) No person shall carry bananas intended for export on any vehicle unless such
vehicle is registered with the Society as being suitable for the transport of fruit.
(2) No person shall despatch, load or drive a vehicle containing bananas intended for
export on which the fruits project over the sides thereof, unless such vehicle is provided
with wings for the support of such fruit.
(3) No person shall transport bananas intended for export unless such bananas are
thoroughly padded or sleeved in approved paper wrappers, and fruit shall be stored on
vehicles between layers of soft, clean banana trash.
(4) No person shall sit, stand or lie upon bananas intended for export while such fruit
is in transit for shipment, nor shall any driver or person in charge of a vehicle in which
bananas are being transported for shipment permit any person to stand, sit or lie on such
bananas.
(5) No person shall deliver bananas intended for export which are not clean and free
from foreign matter such as nests, twigs, grass, mud or dirt.
(6) No person shall place any article on bananas in a truck intended for export unless
such article is used or intended to be used for the protection of such bananas:
Provided that bananas clearly marked “not for export” may be placed in such a truck.

### 3.

Vehicles conveying bananas

(1) The body of vehicles conveying bananas intended for export shall be rigid and
shall be so constructed as to preclude the fruits stored thereon being damaged by any
uprights or other fixture on such vehicles.
(2) The inside of the body of vehicles intended for conveying bananas for export shall
be so padded with banana trash or rubberised fibre on the sides and floor thereof as to
prevent the fruits loaded thereon from being bruised.

### 4.

Certificate of registration
(1) Upon application by the owner or other person for the registration of a vehicle
intended to be used in the transportation of bananas intended for export, the Committee
shall cause such vehicle to be inspected by one of its officers who shall certify that such
vehicle is suitable for such purpose before the issue to such owner or other person of a
certificate of registration.
(2) Such certificate may be suspended at the discretion of the Committee if satisfied
that there has been delivered from such vehicle bananas having transport bruises
exceeding five per cent on each of six successive trips or that bananas on such vehicle
have been persistently handled in a careless manner.

### 5.

Number of persons on vehicle
The driver of any vehicle conveying bananas intended for export shall not permit
persons to ride on the tray thereof other than the accredited loaders thereof who shall not
exceed five in number.

### 6.

Exposure of bananas
(1) Bananas intended for export shall not be left exposed to the direct rays of the sun
after having been cut, but shall be adequately covered to the satisfaction of a duly
authorised officer of the Society or, of the Ministry of Agriculture.
(2) Bananas found exposed to the direct rays of the sun after having been cut for
export may be marked by a duly authorised officer of the Society or of the Ministry of
Agriculture.

### 7.

Loading bananas into lighters
Bananas intended for export shall not be stacked or loaded into a lighter, boat or other
vessel unless such lighter, boat or vessel is adequately padded to the satisfaction of an
officer of the Society or of the Ministry of Agriculture and such bananas shall be
adequately protected from the direct rays of the sun while stacked thereon.

### 8.

Receiving bananas for export
(1) The following rules shall apply in relation to bananas received for export—
(a)

any person who intends to purchase, receive or transport bananas intended
for export in the capacity of an itinerant buyer or agent shall first obtain from
the Committee a permit authorising him or her to do so, and such bananas
shall be delivered by him or her at an approved receiving station;

(b)

such permit shall prescribe the area within which such itinerant buyer or
agent may operate, and such itinerant buyer or agent shall not operate
outside the area prescribed by the permit;

(c)

the Society may, in its discretion, limit the number of permits for any area
according to requirements;

(d)

a receipt in the form in the Schedule hereto shall be issued by such itinerant
buyer or agent to every producer of bananas showing the number of stems
delivered, the weight of each stem, the total weight of all stems delivered
and the price paid or to be paid to the producer therefor;

(e)

a duplicate copy of every receipt so issued as aforesaid shall be sent by the
itinerant buyer or agent to the Secretary of the Society within forty-eight
hours of delivery of such bananas by the producer and shall be filed by the
Secretary.

(2) The Committee may, from time to time, specify the rates for freight and
commission to be paid by producers for services rendered by itinerant buyers or agents,
and shall be at liberty to vary such rates.

### 9.

Suspension or cancellation of permit
The Committee may, in its discretion, suspend or cancel a permit issued under the
provisions of rule 8 of these Rules.

### 10.

Statement to be delivered
(1) Any person delivering bananas at a reception station shall hand in a statement to
the officer of the Society thereat showing the name of the producer, the number of stems
delivered and the name of the property where the bananas were produced.
(2) Any person delivering bananas intended for export at a reception station in a
vehicle containing bananas from more than one producer shall deliver to an officer of the
Society at the reception station a signed statement showing the names of the producers
and the number of stems received from each such producer, and in addition shall show
the weights accepted from each producer. This statement shall be delivered to the
Secretary before payment is made to the person in whose name the bananas were
delivered.

### 11.

Banana book
(1) The Committee shall issue a banana book, in the form contained in the Schedule
hereto, upon application by a producer containing—
(a)

the description, area and situation of the land cultivated in bananas and the
number of stools;

(b)

the estimated production of bananas in stems in the ensuing year; and

(c)

the nature of tenure of the land.

(2) Every producer delivering bananas at a reception station or to an itinerant buyer or
to an agent or to any other person or society shall present the banana book issued to him
or her in which shall be recorded by the person accepting delivery of the bananas the
particulars called for in the book.
(3) No new banana book shall be supplied to any producer for the replacement of a
book unless the book already in use is returned to the Committee for cancellation.
(4) Any person who uses or authorises the use of a banana book other than the banana
book issued to him or her shall be guilty of an offence against these Rules.
(5) Every producer who ceases to produce bananas intended for export shall return his
or her banana book to the Committee for cancellation.
(6) In the event of the loss of a book, the producer shall swear to an affidavit setting
forth, to the satisfaction of the Society, the circumstances under which such book was
lost. Upon such satisfaction, the Committee shall cause a new book to be issued on payment
of a sum not exceeding one dollar in the discretion of the Society for such replacement.

(7) No person shall wilfully destroy or mutilate a banana book or make a false entry
therein.

### 12.

Production of banana book
(1) Any Police Officer or Officer of the Ministry of Agriculture or of the Society may
demand the production of a banana book and may make a copy of any entry therein, and
may also retain such book upon giving a receipt therefor to the producer or other person
from whom such book is taken. Any such officer taking custody of a banana book shall
immediately inform the Secretary of the purpose for which the book is so retained.
(2) If any person having the custody or control of any such book neglects or refuses to
produce or deliver up such book as aforesaid, he or she shall be guilty of an offence
against these Rules.

### 13.

Time for cutting bananas
No person shall cut bananas intended for export before the time specified for such
cutting on each cutting day, and such time shall not be more than forty-eight hours before
the time at which the reception station closes.

### 14.

Rejected bananas
(1) No person shall offer to the Society for sale or acceptance for export any bananas
which have been previously rejected for export, or attempt to deliver for export such
rejected bananas except with the written permission of the Society. Any bananas rejected
for export may be marked on the stem by a duly authorised Officer of the Society or of
the Ministry of Agriculture:
Provided that such mark shall not render the hands on the stem to be unsaleable.
(2) All rejected bananas shall be returned to the person who delivered them.

### 15.

Penalty for offences
Any person who contravenes the provisions of rule 2(1), 2(2), 2(3), 2(6), 7, 8(1)(a), 10
or 12(2) of these Rules shall be guilty of an offence against these Rules and shall, on
summary conviction, be liable to a fine not exceeding one hundred and fifty dollars or to
a term of imprisonment not exceeding one month; and any person who contravenes the
provisions of rule 2(4) or 11(4) of these Rules shall be guilty of an offence against these
Rules and shall, on summary conviction, be liable to a fine not exceeding five hundred
dollars or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding two months.

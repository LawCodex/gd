## CHAPTER 138A

HOTELS AID (TAX HOLIDAY) (VALIDATION) ACT
An Act to regularise tax holidays granted to certain hotels.
[Act No. 13 of 1993.]

[9th July, 1993.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Hotels Aid (Tax Holiday) (Validation) Act.
2\. Tax on income exempted
The income arising from an hotel mentioned in the Schedule hereto shall be exempt
from tax for the period specified in the Schedule.
3\. Validation
The exemption, up to the 31st December, 1992, of customs duties on the importation
by a person of building materials and hotel equipment for the purpose of constructing and
equipping an hotel where no licence has been granted under section 3 of the Hotels Aid
Act; and the local purchase, subject to drawback, by a person of materials and equipment
for a like purpose and no such licence had been granted and, in each case, would have
been valid if a licence had been granted under section 3 of that Act is hereby validated
and declared to have been lawfully imported or locally purchased as the case may be.

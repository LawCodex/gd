## CHAPTER 234

PENSIONS (AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS) ACT
An Act to provide for the payment of pensions to monthly paid officers and
employers of the Grenada Banana Co-operative Society, the Grenada Cocoa
Association and the Grenada Co-operative Nutmeg Association.
[Act No. 27 of 1978.]

[11th August, 1978.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Pensions (Agricultural Associations) Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“agricultural association” means the—
(a)

Grenada Banana Co-operative Society established by section 3 of the
Banana Act, Chapter 23;

(b)

Grenada Cocoa Association established by section 3 of the Grenada Cocoa
Association Act, Chapter 128;

(c)

Grenada Co-operative Nutmeg Association established by section 3 of the
Nutmeg Industry Act, Chapter 215;

“association officer” means any officer or employee of an agricultural association
employed on the permanent staff who is paid a monthly salary;

“salary” includes the personal, house and lodging allowances (including the
estimated value of free quarters) which an association officer is receiving at the time
when he or she ceases to be an association officer:
Provided that the amount to be allowed for house and lodging allowances shall not
exceed one-sixth of the pay of such officer or employee.

### 3.

Pensions to be charged on funds of agricultural association
Notwithstanding any written law to the contrary, there shall be charged and paid out of
the funds of an agricultural association such sums of money as may, from time to time, be
granted by way of pension or gratuity to association officers.

### 4.

Grant of pension
Subject to this Act, an association officer who has served as such for ten years or
upwards may be granted on retirement a pension at the annual rate of one six-hundreth of
his or her salary for each complete month of his or her service with that agricultural
association:
Provided that the pension shall not exceed two-thirds of the highest salary drawn by
such association officer during his or her period of service.

### 5.

Pension may be granted on basis of age or infirmity
No pension shall be granted to an association officer who has not attained the age of
fifty-five years unless, on medical evidence to the satisfaction of the agricultural
association, the officer is incapacitated by reason of some infirmity of mind or body for
further service with the association and that such infirmity is likely to be permanent.

### 6.

Gratuity
An association officer who has not completed ten years service with an agricultural
association may be granted a gratuity at the rate of half a month’s salary for each
complete six months of service if the agricultural association is satisfied upon medical
evidence that the officer is incapacitated by reason of some infirmity of mind or body for
further service with the association and that such infirmity is likely to be permanent.

### 7.

No pension as of right, etc.
(1) No association officer shall have a right to any pension or gratuity under this Act.
(2) When an agricultural association is satisfied that an association officer has been
guilty of negligence, irregularity, misconduct or a failure to perform his or her duties
efficiently and with fidelity and diligence, the pension or gratuity may be reduced or
altogether withheld.

### 8.

Compulsory retirement
An agricultural association may require an association officer to retire at any time
after he or she attains the age of fifty-five.

### 9.

Right to take reduced pension and gratuity
(1) Every association officer who becomes eligible for pension under this Act may
elect to take on retirement a reduced pension and gratuity in lieu of the pension provided
for by this Act.

(2) Every association officer who becomes eligible for pension shall notify the
agricultural association of his or her election within three months from the date on which
he or she became so eligible.
(3) An election once exercised shall be irrevocable.
(4) Reduced pension shall be a pension equal to three-fourths of the amount of
pension which would be payable under this Act, and a gratuity shall be the amount which
one-fourth of the pension payable thereunder when multiplied by twelve and one-half
shall represent.

### 10.

Provision for gratuity on death
When an association officer dies whilst in the service of an agricultural association
and had, before his or her death, served for a period of five years and upwards, the
agricultural association may grant to his or her legal personal representatives a gratuity
not greater than his or her annual salary.

### 11.

Pension not assignable, etc.
No pension granted under this Act shall be assignable or transferable, or liable to be
attached, sequestered, or levied upon for or in respect of any debt or claim whatsoever.

## CHAPTER 223

OYSTER FISHERY ACT
An Act to empower the Minister to make rules for the control and regulation of the
oyster fishery.
[15th December, 1893.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Oyster Fishery Act.

### 2.

Power of Minister to make rules
The Minister may make rules for the control and regulation of the oyster fishery and
may impose penalties (provided the same shall in no case exceed five hundred dollars) for
any breach of or failure to observe such rules.

### 3.

Rules to be published, etc.
After the rules have been published in the Gazette and one of the local newspapers,
and have been submitted to the House of Representatives, they shall have the force and
effect of law.

OYSTER FISHERY RULES
[Section 2. SRO 40 of 1963.]

[30th November, 1963.]
1\. Citation
These Rules may be cited as the Oyster Fishery Rules.
2\. Definition
In these Rules, “box” means a wooden box measuring one cubic foot.
3\. Conditions under which oysters may be cut
(1) No person shall cut oysters in the lagoon at Harvey Vale Bay, commonly known
as “the Careenage”, in the Island of Carriacou without first having obtained permission in
writing so to do from the District Officer in the Form hereto.
(2) Such permission shall be presented to the Caretaker and no cutting shall be
undertaken by any person, except the Caretaker or by someone deputising for him or her.
(3) Permission so given as aforesaid may at any time be withdrawn by the District
Officer for good and sufficient cause.
(4) No oysters shall be cut before 8 a.m. or after 5 p.m. on any day.
(5) The sum of seventy-five cents shall be paid to the Caretaker in advance as his or
her fee in respect of each box of oysters intended to be cut, and a further sum of fifty
cents shall be similarly paid into general revenue in respect of each such box.
4\. Duties of Caretaker
It shall be the duty of the Caretaker when cutting oysters to see that care is taken in
selecting only such as are of full size. The indiscriminate removal of young and fullgrown oysters growing together on the same branch is absolutely prohibited.
5\. Penalty
Any person guilty of a breach of, or failing to observe, these Rules shall be liable on
summary conviction to a fine not exceeding two hundred and fifty dollars, and any
Revenue Officer or Police Constable may prosecute cases brought hereunder.

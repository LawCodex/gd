## CHAPTER 238A

PETROL TAX ACT
An Act to impose a Petrol Tax.
[Act No. 15 of 2005 amended by Act No. 9 of 2006.]

[30th September, 2005.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Petrol Tax Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for finance;
“Petrol” means gasoline and diesel.
3\. Imposition of tax
There shall be charged and paid by the importer of petrol to the Comptroller of
Customs a tax of three dollars on each gallon of petrol imported into the State for local
consumption.
3A. Price of petrol

(1) The domestic price per gallon of petrol shall be the equivalent of the tax paid
under section 3 together with an average over eight consecutive weeks of the c.i.f. on the
importation of a gallon of petrol plus the whole sale and retail margins as approved by the
Minister of Energy by Order.
(2) A determination of the domestic price of petrol under subsection (1) shall only be
made once in every eight weeks.
(3) The period used in calculating the domestic price per gallon of petrol under
subsection (1) shall be determined by the Minister save and except that a particular week
may only be used on one occasion.
(4) The Minister may, by Order—
(a)

if the domestic price per gallon of petrol goes under nine dollars, increase
the rate of tax imposed under section 3;

(b)

if the domestic price per gallon of petrol goes over thirteen dollars, decrease
the rate of tax imposed under section 3.

(5) Notwithstanding subsection (4), each variation of the rate of tax made by the
Minister shall not exceed fifty cents on each gallon of petrol.
(6) The Minister may, by Order, establish guidelines to govern the components with
respect to the importation and shipping of petrol.
3B. Duty to provide information
(1) Within one month after the commencement of this Act and thereafter at the end of
every month, an importer of either petrol kerosene or liquid petroleum gas (LPG) or all or
some of the aforementioned shall provide to the Minister responsible for energy the
following information—
(a)

a record of imports in the prescribed manner on petrol, kerosene and liquid
petroleum gas (LPG);

(b)

a record of sales provided in the manner specified in the Schedule, of petrol,
kerosene and liquid petroleum gas (LPG).

(2) A person who contravenes the provisions of subsection (1) commits an offence
and is liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of ten thousand dollars and to a further
fine of five hundred dollars for each day in which the contravention continues.
4\. Payment of tax
(1) The tax shall be paid at the time of importation of the petrol into the State.
(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1), if petrol is stored in a bonded warehouse, the tax
on that petrol shall be paid at the time of its delivery from the bonded warehouse for local
consumption.
5\. Minister may amend definition of petrol
The Minister may, by Order, amend the definition of petrol to include any other form
of fuel that may be used by a motor vehicle.
6\. Repeal
The Petrol Tax Act, 1986, is hereby repealed.

PETROL TAX (RATE OF TAX) ORDER
[SRO 8 of 2006.]

[10th January, 2006.]

### 1.

Citation
This Order may be cited as the Petrol Tax (Rate of Tax) Order.

### 2.

Rate of Tax
The rate of tax imposed by section 3(1) of the Petrol Tax Act, Chapter 238A, is varied
from fifty cents to twenty-two cents.

### 3.

Commencement
This Order shall take effect on the 10th day of January, 2006.

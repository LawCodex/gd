## CHAPTER 70

COTTON CONTROL ACT
An Act to provide for the establishment of a growers’ association to control the
production and marketing of cotton intended for export, and for connected
purposes.
[Act No. 24 of 1966 amended by Act No. 10 of 1990.]

[8th October, 1966.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Cotton Control Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“Association” means the Carriacou Cotton Association established and constituted
by this Act;
“Board” means the Cotton Board established and constituted by this Act;
“chairperson” means the chairperson of the Board;
“cotton year” means the period beginning on the first day of December in each year
and ending on the thirtieth day of November of the year following, both days
inclusive.

### 3.

Establishment of Carriacou Cotton Association
There is hereby established a body corporate called the Carriacou Cotton Association
which in that name shall have power to enter into contracts, to sue and be sued, to
acquire, hold and dispose of all kinds of property both real and personal and, in particular,
to control in accordance with the provisions of this Act the production and marketing of
cotton intended for export.

### 4.

Qualification for membership, etc.
Every grower of cotton shall be eligible to apply for membership of the Association.
Where a corporation or a partnership becomes a member of the Association that member
shall nominate a representative to the Association. The nominated representative may,
subject to the provisions of this Act, exercise on behalf of such member the rights
conferred by this Act on an individual member which cannot conveniently be exercised
by the corporation or partnership itself (including the right to stand for election to
membership of the Board).

### 5.

Power to appoint attorney to attend meetings, etc.
A member may be represented at all meetings of the Board and of the Association by
an attorney on record duly authorised for that purpose who may exercise on behalf of that
member all the rights conferred by this Act on the member as if the member were himself
or herself present.

### 6.

Board to control affairs of Association

(1) The affairs of the Association shall be managed and controlled by a Board to be
called the Cotton Board which shall be the governing and executive body of the
Association and shall consist of not less than seven nor more than nine persons.
(2) The functions of the Board shall be—
(a)

to regulate and control the export of cotton;

(b)

to promote, protect and develop the cotton industry;

(c)

to consider and advise the Minister concerning all matters affecting the
industry as may be referred to the Board by Government, or which the Board
considers should be submitted to Government, or which are referred to the
Board by the Association; and

(d)

to expend and account for all such monies as may be collected by a cess on
the industry, or which may be placed at the disposal of the Board by vote of
the House of Representatives for the purpose of research, marketing or other
service to the industry.

### 7.

Association to have head office, Common Seal and may own or rent warehouses
The Association shall have a head office in the town of Hillsborough and a Common
Seal, and may own or rent warehouses in the said town and such other places within
Grenada as it may deem necessary for the efficient administration of the affairs of the
Association.

### 8.

Provision for use of Common Seal
The Common Seal of the Association shall not be affixed to any instrument except by
the authority of a resolution of the Board and in the presence of at least two members of
the Board and of the secretary who shall sign in their presence every instrument to which
the Seal of the Association is so affixed.

### 9.

Applicant for membership may be required to furnish evidence of qualification
Upon application made by any person to the secretary of the Association for
membership thereof, the applicant may be required to furnish such evidence of
qualification for membership as may reasonably be demanded by the Board.

### 10.

Right of appeal of applicant
A person whose application for membership has been rejected by the Board may,
within fourteen days thereafter, appeal to the magistrate of the district in which the person
resides, and if he or she shall satisfy the magistrate of his or her eligibility for
membership of the Association the magistrate shall make an order directed to the
chairperson requiring that person (upon his or her complying with the regulations
controlling the admission to membership of eligible persons generally) to be admitted a
member of the Association forthwith.

### 11.

Notice of hearing to be given to secretary
Notice of the hearing of the appeal to the magistrate shall be served upon the secretary
not less than seven clear days before the date fixed for the hearing.

### 12.

Chairperson liable to penalty for failing to obey order of magistrate
The chairperson shall be liable to a penalty of fifteen dollars for each day after the day
of receipt by him or her of the order of the magistrate during which he or she shall fail to
admit the applicant to membership of the Association:

Provided that the applicant shall have complied with all conditions precedent with
respect to the admission of eligible persons to membership of the Association.

### 13.

Magistrate’s decision to be final, etc.
The decision of the magistrate shall be final, but an unsuccessful appellant shall be
entitled to submit to the secretary of the Association a fresh application for admission to
membership after the expiration of six months from the date of the Magistrate’s decision.

### 14.

Election and nomination of Board members
(1) In the month of November in each year, on a date to be fixed by the Board notice
whereof shall be published in the Gazette and publicly displayed on a notice board
outside the office of the Association at least one month beforehand, six members of the
Association shall be elected by ballot to be members of the Board for the ensuing cotton
year.
(2) The names of the members elected to the Board shall forthwith be submitted to the
Minister who shall, within fourteen days of such election, nominate a public officer to
serve on the Board. The Minister may also, at any time, nominate not more than two other
persons to serve on the Board.
(3) Every member of the Board shall be eligible for re-election or re-nomination, as
the case may be.

### 15.

On failure to elect members of Board Minister to fill vacancies by nomination
If no election for the proper constitution of the Board shall take place, or if no person
or an insufficient number of persons shall offer themselves for election to the Board or be
elected thereto, the Minister may nominate the members whose election has hereinbefore
been provided for, or as many of such members as shall, with any members elected by the
Association, make up the number required to be elected.

### 16.

Proceedings of Board not invalidated by vacancy
No act or proceeding of the Board shall be invalidated on account of any vacancy in
its membership.

### 17.

Minister may alter constitution of Board
If for any cause it shall be deemed expedient the Minister may, with the consent of the
House of Representatives, declare by proclamation that the constitution of the Board shall
be altered either by increasing or reducing the number of the members thereof:
Provided that any such alteration shall not affect the relative proportions of elected
and nominated members constituting the Board.

### 18.

Disqualification from membership of Board
(1) Subject to subsections (2) and (3), a person shall be disqualified from becoming
and from being a member of the Board if and while he or she—
(a)

is bankrupt; or

(b)

holds an office of profit in the gift or disposal of the Board; or

(c)

has, directly or indirectly, any share or interest in any contract or
employment with, by, or on behalf of the Association:

Provided that a person shall not be so disqualified nor be deemed to have a
share or interest in any such contract or employment by reason only of his or
her being the owner of, or having a share or interest in—
(i) cotton subject to the control of the Association, or
(ii) a newspaper in which an advertisement relating to the affairs of the
Association is published, or
(iii) a company with which the Association contracts for insuring, against
fire, marine or other risk, any property in which the Association shall
have an insurable interest, or
(iv) a company which has entered into contracts with, or done any work
for, the Association.
(2) A member of the Board shall not be disqualified if, being subject only under
subsection (1)(c) to disqualification, it is found by a majority of the other members of the
Board that the share or interest of the member in the contract will be beneficial to the
Association and the making of the contract has been approved by the Cabinet.
(3) For the purposes of subsection (1)(b), the office of chairperson is not an office of
profit within the gift or disposal of the Board.

### 19.

Membership of Board ceases on absence without leave from meetings or from
Grenada
A member of the Board shall ipso facto cease to be a member thereof if he or she is
absent—
(a)

from three consecutive meetings of the Board without leave of the Board; or

(b)

from Grenada for more than one month without such leave.

### 20.

Voting by show of hands
On a show of hands every member present in person shall have one vote only.

### 21.

Mode of voting at general meetings
(1) On all matters to be decided by the votes of members at general meetings the
voting shall be by show of hands, except in the case of the election of members of the
Board.
(2) An illiterate member desiring to vote by ballot shall do so through an official
attestor. The Association shall ensure the presence of an official attestor at all such
meetings.

### 22.

Register of members
Subject to regulations made under this Act with respect to procedure pertaining to the
writing up of registers of members, the secretary shall in the month of August in each
year compile in alphabetical order a register of the names of all members of the
Association and shall place opposite to the name of each member entitled to more than
one vote the number of votes to which that member is entitled. The register shall be
signed by the chairperson and committed to the custody of the secretary. A copy of the
register shall be displayed in the office of the Association in such manner as will enable
all persons interested therein readily to inspect the same.

### 23.

Revision and amendment of register

(1) On or before 15th October in each year the chairperson and two members of the
Board to be nominated by him or her shall meet at the office of the Association and there
revise and amend the register. Notice of the day and hour fixed for such revision and
amendment shall be prominently given on the copy of the register of members displayed
at the office of the Association and the claim of any members whose name does not
appear in the register to be included therein, or of any other person whose name does
appear therein to be excluded therefrom, or of any member with respect to the
incorrectness of the number of votes to which he or she or any other member has therein
been made to appear entitled, shall be heard and determined.
(2) The votes to which a member has been made to appear in the register entitled shall
not be reduced without notice of an intention to do so having been given to him or her
and a reasonable opportunity to be heard thereon afforded him or her.

### 24.

Compulsory general meetings of Association
There shall be at least two general meetings of the Association held in each cotton
year, one of which shall be in the month of November at which members of the Board
shall be elected, and the other at such date, not less than three nor more than nine months
before the date of the meeting held in the month of November as shall be fixed by the
Board. At such meeting the Board shall present a full report of the affairs of the
Association. At least seven clear days before the date fixed for the aforesaid general
meetings of the Association in each cotton year the Board shall provide each member of
the Association with a statement setting out, simply and clearly, the financial position of
the Association and containing reasonable details of its assets and liabilities.

### 25.

Other general meetings of the Association
Other general meetings of the Association may be convened by the Board and shall be
convened upon the request in writing of any number of members in excess of one-fifth of
the total number of members or of twenty-five members, whichever is the less, of the
Association appearing on the last register of members.

### 26.

Provision for convening general meeting in case of refusal or neglect of Board
to do so
If the Board shall refuse to convene a general meeting of the Association upon the
request in writing of the necessary number of members, or (although not refusing) shall
neglect to convene such meeting for fifteen days after receipt of such request in writing, a
general meeting of the Association may be convened by the members making such
request, or by a like number of members who shall together be entitled to a number of
votes at least equal to one-third of the aggregate number of votes to which all members of
the Association appearing on the last register of members shall be entitled.

### 27.

Expulsion of member of Board
If any member of the Board, other than a public officer nominated to the Board by the
Minister, refuses to obey and conform to the rules of the Board it shall be lawful for the
Board after affording such member a full opportunity of explaining his or her case, by
resolution passed by the affirmative votes of not less than five members of the Board, to
expel such member and to declare his or her seat vacant, and the seat of such member
shall thereupon become vacant.

### 28.

Resignation by member of Board and by chairperson
A member of the Board may, by notice in writing to the chairperson, resign his or her
office. The chairperson may resign his or her chairmanship by notice in writing to the
Board.

### 29.

Secretary to publish any vacancy in Board
A notification of the fact and circumstances of any vacancy in the Board shall, in the
case of an elected member, be published by the secretary in the Gazette and in a notice
publicly displayed on a board outside the office of the Association, and in the case of a
nominated member, notified promptly by the secretary to the Minister.

### 30.

Filling of vacancy in Board
(1) Any vacancy in the Board occurring by death, resignation, disqualification or
otherwise shall be filled, in the case of an elected member, by the Board within fourteen
days, or in the case of a nominated member, by the Minister.
(2) If a vacancy occurs so as to leave the Board without a member who is a public
officer, the Minister shall forthwith nominate another public officer for membership of
the Board. The member or public officer elected or nominated under subsection (1) shall
retain his or her seat for no longer than the vacating member would have retained the seat
if no vacancy had occurred.

### 31.

Dissolution of Board
If in the opinion of the Cabinet the Board shall have persistently made default in the
performance of its duties under this Act or any other written law or exceeded or abused
its powers, it shall be lawful for the Minister, by Order published in the Gazette and by a
notice publicly displayed outside the office of the Association, to dissolve the Board.

### 32.

Consequences of dissolution
(1) If the Board is dissolved under section 31, the following consequences shall
ensue, namely—
(a)

members of the Board shall, from the date of such Order, vacate their
offices;

(b)

the powers and duties of the Board shall, until the constitution of a new
Board under this Act, be exercised by such person or persons as the Minister
may appoint in that behalf and any payments made to such person or persons
for his or her or their services shall be a charge upon the funds of the
Association;

(c)

contracts or other obligations lawfully entered into by the Board before its
dissolution shall be carried out, as far as possible, by such person or persons
who shall, for such purposes, be deemed to act as agent of the Association.

(2) No Order for dissolution as aforesaid shall be valid unless it is either made after
the thirty-first day of August in any cotton year or, if made at any other time, unless in
such Order provision is made for the constitution under this Act of a new Board in lieu of
the Board so dissolved within a period not exceeding two months from the date of such
Order.
(3) If an Order for dissolution has been made after the thirty-first day of August in
any cotton year, the person or persons appointed by the Minister under subsection (1)
shall perform the duties of the Board until the end of the year which shall expire on the
thirtieth day of November next after the appointment when the new Board to be
constituted as hereinbefore provided shall come into being.

### 33.

Appointment of chairperson
The chairperson, who shall be a member of the Association, shall be elected by a
majority of the votes of members of the Board at the first meeting thereof after its
election and shall remain in office until the appointment of his or her successor unless he

or she shall previously resign the same or be or become disqualified for membership of
the Board. If the chairperson ceases, for any cause whatsoever, to be chairperson of the
Board during the period for which he or she shall have been elected, the Board shall
proceed to elect another member thereof to be chairperson who, when elected, shall hold
office for the remainder of the time for which his or her predecessor would have held the
same if he or she had not ceased to be chairperson.

### 34.

Chairperson of Board to be President of Association
The chairperson shall be, ex officio, President of the Association and shall receive
such salary as may be determined by the Board with the approval of the Minister to be
paid from the funds of the Association.

### 35.

Retiring chairperson eligible for re-election
A retiring chairperson shall be eligible for re-election.

### 36.

Duties of chairperson
The chairperson shall preside at every meeting of the Board and at general meetings of
the Association and shall have such powers and perform such other duties as may, from
time to time, be assigned to him or her by rules made by the Board.

### 37.

Chairperson to have casting vote
The chairperson shall have an original vote and, in the case of an equal division of
votes on any question before the Board or the Association, a second or casting vote.

### 38.

Deputy chairperson
The Board may, from time to time, appoint any member thereof to act as deputy
chairperson during the illness or absence of the chairperson or during any vacancy in the
office of chairperson. A deputy chairperson shall, while acting as such, have all the
powers and perform all the duties of the chairperson.

### 39.

Meetings of the Board
A meeting of the Board shall be held within the first fifteen days of the month of
December in each year, and thereafter the Board shall meet at intervals not exceeding two
months at such times as shall be fixed by rules made by the Board.

### 40.

Convening of special meetings of Board
If any three members of the Board, or any number of members in excess of one-fifth
of the total number of members or of twenty-five members (whichever is the less) of the
Association appearing on the last register, shall present to the chairperson a request in
writing that a meeting of the Board be convened for the consideration of some particular
matter, it shall be the duty of the chairperson to cause a meeting of the Board to be
promptly convened.

### 41.

Members may convene special meeting
If, after a request as provided by section 40, the chairperson refuses to convene a
meeting of the Board or if (although not refusing) he or she does not, within seven days
after the receipt of such request in writing, cause a meeting of the Board to be convened,
any three members of the Board or number of members of the Association as aforesaid
may forthwith convene a meeting of the Board.

### 42.

Adjournment of meetings
The Board may adjourn its meetings from time to time.

### 43.

Quorum
Any four members of the Board shall form a quorum for the transaction of business.

### 44.

Questions before Board to be decided by majority of votes
Every question before the Board shall be decided by a majority of the votes of
members of the Board present. Every member of the Board, other than the chairperson,
shall have one vote.

### 45.

Non-participation of member with pecuniary interest
No member of the Board shall vote upon or take part in the decision of any matter
before the Board in which he or she has, directly or indirectly, a pecuniary interest.

### 46.

Minutes to be received in evidence
The minutes of the proceedings at a meeting of the Board or of a committee thereof
signed at the same or the next ensuing meeting by the chairperson, or by a member of the
committee describing himself or herself as or appearing to be chairperson thereof, shall
be received in evidence without further proof in all courts and for all other purposes
whatsoever.

### 47.

Presumption of regularity
Until the contrary has been proved, every meeting of the Board or of a committee
thereof, in respect of whose proceedings minutes have been so signed shall be deemed to
have been duly convened and held, and all members present at the meeting shall be
deemed to have been duly qualified; and where the proceedings are those of a committee,
the committee shall be deemed to have been duly constituted and to have had power to
deal with the matter referred to in the minutes.

### 48.

Power of Board to make rules
(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Board may, from time to time, make
rules relating to all or any of the following matters—
(a)

the time and place of its meetings and of the general meetings of the
Association;

(b)

the mode of convening its meetings and the general meetings of the
Association;

(c)

the attendance of its members;

(d)

the form and order of its debates and of the debates at the general meetings
of the Association;

(e)

the powers and duties of its staff; and

(f)

the general regulation of proceedings of the Board (including committees
thereof) and of the Association, and the mode of conducting the business of
the Board and of the Association.

(2) The rules shall not be made or amended unless special notice of the intention to do
so has been given at a meeting of the Board held at least seven days before that at which
the making or amendment is proposed, and unless special notice as aforesaid has been
transmitted to the members of the Board at least three days before such meeting.

(3) The making or amendment of rules which relate to the meetings of the
Association or which purport to regulate the proceedings of the Association shall not be
binding upon the Association until the same shall have been confirmed by a general
meeting of the Association held not less than fourteen days after the making or
amendment of those rules by the Board and then only if special notice of the making or
amendment shall have been given in the notice convening that general meeting or to the
members of the Association at least three clear days before the day of the meeting.

### 49.

Association to be sole purchaser of cotton
(1) It shall not be lawful for any person to purchase cotton in Grenada except for
domestic use or, under permit from the Board, for other approved purposes.
(2) Except as aforesaid, any person who buys cotton and any person who sells cotton
to any person except for domestic use by the purchaser, or under permit by the Board for
other approved purposes, shall be guilty of an offence against this Act.

### 50.

Sale of cotton abroad
Notwithstanding the provisions of section 51, the Board may engage the services of
any person upon such terms as may be agreed between the Board and such person for the
purpose of effecting the sale of cotton abroad.

### 51.

Association to be sole exporter of cotton
(1) The Association shall be the sole exporter of cotton.
(2) Any person who exports cotton in contravention of the provisions of this Act shall
be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of five thousand dollars, and on a second or
subsequent conviction to a fine of seven thousand dollars and to imprisonment for six
months.

### 52.

Board may engage agents
Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act, the Board may, by contract, engage
agents to carry out on its behalf any of its duties in connection with the collection,
storage, processing and packing of cotton, and for payment on its behalf of any monies as
advances or otherwise.

### 53.

Board may appoint representatives in markets abroad
Notwithstanding anything contained in this Act, the Board may, by contract, appoint
representatives in markets abroad.

### 54.

Advances against cotton delivered
The Association shall advance to its members and other persons who shall deliver
cotton to the Association such proportion of the value of the cotton delivered as shall,
from time to time, be determined by the Board. More than one advance may be made at
the discretion of the Board in respect of deliveries of cotton to the Association during any
cotton year.

### 55.

Board to fix value of cotton
The Board shall as frequently as it may deem necessary determine and fix the values
of cotton for the purposes of this Act and the respective proportions of such values which
may be advanced by the Association to persons delivering cotton to the Association. The
values of cotton and the proportions of such values to be advanced, as determined and

fixed by the Board, shall be publicly displayed on a notice board outside the office of the
Association and shall be conclusive.

### 56.

Cotton delivered to become property of Association
(1) All cotton delivered to the Association shall become the property of the
Association, and the rights of the owner and of all other persons claiming through, under
or against the owner shall be none other than a right to receive such sums, if any, as shall
be payable by the Association under this Act to the persons entitled to receive the same.
(2) Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (1), the delivery of cotton to the
Association by members or other persons shall be deemed not to be a sale of licensable
produce within the meaning of the Sale of Produce Act, Chapter 296.
(3) Every member or other person when making a delivery of cotton to the
Association shall produce a book called a Cotton Producer’s Book in which the
prescribed particulars shall be entered by the Association.
(4) Subject to subsection (6), such book shall be issued by the Permanent Secretary
(Finance) to every applicant who pays a fee of ten cents and presents to the Permanent
Secretary (Finance) a statement signed by him or her containing particulars of—
(a)

the description, area and situation of the applicant’s cotton cultivation; and

(b)

the estimated annual production thereof.

(5) The particulars required by subsection (4) shall be entered by the Permanent
Secretary (Finance) on the inside cover of the book.
(6) Every Cotton Producer’s Book shall be in the form specified in the Schedule and a
register of such books shall be kept by the Permanent Secretary (Finance) in such form as
he or she may think proper. No such book shall be supplied to any cotton producer unless
the existing book is returned to the Permanent Secretary (Finance) for cancellation, or
unless the producer has—
(a)

presented to the Permanent Secretary (Finance) a true statement signed by
him or her of the fact that such book has been lost or destroyed; or

(b)

otherwise satisfied the Permanent Secretary (Finance) that such is the case;
and

(c)

paid a fee of twenty-five cents for a replacement book.

(7) Any officer of the Association or police officer or any person appointed by the
Permanent Secretary (Finance) may demand the production of a Cotton Producer’s Book
and may inspect such book and make a copy of any entry therein and, if he or she thinks
it necessary, may retain such book.
(8) Any person who shall fail or neglect to produce such book when lawfully required
to do so or who makes use of a book not his or her own, or not issued by the Permanent
Secretary (Finance), or who refuses to allow a copy of any entry in such book to be taken,
shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of one hundred and fifty dollars and in
default of payment thereof to imprisonment for one month.
(9) Any person who on application for a new book under the provisions of
subsection (6) signs a false statement shall be guilty of an offence against this Act.

### 57.

Appointment of committee by Board
(1) The Board may appoint from its own membership such and so many committees
consisting of such number of persons as it may think fit for the exercise of any powers of
an administrative character which, in the opinion of the Board, can be properly exercised
by committees, and the actions of any such committee shall be reported and submitted to
the Board for its approval.

(2) Nothing in this section authorises the Board to delegate to a committee the power
to fix any toll to be levied upon cotton for export.

### 58.

Actions of committee not invalidated by vacancy
No action or proceeding of a committee shall be invalidated on account of any
vacancy.

### 59.

Rules regulating meetings of Board to apply to meetings of committees
Any rules made for the regulation of meetings of the Board shall, subject to any
special rules for committee meetings made by the Board, apply as far as possible to
meetings of committees.

### 60.

Non-participation of member of committee with pecuniary interest
No member of a committee shall vote upon, or take part in the discussion of, any
matter in which he or she has, directly or indirectly, a pecuniary interest.

### 61.

Public officer on Board to be member of finance committee
If the Board shall appoint a finance committee the public officer nominated to the
Board by the Minister shall be a member of such committee, and if there be more than
one such officer on the Board the officer first appointed or first named in the list of
nominations by the Minister shall be the member of such committee:
Provided that the Minister may signify his or her desire that such officer shall not be
a member of the committee or that some other public officer on the Board shall be a
member of the committee instead of the officer first nominated or first listed in which
case the Board shall act in no way that controverts the Minister’s desire.

### 62.

Board may appoint manager
(1) The Board may appoint a fit and proper person to be manager at such salary and
upon such terms as to his or her powers, duties and functions and on such conditions as
the Minister may approve.
(2) The manager shall be the chief executive officer of the Association. He or she
shall exercise such powers and perform such functions and duties as are required of him
or her by the Board and, in particular, he or she shall attend all meetings of the
Association and, if required by the chairperson of the Board to do so, the meetings of the
Board and of every committee of the Board.

### 63.

Board to appoint secretary of Association subject to approval of the Minister
(1) The Board shall, from time to time and subject to the approval of the Minister,
appoint a fit and proper person to be secretary of the Association and such person shall
hold office during the pleasure of the Minister and of the Board.
(2) Notwithstanding the dissolution or other the determination of the Board, the
secretary shall continue to hold office until his or her employment shall be terminated by
the Board at its own pleasure or in pursuance of a request in that behalf made by the
Minister.

### 64.

Salary of secretary
The secretary shall receive such salary as may be determined by the Board, with the
approval of the Minister; the salary shall be paid from the funds of the Association.

### 65.

When secretary to be chief executive officer
Whenever the office of manager is vacant, the secretary shall be the chief executive
officer of the Association. He or she may exercise the powers conferred upon and shall
perform the functions and duties required of the manager under section 62 and, in
particular, he or she shall attend all meetings of the Association and of the Board and of
every committee of the Board and shall keep minutes of the proceedings at every such
meeting.

### 66.

Secretary to be treasurer of the Association
The secretary shall be ex officio treasurer of the Association.

### 67.

Appointment of assistant secretary
The Board may, with the consent of the Minister, appoint some fit and proper person
to be assistant secretary who shall hold office during the pleasure of the Board and of the
Minister, and the Board shall, from time to time, define the duties of the assistant
secretary.

### 68.

Assistant secretary to have like powers as secretary
All things required or authorised by law or by any regulation or order of the Board to
be done by the secretary may be done by the assistant secretary.

### 69.

Remuneration of assistant secretary
The assistant secretary, whilst acting as the secretary, may receive such remuneration
(to be drawn from the salary of the secretary or paid from the funds of the
Association) as the Board shall determine.

### 70.

Board may appoint staff
The Board may from time to time appoint such staff as is required for the efficient
administration of the affairs of the Association. Such staff shall receive such salary as is
determined, and shall perform such duties as are from time to time assigned, by the
Board; and such staff shall hold office during the pleasure of the Board.

### 71.

Staff may be required to furnish security
The Board may require any or all staff appointed under section 70 of this Act to give
such security as may be thought proper for the true and faithful performance of the duties
of, and for the due accounting for, paying over and delivery of all monies, goods and
chattels which may be received by virtue of, their employment by the Association.

### 72.

Staff to be appointed on monthly contract
Staff of the Association shall not be entitled to claim more than one month’s notice of
termination of employment and the Board shall have no power to offer or agree a longer
period of notice or to make provision for pension or compassionate allowance except to
such extent as is authorised by the Minister with the approval of the House of
Representatives.

### 73.

Officers and staff having pecuniary interest to report fact to Board
If the manager or the secretary or any member of the staff of the Association
appointed by the Board shall, directly or indirectly, have any pecuniary interest in any
agreement or contract entered into by the Association, or any matter affecting the

Association, he or she shall report the circumstances of such pecuniary interest to the
Board through the chairperson as soon as practicable after he or she becomes aware of his
or her having such interest, and in default of his or her so reporting he or she shall be
liable to summary dismissal from his or her employment with the Association.

### 74.

Staff to account
Every member of the staff appointed by the Board shall give (at such times during the
continuance of his or her office, or within ten days after his or her ceasing to hold it, and
in such manner as the Board may direct) a true account in writing of all matters
committed to his or her charge, and of his or her receipts and payments with vouchers
therefor.

### 75.

Staff to pay monies to secretary, etc.
(1) Every member of the staff of the Association shall pay all monies due from him or
her to the secretary or as the Board may otherwise direct.
(2) If any member of the staff—
(a)

refuses or neglects to deliver an account which he or she ought to deliver or
a voucher relating thereto or to make payment which he or she ought to
make; or

(b)

after three days notice in writing signed by the chairperson and given or left
at his or her usual or last known place of abode refuses or neglects to deliver
to the secretary or as the Board may otherwise direct, anything whatsoever
which he or she ought to deliver or to give satisfaction respecting it to the
Board or as he or she may be directed, the magistrate of the district may on
the complaint of the chairperson, or any other member of the Board, by
summary order require him or her to make such delivery or payment or to
give such satisfaction, and if he or she shall fail to make such delivery or
payment or to give such satisfaction, he or she shall be guilty of an offence
against this Act.

(3) Nothing in this section shall affect any remedy by action against a member of the
staff or his or her surety, except that he or she shall not be both proceeded against
summarily under this section and sued in proceedings in any court for the same cause.

### 76.

Power to make regulations
It shall be lawful for the Association to make and amend regulations for the good
management of the affairs of the Association generally, and in particular with respect
to—
(a)

regulating the export of cotton by the Association and the marketing of the
same;

(b)

the registration of a trade mark and governing the use thereof;

(c)

prescribing the manner in which the accounts of the Association shall be
kept;

(d)

controlling the finances of the Association and regulating the time at and the
terms under which advances and payments to members and other persons
shall be made;

(e)

prescribing the conditions of membership of the Association including the
payment of fees on admission to membership and periodically thereafter;
and

(f)

regulating and controlling every other matter within the scope of the
operations of the Association whether the same shall be of the same kind as
any of the matters hereinbefore particularly mentioned or otherwise.

### 77.

Power of Association to levy and collect toll
The Association shall have power to levy and collect, and when levied to alter by
increasing or decreasing the amount thereof, a toll on all cotton exported and to charge
the same against the owner of any cotton delivered to the Association or, when a licence
to export cotton shall have been given to any person by the Board, to recover the same
from the person to whom such licence has been given or to demand from him or her
payment of the same as a condition precedent to the granting of such licence. The amount
of the toll shall, from time to time, be determined by the Board but the same or any
altered amount shall not be levied or collected until approved by the Association at a
general meeting and by the Minister. When determining the amount of the toll the Board
shall have regard to the requirements of the Association for meeting current expenses, for
the payment of interest, for the creation of a sinking fund, for the repayment of monies
loaned to the Association, and for the creation of a reserve fund:
Provided that the reserve fund, or any part thereof may from time to time, at the
discretion of the Board, be used for the purpose of price stabilisation.

### 78.

Funds of the Association
The funds of the Association shall consist of the subscriptions of members of the
Association and of all monies received by the Association in pursuance of any toll levied
upon exported cotton and of any monies granted to the Association by Parliament, by the
Government or by any body authorised by the British Government to make grants for
overseas development, and of monies donated or bequeathed to the Association and of all
monies payable to the Association whether under any of the provisions of this Act or
otherwise.

### 79.

Power of Association to negotiate instruments
The Association shall have power to draw, make, accept, endorse, discount, execute,
issue and negotiate bills of exchange, bills of lading, cheques, orders and other negotiable
or transferable instruments for the purpose of or in connection with the affairs of the
Association.

### 80.

Borrowing powers of Association
The Association may, with the consent of the Minister, borrow money for the carrying
out of its purposes and may charge in any manner its property, funds and revenues with
the repayment of such borrowing.

### 81.

Inspection and audit of accounts
(1) The Board shall, immediately after the end of each cotton year, cause the accounts
of the Association and of the Board to be audited by some competent person approved for
the purpose by the Minister.
(2) The audit under subsection (1) of this section shall include an examination of
overdue debts (if any) and a valuation of the assets and liabilities of the Association.
(3) Notwithstanding subsection (1), the books of account of the Association and all
monies, documents and things pertaining to the Association in the custody or under the
control of the Association or of the Board or of any member of the staff of the
Association shall be liable to inspection at any time and audit by the Director of Audit in
like manner as if such books of account, monies, documents and things were in the

custody or under the control of a public officer in his or her official capacity, or by any
other auditor as the Minister may direct.

### 82.

Board to furnish statements half-yearly
The Board shall furnish to the Minister at the end of each half-year a statement of the
financial position and stock of the Association in such form as the Minister may, from
time to time, direct.

### 83.

Minister may give directions to Board
The Minister may after consultation with the Board give directions of a general
character to the Board as to the exercise and performance of its functions in relation to
matters appearing to the Cabinet to concern the public interest, and the Board shall give
effect to any such directions.

### 84.

Penalty for offences
A person convicted of an offence against this Act or against any regulations made
under section 76 shall, if no other penalty is hereinbefore provided, be liable to a fine of
five hundred dollars and, in default of payment, to imprisonment for five months.

### 85.

Institution of proceedings against offenders
Every proceeding instituted against an offender may be taken in the name and at the
instance of the secretary or of some other person authorised by the Board, or in the name
and at the instance of the Chief of Police.

### 86.

Hearing of proceedings against offenders
Proceedings for an offence against this Act and the regulations shall be heard and
determined by a magistrate as an offence punishable on summary conviction.

### 87.

No court fees, etc., payable in respect of proceedings
No court fees or stamp duty shall be payable in the first instance in respect of any
proceedings taken against a person under this Act.

### 88.

Court may order either party to pay full costs
A court may order either party to pay full costs or, if the court convicting a defendant
orders him or her to pay a fine but does not order payment of the costs, the fine shall be
applied in payment of the costs or, as the case may be, such an amount as will pay for the
costs shall be deducted therefrom.

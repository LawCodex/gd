## CHAPTER 194C

MINISTERIAL COMPLEX (FINANCING) ACT
An Act to provide for the financing of the Ministerial Complex, St. George’s, and for
matters connected therewith and incidental thereto.
[Act No. 18 of 2000.]

[1st January, 2000.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Ministerial Complex (Financing) Act.
2\. Definitions
In this Act—
“lease” means the lease, as contained in the First Schedule, between His
Excellency Sir Daniel Charles Williams the Governor-General and the Botanical
Gardens Project Corporation Limited with respect to land situate at the Botanical
Gardens, St. George’s;
“Ministerial Complex” means the office building complex on a parcel of land
situate at the Botanical Gardens, St. George’s and containing by admeasurement

---

### 1.

5349 hectares;
“Project Company” means the Botanical Gardens Project Corporation Limited
incorporated under the Laws of Grenada whose registered office is situate at NCB
House, Grand Anse, St. George’s;
“sub-lease” means the sub-lease, as contained in the Second Schedule between the
Botanical Gardens Project Corporation Limited and His Excellency Sir Daniel Charles
Williams, Governor-General, with respect to land situate at the Botanical Gardens, St.
George’s.

### 3.

Authorisation to execute sub-lease
(1) For the purpose of financing the ownership of the Ministerial Complex the
Government is hereby authorised to enter into a lease and sub-lease arrangement with the
Project Company hereunder the Complex will be sub-leased to the Government for a
period of twenty years in consideration of payments set out in the Second Schedule to the
sub-lease.
(2) The lease and sub-lease shall not be executed unless the Cabinet approves such
execution.

### 4.

Charge on the Consolidated Fund
All monies payable by the Government pursuant to the sub-lease between the Project
Company and the Government with respect to the Ministerial Complex shall be a charge
on the Consolidated Fund.

### 5.

Commencement
This Act shall be deemed to have come into operation on 1st January, 2000.

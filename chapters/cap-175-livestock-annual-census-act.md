## CHAPTER 175

LIVESTOCK (ANNUAL CENSUS) ACT
An Act to provide for the taking of an annual census of livestock.
[27th June, 1953.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Livestock (Annual Census) Act.

### 2.

Application
This Act applies to the whole of Grenada or to such part or parts thereof as the
Minister responsible for agriculture may, by Order, direct.

### 3.

Return of livestock
(1) Annually, on or before the last day of March, the owner of livestock shall furnish
to the Chief Technical Officer (Extension) a return, in the form in the Schedule, setting
out the number of livestock kept at the last day of the immediately preceding year and
their increase during that year.
(2) The form shall be signed by the owner of the livestock together with a declaration
that the statements contained in the return are to the best of his or her knowledge and
belief true and accurate.
(3) If the owner of the livestock is absent from Grenada, his or her attorney or agent
in Grenada shall furnish the return.
(4) The Minister may, from time to time, by Order, substitute any other day for the
last day of March for furnishing the return and may in the like manner substitute or vary
the form contained in the Schedule, and in that case the return shall give the information
required for the twelve months ending one calendar month before the date of the return,
but the Minister may from time to time prescribe a different period and also call for a
special return covering any specified period.

(5) Printed forms of every return required to be furnished under this Act shall on
request be supplied by the Chief Technical Officer (Extension) to owners or their
attorneys or agents.

### 4.

Power of entry
The Chief Technical Officer (Extension) or a person authorised by him or her in that
behalf, may enter between 6 a.m. and 6 p.m. upon any land after giving three days
previous notice in writing to the occupier of that land, or his or her attorney or agent, and
may count and inspect the livestock thereon.

### 5.

Offences
(1) A person owning twenty head of livestock or upwards who refuses or wilfully
neglects to furnish to the Chief Technical Officer (Extension) within the time prescribed
by this Act any return hereby required duly filled up so far as he or she is able to do so
shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of one hundred and fifty dollars, and shall
be liable to a like fine for each period of seven days during which the default continues
after the conviction, and a person owning less than twenty head of livestock shall in like
case be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of fifty dollars.
(2) A person wilfully making a false statement in any return shall be guilty of an
offence and liable to a fine of five hundred dollars.
(3) A person who obstructs the Chief Technical Officer (Extension) or a person
authorised by him or her in that behalf while acting under section 4 shall be guilty of an
offence and liable to a fine of two hundred and fifty dollars.

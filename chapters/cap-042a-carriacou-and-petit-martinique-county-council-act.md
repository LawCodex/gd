## CHAPTER 42A

CARRIACOU AND PETIT MARTINIQUE COUNTY COUNCIL ACT
An Act to constitute Carriacou and Petit Martinique a County and to provide
for the establishment, constitution, powers, functions and duties of a
Council for the County as an organ of Local Government.
[Act No. 20 of 1995.]

[5th April, 1995.]

## PART I

Preliminary

Short title

This Act may be cited as the Carriacou and Petit Martinique County Council Act.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“Carriacou and Petit Martinique County” means the islands of Carriacou and Petit
Martinique and the neighbouring islets lying to the north of the parallel of twelve
degrees and twenty minutes of north latitude, and included within the State; which
islands and islets shall for purposes of Local Government be called a County;
“Council” means the Carriacou and Petit Martinique County Council established
by this Act;
“County” means the Carriacou and Petit Martinique County established by this
Act;
“Minister” means the Minister for the time being charged with responsibility for
the subject of Local Government;
“State” means the State of Grenada, including Carriacou and Petit Martinique;
“Zonal Agency” means the Agency constituted by this Act to help provide for
Local Government in the island of Petit Martinique.

## PART II

Establishment of County and Division of County into Wards

3\.

Establishment of County

(1) For the purposes of this Act, the District of Carriacou and Petit Martinique
established by section 3 of the District Councils Act 1995 with the boundaries referred
to by sections 3, 4 and 5 of that Act, is hereby constituted a County.
(2) The Carriacou and Petit Martinique County shall comprise such number of
Wards in the County as may be prescribed under and in accordance with sections 3, 4
and 5 of the District Councils Act 1995 as if for this purpose the County were a District
within the meaning of those sections of that Act.

## PART III

Establishment of the Carriacou and Petit Martinique County Council
4\.

Establishment of Carriacou and Petit Martinique County Council

(1) A body corporate to be called the “Carriacou and Petit Martinique County
Council” is hereby established and constituted in and for the Carriacou and Petit
Martinique County as a local government authority with the powers, functions and
duties provided for in this Act.
(2) The Carriacou and Petit Martinique Council established by and under
subsection (1) shall be entitled to make contracts and to acquire, hold and dispose of
all kinds of property real and personal in its corporate name the “Carriacou and Petit
Martinique County Council” and shall have and use a common seal and by and in its
said corporate name may sue and be sued in all Courts of Law without the licence of
the Minister. The official seal of the Corporation shall be judicially noticed.

Membership of County Council

(1) The membership of the Carriacou and Petit Martinique County Council shall be
as follows—

(i) The Chairperson;
(ii) The elected Councillors—
(a)

One (1) each elected for each of the Wards on the island of
Carriacou by the voters of that Ward, and

(b)

Two (2) elected for the Ward on the island of Petit Martinique by the
voters of that Ward;

(iii) The co-opted Councillors—
(a)

Two (2) chosen by the elected Councillors from the island of
Carriacou in secret ballot, and

(b)

One (1) chosen by the elected Councillors from the island of Petit
Martinique in secret ballot; and

(iv) The Member of the House of Representatives elected to represent the
constituency of Carriacou and Petit Martinique County under the
Representation of the People Act, Chapter 286A, or his or her nominee.
(2) The elected Councillors shall be elected in local government elections
conducted in accordance with elections referred to in sections 6(1) and 7 of this Act.
(3) If no one is elected to represent a Ward in the elections referred to in
subsection (2), the Minister may select from that Ward a person to serve as the elected
Councillor for that Ward, and the person so selected by the Minister shall for all intents
and purposes be the elected Councillor for that Ward. If any of the two persons to be
elected to represent Petit Martinique is not elected, the Minister may select from Petit
Martinique a person to serve in that post, and the person so selected by the Minister
shall for all intents and purposes be an elected Councillor for Petit Martinique. In
making such a selection, the Minister shall act fairly, and shall consult both the
Member of the House of Representatives elected to represent the constituency of
Carriacou and Petit Martinique and the Leader of the Opposition in the House of
Representatives.
(4) The elected Councillors of the County may, for the purpose of assisting them in
the carrying out of their responsibilities, co-opt the Councillors referred to in clause (ii)
of subsection (1), which co-opted Councillors may be public officers.
6\.

Term of office

(1) Elected and co-opted Councillors shall hold office for four years, the elected
Councillors of each Council being elected in accordance with elections referred to in
section 18 of the District Councils Act, Chapter 83A:
Provided that Councillors shall be eligible to be re-elected or to be co-opted again.
(2) A person who is a member of the Council by virtue of section 5(1)(iv) of this
Act shall cease to be such member immediately he or she ceases to represent the
relevant constituency in the House of Representatives.

## PART IV

Qualifications and Disqualifications of County Councillors
7\. Qualification and disqualification of members, expulsion, resignation,
recall, elections, etc.

## Part IV, comprising sections 10 to 21, of the District Councils Act 1995, governing

District Councils, shall apply mutatis mutandis to the County Council established by
and under this Act as if this County Council were a District Council, in relation to
qualification of members, disqualification of members, members’ seats being vacated
in certain events, statement and declarations by members regarding their qualification,
the power of the Council to expel its members, resignation of members, the recall of

Councillors, notification of vacancies in the Council, the time of elections to the
Council, the swearing in of Councillors with their taking and subscribing the oath or
affirmation of office, and occasional elections to the Council.

## PART V

Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson

Functions of Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson
(1) The Council shall have a Chairperson and a Deputy Chairperson.

(2) The Chairperson shall preside at every meeting of the Council at which he or
she is present. He or she shall also have such other duties as may, from time to time,
be assigned to him or her by the Council.
(3) The Chairperson shall have an original vote and in case of an equal division of
votes on any question before the Council, he or she shall have a second or casting
vote.
(4) In the temporary absence or inability of the Chairperson to perform his or her
functions through illness or otherwise, the Deputy Chairperson shall perform those
functions, including the presiding at meetings of the Council. In the temporary absence
or inability of both the Chairperson and the Deputy Chairperson, such other elected
member of the Council as may be appointed by the Council for that purpose shall
perform those functions.
(5) The Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson may be granted leave of absence by
the Council for any period not exceeding six (6) weeks in the aggregate in any
calendar year in the case of the Chairperson and not exceeding four (4) weeks in the
aggregate in any calendar year in the case of the Deputy Chairperson.
9\.

Election and term of office of Chairperson

(1) The Chairperson shall be elected directly by the people in the County in
accordance with elections referred to in sections 6(1) and 7 of this Act.
(2) The qualification and disqualification of the Chairperson shall be the same as
those applying to an elected Councillor, referred to in section 7 of this Act.
(3) The elections for Chairperson shall be on the same day and at the same time
as elections for the elected Councillors.
(4) The term of office of the Chairperson shall be four (4) years, to coincide with
the term of office of the elected Councillors but the holder of the office shall be eligible
for re-election:
Provided always that no person shall serve as Chairperson for more than two terms
in succession.
10\.

Election and term of office of Deputy Chairperson

(1) At the meeting for the swearing in of the elected Councillors referred to in
section 7 of this Act, and immediately after the elected Councillors take and subscribe
the oath or affirmation of office referred to in that section, the elected Councillors shall
elect out of their number a Deputy Chairperson.
(2) A person who is a member of the Council by virtue of section 5(1)(iii) of this
Act shall be disqualified from taking part and voting in the electing of the Deputy
Chairperson, and shall not be eligible to be elected Deputy Chairperson.
(3) The term of office of the Deputy Chairperson shall be one (1) year but the
holder of the office shall be eligible for re-election:

Provided always that no person shall serve as Deputy Chairperson for more than
two (2) years in succession.

Occasional vacancy in office of Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson

(1) An occasional vacancy in the office of the Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson
shall be deemed to have occurred under this section where—
(a)

The Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson dies, or ceases to be a member of
the Council or his or her office as member is declared to be vacant for
disqualification or otherwise;

(b)

The Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson notifies in writing to the Council
and the Minister through the Commissioner his or her resignation as
Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson;

(c)

The Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson is granted leave of absence by the
Council;

(d)

The Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson departs from the State without
leave from the Council:
Provided that if the Council refuses such leave to the Chairperson he or
she may apply to the Commissioner for such leave and it shall be
competent for the Commissioner to grant the Chairperson such leave; or

(e)

The Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson is voted out of office by a majority
of three-fourths of all the elected members of the Council in the case of
the Chairperson or by a majority of the elected members present and
voting in the case of the Deputy Chairperson in either case on the ground
that the respective number of members have lost confidence in the
Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson, as the case may be, voting
accordingly by secret ballot conducted in accordance with subsection (6).

(2) In the event of the death, resignation, disqualification for any cause of the
Chairperson or other occasional vacancy in the office of Chairperson, the Deputy
Chairperson shall forthwith succeed to the office of Chairperson, and shall continue in
such office until the time when the Chairperson whom he or she succeeds would have
gone out of office, and he or she shall then go out of office:
Provided that where the occasional vacancy arises on account of the Chairperson
being granted leave of absence by the Council under section 8(5) of this Act, the
Deputy Chairperson shall hold the office of Chairperson only until such time as the
Chairperson who was granted leave of absence by the Council has returned from such
leave and has notified the Minister in writing through the Commissioner that he or she
has resumed the office of Chairperson:
Provided further that if a vacancy arises in the office of Chairperson for any reason
before the end of the third year of the term of the holder of that office, an election for
Chairperson shall be held within forty-five days of the vacancy so arising, but
meanwhile the Deputy Chairperson shall act as Chairperson.
(3) The Chairperson may resign his or her office as Chairperson at any time by
notifying the Council and the Minister through the Commissioner that he or she has
resigned as Chairperson. Such resignation letter must be sent within two (2) days of
the resignation, and it takes effect upon receipt by the Council.
(4) If the Chairperson departs from Grenada without the leave of the Council, the
Deputy Chairperson shall forthwith succeed to the office of Chairperson in accordance
with subsection (1).
(5) In the event of the death, resignation, disqualification for any cause of the
Deputy Chairperson, or other occasional vacancy in the office of Deputy Chairperson,
another elected member shall, in accordance with section 10 of this Act, be elected as
Deputy Chairperson, and such newly elected Deputy Chairperson shall hold office for
the unexpired portion of the term in relation to which the occasional vacancy has
occurred:

Provided that where the occasional vacancy arises on account of the Deputy
Chairperson being granted leave of absence by the Council under section 8(5) of this
Act, such newly elected Deputy Chairperson shall hold the office of Deputy Chairperson
only until such time as the Deputy Chairperson who was granted leave of absence by
the Council has returned from such leave and has notified the Minister in writing
through the Commissioner that he or she has resumed the office of Deputy
Chairperson.
(6) The Council may remove the Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson if it has lost
confidence in him or her, as provided for in subsection (1)(e):
Provided that, subject to this section, the Council adheres to the relevant provisions
of Part VI of the District Councils Act, 1995, in particular section 29(5) thereof and:
Provided further that proceedings for the removal of the Chairperson or Deputy
Chairperson under this subsection shall be chaired by the Commissioner.
12\.

If no nomination for Chairperson

If there is no nomination for Chairperson under section 9 of this Act, the elected
Councillors shall elect out of their number a Chairperson, and section 11(1) and (2) of
this Act shall apply, mutatis mutandis, to such election.
13\.

Oath to be taken by Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson

The Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson shall, before assuming office as such,
take and subscribe the oath or affirmation of office specified in section 87 of the
District Councils Act 1995 and applied mutatis mutandis by section 27(1) of this Act to
the County Council established by and under this Act. The Chairperson and Deputy
Chairperson shall not assume office as such without taking and subscribing such oath
or affirmation of office.
14\.

Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson to be Justices of the Peace

(1) The Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson shall be ex officio Justices of the
Peace for the State and, unless disqualified for re-election to their respective offices,
shall continue as Justices of the Peace during the year next after they cease to be
Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson respectively.
(2) The disqualification referred to in subsection (1) of this section shall not include
disqualification due to ineligibility for re-election as Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson
by reason only of any person having served as Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson for
four successive years.
(3) Before acting as Justices of the Peace, the Chairperson and Deputy Chairperson
shall take the oaths required by law to be taken by such justices unless, on the day on
which they become entitled to act as Chairperson or Deputy Chairperson, they have
already taken the oaths required by law to be taken to act as such justices.
(4) That an elected member of the Council serves temporarily as Chairperson or
Deputy Chairperson under section 8(4) of this Act does not entitle him or her to be an
ex officio Justice of the Peace under this section.
15\.

Exemption for jury service

The Chairperson, Deputy Chairperson and other members of the Council shall,
during their terms of office, be exempted from jury service.

## PART VI

Powers and Functions of the Council

General powers of administration

The Council is hereby authorised to take decisions and implement measures in
relation to the County to—
(i) improve and maintain roads, public buildings, jetties and harbour
facilities;
(ii) regulate the provision of electricity, water services and other public utilities;
(iii) promote hotel and tourist development;
(iv) administer agricultural, forestry and fisheries services;
(v) administer public health and sanitation services;
(vi) provide and maintain social services;
(vii) administer, upon the delegation of the Central Government and on an
agency basis, postal services in the Carriacou and Petit Martinique County
upon terms agreed between the Council and the Central Government.
17\.

Council entitled to share in the control of education

The Council shall be entitled to share in the control of education in the County while
education remains a responsibility of the Central Government. In particular, in relation
to the County, the Council shall be able to—
(i) inspect school premises and equipment;
(ii) improve school facilities and equipment;
(iii) care for school facilities and equipment;
(iv) assist the administration of schools with maintaining discipline; and
(v) provide scholarships.
18\.

County Police unit

(1) The Council may establish a Police Unit to serve the County and to be called
the Carriacou and Petit Martinique County Police Unit.
(2) Members of the said County Police Unit shall be eligible for transfer to the
Royal Grenada Police Force.
(3) The said County Police Unit shall have its own immediate commanding officer
in the County, but shall ultimately fall under the control of the Commissioner of Police
in the Royal Grenada Police Force.
(4) The services to be rendered by the said County Police Unit shall be paid for by
the County out of its own ordinary revenue.
19\.

Council to have all the powers of a district council

(1) In addition to the powers and functions vested in the County Council by
sections 16, 17 and 18 of this Act, there are also hereby vested in the County Council
all the rights, powers, privileges and facilities enjoyable by a District Council by virtue
of the District Councils Act 1995, especially Part VIII of that Act.
(2) Where a public market exists at the commencement of this Act within the
jurisdiction of the Council, the Council shall have the sole management, control and
care of that public market, and shall maintain and keep the same in good condition
and may enclose the same with proper railings and gates, and may drain, level, lay
out, ornament or otherwise improve the same, and erect and maintain buildings and
also do all such works and things as may be requisite for the use of the same as a
public market, subject to any relevant town and country or other planning legislation.

## PART VII

Financial Provisions

Council may raise revenue

The Council may raise revenue from the following sources—
(i) Wharf and Harbour Dues;
(ii) Postal Agency Fees as agreed between the Council and the Central
Government;
(iii) Public Utilities Service Charges in relation to utilities provided by the
Central Government or by a statutory corporation or by a company in
which the Central Government has majority shares; and
(iv) Rents, dues, fees, stallages and other such charges made by the Council
for the use by others of its facilities.
21\. Council to share equitably in grants to the State
The Council shall be entitled to such an equitable share of grants made to the State
for development purposes as may be agreed between itself and the Central Government.
22\.

Power to borrow sums

The Council shall have power to borrow in any year as it sees fit sums not exceeding
fifteen per cent (15%) of its recurrent budget for the purpose of maintaining and
operating its public services:
Provided that such recurrent budget is first approved by a resolution of Parliament:
Provided further that no such borrowing shall be made from persons or bodies
ordinarily resident abroad:
Provided further that no such borrowing shall be made without the prior written
approval of a resolution of Parliament.
23\.

Council to enjoy revenue raising powers of a district council

Subject to sections 20 to 22 of this Act, the provisions of the District Councils Act,

## Chapter 83A, especially sections 48 to 52, empowering a District Council to raise

revenue from the sources identified in and by that Act shall apply mutatis mutandis to
the County Council established by this Act as if this County Council were a District
Council.
24\.

Financial accountability of Council

The provisions of the District Councils Act, Chapter 83A, especially sections 53 to
57, imposing financial accountability on District Councils shall apply mutatis mutandis
to the County Council established by this Act as if this County Council were a District
Council.

## PART VIII

Liaison between the Council and the Central Government
25\.

Standing Co-ordinating Forum

(1) There shall be a Standing Co-ordinating Forum comprising five members of the
Council and five members of the Central Government, or such other equal number of
representatives from both sides as agreed between the Council and the Central
Government. The Council shall choose its five members of the Forum, and the Minister
shall choose the five members of the Central Government on the Forum.

(2) The Standing Co-ordinating Forum constituted by subsection (1) shall be the
medium for high level consultation between the Central Government and the Council.

Chairperson of Standing Co-ordinating Forum

(1) The Standing Co-ordinating Forum constituted by section 25 of this Act shall
annually select its Chairperson, who shall come from among the elected members of
the Forum.
(2) The Chairperson of the Forum shall preside at all meetings of the Forum at
which he or she is present. If he or she is absent from any meeting of the Forum,
members present shall select another elected member to deputise for him or her at
that meeting.

## PART IX

General Applicability of District Councils Act, 1995
27\.

District Councils Act, 1995, applies to County Council mutatis mutandis

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the District Councils Act, Chapter 83A
shall apply mutatis mutandis to the County Council established by and under this Act
as if this County Council were District Council. In particular there shall thus apply to
this County Council, the provisions of the District Councils Act, 1995 regarding
procedural matters, sections 27 to 34; officers and staff, sections 35 to 42; the powers
and functions of the Council, sections 43 to 47; financial matters, sections 48 to 57;
legal proceedings, sections 58 to 80; the right of the public to attend meetings of the
Council, section 86; the oath or affirmation of office to be taken on assuming an office
on the Council, section 87; central government control of local government authorities,
sections 88 to 91; and miscellaneous matters, sections 92 to 94.
(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1) of this section, sections 95 and 96 of the
District Councils Act, 1995 regarding repeals and commencement respectively shall not
apply to the County Council established by and under this Act.

## PART X

Dissolution of the Council
28\.

Machinery for dissolving Council

The Council shall not be dissolved except at its own request signified by a resolution
passed at a meeting of the Council supported by the votes of not less than two-thirds
of all its members; or except by an Act of Parliament the bill for which is supported in
the House of Representatives by the votes of not less than two-thirds of all the
members of that House.

## PART XI

The Petit Martinique Zonal Agency
29\.

Establishment of Petit Martinique Zonal Agency

There is hereby established and constituted the Petit Martinique Zonal Agency in
and for the island of Petit Martinique as a local government authority.

Membership of Zonal Agency

(1) The membership of the Petit Martinique Zonal Agency constituted by section 29
of this Act shall be as follows—

(i) The two (2) elected Councillors for Petit Martinique sitting on the
Carriacou and Petit Martinique County Council referred to in section
5(1)(i)(b) of this Act;
(ii) The one (1) co-opted Councillor for Petit Martinique sitting on the
Carriacou and Petit Martinique County Council referred to in section
5(1)(ii)(b) of this Act;
(iii) Two (2) persons co-opted by the six (6) County Councillors referred to in
clauses (i) and (ii), subject to section 7 of this Act; and
(iv) The Member of the House of Representatives elected to represent the
constituency of Carriacou and Petit Martinique under the Representation of
the People Act, Chapter 286A.
(2) The members of the Zonal Agency referred to in subsection (1)(i) and (ii) shall
retain their membership of the Zonal Agency only so long as they remain members of
the County Council. The members of the Zonal Agency referred to in of subsection
(1)(iii) shall retain their membership of the Zonal Agency, unless they sooner resign or
are removed by those co-opting them, only so long as those co-opting them remain on
the County Council.
31\.

Chairperson of Zonal Agency

(1) The Petit Martinique Zonal Agency shall annually select its Chairperson, who
shall come from among the elected members of the Agency.
(2) The Chairperson of the Zonal Agency shall preside at all meetings of the
Agency at which he or she is present. If he or she is absent from any meeting of the
Agency, members present shall select another elected member to deputise for him or
her at that meeting.
32\.

Further provisions regarding the Chairperson

The provisions of sections 13 to 15 of this Act regarding the Chairperson of the
County Council shall apply to the Chairperson of the Zonal Agency.
33\.

Powers of Zonal Agency

The Petit Martinique Zonal Agency shall be empowered to advise the Carriacou and
Petit Martinique County Council in relation to local government matters in and
concerning Petit Martinique.
34\.

District Councils Act, 1995, applies to Zonal Agency

Subject to the provisions of this Act, the provisions of the District Councils Act 1995
shall apply to the Zonal Agency as if the Zonal Agency were a District Council,
regarding procedural matters, sections 27 to 34; the right of the public to attend
meetings of the Agency, section 86; the oath or affirmation of office to be taken on
assuming an office on the Agency, section 87; central government control of local
government authorities, sections 88 to 91; and miscellaneous matters, sections 92 to
94\.

## PART XII

Repeal and Commencement
35\.

Repeals

The Carriacou Area Local Government Act, No. 27 of 1968, is repealed.

Commencement

This Act comes into force on the 5th day of April, 1995.

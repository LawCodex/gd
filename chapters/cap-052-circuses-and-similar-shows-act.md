## CHAPTER 52

CIRCUSES AND SIMILAR SHOWS ACT
An Act to provide for the regulation of circuses, merry-go-rounds and other
shows of a like kind.
[23rd May, 1925.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Circuses and Similar Shows Act.

### 2.

Prohibition of operating circuses, etc., without licence

(1) No person shall conduct or operate any circus, merry-go-round, or other show of
a like kind without a licence granted and issued subject to the conditions hereinafter
provided.
(2) Any person acting in contravention of this section shall be guilty of an offence.

### 3.

Issue of licences, conditions of licences

(1) It shall be lawful for the Chief of Police to issue licences under this Act, subject
to the conditions mentioned in subsection (2).
(2) Each licence shall specify—
(a)

the site on which the show shall be erected;

(b)

the days of the week and hours between which the show shall be
operated;

(c)

the period during which the show may be operated.

(3) If the holder of a licence or any person employed by him or her infringes any
condition subject to which the licence is granted he or she shall be guilty of an offence.

### 4.

Fee for licence

There shall be paid into the Treasury for every licence issued under this Act a fee of
five dollars for each fortnight during which the show is licensed to be operated.

### 5.

Penalty

Any person guilty of an offence under section 2 or 3 shall be liable on summary
conviction to a fine of two thousand dollars.

### 6.

Cancellation of licences

In the event of the infringement by the holder of a licence of any of the conditions
subject to which the licence is granted, or of the conviction of the holder or any person
employed by him or her of an offence under this Act, the Minister may, without
compensation, cancel the licence.

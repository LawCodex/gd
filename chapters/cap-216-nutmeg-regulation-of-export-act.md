## CHAPTER 216

NUTMEG (REGULATION OF EXPORT) ACT
An Act to regulate the curing, packing and exporting of nutmegs.
[1st November, 1935.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Nutmeg (Regulation of Export) Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“cured nutmeg” means a nutmeg which has been thoroughly dried in such a manner
that when the shell is cracked the kernel is sound at the base and at the top and does
not yield to manual pressure and which, when cut, is free from interior mould, and for
the purposes of this Act any other nutmeg shall be deemed to be an uncured or
defective nutmeg;
“export” means export by sea from Grenada for purposes of sale thereout and
includes the causing or permitting to export, an attempt to export and a preparation for
export;
“package” includes every means by which goods for carriage may be cased,
covered, enclosed, contained, or packed;
“person”, in relation to any proceedings for exporting uncured nutmegs, means the
person being or holding himself or herself out to be the owner, exporter, agent or person
possessed of or beneficially interested in such nutmegs other than a carrier or shipping

agent acting on behalf of such person or a person having a lien on such nutmegs on
account of money advanced;
“vessel” includes any ship or boat or any other description of vessel used in
navigation except a steamship.

### 3.

Prohibition of export
Except as hereinafter provided, no person shall export any uncured or defective nutmegs.

### 4.

Regulations permitting export
The Minister may make regulations permitting the export of uncured or defective
nutmegs to certain countries named in such regulations, and such permission may be
given unconditionally or subject to such conditions as may be deemed expedient and the
conditions, if any, may vary according to the country to which such export shall be
permitted. Such regulations may, from time to time, be altered or revoked by the
Minister.

### 5.

Appointment of inspectors
The Minister shall appoint one or more inspectors whose duty it shall be to enforce and
carry out the provisions of this Act and the regulations made in pursuance thereof.

### 6.

Powers of inspectors
(1) An inspector appointed under this Act shall for the purposes of the execution of
this Act have power to do all or any of the following things—
(a)

enter at all reasonable times any premises or place where he or she has reason
to believe that nutmegs are being prepared or otherwise dealt with, whether
such nutmegs are on the premises of the owner or on other premises or in the
possession of a shipping company;

(b)

make such examination and inquiry on such premises as may be necessary
for ascertaining whether nutmegs are being exported or prepared for export
or being dealt with in any manner contravening the provisions of this Act;

(c)

examine any package containing or thought to contain nutmegs;

(d)

board any vessel on which nutmegs may have been stored for the purpose of
being exported and to examine such nutmegs;

(e)

examine any nutmegs whilst in transit to any vessel for purposes of export;

(f)

exercise such other powers as may be necessary for carrying this Act into
effect.

(2) In the exercise of his or her powers under this section an inspector may take for
examination such samples from any package containing nutmegs or from any quantity of
nutmegs in bulk as may be necessary for the enforcement of this Act.

### 7.

Seizure and detention
In the exercise of his or her powers under section 6, an inspector may seize and detain
any package containing uncured or defective nutmegs which he or she has reasonable
suspicion and probable cause for believing is being exported as though containing cured
nutmegs, or contrary to any regulation permitting the export of uncured or defective
nutmegs, and all such uncured or defective nutmegs may, as hereafter provided, be
forfeited by order of the court.

### 8.

Obstruction of inspectors
A person who obstructs, hinders or resists or in any way opposes an inspector in the
execution of any duty imposed upon him or her by this Act or by a regulation made
thereunder, or refuses to allow an inspector to take samples or to give him or her facilities
for the purpose, shall be guilty of an offence.

### 9.

Penalty for export of uncured or defective nutmegs
(1) A person who either by himself, herself or by an agent or employer exports,
otherwise than in accordance with regulations made by the Minister pursuant to the
provisions of this Act, uncured or defective nutmegs shall be guilty of an offence and
liable, on conviction, to a fine of five thousand dollars, or in default of payment, to
imprisonment for three months. And the convicting court may order that the uncured or
defective nutmegs in respect of which such offence is committed, be forfeited; whereupon
such uncured or defective nutmegs shall and may be dealt with and disposed of in such
manner as may be prescribed by rules under this Act or, in the absence of any such
prescription, in such manner as the Minister may direct:
Provided that no person shall be liable to a fine or to a sentence of imprisonment if he
or she satisfies the convicting court that he or she could not, by the exercise of all
reasonable diligence, discover that nutmegs exported by him or her were uncured or
defective by any known means of examination of the exterior of such nutmegs.
(2) The forfeiture of any uncured or defective nutmegs shall be deemed to include the
package in which they are found and the entire contents thereof.

### 10.

Burden of proof
In a prosecution under this Act in relation to the seizure of uncured or defective
nutmegs, or cured nutmegs containing any uncured or defective nutmegs, the proof of any
illegality of the seizure and the circumstances, if any, alleged to exempt the person
charged with the offence from the penalty, shall be on the defendant.

### 11.

Proceedings for offences
(1) All proceedings in respect of offences against this Act or against regulations made
thereunder shall be taken summarily.
(2) There shall be served with the summons a copy of the inspector’s report on which
the proceedings are based.

### 12.

General penalty
Unless otherwise provided for in this Act or in regulations made thereunder, a person
who commits an offence against this Act or regulations thereunder shall be liable, on
summary conviction, to a fine of three thousand dollars or, in default of payment, to be
imprisoned for three months.

### 13.

Appointment of Board of appeal
(1) If a person is aggrieved by any decision of or action taken by an inspector under
this Act the inspector shall, if required by such person, state in writing the reasons for his
or her decision or action and the matter shall thereupon be referred to a Board which shall
be appointed by the Minister. The decision of the Board in the matter shall be final.
(2) Before the matter is referred to the Board, the person aggrieved shall deposit with
the inspector such reasonable amount as in the opinion of the Permanent Secretary in the
Ministry responsible for agriculture shall be sufficient to defray the expenses which will

necessarily be incurred by the Board, but the amount so deposited shall be refunded to
such person if his or her contention is upheld by the Board.

### 14.

Power of the Minister to make regulations
The Minister may make regulations not inconsistent with this Act to provide for the
following matters—
(a)

the appointment of an Advisory Board which may at his or her request
prepare and recommend such regulations as in its opinion should be made
under this Act;

(b)

the percentage of nutmegs which shall be inspected in any one consignment;

(c)

the inspection of nutmegs intended for export and the inspection of premises
in which nutmegs are prepared or otherwise dealt with;

(d)

the circumstances under which and the conditions on which nutmegs
intended for shipment may be withdrawn from shipment;

(e)

prescribing the powers and duties of inspectors appointed under this Act;

(f)

the abstraction or removal of samples by an inspector for examination;

(g)

the disposal of uncured or defective nutmegs forfeited under this Act; and

(h)

generally for carrying the purposes and provisions of this Act into effect.

Citation

NUTMEG (REGULATION OF EXPORT) REGULATIONS
[Section 14. Amended by SRO 34 of 1964.]

[2nd March, 1936.]
1\. Citation
These Regulations may be cited as the Nutmeg (Regulation of Export) Regulations.
2\. Examination before export

The Inspector shall examine not less than ten per cent of the packages in each
consignment of nutmegs for export by any one steamer or vessel, if such consignment is
for export.
3\. Contaminated nutmegs
Where nutmegs intended for export are found to contain more than ten per cent by
count of nutmegs wormy, worm eaten and/or mouldy, such nutmegs may be returned to
the owner or his or her agent if the Chief Technical Officer (Extension) is satisfied that
such nutmegs will be exported or that such nutmegs will be reconditioned and presented
again for inspection prior to export.
4\. Removal for analysis
An Inspector may remove a sufficiently large sample of nutmegs (which shall be
representative of the condition of the shipment as a whole) for inspection or analysis, when
such nutmegs are intended for shipment and a receipt for the quantity removed shall be
given to the owner or his or her agent from a counter-foil receipt book kept for the purpose.
5\. Cleanliness of warehouses
Premises or warehouses in which nutmegs are handled shall at all times be clean,
sanitary and free from weevils.
6\. Notice to report
Every person exporting nutmegs shall give reasonable notice to the Chief Technical
Officer (Extension) of his or her intension to ship nutmegs before the consignment is
placed in any vessel. Inspection shall take place in the city of St. George’s.
7\. Notification regarding warehouses
Notification of a warehouse in which nutmegs intended for export are stored shall be
given to the Inspector who shall keep a register of such warehouse and each such
warehouse shall be designated a Nutmeg Export Warehouse.
8\. Register
Every owner of a nutmeg export warehouse shall keep a register in which shall be
shown the date of receipt, the name of the owner, the marks on the package, the number
and description of the packages and the date of removal of the nutmegs.
9\. Proceedings
In any proceedings that may be taken under the Act or regulations made thereunder
the name of the owner as appears in the register kept at the nutmeg export warehouse
shall be sufficient proof of the ownership of the nutmegs in that warehouse.
10\. Certificate for export
No nutmegs intended for export shall be shipped in any vessel without a certificate
from the proper authority to the effect that the nutmegs contain not over ten per cent of
nutmegs wormy, worm eaten and/or mouldy. These percentages shall not be applied to
nutmegs exported solely for distillation purposes of the essential oil of nutmeg and where
satisfactory evidence is furnished from the station at the port of landing that the nutmegs
will actually be so used.

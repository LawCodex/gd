## CHAPTER 164A

LAURISTON AIRPORT USER FEE (IMPOSITION AND VALIDATION) ACT
An Act to impose the Lauriston Airport User Fee, and to validate the purported
collection of such fee.
[Act No. 16 of 1999.]

[1st January, 1994.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Lauriston Airport User Fee (Imposition and Validation)
Act.
2\. Imposition of fee
The Airports Authority shall levy and collect a fee of ten dollars, to be known as a
user fee, on all passengers departing from Lauriston Airport, Carriacou.
3\. Validation
Notwithstanding anything contained in any enactment or rule of law, the Lauriston
Airport User Fee purported to be imposed and collected by the Airports Authority from
1994 onwards shall be deemed to have been validly imposed and collected.
4\. Indemnity
No action shall lie against any employee of the Airport Authority with respect to the
imposition and collection of the Lauriston Airport User Fee.
5\. Commencement
This Act shall be deemed to have come into operation on 1st January, 1994.

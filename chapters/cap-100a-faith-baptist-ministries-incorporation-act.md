## CHAPTER 100A

FAITH BAPTIST MINISTRIES INCORPORATION ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of a body to be known as Faith Baptist
Ministries.
[Act No. 45 of 1992.]

[8th January, 1993.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Faith Baptist Ministries Incorporation Act.
2\. Incorporation Trustees
The said Denis Celestine, Casimir Thomas, David Bubb and Robert W. Mathena and
their successors in office as Trustees of Faith Baptist Ministries shall be and are hereby
created a body corporate under the name of “THE INCORPORATED TRUSTEES OF
FAITH BAPTIST CHURCH” (hereinafter called “the Trustees”) and by that name shall
have perpetual succession and also by that name shall and may sue and be sued in all
courts of justice in Grenada and shall have and use a Common Seal which may from time
to time be changed, altered or made a new.
3\. Trustees may acquire property
The Trustees shall have full power to acquire for the Organisation by purchase,
transfer, exchange, devise, demise, grant, gift, bequest or otherwise, all lands or other
property of whatsoever nature of kind in perpetuity or for life or lives or for any term of
years and also any money, securities for money, goods or chattels whatsoever may have
been acquired.

4\. Trustees may dispose of property
It shall be lawful for the Trustees subject to the performance of any trust as aforesaid,
from time to time by deed under their seal, to sell or demise, grant, convey, transfer,
exchange, surrender or otherwise dispose of any lands or other property vested or which
may hereafter be vested in them.
5\. Trustees may borrow and give mortgages
It shall be lawful for the Trustees subject to the performance of any trust as aforesaid,
to borrow money, and for the payment of the same, by deed under their seal, to give and
execute mortgages, charges, pledges or other securities of their real or personal property
or to make any such arrangements as they may consider convenient, with such covenants,
powers and provisions as they may think proper.
6\. Vesting of property
All churches, chapels, mission houses, rectories, vicarages, parsonages, glebes,
buildings, appurtenances and lands now held by or otherwise vested in the said Faith
Baptist ministries or in any other person or body for and on behalf or appropriated to the
use of the Organisation and all other property (if any) of whatever nature in Grenada now
held by or vested in the said Faith Baptist ministries shall be held by and the same are
hereby vested in the Trustees its successors and assigns for the same estates and interests
and to the extent to which the same were respectively at the time of the passing of this
Act held or vested as aforesaid subject to the rights, reservations and charges, if any, now
affecting the same.
7\. Organisation not to have alien status
Notwithstanding that the Trustees may at any time and from time to time for the
purposes of the Aliens (Land-Holding Regulation) Act or any law or enactment for the
time being amending or replacing that Act, be or be deemed to be under alien control, the
Trustees shall not be or be deemed to be an alien for such purposes but shall be entitled to
hold land for any estate or interest therein and mortgages on land in Grenada without
licence and such land and mortgages shall not be liable to forfeiture or other penalty
under the said Act or any other such enactment.
8\. Mode of execution of documents
All deeds and other documents requiring the seal of the Trustees shall be sealed with
the Common Seal of the Trustees in the presence of at least two of the Trustees who shall
sign every such deed or other document to which the said seal is affixed; and such signing
shall be prima facie evidence that the said seal was duly and properly affixed and that the
same is the lawful seal of the Trustees.
9\. Service of documents on Trustees
The Trustees shall at all times have a fixed address in Grenada for the service of
documents on them and such address shall be registered with the Registrar of Companies.
All documents to be served on the Trustees may be served by leaving the same at or
sending the same by registered post to the address so registered.
10\. Saving of Crown and other rights
Nothing in this Act shall affect or be deemed to affect the rights or Her Majesty, Her
Heirs and Successors, or of any bodies politic or corporate or of any other persons except
such as are mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, from or under them.

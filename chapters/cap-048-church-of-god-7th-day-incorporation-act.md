## CHAPTER 48

CHURCH OF GOD (7TH DAY) (INCORPORATION) ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of a body to be known as the
Incorporated Trustees of the Church of God (7th Day), and for matters
connected therewith
[Act No. 51 of 1980.]

[24th October, 1980.]
1\.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Church of God (7th Day) (Incorporation) Act.

2\.

Incorporation of trustees

Hubert Matthew, Peter Tyson, Septimus John, Nicodemus Gittens, Nimrod George,
Errol Collins and their successors in office as trustees of the Church of God (7th day)
shall be a body corporate known as The Incorporated Trustees of the Church of God
(7th Day) (hereinafter called “the Trustees”) with perpetual succession and a Common
Seal and with power in that name to sue and be sued in all courts.
3\.

Power of trustees to acquire property

The Trustees shall have power to acquire for the Church of God (7th Day) by
purchase, transfer, exchange, devise, grant, gift, demise, bequest or otherwise any
land or other property of whatever kind whether in perpetuity or for life or for a term
of years and any money, securities, goods or chattels whatever, to take, hold and
enjoy the same subject to the performance of any trust upon which the same may
have been acquired.
4\.

Power of trustees to dispose of property, and to borrow, etc.
Subject to the performance of any trust as aforesaid the Trustees may—

5\.

(a)

by deed under the Common Seal sell, demise, grant, convey, transfer,
exchange, surrender or otherwise dispose of any real or personal property
vested in or which may hereafter be acquired by or become vested in the
Trustees;

(b)

borrow money and, for the payment of the same, by deed under the
Common Seal, give and execute mortgages, charges, pledges and other
securities of any real or personal property or make any arrangement that
they may consider convenient with such covenants, powers and provisions
as they think proper.

Vesting of property

All chapels, buildings, appurtenances and leasehold land now held by the Church of
God (7th Day) are vested in the Trustees.
6\.

Exemption from licensing and forfeiture under Chapter 13

Notwithstanding that the Trustees may at any time be or be deemed to be under
alien control for the purposes of the Aliens (Land-Holding Regulation) Act, Chapter 13,
they shall be entitled to hold land and mortgages without licence, and such land and
mortgages shall not be liable to forfeiture under that Act.
7\.

Affixing of Common Seal

All documents requiring the Common Seal shall be sealed in the presence of at
least two of the Trustees who shall sign every such document.
8\.

Address for service, etc.

(1) The Trustees shall at all times have a fixed address in Grenada for the service
of documents on them and such address and any change thereof shall be registered
with the Registrar of Companies.
(2) Any document may be served on the Trustees by leaving the document at, or
by sending it by registered post to, the address registered pursuant to subsection (1).

## CHAPTER 57

COMMISSION OF THE EUROPEAN COMMUNITIES ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of the European Communities and to
confer privileges and immunities on the Delegation thereof and on other
persons.
[Act No. 45 of 1986.]

[31st December, 1986.]
1\.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Commission of the European Communities Act.

2\.

Incorporation

The European Communities are deemed to be a body corporate established by this
Act.
3\.

Powers of the European Communities in Grenada

The European Communities shall have the power to contract, to acquire and
dispose of movable and immovable property, and to sue and to be sued, and shall for
those purposes be represented by the Commission of the European Communities in
Grenada.
4\. Privileges and immunities of representatives
Commission of the European Communities

in

Grenada

of

the

(1) The Head of the Delegation of the Commission in Trinidad and Tobago when in
Grenada, the Head of the office of the Commission in Grenada known as the Resident
Adviser, the office, members of any staff of the Delegation and of the office being
citizens of a member State of the European Communities, and any members of their
families who form part of their respective households, shall have the privileges and
immunities corresponding to those accorded, in conformity with the Vienna Convention
on diplomatic relations of 18th April, 1961, to diplomatic missions accredited to the
Government, and to the Heads and the members thereof, and to members of their
families who form part of their respective households subject to the same conditions
and obligations:

Provided that the member states of the European Communities accord privileges
and immunities of the like kind to the Mission of the Government to the European
Communities, and to the Head and to the members of the staff thereof, and any
members of their families who form part of their respective households, in accordance
with Article 17 of the Protocol annexed to the Treaty establishing a Single Council and
a Single Commission of the European Communities signed at Brussels on 8th April,
1965\.
(2) The privileges and immunities conferred by subsection (1) shall not be
accorded in Grenada to any person who is not a national of a member State of the
European Communities nor to any person who is a citizen of Grenada.

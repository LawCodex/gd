## CHAPTER 132

GRENADA UNITED SPIRITUAL BAPTIST CHURCH (INCORPORATION)
ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of a body to be known as the Incorporated
Trustees of the Grenada United Spiritual Baptist Church.
[Act No. 25 of 1975.]

[5th December, 1975.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Grenada United Spiritual Baptist Church (Incorporation)
Act.
2\. Incorporation of trustees
Norton Strachan, Adina Noel, Edward Clyne, Harbin Williams, Lionel Andrew and
their successors in office as trustees of the Grenada United Spiritual Baptist Church
(hereinafter called “the Church”) shall be a body corporate known as The Incorporated
Trustees of the Grenada United Spiritual Baptist Church (hereinafter called “the
Trustees”) with perpetual succession and a Common Seal and with power in that name to
sue and be sued in all courts.
3\. Vesting of property
All churches, chapels, mission houses, rectories, vicarages, parsonages, glebes,
buildings and land which may be acquired or appropriated to the uses of the Church are
vested in the Trustees.
4\. Power of trustees to acquire property

Subject to the rules, regulations and control of the Church, the Trustees shall have full
power to acquire for the Church by gift, grant, purchase, devise or otherwise any land or
other property of whatever kind whether in perpetuity or for a term of years and any
money, securities, goods or chattels whatever, to take, hold and enjoy the same subject to
the performance of any trust upon which the same may have been acquired.
5\. Power of trustees to dispose of property, and to borrow, etc.
Subject to the rules, regulations and control of the Church and to the performance of
any trust as aforesaid the Trustees may—
(a)

sell, demise, grant, convey, transfer, alienate or otherwise dispose of any
property which may hereafter be acquired or become vested in them;

(b)

borrow money and, for the payment of the same, by deed under the Common
Seal, give and execute mortgages, charges, pledges and other securities of
their real and personal property, with any covenants, powers and provisions
they think proper.

6\. Affixing of Common Seal
All documents requiring the Common Seal shall be sealed in the presence of at least
two of the Trustees who shall sign every such document.
7\. Address for service, etc.
(1) The Trustees shall at all times have a fixed address in Grenada for the service of
documents on them and such address and any change thereof shall be registered with the
Registrar of Companies.
(2) Any document may be served on the Trustees by leaving the document at, or by
sending it by registered post to, the address registered pursuant to subsection (1).

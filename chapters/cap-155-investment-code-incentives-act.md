## CHAPTER 155

INVESTMENT CODE INCENTIVES ACT
An Act to empower the Minister responsible for finance, with the approval of the
Cabinet, to extend or increase concessions or incentives designed to encourage
investment in Grenada.
[Act No. 13 of 1983.]

[2nd March, 1983.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Investment Code Incentives Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“Certificate” means an Investment Code Qualification Certificate issued by the
Minister under section 3(1);
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for finance.
3\. Investment Code Qualification Certificates
(1) Where the Minister is satisfied that a proposal for investment which has been
submitted to him or her will, if implemented, meet the criteria or sufficient of the criteria
required for the encouragement of investment under the Investment Code approved by the
Government as published in the Gazette on the 2nd March, 1983, or as the same may be
subsequently amended, the Minister may, with the approval of the Cabinet, issue an
Investment Code Qualification Certificate in respect of such investment.
(2) Where a Certificate has been issued there shall apply to the investment named
therein such concessions and financial and other incentives contained in section 5 of this
Act as are specified in the Certificate to the extent specified in the Certificate.

4\. Power of Minister to make regulations
The Minister may make regulations governing applications for, and the issue of,
Certificates and such other matters in relation to Certificates as may be required to
facilitate and regulate the implementation of this Act and the application and realisation
of the concessions and incentives for which this Act provides.
5\. Entitlement assured by Certificate
Notwithstanding the provisions of any written law to the contrary, an investment in
respect of which a Certificate has been issued shall be entitled to the benefit of the
concessions and incentives set out in the Certificate.
6\. Concessions and incentives
In a Certificate the Minister, with the approval of the Cabinet, may—
(a)

authorise and permit the repatriation or remittance out of Grenada at any
time of the whole or part of the capital invested in an investment in Grenada
(being capital originating outside, or brought into, Grenada for the purpose
of such investment) to the country of origin of such capital or such other
country as the Minister shall approve;

(b)

cancel any licence fee payable under the provisions of the Aliens (Land
Holding Regulation) Act, Chapter 13, upon a transfer in pursuance or
forming part of an investment in industry, manufacturing, tourism, or any
other investment approved by the Minister;

(c)

exempt from taxation the whole or part of the income accruing from an
investment in industry, manufacturing, or the construction or establishment
of a hotel or guest house or condominium, for a period not exceeding ten
years, notwithstanding any other written law.

7\. Avoidance of duplication of benefit
Any person enjoying the benefit of an incentive or concession provided under a
Certificate shall not be entitled to receive in addition a benefit by way of a concession or
incentive under any other written law unless such additional benefit is specifically
authorised in the Certificate.

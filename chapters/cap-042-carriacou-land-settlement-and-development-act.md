## CHAPTER 42

CARRIACOU LAND SETTLEMENT AND DEVELOPMENT ACT
An Act to establish and empower a body corporate to regulate and control
land settlement and development in Carriacou.
[1st January, 1955.]

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Carriacou Land Settlement and Development Act.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“Board” means the Land Settlement and Development Board as by this Act
established and constituted;
“owner”, in respect of land, includes the person in possession of land or his or
her duly authorised attorney on record, or any of such persons or attorneys.

## PART I

Constitution of the Board

3\.

Creation of Board

(1) There is hereby established and constituted a body corporate called the
Carriacou Land Settlement and Development Board which body corporate shall as such
be entitled to make contracts and to sue and be sued in its corporate name, to
acquire, hold and dispose of all kinds of property, real and personal, in accordance
with the provisions of this Act, and with power to regulate, control and develop land
settlement in the island of Carriacou as provided by this Act.
(2) The Board shall have an official seal which shall be authenticated by the
signature of the chairperson or deputy chairperson and one other member of the
Board authorised by the Board to act in that behalf, and such seal shall be officially
and judicially noticed.
(3) Until a seal is provided in accordance with subsection (2), a stamp bearing the
words “The Carriacou Land Settlement and Development Board, Grenada”, if
authenticated as aforesaid, may be used instead of such seal.
4\.

Members of the Board
(1) The Board shall consist of—
(a)

a chairperson and a deputy chairperson both of whom shall be appointed
by the Minister and shall be persons holding offices of emolument in the
public service; and

(b)

not less than five other members appointed by the Minister who shall hold
office for one year and shall be eligible for re-appointment.

(2) The Minister may appoint any person to act in the place of any member of the
Board in case of his or her temporary absence.
(3) The Board may employ such person as it may deem fit to act as secretary.
Such person shall have no voice in the deliberations of the Board.
(4) Any unofficial member of the Board may at any time resign his or her office by
instrument in writing addressed to the secretary thereof who shall forthwith forward
the same to the Minister and from the date of the receipt by the secretary of such
instrument, such member shall cease to be a member of the Board and the vacancy
caused by such resignation or by the death or removal of a member shall be filled by
the Minister subject to the provisions of subsection (1).
(5) Notice of the appointment, removal or resignation of any member of the Board
shall be published in the Gazette.
5\.

Meetings of the Board

(1) The Board may act by any five of its members including either the chairperson
or the deputy chairperson, and may so act notwithstanding any vacancy in its number.
(2) The Board shall meet at such times as may be necessary or expedient for
transacting its business. The chairperson or any three members may convene a special

meeting of the Board by giving seven days notice in writing to the secretary of his or
her or their desire so to do. Minutes of each meeting shall be kept in proper form by
the secretary and shall be confirmed by the chairperson at the next succeeding
meeting. Certified copies of such minutes when so confirmed shall be forwarded to the
Minister.
(3) At every meeting of the Board the presiding chairperson shall have a casting as
well as an original vote.
(4) Subject to this section the Board shall have power to regulate its own
procedure.
6\.

Indemnity of members of the Board

No personal liability shall attach to any member of the Board in respect of anything
done or suffered in good faith under the provisions of this Act and any sums of money,
damages or costs which may be recovered against them or any of them for anything
done or suffered as aforesaid shall be paid out of the funds of the Board.
7\.

Power to employ agents, etc.

It shall be lawful for the Board to employ agents, clerks, labourers, and any other
persons whose services may be required by the Board for the purpose of carrying into
effect the powers conferred by this Act. Such persons shall be employed at such
remuneration and on such terms and conditions as the Board may determine.
8\.

Powers of the Board
The Board shall have power—

9\.

(a)

to control, develop and re-organise in any way whatever such lands as
may be vested in it as hereinafter provided in accordance with the
economic and social requirements of the community and with the need for
conserving the natural resources of soil, forest and water;

(b)

to lease any part of such lands on such terms and conditions as the Board
may from time to time deem necessary.

Funds of the Board
The funds of the Board shall consist of—

10\.

(a)

such sum or sums of money as may be granted to the Board by statute;

(b)

such sum or sums of money as may be granted to the Board from time to
time by resolution of the House of Representatives;

(c)

such sum or sums of money as may, from time to time, accrue to the
Board in respect of rental of properties, or interest on such funds as may
from time to time be on deposit;

(d)

such sum or sums of money as may be borrowed with the approval of the
House of Representatives under section 10.

Borrowing powers of the Board

The Board may, with the approval of the House of Representatives, borrow money
for the carrying out of its purposes and may charge in any manner any property of the
Board of every nature and kind whatsoever and all the funds and all the revenues of
the Board, present and future, with the repayment of money borrowed for such
purposes.

Accounts

The Board shall keep such accounts of receipts and expenditure as may be
prescribed by the Permanent Secretary (Finance).

Inspection and audit

All accounts kept by the Board shall be audited annually by the Director of Audit or
such other auditor as may be approved by the Minister, and the Director of Audit and
such other auditor shall have the right of inspection thereof at all reasonable times.
13\.

Reports and audited accounts to be forwarded annually to the Minister

The Board shall forward to the Minister as soon as possible after the 31st December
of each year a report upon the administration of the affairs of the Board during the
year together with a balance sheet and statement of revenue and expenditure
approved and signed by the Director of Audit or by such other auditor as may have
been approved by the Minister. The report, balance sheet and statement of revenue
and expenditure shall be laid before the House of Representatives.
14\.

Board to be liable for tax on real property

The Board shall be liable for ensuring the payment of the tax on real property
required to be paid by virtue of the Real Property Act, Chapter 275, in respect of all
real property vested in the Board.

## PART II

Acquisition of Land for Land Settlement Purposes
15\.

Power to select and purchase land by agreement

(1) The board may, from time to time, select any land within the island of
Carriacou which may be deemed necessary for the establishment and location of
small-holders and for other public purposes connected therewith.
(2) The Board may contract and agree with the owner of any selected land and
with all parties having any estate or interest in such land, or enabled to sell the same,
for the absolute purchase of such land and of all estates in such land of whatsoever
kind.
16\. Approval of House of Representatives necessary before acquisition by
Board
The acquisition of any land whatsoever by the Board, whether it be acquired by
private treaty or otherwise, shall be subject in all cases to the prior approval of the
House of Representatives.
17\.

Acquisition of land

Where the Board is unable to acquire by agreement and on reasonable terms any
land or building which they consider should be acquired for the purposes of this Act,
the Board shall report the fact to the Minister, and if the Board so requests, the
Minister may, if he thinks fit, take the necessary steps to acquire such land or building
compulsorily under the provisions of the Land Acquisition Act, Chapter 159.
18\.

Appropriation of lands vested in the Board

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, land becoming vested in the Board under
this Act shall be deemed to be appropriated to the uses of establishing and locating
small-holders thereon and other uses incidental thereto under such a system of
administration and disposal thereof as the Board may by regulations made under this
Act from time to time prescribe.

(2) The Board may sell, exchange, lease, let or otherwise dispose of any land not
required for such uses.
(3) Any such sale, lease, or other disposal of such land shall be subject to the
approval of the Minister, and all monies derived therefrom, after payment of all
necessary charges and expenses, shall be paid into the Treasury to the credit of either
the Consolidated Fund or a special fund which may be provided for the purposes of this
Act.
(4) In case of any such exchange, the land taken in exchange shall be subject to the
provisions of this Act, and the Board is hereby authorised to pay out of the Consolidated
Fund or, as the case may be, such fund as aforesaid all expenses attendant on such
exchange.
Miscellaneous
19\.

Regulations

The Board may make regulations for further or better carrying into effect the
purposes of this Act, and particularly with respect to the following—

20\.

(a)

for the purpose of regulating the system of establishing and locating
small-holders on land acquired under the provisions of this Act;

(b)

the administration and disposal of such land and all matters connected
therewith or incidental thereto;

(c)

the forfeiture of all rights under the system thereby regulated with or
without the right of appeal to any court in case of breach of any of the
conditions of tenure under such system;

(d)

the payment of compensation, on forfeiture of such rights, in respect of
improvements effected on any such land by the allottee thereof.

Penalties for offences in respect of charcoal burning

(1) Any grantee, allottee or lessee of land acquired under the provisions of this
Act or any other person who—
(a)

without the written permission of the Board, makes, attempts to make or
is concerned in making charcoal on such land; or

(b)

removes or assists or is concerned in the removal of charcoal made
contrary to the provisions of this section from the place where such
charcoal has been made to any other place with knowledge or reason to
believe that such charcoal has been so made; or

(c)

receives, is found in possession of, or disposes of, charcoal made contrary
to the provisions of this section, with knowledge or reason to believe that
such charcoal was so made or removed,

shall be guilty of an offence under this section and, on summary conviction, liable to a
fine of five hundred dollars and to imprisonment for three months.
(2) If any person, who has been convicted of an offence under this section, is
convicted on a second or any subsequent occasion of any like offence or any other
offence under this section he or she shall for such second or other offence be liable to
a fine of one thousand dollars and to imprisonment for six months.
(3) The Board or any of its agents or employees or any police officer may without
warrant apprehend any person whom he or she may find committing an offence under
this section, and every person so apprehended shall be taken without delay before a
magistrate to be dealt with according to law.
(4) The court may, if so requested by the Board, award part of a fine to any person
or persons who appear to have a meritorious claim thereto.

(5) The Chief of Police or a person authorised by the Board may prosecute for an
offence under this section, and the mode of procedure shall be according to the
provisions of the law for the time being in force relating to the procedure with respect
to offences punishable on summary conviction.
(6) A prosecution in the case of an offence committed under this section shall be
brought within twelve calendar months next after the commission of the offence and
not afterwards.
(7) (a) In a prosecution for an offence committed contrary to the provisions of this
section, the onus of proving the written permission of the Board referred to in
subsection (1) (a) shall be on the defendant or person charged.
(b) Where a person acts as an employee or agent of the Board his or her acts
shall be taken as proof of his or her having been duly appointed as such employee or
agent unless the defendant or person charged shall give proof that he has not been so
appointed.
(c) Where a person lays information or prosecutes as being authorised so to do
by the Board, such authority shall be presumed unless it is proved that such person is
not so authorised.
21\. An offence to remove, etc., any boundary mark, etc., on lands acquired
under this Act
(1) Any grantee, allottee or lessee of land acquired under the provisions of this
Act or any other person who without the consent in writing of the Board removes, cuts
down or otherwise damages any mark, tree, shrub, hedge or contour line which the
Board has caused to be placed, used or planted as a boundary mark or contour line on
any such land shall be guilty of an offence against this section.
(2) The consent referred to in subsection (1) may be given subject to any
conditions as to the placing or planting of new marks, trees, shrubs or hedges as
boundary marks or contour lines within a specified time, and may be subject to such
other conditions as the Board may think desirable, and if the person to whom such
consent in writing is given, removes, cuts down or otherwise damages or causes to be
removed, cut down or otherwise damaged, any such mark, tree, shrub, hedge or
contour line as referred to in this section, and fails to observe any such conditions as
aforesaid as are embodied in the Board’s written consent, he or she shall be guilty of
an offence against this section.
(3) It shall not be a defence to a charge under this section to prove that any mark,
tree, shrub, or hedge removed, cut down or otherwise damaged has been placed, used
or planted as a boundary mark or contour line on the boundary of land of which the
person charged is grantee, allottee, or lessee, and that he or she has removed, cut
down or otherwise damaged such mark, tree, shrub, or hedge with the consent of any
grantee, allottee or lessee, of the adjoining land, contiguous to such boundary.
(4) For the purposes of a prosecution for any offence under this section, the Board
shall be deemed to have caused any mark, tree, shrub or hedge placed, used or
planted as a boundary mark or contour line to have been so placed, used or planted,
unless the contrary is proved.
(5) Any person guilty of an offence against this section shall on summary
conviction be liable to a fine of five hundred dollars and to imprisonment for three
months.
22\.

Penalty for obstruction, etc.

Any person who shall obstruct, hinder or molest a member or employee of the
Board or a person acting under the authority of the Board or of this Act shall be guilty
of an offence against this Act and shall, on summary conviction, be liable to a fine of
one thousand dollars and to imprisonment for six months.

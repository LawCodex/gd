## CHAPTER 220B

OECS EDUCATION DEVELOPMENT PROJECT LOAN AUTHORISATION
ACT
An Act to authorise the Minister of Finance to borrow from the International
Development Association an amount as nearly as may be a sum equivalent to 1.9
million United States Dollars.
[Act No. 16 of 2009.]

[10th July, 2009.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the OECS Education Development Project Loan
Authorisation Act.
2\. Authority to raise loan
(1) The Minister of Finance is hereby authorised to raise a loan as nearly as may be a
sum equivalent to 1.9 million United States Dollars to extend and continue activities
related to the original OECS Education Development Project, for the purpose of
increasing equitable access to Secondary Education, improving the quality and efficiency
of Secondary Education and strengthening the institutional capacity of the educational
sector.
(2) All monies borrowed under this Act shall be effected and secured pursuant to the
terms and conditions specified in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE 1

Project Description
The objectives of the Project are the same as those of the Original Project, namely: (a)
to increase equitable access to Secondary Education; (b) to improve quality and
efficiency of Secondary Education; and (c) to strengthen the institutional capacity of the
educational sector.
The Project consists of the following parts, subject to such modifications thereof as the
Recipient and the Association may agree upon from time to time, to achieve such
objectives:

## PART A

Improving the Quality of Teaching and Learning
1\. Development and implementation of literacy and numeracy programs for the
Primary Education and lower Secondary Education level, through: (i) the provision of
training to teachers in literacy and numeracy; (ii) the acquisition and development of
resources to support literacy and numeracy programs; (iii) development of a standardised
performance measurement system; and (iv) equipment upgrades and training for the
Ministry of Education’s Materials Production Unit to strengthen its capacity to develop
and produce instructional materials for use in schools.
2\. Improvement of the quality of teaching at the Secondary Education level, through
the provision of specialised training to school teachers in technical and vocational
education.
3\. Improvement of schools’ student support services, through the provision of
specialised training to selected counsellors and assistant counsellors to enable them to
provide guidance and counselling to secondary students.

## PART B

Improved Governance and Management in the Education System
Provision of support for the Recipient’s efforts to improve the level and efficiency of
super-vision and management activities at the school level, through the provision of
training, necessary equipment, and resource materials for the district school management
teams and student council members to ensure support for implementation of the literacy,
numeracy and other teaching and learning programs.

## PART C

Project Management
Provision of staff and staff training, communications, and supplies for the operation of
the PCU.

## SCHEDULE 2

Project Execution

SECTION I
Implementation Arrangements
A. Institutional Arrangements
1\. The Recipient shall, throughout the implementation of the Project, maintain, in its
Ministry of Finance, a Project Coordinating Unit (PCU) with functions, staffing and
responsibilities, satisfactory to the Association, including inter alia: (a) coordination,
implementation, monitoring and evaluation of the Project; (b) processing of
documentation required for disbursement of the Credit proceeds and for procurement of
goods and services under the Project; and (c) preparation and maintenance of the records,
accounts and financial statements referred to in sections II.A and II.B of this Schedule.
2\. The Recipient shall ensure that the PCU is at all times: (a) staffed with at least a
Project (Portfolio) manager, a procurement officer, a financial management specialist, a
planner/programmer and an administrative assistant; and (b) that the PCU is provided
with adequate facilities, satisfactory to the Association, to carry out its responsibilities.
B. Manuals
1\. The Recipient shall carry out the Project in accordance with a manual (the
Operational Manual), satisfactory to the Association, containing the detailed procedures for
the implementation of the Project, including, inter alia: (a) the organisation, functions and
staffing of the PCU; (b) the financial management procedures for the Project; (c) terms of
reference for all PCU staff; (d) standard bidding documents and letters of invitation to
consultants; and (e) the performance indicators.
2\. Except as the Association shall otherwise agree, the Recipient shall not amend, waive
or fail to enforce the Operational Manual or any provision thereof. In case of any conflict
between the provisions of this Agreement and those of the Operational Manual, the
provisions of this Agreement shall prevail.
C. Anti-Corruption
The Recipient shall ensure that the Project is carried out in accordance with the
provisions of the Anti-Corruption Guidelines.
SECTION II
Project Monitoring Reporting and Evaluation
A. Project Reports
1\. The Recipient shall monitor and evaluate the progress of the Project and prepare
Project Reports in accordance with the provisions of Section 4.08 of the General
Conditions and on the basis of the indicators agreed with the Association. Each Project
Report shall cover the period of one calendar semester, and shall be furnished to the
Association not later than forty-five days after the end of the period covered by such
report.
2\. For purposes of Section 4.08(c) of the General Conditions, the report on the
execution of the Project and related plan required pursuant to that Section shall be
furnished to the Association not later than four months after the Closing Date.
B. Financial Management, Financial Reports and Audits
1\. The Recipient shall maintain or cause to be maintained a financial management
system in accordance with the provisions of Section 4.09 of the General Conditions.
2\. Without limitation on the provisions of Part A of this Section, the Recipient shall
prepare and furnish to the Association not later than forty five days after the end of each
calendar quarter, interim unaudited financial reports for the Project covering the quarter,
in form and substance satisfactory to the Association.

3\. The Recipient shall have its Financial Statements audited in accordance with the
provisions of Section 4.09(b) of the General Conditions. Each audit of the Financial
Statements shall cover the period of one fiscal year of the Recipient. The audited
Financial Statements for each such period shall be furnished to the Association not later
than four months after the end of such period.

## CHAPTER 278

RED CROSS SOCIETY ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of the Grenada Red Cross Society, and for
connected purposes.
[Act No. 24 of 1981 amended by Act No. 38 of 1986.]

[21st August, 1981.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Red Cross Society Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“Branch” means the Branch of the British Red Cross Society that existed in
Grenada before the commencement of this Act;
“Geneva Conventions” means the Geneva Conventions specified in the Schedule;
“rules” means the rules of the Society made under section 8 and for the time being
in force;

“Society” means the Grenada Red Cross Society established by section 3.
3\. Incorporation of the Grenada Red Cross Society
For the purpose of maintaining and carrying on the objects set out in section 4(1) the
members of the Branch and all persons who may hereafter become members of the
Society pursuant to this Act and the rules are hereby constituted a body corporate by the
name of the Grenada Red Cross Society, which shall have perpetual succession and a
Common Seal, with power to sue and be sued in its corporate name and to acquire, hold
and dispose of movable and immovable property.
4\. Objects and principles of the Society
(1) The objects of the Society shall be to carry on and assist in work for the
improvement of health, the prevention of disease and the mitigation of suffering
throughout the world in time of peace or war.
(2) In pursuing the above mentioned objects the Society shall not make any adverse
distinction founded on sex, race, nationality, religion or faith, political opinions or any
other similar criteria and shall act in accordance with the spirit and principles of the
Geneva Conventions.
5\. Use of heraldic emblem
The Society is hereby recognised by the Government as a voluntary aid society
auxiliary to the public authorities both for the purpose of the Geneva Conventions and
otherwise and shall have the right in conformity with the Geneva Conventions to use the
heraldic emblem of a red cross on a white background, formed by reversing the Swiss
Federal colours.
6\. Nature of the Society to be respected
The independent and voluntary nature of the Society shall at all times be respected in
accordance with the resolution relative to National Red Cross Societies adopted by the
General Assembly of the United Nations on the 19th November, 1946.
7\. Governing body
The governing body of the Society shall be a General Assembly constituted in
accordance with the rules. The General Assembly may from time to time appoint a
Central Council with such powers and duties as may be prescribed by the rules.
8\. Rules
(1) The General Assembly shall make rules for the management of the affairs of the
Society and the accomplishment of its objects including rules providing—
(a)

that membership of the Society shall not be withheld from any citizen of
Grenada on the grounds of race, class, sex, religion or faith, or political
opinion;

(b)

that all members and associates of the Society have representation on the
General Assembly.

(2) The rules may, from time to time, be altered, added to or repealed by the General
Assembly and new rules may from time to time be made in like manner.

9\. Offences
(1) It shall not be lawful for any person, other than as authorized under section 5 or
under the provisions of the Geneva Conventions, to use for any purpose whatever the
emblem of the red cross on a white background mentioned in section 5 or the emblems of
the red crescent or red lion on a white background or any colourable imitation thereof or
the words “Red Cross”.
(2) A person who contravenes the provisions of subsection (1) shall be guilty of an
offence and liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of two hundred and fifty dollars and
to imprisonment for six months.
10\. Vesting of assets and liabilities, etc.
(1) On the date of commencement of this Act—
(a)

all the assets and liabilities of the Branch shall be transferred to and vested in
the Society by virtue of this section, without further assurance, and the
Society shall have all the powers necessary to take possession of, recover
and deal with such assets and discharge such liabilities;

(b)

all deeds, bonds, instruments and contracts subsisting immediately before the
date of commencement of this Act to which the Branch is party shall be of as
full force and effect against or in favour of the Society and enforceable as
fully and effectually as if, instead of the Branch, the Society had been a party
thereto.

(2) The first members of the General Assembly shall be those persons who
immediately before the commencement of this Act are the members of the Committee of
the Branch.
(3) Officers of the Branch shall hold corresponding appointments in the Society until
other provision is made pursuant to the rules, and life associates and associates of the
Branch shall become corresponding life associates and associates of the Society, but
subject to any provision in the rules.

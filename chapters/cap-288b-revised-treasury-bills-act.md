## CHAPTER 288B

REVISED TREASURY BILLS ACT
An Act to provide a means of authorised borrowing of money for the public uses of
Grenada by the issue of Treasury Bills.
[Act No. 4 of 2003 amended by Act No. 9 of 2011.]

[28th March, 2003.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Revised Treasury Bills Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“bidders” refers to a person, group, broker, dealer, organisation, firm or company
that purchases Treasury Bills;
“Central Bank” means the Eastern Caribbean Central Bank;

“conditions” means in respect of any Treasury Bill, the terms and conditions of that
Treasury Bill, which terms and conditions may be contained in the offering
announcement that relates to that Treasury Bill or in any other document;
“holder” means the person for the time being entered in the register as the holder of
a Treasury Bill and in each case includes such person’s personal representative;
“investor” refers to a person, group, broker-dealer, organisation, firm or company
that purchases Treasury Bills;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for finance;
“participating Government” means a Government which is party to the Agreement
establishing the Eastern Caribbean Securities Regulatory Commission made on the
24th day of November, 2000, the text of which is set out in the Schedule to the
Securities Act. The term “participating territory” shall be construed accordingly;
“Permanent Secretary” means the Permanent Secretary (Finance);
“register” means the register on which holders are by virtue of any enactment
required to be recorded and includes a register maintained in a computer-based
system;
“Securities Act” means the Securities Act, Chapter 299A;
“Securities Registry” means the Eastern Caribbean Central Securities Registry
Limited or such other securities registry approved by the Government of the State to
carry out the functions of a securities registry under the provisions of this Act;
“State” refers to the country of Grenada;
“via the internet” refers to the website of the Securities Exchange and of the
Eastern Caribbean Central Bank respectively.
(2) References in this Act to any particular Act include references to any subsidiary
legislation made under that Act.
3\. Authorisation of Minister to borrow by issue of Treasury Bills
(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the Minister is hereby authorised to borrow
by the issue within or outside Grenada of Treasury Bills, and the Permanent Secretary
when authorised in writing in that behalf by the Minister, may for that purpose issue
within or outside Grenada, within the terms of the authority and subject to any direction
given by the Minister, such Treasury Bills together with such further Treasury Bills as
shall be required to pay off at maturity Treasury Bills already lawfully issued.
(2) The principal sums represented by any Treasury Bills outstanding at any one time
shall not exceed forty per cent of the estimated revenue of the Government during the
financial year then current.
(3) All Treasury Bills shall be recorded in a computer-based system or in such form
as may be prescribed by regulations made under this Act.
4\. Principal sum and interest charged upon the Consolidated Fund
The principal sum represented by a Treasury Bill issued under the provisions of this
Act and interest thereon are charged on and shall be payable out of the Consolidated
Fund.
5\. Proceeds of Treasury Bills
The proceeds of the issue of Treasury Bills under this Act shall be paid into the
Consolidated Fund.

6\. Expenses of issue of Treasury Bills
The expenses necessarily incurred in connection with the issue of Treasury Bills under
this Act shall constitute a charge on and shall be paid from the Consolidated Fund.
7\. Amount and currency of Treasury Bills
Every Treasury Bill shall be expressed to be issued in respect of a sum of five hundred
dollars in Eastern Caribbean Currency or a multiple thereof and shall be payable at par on
a date no more than twelve months after the date of issue.
8\. Issue of Treasury Bills to be sufficient evidence of Minister’s authority
The issue of Treasury Bills by the Permanent Secretary shall be sufficient evidence on
behalf of any holder thereof that the authority of the Minister has been given for such
issue in accordance with section 3, and that such issue has been made in accordance with
the provisions of this Act.
9\. Provisions with respect to issue of Treasury Bills
With respect to the issue of Treasury Bills the following provisions shall have effect—
(a)

each Treasury Bill shall be for the amount directed by the Minister;

(b)

the ownership of Treasury Bills, which are issued under the provisions of
this Act, may subject to the provisions of this Act, be evidenced and
transferred without a written instrument; and

(c)

the Securities Registry shall keep a register containing particulars of every
Treasury Bill issued under the provisions of this Act.

10\. Sum to pay principal and interest to be appropriated from Consolidated Fund
The Minister shall appropriate out of the Consolidated Fund of the State the necessary
sum to pay the principal and interest represented by the Treasury Bills under this Act and
out of the sum so appropriated shall remit such amount to the Securities Registry at such
time as will enable it to pay the principal and interest on every Treasury Bill when such
Treasury Bill falls due, and shall cause every Treasury Bill to be paid when it falls due.
11\. Principal and interest of Treasury Bills to be paid on instructions to the
Securities Registry
The Securities Registry shall effect payment of the principal and interest on every
Treasury Bill in accordance with instructions issued to the Securities Registry by the
holder of such Treasury Bills.
12\. Power of Minister to make rules
The Minister may make rules to prescribe or provide for—
(a)

the preparation, form, mode of issue, mode of payment, and cancellation of
Treasury Bills;

(b)

the procedures for making an offer of Treasury Bills;

(c)

the procedures for recording and transferring title to Treasury Bills;

(d)

containing such safeguards as appear to the Minister appropriate for the
protection of investors;

(e)

providing for the transmission of title to Treasury Bills by operation of law;
and

(f)

such supplementary, incidental and transitional provisions as appear to the
Minister to be necessary or expedient.

13\. Restriction on disclosure of acquired knowledge
(1) No person who has acquired knowledge in his or her capacity as secretary, officer,
employee or agent of the State shall disclose to any person or governmental authority the
identity, assets, liabilities, transactions or other information in respect of a holder
except—
(a)

with the written authorisation of the holder or of his or her heirs or legal
personal representatives;

(b)

for the purpose of the performance of his or her duties within the scope of
his or her employment in conformity with the provisions of this Act or rules
governing the operations of the Securities Exchange, the Securities Registry;

(c)

when lawfully required to make disclosure by any court of competent
jurisdiction within or outside the State; or

(d)

under the provisions of any laws of the State or of Participating
Governments, or agreement among the participating Governments.

(2) Every person who contravenes the provisions of subsection (1) commits an
offence and is liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding fifteen thousand
dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding two years, or to both such fine and
imprisonment.
14\. Repeal
(1) The Treasury Bills Act, Chapter 328 of the Revised Laws of Grenada, is hereby
repealed.
(2) Any Treasury Bill issued under the Repealed Act continues to be valid and may be
acted on or continued as if made under this Act.

TREASURY BILLS RULES
[SRO 7 of 1984.]

---

### 1.

Citation
These Rules may be cited as the Treasury Bills Rules.

### 2.

Contents of authority to issue Bills
The authority of the Minister in writing to the Permanent Secretary under the
provisions of section 3(1) shall state the nominal amount of Treasury Bills to be issued,
and the terms and the mode of issue of such Bills.

### 3.

Mode of issue of Treasury Bills
(1) Treasury Bills may be issued at fixed rates of discount or at rates of discount to be
determined after bids have been received.
(2) When Treasury Bills are to be issued at a fixed rate of discount such rate shall be
fixed by the Minister after consultation with the Permanent Secretary or the Central Bank.
(3) (a) The Permanent Secretary may, when so authorised in that behalf by the
Minister, invite bids for Treasury Bills by issuing an offering announcement in the
Gazette, or in any newspaper published in Grenada or in any participating territory, not
less than ten clear days before the issue date. The Permanent Secretary may also give
notice via the internet or in any official publication of the Government or the Central
Bank.
(b) The notice referred to in subsection (3)(a) shall specify—
(i) the period, not exceeding twelve months, for which the Bills are to be issued,
(ii) the subscription period during which bids will be accepted from investors for
the issue of Treasury Bills, and
(iii) the conditions which will apply to the issue of the Treasury Bills.
(4) Notwithstanding anything contained in these Rules, Treasury Bills may be sold by
Private treaty through private negotiation.

### 4.

Mode of bids and applications
Bids and applications for Treasury Bills shall be made to the Securities Exchange
through intermediaries in the manner prescribed by the rules of operation of the Securities
Exchange.

### 5.

Notification of allotment
(1) The Securities Exchange shall by the end of trading day advise successful bidders
or applicants for Treasury Bills of any allotment made to them.

(2) The Securities Registry shall provide successful bidders and investors with a
notification statement within ten days of trading day.

### 6.

Repayment of Treasury Bills
Upon maturity of Treasury Bills, the principal sum and interest shall be payable from
the proceeds of the Consolidated Fund in keeping with payment instructions
communicated by the holder to the Securities Registry. Payment shall be made on the
dates specified in the notification statement.

### 7.

Register of holders
(1) The Securities Registry shall record the names of holders of Treasury Bills on the
register, giving the requisite particulars and the terms of issue of the Treasury Bill.
For this purpose, the requisite particulars are—
(a)

the address of the holder of the Treasury Bill; and

(b)

the number of Treasury Bills concerned.

(2) The Securities Registry shall maintain proper records of all documents received
by it relating to or affecting the title to such securities. For this purpose, document
includes but is not limited to—
(a)

transfer forms;

(b)

instructions to transfer;

(c)

probate or letters of administration; and

(d)

powers of attorney.

### 8.

Application of Securities Regulations
In all matters of procedure not provided for by these Rules, the provisions of
regulations made pursuant to the Securities Act, Chapter 299A, shall apply to Treasury
Bills in the same and like manner as such provisions apply to securities in general and
shall in all respects govern the same.

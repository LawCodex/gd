## CHAPTER 194B

MINISTER OF FINANCE (INCORPORATION) ACT
An Act to provide for the incorporation of the Minister of Finance as a corporation
sole.
[Act No. 16 of 1994 amended by Act No. 2 of 1998.]

[15th July, 1994.]

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Minister of Finance (Incorporation) Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“Corporation” means the corporation sole established under section 3;
“Minister” means the Minister to whom responsibility for finance is assigned;
“property” means shares or stock in any company.

### 3.

Incorporation of Minister as corporation sole

(1) The Minister for the time being shall be a corporation sole by the name of the
Minister of Finance and all property transferred to and vested in the Minister by this Act
or otherwise acquired by the Minister shall be held in trust for the State.
(2) Upon and by virtue of the appointment of any person to be Minister, the benefit of
all deeds, contracts, bonds, securities or things in action vested in his or her predecessor
at the time of his or her predecessor ceasing to hold office shall be transferred to and
vested in and ensured for the benefit of the person so appointed in the same manner as if
he or she had been contracted with instead of his or her predecessor and as if his or her
name had been inserted in all such deeds, contracts, bonds or securities instead of the
name of his or her predecessor.
(3) Notwithstanding the provisions of the Crown Proceedings Act, Chapter 74, the
Minister may sue or be sued in his or her corporate capacity but only with respect to any
real or personal property for the time being vested in him or her.

### 4.

Seal of Corporation
(1) The Corporation shall have a seal which shall be kept in the custody of the
Minister and shall be judicially noticed as such.
(2) The seal of the Corporation may be affixed to documents and instruments in the
presence of the Minister and shall be attested by the signature of the Minister; and the
signature shall be sufficient evidence that the seal was duly and properly affixed and is
the lawful seal of the Corporation.
(3) All documents, other than those required by law to be under seal, made by, and all
decisions of, the Corporation may be signified under the hand of the Minister.
(4) Notwithstanding the provisions of the Deeds and Land Registry Act, Chapter 79,
and the Conveyancing and Law of Property Act, Chapter 64, relating to matters
thereunder required to be performed and to the mode of their performance prior to the
registration of a deed, document or other instrument the affixing of the seal of the
corporation and the signing by the Minister in the manner set out in subsection (2) shall
be, and shall be taken as, sufficient evidence for the purposes of those Acts of the due
execution by the Corporation of any deed, document or other instrument.

### 5.

Office of the Corporation
(1) The office of the Corporation shall be situated at the office of the Minister in the
Ministry of Finance in the city of St. George’s.
(2) Service upon the Minister of any notice, order or other document shall be effected
by delivering the same or by sending it by registered post addressed to the Minister at the
office of the Corporation.

### 6.

Documents
(1) Any document required to be executed by the Corporation shall be deemed to be
duly executed—
(a)

if signed by the Minister; or

(b)

if signed outside Grenada by the person or persons authorised by the
Corporation so to sign, but in such case the instrument so authorising such
person or persons shall be attached to and form part of the document.

(2) Any cheque, bill of exchange or order for the payment of money required to be
executed by the Corporation shall be deemed to be duly executed if signed by a person or
persons authorised to do so by the Corporation.

### 7.

Functions of the Corporation

The functions of the Corporation shall be—
(a)

to hold such property as is vested in it from time to time by virtue of this
Act, any other written law or any agreement, sale or transfer;

(b)

to exercise its corporate powers in relation to such property as it thinks fit
subject to any conclusion of the Cabinet with respect thereto.

### 8.

Vesting of property in Schedule in the Corporation
(1) Notwithstanding any written law to the contrary on and after the commencement
of this Act—
(a)

subject to subsection (2), the property specified in the Schedule shall be
transferred to and vested in the Corporation;

(b)

all the rights and privileges, and advantages and all the liabilities and
obligations that the Government was entitled or subject to, as the case may
be, in relation to all property referred to in paragraph (a) shall be transferred
to and conferred or imposed upon the Corporation for the purposes of this Act.

(2) For the purposes of the transfer of any shares vested in the Corporation under
subsection (1) notwithstanding any written law to the contrary, the companies referred to
in Part II of the Schedule shall within three months of the commencement of this Act
effect the transfer to the Corporation of all shares specified in Part II of the Schedule held
by the State and by all persons on behalf of the State, in accordance with the directions of
the Minister.
(3) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1), the effect of the vesting of
property referred to in subsection (1) shall be that on and from the commencement of this
Act—
(a)

every existing contract or security to which the Government was a party
shall be construed and have effect as if––
(i) the Corporation had been a party thereto instead of the Government,
(ii) for any reference (however worded whether expressed or implied) to
the Government there was substituted, as respects anything falling to
be done on or after the commencement of this Act, a reference to the
Corporation.

(4) In this section, “security” includes a mortgage or charge (whether legal or equitable).

### 9.

Expenses charged on Consolidated Fund
All expenses of the Corporation that are directly connected with its functions under
this Act shall be charged on and be paid out of the Consolidated Fund.

### 10.

Audit
The accounts of the Corporation shall be audited by the Auditor-General in
accordance with the Audit Act, Chapter 22A of the Laws of Grenada.

### 11.

Minister may amend Schedule
The Minister may by order amend the Schedule and transfer and vest in the
Corporation any land or other property acquired by the Government after the
commencement of this Act and to divest the Corporation of any such property which no
longer vests in the Corporation.

### 12.

Saving

Nothing in this Act shall affect or be deemed to affect the rights of the State or of any
bodies politic or corporate or of any other persons except such are mentioned or are
referred to in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.

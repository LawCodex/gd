## CHAPTER 45

CHILDREN’S HOME ACT
An Act to incorporate Trustees and to make provision for the establishment of
a Home for Grenadian children having no responsible parental control.
[Act No. 9 of 1960.]

[14th May, 1960.]
Preamble
Whereas in the year one thousand nine hundred and thirty-five, several citizens of
Grenada formed themselves into a Committee called the Children’s Home Advisory
Board with the object of raising money by subscriptions and other voluntary efforts
to provide a Fund for the purpose of purchasing suitable land for the erection
thereon of buildings for the establishment of a Home for Grenadian children having
no responsible parental control.
And whereas the Advisory Board collected by means of subscriptions and other
voluntary efforts certain sums of money with the view to the same being applied in
the purchase of land for the purpose aforesaid.
And whereas the Advisory Board appointed Arnold Williamson and Albert Oliver Payne
also one Charles Henry Wells Garraway Lucas to be the Trustees of the said Fund
and authorised them to purchase with money belonging to the said Fund the
hereditaments described in the Schedule to this Act.
And whereas by Deeds of Indenture dated the twelfth day of August, one thousand
nine hundred and fifty-two and the third day of December one thousand nine
hundred and fifty-two recorded in the Deeds and Land Registry of Grenada in Liber
M. 9 on page 943 and Liber R. 9 on page 95, respectively, the lands described in
the Schedule to this Act were conveyed to the said Arnold Williamson the said
Albert Oliver Payne and to George Edwin Palmer Gentle as trustees in fee simple
upon trust that the trustees and the survivors or survivor of them and all persons
hereafter becoming trustees in succession to them should permit buildings to be
from time to time erected on any part or parts of the said hereditaments and also
permit any part or parts of the same to be laid out in gardens or as yards or

grounds in connection therewith to hold the said hereditaments and all buildings as
aforesaid upon trust to permit the same to be appropriated and used in perpetuity
under the name of the “Children’s Home” as and for the purpose of the
establishment of a Home for Grenadian children having no responsible parental
control.
And whereas the said George Edwin Palmer Gentle died on the second day of April,
one thousand nine hundred and fifty-eight, and on the twenty-fourth day of
November, one thousand nine hundred and fifty-nine, Clarence Renwick was by
deed appointed a trustee in place of the said George Edwin Palmer Gentle.
1\.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Children’s Home Act.

2\.

Trustees

(1) There shall be established a Board of Trustees (hereinafter referred to as “the
trustees”) for the purpose of carrying out the trust contained in this Act constituted as
follows—
(a)

Arnold Williamson, Albert Oliver Payne and Clarence Renwick, or the
survivors or survivor of them and such other persons hereafter becoming
trustees in succession to them as hereafter provided;

(b)

such other persons not exceeding three in number as may be appointed
from time to time by the Governor-General to be trustees whose
appointments shall in the absence of any definitive term fixed in the
instruments appointing all or any of them be for a period of three years
but who shall be eligible for re-appointment.

(2) Any of the trustees may at any time by instrument in writing addressed to the
Governor-General resign or retire from his or her office and from the date of such
instrument such trustee shall cease to be a trustee and the vacancy caused by such
resignation or retirement or by death of any trustee shall thereupon be filled by the
Governor-General—
(a)

in the case of any of the trustees named in subsection (1)(a) or their
successors, upon the nomination of the surviving trustee or trustees in
that class;

(b)

in the case of trustees appointed under subsection (1)(b), by the
Governor-General.

(3) The trustees shall have power to elect from among themselves a chairperson of
the trustees.
3\.

Incorporation of trustees

The trustees are hereby incorporated by the name “the Children’s Home Trustees”
and may sue and be sued in that name and shall have perpetual succession and an
official seal which shall be officially and judicially noticed, and may by instrument
under such seal convey, lease, assign, exchange, demise or otherwise dispose of any
land or any interest therein or any other property, and the seal shall be authenticated
by the signature of the chairperson and one other of the trustees.
4\.

Vesting of property in trustees

The property specified in the Schedule and all monies now vested in Arnold
Williamson, Albert Oliver Payne and Clarence Renwick as trustees for the “Children’s
Home”, shall be and the same are hereby vested in the trustees upon trust, that the
said trustees shall cause or permit a building or buildings to be erected upon any part
or parts of the said property the same to be appropriated and used in perpetuity as a
Home for Grenadian children having no responsible parental control together with so
much of the said property as the trustees may determine to be occupied therewith for

the accommodation and use of the said Home and subject to the powers contained in
section 3 to deal with the said property for the maintenance and support of the said
Home in perpetuity.
5\.

Investment of funds

The trustees may at such times as they in their absolute discretion think fit realise
any of the property of the trust and re-invest any of the monies arising from such
realisation in securities of whatever nature they shall think fit.
6\.

Trustees’ power of management of lands

The trustees shall manage or superintend the management of land in their
possession with full power to appoint and pay such persons as the trustees think fit for
the management, cultivation or maintenance of such land or any part thereof either in
its existing form of cultivation or plantation or otherwise and may do all things needful
or requisite for the development, preservation or maintenance of such land and for the
protection of the same including the power to let any part of the same for any purpose
and to accept surrender of tenancies as the trustees think fit and any expenses
incurred in the exercise of the powers of the trustees may be charged against the
income of the trust property.
7\.

Report and audit of accounts

The trustees shall furnish to the Governor-General as soon as possible and not later
than three months after the expiration of each financial year a report upon the
administration of the trust together with a balance sheet and statement of the revenue
and expenditure of the trust duly audited by some fit and proper person approved by
the Governor-General for the purpose.
8\.

Indemnity of trustees

No trustee shall incur any personal liability for any loss or damage occasioned by
any act or omission in the administration of the trust unless such loss or damage was
occasioned by wilful act or omission on his or her part.
9\.

Rules

The Governor-General may make rules for the better carrying out of the purposes
of the trust and without prejudice to the generality of the said power—
(a)

for the regulation of meetings of the trustees;

(b)

for the management of the Children’s Home;

(c)

for the form of the trustees’ report and accounts.

### 1.

All that lot piece or parcel of land originally part of Mount Parnassus Estate situate
in the parish of Saint George and Island of Grenada containing by admeasurement ten

the twelfth day of August one thousand nine hundred and fifty-two.

All that lot piece or parcel of land originally part of Tempe Estate situate in the

to the Indenture dated the third day of December one thousand nine hundred and
fifty-two.

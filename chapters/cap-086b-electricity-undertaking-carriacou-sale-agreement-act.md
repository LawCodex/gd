## CHAPTER 86B

ELECTRICITY UNDERTAKING (CARRIACOU SALE AGREEMENT) ACT
An Act to authorise and empower the Government to enter into a Sale agreement
with the Grenada Electricity Services Limited.
[Act No. 46 of 1992.]

[8th January, 1993.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Electricity Undertaking (Carriacou Sale Agreement) Act.
2\. Approval of agreement
The approval of Parliament is hereby given to the Agreement set out in the Schedule
to this Act, and it shall be lawful for the Minister for Public Utilities to do everything that
may be necessary and proper for carrying that Agreement into effect.

Schedule
ELECTRICITY UNDERTAKING (CARRIACOU SALE AGREEMENT) ACT
Electricity Sale Agreement
This Agreement is made the 13th day of October, 1992, between the GOVERNMENT
OF GRENADA (hereinafter called “the Government”) of the one part and the
GRENADA ELECTRICITY SERVICES LIMITED a private company incorporated in
Grenada (hereinafter called “the Company”) of the other part.
Now it is agreed as follows.

---

### 1.

In this Agreement unless stated otherwise the “Appointed Day” means the date appointed or
to be appointed by Proclamation in the Gazette as the appointed Day for the purpose of the
ownership of all the assets described in clause 2 being passed from the Government to the
Grenada Electricity Services Limited.

### 2.

The Government shall sell and the Company shall purchase
Firstly, all plant, machinery, equipment, tools, meters, instruments, transmission &
distribution lines, furniture, vehicles, stocks of spares, spare parts, wire and cable and other
things to which the Government was entitled before the “Appointed Day” in connection
with its business of generating and supplying electricity in Carriacou and Petit Martinique
(which business is hereinafter referred to as “the said business”). A brief description of the
major plants is attached in Appendix I.
Secondly, all freehold and leasehold land and all Crown land and all buildings occupied by
the Government before the Appointed Day in connection with the said business.
Thirdly, the benefit of all way leave agreements, rights of way, easement and other rights
and privileges affecting lands which were vested in Government before the Appointed Day
in connection with the said business.

### 3.

The consideration for such sale and for the agreement on the part of the Government
hereinafter contained shall be the payment by the Company to the Government the sum of
E.C. $1,171,800.00 on the Appointed Day.

### 4.

(1) The property hereby agreed to be sold is sold, subject as hereinafter mentioned, but
otherwise free from all incumbrances.
(2) The leasehold properties hereby agreed to be sold are sold in accordance with clause 5.
(3) The way leave agreement, rights of way, easements and other rights or privileges
affecting land, the benefit of which is hereby agreed to be assigned or otherwise vested
in the Company shall be assigned or otherwise vested in the Company subject to the
rent, covenants and agreements contained in the agreement or grants under which the
same are respectively held.

### 5.

The Government agrees to make good in the name of the Company the title of all lands used
in connection with the said business while the said business was under the control of the
Government.

### 6.

The said sale and purchase shall be completed on the Appointed Day when possession of the
property hereby agreed to be sold shall be given to the Company and when (or as soon as
may be reasonably practicable thereafter) the Government shall at its own expense execute
and do all such assurances and things as the Company may reasonably require for the
purpose of vesting in the Company the property hereby agreed to be sold.

### 7.

The Government agrees with the Company that until the Appointed Day the Government
shall—
(a) carry on the said business as a going concern and shall use its best endeavours to
promote the efficiency of and expand the goodwill of the same;
(b) maintain the property hereinbefore firstly and secondly described in at least as good a
condition and state of repair as at the date of the agreement.

### 8.

The Government warrants to the Company that, save as otherwise disclosed in writing to the
Company before the date thereof, the Government is not party to any subsisting contract for
the supply of electricity which cannot be discharged or otherwise determined without
penalty within six months from the date hereof or which is unusual or onerous in any respect
and the Government agrees with the Company that the Government will not, except with the
previous written consent of the Company, enter between the date hereof and the Appointed
Day into any such contract if such contract cannot be discharged or determined or is in any
respect unusual or onerous.

### 9.

The Government shall advise the Company of all existing contracts in relation to the said
business and all contracts so declared shall be assumed by the Company on the Appointed
Day.

### 10.

The Government agrees that it shall amend the Electricity Supply Act, 1960, to allow for a

3 cents per kwhr increase in the price of electricity charged to consumers.

### 11.

Both parties agree that the electricity rates in Carriacou shall be brought in line with that in
Grenada over a period of at least six month from the Appointed Day.

### 12.

The Government further agrees with the Company that between the date hereof and the fifth
anniversary of the Appointed Day, the Government will permit the Company to inspect and
take copies of all circuit diagrams, route plans, maintenance records, lists of customers and
other documents in the Government’s possession which relate in any way to the property
hereby agreed to be sold or to the said business.
In witness whereof the HON. PHINSLEY ST. LOUIS acting on behalf of the Government has
set his hand and Government seal and the Company has caused its common seal to be hereto
affixed, the day and year first before written.

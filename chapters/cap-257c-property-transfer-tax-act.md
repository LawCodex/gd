## CHAPTER 257C

PROPERTY TRANSFER TAX ACT
An Act to provide for the imposition of tax on the transfer of certain types of
property, and for related matters.
[Act No. 37 of 1998 amended by Act No. 5 of 1999, Act No. 5 of 2002, Act No. 22 of 2002, Act
No. 17 of 2005, Act No. 12 of 2007, Act No. 15 of 2010.]

[15th December, 1998.]

## PART I

Preliminary

---

### 1.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Property Transfer Tax Act.

### 2.

Interpretation
(1) For the purposes of this Act—
“citizen” includes—
(a)

a dependant of a citizen;

(b)

a firm in which the partners who are citizens have a right to more than onehalf of the assets or income of the firm; or

(c)

a company that is controlled by individuals who are citizens or by a
combination of one or more such individuals or companies;

“company” means any body corporate whether incorporated in Grenada or
elsewhere;
“Comptroller” means the Comptroller of Inland Revenue, Department of Inland
Revenue;
“dependant”, in relation to another person, means—
(a)

the spouse of such person unless she is living apart from him or her under a
deed of separation or the decree of a court;

(b)

a child under the age of sixteen years of such person;

(c)

any other relative of such person who is wholly dependent on such person
for his or her subsistence;

“firm” means an unincorporated body of two or more individuals, or one or more
individuals and one or more bodies corporate, who have entered into a partnership
with one another for the purpose of carrying on business in common for profit;
“Minister” means the Minister of finance;
“property” means land (including any estate, right, title or interest in, to or over
land, and includes a lease for a period of ten years or more) and shares issued by or on
behalf of a company;
“Registrar” means the Registrar of the Supreme Court;
“shares” includes debentures;
“tax” means the property transfer tax chargeable under this Act;
“transfer of property” means any transfer, whether by sale, exchange, gift or other
disposition, inter vivos, whereby any property or any estate or interest in any property
is legally or equitably transferred to or vested in a purchaser or any other person on his
or her behalf or by his or her direction;
“Valuations Officer” means the Valuations Officer attached to the Department of
Inland Revenue.
(2) For the purposes of this Act, a company shall be deemed to be controlled by
citizens, if—
(a)

more than one-half of the shares of the company are held by citizens; or

(b)

citizens possess sufficient voting power to ensure that the affairs of the
company are conducted in accordance with their wishes.

(3) For the purposes of this Act a company not having share capital shall be deemed
to be controlled by citizens if its effective place of management is situated in Grenada and
more than one-half of its directors are citizens.
(4) The provisions of the Act shall not apply to transfers of securities listed on an
exchange licensed but the Eastern Caribbean Securities Regulatory Commission under
the Securities Act, Chapter 299A.

### 3.

Administration
The administration of this Act is vested in the Comptroller, but the Minister may by
notice published in the Gazette direct that the administration of this Act shall be vested in
such other public officer, and from such date as may be specified in the notice and from
that date all references in this Act to the Comptroller shall be construed as references to
that other public officer.

## PART II

Imposition, Extent and Payment of Tax

### 4.

Charge to property transfer tax
Subject to and in accordance with this Act, there shall be charged, in respect of every
transfer of property which takes effect on or after the commencement of this Act, a tax to
be called the property transfer tax at the rates specified in section 5.

### 5.

Rate of tax
(1) Subject to this section and section 6, the rate of tax payable on the transfer of
property shall be as follows—

where the property to be transferred consists of land and the vendor is a
citizen, such vendor shall pay five per cent of the market value of such land;

where the property to be transferred consists of land and the vendor is not a
citizen, such vendor shall pay fifteen per cent of the market value of such
land;

(c)

where any property, other than land, is to be transferred and the vendor is not
a citizen, such a vendor shall pay ten per cent of the market value of such
property;

(d)

where any property is to be transferred and the purchaser is not a citizen,
such a purchaser shall pay ten per cent of the market value of such property
where the purchaser is not a citizen.

(1a) For the purposes of this section, where a mortgagee transfers property pursuant
to the enforcement of a mortgage whether by way of the exercise of his powers of sale or
consequent to an order for foreclosure or otherwise, the mortgagor of the property to be
transferred shall be deemed to be the vendor.
(2) Where property is transferred by way of deed of gift, tax shall be payable on the
value of such property exceeding one hundred and fifty thousand dollars at the rate
specified in subsection (1)(a), (b) or (c), as the case requires, mutatis mutandis.
(3) Where a transfer for the purposes of subsection (1) consists of land, other than a
transfer of land by way of a deed of gift, tax shall only be payable on the value exceeding
twenty thousand dollars.
(4) For the purposes of subsection (1), where there has been a number of transactions
between the same or connected persons relating to the transfer of shares and the aggregate
amount or value of the consideration for all the shares transferred exceeds twenty
thousand dollars, then, if the transactions have taken place over a period of two years or
less and relate to shares issued by or on behalf of the same company, tax shall be payable
as if all the transactions together constituted one transaction.

### 5A.

Transfer of property in approved tourism development
Where there is a transfer of property in any villa or apartment which is declared to be
an approved tourism development pursuant to the provisions of this Act, the rate of tax
payable on such transfer of property shall be as follows—
(a)

on the first transfer—
(i) five per cent of the market value of such property, payable by the
vendor, and
(ii) five per cent of the market value of such property, payable by the
purchaser where the purchaser is not a citizen of Grenada;

(b)

on any subsequent transfer—
(i) two and one-half per cent of the market value of such property,
payable by the vendor, and
(ii) two and one-half per cent of the market value of such property, payable
by the purchaser where the purchaser is not a citizen of Grenada.

### 5B.

Approval of tourism development
(1) A person who wishes to have a tourism development declared as an approved
tourism development pursuant to the provisions of this Act, shall make an application in
writing in the prescribed manner to the Board and shall provide such information as the
Board may require.

(2) On receipt of the application submitted pursuant to subsection (1), the Board shall
review the application and issue its recommendation to Cabinet.
(3) On receipt of the recommendation from the Board, Cabinet may by Order, declare
that the development is an approved tourism development pursuant to the provisions of
this Act.
(4) In order for the owner to benefit from the rates of annual property tax available to
a hotel as determined by Cabinet pursuant to section 3 of the Property Tax Act, the owner
shall make the property available for rent.

### 6.

Exemptions from tax
(1) Subject to this section, the following transfers shall be exempt from tax—
(a)

transfer by way of mortgage, a release or re-conveyance where the mortgage
release or re-conveyance relates to—
(i) the purchase of that property,
(ii) the erection, extension or repair of a dwelling house or other building
on the property,
(iii) the carrying on by the transferor of any trade, manufacture,
profession, vocation, undertaking or other kind of business;

(b)

any transfer of property effected by operation of law to a liquidator or a
trustee in bankruptcy;

(c)

any transfer of property from one trustee to another on a charge of trust;

(d)

any transfer by the Crown or by a statutory board which is in receipt of
public funds;

(e)

any transfer by way of deed of gift where the value of the gift does not
exceed one hundred and fifty thousand dollars;

(f)

any transfer of property in trust solely for the transferor;

(g)

any transfer by a trustee to the beneficiary of a trust where the beneficiary
was the settlor of the trust;

(h)

any transfer of property by way of testamentary disposition or on or pursuant
to an intestacy, including a transfer to the beneficiary of a trust created by a
testamentary disposition or on or pursuant to an intestacy;

(i)

any transfer by a charitable organisation or a non-profit company registered
under the Companies Act, Chapter 58A.

(2) A transfer of property shall not be treated by the Comptroller as exempt under
subsection (1)(a) from the tax payable under this Act unless a statement certifying that
the transaction does not form part of a larger transaction or of a series of transactions
between the same or connected persons is contained in the instrument by which the
transaction is effected or in a supplementary declaration relating to the transaction.
(3) For the purposes of section 5 and this section—
(a)

a person is connected with an individual if that person is the individual’s
husband or wife, or is a relative, or the husband or wife of a relative of the
individual or of the individual’s husband or wife;

(b)

except in relation to acquisitions or disposals of partnership assets pursuant to
bona fide commercial arrangements, a person is connected with any person
with whom he or she is in partnership and with the husband or wife or a
relative of any individual with whom he or she is in partnership;

(c)

a company is connected with another company—

(i) if the same person has control of both or a person has control of one
and persons connected with him or her, or he or she and persons
connected with him or her have control of the other, or
(ii) if a group of two or more persons has control of each company and the
groups either consist of the same persons or could be regarded as
consisting of the same persons by treating (in one or more cases a
member of either group as replaced by a person with whom he or she
is connected;
(d)

a company is connected with another person, if that person control of it or if
that person and persons connected with him or her together have control of
it;

(e)

any two or more persons acting together to secure or exercise control of a
company shall be treated in relation to that company as connected with one
another and with any person acting in accordance with the directions of any
of them to secure or exercise control of the company.

(4) In subsection (3), the expression “relative” means a person who is related by
blood to another person, and for the purposes of this definition a legally adopted child
shall be deemed to be related by blood to its adopted parent and to every person to whom
the adopted parent is related by blood.

### 7.

Payment of tax and power to remit
(1) All amounts due as tax under this Act shall, subject to the provisions of this
section, be paid to the Comptroller by or on behalf of a transferor or transferee within the
meaning of section 5(1).
(2) Where pursuant to any rules of court or to an order of the High Court there is a
transfer by the Comptroller of any property, being a transfer which is chargeable with tax
under this Act, the Registrar shall deduct from the proceeds of the sale or other
disposition of such property any amount payable as tax under this Act.
(3) Every document or instrument relating to the transfer of any property in respect of
which tax is payable shall, on payment of the tax, be stamped by the Comptroller—
(a)

with a stamp denoting that the amount of tax due in respect thereof has been
paid;

(b)

with a stamp denoting that no tax is payable in respect thereof.

(4) Where any instrument relating to the transfer of any property is stamped by the
Comptroller pursuant to subsection (3), such stamp shall, from the date thereof, be
conclusive evidence in favour of any person to whom such property, or any estate or
interest therein, is subsequently transferred that no tax is payable in respect of the transfer
to which the Comptroller’s stamp relates.
(5) Notwithstanding section 5(1), the Minister may, on application made by or behalf
of a person by whom taxes payable or paid, remit or refund the whole or any part of the
tax if he or she considers it equitable so to do having regard to all the circumstances of
the case.

## PART III

Determination of Value of Transferred Property, Objections and Appeals against
Determination

### 8.

Power of Comptroller to cause value of transferred property to be determined

(1) Subject to subsection (3), where on the transfer of any property after the
commencement of this Act—

there is no money consideration for such transfer; or

the Comptroller is not satisfied that the consideration for the transfer
represents the fair market value of the property,

the Comptroller shall, for the purposes of the tax payable under section 5 cause the fair
market value of property to be determined by the Valuations Officer in accordance with
established valuation principles and the tax payable, under section 5 shall, subject to this
Part, be paid on the value of any such property as determined in accordance with this Part.
(2) Where there is a transfer of property by way of lease for a period often years or
more, the Comptroller shall for the purposes of the tax payable under section 5, cause the
amount of consideration on which the tax is payable in respect of such transfer, to be
determined by the Valuations Officer.
(3) Where the property transferred consists of shares, the Comptroller may cause the
value of such shares to be determined by an independent and qualified valuer; and any
such valuation shall, for the purposes of an objection or appeal, be deemed to be a
valuation by the Comptroller.
(4) For the purposes of subsection (2), “lease” includes an underlease, sublease,
tenancy or licence and any agreement for a lease, underlease, sublease, tenancy or
licence.

### 9.

Power of Comptroller on discovery of non-payment of tax, etc
(1) Notwithstanding section 7(3) and (4), if the Comptroller discovers or it is drawn to
his or her attention that the tax chargeable under section 5 on the transfer of property after
the commencement of this Act—
(a)

has not been paid; or

(b)

has been paid on a consideration which is less than the fair market value of
or the actual price paid for the property in any case where the fair market
value has not previously been determined by the Valuations Officer,

the Comptroller may within five years after the date of the transfer cause the fair market
value of the property to be determined by the Valuations Officer or an independent and
qualified valuer, as the case requires, who shall forthwith give written notice of his or her
determination to the Comptroller and to every person by whom tax is payable in respect
of the transfer.
(2) Where the fair market value of property transferred is determined by the
Valuations Officer under subsection (1), the tax chargeable under section 5 shall, subject
to sections 10 to 12, be paid, within thirty days of the service of notice of the
determination by the person liable to pay such tax, on the value of the property so
determined, less any sum previously paid to the Comptroller.

### 10.

Objections to Valuation Officer’s valuation
(1) Any person who is dissatisfied with a determination by a valuation officer under
section 8 or 9 of the fair market value of any property transferred may, within fourteen
days of service of notice on him or her of that determination, stating the grounds upon
which he or she relies and the reasons therefor.
(2) The Comptroller shall with all reasonable despatch consider every objection made
under subsection (1) and may disallow it either wholly or in part.
(3) Written notice of the Comptroller’s decision shall be served on the objector.

### 11.

Appeal against decision of Comptroller
(1) Any person who is dissatisfied with the decision of the Comptroller on any
objection may, within fourteen days of the service of notice of that decision, in writing
request the Comptroller to refer the decision for review by the Land Valuation Appeal
Board established by the Property Tax Act, Chapter 257B.
(2) An appeal shall be limited to the grounds stated in the objection, but the Board
may in its discretion permit any ground of appeal to be amended.
(3) Where the Comptroller has been requested to refer a decision to the Land
Valuation Appeal Board he or she shall refer the decision accordingly with all reasonable
despatch.
(4) On the review of any decision referred to it under this section, the Land Valuation
Appeal Board may confirm, increase or reduce the amount determined by the Comptroller
under section 8 or 9 as the fair market value of the property transferred.
(5) Subject to the provisions of this section, the provisions of the Property Tax Act,

## Chapter 257B, relating to the powers, rights, privileges and procedure of the Land

Valuation Appeal Board shall apply for the purposes of the exercise by that Board of its
functions under this Act.

### 12.

Appeal against decision of Board
(1) A person who, pursuant to section 11, has requested the Comptroller to refer a
decision to the Land Valuation Appeal Board for review and is dissatisfied in any respect
with the decision of that Board, may appeal from the decision of the Board to the High
Court with thirty days after the date on which notice of the decision of the Board was
served on him or her.
(2) The decision of the High Court on an appeal under this section shall be final.

## PART IV

Miscellaneous

### 13.

Duty of companies in relation to transfer of shares
(1) No transfer of shares which is chargeable with tax under this Act shall be
registered for the purposes of the Companies Act unless there is first produced to the
directors, secretary or other authorised officer or agent of the company by or on behalf of
which the shares were issued a receipt for the payment of such tax or a certificate issued
by or on behalf of the Comptroller stating that such transfer is exempt from tax.
(2) Every company shall furnish the Comptroller with such particulars as the
Comptroller may require for the purposes of this act of any instrument of transfer of any
shares in the company.
(3) Where any company contravenes any of the provisions of subsection (1) or (2),
every director, manager, agent and officer of the company who is knowingly a party to
the contravention commits an offence and shall be liable, on summary conviction, thereof
to a fine of one thousand dollars or to imprisonment for three months, or to both such fine
and imprisonment.

### 14.

Tax to be charge on land
(1) Any tax due under this Act shall, until paid, be a charge on the land in respect of
the transfer of which such tax is due and payable.

(2) Where any document or instrument relating to the transfer of any property is
stamped by the Comptroller with a stamp denoting that the amount of the tax due in
respect thereof has been paid, that stamp shall, as from the date thereof, operate to release
the property from the charge under subsection (1).
(3) Notwithstanding subsection (2), subsection (1) applies to tax or additional tax due
as the result of the determination under section 9 of the fair market value of any property
transferred.

### 15.

Recovery of unpaid tax
Without prejudice to the charge created by section 14, all amounts payable as tax
under this Act are debts due to the Crown and are recoverable as such in civil proceedings
in the High Court or in a magistrate’s court, at the option of the Crown, notwithstanding
that the amount involved exceeds the normal monetary limit on the jurisdiction of a
magistrate.

### 16.

General penalty
(1) Any person who—
(a)

in any statement issued or claim made for the purposes of section 6(1)(a);

(b)

in any certificate issued under section 6(1)(i); or

(c)

otherwise for the purpose of avoiding the payment of tax or reducing the
amount of tax payable under this Act,

wilfully, or knowingly or negligently—
(i) makes any false or deceptive statement, or
(ii) gives any false or deceptive information,
commits an offence and is liable, on summary conviction, to fine of five thousand dollars,
or imprisonment for six months, or both such fine and imprisonment.
(2) Any person who aids, abets, counsels, incites, conspires with or induces any other
person to commit an offence described in subsection (1) commits an offence and shall be
liable, on summary conviction, to a fine of one thousand dollars or to imprisonment for
three months.

### 17.

Time limit for prosecutions
(1) Notwithstanding the provisions of any other enactment, any information in respect
of an offence under this Act may be laid three years after the offence was committed or
within one year from the day on which evidence, sufficient in the opinion of the
Comptroller to justify a prosecution for the offence, came to his or her knowledge,
whichever is later.
(2) The Comptroller’s certificate as to the day on which such evidence came to his or
her knowledge is conclusive evidence thereof.

### 18.

Prohibition on recording of deeds, etc.
No deed or other instrument relating to the transfer of property may be recorded by the
Registrar unless he or she is satisfied that the tax, if any, payable under this Act, in
respect of such transfer, has been paid.

### 19.

Regulations
(1) The Minister may make regulations—

for the registration of agreements, instruments and other documents relating
to the transfer of property; and

generally to give effect to the provisions of this Act.

(2) The form of the stamps contained in the Schedule shall be used for the purposes of
this Act, as appropriate.

### 20.

Savings
(1) Nothing contained in the Aliens (Land-Holding Regulation) Act, Chapter 13, shall
effect the operation of this Act.
(2) Subject to section 21, nothing contained in this Act shall affect or modify the
requirements of the Aliens (Land-Holding Regulation) Act.

### 21.

Amendment and repeal
(1) The Aliens Land-Holding Regulation Act, Chapter 13, is amended by repealing
section 19 thereof.
(2) The Land Transfer Tax Act (No. 8 of 1987), is hereby repealed.

### 22.

Commencement
This Act comes into force on the 15th day of December, 1998.

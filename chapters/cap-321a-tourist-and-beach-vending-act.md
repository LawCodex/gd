## CHAPTER 321A

TOURIST AND BEACH VENDING ACT
An Act to regulate vending to tourists and on beaches.
[Act No. 18 of 2001.]

[17th August, 2001.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Tourist and Beach Vending Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—
“approved area” means an area of a Designated Beach approved by the Minister
under section 5(2);
“beach” means an area of land adjoining the foreshore covered by sand and which
is not more than fifty metres in width;
“Board” means the Tourist Board established under section 3 of the Tourist Board
Act, Chapter 321;
“Declared Zone” means an area declared by order of the Minister under section 3
to be a Declared Zone;

“Designated Beach” means a beach or any area adjacent thereto designated by the
Minister by order under section 5(1);
“products” includes agricultural products;
“tourist vendor” means a person who holds a tourist vendor’s licence;
“tourist vendor’s licence” or “licence” means a licence issued by the Board under
section 7(2);
“vending” means the selling, offering or displaying for sale of any goods or
products or the offering of any service for money.
3\. Declared Zones
The Minister may by order declare an area or areas in Grenada to be a Declared Zone
for the purposes of this Act.
4\. Vending in Declared Zones
(1) Subject to this Act, no person may engage in vending in a Declared Zone unless
the person holds a valid tourist vendor’s licence for that Declared Zone.
(2) A person who contravenes subsection (1) commits an offence and is liable on
summary conviction to a fine of two thousand dollars and to imprisonment for six
months.
5\. Designated Beaches and approved areas
(1) The Minister may by notice in the Gazette approve areas of any Designated Beach
for the purposes of this Act.
(2) The Minister may by notice in the Gazette approve areas of any Designated Beach
for the purposes of this Act and such areas must be marked by appropriate signs.
6\. Prohibition of beach vending
(1) Subject to this Act, no person may engage in vending on any Designated Beach
unless the person holds a valid tourist vendor’s licence for that Designated Beach.
(2) No person may engage in vending on any Designated Beach except in an
approved area.
(3) A person who—
(a)

engages in beach vending without a valid licence; or

(b)

engages in vending on any area of a Designated Beach, other than an
approved area,

commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of two thousand dollars
and to imprisonment for six months.
7\. Application for tourist vendor’s licence
(1) A person who wishes to obtain a tourist vendor’s licence must—
(a)

apply in person to the Board in a manner approved by the Board;

(b)

be 17 years of age or over;

(c)

be of good character;

(d)

be in good health.

(2) If the Board accepts an application for a tourist vendor’s licence, and upon receipt
of the prescribed fee, the board must—
(a)

issue the licence to the applicant, with or without conditions as provided by
section 8(2); and

(b)

enter details of the licence in a register kept for the purpose.

8\. Conditions of licence
(1) The Board must specify in every tourist vendor’s licence—
(a)

the Declared Zone or Designated Beach to which its relates;

(b)

the activity to which it relates;

(c)

any conditions or limitations attached to the licence.

(2) The Board may specify more than one Declared Zone and more than one
Designated Beach in a single licence.
(3) The Board may attach to a licence any condition the Board considers reasonably
necessary and the conditions may vary for different Declared Zones or Designated
Beaches.
(4) When operating within a Declared Zone or on a Designated Beach a tourist
vendor must wear the licence in such a manner that it is clearly visible.
9\. Duration of licence
(1) Subject to section 10, a tourist vendor’s licence is valid for twelve months in the
first instance but may be renewed annually on payment of the prescribed fee.
(2) The Board may refuse to renew a licence on any of the grounds on which a licence
may be cancelled or suspended under section 10(1).
10\. Cancellation or suspension of licence
(1) The Board may cancel or suspend a licence if at any time the holder—
(a)

suffers from a communicable disease or from mental disorder making the
holding of a licence inappropriate;

(b)

is convicted of an offence under this Act or the regulations;

(c)

is in breach of any condition of the licence; or

(d)

otherwise behaves in a manner likely to bring the tourist industry in Grenada
into disrepute by his or her conduct as a tourist vendor.

(2) Before taking action under subsection (1) or section 9(2) the Board must make
due inquiry and give the tourist vendor an opportunity to be heard.
(3) A tourist vendor whose licence has been cancelled or suspended must return it to
the Board within 48 hours and a vendor who fails to do so commits an offence and is
liable on summary conviction to a fine of five hundred dollars and to imprisonment for
one month.
(4) The name of a tourist vendor whose licence is cancelled must be removed from
the register referred to in section 7(2)(b).
11\. Review by Minister
An applicant for or holder of a licence who is dissatisfied with a decision of the Board
under section 7(2), 8(1), 9(2) or 10(1) may in writing ask the Minister to review the

decision and the Minister, after giving the applicant or holder and the Board an
opportunity to be heard, may confirm, reverse or vary the decision.
12\. Harassment and offensive language
(1) A person who—
(a)

uses insulting, abusive or offensive language in a Declared Zone or on a
Designated Beach; or

(b)

harasses any person in a Declared Zone or on a Designated Beach,

commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of two thousand dollars
and to imprisonment for six months.
(2) It is a defence to a charge under subsection (1)(b) for the person charged to prove
that he or she had lawful authority or excuse to harass the person with respect to whom
the charge was brought.
(3) For the purposes of this section, “harass” means to molest, trouble, threaten, beg,
worry, accost, jeer at, insult, entice, induce or follow about any person and “harassing” is
to be construed accordingly.
(4) A police officer in uniform or who suitably indentifies himself or herself may,
without a warrant, arrest any person who commits an offence under subsection (1) in the
sight or hearing of the police officer.
13\. Regulations
The Tourist Board may by regulations prescribe—
(a)

the fee for a tourist vendor’s licence;

(b)

the form of application for a licence;

(c)

the form of a tourist vendor’s licence;

(d)

the conditions which must be attached to a licence;

(e)

any other matter reasonably necessary to give this Act full force and effect.

14\. Exemption
Sections 4(1) and 6(1) do not apply to a person in a Declared Zone or on a Designated
Beach respectively who holds a licence under the Licences Act, Chapter 172, which
authorises vending in that Declared Zone or on that Designated Beach.
15\. Repeals and savings
(1) The Tourist Industry (Vendor’s Licence) Regulations, 1986, are repealed and are
referred to in this section as “the repealed regulations”.
(2) The Beach Vending Act, 1998 (Act No. 3 of 1998) is repealed and is referred to in
this section as “the repealed Act”.
(3) A tourist vendor’s licence issued under the repealed regulations continues in force
until its expiry and may then be renewed under this Act, but subject to sections 9(2)
and 10 of this Act.
(4) The Declared Zone and Designated Beaches declared and designated under the
repealed regulations and the repealed Act respectively continue to have that status until
new orders are made under this Act.

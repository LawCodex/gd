## CHAPTER 74A

CRUISE SHIP COMPLEX DEVELOPMENT ACT
An Act to provide for the development of the Cruise Ship Complex in St. George’s;
to authorise the Government to guarantee the borrowing of monies for the
development of the Cruise Ship Complex, and to make provision for the payment
of the monies borrowed.
[Act No. 7 of 2003.]

[11th April, 2003.]
1\. Short title
This Act may be cited as the Cruise Ship Complex Development Act.
2\. Interpretation
In this Act—
“Authority” means the Grenada Ports Authority established by the Ports Authority
Act, Chapter 247;
“Cruise Ship” has the same meaning as in the Cruise Ship (Visitors Levy) Act,

## Chapter 74B;

“Cruise Ship Passenger” means a person who arrives in Grenada on a Cruise Ship;
“Fund” means the Cruise Ship Complex Development Fund established under
section 6;
“Loan” means the loan agreement executed for the purposes of section 4 and any
amendments made to the same as therein provided;

“Marine and Dockside Facilities” means the marine and dockside facilities referred
to in section 3;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for finance;
“Paying Agent” means Republic Finance and Merchant Bank Limited or any such
other paying agents as may, from time to time, be appointed by the Authority.
3\. Authority to construct the marine and dockside facilities
The Authority is hereby authorised to procure and cause the construction of the
Marine and Dockside Facilities as part of a Cruise Ship Development at St. George’s
Harbour off Melville Street.
4\. Authority to borrow money
(1) For the purpose of financing the construction of the Marine and Dockside
Facilities the Authority is authorised to borrow a sum not exceeding twenty six million
five hundred thousand United States dollars (U.S. $26,500,000) the same being subject to
prevailing interest rates in terms of the loan.
(2) Any loan agreement executed for the purposes of the Loan shall be laid in
Parliament within ninety days.
5\. Authority to guarantee money borrowed
Notwithstanding the provisions of any other Act, the Government is hereby authorised
to guarantee the payment of the sums of money extended to the Authority through
overdraft facilities granted by the Paying Agent its agents and or subsidiaries for the
purpose of meeting principal and interest payments due on the Loan.
6\. Establishment of Cruise Ship Development Fund
(1) For the purpose of financing the construction of the Marine and Dockside
Facilities there is hereby established a Fund to be known as “the Cruise Ship Complex
Development Fund” the operation of which shall commence and terminate on dates
appointed by the Minister by notice published in the Gazette.
(2) The Fund established under subsection (1) shall not form part of the Consolidated
Fund and shall be kept in separate account to be managed by the Accountant-General in
the manner provided under this Act.
(3) Notwithstanding the provisions of any other Act, there shall be paid into the fund
established under this section all monies levied on or collected from Cruise Ship
passengers by the Authority pursuant to the Cruise Ship (Visitors Levy) Act, Chapter
74B.
(4) All payments made under this section shall be accompanied by such returns as the
Minister may direct.
7\. Transfer of monies from Fund to escrow account
The Authority shall transfer monies from the Fund into an escrow account designated
and managed by the Paying Agent for the purpose of making all payments to and in
connection with the loan.
8\. Authority to pay out of the Fund for the purposes specified
(1) The Authority is hereby appointed agent of the Accountant-General for the
purpose of collecting all monies for the Fund and is hereby authorised to pay out monies
from the Fund for the purposes of—

(a)

making all payments to and in connection with the loan; and

(b)

financing the general operations of the Marine and Dockside Facilities of the
Cruise Ship Complex.

(2) The Authority shall forward to the Accountant-General—
(1)

quarterly returns of all receipts of monies collected under section 6(3) and of
all disbursements from the Fund; and

(2)

any other information that the Accountant-General may require from time to
time.

9\. Application of surplus monies from the Fund
Subject to section 8(1), the Minister may direct the Accountant-General to pay any
surplus monies remaining in the Fund into the Consolidated Fund.
10\. Tax exemptions
Notwithstanding the provision of any law to the contrary, all remittances made
pursuant to or in connection with the loan are exempt from withholding tax and any other
taxes imposed under any law.
11\. Act No. 3 of 1990 amended
Section 2 of the Cruise Ship (Visitors Levy) Act, 1990, is amended by deleting the
definition for “Cruise Ship” and substituting the following—
“Cruise Ship” means a ship which arrives in Grenada whether on a regular
schedule, as a pleasure charter or howsoever otherwise and which transports people
who are called visitors, tourists, holiday travelers or howsoever otherwise called”.

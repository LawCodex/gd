## CHAPTER 44

CENTRAL GARAGE CORPORATION ACT
An Act to establish the Central Garage Corporation with responsibility to
supply and maintain vehicles and mobile plant and equipment principally
for the use of the Government, and to make provision for incidental and
connected purposes.
[Act No. 36 of 1986.]

[8th September, 1986.]
1\.

Short title
This Act may be cited as the Central Garage Corporation Act.

2\.

Interpretation
In this Act—
“Corporation” means the Central Garage Corporation established under section 3;
“financial year” means the period of twelve months from the 1st of January to
the 31st of December in any year;

“Minister” means the Minister for the time being responsible for public works.

Establishment of Central Garage Corporation

(1) A body corporate is hereby established, to be known as the “Central Garage
Corporation”, with perpetual succession and a Common Seal and power in that name
to sue and to be sued, and to acquire, hold and dispose of movable and immovable
property, and to do all other things necessary for or incidental to the proper
performance of its functions under this Act.
(2) The provisions of the Schedule have effect with respect to the composition and
proceedings of the Board of Directors of the Corporation.
(3) The Board shall comply with any special or general directions given to it by the
Minister.
4\.

Functions of the Corporation
The functions of the Corporation are to—

5\.

(a)

supply, on hire, vehicles and mobile plant and equipment including earthmoving plant and other heavy plant and machinery;

(b)

undertake repairs and periodic maintenance of vehicles, plant, equipment
and machinery of all kinds;

(c)

provide vehicle workshop facilities;

(d)

construct, alter and maintain buildings;

(e)

operate a fuel station;

(f)

supply fuel and oil duty free to the Government;

(g)

provide consultancy services in respect of mobile equipment.

Appointment and duties of General Manager

(1) The Board shall appoint, with the written approval of the Minister, a General
Manager at such salary and upon such terms as it may determine.
(2) The General Manager is the chief executive officer of the Corporation and is
responsible for carrying out the policy and supervising the activities of the Corporation.
He or she shall have general administrative control of the officers, employees and
agents of the Corporation. He or she shall be present at every meeting of the Board
unless he or she is incapacitated by illness or other cause or has obtained leave of
absence from the chairperson.
(3) The Board may, with the written approval of the Minister, terminate the
appointment of the General Manager if he or she has been found guilty of gross
misconduct, but before any termination of his or her appointment he or she shall be
given details in writing of the charge made against him or her and has the right to
appear before the Board and to be heard in his or her defence.
(4) In the absence of the General Manager, the Board may, by instrument in
writing and with the written approval of the Minister, appoint a suitable person to act
as a temporary General Manager during the period of such absence upon such terms
and conditions as it determines.
6\.

Appointment of officers and employees

The Board shall appoint, on such terms and conditions as it thinks fit, such number
of officers and employees as it considers necessary for the proper performance of the
functions of the Corporation.

Borrowing power

Subject to the provisions of this Act the Corporation may, with the written approval
of the Minister, borrow sums of money required for meeting any of its obligations or
discharging any of its functions.

Funds of the Corporation
(1) The funds of the Corporation shall consist of—
(a)

such sums of money as may be provided by Parliament for the use of the
Corporation;

(b)

any loan granted to the Corporation by the Government, or by any other
person with the approval of the Minister;

(c)

such sums of money as shall be earned by or otherwise from time to time
become payable to the Corporation.

(2) All sums of money referred to in subsection (1) shall be paid into and placed to
the credit of an account of the Corporation at a bank approved by the Minister.
9\.

Application of funds
The funds of the Corporation shall be applied towards—

10\.

(a)

the payment of salaries, remuneration and other allowances of the
persons employed by the Corporation;

(b)

the payment of interest and sinking fund contributions payable by the
Corporation;

(c)

the repayment of loans made to and of sums placed at the disposal of the
Corporation;

(d)

the creation and maintenance of a reserve fund;

(e)

expenditure authorised by the Board.

Temporary investment of funds

The Board may from time to time temporarily invest, with the written approval of
the Minister, any of the funds of the Corporation not required to be expended in the
meeting of its obligations or the discharge of its functions.
11\.

Land acquired compulsorily

If any land is acquired by the Government compulsorily for the purposes of the
Corporation under the provisions of the Land Acquisition Act or any other written law—

12\.

(a)

the Government shall be indemnified by the Corporation against all costs,
charges and expenses incurred by the Government in relation to the
acquisition;

(b)

the Board shall not, without the written consent of the Minister, sell,
exchange or otherwise dispose of any such land or any interest therein.

Accounts

(1) The Board shall ensure that proper accounts are kept, to the satisfaction of the
Minister, of the business of the Corporation, and a statement of accounts shall be
prepared in respect of each financial year.
(2) Each statement of accounts shall present a true and fair view of the financial
position of the Corporation and of the results of the operations of the Corporation in
respect of the immediately preceding financial year.

Audit and auditor’s report

(1) The accounts of the Corporation shall be audited in each financial year by a
qualified accountant (in this section called “the auditor”) appointed by the Minister.
(2) The officers and employees of the Corporation shall permit the auditor to have
access to and examine all relevant documents, and the auditor may require any
person holding or accountable for any such document to appear before him or her and
make a signed statement in relation thereto and may require such person to give such
information as he or she may reasonably demand.
(3) Any person required to produce a document, or to appear and make and sign a
statement, or to give information, who without reasonable excuse fails or refuses to
comply with such requirement is guilty of an offence and is liable on summary
conviction to a fine of five hundred dollars and to imprisonment for three months.
(4) As soon as practicable and in any event not later than three months after the
accounts have been submitted for audit, they shall be examined by the auditor who
shall send an audited statement of accounts and his or her report in respect of the
audit to the chairperson of the Board.
14\.

Reports to Minister and Parliament

(1) As soon as practicable, and in any event not later than one month after the
receipt of such audited statement of accounts and auditor’s report as provided in
section 13, the chairperson, on behalf of the Board, shall submit an annual report to the
Minister containing—
(a)

a copy of the auditor’s report and audited statement of accounts; and

(b)

a summary of the activities of the Corporation during the preceding
financial year in such form as the Minister may direct.

(2) The Minister shall lay a copy of the summary together with a copy of the
audited statement of accounts and the auditor’s report in each year before Parliament.
15\.

Annual estimates

The chairperson, on behalf of the Board, shall, not later than the 1st of September
in each year, submit to the Minister estimates of the revenue and expenditure of the
Corporation for the next financial year.
16\.

Transfer of public officers to the service of the Corporation

The Public Service Commission may, subject to such conditions as it thinks fit and
with the consent of the Minister, approve the transfer of any public officer to the
service of the Corporation, and any officer so transferred shall, in relation to pension,
gratuity or other allowance, and concerning any rights as a public officer, be treated as
continuing in the service of the Government.
17\.

Goods imported by Corporation exempt from duty, etc.

Machinery equipment, instruments, vehicles, materials and spare parts imported for
the uses of the business of the Corporation shall be exempt from customs and other
import duties and charges and from Value Added Tax.
18\.

Exemption from tax and rates on real property

Notwithstanding the provisions of any other written law, the Corporation is exempt
from tax, rates and other charges in respect of its ownership of real property.

Authority to stop and search vehicles

The General Manager of the Corporation may in writing signed by him or her
authorise any employee of the Corporation to stop and search any vehicle entering or
leaving the operating or maintenance sites of the Corporation.

Regulations

The Corporation may, with the written approval of the Minister, make regulations
for—

21\.

(a)

the appointment and remuneration of officers and employees, and
instituting a code of conduct and grievance procedure;

(b)

the payment of travelling and subsistence allowances and the rates of
such allowances;

(c)

defining the powers and duties of officers appointed to carry out the
provisions of this Act;

(d)

controlling the use of vehicles and equipment;

(e)

regulating and restricting the admission of persons to the premises of the
Corporation;

(f)

restricting and controlling the deposit of substances likely to cause
pollution;

(g)

securing the protection of persons and property generally;

(h)

prescribing and regulating the payment and collection of fees for services
rendered or facilities provided by the Corporation;

(i)

schemes for pensions, gratuities and other retiring benefits for officers
and employees of the Corporation, their dependants or legal personal
representatives.

Vesting of assets

All the assets of, or assigned to, the former Central Garage Unit vest in the
Corporation by virtue of this section without further assurance.

Composition and Proceedings of the Board of Directors
[Section 3(2).]

### 1.

Board of Directors

(1) There shall be a Board of Directors of the Corporation which, subject to the
provisions of this Act, shall be responsible for the policy and the general administration
of the affairs of the Corporation.
(2) The Board shall consist of not less than seven nor more than nine members
appointed by the Minister.

### 2.

Chairperson

The Minister shall appoint a chairperson and deputy chairperson of the Board from
among the members of the Board.

Tenure of Office

A member of the Board shall hold office for such period, not exceeding two years,
as the Minister may specify in the instrument of appointment, and each member shall
be eligible for re-appointment.

Temporary appointment

The Minister may, by instrument in writing, appoint a suitable person as a member
of the Board to act temporarily in the place of any member of the Board who is absent
or unable to act.

### 5.

Resignation

(1) Any member of the Board, except the chairperson and deputy chairperson,
may at any time resign his or her office by notice in writing sent to the chairperson
who shall forthwith cause the written notice to be forwarded to the Minister, and upon
the date of receipt by the Minister of the notice that member shall cease to be a
member of the Board.
(2) The chairperson or deputy chairperson may resign his or her office by notice in
writing sent to the Minister and upon the date of receipt by the Minister of the notice
the resignation shall become effective.

### 6.

Publication of Board membership

The names of the members of the Board and the details of every change of
membership thereof shall be published in the Gazette as occasion requires.

### 7.

Authentication of Seal

The Common Seal of the Corporation shall be kept in the custody of the chairperson
or deputy chairperson or such officer of the Corporation as the Board may approve and
shall be affixed to all documents required by law to be under seal. It is to be
authenticated by the signature of—

### 8.

(a)

the chairperson or one other director authorised to act in that behalf; and

(b)

the General Manager or secretary.

Procedure at meetings

(1) The Board shall meet at such times as may be necessary or expedient for the
transaction of business, and such meetings shall be held at such places and times and
on such days as the Board shall determine.
(2) The quorum of the Board including the chairperson shall be four if there are
seven members of the Board, and five if there are nine members.
(3) Subject to the provisions of this Schedule the Board may regulate its own
proceedings.
(4) The validity of any proceeding of the Board shall not be affected by any
vacancy nor by any defect in the appointment of a director thereof.

### 9.

Remuneration of Board members

The chairperson and the members of the Board shall be paid such remuneration, if
any, whether by way of salaries, travelling or other allowances, as the Minister may
determine.

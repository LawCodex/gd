Act 13 391

GRENADA
ACT NO. 13 OF 2012
I assent,

4th April, 2012.

CARLYLE ARNOLD GLEAN
Governor-General.

AN ACT to appropriate a sum not exceeding one billion,
twenty three million, four hundred and eighty-two
thousand, six hundred and twenty-three dollars
($1,023,482,623) to the service of the State of Grenada for
the period January 1st to December 31st, 2012.
[5th April 2012].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same as
follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

APPROPRIATION ACT, 2012.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund Appropriation.
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the
period January 1st to December 31st, 2012, a sum not

392 Act 13

exceeding one billion, twenty three million, four hundred and
eighty-two thousand, six hundred and twenty-three dollars
($1,023,482,623).
(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is appropriated
for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of twenty-five
million, one hundred and fifteen thousand, three hundred
and one dollars and thirteen cents ($25,115,301.13) to the
service of the State of Grenada for the period 1st October
to 31st December, 2010.
[5th April, 2012].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same as
follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION (NO. 4)
(2010) ACT, 2012.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund Appropriation.
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the
period October 1st to December 31st, 2010, a sum not
exceeding twenty-five million, one hundred and fifteen

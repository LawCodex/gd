## PART II

CONSTITUTION AND ADMINISTRATION

### 3.

––(1) The Body commonly known as the Grenada Cadet
Corps is hereby established and continued in accordance
with the provision of this Act and shall for that be called the
Grenada Cadet Corps.

Establishment
of Cadet
Corps.

(2) The Cadet Corps shall be under the command of
a Commandant appointed by the Governor-General.
(3) The appointment of the Commandant shall be for
a period of three years in the first instance and he may be
eligible for re-appointment for a further term of three years.
(4) The Cadet Corps shall consist of the rank of Commandant and such other ranks as prescribed in Schedule 1.

### 4.

The Minister shall have general control and administration of the Cadet Corps and the powers vested in the
Commandant shall be exercised subject to and in accordance
with any directions of the Minister.

Power of
Minister in
relation to the
Cadet Corps.

Functions of the
Cadet Corps.

### 5.

The functions of the Cadet Corps include the following–
(a) inspire young persons to achieve success
with a spirit of service and community, and
to develop the qualities of good citizenship,
responsibility and leadership;
(b) to provide a formal, well-regulated and highly
disciplined organisation operating within
schools and communities;
(c) to attract and train as cadets, a significant
number of young persons between the ages of
eleven and twenty;
(d) to promote self-respect, self-confidence, selfreliance, self-discipline and loyalty among
members of the Cadet Corps;
(e) to promote the value of leadership and the
ability to work and function as a team;
(f) to provide progressive training of a challenging
and exciting nature in order to foster
alertness, physical and mental endurance and
camaraderie;
(g) to provide military-style training and discipline
among the membership of the Cadet Corps;
(h) to establish the highest possible standards of
excellence in all areas of operations of the
Cadet Corps.

### 6.

There is hereby established a Committee which shall
be known as the Cadet Corps Committee for the purpose of–
(a) advising the Minister on matters of policy
relating to the Cadet Corps;

––(1) The Advisory Committee shall consist of–

(b) may at any time resign his office by instrument
in writing addressed to the Governor-General
and transmitted through the Chairperson of the

(3) The Governor-General may at any time giving
reasons therefor, revoke the appointment of a member of the
Committee appointed under paragraph (f).
(4) The procedure of the Committee shall be
prescribed by regulations made under this Act.
Secretary to the

### 8.

A member of the Committee shall be appointed as
Secretary to the Committee.

Meetings of the
Committee.

### 9.

The Committee shall hold quarterly meetings in each
year and may adjourn such meetings from time to time but
the Committee may hold meetings more frequently at such

times as it thinks fit for the purpose of dealing with any
matters referred to it.

### 10.

The Committee shall cause to be prepared an annual
report of its proceedings which shall be sent to the Minister.

### 11.

The daily administration of the Cadet Corps shall be
vested in the Commandant.

### 12.

The funds of the Cadet Corps shall consist of–
(a) monies appropriated for the purposes of the
Cadet Corps by Parliament; or
(b) grants made to the Cadet Corps.

## PART III

POWERS, DUTIES AND PRIVILEGES OF MEMBERS
OF THE CADET CORPS

### 13.

––(1) Subject to this Act, the Commandant shall have
the command of the Cadet Corps and may–
(a) with the advice of the Committee make such
appointments and promotions in respect of
members of the Cadet Corps as he may see fit;
(b) make standing orders for the general governance
of members of the Cadet Corps in relation
to their control, recruitment, enlistment,
enrolment, testing, grading, disciplining,
discharge, training, arms, clothing, equipment
and other appointments, and particular services

Act 8

275

Report of the
Committee.

Administration.

Funds of the
Cadet Corps.

General
powers of the
Commandant.

as well as their distribution and inspection, and
such other orders as he may deem expedient
for preventing neglect and for promoting
efficiency and discipline; and
(c) issue a hand book, code of practice or such other
guidelines as he may see fit, for the guidance,
governance and regulation of members of the
Cadet Corps.
(2) The Commandant may delegate his powers to
any authorised officer, but shall not delegate his power to
hear any appeal relating to an offence against discipline or to
impose upon a member of the Cadet Corps any punishment
which includes reduction in rank or dismissal.
(3) The Commandant and any member of the Cadet
Corps shall perform such duties as the Minister may from
time to time determine as well as such other duties as may be
prescribed by this Act, regulations, or standing orders made
under this Act.
(4) The Governor-General may revoke the appointment of the Commandant giving reasons therefor.
Resignation.

### 14.

––(1) A Commissioned or Adult Warrant Officer may
resign at any time giving to the Commandant in writing not
less than one month notice of his intention so to do; and the
Commandant may, in any particular case, waive or shorten
the period of notice.
(2) A Cadet may resign from the Cadet Corps at
any time by handing in his uniform and notifying the Unit
Commander of his resignation.

### 15.

––(1) The Governor-General on the advice of the
Committee may, discharge a person appointed under section
18 if he is advised by the Commandant that the person is not
efficient in the discharge of his duties.

Act 8

277

Discharge.

(2) A member of the Cadet Corps may at any time
during his term of appointment be discharged if he has–
(i) been pronounced by a medical practitioner
to be physically or mentally unfit for further
service; or
(ii) in the opinion of the commandant, ceased to
be efficient in the discharge of his duties; or
(iii) been dismissed by order of the Commandant.

### 16.

––(1) A person ceasing to be a member of the Cadet
Corps shall forthwith deliver up to the person appointed by
the Commandant for that purpose or to the officer in charge
at the place at which he was last stationed, his equipment,
uniform and other items which have been supplied to him
which are the property of the Government of Grenada.

Return of
property.

(2) A person who fails to comply with sub-section
(1) commits an offence and is answerable to the Advisory
Committee through the Commandant.

## PART IV

CADETS

### 17.

––(1) A person attending an authorised secondary
school may apply to be enrolled in the Cadet Corps.
(2) Such a person shall be enrolled in the Cadet
Corps where–

Enrolment in
Cadet Corps.

(a) a unit has been established for the school which
he is attending;
(b) he is not less than eleven and not more than
twenty years old; and
(c) he has the written permission of his parent or
guardian to become a Cadet and;
(d) he is recommended by the principal of the
school he attends.
Grants of
commission.

### 18.

––(1) The Governor-General shall in his discretion
with the advice of the Committee by commission under
hand appoint fit and proper persons as Non Commissioned
Officers in the Cadet Corps.
(2) A person appointed under sub-section (1) shall
hold such rank as specified in the Schedule I, and every
commission granted under subsection (1) shall be published
in the Gazette.
(3) A commission granted under subsection (1) shall
be issued in the form set out in the Schedule II.

## PART V

MISCELLANEOUS

Salaries and
Allowances.

### 19.

There shall be paid out of money appropriated by
Parliament to the Commandant and members of the Cadet
Corps such pay and allowances as may be determined from
time to time.

### 20.

The Cadet Corps, the Cadet Corps Advisory Committee, the Commandant, or any other member of the Cadet
Corps, shall not be liable in damages for anything done or
omitted to be done in the discharge or purported discharge of
their respective functions or duties under this Act unless it is
shown that the act or omission was in bad faith or constituted
wilful misconduct or negligence.

### 21.

––(1)A person appointed under section 18 shall, before
assuming his or her duties, take before the Governor-General
the Oath or Affirmation of Allegiance set out in Schedule II.

Immunity.

Oath of

Etc. Schedule.

(2) A Cadet shall, upon enrolment in the Cadet
Corps, make before an Officer the Cadet’s promise set out in
the Schedule II.

### 22.

––(1) A person other than a member of the Cadet
Corps who, without permission of the Commandant, wears
any uniform of the Cadet Corps, or any dress having the
same appearance or bearing any of the regimental or other
distinctive marks of any such uniform, is guilty of an offence
and liable on summary conviction to a fine of one thousand
dollars and to imprisonment for six months.

Cadet Uniform
not to be
worn without
authority.

(2) Sub-section (1) does not apply to a person who
wears a uniform or dress in the course of a stage play
performed in a place duly authorised for public performance
of stage plays, provided that permission in writing for so
doing is granted by the Commandant.

### 23.

––(1) In accordance with section 3, the person who
immediately before the commencement of this Act held the
office of Commandant, shall be deemed for all purposes to
have been validly appointed to the office.

Validation of
acts done.

(2) All persons who immediately before the
commencement of the Act held commissions shall be
deemed to have been validly appointed to the rank he held
immediately before the commencement of this Act.
(3) All acts, appointments and decisions made by the
management committee and the Commandant immediately
before the commencement of the Act, shall be deemed for
all purposes to have been legally done.
Use of firearms
during parades.

Regulations and
Standing Orders.

### 24.

Notwithstanding the provisions of any law, members
of the Cadet Corps shall be entitled, to carry firearms during
parades.

### 25.

––(1) The Minster may make regulations for the better
carrying out of the provisions of this Act.
(2) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection
(1), regulations made under this section may provide for the(a) administration of the Cadet Corps, in
particular the discipline, training, control, and
organisation of the Cadet Corps;
(b) enlistment, appointment, rank, precedence,
promotion and command of members of the
Cadet Corps, so far as not provided by this Act;
(c) discharge of duties by the Cadet Corps;
(d) uniform, insignia and badge to be worn by
members of the Cadet Corps;
(e) medals, badges and other awards to be granted
to members of the Cadet Corps for such

services or acts, and the conditions for the
granting thereof;
(f) colours and awards of the Cadet Corps; and
(g) constitution, organisation and strength of the
Cadet Corps.

(3) Subject to subsection (4), the Commandant may,
after consultation with the Committee, issue standing orders
to regulate matters of details.
(4) Standing orders issued under this Act by the
Commandant must be consistent with regulations, orders or
directions made by the Minster.

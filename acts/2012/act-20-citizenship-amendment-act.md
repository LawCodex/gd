AN ACT to amend the Citizenship Act Cap. 54 of the
Continuous Revised Edition of the Laws of Grenada 2010.
[ 3rd August, 2012 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Representatives and by the authority of the same as
follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

CITIZENSHIP (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2012.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Citizenship Act Cap. 54 of
the Continuous Revised Edition of the Laws of
Grenada 2010.

Interpretation.

578 Act 20

Amendment of
Section 7 of
principal Act.

### 3.

Sub-section (1)(b) of section 7 of the principal Act
is hereby repealed and replaced as follows:
“that he or she is the holder of a permit of residence
issued by the Minister with responsibility for
Immigration, and has resided in Grenada
throughout the period of twelve months
immediately preceding the date of his or her
application”.”

Passed by the House of Representatives this 26th day of June, 2012.

RAPHAEL DONALD
Acting Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 10th day of July, 2012.

RAPHAEL DONALD
Acting Clerk to the Senate.

## PART I

PRELIMINARY

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

REGISTRATION OF BUSINESS NAMES ACT, 2012.

### 2.

The objects of this Act are to–

Objects of Act.

Interpretation.

require the disclosure of the true name of any
person who is conducting business in Grenada
under a name other than that person’s true name;
and

(b)

provide for a Register of Business Names.

### 3.

In this Act–
“applicant” means an individual applying for registration of a business name, whether on that individual’s own behalf or on behalf of a body
corporate;
“business” includes a profession;
“business name” means a word or name, other than a
person’s true name, used by that person to
identify that person’s business;
“Companies Act” means the Companies Act No. 35 of
1994;
“contact details” include name, address, telephone
numbers and email addresses;
“electronic” means created, recorded, transmitted or
stored in digital or other intangible form, by
electronic, magnetic, optical or by any other
means that has capabilities for creation,
recording, transmission or storage similar to
those means;
“former Act” means the Registration of Business
Names Act Cap. 281;

“initial” includes any recognized abbreviation of a
name;
“International Companies Act” means the International
Companies Act Cap. 152;
“person” includes any individual, partnership, company, or other body corporate;
“prescribed” means prescribed in regulations made
under this Act;
“Registrar” has the meaning given by Section 2 of the
Corporate Affairs and Intellectual Property Act,
No. 19 of 2009;
“responsible individual” means–
(a)

in the case of a business owned by an
individual, that individual;

(b)

in the case of a partnership, each of the
partners;

(c)

in the case of a company, each of the
directors; and

(d)

in any other case, the individual notified
to the Registrar as authorized to act for
the business;

“true name” has the meaning assigned in section 4.
Persons subject
to this Act.

### 4.

—(1) This Act shall apply to any person who has a place
of a business in Grenada, and who carries on business in
Grenada under a name which is not the true name of that
person.

(2) For the purposes of sub-section (1), a true name
shall apply to–
(a) an individual, the surname of that inindividual coupled with one or more of the
individual’s other names, one or more of
the individual’s initials, or any combination;
(b)

a partnership, a name consisting of the
surnames of all the partners who are
individuals, and the corporate names of all
partners who are bodies corporate;

(c)

a company, its corporate name as registered
under the Companies Act; or

(d)

a body corporate other than a registered
company, the name of that body corporate
as set out in the legislation or constitutional
documents establishing the body corporate.

(3) A true name may also include–
(a)

in the case of a partnership, the forenames of individual partners or the
initials of those forenames or where two
or more individual partners have the
same surname, the addition of “s” at the
end of that surname; and

(b)

in any case, any addition indicating that
the business is carried on in succession
to a former owner of the business.

## PART II

### 5.

—(1) Every person who carries on business in Grenada Application
under a name which is not the true name of that person, shall for Registration.
apply to the Registrar for the business name to be registered.
(2) An application for registration shall state–
(a)

the contact details of the applicant;

(b)

the proposed business name;

(c)

the nature of the business;

(d)

the address of the business;

(e)

if the business is not owned by the sole
applicant, the ownership of the business; and

(f)

the date of commencement of the
business,

except where the application is made on the behalf of a
company, the applicant shall not be required to repeat
information already provided to the Registrar.
(3) Notwithstanding cases where an application is
made on behalf of a company, the applicant may be required
to state whether the business is registered as–

a taxpayer for the purposes of the
Income Tax Act; and

(b)

an employer for the purposes of the
National Insurance Act Cap. 205.

(4) An application shall be accompanied by the
prescribed fee.
(5) The Registrar may prescribe the form(s) and
procedure(s) of submission in which an application is to be
made, which may include electronic submission.

Confirmation of
Registration.

### 6.

On receiving an application made in accordance with
this section, the Registrar shall forthwith register the business
name and shall issue a certificate confirming registration.

Register of
Business
Names.

### 7.

—(1) The Registrar shall maintain a Register of Business
Names.
(2) The Register of Business Names shall be kept in
electronic form and shall be accessible to the public.
(3) The Registrar shall send information about additions
to, or changes in, the Register of Business Names, to such
persons as prescribed.
(4) Sub-section (3) shall not preclude the sending of
information from the Register of Business Names to such
other person as the Registrar may determine.

## PART III

RULES REGARDING NAMES

### 8.

—(l) A business name shall not be–
(a)

the same as a registered name, and

(b)

similar to a registered name, if such similarity
would be liable to confuse or mislead.

Prohibition
of duplicate
names.

(2) For the purposes of sub-section (1), a registered
name is–
(a)

a business name already registered in
accordance with the provisions of this Act;

(b)

the name of the company incorporated under
the Companies Act; or

(c)

the name of an international business
company incorporated under the provisions of
the International Companies Act.

### 9.

A business name shall not be registered if it–
(a)

includes a word or phrase prohibited by
regulations;

(b)

includes a word or phrase, the use of which is
restricted by regulations, and any conditions
applying to the restriction have not been
complied with;

Prohibition
of certain
words and
phrases.

(c)

suggests or implies a connection with the
State or of any Ministry, department, branch,
bureau, service, agency or activity of the
Government, unless consent in writing to the
proposed name is duly obtained from the
Minister;

(d)

suggests or implies a connection with a
political party or a leader of a political party;

(e)

suggests or implies a connection with a
university or a professional association
recognized by the laws of Grenada, unless the
university or professional association concerned consents in writing, to the use of the
proposed name;

(f)

is defectively inaccurate in describing–
(i)

the business, goods or services in
association with which it is proposed to
be used;

(ii)

the conditions under which the goods or
services will be produced or supplied;

(iii) the persons to be employed in the
production or supply of those goods or
services; or
(iv) the place of origin of those goods and
services;
(g)

is found to be objectionable by the Registrar.

### 10.

Where, through inadvertence or otherwise, a name
is registered which contravenes section 8 or section 9, the
Registrar may cancel the registration and direct the owner
of the business concerned to cease using that name.

## PART IV

REPORTING REQUIREMENTS

### 11.

—(1) When any change occurs in the information Notification
of changes.
provided to the Registrar in accordance with section 5, the
responsible individual or individuals shall notify the fact to
the Registrar.
(2) Notification of a change as required by subsection (1), shall take place within 14 days of the occurrence
of the change in question.
(3) The Registrar may determine the form in which
notification is to be provided, which may include electronic
submission.
(4) The Registrar shall record such change referred
to in subsection (1) in the Register of Business Names, and
issue a certificate as confirmation of such change.
(5) If the business has ceased to legally exist, or the
business name is no longer required, the Registrar shall cancel
the registration of the business name.

### 12.

—(1) Every business with a registered business name Annual
and
shall, once in every year, send to the Registrar an annual return
annual fee.
return together with the prescribed fee.

(2) The return shall–

include information current at such date;
and

(b)

be submitted within such time as may be
prescribed.

(3) The return shall confirm or amend the information
provided–
(a) in accordance with section 5 at initial
registration; and
(b)

if that information has been subsequently

information as most recently amended.

(4) The Registrar may determine the form in which an

Cancellation of
registration.

### 13.

—(1) If the Registrar has reason to believe that–
(a)

a business owned by an individual or a
partnership has ceased operation; and

(b)

that a business name for that business is
included in the Register of Business Names,

the Registrar may send a letter by registered post to the
responsible individuals, enquiring whether the registration of
the business name is still required, and stating that, in the
absence of a reply, the registration will be cancelled after a
period of 60 days.

(2) If the Registrar–

receives a reply to the letter sent under subsection (1), stating that the business name
is not required; or

(b)

does not receive a reply within 60 days,

the Registrar may cancel the registraion.
(3) The Registrar shall cancel the registration of a
business name where–
(a)

the name of a company is struck off the
Register of Companies, in accordance with
section 483 of the Companies Act; or

(b)

the name of a company or other body
corporate is struck off the Register of
Companies, in accordance with section 511
of the Companies Act; and

(c)

a business name is registered in respect of
that company or body corporate.

### 14.

—(1) A responsible individual commits an offence if Offences.
that individual, without reasonable excuse–
(a)

operates a business under a business name
which has not been registered, and without
applying for registration as required by section
5; or

(b)

fails to notify the Registrar of changes in the
address or ownership of the business in
respect of which a business name has been
registered as required by section 11; and

(c)

fails to deliver an annual return as required by
Section 12,

that individual shall be liable on summary conviction to a
fine.
(2) Where there is more than one responsible
individual, each responsible individual who knowingly
permits an offence under subsection (1), commits an offence,
and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine.

## PART V

ADMINISTRATIVE ARRANGEMENTS
Forms.

### 15.

—(1) Where this Act permits the Registrar to determine
the form in which an application, notification or return is to
be provided, the Registrar shall prescribe the details of the
form or the manner of submission of the relevant information.
(2) The information required by sub-section (1), may be
published on a website accessible to the general public.

Regulations.

### 16.

The Minister may make regulations–
(a)

prohibiting or imposing conditions upon the
use of specified words or phrases in a business
name;

(b)

concerning fees to be paid to the Registrar in
respect of any function required or permitted
by this Act;

(c)

concerning the persons to whom information
is to be sent in accordance with section 6;

(d)

concerning the timing of annual returns
required by section 12; and

(e)

generally, for the more effective achievement
of the objects of this act.

Transitional
provisions.

### 17.

Any business name which, at the date of commencement of this Act, is included in the index of firms and
persons maintained in accordance with section 14 of the
Registration of Business Names Act Cap. 281, shall be
deemed to be registered under this Act.

Repeal.

### 18.

The Registration of Business Names Act Cap. 281 is
hereby repealed.

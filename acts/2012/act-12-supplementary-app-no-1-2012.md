AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of twenty-one
million, seven hundred and sixty-five thousand, nine
hundred and thirty-two dollars and forty-five cents
($21,765,932.45) to the service of the State of Grenada for
the period 1st January to 30th September, 2011.
[5th April, 2012].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same as
follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION (NO. 1)
(2011) ACT, 2012.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund Appropriation.
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the
period January 1st to September 30th, 2011, a sum not
exceeding twenty- one million, seven hundred and sixty-five
thousand, nine hundred and thirty-two dollars and forty-five

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the
CO-OPERATIVE SOCIETIES (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2012.
and shall be read as one with the Co-operative Societies Act
Cap. 66A of the 2010 Continuous Revised Edition of the
Laws of Grenada, hereinafter referred to as the principal Act.

Short title.

Amendment of
section 121 of
the principal
Act.

Co-operative Societies (Amendment)

2012

### 2.

Section 121 of the principal Act is amended by repealing
sub-section (4) and substituting with the following
sub-section–
“(4) A loan shall not be made to a director or
committee member, on any terms relating to interest
rates, discounts or waivers or any other terms more
favourable than those offered to the general
membership”.

Passed in the House of Representatives this 20th day of July, 2012.

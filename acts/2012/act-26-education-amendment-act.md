### 4.

Section 132 of the principal Act is amended by inserting
immediately after sub-section (2), the following sub-section–
“(2a) Where a report of misconduct is made against a
teacher or a principal, and such conduct involves
misconduct of a sexual nature between the
teacher or the principal and a student, the
following emergency measures shall be taken by
the Ministry of Education and the Public Service
Commission–
(i)

the Chief Education Officer shall notify the
Public Service Commission immediately
after receiving a report provided by the
relevant school and parent(s) of the child;
and

(ii)

the Public Service Commission may,
within one week of receiving such
notification, place the teacher or the
principal on a temporary leave of absence
from his or her duties with pay, for the
duration of both a preliminary investigation
under section 133 and a pending
disciplinary enquiry under section 134, the
total period of leave of absence not
exceeding 60 working days.”

### 5.

section in the
after section 179, the following new section–
principal Act.

“179A. An educational institution under Parts IV, V
and VI of this Act shall, where applicable, apply

for a certificate of accreditation pursuant to the
provisions of the Accreditation Act No. 15 of
2011.”

# Electronic Crimes (Amendment)

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

1\. Short title
2\. Interpretation
3\. Repeal

GRENADA
ACT NO. 10 2014
I assent,

28th April, 2014.

CECILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

An Act to amend the Electronic Crimes Act No. 23 of 2013.
[16th May, 2014].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

ELECTRONIC CRIMES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2014

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Electronic Crimes Act No.
23 of 2013.

Interpretation.

Repeal.

### 3.

Sections 6, 16 and 25 of the principal Act are hereby
repealed.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 6th day of March, 2014.
ADRIAN FRANCIS
Acting Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 4th day of April, 2014.
RAPHAEL DONALD
Acting Clerk to the Senate.

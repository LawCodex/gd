CECILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of $14,696,828.61
to the service of the State of Grenada for the period July
1st to October 31st 2013.
[ 21st February, 2014 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION
ACT, 2014.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated
Fund and applied to the service of the State of
Grenada for the period July 1st to October 31st 2013,
a sum not exceeding fourteen million, six hundred and

Appropriation.

ninety-six thousand, eight hundred and twenty-eight
dollars and sixty-one cents in addition to the sums
already appropriated for that year.
(2) The sum mentioned in sub-section (1) is
appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

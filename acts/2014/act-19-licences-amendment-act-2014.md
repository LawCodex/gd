### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

LICENCES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2014.

### 2.

In this Act–

Interpretation.

“principal Act” means the Licences Act, Cap. 172.

### 3.

The principal Act is amended by deleting the words General
“Permanent Secretary” wherever it appears throughout the amendment.
Act and substituting with the words “Comptroller of Inland
Revenue”.

### 4.

Section 4(4) of the principal Act is deleted and
substituted as follows–
“(4) (a) A person who is liable to follow a trade,
profession or calling under this Act, shall pay a
yearly licence as prescribed in Part II of the First
Schedule.
(b) The licence shall expire on the thirty-first
day of December in each year.
(c) In all cases the full licence fee shall be paid
annually except in circumstances where a person
becomes qualified to follow a trade, profession,
business or calling after the 1st day of July of that
year. That person shall be liable to pay only onehalf the full licence fee.

AN ACT to amend the Accreditation Act Cap. 2A of the 2010
Continuous Revised Edition of the Laws of Grenada.
[ 11th July, 2014 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the
ACCREDITATION (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2014,
and shall be read as one with the Accreditation Act Cap. 2A
of the 2010 Continuous Revised Edition of the Laws of
Grenada, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

Short title.

238 Act 31

Amendment of
section 2 of
principal Act.

### 2.

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by–
(a)

2014

repealing the definition of Board and substituting
with the following definition–
““Board” means the Grenada National
Accreditation Board established pursuant
to section 3 of the Act; and”

(b)

repealing the definition of “Board member” and
substituting the following definition–
““Board member” means a person
appointed to the Grenada National
Accreditation Board pursuant to section 4 of
this Act.”

Amendment of
section 3 of
principal Act.

### 3.

Section 3 of the principal Act is hereby repealed and
substituted with the following–
“Establishment of Board
3\. There is hereby established a Board to be known
as the “Grenada National Accreditation Board” and the
Board shall be a body corporate.”

Amendment of
section 16 of
principal Act.

### 4.

Section 16 of the principal Act is amended in
sub-section (1) by inserting the word “Grenada” before the
words “National Accreditation Board”.

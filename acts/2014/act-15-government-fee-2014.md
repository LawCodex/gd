### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

GOVERNMENT FEES ACT, 2014.

### 2.

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires–
“function” in relation to a public body includes
the consideration of any application, any
examination and/or inspection, the grant of
any right, sanction, permit, licence, certificate

Interpretation.

or other document of a similar nature, by that
public body in accordance with any power or
duty under any provision of law, regulation or
administrative direction.
“public body” means
(a) the Government, and any department,
institution or undertaking thereof;
(b) any local authority;
(c) any authority, board, commission,
committee or other similar body, whether
permanent or temporary, for remuneration
or otherwise, providing a public function.
Minister of
Finance may
make fees
regulations.

### 3.

—(1) The Minister of Finance may make regulations
providing for the payment of fees in relation to the
performance of any function by a public body and for the
payment of fees for any matters incidental to the performance
of any such function.
(2) Regulations made under sub-section (1) may
provide for the time and manner of the payment of fees
and for all matters incidental to their due collection and
accounting.
(3) Regulations made pursuant to this Act shall be
subject to negative resolution of the House of Representatives.

General powers
under this Act
additional to
specific powers.

Fees etc.
prescribed by
written law.

### 4.

The powers conferred by this Act shall be in addition
to any specific or general power conferred on any public
body under any other provision of law and any such power
shall be construed as being without prejudice to the powers
provided for under this Act.

### 5.

Nothing in this Act shall be held to affect the amount
of any fees or payments chargeable under any written law in
force when express provision is made for the amount of such
fees or payments under that written law.

### 6.

All sums received by a public body shall be accounted
for in the manner prescribed by regulation and shall be paid
into the Consolidated Fund.

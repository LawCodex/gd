AN ACT to amend the Exchange of Information Act Cap.
93A.
[4th July, 2014].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives and by the authority of the
same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

EXCHANGE OF INFORMATION (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2014.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Exchange of Information
Act Cap. 93A.

Interpretation.

234 Act 30
Amendment of
section 3 of the
principal Act.

### 3.

Section 3 of the principal Act is amended by inserting
after sub-section (5) the following new sub-section–
“(6) A request for information sent by a foreign
regulatory authority should not be refused solely
on the ground that it relates to fiscal matters.”

Passed by the House of Representatives this 9th day of May, 2014.

the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

INTERNATIONAL TRUSTS (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2014.

### 2.

In this Act–

Interpretation.

“principal Act” means the International Trusts Act,
Cap. 152C.

### 3.

Section 50 of the principal Act is amended by inserting Insertion of a
new section in
after section 50, the following new section–
the principal
Act.

“50A. (1) An International Trust registered under
this Act shall not later than the first day of April in
each year after its registration, send to the Registrar
an annual update on its ownership information using
the prescribed forms.
(2) A Director or officer of an international trust
shall certify the contents of any update made under
this section.
(3) Where an international trust fails to comply
with the requirements of this section, the Authority
shall within one month of the date referred to in subsection (1) send a written notice to the International
Trust requesting that it complies within the time
specified in the notice and failure to do so could
result in the International trust being struck off the
register.
Accounting records
50B. An international trust as provided for under
this Act shall keep and maintain reliable accounting
records which shall–
(a) accurately reflect the financial position of
the international trust;
(b) include underlying documents such as
contracts, invoices and any other relevant
documents detailing:
(i) all sums of money received and
expended;
(ii) matters in respect of which the receipt
and expenditure takes place;

(iii) all sales, purchases and other transactions; and
(iv) the assets and liabilities of the relevant
entity arrangement;
(c) be kept for a minimum period of five years
from the date of the transaction to which the
record relates.
50C. Notwithstanding any of the provisions of
this Act, the Tax Co-operation Authority designated
pursuant to the Mutual Exchange of Information on
Tax Matters (Amendment) Act No. 24 of 2011, may
at any time–
(a) request an international trust to disclose any
document or information with respect to
the name of any settlor or beneficiary of an
international trust;
(b) inspect or make copy of extracts from the
Register without payment of any fee.”

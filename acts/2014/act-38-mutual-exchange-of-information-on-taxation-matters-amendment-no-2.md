### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–
MUTUAL EXCHANGE OF INFORMATION ON
TAXATION MATTERS (AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) ACT,
2014\.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Mutual Exchange of
Information on Taxation Matters Act No. 24
of 2011.

### 3.

Section 10 of the principal Act is amended as
follows–
(a) in sub-section (1) by repealing the number “(2)”
where it appears after the word “subsection” and
replacing it with the number “(1A)”;
(b) in sub-section (2) by repealing the number “(2)”
where it appears before the words “the notice” and
replacing it with the figure “(1A)”;
(c) in sub-section 3 by repealing–
(i) the number “(3)” where it appears before
the word “notwithstanding” and replacing it
with the number “(1B)”;
(ii) the number “(2)” where it appears after the
word “and” and replacing with the number
“(1A)”.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

PROCEEDS OF CRIME (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2014.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Proceeds of Crime Act
No. 6 of 2012.

Interpretation.

Amendment of
section 63 of
the principal
Act.

Proceeds of Crime (Amendment)

2014

### 3.

Sub-section (2) of the principal Act is hereby repealed
and the following substituted therefor;
“(2) (a) The staff of the Commission shall consist of
an Executive Director and such other staff as
shall be necessary for the efficient operation
of the Commission.
(b) The functions of the office of the Executive
Director and other staff shall be exercised by
persons with the requisite qualifications who
hold or act in such public offices designated in
that behalf by the public Service Commission
acting on the recommendation of the Anti
Money Laundering and Combating Terrorism
Financing Commission”.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 6th day of March, 2014.

ADRIAN FRANCIS
Acting Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 4th day of April, 2014.
RAPHAEL DONALD

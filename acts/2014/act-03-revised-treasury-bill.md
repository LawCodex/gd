# Revised Treasury Bill

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

1\.

Short title

2\.

Interpretation

Amendment of section 3 of principal Act.

GRENADA
ACT NO. 3 OF 2014
L.S.
I assent,

CECILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.
4th February, 2014.

AN ACT to amend the Revised Treasury Bills Act Cap.
288B.
[21st February, 2014].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and House of Representatives, and by the authority of the
same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

REVISED TREASURY BILLS (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2014.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Revised Treasury Bills Act
Cap. 288B.

Interpretation.

Amendment
of section 3 of
principal Act.

2014

### 3.

Section 3 of the principal Act is amended in subsection
(2) by deleting the words “sixty per cent” where they appear
and substituting therefor the words “seventy five per cent”.

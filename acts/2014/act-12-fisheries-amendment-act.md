# Fisheries (Amendment) Act

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

1\.

Short title

2\.

Amendment of section 8 of principal Act

GRENADA
ACT NO. 12 OF 2014
I assent,

28th April, 2014.

CECILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to amend the Fisheries Act Cap. 108.
[16th May, 2014 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada and by the authority of
the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–
FISHERIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2014
and shall be read as one with the Fisheries Act Cap. 108
hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

Short title.

Amendment of
section 8 of
principal Act.

2014

### 2.

Section 8 of the principal Act is amended in sub-section
6 by deleting the words “five thousand dollars” and
substituting the words “five hundred thousand dollars”.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 6th day of March, 2014.

ADRIAN FRANCIS
Acting Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 4th day of April, 2014.

RAPHAEL DONALD
Acting Clerk to the Senate.

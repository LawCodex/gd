GRENADA

CECILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to amend the Grenada Citizenship by Investment
Act, No. 15 of 2013.
[ 16th May, 2014 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

GRENADA CITIZENSHIP BY INVESTMENT
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2014.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Grenada Citizenship by
Investment Act, No. 15 of 2013.

Interpretation.

Amendment of
section 15 of
principal Act.

(Amendment)

2014

### 3.

Section 15 of the principal Act is hereby amended in
sub-section (3) by deleting paragraph (b).

Passed by the House of Representatives this 6th day of March, 2014.

ADRIAN FRANCIS
Acting Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 4th day of April, 2014.

RAPHAEL DONALD

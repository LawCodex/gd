AN ACT to amend the Companies Act Cap. 58A.
[ 21st February, 2014 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives in Grenada, and by the
Authority of the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

COMPANIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2014.

### 2.

—(1) In this Act–

Interpretation.

“principal Act” means the Companies Act, Cap. 58A.

### 3.

Section 344 of the principal Act is amended as
follows–

Amendment to
section 344 of
the principal Act.

(a) in sub-section (1) by inserting after paragraph “m”
the following new paragraph–
“(n) information on the ownership of the
company.”
(b) by inserting after section 344 the following new
section–
“Updating of ownership information
344A. (1) An external company registered
under this Act shall, not later than the first day of
April in each year after its registration, send to
the Registrar an annual update on its ownership
information using the prescribed forms.
(2) A Director or officer of the external
company shall certify the contents of any
update made under this section.
(3) Where an external company fails to
comply with the requirements of this section,
the Registrar shall within one month of the date
referred to in sub-section (1) send a written
notice to the external company requesting that
it complies within the time specified in the
notice and failure to do so could result in the
external company being struck of the register.”

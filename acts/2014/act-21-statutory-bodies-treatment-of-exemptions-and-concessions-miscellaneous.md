### 1.

—(1) This Act may be cited as the
STATUTORY BODIES (TREATMENT
OF EXEMPTIONS AND CONCESSIONS)
(MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS) ACT, 2014.
(2) This Act shall come into force on the 1st day of
August, 2014.

Short title and
commencement.

176 Act 21

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act–
“relevant authority” includes the Comptroller of Inland
Revenue and the Comptroller of Customs and
Excise; and
“Statutory body” means a body established by an
Act of Parliament with statutory powers and
operational autonomy to carry out government
related functions and which is part of or under
the direct control of the Government.

Treatment of
exemptions and
concessions.

### 3.

Where a statutory body now enjoys any percentage of
exemption or concession from the payment of custom duties
and taxes, any such percentage is now reduced by fifty
percent.

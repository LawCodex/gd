# Supplementary Appropriation (No 1)

Supplementary Appropriation (No. 1)

Act 35 901

GRENADA
ACT NO. 35 OF 2015
L.S.

I assent,
24th September, 2015.

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of $48,515,558.87
to the service of the State of Grenada for the fiscal
year ending December 31st, 2015.
[25th September, 2015].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives of Grenada and by the
authority of the same as follows—

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION (NO. 1)
ACT, 2015.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Appropriation.
Fund and applied to the service of the State of Grenada

902 Act 35

Supplementary Appropriation (No. 1)

for the period August 1st to December 31st, 2015, a sum
not exceeding forty-eight million, five hundred and fifteen
thousand, five hundred and fifty-eight dollars and eightyseven cents in addition to the sums already appropriated for
that year.
(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is appropriated
for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

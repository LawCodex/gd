### 2.

The principal Act is amended by inserting after section
12 the following–

Insertion of
section in the
principal Act.

924 Act 39

“Temporary certificate of registration
12A (1) Where the Minister deems it urgent or expedient in the
interest of public health, the Chief Medical Officer shall issue a
temporary certificate of registration to a person whose application
for registration is pending under section 13.
(2) A certificate of registration issued under this section shall be
valid for a period not exceeding twelve months, but may be extended
for a further period of twelve months.
(3) A certificate of registration issued under this section may be
subject to any condition as the Chief Medical Officer may deem
necessary”.

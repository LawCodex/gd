Governor-General.

AN ACT to amend the citation the Botanical Gardens Act
1968 to be Sir Eric Matthew Gairy Botanical Gardens Act
1968 and to rename the Botanical Gardens to the Sir Eric
Matthew Gairy Botanical Gardens in honour of Grenada’s
first Prime Minister Sir Eric Matthew Gairy.
[ 8th January, 2016 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–
BOTANICAL GARDENS (CITATION)
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2015.

Short title.

Act 42

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act, “Gardens” means the Botanic Gardens as
delineated and set forth in a Plan drawn by J. Landreth Smith
in February, 1891, numbered 31/31 and deposited in the
Public Works Department.

Citation.

### 3.

—(1) From the commencement of this Act, the
Botanical Gardens Act 1968 shall be known and cited as the
Sir Eric Matthew Gairy Botanical Gardens Act 1968.
(2) From the commencement of this Act, any reference
in any enactment, any regulation or any legal instrument to
the Botanic Gardens or the Botanical Gardens, as the case
may be, shall be read and construed as a reference to the Sir
Eric Matthew Gairy Botanical Gardens.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 23rd day of October, 2015.

GRENADA
ACT NO. 8 OF 2015
L.S.
I assent,

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.
6th January, 2015.

AN ACT to amend the Tourist and Beach Vending Act Cap.
321A.
[9th January, 2015].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the
authority of the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

TOURIST AND BEACH VENDING (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2015,
and shall be read as one with the Tourist and Beach Vending
Act Cap. 321A, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by repeal- Amendment
Section 2 of
ing the definition of “Board”, and substituting the following of
principal Act.
definition–

“Board” means the Board of Directors of the Grenada
Tourism Authority, established under section 5
of the Grenada Tourism Authority Act No. 42
of 2013.”

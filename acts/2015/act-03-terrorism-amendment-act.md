### 4.

Section 14B of the principal Act is amended by
repealing sub-section (4) and substituting therefor the
following–
“(4) (1) An Order made under sub-section (3) may
make such provision for the payment of the basic
and necessary expenses of a designated entity,
including–
(a)

any payment for food stuffs, rent or
mortgage, medicines and medical
treatment, taxes, insurance premiums
and public utility charges;

(b)

any reasonable professional fees and
reimbursement of expenses associated
with the provision of legal services;

(c )

any fees or service charge incurred for
the routine holding or maintenance of
frozen funds or other financial assets or
economic resources; or

(d)

any extraordinary expenses.

(2) In making provisions for the basic and
necessary expenses of a designated entity under
sub-section (1) the court must be satisfied that–
(a)

the United Nations Sanctions
Committee received notification
of the application of the designated entity; and

(b)

a request was made by the
Financial Intelligence Unit to the
United Nations Sanctions Committee for its recommendations on
the application of the designated
entity.

(3) The Court may–
(a)

within seven days of the notification referred to in sub-section
2; and

(b)

in the absence of a negative
decision by the United Nations
Sanctions Committee within forty-eight hours of such notification,

order the release of funds from the frozen
account as it may consider reasonable to cover
the basic and necessary expenses of a
designated entity.
(4) Where an application by a designated
entity is for the release of funds to cover the
extraordinary expenses of a designated entity,
the court must not grant an authorisation for
the release of any funds unless prior written
approval of the United Nations Sanctions
Committee is obtained.”

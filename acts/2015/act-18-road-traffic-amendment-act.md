### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

ROAD TRAFFIC (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2015

### 2.

Section 54 of the Road Traffic Act CAP. 289 is amended
by repealing sub-sections (4) and (5) and substituting
therefor the following–
“(4) The holder of a valid driving licence issued
in any of the other member states of the Caribbean
Community and Common Market (CARICOM) and
valid for a period of not less than six months from

the date of entry into Grenada shall be deemed to be
the holder of a licence to drive in Grenada, for the
ensuing six month period.
(5) A person to whom sub-section (4) applies shall
only be entitled to drive in Grenada a motor vehicle
of the class or classes for which he or she holds
an authorization to drive in the other CARICOM
member state in which his or her driving licence was
issued.
(6) In the event that a person referred to in subsection (4), is desirous of driving in Grenada beyond
the six month period referred to in sub-section (4),
he or she shall make the necessary application in
accordance with the requirements of this Act and
upon payment of the prescribed fee may be granted a
permit to drive for the period specified therein.
(7) The holder of a driving licence issued in
an OECS member state shall, while such licence
remains valid, be deemed to be the holder of a licence
to drive in Grenada.
(8) A person to whom sub-section (7) applies shall
only be entitled to drive in Grenada a motor vehicle
of the class or classes for which he or she holds an
authorization to drive in the other OECS member
state in which his or her driving licence was issued.
(9) For the purposes of sub-section (7) and (8)
the expression “OECS” means the Organisation
of the Eastern Caribbean States established under
the Treaty of Basseterre 1981 and continued under
Article 2 of the Treaty of Basseterre Establishing the

Organisation of Eastern Caribbean States Economic
Union 2010.
(10) The Licensing Authority shall keep a register
of all holders in the State who hold valid international
driving licences issued elsewhere and of all holders
of international driving licences and temporary
licences issued by Authority.”
Passed by the House of Representatives this 15th day of May, 2015.

AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of forty million,
thirty-three thousand, ninety-seven dollars and
sixty-nine cents ($40,033,097.69) to the service of
the State of Grenada for the period October 1st to
December 31st, 2014.
[24th December, 2015].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives, and by the authority of
the same as follows—

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION (NO. 4)
(2014) ACT, 2015.

### 2.

There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund Appropriation.
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the
period October 1st to December 31st, 2014, a sum not ex-

914 Act 37

ceeding forty million, thirty-three thousand, ninety-seven
dollars and sixty-nine cents ($40,033,097.69) in addition to
the sums already appropriated for that year.

### 3.

The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is appropriated
for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

AN ACT to amend the International Companies Act Cap.
152\.
[9th January, 2015].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same as
follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

INTERNATIONAL COMPANIES (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2015.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the International Companies
Act, Cap. 152.

Interpretation.

Amendment of
section 27A of
principal Act.

2015

### 3.

Section 27A of the principal Act is amended as follows–
(a)

by inserting after sub-section (3) the following
sub-section–
“(3A) The bearer share agent must retain
custody and control of the bearer share
certificate at all times, and must also
retain current information on the
identity of the owners.”

(b)

by inserting in sub-section (5) after the figure
“(3)” where it appears the following “or
(3A)”.

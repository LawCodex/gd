AN ACT to amend the Income Tax Act Cap. 149.
[ 8th January, 2016 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–
INCOME TAX (AMENDMENT) (NO. 3)
ACT, 2015
and shall be read as one with the Income Tax Act CAP. 149,
hereafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

Short title.

958 Act 43

Amendment of
section 68 of
principal Act.

### 2.

Section 68 of the principal Act is amended in
sub-section (1) by inserting before the words “For the
purposes of the administration” the words “Notwithstanding
any other enactment, arrangement, agreement or contractual
duty of confidentiality,”.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 23rd day of October, 2015.

WILLAN A. THOMPSON
Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 30th day of October, 2015.

WILLAN A. THOMPSON
Clerk to the Senate.

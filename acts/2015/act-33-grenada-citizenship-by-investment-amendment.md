### 1.

This Act may be cited as the
GRENADA CITIZENSHIP BY INVESTMENT
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2015.
and shall be read as one with the Grenada Citizenship by
Investment Act No. 15 of 2013 hereinafter referred to as the
“principal Act”.

### 3.

Section 8 of the principal Act is amended in subsection

“(4) Within a period as may be prescribed by the

### 4.

Section 16 of the principal Act is amended by inserting
after subsection (2) the following new subsection (3)–
“(3) Without restricting the generality of subsection
(1), the Minister may make regulations prescribing
procedures for and relating to payments into the
National Transformation Fund or for the investment
of sums due for involvement in an approved project.”

AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of $31,099,922.15
to the service of the State of Grenada for the period
November 1st to December 31st 2013.
[25th September, 2015].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives of Grenada and by the
authority of the same as follows—

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION (NO. 3)
(2013) ACT, 2015.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund Appropriation.
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the
period November 1st to December 31st 2013, a sum not

AN ACT to amend the Grenada Tourism Authority Act
No. 42 of 2013.
[9th January, 2015].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the
GRENADA TOURISM AUTHORITY (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2015
and shall be read as one with the Grenada Tourism Authority
Act No. 42 of 2013, hereinafter referred to as the “principal
Act.”

Short title.

34 Act 7

Amendment of
section 29 of
principal Act.

### 2.

Section 29 (2) of the principal Act is amended by
inserting the words “duly licensed” after the words “operating
a”.

Amendment of
section 38 of
principal Act.

### 3.

Section 38 of the principal Act is hereby repealed and
substituted with the following:–
“Fees.

38\. The Authority may charge such fees as may
be prescribed by Regulations made by the Minister–
(a)

for the issue and renewal of licences;
and

(b)

for any service performed by it.”

Passed by the House of Representatives this 13th day of November,
2014\.
WILLIAM THOMPSON
Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 21st day of November, 2014.

WILLIAM THOMPSON
Clerk to the Senate.

5th June, 2015.

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to amend the National Lotteries Authority Act,
CAP. 205A.
[ 12th June, 2015 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

NATIONAL LOTTERIES AUTHORITY
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2015.

### 2.

Section 2 of the National Lotteries Authority Act, Amendment
of section 2.
CAP. 205A is amended by repealing the definition of
“Minister” and substituting with the following–

““Minister” means the Minister appointed with
responsibility for the National Lotteries
Authority.”

Passed by the House of Representatives this 17th day of April, 2015.

WILLAN A. THOMPSON
Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 29th day of April, 2015.

WILLAN A. THOMPSON

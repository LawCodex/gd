AN ACT to amend the Proceeds of Crime Act No. 6 of 2012.
[9th January, 2015].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

PROCEEDS OF CRIME (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2015.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Proceeds of Crime Act,
No. 6 of 2012.

Interpretation.

18 Act 4

Amendment of
section 32 of
principal Act.

### 3.

Section 32 of the principal Act is amended as follows–
(a)

in sub-section (4) by deleting the figure
“50,000” where it appears and substituting
therefor the figure “250,000.”

(b)

in sub-section (7), by deleting the figure
“40,000” where it appears and substituting
therefor the figure “250,000.”

Passed by the House of Representatives this 13th day of November,
2014\.
WILLIAM THOMPSON
Clerk to the House of Representatives.

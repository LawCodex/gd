AN ACT to amend the Environmental Levy Act Cap. 90B.
[9th January, 2015].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and House of Representatives and by the authority of the
same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

ENVIRONMENTAL LEVY (AMENDMENT) ACT,
2015\.

### 2.

In this Act–

Interpretation.

“principal Act” means the Environmental Levy Act
Cap. 90B.

### 3.

Section 3 of the principal Act is amended–

Amendment
of Section 3 of
principal Act.

(a) in sub-sections (1) and (2) by deleting the words
“Second Schedule” where they appear and
substituting therefor the words “Third Schedule”;
and
(b) in sub-section (3) by inserting the word “Third”
before the word “Schedule”.
Amendment
of section 5 of
principal Act.

### 4.

Section 5 of the principal Act is amended by–
(a) inserting the figure “(1)” after the figure “5”;
(b) inserting the words “Subject to sub-section (2)
and section 7” before the words “the proceeds”;
and
(c) inserting the following after sub-section (1)–
“(2) The levy payable on tyres shall not be paid
over to the Authority.”

Amendment
of section 8 of
principal Act.

### 5.

Section 8 of the principal Act is amended by–
(a) deleting the word “or” after the word “First”; and
(b) inserting the words “or Third” after the word
“Second”.

Amendment of
section 11A of
principal Act.

### 6.

Section 11A of the principal Act is amended by deleting
sub-section (2).

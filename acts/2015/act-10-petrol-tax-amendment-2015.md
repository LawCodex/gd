# Petrol Tax (amendment) 2015

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

1\.

Short title

2\.

Amendment of section 3 of principal Act

3\.

Repeal of section 3A of principal Act

GRENADA
ACT NO. 10 OF 2015
I assent,

17th March, 2015.

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

An Act to amend the Petrol Tax Act CAP. 238A.
[17th March, 2015].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

PETROL TAX (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2015
and shall be read as one with the Petrol Tax Act Cap. 238A,
hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 3 of the principal Act is repealed and the Amendment
of section 3
following is substituted therefor–
of principal
Act.

“Imposition of
tax

### 3.

— (1) There shall be charged and paid
by the importer of petrol to the Comptroller
of Customs a tax of three dollars on each
gallon of petrol imported into the State for
local consumption.
(2) The Minister may by Order, amend
the quantum of the tax rate provided under
sub-section (1).”

3\. Repeal of section 3A of principal Act. Section 3A of
the principal Act is repealed and the following is substituted
therefor–
“Price of
petrol

### 3A.

—(1)The Minister may, by Order,
determine the domestic price per gallon of
petrol, which shall be the average over four
consecutive weeks of the c.i.f on the
importation of a gallon of petrol plus the
wholesale and retail margins and any tax as
may be approved by the Minister.
(2) A determination of the domestic
price of petrol under subsection (1) shall
only be made once in every four weeks.
(3) Each consecutive four week
period used in calculating the domestic
price per gallon of petrol, as provided
under subsection (1), shall start immediately after the last week of the previous
period so that there is no interval between
the periods and the data for any day used to
calculate the average for a particular period
may only be used on one occasion.

(4) The Minister may, by Order
establish guidelines to govern the
components with respect to the importation
and shipping of petrol.”

Passed by the House of Representatives this 27th day of February,
2015\.
WILLAN THOMPSON
Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 13th day of March, 2015.

WILLAN THOMPSON

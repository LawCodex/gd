AN ACT to amend the Exchange of Information Act CAP.
93A.
[ 4th March, 2022 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the
authority of the same as follows–

---

### 1.

—(1) This Act may be cited as the–
EXCHANGE OF INFORMATION (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2022
and shall be read as one with the Exchange of Information
Act, Chapter 93A hereinafter referred to as the “principal
Act”.

Short title.

Insertion of new

### 2.

The principal Act is amended by inserting after section
section 4A to
4 the following new section–
principal Act.
“Information on
money laundering and terrorist
financing.

### 4A.

Notwithstanding sections 3 and 4,
where a regulatory authority is satisfied that
it is in possession of information indicating
that a person is engaged or involved in money
laundering, any associated predicate offence or
terrorist financing, which may be relevant to a
foreign competent authority for the purposes
of combating money laundering or terrorist
financing, the regulatory authority may–
(a) furnish the foreign competent authority
with the information;
(b) produce any documents relevant to the
information; and
(c) provide the foreign competent authority
with any assistance in relation to the
information.”.

Amendment of
the Schedule to
principal Act.

3\. The Schedule to the principal Act is amended by
inserting after item 7 the following new items–
“8. The Financial Intelligence Unit.
9\. The Anti-Money Laundering and Combating
Terrorism Financing Commission.
10\. The Royal Grenada Police Force.”.

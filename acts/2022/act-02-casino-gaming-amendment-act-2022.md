---

### 1.

—(1) This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

CASINO GAMING (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2022
and shall be read as one with the Casino Gaming Act No. 40
of 2014 hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 3 of the principal Act is amended by deleting
the words “the Gambling, Lotteries and Betting Act Cap. 120
and the National Lotteries Act Cap. 205A” after the word
“under” and substituting therefor the words “the Gaming
Act, 2016”.

### 3.

Section 4 of the principal Act is amended by numbering
the subsection as subsection (1) and inserting thereafter the
following new subsections–
“(2) No person shall operate a casino without a
licence granted under Part II.
(3) A person who contravenes subsection (2)
commits an offence and is liable on summary
conviction to a fine of one hundred thousand dollars.”.

Amendment of
section 12 of

### 4.

Section 12 of the principal Act is amended as follows–
(a) in subsection (2) (a) by deleting the words “or
officer” after the word “director” and substituting
therefor a “comma” and the words “officer or
beneficial owner”;
(b) by inserting after subsection (3) the following
new subsection–
“(3A) For the purposes of paragraph (a)
of subsection (2), the expression “beneficial
owner” means the natural person who
ultimately owns or controls the company,
hotel or resort or on whose behalf the business
activity is being conducted and includes,
though not restricted to–
(a) in the case of a juridical person other than a
company whose securities are listed on a
recognised stock exchange, a natural person
who ultimately owns or controls, whether
directly or indirectly, ten or more per cent
of the shares or voting rights in the juridical
person; or

(b) in the case of a juridical person, a natural
person who otherwise exercises control over
the management of the juridical person; or
(c) in the case of a legal arrangement–
(i) the partner or partners who control the
partnership;
(ii)the trustee or other person who controls
the applicant; and
(iii) the settlor or other persons by whom the
legal arrangement is made,
and includes ownership through a trustee, legal
representative, agent or other intermediary.”.

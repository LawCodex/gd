### 3.

Section 32 of the principal Act is amended by repealing
subsection (4) and substituting therefor the following new
subsection–
“(4) Where a person–
(a) fails to comply with or contravenes a
provision of the Guidelines; or
(b) intentionally or recklessly, makes a false
statement or provides false information
in relation to statements or information
required or requested under the Guidelines,

he commits an offence, and is liable on summary
conviction or conviction on indictment, to a fine not
exceeding $250,000 or to a term of imprisonment
not exceeding four years, or both.”.

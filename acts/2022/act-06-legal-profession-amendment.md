### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

LEGAL PROFESSION (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2022,
and shall be read as one with the Legal Profession Act No.
25 of 2011, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended as follows–
(a) in the definition of “member” by repealing the
numbers “7(3)” where it appears and substituting
therefor the numbers “5(2)”; and
(b) by inserting the following new definition in the
appropriate alphabetical order–

Amendment
of section 2 of
principal Act.

““Practice of law” is–
(1) the application of legal principles
and judgment regarding the circumstances or
objectives of another person that requires the
knowledge and skill of a person trained in law
and includes–
(a) giving legal advice or legal
counsel to another person with
respect to his or her legal rights or
responsibilities or to the legal rights
and responsibilities of others;
(b) drafting or completing legal
documents or agreements that affect
the legal rights or responsibilities of
another person;
(c) representing another person before
an adjudicative body, including,
preparing, or filing documents or
conducting discovery; or
(d) negotiating
legal
rights
or
responsibilities on behalf of another
person.
(2) For the purposes of section 1(c),
“adjudicative body” includes a court, an arbitrator, a mediator, a legislative body or an
administrative body.
(3) Notwithstanding subsection (1), this
section shall not apply to–

(a) a public officer or an officer of a
statutory board–
(i)

drawing up
instruments;

or

preparing

(ii) appearing for an informant,
or a plaintiff in a Magistrate’s
Court, in the course of his duty;
or
(iii) employed merely to engross
any instrument or proceeding;
(b) a person who provides services
under the supervision of an
attorney-at-law in compliance with
the standards in the Code of Ethics;
or
(c) litigants or parties representing
themselves in a Court;”.

### 3.

The principal Act is amended by inserting after section Insertion of new

“Virtual meetings of the
Council.

12A.—(1) The Council may hold meetings virtually in whole or in part by means of
electronic communications technology, that
permit members to attend and participate in the
meetings.
(2) The provisions of section 12 apply to
meetings held under this section.

(3) For the purposes of section 12 (1),
reference to a “place” where meetings shall be
held, includes reference to more than one place
including electronic, digital or virtual locations.”.

### 4.

Section 26 of the principal Act is amended as follows–
(a) in subsection (1) by inserting the words “in
the prescribed form and accompanied by the
prescribed fee” after the word “application”
where it appears;
(b) by repealing subsection (3) and substituting
therefor with the following–
“(3). A Practising Certificate shall be
valid for a period of one year.”; and
(c) by repealing subsections (4), (5), (6), (7), (8)
and (9).

Insertion of
new section
59A to principal Act.

### 5.

The principal Act is amended by inserting after section
59 the following new section–
“Power to make
Regulations.

59A. The Minister in consultation with
the Council may make regulations prescribing
the fees that are payable in relation to issuing of
Practising Certificates.”.

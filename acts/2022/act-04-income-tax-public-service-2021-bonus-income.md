AN ACT to stipulate the personal income tax payable by
individuals employed by or providing services to the
Government of Grenada, in respect of a bonus income
received from the Government of Grenada.
[ 4th March, 2022 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives and by the authority of the same
as follows–

### 1.

—(1) This Act may be cited as the
INCOME TAX (PUBLIC SERVICE 2021 BONUS
INCOME) ACT , 2022.

Short title and
commencement.

(2) This Act shall be deemed to have entered into force
on the 31st day of December, 2021.

### 2.

In this Act–

Interpretation.

Income Tax (Public Service 2021 Bonus Income) 2022
“bonus income” means the bonus income received by
a public worker, in respect of services provided to
the Government during the COVID-19 pandemic
between March 2020 and December 2021, whether
paid in December 2021 or such other time, as the case
may be;
“Income Tax Act” means the Income Tax Act, Chapter
149;
“public worker” means, solely for the purposes of this
Act, any individual employed by or providing services
to the Government of Grenada.

Rate of tax for
bonus income.

### 3.

Notwithstanding the Fifth Schedule to the Income Tax
Act, the income tax on a bonus income received by any
public worker shall be charged as follows–
(a) in the case of an individual whose total
gross assessable income received from the
Government of Grenada in respect of December
2021 would not have exceeded $3,000 but for
the bonus income, no tax is chargeable on the
bonus income;
(b) in the case of an individual whose gross
assessable income, excluding the bonus income,
received from the Government of Grenada in
respect of December 2021 exceeded $3,000, tax
is chargeable on the bonus income as follows—
(i) at a rate of 10% on sums of the bonus
income exceeding $3,000 but not
exceeding $5,000; and

(ii) at a rate of 28% on sums of the bonus
income exceeding $5,000,
and shall be paid no later than the 31st day of March,
2022\.

### 4.

—(1) Notwithstanding section 65 of the Income Tax Declaration
form: bonus
Act, a person to which section 3 (b) applies shall furnish income.
a declaration form in respect of the bonus income, either
personally or in a representative capacity, no later than the
31st day of March, 2022.
(2) A declaration form under subsection (1) shall–
(a) be in the form set out in the Schedule;
(b) be signed by the person liable to furnish the
form or by an agent authorised to sign on
his or her behalf; and
(c) contain a calculation of the bonus income
and the tax payable thereon.

### 5.

For the avoidance of doubt–
(a) notwithstanding Part V of the Income Tax Act,
“assessable income” under the Income Tax Act
excludes the bonus income; and

Application of
Income Tax Act.

(b) a return of income under section 65 of the
Income Tax Act furnished by a public worker
shall not include the bonus income.

### 6.

The Tax Administration Act, 2016 shall apply mutatis Application
of Tax
mutandis to this Act.

AN ACT to amend the Customs (Service Charge) Act,

## Chapter 75D.

[ By Order ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

---

### 1.

—(1) This Act may be cited as the
CUSTOMS (SERVICE CHARGE) (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2016
and shall be read as one with the Customs (Service Charge)
Act, Chapter 75D hereinafter referred to as the “principal
Act”.
(2) This Act shall come into force on such a day as the
Minister of Finance may appoint by Order in the Gazette.

Short title and
commencement.

Amendment to
section 4 of
principal Act.

2016

### 2.

Section 4 of the principal Act is amended by inserting
after subsection (1) the following new subsection–
“(1A) In the case of raw materials imported by a
person engaged in manufacturing for the purposes of
the Investment Act, 2014 and any regulations made
thereunder, the customs service charge shall be
payable to the Comptroller and, subject to section 6,
shall be the equivalent of three per cent of the CIF
value of the raw materials imported into the State
under any Customs law whether or not the goods are
subject to Customs duties.”

Amendment to
section 6 of
principal Act.

### 3.

Section 6 of the principal Act is amended by repealing
paragraph (b).

Transitional
provisions.

### 4.

Upon commencement of this Act, every subsisting
varied rate of customs service charge granted pursuant to
paragraph (b) of section 6 of the principal Act shall remain in
effect for six months or until the varied rate was set to expire,
whichever first occurs.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 19th day of February,

An Act to appropriate an additional sum of thirty-one
million, eight hundred and eighty seven thousand, three
hundred and ninety-three dollars and ninety-five cents
($31,887,393.95) to the service of the State of Grenada
for the period August 1st to December 31st, 2016.
[ 29th December, 2016 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION (NO. 1) ACT,
2016\.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated
Fund and applied to the service of the State of Grenada
for the period August 1st to December 31st, 2016, a sum

Appropriation.

not exceeding thirty-one million, eight hundred and eighty
seven thousand, three hundred and ninety-three dollars and
ninety-five cents ($31,887,393.95) in addition to the sums
already appropriated for that year.
(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is
appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

GRENADA
ACT NO. 12 OF 2016
L.S.
I assent,
6th April, 2016.

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to amend the Excise Tax Act, Chapter 94.
[ By Order ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives and by the authority of the
same as follows–

---

### 1.

—(1) This Act may be cited as the
EXCISE TAX (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2016

Short title and
commencement.

and shall be read as one with the Excise Tax Act, Chapter 94
hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.
(2) This Act shall come into force on such a day as
the Minister of Finance may appoint by Order in the Gazette.

### 2.

The Second Schedule of the principal Act is amended by
inserting after paragraph (5) the following new paragraphs–

Amendment to
Second Schedule
to principal Act.

218 Act 12

“(6) An import of rum or tafia as raw materials
for a facility in manufacturing for the purposes of
the Investment Act, 2014 and any regulations made
thereunder.
(7) An import of motor vehicles to the extent
provided in an Order made pursuant to section 108
of the Customs Act, 2015 and if it is exempt from
customs duty under one of the items in the List of
Conditional Duty Exemptions in the Common
External Tariff.”

Passed by the House of Representatives this 19th day of February, 2016.

WILLAN A. THOMPSON
Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 1st day of March, 2016.

WILLAN A. THOMPSON
Clerk to the Senate.

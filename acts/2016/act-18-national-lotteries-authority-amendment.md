### 1.

—(1) This Act may be cited as the–
NATIONAL LOTTERIES AUTHORITY (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2016
and shall be read as one with the National Lotteries Authority
Act, Chapter 205A, hereinafter referred to as the “principal
Act”.
(2) This Act shall come into force on a day fixed by
Order by the Minister with responsibility for the National
Lotteries Authority, published in the Gazette.

### 2.

Section 5 of the principal Act is amended as follows–
(a) by repealing subsections (1), (2) and (3)
and substituting therefor the following new
subsections–
“(1) The Authority may, with the approval
of the Minister, provide and operate
facilities for gaming under the Gaming
Act, 2016 and any other enactment
regulating the providing of facilities for
gaming.
(2) The Authority may authorise a person
to, on the behalf of the Authority, provide
facilities for gaming pursuant to an
operating licence issued to the Authority
under the Gaming Act, 2016 or any other
licence under any other enactment under
which the Authority may be authorised to
provide facilities for gaming.
(3) Where the Authority authorises
a person under subsection (2), the
Authority–
(a) may issue to the person a permit
in a prescribed form to certify
that the person is authorised
under subsection (2); and
(b) shall be liable for any failure of the
third party to comply with the law
regulating the operating licence
and any condition attached to the
operating licence.”;

(b) in subsection (4), by deleting the words
“Without prejudice to the generality of the
provisions of subsection (1)”.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 11th day of May, 2016.

2016

### 2.

The Interpretation and General Provisions Act Cap. 153
is amended by inserting after section 33 the following
section–
“33A During any period in which there is a
vacancy in the office of Leader of the Opposition by
reason of the fact that no person is both qualified for
appointment to that office in accordance with the
Constitution and willing to accept the appointment,
the Governor-General may act without the advice or
without consulting the Leader of the Opposition and
in his own deliberate judgment, in the exercise of
any power in respect of which it is provided in any
written law that the Governor-General shall act on
the advice of or in consultation with the Leader of
the Opposition.”.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 2nd day of February, 2016.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

IMMIGRATION (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2016,
and shall be read as one with the Immigration Act, Chapter
145 hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

The principal Act is amended by inserting after section
14 the following section–

Insertion of
new section in
principal Act.

“Visa waiver and length of stay for citizens
of the Schengen area

14A. (1) A person who is a citizen of a
Schengen Area Member State, mentioned in
the Third Schedule who holds a valid ordinary,
diplomatic, service, official or special Passport
issued by any Schengen Area Member State
may enter Grenada as a visitor without a Visa
and may remain in Grenada for a period not
exceeding ninety days in any period of one
hundred and eighty days.
(2) Where a citizen of a Schengen Area
Member State desires to remain as a visitor in
Grenada for a period exceeding ninety days,
an immigration officer may, if so requested by
or on behalf of that visitor, grant him or her
permission to remain in Grenada for such an
extended period as may be deemed fit in the
discretion of the Chief Immigration Officer.
(3) The Minister may by order amend the
Third Schedule as he or she deems necessary.
(4) In this section, “Schengen Area
Member State” means any European country
that applies the Schengen acquis in full.”.

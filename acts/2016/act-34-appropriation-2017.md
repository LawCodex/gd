## SCHEDULE

GRENADA
ACT NO. 34 OF 2016
L.S.
I assent,
23rd December, 2016.

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

An Act to appropriate a sum not exceeding one billion,
one hundred million, five hundred and seventyeight thousand, one hundred and forty-four dollars
($1,100,578,144) to the service of the State of Grenada
for the period January 1st to December 31st 2017.
[ 29th December, 2016 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

APPROPRIATION (2017) ACT, 2016.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the
period January 1st to December 31st 2017, one billion, one
hundred million, five hundred and seventy-eight thousand,
one hundred and forty-four dollars ($1,100,578,144).

Appropriation.

(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is
appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

# Investment (Amendment)-1

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

1\.

Short title

2\.

Amendment to section 15 of principal Act

GRENADA
ACT NO. 7 OF 2016
L.S.
I assent,
6th April, 2016.

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to amend the Investment Act, No. 42 of 2014.
[ 15th April, 2016 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives and by the authority of the
same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

INVESTMENT (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2016
and shall be read as one with the Investment Act, No. 42 of
2014 hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

The principal Act is amended by repealing section 15
and substituting therefor the following new section–
“Regulations
15.—(1) The Minister may make regulations for
the purpose of implementing the provisions of this
Act.

Amendment to
section 15 of
principal Act.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of
subsection (1), the Minister may make regulations
relating to–
(a) priority sectors;
(b) large scale projects; and
(c) projects with specified projected
amounts of capital investment.”

Passed by the House of Representatives this 19th day of February, 2016.

WILLAN A. THOMPSON
Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 1st day of March, 2016.

WILLAN A. THOMPSON
Clerk to the Senate.

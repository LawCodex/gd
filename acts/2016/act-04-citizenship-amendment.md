An Act to amend the Citizenship Act, Chapter 54 to provide
for a person with a grandparent who is registered as a
citizen to be registered as a citizen.
[29th January, 2016].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives and by the authority of the same as
follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the
CITIZENSHIP (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2016
and shall be read as one with the Citizenship Act, Chapter 54
hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

Short title.

Insertion of new
section 5A.

### 2.

The principal Act is amended by inserting after section
5 the following new section–
“Registration of descendants of citizens of Grenada

### 5A.

—(1) Upon application made to the Minister in
the prescribed manner, the Minister may cause any
person of full age and capacity who was born
outside of Grenada to parents who were at no time
citizens of Grenada to be registered as a citizen if the
Minister is satisfied–
(a)

that the person has a grandparent who is or
was a citizen of Grenada;

(b)

that he or she is of good character;

(c)

that he or she intends, if registered, to be or
continue to be in service to the State of
Grenada.

(2) A person shall not be registered under
subsection (1) unless he or she takes the oath of
allegiance.
(3) Citizenship granted under subsection (1)
shall be subject to the grounds of deprivation set out
in section 9 and such other grounds as the Minister
may by order prescribe, which order shall be subject
to affirmative resolution of Parliament.
(4) A person married to a citizen registered
under subsection (1) shall not be entitled to be
registered as a citizen, unless the person is qualified
to be registered as a citizen of Grenada under–
(a)

a section of this Act other than section 5; or

(b)

a section of the Constitution other than
section 98 of the Constitution.

(5) Citizenship granted under subsection (1)
shall be subject to such conditions as the Minister
may by order prescribe, but in no case may the order
be given retrospective effect.
(6) A person registered as a citizen under
subsection (1) shall enjoy all the rights of a citizen,
subject to subsections (3) and (4) and the
Representation of the People Act, Chapter 286A.”.

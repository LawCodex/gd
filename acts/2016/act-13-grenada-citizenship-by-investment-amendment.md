AN ACT to amend the Grenada Citizenship by Investment
Act No. 15 of 2013.
[ 15th April, 2016 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and House of Representatives and by the authority of the
same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

GRENADA CITIZENSHIP BY INVESTMENT
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2016
and shall be read as one with the Grenada Citizenship by
Investment Act No. 15 of 2013 hereinafter referred to as the
“principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 9 of the of the principal Act is amended as
follows–

Amendment of
section 9 of the
principal Act.

222 Act 13

(a) by repealing subsection (1) and substituting
therefor the following–
“(1) Where the Minister deems it necessary,
he or she may, on a written application by
the principal applicant, appoint a panel to
review any application that has not been
approved.
(1A) The Minister shall appoint a person
from among the members of the review
panel to be the Chairperson, who shall
within thirty-five (35) days of his or her
appointment, convene the first meeting
of the panel to consider any application
referred by the Minister for review.
(1B) An application for a review under
sub-section (1) shall be accompanied by a
non-refundable fee of five thousand dollars
United States currency.”
(b) by repealing subsection (4) and substituting
therefor the following–
“(4) Upon completion of a review under
sub-section (1) the review panel shall
within ninety (90) days submit its findings
and make its recommendations to the
Minister”.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 19th day of February, 2016.

WILLAN A. THOMPSON

Passed by the Senate this 1st day of March, 2016.

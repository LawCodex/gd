# Integrity in Public Life (Amendment) Act

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

1\. Short title and commencement
2\. Amendment to section 42 of principal Act
3\. Amendment to section 43 of principal Act
4\. Amendment to section 44 of principal Act

Act 29 989

GRENADA
ACT NO. 29 OF 2016
I assent,

22nd September, 2016.

DANIEL CHARLES WILLIAMS
Deputy to the Governor-General.

AN ACT to amend the Integrity in Public Life Act No. 24 of
2013\.
[30th September, 2016].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

---

### 1.

—(1) This Act may be cited as the
INTEGRITY IN PUBLIC LIFE (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2016,
and shall be read as one with the Integrity in Public Life Act
No. 24 of 2013 hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

Short title
and commencement.

(2) This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on
the 3rd day of October, 2013.
Amendment to
section 42 of
principal Act.

### 2.

Section 42 of the principal Act is amended in
subsection (1) by deleting the number “42” after the word
“section” and substituting therefor the number “41”.

Amendment to
section 43 of
principal Act.

### 3.

Section 43 of the principal Act is amended in
subsection (1) by deleting the number “42” after the word
“section” and substituting therefor the number “41”.

Amendment to
section 44 of
principal Act.

### 4.

Section 44 of the principal Act is amended in
subsection (1) by deleting the number “44” after the word
“section” and substituting therefor the number “43”.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 2nd day of September,
2016\.
ADRIAN FRANCIS
Acting Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 9th day of September, 2016.

ADRIAN FRANCIS
Acting Clerk to the Senate.

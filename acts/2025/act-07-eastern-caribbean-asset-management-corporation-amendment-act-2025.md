# Eastern Caribbean Asset Management Corporation (Amendment) Act, 2025

## ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS

1\.

Short title

2\.

Amendment of section 5 of the principal Act

GRENADA
ACT NO. 7 OF 2025
L.S.
I assent,
16th June, 2025.

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to amend the Eastern Caribbean Asset Management
Corporation Act, No. 2 of 2016.
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority
of the same as follows–
[ 20th June, 2025 ].

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–
EASTERN CARIBBEAN ASSET MANAGEMENT
CORPORATION (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2025,

Short.

and shall be read as one with the Eastern Caribbean Asset
Management Corporation Act, No. 2 of 2016, hereinafter
referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 5 of the principal Act is amended in subsection
(1) by deleting paragraph (a) and substituting therefor the
following new paragraph–

Amendment of
section 5 of the
principal Act.

“(a) The Banking Act, No. 20 of 2015, except Part
X and section 178; and”.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 13th day of May, 2025.

ANDREW AUGUSTINE
Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 27th day of May, 2025.

ANDREW AUGUSTINE
Clerk to the Senate.

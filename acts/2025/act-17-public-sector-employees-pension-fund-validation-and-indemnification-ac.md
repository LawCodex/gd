Governor-General.

AN ACT to validate and confirm as lawful, the addition
of statutory bodies to Schedule I of the Public Sector
Employees (Pension Fund) Act No. 19 of 2024,
(hereinafter referred to as “the Act”) by virtue of SRO
No. 30 of 2025, (hereinafter referred to as “the Order”)
and as a result thereof, the deduction and payment of
contributions of members employed by the said statutory
bodies so added, as prescribed under Part V of the Act,
and paid into the Public Sector Employees Pension Fund
during the period commencing January 2025 and ending
on the date of commencement of this Act, in variance as
to amount, and to indemnify persons liable in respect of
such deduction and payment pursuant to the Order.
WHEREAS the Act was enacted to provide for the
establishment of a contributory pension scheme for public
sector employees and employees of statutory bodies listed
in Schedule I to the Act, and to provide for the establishment

of the Public Sector Employees Pension Fund from which
payment of pensions, gratuities and other allowances are to
be paid to such employees;
WHEREAS all statutory bodies whose employees are to
be members of the Pension Scheme and contribute to the
Fund are listed in Schedule I to the Act;
WHEREAS section 73 of the Act empowers the Minister
responsible for Public Administration, to amend Schedule I
to the Act, by Order subject to affirmative resolution;
WHEREAS in exercise of that power, the Minister made
the Order which purported to add statutory bodies to the said
Schedule;
WHEREAS the Order was inadvertently published
in the Gazette without parliamentary approval subject to
affirmative resolution;
WHEREAS notwithstanding same, the servants or agents
acting in good faith on behalf of the Government and the
said statutory bodies so added made deductions and paid
contributions into the Public Sector Employees Pension
Fund on behalf of or to the credit of their employees and
carried out acts in relation thereto;
AND WHEREAS it is desirable to validate and confirm
as lawful the addition of the said statutory bodies to Schedule
I and the deduction and payment of the contribution of
employees from the statutory bodies that were paid into the
Public Sector Employees Pension Fund along with other
related acts, and to indemnify all persons who, pursuant

to the Order, did so in good faith and inadvertent as to the
absence of such proper statutory authority;
[ 28th November, 2025 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority
of the same as follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

PUBLIC SECTOR EMPLOYEES (PENSION FUND)
(VALIDATION AND INDEMNIFICATION) ACT, 2025.

### 2.

Notwithstanding anything to the contrary–
(a) the purported addition by the Public Sector
Employees (Pension Fund) (Amendment
of Schedule I) Order, No. 30 of 2025 of the
statutory bodies listed in the Schedule to this Act,
to Schedule I of the Public Sector Employees
(Pension Fund) Act, (“the Act”) without the said
Order being subject to affirmative resolution
pursuant to section 73 of the Act, and;
(b) all other acts done as a result thereof, including
the deduction of contributions from employees
of such statutory bodies so added, and the
payment of said contributions into the Public
Sector Employees Pension Fund pursuant to Part
V of the Act;
from the period commencing January 2025 and ending on
the date of commencement of this Act, in good faith and

Validation.

inadvertent as to the Order being published in the Gazette
without being subject to affirmative resolution, are hereby
declared to have been validly, properly and lawfully done
to all intents and purposes and with effect as if authorised in
accordance with provisions of the Act.
Indemnity.

### 3.

Every person liable to be legally proceeded against
on the ground that any of the acts referred to in section 2
was unauthorised, unlawfully done, or otherwise illegal
or improper; for any such reason, is hereby discharged
and indemnified as well against the King’s Most Gracious
Majesty His Heirs and Successors as well as against all
persons whatsoever, from such liability.

---

### 1.

—(1) This Act may be cited as the
CONSTITUTION (OATH OF ALLEGIANCE)
(AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) ACT, 2025.
(2) This Act shall enter into force on the 1st day of
August, 2025.

Short title and
commencement.

262 Act 14
Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act–
“oath” includes affirmation;
“the Constitution” means the Constitution of Grenada,
set out in Schedule 1 to the Grenada Constitution
Order 1973, S.I. 1973 No. 2155 [UK];

Amendment of
Schedule 3 to
the Constitution.

### 3.

Schedule 3 to the Constitution in the Oath of Allegiance
is amended by deleting the words “His Majesty King Charles
the Third, His Heirs and Successors”, and substituting
therefor the word “Grenada”; in relation to the following
provisions of the Constitution–
(i)

section 21(2) (Acting Governor-General taking
oath);

(ii) section 35(3) (Supervisor of Elections taking
oath); and
(iii) section 83(11) (PSC members taking oath).
Transitional
Provisions
as to Oath of
Allegiance.

### 4.

—(1) On the expiry of twelve hours immediately
following the commencement of the appointed day, the
Oath of Allegiance sworn or solemnly affirmed to by any
functionary prior to the appointed day shall cease to have
force, and such functionary shall, within seven (7) days
immediately following the commencement of the appointed
day, swear or solemnly affirm to the Oath of Allegiance which
shall come into force with effect from the commencement of
the appointed day–
Provided that if for any reason any functionary did not
swear or solemnly affirm to the Oath of Allegiance
within those said seven (7) days such functionary
shall be deemed to have sworn or solemnly affirmed
thereto immediately upon the expiry of those said
seven (7) days.

(2) In this section–
“appointed day” means the 1st day of August 2025.
Passed by the House of Representatives this 24th day of July, 2025.

AN ACT to amend the Bank Holidays Act CAP. 25.
[ 4th July, 2025 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority
of the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

BANK HOLIDAYS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2025,
and shall be read as one with the Bank Holidays Act, Chapter
25 hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

The Schedule to the principal Act is amended by
deleting the following–

Amendment
of Schedule to
principal Act.

“The first Monday in August which shall be known
and referred to as Emancipation Holiday.”,
and substituting therefor the following–
“The first day in August which shall be known and
referred to as Emancipation Day.”.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 17th day of June, 2025.

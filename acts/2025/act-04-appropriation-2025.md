CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to appropriate a sum of One Billion, Nine Hundred
and Twelve Million, Eight Hundred and Seventy-Two
Thousand, and Fourteen Eastern Caribbean Dollars
(EC$1,912,872,014) to the service of the State of
Grenada for the period January 1st to December 31st,
2025\.
[ 26th March, 2025 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives and by the authority of the same
as follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

APPROPRIATION (2025) ACT, 2025.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the

Appropriation.

period January 1st to December 31st, 2025, One Billion,
Nine Hundred and Twelve Million, Eight Hundred and
Seventy-Two Thousand, and Fourteen Eastern Caribbean
Dollars (EC$1,912,872,014).
(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is
appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

FISHERIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2025,
and shall be read as one with the Fisheries Act, Chapter 108,
hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended as follows–
(a) in the definition of “fish” by inserting after the
word “eggs” a “Comma” and the words “and
marine mammals”;

### 3.

The principal Act is amended by inserting after section
28 the following new section–
“Observer and
monitoring
programme

28A.—(1) The Chief Fisheries Officer shall
establish, plan and manage an observer and
monitoring programme for the purpose of
collecting and reporting reliable and accurate
information for scientific, management and
compliance purposes under this Act.

(2) For the purposes of this section, an
observer shall exercise scientific, monitoring
and any other relevant functions that may be
on a fishing vessel or facility related to fisheries
that have been granted a licence, permit or
authorisation under this Act.
(3) A person who desires to be certified as
an observer may apply in writing to the Chief
Fisheries Officer, demonstrating that he or she
has acquired training as a fisheries biological
technician, including skills in gathering data
on the fish that is caught or discarded by a
commercial fishing vessel.
(4) Upon the recommendation of the
Chief Fisheries Officer, the Minister may
appoint a person who applies under subsection
(3) as an observer for the purposes of this Act.
(5) For the avoidance of doubt, the
Chief Fisheries Officer may utilise electronic
monitoring systems as an alternative, or in
addition, to monitoring by observers.
(6) The Minister may make regulations to
give effect to this section.”.

---

### 1.

—(1) This Act may be cited as the
NATIONAL INSURANCE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2023

Short title and
commencement.

and shall be read as one with the National Insurance Act,

## Chapter 205 hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

(2) This Act shall enter into force on the 1st day of
August 2023.

### 2.

—(1) Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by Amendment
section 2 of
deleting the definition of “child” and substituting therefor of
principal Act.
the following new definition in the appropriate alphabetical
order–

““child”, in relation to an insured person, means–
(a) a biological child or a person declared or
presumed to be a biological child;
(b) a step-child;
(c) an adopted child; or
(d) a person expressly acknowledged by the
insured person as a biological child or stepchild, under the age of eighteen;”.
(2) For the avoidance of doubt, this section
applies in respect of an insured person who dies after the
commencement of this Act.

of section 56

### 3.

The post-amble of section 56 of the principal Act
is amended by deleting the word “one” before the words
“thousand dollars” and substituting therefor the word “five”.

of section 64
of principal
Act.

### 4.

Section 64 (2) of the principal Act is amended by
deleting the words “five hundred” before the word “dollars”
and substituting therefor the words “two thousand”.

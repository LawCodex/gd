AN ACT to validate International Companies that failed to
meet the statutory requirements for continuing to carry
on business in accordance with the Companies Act,

## Chapter 58A (“the Companies Act”) within the time

prescribed under the International Companies (Repeal)
Act No. 17 of 2018 (hereinafter called “the repeal Act”);
to deem the International Companies that are listed in
the Schedule to have transitioned to continue to carry on
business and to provide services in accordance with the
Companies Act; and for related matters.
WHEREAS the International Companies Act, Chapter
152 (“the International Companies Act”) was enacted to
provide for the incorporation, operation, and regulation of
international companies, and for connected matters;
WHEREAS all International Companies were
incorporated pursuant to section 4 of the International
Companies Act Chapter 152;

WHEREAS the International Companies Act Chapter
152 was repealed by section 2 of the repeal Act;
WHEREAS by virtue of section 3 of the repeal Act,
the incorporation of an International Company under the
International Companies Act prior to the commencement of
the repeal Act remained valid upon the commencement of
the repeal Act, until the operative date;
WHEREAS section 3(3) of the repeal Act defines “the
operative date” to mean–
(a) in the case of an International Company that
is a trust corporation for the purposes of the
International Trusts Act, Chapter 152C, the date
on which the international trust is terminated in
accordance with section 9 of the International
Trusts Act, Chapter 152C; or
(b) in any other case–
(i) the date on which the International Company
has been wound-up or dissolved in
accordance with Part VIII of the International
Companies Act; or
(ii) 31st December 2021,
whichever is earlier.
WHEREAS the repeal Act was amended by Act No. 2 of
2021;
WHEREAS section 3(4) of the repeal Act saved the
rights and benefits conferred upon International Companies

under the International Companies Act, Chapter 152 until
the operative date;
WHEREAS by virtue of section 3(5) of the repeal Act,
any obligation or penalty incurred by an International
Company during the period of operation of the International
Companies Act was not affected, and any investigation, legal
proceeding or remedy in respect thereof may be instituted,
continued, or enforced and such penalty imposed as if the
International Companies Act, Chapter 152 had not been
repealed;
WHEREAS by virtue of section 3(6) of the repeal Act,
an International Company carrying on business under the
International Companies Act Chapter 152 that was in good
standing as of 31st August 2021, as of 1st September 2021,
was deemed as continuing to carry on business in accordance
with the Companies Act Chapter 58A from 1st September
2021 and–
(a) continued to provide services in accordance with
the Companies Act Chapter 58A; and
(b) be subject to all liabilities and penalties under the
Companies Act Chapter 58A for non-compliance
therewith;
WHEREAS the International Companies that are listed
in the Schedule were not transitioned to continue to carry
on business and to provide services in accordance with the
Companies Act, Chapter 58A as they failed to meet the
statutory requirement of good standing as of the 31st August
2021;

WHEREAS it is desirable to deem the International
Companies that are listed in the Schedule to have transitioned
to continue to carry on business under and to provide services
in accordance with the Companies Act, Chapter 58A from as
of the 1st September 2021;
AND WHEREAS it is also desirable to validate the
actions taken by these International Companies after the
operative date, which would have been valid and lawful
but for their failure to transition to continue to carry on
business under and to provide services in accordance with
the Companies Act Chapter 58A;
[ 22nd December, 2023 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority
of the same as follows–
Short title.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–
INTERNATIONAL COMPANIES (REPEAL ACT)
(VALIDATION) ACT, 2023.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act–
“Authority” means the Grenada Authority for the
Regulation of Financial Institutions established
under section 3 of the Grenada Authority for the
Regulation of Financial Institutions Act, Chapter
125A;
“International Company” means a company that is
listed in the Schedule, that was incorporated under

the International Companies Act, Chapter 152,
and which failed to comply with the transitional
provisions of the repeal Act that provided for
the International Company to continue carrying
on business under the Companies Act Chapter
58A, from the first day of September 2021, if the
International Company was in good standing as
of August 31st 2021;
“Registrar” means the Registrar of the Corporate
Affairs and Intellectual Property Office under
the Corporate Affairs and Intellectual Property
Office Act, Chapter 69A; and
“repeal Act” means the International Companies
(Repeal) Act, No. 17 of 2018.

### 3.

—(1) Notwithstanding section 3(3) of the repeal Act,
an International Company shall be deemed to continue to
carry on business and to provide services in accordance with
the Companies Act, Chapter 58A if, within six months from
the date of commencement of this Act, the International
Company pays all outstanding fees and penalties as shall be
determined by the Authority.
(2) Pursuant to subsection (1), the outstanding fees
and penalties shall be paid to the Registrar.
(3) An International Company that satisfies the
requirements under subsection (1) shall–
(a) continue to provide services in accordance
with the Companies Act, Chapter 58A; and

International
companies
deemed to
continue
to carry on
business under
the Companies
Act.

(b) be subject to all liabilities and penalties
under the Companies Act, Chapter 58A for
non-compliance therewith.
(4) Upon the date of commencement of this Act, the
Authority shall notify an International Company in writing
of the requirements of section 3.
Validation.

### 4.

Any act or thing done or omitted to be done by an
International Company that is deemed to continue to carry
on business and to provide services in accordance with
the Companies Act, Chapter 58A, which would have been
valid and lawful but for the International Company’s failure
to meet the requirement of good standing as at the 31st of
August, 2021 in accordance with section 3(6) of the repeal
Act, is hereby deemed to be valid and lawful for all intents
and purposes.

Failure to
comply

### 5.

Where an International Company fails to comply with
the requirements of section 3, the provisions of section 368
of the Companies Act, Chapter 58A shall apply mutatis
mutandis to the International Company.

Right of
person to legal
remedy.

### 6.

Nothing in this Act shall affect the right of any person
to any legal remedy that he or she would have had in
respect of any act or thing done or omitted to be done by an
International Company deemed under section 3 to continue
to carry on business and to provide services in accordance
with the Companies Act, Chapter 58A.

Amendment of
Schedule.

### 7.

The Minister may amend the Schedule by Order.

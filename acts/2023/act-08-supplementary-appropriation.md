Governor-General.

AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of One Hundred
and Forty-Eight Million, Nine Hundred and Ninety
Thousand, Two Hundred and Thirty-Four Dollars
Eastern Caribbean Currency (EC$ 148,990,234.00) to
the service of the State of Grenada for the fiscal year 1st
January to 31st December, 2023.
[ 1st September, 2023 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same
as follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION ACT, 2023.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated
Fund and applied to the service of the State of Grenada
for the fiscal year 1st January to 31st December, 2023, a
sum not exceeding One Hundred and Forty-Eight Million,

Appropriation.

Nine Hundred and Ninety Thousand, Two Hundred and
Thirty-Four Dollars Eastern Caribbean Currency (EC$
148,990,234.00) in addition to the sums already appropriated
for that fiscal year.
(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is
appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

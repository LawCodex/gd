AN ACT to amend the Public Finance Management Act No.
17 of 2015.
[ 19th May, 2023 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority
of the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

PUBLIC FINANCE MANAGEMENT (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2023,
and shall be read as one with the Public Finance Management
Act No. 17 of 2015 hereinafter referred to as the “principal
Act”.

### 2.

Section 78 of the principal Act is amended as follows– Amendment of
(a) in subsection (5), by deleting the words
“provided however that a Statutory Body may
not invest its money in government securities”
where they appear;

section 78 of
principal Act.

(b) by inserting after subsection (7) the following
new subsection–
“(7A) Subsection (7) shall not apply to
the National Insurance Scheme and shall
only apply to a State-owned enterprise or a
Statutory Body–
(a) that receives transfers of any
kind from the National Budget,
including
subventions
or
guarantees for any year in the
five-year period immediately
preceding the fiscal year;
(b) which, for three consecutive
quarters in the preceding
fiscal year, has not met the
quarterly reporting requirements
established by the Minister; or
(c) whose audited balance sheet
has recorded a negative equity
position as at the end of one of
the preceding three financial
years.”;
(c) by repealing subsection (9) and substituting
therefor the following new subsection–
“(9) Where a law establishing a Statutory
Body does not prescribe an expenditure
ceiling, the Minister may prescribe expenditure ceilings for that Statutory Body by
Order.”; and
(d) by inserting after subsection (9) the following
new subsections–

“(10) Where the Minister prescribes an
expenditure ceiling under subsection (9), a
Statutory Body shall not incur any liability
or make any commitment above any ceilings prescribed by the Minister without the
prior written approval of the Minister.
(11) Where a Statutory Body incurs any
liability or makes any commitment above
the expenditure ceiling prescribed under
subsection (9), the Minister shall have the
power to override any financial decision or
commitment made by the Statutory Body.
(12) For the purposes of this section,
National Insurance Scheme means the
national insurance scheme established
under the National Insurance Act, Chapter
205.”.
Passed by the House of Representatives this 18th day of April, 2023.

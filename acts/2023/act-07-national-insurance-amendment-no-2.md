## SCHEDULE

CONSEQUENTIAL AMENDMENTS
(section 6)
1\. Amendment of National Insurance (Collection of Contributions) Regulations.
Regulation 5 (b) of the National Insurance (Collection of Contributions) Regulations,

## Chapter 205 is amended by deleting the words “age of sixty years” after the words

“attaining the” and substituting therefor the words “pensionable age”.
2\. Amendment of National Insurance (Contributions) Regulations. Regulation
11 of the National Insurance (Contributions) Regulations, Chapter 205 is amended as
follows–
(a) by deleting the words “age of sixty years” and substituting therefor the
words “pensionable age”, wherever they appear and;
(b) in subregulation (3) (a) by deleting the words “sixty years” after the
word “attained” and substituting therefor the words “pensionable age”.
3\. Amendment of National Insurance (Voluntary Contributions) Regulations.—
(1) The National Insurance (Voluntary Contributions) Regulations, Chapter 205 is
amended as follows–
(a) in regulation 2 in the definition of “Voluntary Contributions” in the
chapeau by deleting the words “age sixty” after the words “not being of
the” and substituting therefor the words “pensionable age” and;
(b) in regulation 3 (4) (a) by deleting the word “sixty” after the words
“sixteen and” and substituting therefor the words “the pensionable age”.
4\. Amendment of National Insurance (Employment Injury Benefit) Regulations.
Regulation 2 of the National Insurance (Employment Injury Benefit) Regulations,

## Chapter 205 is amended in the definition of “insured person” by deleting the words

“sixty years” after the words “sixteen years and” and substituting therefor the words
“the pensionable age”.

5\. Amendment of the Employment Act, Chapter 89. Section 84 (4) (d) of the
Employment Act, Chapter 89 is amended by deleting the words “age of sixty” after
the words “is over the” and substituting therefor the words “pensionable age for the
purposes of the National Insurance Act, Chapter 205”.
6\. Amendment of the Employment Act, 2016. Section 86 of Employment Act No.
33 of 2016 is amended in subsection (4) (d) by deleting the words “age of sixty” after
the words “is over the” and substituting therefor the words “pensionable age for the
purposes of the National Insurance Act, Chapter 205”.

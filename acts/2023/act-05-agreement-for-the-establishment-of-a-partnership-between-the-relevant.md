AN ACT to give the force of law to the Agreement for
the Establishment of a Partnership between Member
States of the Caribbean Community and the African
Export-Import Bank in Grenada; to provide for its
implementation and for related matters.
[ 14th July, 2023 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority
of the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the
AGREEMENT FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A
PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN MEMBER STATES OF
THE CARIBBEAN COMMUNITY AND THE AFRICAN
EXPORT-IMPORT BANK ACT, 2023.

Short title.

and the African Export-Import Bank

### 2.

In this Act–

Interpretation.

“Agreement” means the Agreement for the
Establishment of a Partnership between
Member States of the Caribbean Community
and the African Export-Import Bank done
at Bridgetown, Barbados on the 1st day of
September, 2022 and any revisions thereto, as
set out in the Schedule;
“Article” means an Article of the Agreement; and
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for
Finance.
Agreement to have
the force of law.
Supplementary
agreements.

### 3.

The Agreement shall have the force of law in Grenada.

### 4.

—(1) Where a supplementary agreement is entered into
between the parties to the Agreement in accordance with
Article XV of the Agreement, the Minister may, by Order
published in the Gazette, amend the Schedule for the purpose
of including the supplementary agreement as an amendment
to the Agreement.
(2) Unless the context otherwise requires, where
the Schedule is amended in accordance with this section,
any reference to the Agreement in this Act or any other
enactment or instrument shall be construed as a reference to
the Agreement so amended by the supplementary agreement.

Regulations.

### 5.

The Minister may make regulations to give effect to the
provisions of the Agreement and this Act.

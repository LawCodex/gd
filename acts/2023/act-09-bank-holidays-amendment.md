GRENADA
ACT NO. 9 OF 2023
L.S.
I assent,
30th August, 2023.

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to amend the Bank Holidays Act CAP. 25.
[ 1st September, 2023 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives and by the authority of the same
as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

BANK HOLIDAYS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2023,
and shall be read as one with the Bank Holidays Act, Chapter
25 hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

The Schedule to the principal Act is amended by
deleting the words “The second Monday in August and from
noon the day following which shall be known and referred
to as the Carnival August Celebrations.” where they appear

Amendment of
Schedule to the
principal Act.

and substituting therefor the words “The second Monday in
August and the day following which shall be known and
referred to as the Carnival August Celebrations.”.
Passed by the House of Representatives this 25th day of August, 2023.

ANDREW AUGUSTINE
Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 29th day of August, 2023.

ANDREW AUGUSTINE

Clerk to the Senate.

# Appropriation 2011

GRENADA
ACT NO. 5 OF 2011
I assent,

17th February, 2011.

CARLYLE ARNOLD GLEAN
Governor-General.

AN ACT to appropriate $783,394,935 dollars to the service
of the State of Grenada for the period January 01 to
December 31, 2011.
[18th February, 2011].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same as
follows–

## PART I

PRELIMINARY
Short Title and
commencement.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the
APPROPRIATION ACT, 2011.

Appropriation.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the

period January 01 to December 31, 2011, a sum not exceeding
Seven hundred and eighty-three million, three hundred and
ninety-four thousand, nine hundred and thirty-five dollars.
(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is appropriated
for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

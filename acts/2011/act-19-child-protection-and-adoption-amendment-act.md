“PART VIII
CHILD CARE SERVICES
Interpretation
of VIII.

88\. For the purposes of this Part–
“licence” means a licence granted under
this Part;
“licensed child care services” means a
child care service licensed under
this Part;
“licensee” means the holder of a licence
under this Part.

Principles.

89\. A decision made under this Part shall be
made in accordance with the following
principles–
(a)

the best interests of the child shall be
of paramount consideration;

(b)

a child care service shall provide
care that is safe, positive and
nurturing;

(c)

a child care service shall promote the
educational, social and developmental well-being of a child; and

(d)

a child shall receive services that
meet his individual needs, including
the needs of a child with a disability,
and enhance his physical, emotional,
cognitive, social and cultural development.

Conditions for
foster care
placements.

2011

90.—(1) If a care order has been made
pursuant to Part VII, which requires the
placement of a child, the Director may place
the child with a person who is willing to
undertake the foster care of the child.
(2) A person who wishes to foster a
child may, make an application to the
Director, but a relative of a child without a
parent or guardian may foster the child
without first applying to the Director and, in
such a case, this Part shall not apply.
(3) Foster care placements shall be made
in accordance with such regulations as may be
prescribed.

Licence to
operate child
care service.

91.—(1) A person shall not establish or
maintain a child care service under this Act
unless he has been granted a licence to do so
by the Authority pursuant to this Part.
(2) A person shall not maintain a child
care service at any address or location other
than provided for in the licence granted
pursuant to this Part unless he has obtained
prior written permission from the Authority.
(3) A licence granted pursuant to the
provisions of this part shall be valid for a
period of three years from the date of its issue
and may be renewed successively.

(4) A licence shall not be transferred
into the name of any person other than the
licensee unless the licensee obtains prior
written permission from the Authority.
Application for
licence.

Power of
Authority in
respect of
licences.

92\. A person who desires to establish and
maintain a child care service shall make an
application to the Authority for a licence, in
the prescribed form and manner.
93.—(1) The Authority, in its discretion,
may refuse to grant a licence in respect of any
child care service, or may refuse to permit the
transfer of a licence or to renew a licence.
(2) The Authority shall state its reason
in writing, for refusing to grant a licence or for
refusing to permit a transfer of a licence or to
renew a licence.
(3) The Authority, in granting a licence,
may attach thereto such terms and conditions
as it may, in any case, think fit.
(4) Where, in connection with any
licensed child care service, the Authority is of
the opinion that there has been any contravention of any of the provisions of this Act
or of the terms and conditions of any licence,
the Authority may cancel any licence issued in
respect of such child care service, or may
apply to the court under section (97D) for an
order or an interim order for the removal of
any child from the child care service.

Appeals
against the
decision of the
Authority.

2011

94.—(1) An appeal against the decision of
the Authority shall lie to a Judge in chambers
against any–
(a)

refusal of the Authority to grant,
permit the transfer or renew a
licence;

(b)

term or condition imposed in a
licence;

(c)

decision of the Authority to suspend or cancel a licence.

(2) Every appeal under this
section shall be lodged within fourteen
days of the date on which the aggrieved
party is notified of the refusal or
decision of, or the terms or conditions
imposed by, the Authority and the
procedure on such appeal shall be in
accordance with rules of Court made for
such purposes.
(3) Where an appeal has been
lodged in relation to the cancellation or
suspension of a licence or a refusal to
renew a licence, the Authority pending
the determination of the appeal may
apply to the Judge in chambers under
section (97D) for an interim order for
the removal of any child from the child
care service to which the appeal relates.

Responsibility
of licensee and
staff of
licensed child
care service
and Director.

Act 19

95.—(1) Where a child has been placed in
a licensed child care service under a care
order, the licensee, the manager and staff of
the child care service shall have the parental
responsibility of the child.

(2) It shall be the duty of every licensee, manager and staff of the child care service
to–
(a) act in the best interests of every
child in his child care service;
(b)

ensure that every child in the
child care service receives at all
times adequate and humane
treatment and education suitable
to the needs of the child; and

(c)

ensure that all the provisions of
this Part and all the terms and
conditions of the licence and the
directions of the Authority are at
all times complied with in respect
of the child care service.

(3) A licensed child care service shall
provide substitute family care for a child until
such time as the natural parent of the child is
able to provide adequate care to meet the basic
needs of the child or the child can be reunited
with his family or arrangements are made for
the custody or other permanent placement of
the child.
(4) The licensee, manager and staff of
the licensed child care service and the

Authority shall assist the child to become
reunited with his natural parent.
(5) While a child is in the care of a child
care service, the licensee, manager and staff of
the licensed child care service and Director
shall maintain–
(a)

contact with the parent or
relatives of the child; and

(b)

contact between the child and
the parent or relatives of the
child.

(6) After a child has been returned from
a licensed child care service, the Director shall
keep in regular contact with the child and his
family until the completion of any order made
pursuant to Part VIII or until the discharge of
the order.
(7) If a child is unable to return to his
natural parent or to go to a foster parent, or
has no parent or a foster parent, he shall,
where possible, be cared for and assisted by a
licensed child care service and the Director
until adoption can be arranged for the child.
Notification of
reception of
child.

96.—(1) A licensee shall notify the
Authority in writing of the name, sex, age and
date of reception of every child who is
received into his child care service, and every
notification shall be made within forty-eight
hours after the reception of the child into the

home and shall contain such further
particulars as may from time to time be
prescribed.
(2) The licensee shall, after such notification, supply the Authority with all such
particulars relating to any such child as the
Authority in any case may require.
Removal or
death of child.

97.—(1) Save where a child is removed
from a child care service under an order of a
court, a licensee shall not permit any child to
leave or to be removed or transferred from,
the licensee’s child care service without first
giving the Authority seven days notice in
writing of such impending removal or transfer,
and except in the case of a transfer to another
licensed child care service without first
obtaining the written permission of the
Authority.
(2) A licensee shall forthwith notify the
Authority if any child is absent for more than
twenty-four hours from his child care service
without the permission of the licensee.
(3) A licensee shall forthwith notify the
Authority of the death of any child maintained
in his child care service or of the removal of
therefrom of any such child.
(4) A person shall not remove a child
from a child care service without the consent
of the licensee or the Authority.

(5) A person who contravenes subsection 2 commits an offence and is liable on
summary conviction to a fine not exceeding
five thousand dollars or to a term of
imprisonment not exceeding three months.

(97A) At all reasonable times–

(97B) Where any person empowered under the

(a)

an unlicensed child care service is

( c)

any child is being maltreated,

he may apply to a Magistrate who may issue a

at any time of the day or night and, with such
assistance and by such force as may be
necessary, to carry out the visit and inspection.
Control over
licensed
child care
services.

(97C)—(1) If the Authority is satisfied that
the management of any licensed child care
service, or the accommodation provided for,
or the treatment of, the children therein, is
such as to endanger or to be likely to endanger
their welfare, it may give in writing to the
licensee concerned such general or special
directions with respect to the matters aforesaid, or any of them, as it thinks necessary
for the best interest of the child in the child

(2) A direction under this section–
(a) may be served on the
licensee concerned by being delivered personally to

AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of $48,619,196.22,
to the service of the State of Grenada for the period
October 01 to December 31, 2009.

BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same as
follows–

PRELIMINARY
Short Title and
commencement.

This Act may be cited as the

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the
period October 01 to December 31, 2009, a sum not
exceeding Forty-eight million, six hundred and nineteen
thousand, one hundred and ninety-six dollars and twenty two

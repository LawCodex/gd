# Miro Enterprise Development (Repeal) Act

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

1\. Short title
2\. Interpretation
3\. Repeal of the Principal Act

GRENADA
ACT NO. 13 OF 2011
I assent,
22nd July, 2011.

CARLYLE ARNOLD GLEAN
Governor-General.

AN ACT to repeal the Micro Enterprise Development
Act, No. 23 of 1999.
[ 29th July, 2011 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives and by the authority of the same as
follows–
Short Title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the
MICRO ENTERPRISE DEVELOPMENT (REPEAL)
ACT, 2011.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Micro
Development Act, No. 23 of 1999.

Enterprise

Repeal of the
principal Act.

### 3.

The principal Act is hereby repealed.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 31st day of May, 2011.

ADRIAN C. A. HAYES
Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 16th day of June, 2011.

ADRIAN C. A. HAYES
Clerk to the Senate.

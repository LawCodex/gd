# Passport (Amendment) Act

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

1\. Short title
2\. Interpretation
3\. Amendment of section 6 of principal Act

GRENADA
ACT NO. 6 OF 2011
I assent,
17th February, 2011.

CARLYLE ARNOLD GLEAN
Governor-General.

AN ACT to amend the Passports Act, Cap. 226 of the
1990 Revised Laws of Grenada.
[18th February, 2011].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Representatives and by the authority of the same as
follows–
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the
PASSPORTS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2011.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Passports Act, Cap. 226 of
the 1990 Revised Laws of Grenada.

Amendment of
Section 6 of the
principal Act.

2011

### 3.

Sub-section 2 of section 6 of the principal Act is
amended by deleting the words “up to ten years” where it
appears and substituting therefor the words “five years”.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 20th day of January,
2011\.

ADRIAN C. A. HAYES
Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 2nd day of February, 2011.

ADRIAN C. A. HAYES
Clerk to the Senate.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

CONSUMER PROTECTION (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2020,
and shall be read as one with the Consumer Protection Act
No. 2 of 2018, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 8 of the principal Act is amended by repealing
subsection (1) and substituting therefor the following new
subsection–

Amendment
of section 8 of
principal Act.

“(1) There shall be a Director of the Consumer
Affairs Office who shall be an officer in public service.”

section 10 of
principal Act.

### 3.

Section 10 of the principal Act is amended by repealing
paragraph (c).

Amendment of
section 13 of
principal Act.

### 4.

Section 13 of the principal Act is amended as follows–
(a) by inserting before subsection (1) the following
new subsection–
“(1) The complainant may request that the
matter be referred to the Tribunal if–
(a) the Office is satisfied that the complaint
qualifies under one or more grounds
under section 32;
(b) the complainant has received no
reasonable redress; and
(c) the complainant makes the payment of
the prescribed fee.”;
(b) renumbering subsections (1) and (2) as subsections
(2) and (3) respectively.

Correction of
numbering of
section 31 of
principal Act.
Amendment of
section 33 of
principal Act.

Amendment of
section 34 of
principal Act.

### 5.

The principal Act is amended by correcting the
numbering of the heading “Who may lodge a complaint” as
section “31” as opposed to section “11”.

### 6.

Section 33 of the principal Act is amended in
subsection (1) in the chapeau by deleting the words “and
receipt of payment of the prescribed fee” after the words
“Upon receipt of the complaint”.

### 7.

Section 34 of the principal Act is amended in subsection
(5) (c) by deleting the words “section 38 (4)” after the words
“in accordance with” and substituting therefor the words
“section 36 (4)”.

### 8.

Section 35 of the principal Act is amended in subsection
(2) by deleting the words “section 34 (9)” after the words
“in accordance with” and substituting therefor the words
“section 34 (8)”.

### 9.

Section 54 of the principal Act is amended in subsection
(3) by deleting the words “respondent” and “respondent’s”
wherever they appear and substituting therefor the words
“defendant” and “defendant’s” respectively.

### 10.

Schedule I to the principal Act is amended in section
3 in the chapeau by deleting the word “Tribunal” after the
words “Each member of the” and substituting therefor the
words “Advisory Body”.

### 11.

Schedule II to the principal Act is amended in
paragraph 5 (d) by deleting the words “subsection (14)” after
the words “fails to comply with” and substituting therefor
the words “paragraph 15”.

# Extradition (Amendment) Act, 2020

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

1\.

Short title

2\.

Amendment of section 4 of principal Act

GRENADA
ACT NO. 7 OF 2020
L.S.
I assent,
15th September, 2020.

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to amend the Extradition Act CAP. 98.
[16th September, 2020].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

EXTRADITION (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2020,
and shall be read as one with the Extradition Act, Chapter
98, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 4 of the principal Act is amended in subsections
(1) (a), (1) (b), (2), and (3) (c) by deleting the words “five
years” where they appear and substituting therefor the words
“one year”.

Amendment
of section 4 of
principal Act.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 28th day of August, 2020.

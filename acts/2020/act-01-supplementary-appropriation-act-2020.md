## SCHEDULE

GRENADA
ACT NO. 1 OF 2020
L.S.
I assent,
220th April, 2020.

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of Fifty Million
dollars ($50,000,000.00) to the service of the State of
Grenada for the period 20th April to 31st December,
2020\.
[21st April, 2020].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives, and by the authority of
the same as follows­–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION ACT, 2020.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated
Fund and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for
the period 20th April to 31st December, 2020, a sum not

Appropriation.

exceeding Fifty Million dollars ($50,000,000.00) in addition
to the sums already appropriated for that year.
(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is
appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

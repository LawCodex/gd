---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

GRENADA TOURISM AUTHORITY
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2020,
and shall be read as one with the Grenada Tourism Authority
Act No. 42 of 2013, hereinafter referred to as the “principal
Act”.

### 2.

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended as follows–

Amendment
of section 2 of
principal Act.

Grenada Tourism Authority (Amendment)

(a) in the definition of “Apartment” at paragraph
(a) by deleting the words “three or more” where
they appear;
(b) in the definition of “Guest House” at paragraph
(a) by deleting the words “three or more” where
they appear; and
(c) in the definition of “Villa” at paragraph (a) by
deleting the words “has at least three bedrooms”
and substituting therefor the words “provides
sleeping accommodation”.
Amendment of
section 52 of
principal Act.

### 3.

Section 52 of the principal Act is amended by repealing
subsection (1) and substituting therefor the following new
subsection–
“(1) A person who operates any tourist
accommodation or tourism enterprise in contravention
of section 28 commits an offence and is liable on
summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one
thousand dollars or to a term of imprisonment not
exceeding six months and, where the offence is
continued after conviction, that person is guilty of a
continuing offence and is liable to a fine not exceeding
two hundred and fifty dollars for each day during
which the offence is continued.”.

Transitional
provision.

### 4.

Every person who, prior to the commencement of this
Act, provides tourist accommodation in which sleeping
accommodation is provided in two or less bedrooms shall
within ninety days of the commencement of this Act comply
with the provisions of this Act.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 29th day of July, 2020.

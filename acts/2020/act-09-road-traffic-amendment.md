### 3.

—(1)The principal Act is amended by inserting after
section 2 the following new sections–
“Establishment
and functions
of the Grenada
Transport
Commission

2A.—(1) There is hereby established the
Grenada Transport Commission which shall
be a body corporate to which section 49 of
the Interpretation and General Provisions Act,

## Chapter 153 shall apply.

(2) The Grenada Transport Commission shall advise the Minister on all matters
pertaining to road transport and traffic
including the following matters–
(a) transport rates, fares, tolls, dues
or other charges;
(b) licence duties and fees in respect
of motor and other vehicles;
(c) the needs of any area or areas in
relation to transport (including
the provision of adequate,
suitable and efficient services and
the elimination of unnecessary
or unremunerative services) and
the co-ordination of all forms of
passenger and goods transport;
(d) road safety;
(e) the regulation and control of
traffic;
(f)

regulating road transport;

(g) standards to be prescribed for
vehicles to be used in public,
commercial and private transportation;
(h) regulation of public transport
infrastructure, including car park
facilities, terminal facilities, busstops and roundabout facilities;
(i)

measures for controlling public,
private and commercial transportation and establishing conditions
under which they may operate;
and

(j)

any other matter affecting traffic
or transport that the Minister may
refer to the Grenada Transport
Commission.

(3) Where a person is aggrieved
against any order or decision of the Grenada
Transport
Commission,
the
Licensing
Authority or a Licensing Officer, the aggrieved
person may submit an appeal to the Minister for
consideration by an appeals tribunal.
(4) Where the Minister receives an
appeal under subsection (3), the Minister
shall appoint an appeals tribunal consisting of
three persons, of which one person shall be an
attorney-at-law who shall be the chairperson of
the appeals tribunal and of which two persons
shall have knowledge on the subject area in
question.

(5) No rates, fares, tolls, dues or other
charges charged by public service vehicles shall
be increased except with the prior written
approval of the Minister in consultation with
the Commission under subsection (2).
(6) Any owner, driver or conductor of
any public service vehicle, or any other person
on their behalf, who knowingly demands,
collects, or attempts to collect any increase in
the rate, fare, toll, due or other charge charged
by such vehicle when such owner, driver,
conductor or other person knows or ought to
know that such increase has not had the prior
written approval of the Minister in consultation
with the Commission under subsection (2)
commits an offence.
(7) The Commission shall perform
such other duties as are assigned to it by this Act
or regulations made hereunder.
Board of the
Commission.

2B.—(1) The affairs of the Grenada
Transport Commission shall be managed by a
Board consisting of seven members appointed
by the Minister as follows–
(a) the Commissioner of Police or
his or her designate;
(b) the Permanent Secretary with
responsibility for Transport;
(c) one senior representative of the
Ministry with responsibility for

road infrastructure, with expertise
in engineering; and
(d) four persons with knowledge
and experience in appropriate
disciplines including–
(i)

policy and management of
transport;

(ii) insurance; and
(iii) law.
(2) The Minister shall appoint from the
members under subsection (1)–
(a) a Chairperson who shall preside
at every meeting; and
(b) a Deputy Chairperson who
shall preside in a meeting in the
absence of the Chairperson.
(3) In the absence of the Chairperson
and the Deputy Chairperson, the members
present may elect one of their members present
to preside as Chairperson of the meeting.
(4) Where the Permanent Secretary
with responsibility for Transport is unable to
attend a meeting of the Board, the Minister may
authorise another representative of the Ministry
with responsibility for Transport to attend the
meeting.

(5) The members of the Board may
be remunerated as may be determined by the
Minister.
Tenure of
members of
the Board.

2C.—(1) Subject to subsection (2), a member
of the Board shall be appointed for a period not
exceeding three years and shall be eligible for
re-appointment.
(2) A person shall be disqualified
from being a member of the Board and shall
not become or continue to be appointed as a
member of the Board, if the person has–
(a) been declared bankrupt;
(b) been declared by the Court
to be physically or mentally
incapacitated;
(c) been convicted of a criminal
offence under the Criminal Code,

## Chapter 72A; or

(d) been convicted of an offence
pursuant to the provisions of this
Act, except where the offence is a
fixed penalty offence;
(e) been found guilty of misconduct
which is related to the performance
of his or her functions under this
Act;
(f)

been
absent
from
three
consecutive meetings of the
Board without leave granted by
the Minister; or

(g) has become incapable, wilful
or otherwise of carrying out his
or her functions pursuant to the
provisions of this Act.
(3) A member may resign by giving
written notice to the Minister by delivery to the
Chairperson.
(4) Notwithstanding subsection (3),
a Chairperson may resign by giving written
notice to the Minister.
(5) Resignation under subsection (3)
or (4) shall take effect immediately upon receipt
of the notice by the Minister or the Chairperson,
as the case may be.
(6) In the case of vacancy by reason of
termination, the Minister may appoint a new
member in accordance with subsection (1) for
the remainder of the tenure of the previous
member.
(7) In the case of absence of the
member, the Minister may appoint a temporary
member to act for the period of the absence of
the member.

Quorum,
meetings and
voting of the
Board.

2D.—(1) Quorum of the Board is any four
members notwithstanding any vacancy in the
numbers constituting the Board.
(2) The Board may meet as often as
may be necessary for the efficient performance
of its functions but shall meet at least once in
every two months.

(3) The decisions of the Board shall
be by a majority vote of the members present
and, in any case in which the voting is equal,
the Chairperson or other person presiding shall
have a casting vote.
(4) For the purposes of discharging
his or her duties under section 2E, the Chief
Executive Officer shall be present at all meetings
of the Board, unless he or she has obtained
leave of absence from the Chairperson or is
incapacitated by illness or other cause from
attending.
(5) The Board may regulate its
procedure.
Chief Executive
Officer and
Staff of the
Grenada
Transport
Commission.

2E.—(1) The Board shall appoint a Chief
Executive Officer, on such terms and conditions
as it thinks fit.
(2) The Chief Executive Officer shall
be responsible for the management of the affairs
of the Grenada Transport Commission in
accordance with the decisions of the Board.
(3) The Board may appoint on such
terms and conditions as it thinks fit, such
employees as may be required for the proper
and efficient discharge of the functions of the
Grenada Transport Commission under this
Act, including traffic wardens appointed under
section 3B.
(4) An employee of the Grenada
Transport Commission shall be under the

administrative control of the Chief Executive
Officer for the Grenada Transport Commission.
(5) The Public Service Commission
may, subject to such conditions as it may
impose, approve secondment of an officer in the
public service to any office within the Grenada
Transport Commission and an officer so
seconded shall, in relation to pension, gratuity
or other allowances, and in relation to other
rights as a public officer, be treated as continued
service in the public service in Grenada.

Funds of
the Grenada
Transport
Commission.

2F.—(1) The funds of the Grenada Transport
Commission shall consist of–
(a) moneys appropriated for the
purposes of the Grenada Transport
Commission by Parliament;
(b) grants made to the Grenada
Transport Commission by any
other body; and
(c) any other funds as may lawfully
accrue to the Grenada Transport
Commission.
(2) The Grenada Transport Commission
shall keep proper books of accounts and proper
records in relation thereto and such accounts,
books and records shall be in the form approved
by the Director of Audit.
(3) The financial year of the Grenada
Transport Commission shall be the same as the
financial year of the Government.

(4) The books and accounts of the
Grenada Transport Commission shall each year
be audited by the Director of Audit or by an
auditor appointed or authorised by the Director
of Audit, who shall submit a report on each
audit to the Grenada Transport Commission.
Annual
reports of
the Grenada
Transport
Commission.

2G.—(1) The Chief Executive Officer
shall, within three months after the end of
each financial year, submit for the approval
of the Board an annual report of the activities,
operations, undertakings, property and finances
of the Grenada Transport Commission for that
year.
(2) Subject to subsection (1), an annual
report shall include a copy of the audited
accounts of the Grenada Transport Commission
with the report under section 2F (4).
(3) A copy of the annual report
approved by the Board shall be sent to the
Minister not later than six months after the end
of the year to which the report relates and the
Minister shall, as soon as possible, but not later
than one month of the receipt thereof, lay the
report before Parliament.”.

(2) All contracts and agreements entered into by
the Grenada Transport Board are assumed by the Grenada
Transport Commission and may be enforced by and against
the Grenada Transport Commission as if it had been made
a party thereto.

(3) The real and personal property of the Grenada
Transport Board is transferred and becomes the real and
personal property of the Grenada Transport Commission.
(4) All obligations and liabilities of the Grenada
Transport Board continue as obligations and liabilities of the
Grenada Transport Commission.
(5) All legal proceedings and rights of action by or
against the Grenada Transport Board shall continue in force
as if made by the Grenada Transport Commission pursuant
to the powers conferred by the principal Act as amended by
this Act.
(6) A person in the employment of the Grenada
Transport Board immediately before the commencement of
this Act shall, on the coming into force of this Act be deemed
to have been duly employed by the Grenada Transport
Commission.

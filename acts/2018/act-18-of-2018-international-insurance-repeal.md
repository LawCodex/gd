### 1.

­—(1) This Act may be cited as the–

Short title and
commencement.

INTERNATIONAL INSURANCE (REPEAL) ACT,
2018\.
(2) This Act shall come into force on the 31st day of
December, 2018.

### 2.

The International Insurance Act, Chapter 152B is Repeal.
hereby repealed.

374 Act 188
Transitional
provisions.

### 3.

(1) Notwithstanding section 2, every licence granted
under the International Insurance Act, Chapter 152B prior to
commencement of this Act shall, on the commencement of
this Act, continue to be valid on the terms and conditions on
which it was granted until the operative date.
(2) Notwithstanding section 2, every lawful act by a
licensee under the Offshore Banking Act, Chapter 217A or
any director, officer or agent thereof, after commencement
of this Act but prior to the operative date, shall be valid.
(3) For the purposes of this section, “the operative
date” means–
(a) the date on which the licence expires; or
(b) 31st December, 2021,
whichever is earlier.

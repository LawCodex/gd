### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–
GRENADA CITIZENSHIP BY INVESTMENT
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2018,
and shall be read as one with the Grenada Citizenship by
Investment Act No. 15 of 2013 hereinafter referred to as the
“principal Act”.
(2) This Act shall be deemed to have entered into
force on the 25th day of September, 2015.

### 2.

Section 8 of the principal Act is amended by
repealing subsection (4) and substituting therefor the
following new subsection–
“(4) Within a period as may be prescribed by the
Minister in regulations the applicants shall pay the
balance of the processing fee and–
(a) in relation to the National Transformation Fund–
(i) deposit the required contribution into the
National Transformation Fund established
by the Government, to be held there until
the registration of the permanent residence
or citizenship of the applicant has been
completed, at which time the contribution
shall be applied in accordance with the
terms of their application for permanent
residence or citizenship by investment; or
(b)

in relation to an approved project–
(i) place the contribution payable in respect
of an approved project, in an irrevocable
escrow account controlled and managed
by a local Agent selected by the marketing
agent, pursuant to such guidelines issued
by the Minister in Regulations and any
guidelines which may be issued by the
Committee; and
(ii) complete and execute all necessary
documents for the payment or investment
of all sums due for the involvement in the
proposed approved project within such
period of placing the contribution in an
escrow account under paragraph (b)(i) as

prescribed by the Minister in regulations
and any guidelines which may be issued
by the Committee.”.
Passed by the House of Representatives this 18th day of July, 2018.

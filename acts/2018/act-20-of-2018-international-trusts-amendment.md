### 1.

­—(1) This Act may be cited as the
INTERNATIONAL TRUSTS (AMENDMENT) ACT,
2018,
and shall be read as one with the International Trusts Act,

## Chapter 152C, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

December, 2018.

### 2.

The principal Act is amended by inserting after section
2 the following new section–
“Prohibition
of creation of
international

### 2A.

Notwithstanding any other provision of
this Act, no person shall create an international
trust after the 31st day of December, 2018 and
any such purported international trust shall be
invalid.”.

AN ACT to amend the Pensions Act CAP. 233, the Pensions
(Prison Officers) Act CAP. 235, the Pensions (School
Teachers) Act CAP. 236, and the Police Pensions Act
CAP. 245.
[ July 27th, 2018 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives and by the authority of the
same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the
AMENDMENTS TO PENSIONS ACTS ACT, 2018.

### 2.

—(1) The Pensions Act, Chapter 233 is amended in
section 18 (2) (a) (i) by deleting the words “before the 4th
April, 1983” and substituting therefor the words “before the
22nd February, 1985”.
(2) Notwithstanding the date of commencement
of this Act, the Pensions Act, Chapter 233 as amended

by subsection (1) shall apply in respect of an officer first
appointed to the public service under the Government on or
after the 4th April, 1983 and before 22nd February, 1985.
Amendment of
Pensions (Prison
Officers) Act.

### 3.

—(1) The Pensions (Prison Officers) Act, Chapter 235
is amended in section 20 by deleting the words “on or after
the 4th April 1983” and substituting therefor the words “on
or after the 22nd February 1985”.
(2) Notwithstanding the date of commencement of
this Act, the Pensions (Prison Officers) Act, Chapter 235 as
amended by subsection (1) shall apply in respect of a Prison
Officer first appointed to the Prison Staff on or after the 4th
April, 1983 but before 22nd February, 1985.

Amendment of
Pensions (School
Teachers) Act.

### 4.

—(1) The Pensions (School Teachers) Act, Chapter
236 is amended in section 18 by deleting the words “on or
after the 4th April, 1983” and substituting therefor the words
“on or after the 22nd February, 1985”.
(2) Notwithstanding the date of commencement of
this Act, the Pensions (School Teachers) Act, Chapter 236 as
amended by subsection (1) shall apply in respect of a teacher
first appointed for the purposes of the Pensions (School
Teachers) Act, Chapter 236 on or after the 4th April, 1983
but before 22nd February, 1985.

Amendment
of the Police
Pensions Act.

### 5.

—(1) The Police Pensions Act, Chapter 245 is amended
in section 18 by deleting the words “on or after the 4th April,
1983” and substituting therefor the words “on or after the
22nd February, 1985”.
(2) Notwithstanding the date of commencement of
this Act, the Police Pensions Act, Chapter 245 as amended by
subsection (1) shall apply in respect of a non-commissioned
officer or constable first appointed for the purposes of the

Police Pensions Act, Chapter 245 on or after the 4th April,
1983 but before 22nd February, 1985.

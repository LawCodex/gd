# Act 14 of 2018 Appropriation (2019) 2018

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

1\.

Short title

2\.

Appropriation

## SCHEDULE

GRENADA
ACT NO. 14 OF 2018
L.S.
I assent,
14th December, 2018.

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to appropriate a sum not exceeding one billion,
one hundred and thirty-four million, thirteen
thousand, nine hundred and fifty-six dollars
($1,134,013,956.00) to the service of the State of
Grenada for the period January 1st to December
31st, 2019.
[14th December, 2018].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same
as follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

APPROPRIATION (2019) ACT, 2018.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated
Fund and applied to the service of the State of Grenada

Appropriation.

for the period January 1st to December 31st 2019, a sum
not exceeding one billion, one hundred and thirty-four
million, thirteen thousand, nine hundred and fifty-six dollars
($1,134,013,956.00).
(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is
appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

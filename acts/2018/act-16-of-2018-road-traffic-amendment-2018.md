### 1.

(1) This Act may be cited as the–
ROAD TRAFFIC (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2018,

Short title and
commencement.

and shall be read as one with the Road Traffic Act, Chapter
289A, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.
(2) This Act shall come into force on the 1st day of
January, 2019.

### 2.

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended as follows–

Amendment
to section 2 of
principal Act.

(a) in the definition of “parking” in paragraph
(c) by inserting the words “traffic warden
or” before the words “member of the Police
Force”; and
(b) by inserting in the appropriate alphabetical
order the following new definition–
“traffic warden” means a traffic warden appointed under section 3B;”.
Amendment
to section 3 of
principal Act.

### 3.

Section 3 of the principal Act is amended by inserting
after subsection (4) the following new subsections–
“(4A)The Grenada Transport Board may, with the
approval of the Minister, appoint such staff
as is considered necessary for the efficient
performance of its functions.
(4B)The funds of the Grenada Transport Board shall
consist of–
(a) money appropriated by Parliament for the
purpose of running the Grenada Transport
Board; and
(b) any other funds as may lawfully accrue to
the Grenada Transport Board.”.

Insertion of
new section
3B to principal
Act.

### 4.

The principal Act is amended by inserting after section
3A the following new section–
“Appointment
3B (1) The Grenada Transport
of traffic
with
the approval of the Minister and
wardens.

Board,
on the
recommendation of the Commissioner of Police,
may appoint traffic wardens in accordance with

this section for the purposes of controlling and
regulating traffic in accordance with this Act.
(2) A traffic warden shall–
(a) be not less than 19 years old and not
more than 45 years old;
(b) be of good character;
(c) be physically fit;
(d) have attained secondary school education;
(e) have an aptitude for hard work; and
(f) have passed a written examination set
by the Commissioner of Police.
(3) The uniform to be worn by a traffic warden
in execution of his or her duty shall be–
(a) blue baseball cap with white reflective
stripe around it and the words “RGPF
Traffic Warden” on the front;
(b) white shirt with epaulets, whether with
short sleeves or long sleeves;
(c) black trousers with cargo pockets;
(d) black shoes; and
(e) blue reflective bib with white reflective
vertical and horizontal stripes on the
front and rear, and with patches with–

(i) the words “Royal Grenada Police
Force” on the right breast pocket;
(ii) the words “Ministry of Transportation”
on the left breast pocket; and
(iii) the words “Traffic Warden” at the rear.
(4) The Grenada Transport Board shall pay to
traffic wardens a salary at a rate as determined by
the Minister.
(5) The Minister may, by Order, amend
subsection (2) or (3).”.
Amendment
to section 6 of
principal Act.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

CUSTOMS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2018,
and shall be read as one with the Customs Act No. 9 of 2015,
hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by inserting
the following new definitions in alphabetical order–
““Advance Cargo Information (ACI)” means manifest,
airway bill and bill of lading information or data
set out in Schedule IA in respect of—

Amendment
of section 2 of
principal Act.

(a) the cargo and stores including
all imports, exports in transit and
transhipment of goods;
(b) any package of which there is no bill
of lading or airway bill, on board of a
conveyance;
“Advance Cargo Information System (ACIS)” means
the electronic manifest single window for ACI
data interchange;
“ACI hit” means a cargo manifest, airway bill, bill of
lading or any documentation that has been flagged
by the ACIS;
“Cargo Manifest” means a list of cargo being
transported or warehoused;
“IMPACS” means the Implementing Agency for
Crime and Security established under the
2006 Agreement establishing the CARICOM
Implementing Agency for Crime and Security;
“Long haul” means flights with a duration of eight
hours or more;
“regional space” means the collective territories of the
Parties to the ACIS which comprise the Caribbean
Community (CARICOM); and
“Short haul” means flights with a duration of eight
hours or less;
“watch list” means a list that is used to watch or track
the current activity or movements of criminals
and suspicious or prohibited cargo including
terrorists, other persons and cargo of interest to
the Caribbean community (CARICOM).”.

## Part III

(1) The information required in Part I of Schedule IA shall be provided at the latest–
(a) for incoming cargo (i.e. import),
(i) in the case of short haul, at time of “Wheels Up” of aircraft;
(ii) in the case of long haul, 4 hours prior to arrival at the first port in the
country of destination; and
(b) for outgoing cargo (i.e. export), at the time of departure of the aircraft.
(2) The information required in Part II of Schedule IA shall be provided–
(a) in the case of commercial or private aircraft, not later than 40 minutes prior
to departure from their last port of call;
(b) in the case of a vessel or conveyance arriving from a member state of the
Caribbean Community, not later than one hour prior to its arrival from that
member state;
(c) in the case of a vessel or conveyance arriving from a country which is not
a member state of the Caribbean Community, at least 24 hours prior to its
arrival;
(d) in the case of commercial or private aircraft, not later than 40 minutes prior
to departure from Grenada; and
(e) in the case of a vessel or other conveyance, not later than one hour prior to
its departure from Grenada.

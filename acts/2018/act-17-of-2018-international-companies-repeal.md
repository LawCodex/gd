follows–

---

### 1.

—(1) This Act may be cited as the–

Short title and
commencement.

INTERNATIONAL COMPANIES (REPEAL) ACT,
2018\.
(2) This Act shall come into force on the 31st day of
December, 2018.

### 2.

—(1) The International Companies Act, Chapter 152 is Repeal.
hereby repealed.

368 Act 178

(2) All regulations made pursuant to the International
Companies Act, Chapter 152 are hereby repealed.
Transitional
provisions.

### 3.

(1) Notwithstanding section 2, incorporation of an
International Company under the International Companies
Act, Chapter 152 prior to commencement of this Act shall
continue to be valid upon commencement of this Act until
the operative date.
(2) Notwithstanding section 2, every lawful act by
an International Company or any director, officer or agent
thereof, after commencement of this Act but prior to the
operative date shall be valid.
(3) For the purposes of this section, “the operative
date” means–
(a) in the case of an International Company that
is a trust corporation for the purposes of the
International Trusts Act, Chapter 152C, the
date on which the international trust is terminated in accordance with section 9 of the
International Trusts Act, Chapter 152C; or
(b) in any other case–
(i)

the date on which the International
Company has been wound-up or dissolved in accordance with Part VIII of
the International Companies Act, Chapter 152; or

(ii) 31st December, 2021,
whichever is earlier.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 19th day of December,

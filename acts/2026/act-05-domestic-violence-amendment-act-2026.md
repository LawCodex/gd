### 2.

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended as follow–
(a) by repealing the definition of “cohabitant”
and substituting therefor the following
new definition–
““cohabitant” means–
(a) in relation to a woman, a single
man who is living or has lived
together with a single woman as
husband and wife on a bona fide
domestic basis continuously for a
period of not less than five years;
or
(b) in relation to a man, a single woman
who is living or has lived together
with a single man as husband and
wife on a bona fide domestic basis continuously for a period of not
less than five years;”;
(b) by repealing the definition of “domestic
violence” and substituting therefor the
following new definition–
““domestic
violence”
means
any
controlling or abusive behaviour that
harms the health, safety or well-being of

a person or any child, and includes the
following–
(a) physical abuse or threats of
physical abuse;
(b) sexual abuse or threats of sexual
abuse;
(c) emotional, verbal or psychological
abuse;
(d) economic abuse;
(e) intimidation;
(f) harassment, including online harassment;
(g) stalking, including cyberstalking;
(h) cyberbullying;
(i) damage to or destruction of
property; or
(j) entry into the applicant’s residence
without consent, where the parties
do not share the same residence;”;
(c) in the definition of “emotional, verbal and
psychological abuse”–

(i) in paragraph (b), by deleting the
word “or” where it appears; and
(ii) by inserting after paragraph (c) the
following new paragraphs–
“(d) the repeated making of unwelcome or intimidatory contact with the applicant by any
means, including in person,
verbally or by gesture, letter,
telephone and electronic or
digital means; or
(e) the dissemination of intimate
images of the applicant or
the applicant’s child by any
means, including electronic or
digital means, whether or not
such images have been digitally manipulated or altered;”;
(d) in the definition of “harassment”, by
deleting the word “mail” where it appears,
and substituting therefor the word
“communications”; and
(e) by inserting in the appropriate alphabetical
order, the following new definitions–
““cyberbullying” means the repeated or
continuous use of electronic or digital
means to publish, transmit or distribute

any content or message with the intent to
demean, humiliate, intimidate, threaten or
emotionally abuse the applicant;
“cyberstalking” means the repeated or
continuous use of electronic or digital
means to engage in a pattern of conduct that
harasses, intimidates, threatens or causes
fear or emotional distress to the applicant,
where such conduct is intended to control,
coerce or cause harm to the applicant or
would reasonably be perceived as such by
the applicant;
“online harassment” means engaging in a
pattern of conduct, through electronic or
digital means, that induces the fear of harm
or causes emotional distress or humiliation
to the applicant, including–
(a) repeatedly or continuously sending abusive, threatening or humiliating messages to the applicant;
(b) impersonating the applicant or creating false digital content intended
to harm the applicant’s reputation;
(c) monitoring or tracking the applicant’s digital activity without his
or her consent; or

(d) publishing or threatening to publish private or sensitive information about the applicant;
“private or sensitive information” means
any information, whether recorded
or unrecorded, oral, written, visual,
electronic or otherwise that–
(a) pertains to the intimate, personal
or confidential aspects of a person’s identity, relationships, activities or circumstances;
(b) is not publicly available or lawfully accessible without that person’s
consent; and
(c) which, if disclosed, is likely to result in–
(i) harm, humiliation, distress or
danger to that person; or
(ii) a violation of that person’s reasonable expectation of privacy;”.

# COVID-19 Cancellation of Carnival August Celebrations Act, 2021

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

GRENADA
ACT NO. 6 OF 2021
L.S.
I assent,
21st July, 2021.

LAWRENCE JOSEPH
Deputy to the Governor-General.

AN ACT to cancel the Carnival August Celebrations for
the year 2021 and the recognition of those days as bank
holidays.
[ 23rd July, 2021 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives and by the authority of the
same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

COVID-19 CANCELLATION OF CARNIVAL
AUGUST CELEBRATIONS ACT, 2021.

### 2.

Notwithstanding the Bank Holidays Act, Chapter 25,
for the year 2021 the Carnival August Celebrations under the
Schedule to the Bank Holidays Act are hereby cancelled and
the 9th day of August, 2021 and from noon on the 10th day

Cancellation of
Carnival August
Celebrations.

of August, 2021 shall not be Bank Holidays for the purposes
of the Bank Holidays Act.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 7th day of July, 2021.

ANDREW AUGUSTINE
Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 14th day of July, 2021.

ANDREW AUGUSTINE
Clerk to the Senate.

AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of Fifty-four
Million, Nine Hundred and Ten Thousand, One
Hundred and Forty-six dollars ($54,910,146.00) to
the service of the State of Grenada for the fiscal year
1st January to 31st December, 2021.
[ 22nd September, 2021 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION ACT, 2021.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the fiscal

Appropriation.

358 Act 11

year 1st January to 31st December, 2021, a sum not exceeding
Fifty-four Million, Nine Hundred and Ten Thousand, One
Hundred and Forty-six dollars ($54,910,146.00) in addition
to the sums already appropriated for that fiscal year.
(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is
appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

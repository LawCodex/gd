### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

SECURITIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2021,
and shall be read as one with the Securities Act No. 8 of
2021, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 127 of the principal Act is amended in
subsection (1) by repealing paragraph (a) and substituting
therefor the following new paragraph–

AN ACT to authorise the Minister for Finance to borrow from
various sources, an amount of Three Hundred and Thirty
Million Eastern Caribbean Dollars ($330,000,000.00).
[ 24th December, 2021 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the
2022 BUDGET LOAN AUTHORISATION ACT, 2021.

Short title.

### 2.

—(1)The Minister for Finance is hereby authorised
to raise a loan in an amount of Three Hundred and Thirty
Million Eastern Caribbean Dollars ($330,000,000.00), for
the purpose of financing the 2022 Budget.

Authority to
raise loan.

(2) All monies borrowed under this Act shall be

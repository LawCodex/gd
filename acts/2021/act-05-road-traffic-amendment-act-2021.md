AN ACT to amend the Road Traffic Act CAP. 289A.

BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent

and the House of Representatives and by the authority of the
same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

ROAD TRAFFIC (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2021,
and shall be read as one with the Road Traffic Act, Chapter
289A, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 3B of the principal Act is amended as follows–
(a) by inserting after subsection (3) the following
new subsection–

Amendment to
section 3B of
principal Act.

Act 5

Road Traffic (Amendment)

“(4) The equipment to be issued to and worn
in the prescribed manner by a traffic warden in
execution of his or her duty shall be–
(a) a two-way communication radio; and
(b) a torch.”;
(b) by renumbering subsection (4) as subsection (5);
and
(c) in subsection (5)–
(i) by renumbering subsection (5) as
subsection (6); and
(ii) by deleting the words “subsection (2) or
(3)” where they appear and substituting
therefor the words “subsections (2), (3)
and (4)”.
Passed by the House of Representatives this 7th day of July, 2021.

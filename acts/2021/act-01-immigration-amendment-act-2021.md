GRENADA
ACT NO. 1 OF 2021

L.S.
I assent,

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.
1st July, 2021.

AN ACT to amend the Immigration Act, CAP. 145.
[9th July, 2021].

BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives and by the authority of the same
as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

IMMIGRATION (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2021,
and shall be read as one with the Immigration Act, Chapter
145 hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Part III of the principal Act is amended as follows–
(a) by deleting the words “ten thousand dollars”
wherever they appear and substituting therefor
the words “twelve thousand dollars”;

Amendment
of Part III of
principal Act.

(b) by repealing subsection (6) of section 9B and
substituting therefor the following new subsection–
“(6) Any captain, master, agent, or member
of crew or passenger who–
(a) refuses to answer or knowingly gives
a false answer to any question put to
him by the Competent Authority; or
(b) fails to comply with any requests under subsection (5) (b),
commits an offence and is liable on summary
conviction to a fine of five thousand dollars
or to imprisonment for six months or to both
such fine and imprisonment.”.
Amendment of
section 36 of
principal Act

### 3.

Section 36 of the principal Act is amended in subsection (1) as follows–
(a) in paragraph (c) by deleting the word “or” after
the “semi-colon”;
(b) in paragraph (d) by deleting the “comma” after
the word “provided” and substituting therefor a
“semi-colon” and the word “or”;
(c) by inserting after paragraph (d) the following
new paragraph–
“(e) in a manner other than as specified as
under paragraph (a), assists any other
person to enter or remain in Grenada in
contravention of this Act or any other

law, including knowingly hiding or
accommodating such a person,”;
(d) in the end section by deleting the word “three”
after the words “a fine of” and substituting
therefor the word “twelve” and by deleting
the words “of this subsection” after the words
“paragraph (a)” and substituting therefor the
words “or (e) of this subsection, in addition to
such fine”.

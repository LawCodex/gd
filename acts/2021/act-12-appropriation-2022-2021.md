AN ACT to appropriate a sum of One Billion, Three
Hundred and Fifty-two Million, Two Hundred and
Ten Thousand, Six Hundred and Thirty-five dollars
($1,352,210,635.00) to the service of the State of
Grenada for the period 1st January to 31st December,
2022\.
[ 24th December, 2021 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same
as follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

APPROPRIATION (2022) ACT, 2021.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the

Appropriation.

period 1st January to 31st December, 2022, One Billion,
Three Hundred and Fifty-two Million, Two Hundred
and Ten Thousand, Six Hundred and Thirty-five dollars
($1,352,210,635.00).
(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is
appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

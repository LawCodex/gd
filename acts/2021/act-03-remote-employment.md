AN ACT to establish a framework for non-nationals to be
employed remotely in the State of Grenada.
[9th July, 2021].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives and by the authority of the same
as follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

REMOTE EMPLOYMENT ACT, 2021.

### 2.

In this Act–
“dependant” means–
(a) a spouse;
(b) a child under the age of eighteen years,
being a child of the main applicant or his
or her spouse;

Interpretation.

(c) a person in respect of whom the main
applicant, or his or her spouse, is the legal
guardian;
(d) a parent or parent of his or her spouse;
(e) a grandchild of the main applicant or his or
her spouse;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for
Immigration;
“non-national” means an individual who is not a
citizen of Grenada or any other OECS Protocol
Member State;
“OECS Protocol Member State” means Antigua
and Barbuda, Dominica, Saint ChristopherNevis, Saint Lucia, or Saint Vincent and the
Grenadines;
“permit holder” means a person to whom a remotework permit has been granted;
“police certificate” means a statement on the status
of an individual’s criminal records from the
national law enforcement authority of each
country in which the individual has resided
during the 12-month period immediately
preceding the making of an application;
“remote-work permit” means a remote-work permit
granted under section 3 and, except in section
4 (1), includes a remote-work permit renewed
under section 6;
“remote-work permit register” means the remotework permit register established under section

### 3.

—(1) Notwithstanding the Foreign Nationals and Remote-work
Commonwealth Citizens (Employment) Act, Chapter 115, permit.
upon an application under section 4, the Chief Immigration
Officer with the approval of the Minister may grant a remotework permit to a non-national to work remotely in Grenada
for a specified period in accordance with subsection (3).
(2) A remote-work permit shall be subject to the
following conditions–
(a) the permit holder and the dependants
of the permit holder shall not engage
in employment in Grenada other than
the employment for which the permit is
granted;
(b) for the duration of the remote-work permit,
the permit holder and the dependants of the
permit holder shall not operate a business
in Grenada or offer any service for a fee
to business registered in Grenada or to any
person in Grenada;
(c) the permit holder and every dependant
shall be covered by a health insurance
policy valid for the duration of the remotework permit; and
(d) no dependant shall be enrolled in a public
educational institution for the duration of
the remote-work permit,
and such additional conditions as may be attached to the
permit.
(3) A remote-work permit shall be valid for a period
not exceeding one year, unless renewed under section 6.

(4) A remote-work permit may apply in respect of
the applicant and his or her dependants.
(5) For the avoidance of doubt, subsection (2) (a)
does not preclude the permit holder or a dependant from
making an application for, and being issued, a work permit
under the Foreign Nationals and Commonwealth Citizens
(Employment) Act, Chapter 115.
Application for
permit.

### 4.

—(1) An application for a remote-work permit shall be
made, to the Chief Immigration Officer, in the form set out
as Schedule I, accompanied by the following–
(a) certified evidence of a passport that is valid for
the period for which the remote-work permit is
intended to apply;
(b) proof of the grant, or application for, a visa,
if a visa is required for the purposes of the
Immigration Act, Chapter 145 and the Passport
Act, Chapter 226;
(c) proof of an actual annual income of not less than
EC$100,000.00 generated outside of Grenada,
and proof that the actual income is intended to
continue for the period for which the remotework permit is intended to apply;
(d) proof of a health insurance policy valid for the
period for which the remote-work permit is
intended to apply;
(e) a police certificate; and
(f)

the fee prescribed in Schedule II.

(2) An application under subsection (1) may be in
respect of the main applicant and his or her dependants.
(3) In respect of any dependant included in an
application, paragraphs (a), (b), (d) and (f) of subsection (1)
shall apply and the main applicant shall provide evidence of
the relationship of the dependant to the main applicant.
(4) In respect of a dependant included in an
application who is over the age of sixteen, paragraph (e) of
subsection (1) shall apply.

### 5.

—(1) A permit holder shall, within 7 days and in writing, Modification of
permit.
notify the Chief Immigration Officer if–
(a) the permit holder has been notified that his or
her employment will be terminated prior to the
expiration of the remote-work permit; or
(b) there has been a change in any of the information
provided in the application form.
(2) Where the Chief Immigration Officer receives
notification for the purposes of subsection (1), the Chief
Immigration Officer with the approval of the Minister
may modify the remote-work permit or give notice of the
application of section 7 (1) (e) or (3), as the case may be.

### 6.

—(1) Not less than 30 calendar days prior to the Renewal of
permit.
expiration of a remote-work permit, the permit holder may
make an application in writing to the Chief Immigration
Officer, accompanied by–

(a) certified biodata page of a passport that is valid
for the additional period;
(b) proof of a valid visa for the additional period,
if a visa is required for the purposes of the
Immigration Act, Chapter 145 and the Passport
Act, Chapter 226;
(c) proof of an actual annual income of not less than
EC$100,000.00 generated outside of Grenada
during the period of the remote-work permit,
and proof that the income is intended to continue
for the additional period;
(d) proof of a health insurance policy valid for the
additional period; and
(e) the fee prescribed in Schedule II,
for the remote-work permit to be renewed for a period not
exceeding one year.
(2) Notwithstanding the Foreign Nationals and
Commonwealth Citizens (Employment) Act, Chapter
115, upon an application under subsection (1), the Chief
Immigration Officer, with the approval of the Minister, may
renew a remote-work permit in accordance with section 3
(3).
(3) Renewal of a remote-work permit may be subject
to the same conditions or varied conditions, which shall in
every case include the conditions set out in paragraphs (a) to
(d) of section 3 (2).
(4) An application under subsection (1) may be in
respect of the main applicant and his or her dependants.

(5) In respect of any dependant included in an
application, paragraphs (a), (b), (d) and (e) of subsection (1)
shall apply.

### 7.

—(1) The Minister may revoke a remote-work permit, Revocation of
permit.
if–
(a) the permit holder or a dependant included on
the permit breaches a condition attached to the
permit;
(b) the permit holder provided or provides false
information, made or makes a misrepresentation
or omitted or omits facts in an application under
section 4 (1) or 6 (1);
(c) the permit holder provided or provides a false
document to accompany an application under
section 4 (1) or 6 (1);
(d) the permit holder or a dependant included on the
permit is charged with a criminal offence under
any enactment; or
(e) the permit holder is terminated from his or
her employment prior to the expiration of the
remote-work permit.
(2) The revocation of the remote-work permit may
be in respect of–
(a) the permit holder and every dependant
included on the permit; or
(b) a specified dependant included on the
permit.

(3) The Minister may revoke a remote-work permit
in respect of a dependant included on the permit if the
dependant ceases to qualify as a dependant of the permit
holder.
(4) Without prejudice to section 26 of the
Immigration Act, Chapter 145, a notice of revocation of a
remote-work permit shall be in writing and shall be served
on a permit holder at least seven calendar days before the
notice is intended to take effect.
Remotework permit
register.

### 8.

—(1) There is hereby established a remote-work permit
register.
(2) The remote-work permit register shall contain
such information as may be determined by the Minister.
(3) The remote-work permit register shall be
maintained by the Chief Immigration Officer.

Exemption
from specified
taxes.

### 9.

—(1) For the avoidance of doubt, no income tax
is payable in respect of any income generated by the
non-national, or his or her dependant, outside of Grenada in
accordance with this Act.
(2) Notwithstanding the Customs Act, 2015 and the
CARICOM Common External Tariff, 2014, a permit holder
is entitled to the conditional duties exemptions set out in
Schedule III.

Presumption of
non-residence.

### 10.

For the avoidance of doubt, a permit holder and his
or her dependants are deemed to not be resident for the
purposes of–
(a) the Representation of the People Act, Chapter
286A;

(b) the Citizenship Act, Chapter 54A; and
(c) the Immigration Act, Chapter 145.

### 11.

The Minister may by Order amend a schedule.

Amendment of
schedules.

## SCHEDULE III

List of Conditional Duties Exemptions

2021
(section 9 (2))

Household effects, admitted as such by the Comptroller of Customs, not exceeding
a c.i.f. value of EC$75,000.00, which are for his or her personal use and not for sale
or exchange, provided–
(i) the items are accompanied by the person importing them; or
(ii) are imported within three months before or after the person’s arrivals in
Grenada or within such further period, as the Comptroller of Customs
considers reasonable if the articles would have been exempted from duty
had they accompanied the person importing them.

2\.

100% remission of duty in respect of one motor car or other vehicle of tariff heading
87.03 of the CARICOM Common External Tariff, 2014 principally designed for
the transportation of persons excluding maxi-taxi and similar vehicles–
(i) is accompanied by the person importing it; or is imported within three
months before or after the person’s arrival in Grenada or within such
further period as the Comptroller of Customs considers reasonable if the
vehicle would have been exempted from duty if it had accompanied the
person importing it, and
(ii) is not sold, lent, hired, exchanged or otherwise disposed of before 30
calendar days prior to the expiration of the remote-work permit.

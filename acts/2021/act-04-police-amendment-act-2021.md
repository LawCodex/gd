# Police (Amendment) Act, 2021

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

1\.

Short title

2\.

Amendment to section 7 of principal Act

GRENADA
ACT NO. 4 OF 2021

L.S.
I assent,

LAWRENCE JOSEPH
Deputy to the Governor-General.
16th July, 2021.

AN ACT to amend the Police Act CAP. 244.
[ 16th July, 2021 ].

BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives and by the authority of the same
as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

POLICE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2021,
and shall be read as one with the Police Act, Chapter 244,
hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 7 of the principal Act is amended by inserting Amendment
to section 7 of
after subsection (6) the following new subsection–
principal Act.

“(7) The Chief of Police may make regulations
providing for the promotion of police officers to the
various ranks in the Force in accordance with subsection
(4).”.

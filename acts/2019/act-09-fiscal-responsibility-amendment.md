GRENADA
ACT NO. 9 OF 2019
L.S.
I assent,
14th November, 2019.

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to amend the Fiscal Responsibility Act No. 29 of
2015\.
[ 22nd November, 2019 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives and by the authority of the
same as follows–

---

### 1.

—(1) This Act may be cited as the
FISCAL RESPONSIBILITY (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2019,

Short title and
commencement.

and shall be read as one with the Fiscal Responsibility Act,
No. 29 of 2015, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.
(2) This Act shall come into force on the 31st day of
October, 2020.

### 2.

Section 8 of the principal Act is amended in subsection
(3) in paragraph (f) by repealing subparagraphs (i) and (ii).

Amendment
to section 8 of
principal Act.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 28th day of October,
2019\.

ANDREW AUGUSTINE
Clerk to the House of Representatives (Ag.).

Passed by the Senate this 4th day of November, 2019.

ANDREW AUGUSTINE
Clerk to the Senate (Ag.).

AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of Nine Million,
Nine Hundred and Sixty-Seven Thousand, Seven
Hundred and Ninety Dollars ($9,967,790.00) to service
of the State of Grenada for the period August 1st to
December 31st, 2019.
[ 23rd August, 2019 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION ACT, 2019.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the period
August 1st, to December 31st, 2019, a sum not exceeding
Nine Million, Nine Hundred and Sixty-Seven Thousand,

Appropriation.

Seven Hundred and Ninety Dollars ($9,967,790.00) in
addition to the sums already appropriated for that year.
(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is
appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

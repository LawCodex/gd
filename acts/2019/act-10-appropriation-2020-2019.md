## SCHEDULE

GRENADA
ACT NO. 10 OF 2019
L.S.
I assent,

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to appropriate a sum of One Billion, One Hundred
and Forty-six Million, Ten Thousand, Seven Hundred
and Five dollars ($1,146,010,705) to the service of
the State of Grenada for the period January 1st to
December 31st, 2020.
[20th December, 2019].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same
as follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

APPROPRIATION (2020) ACT, 2019.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the
period January 1st to December 31st, 2020, One Billion,

Appropriation.

One Hundred and Forty-six Million, Ten Thousand, Seven
Hundred and Five dollars ($1,146,010,705).
(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is
appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

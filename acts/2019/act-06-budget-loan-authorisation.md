AN ACT to authorise the Minister of Finance to borrow
from various sources, a total amount not exceeding One
Hundred and Eighteen Million, Eight Hundred Thousand
Eastern Caribbean Dollars (EC$118,800,000).
[ 23rd August, 2019 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives and by the authority of the
same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the
2019 BUDGET LOAN AUTHORISATION ACT, 2019.

Short title.

### 2.

—(1)The Minister of Finance is hereby authorised
to borrow a total amount not exceeding One Hundred
and Eighteen Million, Eight Hundred Thousand Eastern
Caribbean Dollars (EC$118,800,000) for the purpose of
raising funds to cover borrowings for the period July to
December 2019.

Authority to
raise loan.

(2) All monies borrowed under this Act shall be
effected and secured pursuant to the terms and conditions
specified in the Schedule.

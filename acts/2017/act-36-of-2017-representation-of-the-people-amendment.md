AN ACT to amend the Representation of the People Act
Cap 286A.
[ 29th December, 2017 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives and by the authority of the same
as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

REPRESENTATION OF THE PEOPLE (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2017,
and shall be read as one with the Representation of the People
Act Cap. 286A, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

The Second Schedule of the principal Act is amended Amendment
Second
by deleting the words “Representation of the People Act, of
Schedule to
1993” where they appear and substituting therefor the words principal Act.
“Representation of the People Act Cap 286A”.

### 3.

The Third Schedule of the principal Act is amended as
follows–
(a) by deleting the words “Representation of the
People Act, 1993” where they appear and
substituting therefor the words “Representation
of the People Act Cap 286A”;
(b) in Form No. 1 by deleting the words “Section
33” where they appear and substituting therefor
the words “Section 36”;
(c) in Form No. 9 by deleting the words “Sections
42, 57” where they appear and substituting
therefor the words “Sections 46 and 61”;
(d) in Form No. 11 by deleting the words “Sections
47 and 56” where they appear and substituting
therefor the words “Sections 48 and 57”;
(e) in Form No. 12 by deleting the words “Sections
54 and 67” where they appear and substituting
therefor the words “Sections 58 and 71”;
(f) in Form No. 24 by deleting the words “Section
74” where they appear and substituting therefor
the words “Section 78”.

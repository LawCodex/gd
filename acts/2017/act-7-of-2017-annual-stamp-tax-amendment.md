AN ACT to amend the Annual Stamp Tax Act, Chapter 16A.
[ 19th May, 2017 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives and by the authority of the
same as follows–

---

### 1.

—(1) This Act may be cited as the
ANNUAL STAMP TAX (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2017,
and shall be read as one with the Annual Stamp Tax Act,

## Chapter 16A, hereafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

(2) This Act shall come into force on the first day of
January, 2018.

Short title and
commencement.

of the First

### 2.

The First Schedule of the principal Act is amended in
item (1) paragraph (a) by deleting the figure “0.75%” where
it appears and substituting therefor the following “0.7%”.

### 2.

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended in subsection
(1) as follows–
(a) by inserting the following new definition–
““carnival festival or activity” means a public event conducted during the carnival
period;”;
(b) by inserting the following new definition–
““carnival period” means the period that
commences on the date of the launch of
carnival by the Corporation and ends on
the Sunday after the second Tuesday in
August;”;
(c) by repealing the definition “component of
carnival” and substituting therefor the following
new definition–
““component of carnival” includes calypso
associations, mas associations, steelband associations, Parish Carnival
Committees or any other association
recognised by the Corporation as representing a stakeholder of Carnival, excluding the Carriacou and Petite Martinique Festivals Board;”;
(d) by inserting the following new definition–

““publication” includes all written and printed matter, and any record, tape, disc,
wire, perforated roll, film or other contrivance, by means of which any word,
image or idea may be mechanically,
electrically or otherwise produced, reproduced, represented, transmitted or
conveyed, and everything, whether of a
nature similar to the foregoing or not,
containing any visible representation,
or by its form, shape or in any manner
being capable of producing, reproducing, representing or conveying words,
images or ideas, and every copy and reproduction of any publication;”.

### 4.

The principal Act is amended by repealing section 5
and substituting therefor the following new section–
“Holding carnival festival without a licence

### 5.

—(1) No person shall hold a carnival festival or
activity–
(a) in an area declared under section 20 to be
the location for a carnival festival or activity operated by the Corporation; or
(b) at any time during the carnival period,
unless the person obtains a licence under section 6.
(2) A person who promotes or conducts any
carnival festival or activity in contravention of subsection (1)–
(a) is liable to a fine in the amount of
forty percent of the proceeds of the
carnival festival or activity payable to
the Corporation, no later than seven
days after the conclusion of the carnival festival or activity; and
(b) shall not be entitled to any assistance,
financial or otherwise from the Board.
(3) Where a person contravenes subsection
(1) and fails to pay the fine under subsection (2) (a)
within the specified time, the person commits an

offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine
not exceeding fifty thousand dollars or to a term of
imprisonment not exceeding one year, or both.
(4) A person who issues a publication in
respect of a carnival festival or activity for which the
person purports to be conducting and for which no
licence has been granted under section 6 commits
an offence and is liable on summary conviction to
a fine of five hundred dollars for each incident of
the publication which shall in no case exceed an
aggregate of fifty thousand dollars.
(5) Where an offence under subsection (3) or
(4) is committed by a body corporate, every director
or promoter of the body corporate, unless he or she
proves on a balance of probability that the offence
was not committed, shall be liable upon summary
conviction, to a fine not exceeding seventy-five
thousand dollars or to a term of imprisonment not
exceeding two years, or both.”.

“Carnival festival licence

### 6.

—(1) A person who is desirous of holding a
carnival festival or activity may, no later than two
months before the proposed date of the carnival
festival or activity, make an application in writing to
the Board for a licence.
(2) An application under subsection (1) shall
be in the form prescribed and shall be accompanied
by a prescribed fee.

(3) If the Board, in consultation with the
Minister, is satisfied that the application is complete
and the person making the application is eligible to
operate a carnival festival or activity, it may grant
the application and issue to the applicant a licence
subject to such terms and conditions as it considers
necessary.
(4) Notwithstanding subsection (1), the
Board may consider an application made outside of
the prescribed period, if the Board determines that
the carnival festival or activity is likely to enhance
carnival.
(5) Notwithstanding subsection (3), the
Board shall attach the following conditions to every
licence granted to hold a carnival festival or activity
for which an admission fee would be charged–
(a) one ticket shall be issued for each
admission to the carnival festival
or activity;
(b) no later than seven days after the
conclusion of the carnival festival
or activity, the holder of the licence shall pay to the Corporation
a licence fee amounting to five percent of the admission fee in respect
of every individual admitted to the
carnival festival or activity; and
(c) no later than seven days after the
conclusion of the carnival festival
or activity, the holder of the licence

shall file returns in the prescribed
form to account for the admission
of every individual admitted to the
carnival festival or activity.
(6) For the purposes of subsection (5), every
ticketed admission shall constitute an admission notwithstanding the admission was granted at a reduced
rate or free of charge.
(7) Notwithstanding subsection (3), the
Board shall attach the following condition to every
licence granted to a person resident outside of Grenada–
(a) the person shall pay a deposit as
may be determined by the Corporation, or the person shall have at
least one guarantor resident in Grenada for the sum determined by the
Corporation;
(b) the person or an agent identified as
such shall be present in Grenada
for the conduct of the carnival festival or activity; and
(c) the person or the identified agent
thereof shall give an undertaking
that all outstanding fees will be
paid to the Corporation before his
or her departure from Grenada or
no later than seven days after the
conclusion of the carnival festival
or activity, whichever first occurs.

(8) A person to whom a licence has been
granted who contravenes a condition attached to the
licence commits an offence and is liable on summary
conviction to a fine not exceeding fifty thousand
dollars or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding
one year, or both.
(9) In respect of an application under
subsection (1), the Minister, in consultation with
the Board, may as he or she deems fit, waive the
requirement to pay any fee under this section or
regulations made for the purposes of this section.”.

6\. The principal Act is amended by inserting after section
6 the following new section–
“Appeal against decision involving licence

### 6A.

—(1) Any person who is aggrieved by a
decision of the Board under section 6 may appeal to
the Minister against the decision, in the prescribed
form accompanied by the prescribed fees.
(2) Every appeal under subsection (1) shall
be made to the Minister within fourteen days of the
date of the notice of the decision appealed against
and shall set out the ground for appeal and where
applicable be submitted with a copy of the decision
appealed against.
(3) A person who applies to the Minister for
a review of the decision and who is aggrieved by the
decision of the Minister may, in the prescribed form
accompanied by the prescribed fees, appeal against

the Minister’s decision to a tribunal, appointed for
the purpose of an appeal under this Act.
(4) For the purposes of an appeal, the
Tribunal shall consist of three trained mediators
within the court system, currently on the court’s
roster of mediators.
(5) For the purposes of an appeal–
(a) an aggrieved person shall appoint a
mediator;
(b) the Corporation shall appoint a
mediator; and
(c) the court shall designate and appoint a mediator who shall be the
umpire mediator.
(6) The appeal tribunal may regulate its own
procedure.
(7) The appeal tribunal may, after making
such enquiry as it thinks fit and after giving the
appellant a reasonable opportunity to present his or
her case–
(a) allow or dismiss the appeal;
(b) uphold the decision of the Board;
or
(c) reserve or vary the decision of the
Board.
(8) The decision of the tribunal, on an appeal
shall be final.”.

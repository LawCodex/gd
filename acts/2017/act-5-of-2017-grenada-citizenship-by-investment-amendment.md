### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

GRENADA CITIZENSHIP BY INVESTMENT
(AMENDMENT) ACT, 2017,
and shall be read as one with the Grenada Citizenship by
Investment Act No. 15 of 2013 hereinafter referred to as the
“principal Act”.

### 2.

The principal Act is amended by inserting after section
8 the following new section–

Insertion of
new section
8A in principal
Act.

“Commissioners of Oaths
8A. (1) For the purposes of this section, the
Attorney-General duly designated by the GovernorGeneral may appoint fit and proper persons as
Commissioners of Oaths.
(2) A Commissioner of Oaths duly appointed
under this section may tender to an applicant who
is granted citizenship by investment the oath or
affirmation of allegiance in any place outside of
Grenada.
(3) An appointment under sub-section (2) shall
be for the period specified in the instrument of
appointment, unless sooner revoked.
(4) A person who wishes to be appointed as a
Commissioner of Oaths may submit an application
to the Attorney-General in the form set out in the
Schedule accompanied by the prescribed nonrefundable application fee.
(5) The Attorney-General shall, after due
consideration of an application grant, deny or delay
for cause an application.
(6) Within twenty-one days of submission of
a complete application the Attorney-General shall
notify or cause to be notified the applicant of the
decision made regarding the application.
(7) The Minister may by Order published in the
Gazette specify the places outside of Grenada where
a Commissioner of Oaths may tender the oath or
affirmation of allegiance.

(8) In this section “Commissioner of Oaths”
means a person appointed by the Attorney-General
under sub-section (1).”.

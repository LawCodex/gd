### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

CUSTOMS (AMENDMENT) (NO.3) ACT, 2017
and shall be read as one with the Customs Act, No. 9 of
2015, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 248 of the principal Act is repealed and the Repeal and
substitution of
following substituted therefor–
section 248 of
“Regulation
of customs
brokers and
tariff clerks.

248\. (1) The Minister may make regulations
generally respecting the establishment, licensing
and regulation of customs brokers and tariff
clerks.

principal Act.

576 Act 35

(2) Without limiting the generality of
subsection (1), the Minister may by regulations
provide for—
(a) the establishment of the Customs
Brokers and Tariff Clerks Board, its
composition, tenure of members,
functions, powers of the Board,
expenses and revenues, procedures
and other matters relating to the
functions of the Board;
(b) the licensing, registration and
examination of customs brokers and
tariff clerks;
(c) the renewal and revocation of licences
granted to customs brokers and tariff
clerks;
(d) recognition of licences granted in
other CARICOM Member States
to persons to practise as customs
brokers and tariff clerks;
(e) payment of fees in relation to the
application for licences and the grant
and renewal of licences;
(f) Code of Ethics for customs brokers
and tariff clerks;
(g) penalties in accordance with section
264 of the Act for breach of the
Customs Brokers and Tariff Clerks
Code of Ethics, these Regulations
and other professional misconduct of
customs brokers and tariff clerks;

(h) prescribing anything that is required
to be done under the Act.
(3) Regulations made under this section shall
be subject to negative resolution of Parliament.”.

---

### 1.

—(1) This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

PHYSICAL PLANNING AND DEVELOPMENT
CONTROL (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2017,
and shall be read as one with the Physical Planning and
Development Control Act No. 23 of 2016 hereinafter
referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by deleting
the definition of “Physical Planning Unit” where it appears
and substituting therefor the following–

Amendment
of section 2 of
principal Act.

“ “Physical Planning Unit” means a unit, whether
or not called by that name, responsible for the
implementation of this Act;”.

of section 6 of
principal Act.

### 3.

Section 6 of the principal Act is amended as follows–
(a) in subsection (2) by deleting the words “head
of the Physical Planning Unit” and substituting
therefor–
“Senior Planning Officer”; and
(b) in subsection (6) by deleting the words “The
Chief Executive Officer and the Executive
Secretary” where they appear and substituting
therefor–
“The Secretary and the Chief Executive
Officer or a Senior Technical employee of
the Authority approved by the Board,”.

Amendment of
section 22 of
principal Act.

### 4.

Subsection (4) of section 22 of the principal Act is
amended in paragraph (g) by inserting the word “Impact”
after the word “Environmental” where it appears.

Amendment of
section 39 of
principal Act.

### 5.

Subsection (3)(d) of section 39 of the principal Act is
repealed and the following substituted therefor–
“one shall be a representative of the Ministry of the
Environment;”.

Amendment of
Schedule I of
principal Act.

### 6.

Schedule I of the principal Act is amended in subparagraph (2) of paragraph 3 by deleting the word “any”
where it first appears and substituting therefor the number
“6”.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 8th day of September,
2017\.

# Act 1 of 2017 Income Tax (Amendment)

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

1\.

Short title and commencement

2\.

Amendment of section 2 of principal Act

3\.

Amendment of section 25 of principal Act

GRENADA
ACT NO. 1 OF 2017
L.S.
I assent,

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
8th May, 2017.
Governor-General.

AN ACT to amend the Income Tax Act, Chapter 149.
[12th May, 2017].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives and by the authority of the
same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title and
commencement.

INCOME TAX (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2017,
and shall be read as one with the Income Tax Act, Chapter
149 hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.
(2) This Act shall be deemed to have come into force
on the first day of January, 2017.

### 2.

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by inserting in Amendment
section 2 of
the appropriate alphabetical order the following definitions– of
principal Act.

““pension income” means a fixed amount, other than
wages, paid at regular intervals or as a lump
sum to a member of an approved pension fund,
upon that member reaching the retirement
age or upon that member’s death to his or her
surviving beneficiaries;”; and
““retirement age” means the age that is specified as
the retirement age under the pension fund;”.
Amendment of
section 25 of
principal Act.

### 3.

Section 25 of the principal Act is amended in subsection (1) by inserting after paragraph (ff) the following
new paragraphs–
“(gg) any pension income payable to a member of an
approved pension fund in accordance with section
48; and
(hh) any income accrued to a person as a result of
his or her years of employment by way of pension or
a lump sum payment.”.

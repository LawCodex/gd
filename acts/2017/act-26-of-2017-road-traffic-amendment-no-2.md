### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

ROAD TRAFFIC (AMENDMENT) (NO. 2) ACT, 2017,
and shall be read as one with the Road Traffic Act, Chapter
289A, hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.

### 2.

Section 74 of the principal Act is repealed and the
following substituted therefor–
74\. (1) Subject to this section, no person shall
drive or ride on a motor cycle unless wearing
protective gear designed to protect the person
driving or riding on a motor cycle.

(2) Protective gear for the purposes of
subsection (1) shall have such characteristics as
the Minister prescribes by Order.
(3) A person who contravenes subsection (1)
commits an offence and is liable on summary
conviction to a fine of seven hundred and fifty
dollars and to imprisonment for three months.”.

AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of eighty-six
million, seven hundred and sixty-seven thousand,
eight hundred and one dollars and thirty-seven cents
($86,767,801.37) to the service of the State of Grenada
for the period July 1st to December 31st, 2017.
[ 11th August, 2017 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION (NO. 1)
ACT, 2017.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated
Fund and applied to the service of the State of Grenada
for the period July 1st to December 31st 2017, a sum not

Appropriation.

284 Act 16

exceeding eighty-six million, seven hundred and sixtyseven thousand, eight hundred and one dollars and thirtyseven cents ($86,767,801.37) in addition to the sums already
appropriated for that year.
(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is
appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

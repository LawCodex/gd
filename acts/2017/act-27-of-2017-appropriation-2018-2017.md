# Act 27 of 2017 Appropriation (2018) 2017

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

1\.

Short title

2\.

Appropriation

## SCHEDULE

GRENADA
ACT NO. 27 OF 2017
L.S.
I assent,
20th December, 2017.

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to appropriate a sum not exceeding one billion,
one hundred and twelve million, thirty-nine
thousand, nine hundred and thirty-eight dollars
($1,112,039,938.00) to the service of the State of
Grenada for the period January 1st to December 31st
2018\.
[20th December, 2017].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same
as follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

APPROPRIATION (2018) ACT, 2017.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the

Appropriation.

period January 1st to December 31st 2018, one billion,
one hundred and twelve million, thirty-nine thousand, nine
hundred and thirty-eight dollars ($1,112,039,938.00).
(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is
appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

“Advance
passenger and
cargo information.

20A. (1) The master of every conveyance
whether laden or in ballast, and every agent shall,
prior to arrival of the conveyance in Grenada,
provide to the proper officer, the passenger and
cargo information set out in Parts I and II of
Schedule IA within the periods specified in Part
III of that Schedule.
(2) Where the distance from Grenada to a
port of departure of a conveyance arriving in
Grenada does not permit the master to provide
the information within the time specified in Part
III of Schedule IA, the Comptroller–
(a)

may exempt the master of the conveyance from the requirements set
out in that Schedule; and

(b)

shall establish alternative notification
arrangements for the conveyance to
which this subsection applies.

(3) The master of a conveyance and every
agent shall, prior to departure of the conveyance
from Grenada provide to the proper officer, the
passenger and cargo information set out in Part
IV of Schedule IA.
(4) The Minister may, by Order subject to
negative resolution, amend Schedule IA.
(5) Regulations may prescribe procedures
for the reporting and processing of advance
passenger and cargo information.

(6) A master or an agent who fails to comply with the requirements of, or arrangements
established under this section commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a
fine of twenty thousand dollars or to a term of
imprisonment for one year or to both.

## Part III

(1)

The information set out in Parts I and II of this Schedule shall be provided–
(a) in the case of commercial or private aircraft, not later than 40 minutes prior
to departure from their last port of call;
(b) in the case of a vessel or conveyance arriving from a member state of the
Caribbean Community, not later than one hour prior to its arrival from that
member state; and
(c) in the case of a vessel or conveyance arriving from a country which is not
a member state of the Caribbean Community, at least 24 hours prior to its
arrival.

## Part IV

(a) in the case of commercial or private aircraft, not later than 40 minutes prior
to departure from Grenada; and
(b) in the case of a vessel or other conveyance, not later than one hour prior to its
departure from Grenada,

(2)

The passenger and cargo information referred to in subsection (1) shall be–
(a) the country of destination;
(b) the estimated time of departure;
(c) the customs port, customs airport or place of departure;
(d) a list of cargo for export and the country to which the cargo is to be exported;
(e) a list of the names of all passengers and crew on board; and
(f) such other information as may be prescribed by Regulations made under this
Act.

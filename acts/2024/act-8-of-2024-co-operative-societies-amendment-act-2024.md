# Act 8 of 2024 Co-operative Societies (Amendment) Act, 2024

## ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS

1\.

Short title

2\.

Amendment of section 22 of principal Act

GRENADA
ACT NO. 8 OF 2024
L.S.
I assent,
21st June, 2024.

CÉCILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to amend the Co-operative Societies Act CAP.
66A.
[ 21st June, 2024 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority
of the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the
CO-OPERATIVE SOCIETIES (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2024,
and shall be read as one with the Co-operatives Societies
Act, Chapter 66A hereinafter referred to as the “principal
Act”.

Short title.

Amendment of
section 22 of
principal Act.

2024

### 2.

Section 22 of the principal Act is amended by inserting
after subsection (2) the following new subsection–
“(3) A member of the public may, during the
normal business hours of the co-operative society,
examine any of the records specified in section 21(2)
(c), (e) and (f).”.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 4th day of June, 2024.

ANDREW AUGUSTINE
Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed by the Senate this 6th day of June, 2024.

ANDREW AUGUSTINE

Clerk to the Senate.

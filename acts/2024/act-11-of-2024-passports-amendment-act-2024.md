of the same as follows–

---

### 1.

—(1) This Act may be cited as the–
PASSPORTS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2024,
and shall be read as one with the Passports Act, Chapter 226
hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act”.
(2) This Act shall enter into force on the 1st day of
July 2024.

Short title and
commencement.

230 Act 11
Amendment
of section 6 of
principal Act.

### 2.

Section 6 of the principal Act is amended as follows–
(a) in subsection (2) by deleting the word “five”
before the word “years” and substituting therefor
the word “ten”;
(b) by inserting after subsection (2) the following
new subsection–
“(3) Notwithstanding subsection (2), a
passport granted in respect of a person who is
under the age of eighteen years, shall remain
valid for a period not exceeding five years
from the date of issue.”.

Passed by the House of Representatives this 25th day of June, 2024.

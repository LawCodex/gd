AN ACT to provide for the recording and transcribing of
court proceedings and for related matters.
[ 21st June, 2024 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority
of the same as follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

RECORDING OF COURT PROCEEDINGS ACT, 2024.

### 2.

In this Act–
“Chief Registrar” means the Chief Registrar of the
Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court;

Interpretation.

“court” includes the Eastern Caribbean Supreme
Court and the Magistrate’s Court, and any other
court established by law whether before or after
the commencement of this Act;
“court proceedings” includes a matter transpiring
in a court required by law or by the presiding
judicial officer of the court to be recorded;
“interpreter” means a person appointed by the
Public Service Commission or as may be
contracted by the Government to interpret orally
or otherwise from one language to another in
court proceedings;
“law officer of Government” means a law officer of
Government for the purposes of section 20 of
the Legal Profession Act, 2011;
“official transcript” means a transcript that has been
certified in accordance with section 7;
“Registrar” means the Registrar of the High Court;
“transcript” means the record of court proceedings
made under this Act and includes an audio, video
or other digital recording of court proceedings,
written or typed notes made by a presiding
judicial officer or transcribed record of court
proceedings;
“transcriptionist” means a person authorised by
the Chief Registrar, the Registrar or a senior
magistrate to transcribe records of court
proceedings or any authorised person from the

computer-aided transcription unit of the public
service.

### 3.

—(1) The Chief Registrar, the Registrar or a senior
magistrate as the case may be, shall cause all court
proceedings to be recorded.

Recording of
court proceedings.

(2) A recording of court proceedings may be made
electronically by using an audio or video recorder or other
means directed by the presiding judicial officer.
(3) A recording of court proceedings made under
subsection (1) may be reproduced for the use of the court or
parties to the court proceedings.
(4) The Chief Registrar, the Registrar or a senior
magistrate as the case may be, may, subject to subsection
(5), cause court proceedings recorded under subsection (1),
to be made the transcript, by the means and in the manner
directed by the Chief Justice.
(5) A person who is not a transcriptionist shall not
transcribe a recording of court proceedings.

### 4.

In transcribing the evidence of a witness in court
proceedings, where the answers of the witness are not in the
English Language, the transcriptionist shall only transcribe
the answers in the words of an interpreter.

Words of interpreter.

### 5.

A person who prepares a transcript of court proceedings
shall certify, in Form 1 as set out in the Schedule, that the
transcript is an accurate transcript of the court proceedings.

Certification of
transcript.

### 6.

—(1) Subject to subsection (2), where evidence given
by a witness in court proceedings is recorded electronically
or a transcript is prepared, the transcript may not be read or

Witness need not
sign transcript.

played back to the witness and the witness is not required to
sign the transcript.
(2) Evidence of a witness that is recorded
electronically or a transcript that is prepared under subsection
(1), shall be certified in accordance with section 5.
Transcript
or record as
proof of court
proceedings.

### 8.

In the absence of evidence to the contrary, an official
transcript or official record of court proceedings is proof of
matters transpiring at those court proceedings.

Request for
official transcript.

### 9.

—(1) A party to court proceedings or another person
may request a copy of an official transcript of court
proceedings in the form set out as Schedule II.
(2) Upon a request under subsection (1) and without
undue delay, the Chief Registrar, the Registrar or a senior
magistrate shall, furnish a copy of an official transcript to the
person who made the request.
(3) The fees payable for an official transcript, where
applicable, are as specified in Rules made under section
108 of the West Indies Associated States Supreme Court
(Grenada) Act, Chapter 336 or section 17 of the West Indies
Associated States Supreme Court Order 1967.

Exemption
from fees.

### 10.

Notwithstanding section 9, a judicial officer, the
Attorney-General, the Director of Public Prosecutions or
other law officer of Government may obtain an official
transcript of court proceedings for the performance of his or
her duties as a law officer of Government but shall not pay
any fees for the preparation of an official transcript.

Rules.

### 11.

The Chief Justice and two other judges may make
Rules for the purpose of giving effect to this Act.

### 12.

—(1) The Chief Justice may issue Practice Directions
for the purpose of giving effect to this Act.

Practice Directions.

(2) A Practice Direction shall be–
(a) published in the Gazette; and
(b) displayed and made available at each court
office.
(3) A party shall comply with a Practice Direction
unless the court is satisfied that there is good reason for not
doing so.

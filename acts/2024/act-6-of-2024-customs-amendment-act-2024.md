Section 94 of the principal Act is amended by inserting
after subsection (4) the following new subsections–

### 5.

Section 176 of the principal Act is amended by inserting
after subsection (3) the following new subsections–
“(4) Where a person contravenes subsection (1),
the Comptroller or a proper officer shall immediately
file a Suspicious Activity Report with the Financial
Intelligence Unit.
(5) Without prejudice to section 9 (6), where a
person contravenes subsection (1) in respect of goods
being exported, the Comptroller or a proper officer
may notify the Comptroller of Customs in the country
of importation, or the relevant authority in accordance
with the laws of that country, of the particulars of the
contravention.”.

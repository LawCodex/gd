AN ACT to authorize the Minister of Finance to borrow
from the OPEC Fund for International Development,
an amount not exceeding US$10 million Dollars.
[ 13th December, 2013 ].
WHEREAS the Government of Grenada is committed to
economic growth and job creation;
WHEREAS the Government of Grenada is committed
to ensuring an equitable distribution of development
throughout Grenada;
WHEREAS the St. Patrick’s Roads and Rehabilitation
Project aims to support economic growth, through the
rehabilitation and upgrading of two sections of roads in St.
Patrick’s, from Duquesne to Sauteurs and Morne Fendue to
Poyntzfield (each, 8 km length);

WHEREAS section 49 of the Public Finance Management
Act No. 27 of 2007, authorises the Minister of Finance to
secure a liability by borrowing for the purposes specified
therein, including for any purpose of a capital nature;
AND WHEREAS the Minister of Finance proposes to
borrow from the OPEC Fund for International Development,
on behalf of the Government of Grenada, an amount of Ten
Million United States dollars for the purpose aforementioned
in the manner provided for under this Act;
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives and by the authority of the
same as follows:
Short title.

ST. PATRICK’S ROADS REHABILITATION AND UPGRADING PROJECT

## SCHEDULE 1

DESCRIPTION OF THE PROJECT
The Project aims at supporting the economic growth of Grenada, through the
rehabilitation and upgrading of two road sections in St. Patrick, to asphalt standards:
Section I, from Duquesne to Sauteurs, and Section II, from Mt. Fendue to Poyntzfield.
The objectives of the Project will be achieved through the implementation of the
following components:
(a)

Rehabilitation and Upgrading of Roads:

Rehabilitating and upgrading road Sections I and II (each, 8 km length) to a 4.88m
wide asphalt and concrete pavement carriageway. This includes the construction of
four bridges and rehabilitation of twelve small bridges, the usage of concrete pavement
on steep gradients and tight bends, and the provision of the necessary side drainage
facilities.
(b)

Consultancy Services:

This component will cover the costs of the consultancy services required for the
supervision and technical audit of the civil works related to Project implementation.
Consultants will also provide assistance during the tender process.
(c)

Institutional Support (PIU):

This component will cover the costs related to the PIU’s operations, staff
compensation, office and transport equipment.

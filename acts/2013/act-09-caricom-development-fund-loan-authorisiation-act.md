# Caricom Development Fund Loan Authorisiation Act

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

1\. Short Title
2\. Authority to raise loan
3\. Schedule

GRENADA
ACT NO. 9 OF 2013
I assent,

26th July, 2013.

CECILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to authorise the Minister of Finance to borrow from
the CARICOM Development Fund, an amount not
exceeding US$3.5 million Dollars.
WHEREAS the CARICOM Development Fund (CDF) was
established under Article 158 of the Revised Treaty of
Chaguaramas for the purpose of providing financial or
technical assistance to disadvantaged countries, regions and
sectors. It is the centre-piece of the regime to address the
disparities among the Member States of CARICOM which
may result from the implementation of the CSME. The
Agreement Relating to the Operations of the Fund was signed
in July 2008 and the CDF began operating on August 24,
2009\.
WHEREAS Grenada is now a fully paid up member of the
CARICOM Development Fund;

WHEREAS Grenada and the CARICOM Development
Fund will soon agree on a Country Assistance Programme;
WHEREAS the Country Assistance programme (CAP) for
Grenada will focus on addressing needs in the following four
areas:
• Support to Grenada Development Bank;
• Technical Assistance for the Grenada Development
Bank and the Small and Medium size Enterprises
(SMEs);
• Standards, Metrology and Certification;
• Energy development (energy efficiency in the tourism
sector); and
• Information and Communications Technology.
WHEREAS section 49 of the Public Finance Management
Act No. 27 of 2007, authorises the Minister of Finance to
secure a liability by borrowing for the purposes specified
therein, including for any purpose of a capital nature;
AND WHEREAS the Minister of Finance proposes to
borrow from the CARICOM Development Fund, on behalf of
the Government of Grenada, an amount of Three Million and
Five Hundred Thousand United States dollars for the purpose
aforementioned in the manner provided for under this Act;
[ 16th August, 2013 ].

BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same as
follows–
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–
2013 CARICOM DEVELOPMENT FUND LOAN
AUTHORISATION ACT, 2013.

Authority to
raise loan.

### 2.

—(1) The Minister of Finance is hereby authorised to
raise a loan in an amount of Three million, five hundred
thousand United States dollars for the specified purpose.
(2) All monies borrowed under this Act shall be
effected and secured pursuant to the terms and conditions
specified in the Schedule.

## SCHEDULE

(Section 2(2))

Type

Loan

Purpose

To implement the Country Assistance Programme with the
CARICOM Development Fund

Currency

United States Dollars

Lender

CARICOM Development Fund

Interest Rate

Not exceeding 3 percent per annum

Terms & Repayment

Forty equal or approximately equal and consecutive
quarterly installments on each Due Date, commencing on
the first due Date after the expiry of two (2) years following
the date of first disbursement of the Loan or on such later
due Date as the CDF may specify in writing.

Tabling of Agreement

The Loan Agreement signed pursuant to this Loan Authorisation Act will be tabled in Parliament as soon as
practicable

Passed in the House of Representatives this 28th day of May, 2013.

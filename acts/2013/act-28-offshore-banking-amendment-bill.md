### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

OFFSHORE BANKING (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2013

### 2.

In this Act–

Interpretation.

“principal Act” means the Offshore Banking Act,
Cap. 217A.

### 3.

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by inserting Amendment
of section 2 of
the following definition in alphabetical order–
principal Act.

no physical presence involving meaningful

section 13A of
principal Act

### 4.

The principal Act is amended by inserting the following
section after section 13–
“ 13A. Shell banks
The Minister shall not grant a licence to a shell bank.”

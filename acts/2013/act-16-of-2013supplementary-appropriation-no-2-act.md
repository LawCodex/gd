AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of $15,829,395.65
to the service of the State of Grenada for the period
1st October to 31st December, 2011.
[September 27th, 2013 ].
ENACTED by the Parliament of Grenada:–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION NO. 2 (2011)
ACT, 2013.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Appropriation.
Fund and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for
the period October 1st to December 31st 2011, a sum not
exceeding fifteen million, eight hundred and twenty nine
thousand, three hundred and ninety five dollars and sixtyfive cents in addition to the sums already appropriated for
that year.

158 Act 16

Supplementary Appropriation No. 2 (2011)

(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is
appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

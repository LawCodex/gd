GRENADA
ACT NO. 5 OF 2013
I assent,

2nd May, 2013.

CARLYLE ARNOLD GLEAN
Governor-General.

AN ACT to authorize the Minister of Finance to borrow
from various sources, an amount of two hundred
million Eastern Caribbean dollars.
[ 10th May, 2013 ].
WHEREAS the 2013 Budget has a financing gap of one
hundred and thirty six million;
WHEREAS Unpaid Claims in the Treasury are
approximately ninety million;
WHEREAS it is necessary for the Government of Grenada
to secure funding to implement the 2013 Budget as well as
reduce the level of Unpaid Claims existing from previous
years;

WHEREAS section 49 of the Public Finance Management
Act 262B of the 2010 Continuous Revised Edition of the
Laws of Grenada, authorises the Minister of Finance to secure
a liability by borrowing for the purposes specified therein,
including for any purpose of a capital nature;
AND WHEREAS the Minister of Finance proposes to
borrow from various sources, on behalf of the Government of
Grenada an amount of two hundred million Eastern Caribbean
dollars for the purpose aforementioned in the manner
provided for under this Act;
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same as
follows–

Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–
2013 BUDGET LOAN AUTHORISATION ACT, 2013.

Authority to
raise loan.

### 2.

—(1) The Minister of Finance is hereby authorised to
raise a loan in an amount of two hundred million Eastern
Caribbean dollars for the purpose of raising funds to close the
financing gap for the 2013 Budget.
(2) All monies borrowed under this Act shall be
effected and secured pursuant to the terms and conditions
specified in the Schedule.

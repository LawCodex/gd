### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

BANKING (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2013.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Banking Act, Cap. 26A.

Interpretation.

Act 29

Amendment of
section 2 of
principal Act.

2013

### 3.

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by inserting
the following definition in its proper alphabetical order–
“shell bank” means a bank incorporated in a
jurisdiction in which it has no physical presence
involving meaningful decision-making and
management and which is unaffiliated to a
licensed financial institution;”.

Insertion of
section 5A

### 4.

The principal Act is amended by inserting the following
section after section 5–
“5A. Shell banks
The Minister shall not grant a licence to a shell bank.”

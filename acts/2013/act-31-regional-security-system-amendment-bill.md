Authority of the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

REGIONAL SECURITY SYSTEM (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2013

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Regional Security System
Act Cap 279A of the 2010 Continuous Revised
Edition of the Laws of Grenada.

Interpretation.

292 Act 31
Amendment
of section 2 of
principal Act.

### 3.

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended as follows–
(a)

by deleting the definition of the word
“Coordinator”;

(b)

by inserting after the definition of the
term “dependent” the following new
definition–

“Executive Director” means the Executive Director
of the Regional Security System established by
the treaty;
General
amendment.

### 4.

The principal Act is amended by deleting the word
“Coordinator” wherever it appears throughout the Act,
and substituting therefor in each case, the term “Executive
Director”.

# Internatioal Companies (Amendment)

## ARRANGEMENT OF CLAUSES

1\.

Short Title and commencement

2\.

Interpretation

3\.

Amendment of section 110 (1)

GRENADA
ACT NO. 7 OF 2013
I assent,

26th July, 2013.

CECILE E. F. LA GRENADE
Governor-General.

AN ACT to amend the International Companies Act Cap. 152
of the 2010 Continuous Revised Edition of the Laws of
Grenada.
[ 16th August, 2013 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same as
follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–
INTERNATIONAL COMPANIES (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2013.
and shall be deemed to have come into force on the date of
commencement of the principal Act.

Short title and
commencement.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the International companies Act
CAP. 152 of the 2010 Continuous Revised
Edition of the Laws of Grenada.

Amendment of
section 110 (1).

### 3.

Section 110 (1) of the principal Act is amended by
repealing the words “from the commencement of this Act”
and substituting the words “from the incorporation of the
company”.

Passed in the House of Representatives this 28th day of May, 2013.

RAPHAEL DONALD
Acting Clerk to the House of Representatives.

Passed in the Senate this 14th day of June, 2013.

RAPHAEL DONALD
Acting Clerk to the Senate.

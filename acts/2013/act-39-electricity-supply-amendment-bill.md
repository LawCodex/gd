558 Act 39

“Duty free importation of goods
13.—(1) During the continuance of the licence, the
company shall pay fifty percent of all customs and other
import duties and landing tax on the importation of all plant,
machinery, equipment, meters, instruments, vehicles, fuel,
lubricants and materials (including replacement parts and
spares) imported by the Company for the purpose of the
Company’s undertaking.
(2) Where the company imports any plant, machinery,
equipment, meters, instruments, vehicles, fuel, lubricants
or materials (including replacement parts and spares) for
resale, hire or for the private use of any of the company’s
employees the full amount of all customs and other import
duties and landing tax is payable.
(3) Upon the importation of a private motor vehicle
the company shall pay all customs and other import duties
and landing tax. Notwithstanding, the company may apply
for and the Government may grant an exemption of no more
than fifty percent of the duties and taxes payable under this
sub-section.
For the purposes of this section the term “private
motor vehicles has the meaning assigned to it in the Road
Traffic Act Cap 289A”.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

EMBARKATION TAX (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2013

### 2.

Section 3 of the Embarkation Tax Act Cap 87 is Amendment to
section 3
amended by repealing subsection (1) and substituting the
following sub-section–

288 Act 30

“(1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, there shall
be raised, levied and collected, an embarkation tax to be paid
by every traveler who embarks upon a ship, or who proposes
to embark on a ship, for any port or place outside Grenada.”

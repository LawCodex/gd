AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of $11,676,811.97
to the service of the State of Grenada for the period
1st October to 31st December, 2012.
[September 27th, 2013 ].
ENACTED by the Parliament of Grenada:–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION NO. 2 (2012)
ACT, 2013.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Appropriation.
Fund and applied to the service of the State of Grenada
for the period October 1st to December 31st 2012, a sum
not exceeding eleven million, six hundred and seventy six
thousand, eight hundred and eleven dollars and ninety-seven
cents in addition to the sums already appropriated for that
year.

(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is
appropriated for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

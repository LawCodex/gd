### 3.

Section 27 of the principal Act is amended by inserting
after section 27B the following section:
“27C. Prohibition on use of bearer share
(1) A Bearer Share Agent shall not use bearer shares
issued under this Act for the purposes of money
laundering and terrorist financing,
(2) A Bearer Share Agent who contravenes
sub-section (1) commits an offence and is liable on
conviction on indictment to a fine not exceeding one
hundred thousand dollars or to a term of imprisonment
not exceeding ten years.”

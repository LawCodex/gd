AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of $2,659,680.00
to the service of the State of Grenada for the period
January 1st to June 30th 2013.
[September 27th, 2013 ].
ENACTED by the Parliament of Grenada:–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION NO. 1
ACT, 2013.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund Appropriation.
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the
period January 1st to June 30th 2013, a sum not exceeding
two million, six hundred and fifty-nine thousand, six hundred
and eighty dollars and zero cents in addition to the sums
already appropriated for that year.

AN ACT to amend the Income Tax Act Cap. 149 of the 2010
Continuous Revised Edition of the Laws of Grenada.
[31st December, 2013].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and House
of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority of the
same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

INCOME TAX (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2013.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Income Tax Act Cap. 149.

Interpretation.

568 Act 41
Amendment of
section 25 of
principal Act.

### 3.

Section 25 of the principal Act is amended by
repealing paragraph “j” and substituting therefor the
following–
“(j) any payment received from January 1st, 2013,
as severance, unemployment compensation or
termination pay, by an employee who, as a result
of administrative downsizing, is terminated,
laid off, fired or displaced from his or her
employment.
Provided that all income other than the payments
referred to above, earned subsequent to the date
of severance from January 1st 2013, shall not be
exempted from income tax in accordance with
the provisions of this Act.”

Amendment of
section 50 of
principal Act.

### 4.

Section 50 of the principal Act is amended–
(a)

by repealing sub-section (1) and substituting
the following–
“(1) Where a person makes payment
to any other person not resident in
Grenada, not exempt under section 25(1)
(aa), of discounts, commissions, fees,
management charge, rent, lease premium,
licence charge, royalties, or expenses
allocated to a resident branch or agency by
a non resident company or other payment
whether or not the payer is entitled to
deduct such payment in computing the
chargeable income of a business, the payer
shall deduct tax at the rate specified in the
third schedule and pay the amount of tax
so deducted to the Comptroller within
seven days after the date of payment or
credit to the payee.

Provided that the provisions of this section
shall not apply to interest payments on
Bank deposits made to non-resident
persons.”
(b)

by inserting after sub-section 5 the following
sub-section–
“(6) For the purposes of this section a
resident branch of a foreign company and
its headquarters and other non-resident
branches shall be regarded as separate
persons carrying on separate businesses.”

Passed by the House of Representatives this 13th day of December, 2013.

### 3.

Insertion of new

### 4.

The principal Act is amended by inserting after section
section in
33 the following new section–
principal Act.

“Licensing of other entities by Minister on
recommendation of the authority
33A(1) No entity offering financial services and
products shall operate in or from Grenada unless
licensed in Grenada.
(2) Where an entity acts in contravention of
sub-section (1), every person who at the time of
the commission of the offence was a Director,
Manager, Secretary or other officer of the entity
commits an offence, and is liable on conviction
on indictment to a fine not exceeding five
hundred thousand dollars or to a term of
imprisonment not exceeding five years.
(3) The Minister of Finance on the
recommendation of the Authority, may license
any entity offering financial services and products
in or from Grenada not covered under any other
enactment.

(4) The Minister may make regulations
specifically for the payment of fees and the
supervision of the entities referred to in
sub-section (3).
(5) For the purposes of this section, Financial
Services includes any activity involving the
management of funds or risks on behalf of
another person, including, but not limited to, the
acceptance of deposits, granting of loans,
coverage of risks, transfer of funds, purchase and
sale of securities, managing of trusts, purchase
and sale of foreign currency”.

Passed in the House of Representatives this 28th day of May, 2013.

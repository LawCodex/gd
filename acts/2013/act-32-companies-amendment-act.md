---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

COMPANIES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2013.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Companies Act Cap. 58A.

Interpretation.

Act 32

section 328 of

Act.

Companies (Amendment)

### 3.

Section 328 is hereby amended by inserting after subsection (3) the following sub-section:
“(4a) A non-profit company shall be registered in
accordance with the provisions of this Act”.

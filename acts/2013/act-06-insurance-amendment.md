CARLYLE ARNOLD GLEAN

AN ACT to amend the Insurance Act CAP. 150 of the
2010 Continuous Revised Edition of the Laws of
Grenada.
[ 10th May, 2013 ].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives of Grenada, and by the authority of
the same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–
INSURANCE (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2013.
and shall be read as one with the Insurance Act CAP. 150 of
the 2010 Continuous Revised Edition of the Laws of Grenada,
hereinafter referred to as the “principal Act.”

Short title.

Insertion of new

### 2.

The principal Act is amended by inserting after section
section in
212
the following new section–
principal Act

“212A (1) A company conducting general insurance
business and an association of underwriters, shall
pay a levy in accordance with Part I of Schedule
VII.
(2) The levy referred to in subsection (1) must be
paid directly to the Supervisor within twenty-one
days of the end of each month.
(3) A company conducting long term insurance
business shall pay a deposit directly to the
Supervisor, from its insurance fund investments
in accordance with Part II of Schedule VII.
(4) Where a company conducting general
insurance business or an association of underwriters fails to pay the levy or pays the levy
outside the stipulated time period as provided for
in sub-sections (1) and (2) a penalty of–
(i)

10% of the levy for the first month; and

(ii)

1% of the levy for each month the levy
remains unpaid thereafter,

shall be payable, to the Supervisor.
(5) The Minister may by order published in the Gazette,
amend Schedule VII.”
Insertion of new

### 3.

The principal Act is amended by
Schedule in
Schedule
VI the following new Schedule–
principal Act.

inserting after

“SCHEDULE VII
(Section 212A)

## PART I

CLASS OF INSURANCE

LEVY PAYABLE

Company conducting General Insurance Business and Association of
Underwriters

Levy of 1% on Gross Insurance premium
income, net of premium refunds

## PART II

CLASS OF INSURANCE

DEPOSIT PAYABLE

Company conducting Long Term Insurance Business

Deposit in the sum of $500,000.00 from
its Insurance Fund Investments. This
deposit shall be at zero interest rate.”

AN ACT to appropriate an additional sum of twenty million,
one hundred and nine-one thousand, four hundred and
ninety-nine dollars and fifty-eight cents ($20,191,499.58)
to the service of the State of Grenada for the period
January 1st to September 30th, 2012.
[10th May, 2013].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty,
by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the
House of Representatives, and by the authority of the same as
follows–

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

SUPPLEMENTARY APPROPRIATION (NO. 1)
(2012) ACT, 2013.

### 2.

—(1) There may be issued from the Consolidated Fund Appropriation.
and applied to the service of the State of Grenada for the
period January 1st to September 30th, 2012, a sum not
exceeding twenty million, one hundred and ninety-one
thousand, four hundred and ninety-nine dollars and fifty-eight

Act 2

Supplementary Appropriation (No. 1) (2012)

cents in addition to the sums already appropriated for that
year.
(2) The sum mentioned in subsection (1) is appropriated
for the purposes specified in the Schedule.

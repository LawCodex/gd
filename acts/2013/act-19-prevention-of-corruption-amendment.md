AN ACT to amend the Prevention of Corruption Act Cap.
252A of the Continuous Revised Edition of the Laws
of Grenada 2010.
[27th September, 2013].
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent
Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate
and the House of Representatives and by the authority of the
same as follows–

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the

Short title.

PREVENTION OF CORRUPTION (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2013.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Prevention of Corruption
Act Cap. 252A of the Continuous Revised
Edition of the Laws of Grenada 2010.

Interpretation.

Amendment
of section 2 of
principal Act.

### 3.

Section 2 of the principal Act is hereby amended as
follows–
(a) by inserting the following definition in
alphabetical order–
“child” has the meaning assigned to it
under the Domestic Violence Act Cap.
84;
“financial institution” has the meaning
assigned to it under the Proceeds of
Crime Act Cap. 256A;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible
for Legal Affairs; and
(b) by deleting the definition for “public officer”
and substituting the following new definition–
“public officer” has the meaning assigned
to it pursuant to section 111 of the
Constitution, Chapter 128A.

Amendment
of section 3 of
principal Act.

### 4.

Section (3)(c)(ii) of the principal Act is amended by
inserting the letter “s” immediately at the end of the word
“indicate” where it appears.

Amendment
of section 6 of
principal Act.

### 5.

Subsection (3) of section 6 of the principal Act is
amended by–
(a) repealing the word “foreign public official”
where it appears and substituting therefor the
word “public officer”;

(b) repealing the word “public officer” where it
appears and substituting therefor the word
“foreign public official.”

### 6.

Subsection (1) of section 12 is amended as follows–
(a) in paragraph (b) by deleting the word “person”
where it appears immediately after the word
“such” and replace with the word “period”;
and

Amendment of
section 12 of
principal Act.

(b) in paragraph (f) by deleting the word “bank”
where it appears before the word “to” and
replace with the words “financial institution”.

### 7.

Section 19 of the principal Act is amended as follows–
(a) in the heading by deleting the word “employer”
where it appears immediately after the word
“or” and replace it with the word “employee”;
and

Amendment of
section 19 of
principal Act.

(b) by inserting an “s” at the end of the word
“obstruct” occurring immediately after the
word “or”.

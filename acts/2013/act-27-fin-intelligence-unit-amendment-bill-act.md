### 1.

This Act may be cited as the–

Short title.

FINANCIAL INTELLIGENCE UNIT (AMENDMENT)
ACT, 2013.

### 2.

In this Act–
“principal Act” means the Financial Intelligence Unit
Act, No. 14 of 2012.

act.

Financial Intelligence Unit (Amendment)

2013

### 3.

Section 33 of the principal Act is amended by inserting
after section 33 the following section:
“33A. A request for information sent to the Unit by a
foreign Financial Intelligence Unit should not be
refused solely on the ground that it relates to
fiscal matters”.

Passed in the House of Representatives this 30th day of October, 2013.
